# dipajyotir

## śubham
ids: dj-subham
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śubha
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The ending is also nominative. karoti takes it as what is brought about. Anusvāra before karoti.
meaning: auspiciousness

## karoti
ids: dj-karoti
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
class: tan
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: No subject is spoken. The light addressed in the next line is the one that brings these about.
meaning: brings about

## kalyāṇam
ids: dj-kalyanam
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kalyāṇa
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The ending is also nominative. Anusvāra before ārogyam.
meaning: welfare

## ārogyam
ids: dj-arogyam
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ārogya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: a + roga, “health.” The ending is also nominative. Anusvāra before dhanasampadā.
meaning: health

## dhanasampadā
ids: dj-dhana
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dhanasampad
gender: feminine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: pad
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: dhana “wealth” + sampad “prosperity,” from sam + pad. Another common line reads dhanasampadaḥ.
meaning: with wealth

## śatrubuddhivināśāya
ids: dj-satru
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śatrubuddhivināśa
gender: masculine
case: dative
number: singular
root: naś
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: śatru “foe” + buddhi “intent” + vināśa “destruction.” Dative of purpose.
meaning: for the destruction of hostile intent

## dīpajyotiḥ
ids: dj-jyotir
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dīpajyotis
gender: neuter
case: vocative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: dīpa “lamp” + jyotis “light.” Before n, the visarga is written r. The vocative has the same shape as the nominative.
meaning: O light of the lamp

## namaḥ
ids: dj-namo
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: namas
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Before a vowel, namaḥ is written namo and joins astu.
meaning: homage

## astu
ids: dj-astu
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The a of astu is written with the avagraha after namo.
meaning: may it be

## te
ids: dj-te
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: dative
number: singular
note: yuṣmad has no gender. The form is also genitive. Here it is the one who is honored, the lamp-light.
meaning: to you
