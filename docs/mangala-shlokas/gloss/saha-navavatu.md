# saha-navavatu

## oṃ
ids: sn-om-1, sn-om-2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: primordial sound

## saha
ids: sn-saha-1, sn-saha-2, sn-saha-3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: together

## nāv
ids: sn-nau-1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: dual
note: asmad has no gender. nau before a vowel is written nāv. The form is also dative and genitive; avatu takes the person protected.
meaning: us two

## avatu
ids: sn-avatu
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: av
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The a of avatu is written on nāv.
meaning: may he protect

## nau
ids: sn-nau-2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: dual
note: asmad has no gender. The form is also dative and genitive; bhunaktu takes the person nourished.
meaning: us two

## bhunaktu
ids: sn-bhunaktu
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhuj
class: rudh
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Class 7. The nasal is not in the root.
meaning: may he nourish

## vīryam
ids: sn-viryam
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: vīryam is nominative or accusative singular of vīrya.
meaning: vigor

## karavāvahai
ids: sn-karavavahai
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
class: tan
tense: command
voice: ātmanepada
person: 1st
number: dual
note: The -vahai ending is the first-person dual command.
meaning: may we two accomplish

## tejasvi
ids: sn-tejasvi
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: tejasvin
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Agrees with adhītam. The following nāv is written against it.
meaning: brilliant

## nāv
ids: sn-nau-3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: dual
note: asmad has no gender. nau before a vowel is written nāv. The form is also accusative and dative; the study is what the two possess.
meaning: of us two

## adhītam
ids: sn-adhitam
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: adhīta
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: i
note: Built from adhi + i. A past participle used as the noun “what is studied.” Its first a is the inherent vowel of nāv.
meaning: what is studied

## astu
ids: sn-astu
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The a of astu is the inherent vowel of adhītam.
meaning: may it be

## mā
ids: sn-ma
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: do not

## vidviṣāvahai
ids: sn-vidvisavahai
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dviṣ
prefix: vi
class: ad
tense: command
voice: ātmanepada
person: 1st
number: dual
note: The -vahai ending is the first-person dual command. mā forbids it.
meaning: may we two quarrel

## śāntiḥ
ids: sn-santih-1, sn-santih-2, sn-santih-3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śānti
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: śam
note: No verb is spoken. Peace is wished.
meaning: peace
