# samudravasane

## samudravasane
ids: sm-samudra
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: samudravasanā
gender: feminine
case: vocative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: samudra “ocean” + vasana “garment.” Vocative of the feminine ā-stem, addressing devī.
meaning: ocean-clothed

## devi
ids: sm-devi
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: devī
gender: feminine
case: vocative
number: singular
note: The ī-stem vocative shortens ī to i.
meaning: O goddess

## parvatastanamaṇḍale
ids: sm-parvata
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: parvatastanamaṇḍalā
gender: feminine
case: vocative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: parvata “mountain” + stana “bosom” + maṇḍala “circle.” Vocative of the feminine ā-stem, addressing devī.
meaning: mountain-bosomed

## viṣṇupatni
ids: sm-patni
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: viṣṇupatnī
gender: feminine
case: vocative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: viṣṇu + patnī “wife.” The ī-stem vocative shortens ī to i.
meaning: wife of Viṣṇu

## namaḥ
ids: sm-namas
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: namas
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: No verb is spoken. Before t, namaḥ is written namas and joins tubhyam.
meaning: homage

## tubhyam
ids: sm-tubhyam
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: dative
number: singular
note: yuṣmad has no gender. The one who is honored.
meaning: to you

## pādasparśam
ids: sm-pada
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pādasparśa
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: spṛś
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: pāda “foot” + sparśa “touch.” The ending is also nominative. kṣamasva takes the thing forgiven.
meaning: the touch of the feet

## kṣamasva
ids: sm-ksamasva
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: kṣam
class: bhū
tense: command
voice: ātmanepada
person: 2nd
number: singular
meaning: forgive

## me
ids: sm-me
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: dative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The form is also genitive. Here it is the person who asks to be forgiven.
meaning: me
