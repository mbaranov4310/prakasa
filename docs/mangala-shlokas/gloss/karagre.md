# karagre

## karāgre
ids: kg-karagre
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karāgra
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: kara “hand” + agra “tip.”
meaning: at the tip of the hand

## vasate
ids: kg-vasate
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vas
class: bhū
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Spoken once, for Lakṣmī, Sarasvatī, and Govinda.
meaning: dwells
