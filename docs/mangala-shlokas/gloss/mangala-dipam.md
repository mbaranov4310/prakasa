# mangala-dipam

## sājyam
ids: dp-sajyam
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sājya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: sa + ājya “ghee.” Agrees with the lamp. The ending is also nominative.
meaning: with ghee

## trivarti
ids: dp-trivarti
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: trivarti
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: tri “three” + varti “wick.” An i-stem accusative ends in i, the same shape as the nominative. Agrees with the lamp.
meaning: three-wicked

## saṃyuktam
ids: dp-samyuktam
kind: participle

root: yuj
prefix: sam
participle: past
stem: saṃyukta
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Agrees with the lamp. The ending is also nominative.
meaning: joined

## trailokyatimirāpahā
ids: dp-apaha
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: trailokyatimirāpahā
gender: feminine
case: vocative
number: singular
root: han
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: trailokya “the three worlds” + timira “darkness” + apahā “remover.” An ā-stem vocative is normally -e. The verse uses the nominative shape as the address.
meaning: O remover of the darkness of the three worlds

## divyajyotiḥ
ids: dp-jyotir
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: divyajyotis
gender: neuter
case: vocative
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: divya “divine” + jyotis “light.” Before n, the visarga is written r. The vocative has the same shape as the nominative.
meaning: O divine light

## namaḥ
ids: dp-namo
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: namas
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Before a vowel, namaḥ is written namo and joins astu.
meaning: homage

## astu
ids: dp-astu
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The a of astu is written with the avagraha after namo.
meaning: may it be

## te
ids: dp-te
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: dative
number: singular
note: yuṣmad has no gender. The form is also genitive. Here it is the one who is honored.
meaning: to you
