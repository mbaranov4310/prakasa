# suklambara-dharam

## śuklāmbaradharaṃ
ids: sb-sukla
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śuklāmbaradhara
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: śukla “white” + ambara “garment” + dhara “wearing.” The ending agrees with viṣṇum. Anusvāra before viṣṇum.
meaning: wearing white clothes

## viṣṇum
ids: sb-visnum
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: viṣṇu
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The name means the all-pervading one. The verse is also recited for Gaṇeśa. Anusvāra before śaśivarṇam.
meaning: Viṣṇu

## śaśivarṇaṃ
ids: sb-sasi
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śaśivarṇa
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: śaśi “moon” + varṇa “color.” Agrees with viṣṇum. Anusvāra before caturbhujam.
meaning: moon-colored

## caturbhujaṃ
ids: sb-catur
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: caturbhuja
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: catur “four” + bhuja “arm.” Agrees with viṣṇum. Written with anusvāra at the pause.
meaning: four-armed

## prasannavadanaṃ
ids: sb-prasanna
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: prasannavadana
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: prasanna “pleasant” + vadana “face.” Agrees with viṣṇum. Anusvāra before dhyāyet.
meaning: pleasant-faced

## dhyāyet
ids: sb-dhyayet
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dhyā
class: bhū
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: No subject is spoken. The ending -et is the potential.
meaning: one should meditate

## sarvavighnopaśāntaye
ids: sb-sarva
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarvavighnopaśānti
gender: feminine
case: dative
number: singular
root: śam
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: sarva “all” + vighna “obstacle” + upaśānti “calming.” vighna + upa is written vighno. Dative of purpose.
meaning: for the calming of all obstacles
