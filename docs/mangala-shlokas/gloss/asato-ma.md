# asato-ma

## asato
ids: am-asato
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: asat
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: asataḥ before mā is written asato. The ending is also genitive; “from the unreal” is ablative.
meaning: from the unreal

## mā
ids: am-ma-1, am-ma-2, am-ma-3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. This mā is the accusative “me,” not the prohibitive particle.
meaning: me

## sad
