```samhita
अजोऽपि सन्नव्ययात्मा
भूतानामीश्वरोऽपि सन् ।
प्रकृतिं स्वामधिष्ठाय
संभवाम्यात्ममायया ॥
```

```translation
Though I am unborn and my nature does not waste away, and though I am the lord of beings, I take charge of my own nature and come to be by my own maya.
```

```phrase
Though unborn, and of a nature that does not waste away,
---
and though the lord of beings,
---
having taken charge of my own nature,
---
I come to be by my own maya.
```
