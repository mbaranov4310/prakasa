```samhita
चातुर्वर्ण्यं मया सृष्टं गुणकर्मविभागशः ।
तस्य कर्तारमपि मां विद्ध्यकर्तारमव्ययम् ॥
```

```translation
The four orders were brought forth by me, according to the division of the qualities and of actions. Though I am the maker of that, know me as one who does not act, and as unchanging.
```

```phrase
The four orders were brought forth by me, according to the division of the qualities and of actions.
---
Though I am the maker of that, know me as one who does not act, and as unchanging.
```
