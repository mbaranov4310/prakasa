```samhita
न मां कर्माणि लिम्पन्ति न मे कर्मफले स्पृहा ।
इति मां योऽभिजानाति कर्मभिर्न स बध्यते ॥
```

```translation
Actions do not stain me, and I have no craving for the fruit of action. He who knows me in this way is not bound by actions.
```

