```samhita
श्रद्धावांल्लभते ज्ञानं तत्परः संयतेन्द्रियः ।
ज्ञानं लब्ध्वा परां शान्तिमचिरेणाधिगच्छति ॥
```

```translation
The man of faith, intent on the means and with his senses drawn in, obtains knowledge. Having gained it, he reaches the highest peace before long.
```

```phrase
The man of faith, intent on the means, with his senses drawn in, obtains knowledge.
---
Having gained knowledge, he reaches the highest peace before long.
```
