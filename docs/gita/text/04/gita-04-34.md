```samhita
तद्विद्धि प्रणिपातेन परिप्रश्नेन सेवया ।
उपदेक्ष्यन्ति ते ज्ञानं ज्ञानिनस्तत्त्वदर्शिनः ॥
```

```translation
Learn that by a full prostration, by questioning, and by service. Those who know, and who see the truth, will teach you that knowledge.
```

```phrase
Learn that by a full prostration, by questioning, and by service.
---
Those who know, and who see the truth, will teach you the knowledge.
```
