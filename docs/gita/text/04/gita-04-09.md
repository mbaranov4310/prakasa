```samhita
जन्म कर्म च मे दिव्यमेवं यो वेत्ति तत्त्वतः ।
त्यक्त्वा देहं पुनर्जन्म नैति मामेति सोऽर्जुन ॥
```

```translation
He who knows my birth and my action as divine, and knows them as they truly are, does not go on to another birth when he leaves the body. He comes to me, Arjuna.
```
