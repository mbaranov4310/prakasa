```samhita
अज्ञश्चाश्रद्दधानश्च संशयात्मा विनश्यति ।
नायं लोकोऽस्ति न परो न सुखं संशयात्मनः ॥
```

```translation
The ignorant man, the man without faith, and the man whose mind is doubt all perish. For the doubter there is neither this world, nor the next, nor happiness.
```

```phrase
The ignorant man, the man without faith, and the man whose mind is doubt perish.
---
For the doubter there is not this world, nor the next, nor happiness.
```
