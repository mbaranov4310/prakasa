```samhita
वीतरागभयक्रोधा मन्मया मामुपाश्रिताः ।
बहवो ज्ञानतपसा पूता मद्भावमागताः ॥
```

```translation
Many, from whom passion, fear, and anger have gone, who are absorbed in me and have taken refuge in me, have been made pure by the austerity of knowledge and have reached my state.
```

```phrase

have been made pure by the austerity of knowledge and have reached my state.
```
