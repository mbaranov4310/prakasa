```samhita
यज्ञशिष्टामृतभुजो यान्ति ब्रह्म सनातनम् ।
नायं लोकोऽस्त्ययज्ञस्य कुतोऽन्यः कुरुसत्तम ॥
```

```translation
Those who eat what remains of the sacrifice go to the eternal Brahman. Even this world is not for the man who offers none of these, O best of the Kurus. How could another, higher, end be his?
```

```phrase
Those who eat what remains of the sacrifice go to the eternal Brahman.
---
This world is not for the man who offers no sacrifice, O best of the Kurus. How could another be?
```
