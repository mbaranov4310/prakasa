```samhita
ये यथा मां प्रपद्यन्ते तांस्तथैव भजाम्यहम् ।
मम वर्त्मानुवर्तन्ते मनुष्याः पार्थ सर्वशः ॥
```

```translation
In whatever way people resort to me, in that same way I favor them. Men follow my path in every way, O son of Pritha.
```

```phrase

Men follow my path in every way, O son of Pritha.
```
