```samhita
द्रव्ययज्ञास्तपोयज्ञा योगयज्ञास्तथापरे ।
स्वाध्यायज्ञानयज्ञाश्च यतयः संशितव्रताः ॥
```

```translation
There are men whose sacrifice is a gift of wealth, men whose sacrifice is austerity, and men whose sacrifice is yoga; and others, men of effort whose sacrifice is recitation and knowledge, their vows made keen.
```

```phrase
Men whose sacrifice is a gift of wealth, austerity, or yoga, and then others,
---
and men whose sacrifice is recitation and knowledge: they are men of effort, their vows made keen.
```
