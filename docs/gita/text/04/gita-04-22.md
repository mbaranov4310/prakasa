```samhita
यदृच्छालाभसंतुष्टो द्वन्द्वातीतो विमत्सरः ।
समः सिद्धावसिद्धौ च कृत्वापि न निबध्यते ॥
```

```translation
Content with a gain that comes of itself, unshaken by the pairs and free of envy, he is the same when that gain comes and when it does not. Even while doing the act, he is not bound.
```

