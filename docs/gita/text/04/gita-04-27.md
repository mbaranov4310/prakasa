```samhita
सर्वाणीन्द्रियकर्माणि प्राणकर्माणि चापरे ।
आत्मसंयमयोगाग्नौ जुह्वति ज्ञानदीपिते ॥
```

```translation
Others cast all the actions of the senses, and the actions of the vital breaths, into the fire of the yoga of self-restraint, kindled by knowledge.
```

