```samhita
श्रोत्रादीनीन्द्रियाण्यन्ये संयमाग्निषु जुह्वति ।
शब्दादीन्विषयानन्य इन्द्रियाग्निषु जुह्वति ॥
```

```translation
Some offer hearing and the other senses into the fires of restraint. Another offers sound and the other objects into the fires that are the senses.
```

```phrase
Some offer hearing and the other senses into the fires of restraint.
---
Another offers sound and the other objects into the fires that are the senses.
```
