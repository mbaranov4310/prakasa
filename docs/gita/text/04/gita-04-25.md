```samhita
दैवमेवापरे यज्ञं योगिनः पर्युपासते ।
ब्रह्माग्नावपरे यज्ञं यज्ञेनैवोपजुह्वति ॥
```

```translation
Some ritualists attend only to sacrifice offered to the gods. Others offer the sacrifice into the fire of Brahman by the sacrifice itself.
```

```phrase
Some ritualists attend only to the sacrifice offered to the gods.
---
Others offer the sacrifice into the fire of Brahman by that sacrifice itself.
```
