```samhita
गतसङ्गस्य मुक्तस्य ज्ञानावस्थितचेतसः ।
यज्ञायाचरतः कर्म समग्रं प्रविलीयते ॥
```

```translation
For the man whose attachment has withdrawn, who is free, whose mind rests in knowledge, and who acts for the sake of sacrifice, action dissolves together with its fruit.
```

```phrase
Of the man whose attachment has withdrawn, who is free, and whose mind rests in knowledge,
---
action done for the sake of sacrifice dissolves, together with its fruit.
```
