```samhita
अपरे नियताहाराः प्राणान्प्राणेषु जुह्वति ।
सर्वेऽप्येते यज्ञविदो यज्ञक्षपितकल्मषाः ॥
```

```translation
Others, keeping their food limited, offer the breaths into the breath. All of these know sacrifice, and the sacrifices have destroyed their stain.
```

```phrase
Others, keeping their food limited, offer the breaths into the breath.
---
All of these know sacrifice, and their stain is destroyed by the sacrifices.
```
