```samhita
श्रीभगवानुवाच
इमं विवस्वते योगं प्रोक्तवानहमव्ययम् ।
विवस्वान्मनवे प्राह मनुरिक्ष्वाकवेऽब्रवीत् ॥
```

```translation
The blessed Lord said: I taught this imperishable yoga to Vivasvat. Vivasvat told it to Manu, and Manu told it to Ikshvaku.
```

```phrase
The blessed Lord said:
---
I taught this imperishable yoga to Vivasvat.
---
Vivasvat told it to Manu, and Manu told it to Ikshvaku.
```
