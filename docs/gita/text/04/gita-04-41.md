```samhita
योगसंन्यस्तकर्माणं ज्ञानसंछिन्नसंशयम् ।
आत्मवन्तं न कर्माणि निबध्नन्ति धनंजय ॥
```

```translation
Actions do not bind the attentive man who has laid action down by yoga and whose doubt knowledge has cut, O Dhananjaya.
```

```phrase
Him who has laid action down by yoga, and whose doubt knowledge has cut,
---
actions do not bind, the attentive man, O Dhananjaya.
```
