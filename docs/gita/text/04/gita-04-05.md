```samhita
श्रीभगवानुवाच
बहूनि मे व्यतीतानि जन्मानि तव चार्जुन ।
तान्यहं वेद सर्वाणि न त्वं वेत्थ परंतप ॥
```

```translation
The blessed Lord said: Many of my births have passed, Arjuna, and many of yours. I know them all. You do not know them, O scorcher of the foe.
```

```phrase
The blessed Lord said:
---
Many of my births have passed, Arjuna, and many of yours.
---
I know them all. You do not know them, O scorcher of the foe.
```
