```samhita
एवं ज्ञात्वा कृतं कर्म पूर्वैरपि मुमुक्षुभिः ।
कुरु कर्मैव तस्मात्त्वं पूर्वैः पूर्वतरं कृतम् ॥
```

```translation
Knowing this, those who sought release in the past also did action. Therefore do action yourself. It was done by the earlier ones long before.
```

```phrase
Knowing this, those who sought release in the past also did action.
```
