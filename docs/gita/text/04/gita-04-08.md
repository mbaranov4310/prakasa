```samhita
परित्राणाय साधूनां विनाशाय च दुष्कृताम् ।
धर्मसंस्थापनार्थाय संभवामि युगे युगे ॥
```

```translation
For the protection of the good, for the destruction of those who do evil, and to set dharma firm, I come to be in age after age.
```

```phrase
For the protection of the good, and for the destruction of those who do evil,
---
and to set dharma firm, I come to be in age after age.
```
