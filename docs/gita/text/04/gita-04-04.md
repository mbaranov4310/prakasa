```samhita
अर्जुन उवाच
अपरं भवतो जन्म परं जन्म विवस्वतः ।
कथमेतद्विजानीयां त्वमादौ प्रोक्तवानिति ॥
```

```translation
Arjuna said: Your birth was later, and the birth of Vivasvat was earlier. How am I to understand that you taught this in the beginning?
```

```phrase
Arjuna said:
---
Your birth was later, and the birth of Vivasvat was earlier.
---
How am I to understand that you taught this in the beginning?
```
