```samhita
एवं परम्पराप्राप्तमिमं राजर्षयो विदुः ।
स कालेनेह महता योगो नष्टः परंतप ॥
```

```translation
The royal sages knew this yoga, received in this way through the succession. In this world that yoga has been lost through the long lapse of time, O scorcher of the foe.
```

