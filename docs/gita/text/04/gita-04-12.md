```samhita
काङ्क्षन्तः कर्मणां सिद्धिं यजन्त इह देवताः ।
क्षिप्रं हि मानुषे लोके सिद्धिर्भवति कर्मजा ॥
```

```translation
Those who want the success of their actions sacrifice here to the gods. For in the human world, success born of action comes quickly.
```

