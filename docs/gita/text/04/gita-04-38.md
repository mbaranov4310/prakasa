```samhita
न हि ज्ञानेन सदृशं पवित्रमिह विद्यते ।
तत्स्वयं योगसंसिद्धः कालेनात्मनि विन्दति ॥
```

```translation
Nothing here purifies as knowledge does. The man himself, made fit by yoga, finds that knowledge in himself in time.
```

```phrase
Nothing here is as purifying as knowledge.
---
The man himself, made fit by yoga, finds it in himself in time.
```
