```samhita
निराशीर्यतचित्तात्मा त्यक्तसर्वपरिग्रहः ।
शारीरं केवलं कर्म कुर्वन्नाप्नोति किल्बिषम् ॥
```

```translation
One from whom wishes have gone, whose mind and body are held in check, and by whom every acquisition has been given up, does not incur a stain when he does only the action that keeps the body alive.
```

