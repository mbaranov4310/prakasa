```samhita
कर्मणो ह्यपि बोद्धव्यं बोद्धव्यं च विकर्मणः ।
अकर्मणश्च बोद्धव्यं गहना कर्मणो गतिः ॥
```

```translation
Even of action there is something to be known, and of mis-action, and of non-action. The course of action is deep.
```

```phrase
Even of action there is something to be known, and of mis-action.
---
And of non-action there is something to be known. The course of action is deep.
```
