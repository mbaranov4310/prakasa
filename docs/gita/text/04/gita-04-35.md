```samhita
यज्ज्ञात्वा न पुनर्मोहमेवं यास्यसि पाण्डव ।
येन भूतान्यशेषेण द्रक्ष्यस्यात्मन्यथो मयि ॥
```

```translation
Knowing that, you will not fall into confusion again as you have now, O son of Pandu. By it you will see every being in the self, and in Me.
```

```phrase
Knowing that, you will not fall again into confusion as you have now, O son of Pandu.
---
By it you will see every being in the self, and then in Me.
```
