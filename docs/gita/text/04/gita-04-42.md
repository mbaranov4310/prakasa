```samhita
तस्मादज्ञानसंभूतं हृत्स्थं ज्ञानासिनात्मनः ।
छित्त्वैनं संशयं योगमातिष्ठोत्तिष्ठ भारत ॥
```

```translation
Therefore cut this doubt, born of ignorance and seated in the heart, with the sword of your own knowledge. Stand in yoga, and rise, O Bharata.
```

```phrase
Therefore, with the sword of your own knowledge, cut this doubt born of ignorance and seated in the heart.
---
Stand in yoga, and rise, O Bharata.
```
