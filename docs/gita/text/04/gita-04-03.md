```samhita
स एवायं मया तेऽद्य योगः प्रोक्तः पुरातनः ।
भक्तोऽसि मे सखा चेति रहस्यं ह्येतदुत्तमम् ॥
```

```translation
This same ancient yoga I have now taught to you. You are devoted to me, and you are my friend; for this is the highest secret.
```
