```samhita
श्रेयान्द्रव्यमयाद्यज्ञाज्ज्ञानयज्ञः परंतप ।
सर्वं कर्माखिलं पार्थ ज्ञाने परिसमाप्यते ॥
```

```translation
The sacrifice of knowledge is better than a sacrifice made of material things, O scorcher of the foe. All action, the whole of it, comes to rest in knowledge, O son of Pritha.
```

```phrase
Better than a sacrifice made of material things is the sacrifice of knowledge, O scorcher of the foe.
---
All action, the whole of it, comes to rest in knowledge, O son of Pritha.
```
