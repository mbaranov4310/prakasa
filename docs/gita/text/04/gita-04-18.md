```samhita
कर्मण्यकर्म यः पश्येदकर्मणि च कर्म यः ।
स बुद्धिमान्मनुष्येषु स युक्तः कृत्स्नकर्मकृत् ॥
```

```translation
He who sees non-action in action, and action in non-action, is wise among men. He is a yogin, one who has done all that is to be done.
```

