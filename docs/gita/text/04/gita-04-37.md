```samhita
यथैधांसि समिद्धोऽग्निर्भस्मसात्कुरुतेऽर्जुन ।
ज्ञानाग्निः सर्वकर्माणि भस्मसात्कुरुते तथा ॥
```

```translation
As a well-kindled fire makes fuel into ash, O Arjuna, so the fire of knowledge makes all actions into ash.
```

```phrase
As a well-kindled fire makes fuel into ash, O Arjuna,
---
so the fire of knowledge makes all actions into ash.
```
