```samhita
त्यक्त्वा कर्मफलासङ्गं नित्यतृप्तो निराश्रयः ।
कर्मण्यभिप्रवृत्तोऽपि नैव किंचित्करोति सः ॥
```

```translation
Having given up the claim of being the doer in action, and attachment to its fruit, always content and with nothing he leans on, he does nothing at all, even when he is fully engaged in action.
```
