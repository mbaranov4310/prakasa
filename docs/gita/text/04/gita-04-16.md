```samhita
किं कर्म किमकर्मेति
कवयोऽप्यत्र मोहिताः ।
तत्ते कर्म प्रवक्ष्यामि
यज्ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसेऽशुभात् ॥
```

```translation
What is action, and what is non-action? Even the wise are confused about this. I shall declare that action to you. Knowing it, you will be freed from what is unholy.
```

```phrase
What is action, and what is non-action?
---
Even the wise are confused about this.
---
I shall declare that action to you,
---
and, knowing it, you will be freed from what is unholy.
```
