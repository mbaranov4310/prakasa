```samhita
यदा यदा हि धर्मस्य ग्लानिर्भवति भारत ।
अभ्युत्थानमधर्मस्य तदात्मानं सृजाम्यहम् ॥
```

```translation
Whenever dharma declines, O Bharata, and adharma rises, then I bring myself forth.
```

```phrase
Whenever dharma declines, O Bharata,
---
and adharma rises, then I bring myself forth.
```
