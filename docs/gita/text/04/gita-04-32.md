```samhita
एवं बहुविधा यज्ञा वितता ब्रह्मणो मुखे ।
कर्मजान्विद्धि तान्सर्वानेवं ज्ञात्वा विमोक्ष्यसे ॥
```

```translation
Many kinds of sacrifice are spread thus at the door of the Veda. Know them all as born of action. Knowing this, you will be released.
```

```phrase
Thus many kinds of sacrifice are spread at the door of the Veda.
---
Know them all as born of action. Knowing this, you will be released.
```
