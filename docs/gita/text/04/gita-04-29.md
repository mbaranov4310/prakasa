```samhita
अपाने जुह्वति प्राणं प्राणेऽपानं तथापरे ।
प्राणापानगती रुद्ध्वा प्राणायामपरायणाः ॥
```

```translation
Others, given over to restraint of the breath, offer the outgoing breath into the downward breath and the downward breath into the outgoing breath, and stop those two paths.
```

```phrase
Others offer the outgoing breath into the downward breath, and the downward breath into the outgoing breath.
---
Stopping those two paths, they are given over to restraint of the breath.
```
