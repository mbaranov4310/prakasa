```samhita
यस्य सर्वे समारम्भाः कामसंकल्पवर्जिताः ।
ज्ञानाग्निदग्धकर्माणं तमाहुः पण्डितं बुधाः ॥
```

```translation
He whose every undertaking is free of desire and of the resolve that gives rise to it, and whose actions have been burned by the fire of knowledge: him those who know call wise.
```

```phrase

```
