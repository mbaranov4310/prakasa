```samhita
अपि चेदसि पापेभ्यः सर्वेभ्यः पापकृत्तमः ।
सर्वं ज्ञानप्लवेनैव वृजिनं संतरिष्यसि ॥
```

```translation
Even if you are the one who has done the most wrong of all who do wrong, you will cross the whole of that wrong by the raft of knowledge alone.
```

```phrase
Even if you are, of all who do wrong, the one who has done the most,
---
you will cross the whole sea of wrong by the raft of knowledge alone.
```
