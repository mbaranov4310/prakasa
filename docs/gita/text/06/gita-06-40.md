```samhita
श्रीभगवानुवाच
पार्थ नैवेह नामुत्र विनाशस्तस्य विद्यते ।
न हि कल्याणकृत्कश्चिद्दुर्गतिं तात गच्छति ॥
```

```translation
The blessed Lord said: There is no ruin for him, O Pārtha, not in this world and not in the next. For no one who has done good goes to a vile course, dear one.
```

```phrase
The blessed Lord said:
---
O Pārtha, there is no ruin for him, not here and not in the next world.
---
For no one who has done good goes to a vile course, dear one.
```
