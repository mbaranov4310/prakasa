```samhita
पूर्वाभ्यासेन तेनैव ह्रियते ह्यवशोऽपि सः ।
जिज्ञासुरपि योगस्य शब्दब्रह्मातिवर्तते ॥
```

```translation
By that former practice he is carried along even against his will. Even one who only wishes to know yoga passes beyond the Brahman of the word.
```

```phrase
By that very former practice he is carried along, even against his will.
---
Even one who wishes to know yoga passes beyond the Brahman of sound.
```
