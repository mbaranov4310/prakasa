```samhita
सुखमात्यन्तिकं यत्तद्बुद्धिग्राह्यमतीन्द्रियम् ।
वेत्ति यत्र न चैवायं स्थितश्चलति तत्त्वतः ॥
```

```translation
that endless joy, which the intellect grasps and which lies beyond the senses, he knows at that time; and once established, this man does not slip from the truth at all.
```

```phrase
that endless joy, which the intellect grasps and which lies beyond the senses:
---
he knows it at that time; and once established, this man does not slip from the truth at all.
```
