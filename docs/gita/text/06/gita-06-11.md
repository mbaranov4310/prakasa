```samhita
शुचौ देशे प्रतिष्ठाप्य स्थिरमासनमात्मनः ।
नात्युच्छ्रितं नातिनीचं चैलाजिनकुशोत्तरम् ॥
```

```translation
Setting his own seat in a clean place, firm, not too high and not too low, with cloth, deerskin, and kuśa grass, the grass underneath.
```

