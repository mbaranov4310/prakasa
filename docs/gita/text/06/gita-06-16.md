```samhita
नात्यश्नतस्तु योगोऽस्ति न चैकान्तमनश्नतः ।
न चातिस्वप्नशीलस्य जाग्रतो नैव चार्जुन ॥
```

```translation
Yoga is not for one who eats too much, nor for one who does not eat at all, nor for one given to too much sleep, nor for one who stays awake too long, O Arjuna.
```

```phrase
Yoga is not for one who eats too much, nor for one who does not eat at all,
---
nor for one given to too much sleep, nor indeed for one who stays awake too long, O Arjuna.
```
