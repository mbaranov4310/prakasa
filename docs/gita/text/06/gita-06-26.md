```samhita
यतो यतो निश्चरति मनश्चञ्चलमस्थिरम् ।
ततस्ततो नियम्यैतदात्मन्येव वशं नयेत् ॥
```

```translation
Wherever the restless, unsteady mind runs out, from that one should check it, and bring this mind under control in the self alone.
```
