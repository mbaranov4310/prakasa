```samhita
उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानं नात्मानमवसादयेत् ।
आत्मैव ह्यात्मनो बन्धुरात्मैव रिपुरात्मनः ॥
```

```translation
One should lift oneself up by oneself, and not drag oneself down. For the self alone is the friend of the self, and the self alone is the enemy of the self.
```

```phrase
One should lift oneself up by oneself, and not drag oneself down.
```
