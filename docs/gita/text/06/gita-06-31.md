```samhita
सर्वभूतस्थितं यो मां भजत्येकत्वमास्थितः ।
सर्वथा वर्तमानोऽपि स योगी मयि वर्तते ॥
```

```translation
Who worships me as abiding in every being, having taken his stand in oneness: that yogin abides in me, even while living in every way.
```

