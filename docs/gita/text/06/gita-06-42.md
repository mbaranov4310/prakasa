```samhita
अथवा योगिनामेव कुले भवति धीमताम् ।
एतद्धि दुर्लभतरं लोके जन्म यदीदृशम् ॥
```

```translation
Or he is born in a family of yogins, of the wise. For a birth of this kind is harder to gain in the world.
```

```phrase
Or he is born in a family just of yogins, of the wise.
---
For this birth is harder to gain in the world, a birth of this kind.
```
