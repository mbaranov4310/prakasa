```samhita
युञ्जन्नेवं सदात्मानं योगी नियतमानसः ।
शान्तिं निर्वाणपरमां मत्संस्थामधिगच्छति ॥
```

```translation
Practicing in this way, always, with the mind held in, the yogin reaches the peace whose highest end is release, the peace that rests on me.
```

```phrase
Practicing in this way, always, the yogin, with the mind held in,

reaches the peace whose highest end is release, the peace that rests on me.
```
