```samhita
संकल्पप्रभवान्कामांस्त्यक्त्वा सर्वानशेषतः ।
मनसैवेन्द्रियग्रामं विनियम्य समन्ततः ॥
```

```translation
Having left every desire born of imagination, with nothing remaining, and having drawn the host of the senses in from every side by the mind alone,
```

```phrase
Having left every desire born of imagination, with nothing remaining,
---
and having drawn the host of the senses in from every side by the mind alone,
```
