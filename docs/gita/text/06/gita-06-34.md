```samhita
चञ्चलं हि मनः कृष्ण प्रमाथि बलवद्दृढम् ।
तस्याहं निग्रहं मन्ये वायोरिव सुदुष्करम् ॥
```

```translation
For the mind is fickle, O Krishna, agitating, strong, and firm. I think restraining it is harder even than restraining the wind.
```

```phrase
For the mind is fickle, O Krishna, agitating, strong, and firm.
---
I think restraining it is harder even than restraining the wind.
```
