```samhita
यत्रोपरमते चित्तं निरुद्धं योगसेवया ।
यत्र चैवात्मनात्मानं पश्यन्नात्मनि तुष्यति ॥
```

```translation
When the mind comes to rest, checked on every side by the practice of yoga, and when, seeing the self by the self, he rejoices in the self,
```

```phrase
When the mind comes to rest, checked on every side by the practice of yoga,
---
and when, seeing the self by the self, he rejoices in the self,
```
