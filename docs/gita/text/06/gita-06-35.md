```samhita
श्रीभगवानुवाच
असंशयं महाबाहो मनो दुर्निग्रहं चलम् ।
अभ्यासेन तु कौन्तेय वैराग्येण च गृह्यते ॥
```

```translation
The blessed Lord said: No doubt, O mighty-armed, the mind is hard to restrain and restless. But it is held, O son of Kuntī, by practice and by dispassion.
```

```phrase
The blessed Lord said:
---
No doubt, O mighty-armed, the mind is hard to restrain and restless.
---
But by practice, O son of Kuntī, and by dispassion, it is held.
```
