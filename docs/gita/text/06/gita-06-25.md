```samhita
शनैः शनैरुपरमेद्बुद्ध्या धृतिगृहीतया ।
आत्मसंस्थं मनः कृत्वा न किंचिदपि चिन्तयेत् ॥
```

```translation
Little by little one should draw back, by the intellect held in firmness; and having set the mind firm in the self, one should think of nothing at all.
```

