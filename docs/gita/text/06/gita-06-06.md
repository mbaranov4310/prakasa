```samhita
बन्धुरात्मात्मनस्तस्य येनात्मैवात्मना जितः ।
अनात्मनस्तु शत्रुत्वे वर्तेतात्मैव शत्रुवत् ॥
```

```translation
The self is the friend of that self by whom the self has been mastered by the self. But for one who has not mastered himself, the self itself acts in enmity, like an enemy.
```

```phrase
The self is the friend of that self by whom the self has been mastered by the self.
---
But for one who has not mastered himself, the self itself acts in enmity, like an enemy.
```
