```samhita
प्रयत्नाद्यतमानस्तु योगी संशुद्धकिल्बिषः ।
अनेकजन्मसंसिद्धस्ततो याति परां गतिम् ॥
```

```translation
The yogin who strives by effort, his sins washed clean and perfected through many births, then goes to the highest course.
```

```phrase
And the yogin who strives by effort, his sins washed clean,
---
perfected through many births, then goes to the highest course.
```
