```samhita
तत्र तं बुद्धिसंयोगं लभते पौर्वदेहिकम् ।
यतते च ततो भूयः संसिद्धौ कुरुनन्दन ॥
```

```translation
There he gains that union with the understanding which belonged to the former body. And from that he strives still more toward perfection, O joy of the Kurus.
```

