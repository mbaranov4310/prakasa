```samhita
ज्ञानविज्ञानतृप्तात्मा कूटस्थो विजितेन्द्रियः ।
युक्त इत्युच्यते योगी समलोष्टाश्मकाश्चनः ॥
```

```translation
He whose mind is content through knowledge and through direct realization, unshaken, with the senses mastered, is called a yogin, one to whom a clod, a stone, and gold are the same.
```
