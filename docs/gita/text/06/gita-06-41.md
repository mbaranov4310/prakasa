```samhita
प्राप्य पुण्यकृतां लोकानुषित्वा शाश्वतीः समाः ।
शुचीनां श्रीमतां गेहे योगभ्रष्टोऽभिजायते ॥
```

```translation
Reaching the worlds of those who have done merit, and dwelling there for everlasting years, one who has fallen from yoga is born in a house of the pure and the prosperous.
```

