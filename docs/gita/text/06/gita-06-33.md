```samhita
अर्जुन उवाच
योऽयं योगस्त्वया प्रोक्तः
साम्येन मधुसूदन ।
एतस्याहं न पश्यामि
चञ्चलत्वात्स्थितिं स्थिराम् ॥
```

```translation
Arjuna said: O Madhusūdana, this yoga which you have taught as sameness — I do not see a steady state of it, because of fickleness.
```

```phrase
Arjuna said:
---
This yoga which you have taught
---
as sameness, O Madhusūdana:
---
I do not see, of this,
---
because of fickleness, a steady state.
```
