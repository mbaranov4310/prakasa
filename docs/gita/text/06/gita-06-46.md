```samhita
तपस्विभ्योऽधिको योगी
ज्ञानिभ्योऽपि मतोऽधिकः ।
कर्मिभ्यश्चाधिको योगी
तस्माद्योगी भवार्जुन ॥
```

```translation
The yogin is higher than ascetics, and he is deemed higher also than the learned. The yogin is higher than ritualists as well. Therefore become a yogin, O Arjuna.
```

```phrase
The yogin is higher than ascetics.
---
He is deemed higher also than the learned.
---
And the yogin is higher than ritualists.
---
Therefore become a yogin, O Arjuna.
```
