```samhita
सर्वभूतस्थमात्मानं सर्वभूतानि चात्मनि ।
ईक्षते योगयुक्तात्मा सर्वत्र समदर्शनः ॥
```

```translation
He sees his own self standing in every being, and all beings in the self. His mind joined in yoga, he sees the same everywhere.
```

```phrase
His own self standing in every being, and all beings in the self,
---
he sees. His mind joined in yoga, he sees the same everywhere.
```
