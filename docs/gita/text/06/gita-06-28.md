```samhita
युञ्जन्नेवं सदात्मानं योगी विगतकल्मषः ।
सुखेन ब्रह्मसंस्पर्शमत्यन्तं सुखमश्नुते ॥
```

```translation
The yogin whose stain is gone, practicing in this way and ever on the self, easily reaches the endless joy of contact with Brahman.
```

```phrase
The yogin whose stain is gone, practicing in this way and ever on the self,
---
easily reaches the endless joy of contact with Brahman.
```
