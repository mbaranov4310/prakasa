```samhita
यदा हि नेन्द्रियार्थेषु न कर्मस्वनुषज्जते ।
सर्वसंकल्पसंन्यासी योगारूढस्तदोच्यते ॥
```

```translation
When he does not cling to the objects of the senses, nor to actions, then the man who renounces every resolve is said to have climbed to yoga.
```

```phrase
When indeed he does not cling to the objects of the senses, nor to actions,
---
then the man who renounces every resolve is said to have climbed to yoga.
```
