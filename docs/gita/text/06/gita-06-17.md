```samhita
युक्ताहारविहारस्य युक्तचेष्टस्य कर्मसु ।
युक्तस्वप्नावबोधस्य योगो भवति दुःखहा ॥
```

```translation
For one whose food and walking are kept in measure, whose effort in actions is kept in measure, and whose sleep and waking are kept to their times, yoga becomes the destroyer of suffering.
```

```phrase
For one whose food and walking are kept in measure, and whose effort in actions is kept in measure,
---
and whose sleep and waking are kept to their times, yoga becomes the destroyer of suffering.
```
