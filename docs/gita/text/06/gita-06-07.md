```samhita
जितात्मनः प्रशान्तस्य परमात्मा समाहितः ।
शीतोष्णसुखदुःखेषु तथा मानापमानयोः ॥
```

```translation
For the man who has mastered himself and whose mind is at peace, the supreme Self is present as his own self. He is the same in cold and heat, in pleasure and pain, and in honor and disgrace.
```

```phrase

He is the same in cold and heat, in pleasure and pain, and in honor and disgrace.
```
