```samhita
असंयतात्मना योगो दुष्प्राप इति मे मतिः ।
वश्यात्मना तु यतता शक्योऽवाप्तुमुपायतः ॥
```

```translation
Yoga is hard to reach for one whose mind is not under control: that is my view. But for one whose mind is under control and who still strives, it can be reached by the means.
```

