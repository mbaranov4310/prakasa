```samhita
आत्मौपम्येन सर्वत्र समं पश्यति योऽर्जुन ।
सुखं वा यदि वा दुःखं स योगी परमो मतः ॥
```

```translation
Arjuna, who by the measure of himself sees pleasure and pain as the same everywhere: that yogin is held to be the highest.
```

```phrase
Arjuna, who by the measure of himself sees the same everywhere,
---
pleasure and pain alike: that yogin is held to be the highest.
```
