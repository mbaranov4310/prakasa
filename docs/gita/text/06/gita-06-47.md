```samhita
योगिनामपि सर्वेषां मद्गतेनान्तरात्मना ।
श्रद्धावान्भजते यो मां स मे युक्ततमो मतः ॥
```

```translation
Of all yogins, he who worships me with faith, his inner self gone to me, is deemed by me the most yoked.
```

```phrase
Even of all yogins, with the inner self gone to me:
---
he who, full of faith, worships me — he is deemed by me the most yoked.
```
