```samhita
सुहृन्मित्रार्युदासीनमध्यस्थद्वेष्यबन्धुषु ।
साधुष्वपि च पापेषु समबुद्धिर्विशिष्यते ॥
```

```translation
Toward the well-wisher, the friend, the foe, the indifferent, the neutral, the disliked, and the kinsman, and toward the good and the wicked alike, the even-minded man stands above the rest.
```

