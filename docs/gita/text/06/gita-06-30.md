```samhita
यो मां पश्यति सर्वत्र सर्वं च मयि पश्यति ।
तस्याहं न प्रणश्यामि स च मे न प्रणश्यति ॥
```

```translation
Who sees me everywhere, and sees everything in me: for him I do not vanish, and he does not vanish for me.
```

```phrase
Who sees me everywhere, and sees everything in me:

for him I do not vanish, and he does not vanish for me.
```
