```samhita
आरुरुक्षोर्मुनेर्योगं कर्म कारणमुच्यते ।
योगारूढस्य तस्यैव शमः कारणमुच्यते ॥
```

```translation
For the sage who wishes to climb to yoga, action is said to be the means. For that same man once he has climbed, quiet is said to be the means.
```

```phrase
For the sage who wishes to climb to yoga, action is said to be the means.
---
For that same man once he has climbed, quiet is said to be the means.
```
