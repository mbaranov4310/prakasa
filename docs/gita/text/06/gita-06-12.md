```samhita
तत्रैकाग्रं मनः कृत्वा यतचित्तेन्द्रियक्रियः ।
उपविश्यासने युञ्ज्याद्योगमात्मविशुद्धये ॥
```

```translation
There, having made the mind one-pointed, with the acts of mind and senses held in, sitting on that seat, he should practice yoga for the cleansing of the mind.
```

