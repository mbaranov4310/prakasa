```samhita
प्रशान्तमनसं ह्येनं योगिनं सुखमुत्तमम् ।
उपैति शान्तरजसं ब्रह्मभूतमकल्मषम् ॥
```

```translation
For the highest joy comes to this yogin, whose mind has grown deeply quiet, whose passion has died down, who has become Brahman, and who is free of stain.
```

```phrase
For to this yogin, whose mind has grown deeply quiet, the highest joy
---
comes: to him whose passion has died down, who has become Brahman, and who is free of stain.
```
