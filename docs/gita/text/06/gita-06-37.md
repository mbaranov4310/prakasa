```samhita
अर्जुन उवाच
अयतिः श्रद्धयोपेतो योगाच्चलितमानसः ।
अप्राप्य योगसंसिद्धिं कां गतिं कृष्ण गच्छति ॥
```

```translation
Arjuna said: One who does not strive, yet is endowed with faith, whose mind has slipped from yoga and who has not reached yoga's perfection — what course does he go to, O Krishna?
```

```phrase
Arjuna said:
---
One who does not strive, yet is endowed with faith, whose mind has slipped from yoga:
---
not reaching yoga's perfection, what course, O Krishna, does he go to?
```
