```samhita
यदा विनियतं चित्तमात्मन्येवावतिष्ठते ।
निःस्पृहः सर्वकामेभ्यो युक्त इत्युच्यते तदा ॥
```

```translation
When the mind, held to one point, comes to rest in the self alone, and he is free of craving from every object of desire, then he is called concentrated.
```
