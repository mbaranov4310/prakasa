```samhita
योगी युञ्जीत सततमात्मानं रहसि स्थितः ।
एकाकी यतचित्तात्मा निराशीरपरिग्रहः ॥
```

```translation
The yogin should keep the mind steadily fixed, staying in a lonely place, alone, with mind and body held in, free of longing, and owning nothing.
```
