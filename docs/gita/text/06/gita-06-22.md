```samhita
यं लब्ध्वा चापरं लाभं मन्यते नाधिकं ततः ।
यस्मिन्स्थितो न दुःखेन गुरुणापि विचाल्यते ॥
```

```translation
and having gained which, he thinks no other gain greater than it; established in which, he is not shaken even by a heavy pain.
```

```phrase

established in which, he is not shaken even by a heavy pain.
```
