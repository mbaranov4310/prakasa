```samhita
कच्चिन्नोभयविभ्रष्टश्छिन्नाभ्रमिव नश्यति ।
अप्रतिष्ठो महाबाहो विमूढो ब्रह्मणः पथि ॥
```

```translation
Fallen from both, does he not perish like a torn cloud, with no footing, bewildered on the path of Brahman, O mighty-armed?
```

```phrase
Does he not, fallen from both, perish like a torn cloud,
---
with no footing, O mighty-armed, bewildered on the path of Brahman?
```
