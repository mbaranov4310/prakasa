```samhita
यं संन्यासमिति प्राहुर्योगं तं विद्धि पाण्डव ।
न ह्यसंन्यस्तसंकल्पो योगी भवति कश्चन ॥
```

```translation
What they call renunciation, know that to be yoga, O Pandava. For no one becomes a yogin who has not given up the aim at the fruit.
```

