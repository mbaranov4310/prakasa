```samhita
समं कायशिरोग्रीवं धारयन्नचलं स्थिरः ।
संप्रेक्ष्य नासिकाग्रं स्वं दिशश्चानवलोकयन् ॥
```

```translation
Holding body, head, and neck even and still, steady, letting the gaze rest as it were on the tip of his own nose, and not looking about at the directions.
```
