```samhita
तं विद्याद्दुःखसंयोगवियोगं योगसंज्ञितम् ।
स निश्चयेन योक्तव्यो योगोऽनिर्विण्णचेतसा ॥
```

```translation
Know that to be the severing of union with pain, and what is called yoga. That yoga is to be practiced with resolve, by a mind that does not weary.
```

