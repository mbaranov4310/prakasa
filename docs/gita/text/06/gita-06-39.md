```samhita
एतन्मे संशयं कृष्ण च्छेत्तुमर्हस्यशेषतः ।
त्वदन्यः संशयस्यास्य च्छेत्ता न ह्युपपद्यते ॥
```

```translation
This doubt of mine you should cut away completely, O Krishna. No one other than you can be the one to cut this doubt.
```

```phrase
This doubt of mine, O Krishna, you should cut away completely.
---
For no one other than you can be the one to cut this doubt.
```
