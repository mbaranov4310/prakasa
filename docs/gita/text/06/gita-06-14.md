```samhita
प्रशान्तात्मा विगतभीर्ब्रह्मचारिव्रते स्थितः ।
मनः संयम्य मच्चित्तो युक्त आसीत मत्परः ॥
```

```translation
With the mind deeply at peace, fear gone, established in the brahmacārin's vow, having drawn the mind in, he should sit collected, his thought on me, holding me as the supreme.
```
