```samhita
श्रीभगवानुवाच
अनाश्रितः कर्मफलं कार्यं कर्म करोति यः ।
स संन्यासी च योगी च न निरग्निर्न चाक्रियः ॥
```

```translation
The blessed Lord said: He who performs the action that is his to do, not depending on its fruit, is a renouncer and a yogin — and not only the man who keeps no ritual fire, nor the man who does no rites.
```

```phrase
The blessed Lord said:
---
He who performs the action that is his to do, not depending on the fruit of action,
---
he is a renouncer and a yogin, not only the man without the ritual fire, nor the man without rites.
```
