```samhita
यथा दीपो निवातस्थो नेङ्गते सोपमा स्मृता ।
योगिनो यतचित्तस्य युञ्जतो योगमात्मनः ॥
```

```translation
As a lamp set out of the wind does not waver, that is the likeness taught of the yogin whose mind is held in, while he practices yoga, concentration on the self.
```
