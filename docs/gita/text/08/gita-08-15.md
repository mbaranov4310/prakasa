```samhita
मामुपेत्य पुनर्जन्म दुःखालयमशाश्वतम् ।
नाप्नुवन्ति महात्मानः संसिद्धिं परमां गताः ॥
```

```translation
Having reached me, the great-souled do not come to rebirth, that unstable abode of sorrows. They have reached the highest perfection.
```

```phrase
Having reached me, rebirth, an unstable abode of sorrows,
---
the great-souled, who have reached the highest perfection, do not attain.
```
