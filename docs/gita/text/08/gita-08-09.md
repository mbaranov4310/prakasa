```samhita
कविं पुराणमनुशासितार
मणोरणीयांसमनुस्मरेद्यः ।
सर्वस्य धातारमचिन्त्यरूप
मादित्यवर्णं तमसः परस्तात् ॥
```

```translation
One should call to mind the seer, the ancient, the ruler, finer even than an atom: the ordainer of all, whose form cannot be conceived, bright as the sun, beyond the darkness.
```

```phrase
The seer, the ancient, the ruler,
---
finer even than an atom: who should call him to mind.
---
The ordainer of all, of form that cannot be conceived,
---
bright as the sun, beyond the darkness.
```
