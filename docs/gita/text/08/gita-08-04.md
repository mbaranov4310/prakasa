```samhita
अधिभूतं क्षरो भावः पुरुषश्चाधिदैवतम् ।
अधियज्ञोऽहमेवात्र देहे देहभृतां वर ॥
```

```translation
What concerns beings is perishable existence, and the Person is what concerns the deity. I myself am the lord of sacrifice here in the body, O best of embodied beings.
```

```phrase
What concerns beings is perishable existence, and the Person is what concerns the deity.
```
