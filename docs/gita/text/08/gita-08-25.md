```samhita
धूमो रात्रिस्तथा कृष्णः षण्मासा दक्षिणायनम् ।
तत्र चान्द्रमसं ज्योतियोगी प्राप्य निवर्तते ॥
```

```translation
Smoke, night, the dark fortnight, the six months of the southern course: the yogin, the man of rites, reaches the lunar light there and returns.
```

```phrase
Smoke, night, and the dark fortnight, the six months of the southern course:
---
reaching the lunar light there, the yogin returns.
```
