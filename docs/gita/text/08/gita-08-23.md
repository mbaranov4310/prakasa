```samhita
यत्र काले त्वनावृत्तिमावृत्तिं चैव योगिनः ।
प्रयाता यान्ति तं कालं वक्ष्यामि भरतर्षभ ॥
```

```translation
The time at which those who have departed go to non-return, and the time at which they return, I shall declare, O best of Bharatas. Yogins here are both the men of discipline and those who perform rites.
```

```phrase
The time at which there is non-return, and return as well, for yogins,
---
the departed go by that time. I shall declare it, O best of Bharatas.
```
