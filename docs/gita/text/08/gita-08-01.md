```samhita
अर्जुन उवाच
किं तद्ब्रह्म किमध्यात्मं किं कर्म पुरुषोत्तम ।
अधिभूतं च किं प्रोक्तमधिदैवं किमुच्यते ॥
```

```translation
Arjuna said: What is that Brahman? What concerns the self? What is action, O supreme Person? And what is declared to concern beings? What is called what concerns the gods?
```

```phrase
Arjuna said:
---
What is that Brahman, what concerns the self, what is action, O supreme Person?
---
And what is declared to concern beings? What is called what concerns the gods?
```
