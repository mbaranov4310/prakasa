```samhita
यं यं वापि स्मरन्भावं त्यजत्यन्ते कलेवरम् ।
तं तमेवैति कौन्तेय सदा तद्भावभावितः ॥
```

```translation
Whatever being a man remembers as he leaves the body at the end, to that very being he goes, O son of Kuntī, having always been shaped by that state.
```

```phrase
Whatever being one remembers, even as one leaves the body at the end,
---
to that very being he goes, O son of Kuntī, having always been shaped by that state.
```
