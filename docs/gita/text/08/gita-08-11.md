```samhita
यदक्षरं वेदविदो वदन्ति
विशन्ति यद्यतयो वीतरागाः ।
यदिच्छन्तो ब्रह्मचर्यं चरन्ति
तत्ते पदं संग्रहेण प्रवक्ष्ये ॥
```

```translation
That Imperishable which the knowers of the Veda declare, which strivers from whom passion has gone enter, and desiring which they practice the student's discipline: that goal of yours I shall declare in brief.
```

```phrase
That which the knowers of the Veda declare as the Imperishable,
---
which strivers from whom passion has gone enter,
---
desiring which they practice the student's discipline,
---
that goal of yours I shall declare in brief.
```
