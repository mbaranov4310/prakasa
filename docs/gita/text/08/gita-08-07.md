```samhita
तस्मात्सर्वेषु कालेषु मामनुस्मर युध्य च ।
मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्मामेवैष्यस्यसंशयः ॥
```

```translation
Therefore remember me at all times, and fight. With mind and intellect given over to me, you will come to me alone. Of this there is no doubt.
```

```phrase
Therefore, at all times, remember me, and fight.
---
With mind and intellect given over to me, you will come to me alone. Of this there is no doubt.
```
