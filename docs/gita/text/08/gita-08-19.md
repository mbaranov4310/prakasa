```samhita
भूतग्रामः स एवायं भूत्वा भूत्वा प्रलीयते ।
रात्र्यागमेऽवशः पार्थ प्रभवत्यहरागमे ॥
```

```translation
This very same mass of beings, having come to be again and again, dissolves when the night comes, not of its own will, O Pārtha, and comes forth when the day comes.
```

```phrase
This very same mass of beings, having come to be again and again, dissolves
---
at the coming of night; not free, O Pārtha, it comes forth at the coming of day.
```
