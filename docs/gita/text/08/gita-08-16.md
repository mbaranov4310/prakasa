```samhita
आ ब्रह्मभुवनाल्लोकाः पुनरावर्तिनोऽर्जुन ।
मामुपेत्य तु कौन्तेय पुनर्जन्म न विद्यते ॥
```

```translation
The worlds, as far as the world of Brahmā, return again, O Arjuna. But having reached me alone, O son of Kuntī, there is no rebirth.
```

```phrase
As far as the world of Brahmā, the worlds return again, O Arjuna.
---
But having reached me alone, O son of Kuntī, there is no rebirth.
```
