```samhita
अनन्यचेताः सततं यो मां स्मरति नित्यशः ।
तस्याहं सुलभः पार्थ नित्ययुक्तस्य योगिनः ॥
```

```translation
I am easy to reach, O Pārtha, for the yogin whose mind is on no other object and who remembers me unceasingly through life, his mind always collected.
```

```phrase
He whose mind is on no other object, who remembers me unceasingly through life,
---
for him I am easy to reach, O Pārtha, the yogin whose mind is always collected.
```
