```samhita
प्रयाणकाले मनसाचलेन
भक्त्या युक्तो योगबलेन चैव ।
भ्रुवोर्मध्ये प्राणमावेश्य सम्य
क्स तं परं पुरुषमुपैति दिव्यम् ॥
```

```translation
At the time of departure, with an unmoving mind, joined with devotion and by the power of yoga, he who has rightly fixed the breath between the eyebrows attains that supreme divine Person.
```

```phrase
At the time of departure, with an unmoving mind,
---
joined with devotion, and by the power of yoga,
---
having rightly fixed the breath between the eyebrows,
---
he attains that supreme divine Person.
```
