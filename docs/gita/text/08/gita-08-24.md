```samhita
अग्निर्ज्योतिरहः शुक्लः षण्मासा उत्तरायणम् ।
तत्र प्रयाता गच्छन्ति ब्रह्म ब्रह्मविदो जनाः ॥
```

```translation
Fire, light, day, the bright fortnight, the six months of the northern course: the people who depart by that path, knowers of Brahman, the worshippers of Brahman, go to Brahman.
```

```phrase
Fire, light, day, the bright fortnight, the six months of the northern course:
---
departing by that path, the people who know Brahman, the worshippers of Brahman, go to Brahman.
```
