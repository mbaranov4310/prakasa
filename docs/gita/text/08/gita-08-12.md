```samhita
सर्वद्वाराणि संयम्य
मनो हृदि निरुध्य च ।
मूर्ध्न्याधायात्मनः प्राण
मास्थितो योगधारणाम् ॥
```

```translation
Having restrained all the gates and confined the mind in the heart, having placed one's own breath at the crown of the head, engaged in the concentration of yoga,
```

```phrase
Having restrained all the gates,
---
and having confined the mind in the heart,
---
having placed one's own breath at the crown of the head,
---
engaged in the concentration of yoga,
```
