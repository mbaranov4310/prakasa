```samhita
श्रीभगवानुवाच
अक्षरं ब्रह्म परमं स्वभावोऽध्यात्ममुच्यते ।
भूतभावोद्भवकरो विसर्गः कर्मसंज्ञितः ॥
```

```translation
The blessed Lord said: The Imperishable is the supreme Brahman. One's own being is called what concerns the self. The offering that brings beings to birth is what is termed action.
```

```phrase
The blessed Lord said:
---
The Imperishable is the supreme Brahman. One's own being is called what concerns the self.
---
The offering that brings beings to birth is what is termed action.
```
