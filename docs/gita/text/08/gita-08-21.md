```samhita
अव्यक्तोऽक्षर इत्युक्तस्तमाहुः परमां गतिम् ।
यं प्राप्य न निवर्तन्ते तद्धाम परमं मम ॥
```

```translation
That unmanifest, called the imperishable, they call the highest goal. Having reached it, they do not return. That highest place is mine.
```

```phrase
That unmanifest, called the imperishable, they call the highest goal.
---
Having reached it they do not return. That highest place is mine.
```
