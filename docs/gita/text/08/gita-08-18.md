```samhita
अव्यक्ताद्व्यक्तयः सर्वाः प्रभवन्त्यहरागमे ।
रात्र्यागमे प्रलीयन्ते तत्रैवाव्यक्तसंज्ञके ॥
```

```translation
From the unmanifest all manifest beings come forth when the day comes. When the night comes they dissolve into that same unmanifest.
```

```phrase
From the unmanifest all manifest beings come forth at the coming of day.
---
At the coming of night they dissolve right there, in what is called the unmanifest.
```
