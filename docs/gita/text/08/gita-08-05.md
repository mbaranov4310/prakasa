```samhita
अन्तकाले च मामेव स्मरन्मुक्त्वा कलेवरम् ।
यः प्रयाति स मद्भावं याति नास्त्यत्र संशयः ॥
```

```translation
And he who at the time of death departs remembering me alone, having left the body, goes to my state. There is no doubt of this.
```

```phrase
And at the time of death, remembering me alone and leaving the body,
---
he who departs goes to my state. There is no doubt of this.
```
