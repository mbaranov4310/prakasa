```samhita
वेदेषु यज्ञेषु तपःसु चैव
दानेषु यत्पुण्यफलं प्रदिष्टम् ।
अत्येति तत्सर्वमिदं विदित्वा
योगी परं स्थानमुपैति चाद्यम् ॥
```

```translation
Whatever fruit of merit scripture appoints in the Vedas rightly studied, in sacrifices rightly offered, in austerities, and in gifts, the yogin goes beyond it all once he has understood this teaching and put it into practice, and he attains the highest place, the first, which is Brahman.
```

```phrase
In the Vedas, in sacrifices, and in austerities,
---
in gifts, whatever fruit of merit is appointed,
---
he goes beyond all that, having understood this,
---
and the yogin attains the highest place, the first.
```
