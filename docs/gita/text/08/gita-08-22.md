```samhita
पुरुषः स परः पार्थ भक्त्या लभ्यस्त्वनन्यया ।
यस्यान्तःस्थानि भूतानि येन सर्वमिदं ततम् ॥
```

```translation
That supreme Person, O Pārtha, is reached by devotion that goes to no other, devotion whose object is the Self. Beings stand within him, and by him this whole world is pervaded.
```

```phrase
That supreme Person, O Pārtha, is reached by devotion turned nowhere else.
---
Beings stand within him, and by him all this is pervaded.
```
