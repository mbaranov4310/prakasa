```samhita
सहस्रयुगपर्यन्तमहर्यद्ब्रह्मणो विदुः ।
रात्रिं युगसहस्रान्तां तेऽहोरात्रविदो जनाः ॥
```

```translation
They know that day of Brahmā which ends at a thousand yugas, and the night which ends at a thousand yugas. Those people know the measure of day and night.
```

```phrase
They know the day of Brahmā whose end is a thousand yugas,
---
and the night whose end is a thousand yugas. Those people know day and night.
```
