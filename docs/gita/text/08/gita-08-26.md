```samhita
शुक्लकृष्णे गती ह्येते जगतः शाश्वते मते ।
एकया यात्यनावृत्तिमन्ययावर्तते पुनः ॥
```

```translation
These are the two paths of the world, the bright and the dark, held to be eternal. By the one he goes to non-return; by the other he returns again.
```

```phrase
For these are the two paths of the world, the bright and the dark, held to be eternal.
---
By one he goes to non-return; by the other he returns again.
```
