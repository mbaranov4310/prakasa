```samhita
नैते सृती पार्थ जानन्योगी मुह्यति कश्चन ।
तस्मात्सर्वेषु कालेषु योगयुक्तो भवार्जुन ॥
```

```translation
Knowing these two paths, O Pārtha, no yogin at all is confused. Therefore at all times be intent on yoga, O Arjuna.
```

