```samhita
परस्तस्मात्तु भावोऽन्योऽव्यक्तोऽव्यक्तात्सनातनः ।
यः स सर्वेषु भूतेषु नश्यत्सु न विनश्यति ॥
```

```translation
But other than that unmanifest is another being, itself unmanifest and ancient. When all beings perish, that one does not perish.
```

```phrase
But other than that is another being, unmanifest, ancient, beyond the unmanifest.
---
When all beings perish, that one does not perish.
```
