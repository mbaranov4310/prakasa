```samhita
अभ्यासयोगयुक्तेन चेतसा नान्यगामिना ।
परमं पुरुषं दिव्यं याति पार्थानुचिन्तयन् ॥
```

```translation
With a mind joined to the yoga of practice and not going elsewhere, one goes, O Pārtha, meditating, to the supreme divine Person.
```

```phrase
With a mind joined to the yoga of practice, a mind that does not go elsewhere,
---
one goes, O Pārtha, meditating, to the supreme divine Person.
```
