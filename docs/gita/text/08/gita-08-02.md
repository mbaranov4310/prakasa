```samhita
अधियज्ञः कथं कोऽत्र देहेऽस्मिन्मधुसूदन ।
प्रयाणकाले च कथं ज्ञेयोऽसि नियतात्मभिः ॥
```

```translation
How, and who, is the lord of sacrifice here in this body, O Madhusūdana? And how are you to be known at the time of departure by those of steady self?
```
