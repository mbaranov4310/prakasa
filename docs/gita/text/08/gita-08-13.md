```samhita
ओमित्येकाक्षरं ब्रह्म
व्याहरन्मामनुस्मरन् ।
यः प्रयाति त्यजन्देहं
स याति परमां गतिम् ॥
```

```translation
Uttering Om, the one syllable, Brahman, and contemplating me, he who dies, leaving the body, goes to the highest goal.
```

```phrase
Om, the one syllable, Brahman,
---
uttering it and contemplating me,
---
who dies, leaving the body,
---
he goes to the highest goal.
```
