```samhita
कामक्रोधवियुक्तानां यतीनां यतचेतसाम् ।
अभितो ब्रह्मनिर्वाणं वर्तते विदितात्मनाम् ॥
```

```translation
For renunciates freed from desire and anger, their minds held in, who know the self, the peace of Brahman lies on both sides: while they live, and when they are dead.
```

```phrase
For those freed from desire and anger, the strivers whose minds are held in,
---
the peace of Brahman lies on both sides, for those by whom the self is known.
```
