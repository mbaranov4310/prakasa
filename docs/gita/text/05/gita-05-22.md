```samhita
ये हि संस्पर्शजा भोगा दुःखयोनय एव ते ।
आद्यन्तवन्तः कौन्तेय न तेषु रमते बुधः ॥
```

```translation
For the enjoyments born of contact are nothing but sources of pain. They have a beginning and an end, O son of Kuntī, and the wise man does not delight in them.
```
