```samhita
इहैव तैर्जितः सर्गो येषां साम्ये स्थितं मनः ।
निर्दोषं हि समं ब्रह्म तस्माद्ब्रह्मणि ते स्थिताः ॥
```

```translation
Right here, birth is conquered by those whose mind stands still in sameness. For Brahman is flawless and the same, and so they abide in Brahman.
```

