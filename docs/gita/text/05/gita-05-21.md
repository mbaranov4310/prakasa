```samhita
बाह्यस्पर्शेष्वसक्तात्मा
विन्दत्यात्मनि यत्सुखम् ।
स ब्रह्मयोगयुक्तात्मा
सुखमक्षयमश्नुते ॥
```

```translation
He whose mind is unattached amid external contacts finds the joy that is in the self. With his mind held in the yoga of Brahman, he reaches a joy that does not fail.
```

```phrase
He whose mind is unattached amid external contacts
---
finds the joy that is in the self.
---
He whose mind is held in the yoga of Brahman
---
reaches a joy that does not fail.
```
