```samhita
न प्रहृष्येत्प्रियं प्राप्य नोद्विजेत्प्राप्य चाप्रियम् ।
स्थिरबुद्धिरसंमूढो ब्रह्मविद्ब्रह्मणि स्थितः ॥
```

```translation
He should not exult on gaining what is dear, nor be shaken on gaining what is unwelcome. With his understanding firm and free of bewilderment, the knower of Brahman abides in Brahman.
```

```phrase
He should not exult on gaining what is dear, nor be shaken on gaining what is unwelcome.
---
With his understanding firm and free of bewilderment, the knower of Brahman abides in Brahman.
```
