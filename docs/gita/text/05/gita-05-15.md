```samhita
नादत्ते कस्यचित्पापं न चैव सुकृतं विभुः ।
अज्ञानेनावृतं ज्ञानं तेन मुह्यन्ति जन्तवः ॥
```

```translation
The all-pervading one takes no one's evil, nor their good work either. Knowledge is covered by ignorance, and by that creatures are confused.
```

```phrase
The all-pervading one takes no one's evil, nor their good work either.

Knowledge is covered by ignorance, and by that creatures are confused.
```
