```samhita
कायेन मनसा बुद्ध्या केवलैरिन्द्रियैरपि ।
योगिनः कर्म कुर्वन्ति सङ्गं त्यक्त्वात्मशुद्धये ॥
```

```translation
With the body, the mind, and the intellect, and with the senses too, alone, yogins do action, having given up attachment, for the cleansing of the self.
```

