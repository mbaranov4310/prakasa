```samhita
लभन्ते ब्रह्मनिर्वाणमृषयः क्षीणकल्मषाः ।
छिन्नद्वैधा यतात्मानः सर्वभूतहिते रताः ॥
```

```translation
The seers whose stain is worn away, whose doubt is cut, whose senses are held in, and who are intent on the good of every being, gain the peace of Brahman.
```

```phrase
The seers whose stain is worn away gain the peace of Brahman.
---
Their doubt cut, their senses held in, they are intent on the good of every being.
```
