```samhita
स्पर्शान्कृत्वा बहिर्बाह्यांश्चक्षुश्चैवान्तरे भ्रुवोः ।
प्राणापानौ समौ कृत्वा नासाभ्यन्तरचारिणौ ॥
```

```translation
Having put the contacts outside, and the eye between the brows, and having made the out-breath and the in-breath even as they move inside the nostrils,
```

```phrase
Having put the contacts outside, the external ones, and the eye between the two brows,
---
having made the out-breath and the in-breath even, moving inside the nostrils,
```
