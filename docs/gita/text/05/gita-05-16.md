```samhita
ज्ञानेन तु तदज्ञानं येषां नाशितमात्मनः ।
तेषामादित्यवज्ज्ञानं प्रकाशयति तत्परम् ॥
```

```translation
But for those in whom that ignorance of the self has been destroyed by knowledge, knowledge illumines the supreme reality, as the sun illumines every form.
```

```phrase
But that ignorance of the self is destroyed by knowledge, in those for whom it is destroyed.
---
For them, knowledge, like the sun, illumines that supreme reality.
```
