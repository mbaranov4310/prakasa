```samhita
सर्वकर्माणि मनसा संन्यस्यास्ते सुखं वशी ।
नवद्वारे पुरे देही नैव कुर्वन्न कारयन् ॥
```

```translation
Having renounced all actions with the mind, the self-controlled embodied one rests easily in the nine-gated city, not doing at all, and not causing action.
```

```phrase
Having renounced all actions with the mind, the self-controlled man rests easily
---
in the nine-gated city, the embodied one, not doing at all, and not causing action.
```
