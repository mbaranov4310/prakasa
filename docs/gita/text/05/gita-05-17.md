```samhita
तद्बुद्धयस्तदात्मानस्तन्निष्ठास्तत्परायणाः ।
गच्छन्त्यपुनरावृत्तिं ज्ञाननिर्धूतकल्मषाः ॥
```

```translation
Those whose understanding is set on that, whose self is that, who stand in that, and for whom that is the highest course, go to what does not come back, their stain shaken off by knowledge.
```

```phrase
Those whose understanding is set on that, whose self is that, who stand in that, and for whom that is the highest course,
---
go to non-return, their stain shaken off by knowledge.
```
