```samhita
युक्तः कर्मफलं त्यक्त्वा
शान्तिमाप्नोति नैष्ठिकीम् ।
अयुक्तः कामकारेण
फले सक्तो निबध्यते ॥
```

```translation
The steadied man, having given up the fruit of action, reaches the peace that belongs to steadfastness. The unsteadied man, driven by desire and clinging to the fruit, is bound.
```

```phrase
The steadied man, having given up the fruit of action,
---
reaches the peace that belongs to steadfastness.
---
The unsteadied man, driven by desire,
---
clinging to the fruit, is bound.
```
