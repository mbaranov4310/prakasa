```samhita
न कर्तृत्वं न कर्माणि लोकस्य सृजति प्रभुः ।
न कर्मफलसंयोगं स्वभावस्तु प्रवर्तते ॥
```

```translation
The Lord creates neither agency nor the things people produce, nor the joining of an action to its fruit. One's own nature, though, goes on.
```

```phrase
The Lord creates neither agency, nor the things people produce,

nor the joining of an action to its fruit. One's own nature, though, goes on.
```
