```samhita
योगयुक्तो विशुद्धात्मा
विजितात्मा जितेन्द्रियः ।
सर्वभूतात्मभूतात्मा
कुर्वन्नपि न लिप्यते ॥
```

```translation
Yoked to yoga, his mind pure, his body and senses mastered, his self the self of all beings, he is not stained even while he acts.
```

```phrase
Yoked to yoga, his mind pure,
---
his body mastered and his senses mastered,
---
his self the self of all beings,
---
he is not stained even while he acts.
```
