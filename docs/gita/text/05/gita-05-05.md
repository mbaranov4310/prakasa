```samhita
यत्सांख्यैः प्राप्यते स्थानं तद्योगैरपि गम्यते ।
एकं सांख्यं च योगं च यः पश्यति स पश्यति ॥
```

```translation
The place that the men of knowledge reach is reached by the yogins too. He who sees the path of knowledge and yoga as one sees rightly.
```
