```samhita
प्रलपन् विसृजन्गृह्णन्नुन्मिषन्निमिषन्नपि ।
इन्द्रियाणीन्द्रियार्थेषु वर्तन्त इति धारयन् ॥
```

```translation
speaking, letting out, taking, even opening and closing the eyes, holding that the senses move among their objects.
```

```phrase
speaking, letting out, taking, even opening and closing the eyes,

holding that the senses move among their objects.
```
