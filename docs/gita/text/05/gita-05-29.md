```samhita
भोक्तारं यज्ञतपसां सर्वलोकमहेश्वरम् ।
सुहृदं सर्वभूतानां ज्ञात्वा मां शान्तिमृच्छति ॥
```

```translation
Having known me as the enjoyer of sacrifices and austerities, the great lord of all worlds, and the friend of all beings, he reaches peace.
```

```phrase
The enjoyer of sacrifices and austerities, the great lord of all worlds,
---
the friend of all beings: having known me so, he reaches peace.
```
