```samhita
नैव किंचित्करोमीति
युक्तो मन्येत तत्त्ववित् ।
पश्यञ्शृण्वन्स्पृशञ्जिघ्रन्नश्नन्गच्छन्स्वपञ्श्वसन् ॥
```

```translation
"I do nothing at all": so the steadied man who knows the truth should think, seeing, hearing, touching, smelling, eating, going, sleeping, breathing,
```
