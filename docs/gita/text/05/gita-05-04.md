```samhita
सांख्ययोगौ पृथग्बालाः प्रवदन्ति न पण्डिताः ।
एकमप्यास्थितः सम्यगुभयोर्विन्दते फलम् ॥
```

```translation
The childish say that the path of knowledge and yoga are different, with opposed fruits. The wise do not. He who has rightly practiced even one of them obtains the fruit of both.
```

```phrase
The childish say that the path of knowledge and yoga are different. The wise do not.
---
He who has rightly practiced even one of them obtains the fruit of both.
```
