```samhita
यतेन्द्रियमनोबुद्धिर्मुनिर्मोक्षपरायणः ।
विगतेच्छाभयक्रोधो यः सदा मुक्त एव सः ॥
```

```translation
the sage whose senses, mind, and understanding are held in, intent on release, from whom desire, fear, and anger have gone — he who lives so is free already.
```

```phrase
the sage whose senses, mind, and understanding are held in, and whose highest course is release:
---
he from whom desire, fear, and anger have gone, who lives so always, is free already.
```
