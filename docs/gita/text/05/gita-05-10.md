```samhita
ब्रह्मण्याधाय कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा करोति यः ।
लिप्यते न स पापेन पद्मपत्रमिवाम्भसा ॥
```

```translation
He who acts having laid his actions on the Lord and given up attachment is not stained by evil, as a lotus leaf by water.
```

```phrase

is not stained by evil, as a lotus leaf by water.
```
