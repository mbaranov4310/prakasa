```samhita
शक्नोतीहैव यः सोढुं प्राक्छरीरविमोक्षणात् ।
कामक्रोधोद्भवं वेगं स युक्तः स सुखी नरः ॥
```

```translation
He who can endure, right here, the surge born of desire and anger, until he is released from the body: he is a yogin, and he is a happy man.
```

```phrase
He who can endure it right here, before release from the body,
---
the surge born of desire and anger: he is a yogin, and he is a happy man.
```
