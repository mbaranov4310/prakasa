```samhita
संन्यासस्तु महाबाहो दुःखमाप्तुमयोगतः ।
योगयुक्तो मुनिर्ब्रह्म नचिरेणाधिगच्छति ॥
```

```translation
But renunciation, O mighty-armed, is hard to reach without yoga. The sage who is yoked to yoga reaches before long the highest renunciation, steadfastness in knowledge of the self.
```

```phrase
But renunciation, O mighty-armed, is hard to reach without yoga.

The sage yoked to yoga reaches before long the highest renunciation, steadfastness in knowledge of the self.
```
