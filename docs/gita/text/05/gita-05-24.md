```samhita
योऽन्तःसुखोऽन्तरारामस्तथान्तर्ज्योतिरेव यः ।
स योगी ब्रह्मनिर्वाणं ब्रह्मभूतोऽधिगच्छति ॥
```

```translation
The yogin whose joy is within, whose delight is within, and whose light is within alone, reaches the peace of Brahman, having become Brahman while still alive.
```

```phrase
He whose joy is within, whose delight is within, and whose light is within alone,
---
that yogin reaches the peace of Brahman, having become Brahman.
```
