```samhita
ज्ञेयः स नित्यसंन्यासी यो न द्वेष्टि न काङ्क्षति ।
निर्द्वन्द्वो हि महाबाहो सुखं बन्धात्प्रमुच्यते ॥
```

```translation
He who does not hate and does not long is to be known as a constant renouncer. For, free of the pairs, O mighty-armed, he is released from bondage without strain.
```

