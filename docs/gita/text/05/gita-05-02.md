```samhita
श्रीभगवानुवाच
संन्यासः कर्मयोगश्च निःश्रेयसकरावुभौ ।
तयोस्तु कर्मसंन्यासात्कर्मयोगो विशिष्यते ॥
```

```translation
The blessed Lord said: Renunciation and the yoga of action both bring about release. But of these two, the yoga of action excels the mere renunciation of action.
```

```phrase
The blessed Lord said:

---
```
