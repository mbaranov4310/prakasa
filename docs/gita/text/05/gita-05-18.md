```samhita
विद्याविनयसंपन्ने ब्राह्मणे गवि हस्तिनि ।
शुनि चैव श्वपाके च पण्डिताः समदर्शिनः ॥
```

```translation
The wise see the same in a brāhmaṇa endowed with learning and humility, in a cow, in an elephant, in a dog, and in one who cooks dogs.
```

```phrase
In a brāhmaṇa endowed with learning and humility, in a cow, in an elephant,
---
in a dog, and in one who cooks dogs, the wise see the same.
```
