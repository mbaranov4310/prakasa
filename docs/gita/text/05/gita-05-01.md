```samhita
अर्जुन उवाच
संन्यासं कर्मणां कृष्ण पुनर्योगं च शंससि ।
यच्छ्रेय एतयोरेकं तन्मे ब्रूहि सुनिश्चितम् ॥
```

```translation
Arjuna said: You praise the renunciation of actions, O Krishna, and again you praise yoga. Tell me which one of these two is the better, the one you hold as settled.
```

```phrase
Arjuna said:
---
You praise the renunciation of actions, O Krishna, and again you praise yoga.
---
Tell me which one of these two is the better, the one you hold as settled.
```
