```samhita
द्रुपदो द्रौपदेयाश्च सर्वशः पृथिवीपते ।
सौभद्रश्च महाबाहुः शङ्खान्दध्मुः पृथक्पृथक् ॥
```

```translation
Drupada and the sons of Draupadi, all together, O lord of the earth, and the mighty-armed son of Subhadra, blew their conches, each his own.
```

