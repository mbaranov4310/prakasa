```samhita
श्वशुरान्सुहृदश्चैव सेनयोरुभयोरपि ।
तान्समीक्ष्य स कौन्तेयः सर्वान्बन्धूनवस्थितान् ॥
```

```translation
fathers-in-law and friends as well, in both armies. Looking on all those kinsmen standing there, the son of Kunti
```
