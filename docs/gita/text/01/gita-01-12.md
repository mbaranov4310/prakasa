```samhita
तस्य संजनयन्हर्षं कुरुवृद्धः पितामहः ।
सिंहनादं विनद्योच्चैः शङ्खं दध्मौ प्रतापवान् ॥
```

```translation
To raise his spirits, the eldest of the Kurus, the grandsire, mighty in his glory, roared like a lion and blew his conch.
```

