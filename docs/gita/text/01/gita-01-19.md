```samhita
स घोषो धार्तराष्ट्राणां हृदयानि व्यदारयत् ।
नभश्च पृथिवीं चैव तुमुलो व्यनुनादयन् ॥
```

```translation
That roar tore the hearts of Dhritarashtra's men, and the tumult filled both sky and earth with its sound.
```

```phrase
That roar tore the hearts of Dhritarashtra's men,
---
filling both sky and earth as the tumult rang out.
```
