```samhita
ततः श्वेतैर्हयैर्युक्ते महति स्यन्दने स्थितौ ।
माधवः पाण्डवश्चैव दिव्यौ शङ्खौ प्रदध्मतुः ॥
```

```translation
Then Madhava and the son of Pandu, standing in a great chariot yoked with white horses, blew their divine conches.
```

