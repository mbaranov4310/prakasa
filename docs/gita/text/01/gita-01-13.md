```samhita
ततः शङ्खाश्च भेर्यश्च पणवानकगोमुखाः ।
सहसैवाभ्यहन्यन्त स शब्दस्तुमुलोऽभवत् ॥
```

```translation
Then conches and kettledrums, tabors, drums, and cow-horns burst out all at once, and that sound became a tumult.
```

```phrase
Then conches and kettledrums, tabors, drums, and cow-horns
---
burst out all at once, and that sound became a tumult.
```
