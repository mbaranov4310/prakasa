```samhita
युधामन्युश्च विक्रान्त उत्तमौजाश्च वीर्यवान् ।
सौभद्रो द्रौपदेयाश्च सर्व एव महारथाः ॥
```

```translation
the valiant Yudhamanyu and the brave Uttamaujas, the son of Subhadra and the sons of Draupadi, all of them great chariot-warriors.
```

```phrase
the valiant Yudhamanyu and the brave Uttamaujas,
```
