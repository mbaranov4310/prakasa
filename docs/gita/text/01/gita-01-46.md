```samhita
यदि मामप्रतीकारमशस्त्रं शस्त्रपाणयः ।
धार्तराष्ट्रा रणे हन्युस्तन्मे क्षेमतरं भवेत् ॥
```

```translation
If the sons of Dhritarashtra, weapons in hand, should kill me in battle while I do not resist and hold no weapon, that would be better for me.
```

```phrase
If, weapons in hand, they should kill me unresisting and unarmed,
---
the sons of Dhritarashtra in battle, that would be better for me.
```
