```samhita
अथ व्यवस्थितान् दृष्ट्वा धार्तराष्ट्रान्कपिध्वजः ।
प्रवृत्ते शस्त्रसंपाते धनुरुद्यम्य पाण्डवः ॥
```

```translation
Then, seeing Dhritarashtra's men drawn up, as the clash of weapons began, the son of Pandu, whose banner is a monkey, took up his bow
```

