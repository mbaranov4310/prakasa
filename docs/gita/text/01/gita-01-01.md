```samhita
धृतराष्ट्र उवाच
धर्मक्षेत्रे कुरुक्षेत्रे समवेता युयुत्सवः ।
मामकाः पाण्डवाश्चैव किमकुर्वत संजय ॥
```

```translation
Dhritarashtra said: Gathered on the field of dharma, the field of the Kurus, and eager to fight, what did my men and the sons of Pandu do, Sanjaya?
```

```phrase
Dhritarashtra said:
---

what did my men and the sons of Pandu do, Sanjaya?
```
