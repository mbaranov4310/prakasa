```samhita
संकरो नरकायैव कुलघ्नानां कुलस्य च ।
पतन्ति पितरो ह्येषां लुप्तपिण्डोदकक्रियाः ॥
```

```translation
This mixing leads the family, and those who destroyed it, to hell. Their forefathers fall, the offerings of rice and water having ceased.
```

```phrase
Mixing of the orders leads to hell the destroyers of the family, and the family itself.
---
Their forefathers fall, the offerings of rice and water having ceased.
```
