```samhita
अहो बत महत्पापं कर्तुं व्यवसिता वयम् ।
यद्राज्यसुखलोभेन हन्तुं स्वजनमुद्यताः ॥
```

```translation
Alas, we are resolved to commit a great sin, in that greed for the joys of a kingdom has set us to kill our own people.
```

```phrase
Alas, we are resolved to commit a great sin,
---
set by greed for the joys of a kingdom to kill our own people.
```
