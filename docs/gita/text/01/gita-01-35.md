```samhita
एतान्न हन्तुमिच्छामि घ्नतोऽपि मधुसूदन ।
अपि त्रैलोक्यराज्यस्य हेतोः किं नु महीकृते ॥
```

```translation
I do not want to kill them, Madhusudana, even if they kill me, even for the sake of kingship over the three worlds. How much less for the sake of the earth.
```

```phrase
I do not want to kill them, even if they kill me, Madhusudana,
---
even for kingship over the three worlds, let alone for the earth.
```
