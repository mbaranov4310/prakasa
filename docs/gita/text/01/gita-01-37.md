```samhita
तस्मान्नार्हा वयं हन्तुं धार्तराष्ट्रान्सबान्धवान् ।
स्वजनं हि कथं हत्वा सुखिनः स्याम माधव ॥
```

```translation
Therefore we should not kill Dhritarashtra's sons along with their kinsmen. How could we be happy, Madhava, after killing our own people?
```
