```samhita
अधर्माभिभवात्कृष्ण प्रदुष्यन्ति कुलस्त्रियः ।
स्त्रीषु दुष्टासु वार्ष्णेय जायते वर्णसंकरः ॥
```

```translation
When adharma prevails, Krishna, the women of the family are corrupted. When the women are corrupted, Varshneya, the orders of society are mixed.
```

```phrase
When adharma prevails, Krishna, the women of the family are corrupted.
---
When the women are corrupted, Varshneya, the orders of society are mixed.
```
