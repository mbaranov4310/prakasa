```samhita
सीदन्ति मम गात्राणि मुखं च परिशुष्यति ।
वेपथुश्च शरीरे मे रोमहर्षश्च जायते ॥
```

```translation
my limbs sink, and my mouth is dry. A trembling comes over my body, and my hair stands on end.
```

```phrase

---
A trembling comes over my body, and my hair stands on end.
```
