```samhita
अपर्याप्तं तदस्माकं बलं भीष्माभिरक्षितम् ।
पर्याप्तं त्विदमेतेषां बलं भीमाभिरक्षितम् ॥
```

```translation
That army of ours, guarded by Bhishma, is inadequate, while this army of theirs, guarded by Bhima, is adequate.
```

```phrase
That army of ours, guarded by Bhishma, is inadequate,
---
while this army of theirs, guarded by Bhima, is adequate.
```
