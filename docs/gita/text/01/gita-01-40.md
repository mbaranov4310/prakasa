```samhita
कुलक्षये प्रणश्यन्ति कुलधर्माः सनातनाः ।
धर्मे नष्टे कुलं कृत्स्नमधर्मोऽभिभवत्युत ॥
```

```translation
When a family is destroyed, its ancient duties perish. When dharma is lost, adharma overcomes the whole family.
```

```phrase

---
When dharma is lost, adharma overcomes the whole family.
```
