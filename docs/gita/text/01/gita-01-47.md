```samhita
संजय उवाच
एवमुक्त्वाऽर्जुनः संख्ये रथोपस्थ उपाविशत् ।
विसृज्य सशरं चापं शोकसंविग्नमानसः ॥
```

```translation
Sanjaya said: Having spoken so, Arjuna sat down on the seat of the chariot in the midst of the battle, casting aside his bow and arrow, his mind stricken with grief.
```
