```samhita
अर्जुन उवाच
हृषीकेशं तदा वाक्यमिदमाह महीपते ।
सेनयोरुभयोर्मध्ये रथं स्थापय मेऽच्युत ॥
```

```translation
Arjuna said: O lord of the earth, he then spoke this word to Hrishikesha: Place my chariot between the two armies, Acyuta.
```
