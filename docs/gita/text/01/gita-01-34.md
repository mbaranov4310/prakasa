```samhita
आचार्याः पितरः पुत्रास्तथैव च पितामहाः ।
मातुलाः श्वशुराः पौत्राः स्यालाः संबन्धिनस्तथा ॥
```

```translation
Teachers, fathers, sons, and grandfathers as well, maternal uncles, fathers-in-law, grandsons, brothers-in-law, and kinsmen too.
```

```phrase
Teachers, fathers, sons, and grandfathers as well,

maternal uncles, fathers-in-law, grandsons, brothers-in-law, and kinsmen too.
```
