```samhita
यद्यप्येते न पश्यन्ति लोभोपहतचेतसः ।
कुलक्षयकृतं दोषं मित्रद्रोहे च पातकम् ॥
```

```translation
Even if these men, their minds overcome by greed, do not see the evil done by destroying a family, or the sin in betraying friends,
```
