```samhita
पश्यैतां पाण्डुपुत्राणामाचार्य महतीं चमूम् ।
व्यूढां द्रुपदपुत्रेण तव शिष्येण धीमता ॥
```

```translation
Look, teacher, at this great army of the sons of Pandu, drawn up by your wise pupil, the son of Drupada.
```

```phrase
Look at this great army of the sons of Pandu, O teacher,
---
drawn up by your wise pupil, the son of Drupada.
```
