```samhita
संजय उवाच
दृष्ट्वा तु पाण्डवानीकं व्यूढं दुर्योधनस्तदा ।
आचार्यमुपसंगम्य राजा वचनमब्रवीत् ॥
```

```translation
Sanjaya said: Then, seeing the Pandava army drawn up, King Duryodhana approached the teacher and spoke these words.
```
