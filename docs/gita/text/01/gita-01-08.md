```samhita
भवान्भीष्मश्च कर्णश्च कृपश्च समितिंजयः ।
अश्वत्थामा विकर्णश्च सौमदत्तिर्जयद्रथः ॥
```

```translation
Yourself and Bhishma, Karna and Kripa, victorious in battle, Ashvatthaman, Vikarna, and Jayadratha, the son of Somadatta,
```

```phrase
Yourself and Bhishma, Karna and Kripa, victorious in battle,
---
Ashvatthaman, Vikarna, and Jayadratha, the son of Somadatta,
```
