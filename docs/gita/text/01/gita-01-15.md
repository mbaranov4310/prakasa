```samhita
पाञ्चजन्यं हृषीकेशो देवदत्तं धनंजयः ।
पौण्ड्रं दध्मौ महाशङ्खं भीमकर्मा वृकोदरः ॥
```

```translation
Hrishikesha blew the Panchajanya, and Dhananjaya the Devadatta. Vrikodara, whose deeds are terrible, blew his great conch, the Paundra.
```

```phrase
Hrishikesha blew the Panchajanya, and Dhananjaya the Devadatta.

Vrikodara, whose deeds are terrible, blew his great conch, the Paundra.
```
