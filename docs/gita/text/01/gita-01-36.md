```samhita
निहत्य धार्तराष्ट्रान्नः का प्रीतिः स्याज्जनार्दन ।
पापमेवाश्रयेदस्मान्हत्वैतानाततायिनः ॥
```

```translation
What joy would be ours, Janardana, in killing Dhritarashtra's sons? Sin alone would come upon us if we killed these attackers.
```

