```samhita
येषामर्थे काङ्क्षितं नो राज्यं भोगाः सुखानि च ।
त इमेऽवस्थिता युद्धे प्राणांस्त्यक्त्वा धनानि च ॥
```

```translation
Those for whose sake we desired a kingdom, enjoyments, and pleasures stand here in battle, having given up their lives and their wealth.
```

```phrase

stand here in battle, having given up their lives and their wealth.
```
