```samhita
अयनेषु च सर्वेषु यथाभागमवस्थिताः ।
भीष्ममेवाभिरक्षन्तु भवन्तः सर्व एव हि ॥
```

```translation
So, stationed each in his own place in every division, all of you must guard Bhishma.
```

```phrase
Stationed each in his own place in every division,
---
all of you must guard Bhishma alone.
```
