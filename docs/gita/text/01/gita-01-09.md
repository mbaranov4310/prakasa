```samhita
अन्ये च बहवः शूरा मदर्थे त्यक्तजीविताः ।
नानाशस्त्रप्रहरणाः सर्वे युद्धविशारदाः ॥
```

```translation
and many other heroes, ready to give up their lives for my sake, armed with many kinds of weapons, all skilled in war.
```
