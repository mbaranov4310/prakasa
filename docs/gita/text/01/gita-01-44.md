```samhita
उत्सन्नकुलधर्माणां मनुष्याणां जनार्दन ।
नरके नियतं वासो भवतीत्यनुशुश्रुम ॥
```

```translation
We have heard, Janardana, that for men whose family duties have been destroyed, a dwelling in hell is certain.
```
