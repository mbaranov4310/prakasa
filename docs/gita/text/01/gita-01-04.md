```samhita
अत्र शूरा महेष्वासा भीमार्जुनसमा युधि ।
युयुधानो विराटश्च द्रुपदश्च महारथः ॥
```

```translation
Here are heroes, great archers, equal in battle to Bhima and Arjuna: Yuyudhana, Virata, and Drupada, a great chariot-warrior,
```
