```samhita
योत्स्यमानानवेक्षेऽहं य एतेऽत्र समागताः ।
धार्तराष्ट्रस्य दुर्बुद्धेर्युद्धे प्रियचिकीर्षवः ॥
```

```translation
I will look at those gathered here who are about to fight, wishing to please the evil-minded son of Dhritarashtra in the war.
```

```phrase
I will look at those who have come here about to fight,
---
wishing to please in war the evil-minded son of Dhritarashtra.
```
