```samhita
तत्रापश्यत्स्थितान्पार्थः पितृनथ पितामहान् ।
आचार्यान्मातुलान्भ्रातृन्पुत्रान्पौत्रान्सखींस्तथा ॥
```

```translation
There the son of Pritha saw, standing, fathers and grandfathers, teachers, maternal uncles, brothers, sons, grandsons, and companions,
```

```phrase
There the son of Pritha saw them standing: fathers and grandfathers,

teachers, maternal uncles, brothers, sons, grandsons, and companions,
```
