```samhita
अस्माकं तु विशिष्टा ये तान्निबोध द्विजोत्तम ।
नायका मम सैन्यस्य संज्ञार्थं तान्ब्रवीमि ते ॥
```

```translation
Know also, best of the twice-born, those who are distinguished on our side. I name them to you, the leaders of my army, so that you may know them.
```

```phrase
Know also, best of the twice-born, those who are distinguished on our side.
```
