```samhita
अनन्तविजयं राजा कुन्तीपुत्रो युधिष्ठिरः ।
नकुलः सहदेवश्च सुघोषमणिपुष्पकौ ॥
```

```translation
King Yudhishthira, the son of Kunti, blew the Anantavijaya, and Nakula and Sahadeva blew the Sughosha and the Manipushpaka.
```

