```samhita
यावदेतान्निरीक्षेऽहं योद्धुकामानवस्थितान् ।
कैर्मया सह योद्धव्यमस्मिन्रणसमुद्यमे ॥
```

```translation
while I look over these men standing here eager to fight, and see with whom I must fight in this undertaking of battle.
```
