```samhita
गाण्डीवं स्रंसते हस्तात्त्वक्चैव परिदह्यते ।
न च शक्नोम्यवस्थातुं भ्रमतीव च मे मनः ॥
```

```translation
The Gandiva slips from my hand, and my skin burns. I cannot stand, and my mind seems to reel.
```

```phrase
The Gandiva slips from my hand, and my skin burns.
---
I cannot stand, and my mind seems to reel.
```
