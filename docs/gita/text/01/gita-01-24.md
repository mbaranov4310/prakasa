```samhita
संजय उवाच
एवमुक्तो हृषीकेशो गुडाकेशेन भारत ।
सेनयोरुभयोर्मध्ये स्थापयित्वा रथोत्तमम् ॥
```

```translation
Sanjaya said: O descendant of Bharata, Hrishikesha, so addressed by Gudakesha, drew up that finest of chariots between the two armies
```

```phrase
Sanjaya said:
---
Addressed in this way by Gudakesha, O descendant of Bharata, Hrishikesha,
---
having drawn up that finest of chariots between the two armies,
```
