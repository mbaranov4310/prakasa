```samhita
कथं न ज्ञेयमस्माभिः पापादस्मान्निवर्तितुम् ।
कुलक्षयकृतं दोषं प्रपश्यद्भिर्जनार्दन ॥
```

```translation
why should we not know enough to turn back from this sin, we who see the evil done by destroying a family, Janardana?
```

