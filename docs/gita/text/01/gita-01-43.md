```samhita
दोषैरेतैः कुलघ्नानां वर्णसंकरकारकैः ।
उत्साद्यन्ते जातिधर्माः कुलधर्माश्च शाश्वताः ॥
```

```translation
By these evils of the men who destroy a family, evils that mix the orders of society, the everlasting duties of caste and of family are uprooted.
```

```phrase
By these evils of the destroyers of a family, which mix the orders,
---
the everlasting duties of caste and of family are uprooted.
```
