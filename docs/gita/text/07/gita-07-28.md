```samhita
येषां त्वन्तगतं पापं
जनानां पुण्यकर्मणाम् ।
ते द्वन्द्वमोहनिर्मुक्ता
भजन्ते मां दृढव्रताः ॥
```

```translation
But those people of good deeds whose evil has nearly run out, freed from the delusion of the pairs, worship me, firm in their vow.
```

```phrase
But those whose evil has nearly run out,
---
people of good deeds,
---
freed from the delusion of the pairs,
---
worship me, firm in their vow.
```
