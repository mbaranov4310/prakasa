```samhita
भूमिरापोऽनलो वायुः खं मनो बुद्धिरेव च ।
अहंकार इतीयं मे भिन्ना प्रकृतिरष्टधा ॥
```

```translation
Earth, water, fire, air, space, mind, and intellect too, and the thought "I": this, divided eightfold, is my nature.
```

```phrase
Earth, water, fire, air, space, mind, and intellect too.
---
The thought "I": this, divided eightfold, is my nature.
```
