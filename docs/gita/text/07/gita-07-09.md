```samhita
पुण्यो गन्धः पृथिव्यां च
तेजश्चास्मि विभावसौ ।
जीवनं सर्वभूतेषु
तपश्चास्मि तपस्विषु ॥
```

```translation
I am the pure smell in the earth, and the brilliance in fire. I am the life in all beings, and the austerity in ascetics.
```

```phrase
I am the pure smell in the earth, and
---
the brilliance in fire.
---
I am the life in all beings,
---
and the austerity in ascetics.
```
