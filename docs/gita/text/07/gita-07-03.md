```samhita
मनुष्याणां सहस्रेषु कश्चिद्यतति सिद्धये ।
यततामपि सिद्धानां कश्चिन्मां वेत्ति तत्त्वतः ॥
```

```translation
Among thousands of men, someone strives for success. Even of those who strive, and they are the successful, hardly one knows me as I truly am.
```

```phrase
Among thousands of men, someone strives for success.
---
Even of those who strive, the successful, hardly one knows me as I truly am.
```
