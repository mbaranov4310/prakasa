```samhita
रसोऽहमप्सु कौन्तेय प्रभास्मि शशिसूर्ययोः ।
प्रणवः सर्ववेदेषु शब्दः खे पौरुषं नृषु ॥
```

```translation
I am the taste in water, O son of Kuntī, and the light of the moon and the sun. I am the syllable Om in all the Vedas, sound in space, and manhood in men.
```

```phrase
I am the taste in water, O son of Kuntī, and the light of the moon and the sun.

I am the syllable Om in all the Vedas, sound in space, and manhood in men.
```
