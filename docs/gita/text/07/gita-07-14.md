```samhita
दैवी ह्येषा गुणमयी मम माया दुरत्यया ।
मामेव ये प्रपद्यन्ते मायामेतां तरन्ति ते ॥
```

```translation
This divine power of appearance, mine and made of the qualities, is hard to cross. Those who take refuge in me alone cross this power.
```

```phrase
For this divine power of appearance, mine and made of the qualities, is hard to cross.
---
Those who take refuge in me alone cross this power.
```
