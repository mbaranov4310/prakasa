```samhita
वेदाहं समतीतानि वर्तमानानि चार्जुन ।
भविष्याणि च भूतानि मां तु वेद न कश्चन ॥
```

```translation
I know the beings that have passed, O Arjuna, and those that are present, and those that will be. But no one knows me, except the one devotee who has taken refuge.
```

```phrase
I know those that have passed and those that are present, O Arjuna,
---
and the beings that will be; but no one knows me, except the one devotee who has taken refuge.
```
