```samhita
बीजं मां सर्वभूतानां विद्धि पार्थ सनातनम् ।
बुद्धिर्बुद्धिमतामस्मि तेजस्तेजस्विनामहम् ॥
```

```translation
Know me, O Pārtha, as the ancient seed of all beings. I am the intellect of those who have it, and the boldness of those who have that.
```

```phrase
Know me, O Pārtha, as the ancient seed of all beings.
---
I am the intellect of those who have it, and the boldness of those who have that.
```
