```samhita
जरामरणमोक्षाय
मामाश्रित्य यतन्ति ये ।
ते ब्रह्म तद्विदुः कृत्स्न
मध्यात्मं कर्म चाखिलम् ॥
```

```translation
Those who strive, taking refuge in me, for release from old age and death — they know that Brahman, and the whole of what concerns the self, and action in full.
```

```phrase
For release from old age and death,
---
those who strive, taking refuge in me,
---
they know that Brahman,
---
and the whole of what concerns the self, and action in full.
```
