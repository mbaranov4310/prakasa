```samhita
यो यो यां यां तनुं भक्तः श्रद्धयार्चितुमिच्छति ।
तस्य तस्याचलां श्रद्धां तामेव विदधाम्यहम् ॥
```

```translation
Whoever, as a devotee, wishes with faith to worship whichever form of a deity, I make that very faith of his unwavering.
```

```phrase
Whoever, a devotee, wishes with faith to worship whichever form,
---
of that person I make that very faith unwavering.
```
