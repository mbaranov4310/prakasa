```samhita
न मां दुष्कृतिनो मूढाः प्रपद्यन्ते नराधमाः ।
माययापहृतज्ञाना आसुरं भावमाश्रिताः ॥
```

```translation
Evildoers, the deluded, the lowest of men, do not take refuge in me. Their knowledge stolen by the power of appearance, they have taken to a demonic nature.
```

