```samhita
श्रीभगवानुवाच
मय्यासक्तमनाः पार्थ योगं युञ्जन्मदाश्रयः ।
असंशयं समग्रं मां यथा ज्ञास्यसि तच्छृणु ॥
```

```translation
The blessed Lord said: Hear that by which you, with your mind fixed on me, practicing yoga and taking refuge in me alone, will know me without doubt, entire.
```

```phrase
The blessed Lord said:
---
With your mind fixed on me, O Pārtha, practicing yoga, taking refuge in me alone,
---
hear that by which you will know me without doubt, entire.
```
