```samhita
ज्ञानं तेऽहं सविज्ञानमिदं वक्ष्याम्यशेषतः ।
यज्ज्ञात्वा नेह भूयोऽन्यज्ज्ञातव्यमवशिष्यते ॥
```

```translation
I shall declare to you this knowledge together with direct experience, in full. Knowing it, nothing further that is to be known remains here.
```

```phrase
I shall declare to you this knowledge, together with direct experience, in full.
---
Knowing which, nothing further to be known remains here.
```
