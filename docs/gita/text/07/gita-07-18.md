```samhita
उदाराः सर्व एवैते
ज्ञानी त्वात्मैव मे मतम् ।
आस्थितः स हि युक्तात्मा
मामेवानुत्तमां गतिम् ॥
```

```translation
All these are noble, but the knower is my very self: that is my settled view. For he, his mind steadied, has set out toward me alone, the highest goal.
```

```phrase
All these are noble,
---
but the knower is my very self: that is my settled view.
---
For he, his mind steadied, has set out
---
toward me alone, the highest goal.
```
