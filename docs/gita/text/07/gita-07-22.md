```samhita
स तया श्रद्धया युक्त
स्तस्या राधनमीहते ।
लभते च ततः कामा
न्मयैव विहितान्हि तान् ॥
```

```translation
Endowed with that faith, he pursues the worship of that deity, and from her he obtains those desires, for they are ordained by me alone.
```

```phrase
Endowed with that faith,
---
he pursues the worship of that deity.
---
And from that he obtains the desires
---
ordained by me alone, for those are the ones.
```
