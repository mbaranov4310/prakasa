```samhita
कामैस्तैस्तैर्हृतज्ञानाः प्रपद्यन्तेऽन्यदेवताः ।
तं तं नियममास्थाय प्रकृत्या नियताः खया ॥
```

```translation
Those whose knowledge has been seized by one desire and another resort to other deities. They take up this rule and that, held to it by their own nature.
```

```phrase
Those whose knowledge has been seized by one desire and another resort to other deities.
---
Taking up this rule and that, they are held by their own nature.
```
