```samhita
अन्तवत्तु फलं तेषां
तद्भवत्यल्पमेधसाम् ।
देवान्देवयजो यान्ति
मद्भक्ता यान्ति मामपि ॥
```

```translation
But that fruit of theirs comes to an end, for they are of small wit. Those who worship the gods go to the gods; my devotees come to me as well.
```

```phrase
But that fruit of theirs comes to an end,
---
for it belongs to those of small wit.
---
Worshippers of the gods go to the gods;
---
my devotees come to me as well.
```
