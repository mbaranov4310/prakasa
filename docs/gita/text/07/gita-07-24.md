```samhita
अव्यक्तं व्यक्तिमापन्नं मन्यन्ते मामबुद्धयः ।
परं भावमजानन्तो ममाव्ययमनुत्तमम् ॥
```

```translation
The undiscerning think that I, the unmanifest, have taken on manifestation. They do not know my supreme state, imperishable and unsurpassed.
```

```phrase
The undiscerning think of me, the unmanifest, as having taken on manifestation.
---
They do not know my supreme state, imperishable and unsurpassed.
```
