```samhita
ये चैव सात्त्विका भावा राजसास्तामसाश्च ये ।
मत्त एवेति तान्विद्धि न त्वहं तेषु ते मयि ॥
```

```translation
Whatever states arise from sattva, from rajas, or from tamas, know them as come from me alone. Even so I am not in them. They are in me.
```

```phrase
And whatever states there are, arisen from sattva, and from rajas or from tamas,
---
know them as from me alone. I, however, am not in them. They are in me.
```
