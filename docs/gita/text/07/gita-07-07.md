```samhita
मत्तः परतरं नान्यत्किंचिदस्ति धनंजय ।
मयि सर्वमिदं प्रोतं सूत्रे मणिगणा इव ॥
```

```translation
There is nothing else higher than me, O Dhanaṃjaya. All this is strung on me, as a row of gems on a string.
```

```phrase
There is nothing else higher than me, O Dhanaṃjaya.
---
All this is strung on me, as a row of gems on a string.
```
