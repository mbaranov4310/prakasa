```samhita
साधिभूताधिदैवं मां साधियज्ञं च ये विदुः ।
प्रयाणकालेऽपि च मां ते विदुर्युक्तचेतसः ॥
```

```translation
Those who know me as present with what pertains to creatures and to the gods, and with what pertains to sacrifice — they, their minds steadied, know me even at the time of death.
```

```phrase
Those who know me together with what pertains to creatures and to the gods, and with what pertains to sacrifice,
---
they, their minds steadied, know me even at the time of death.
```
