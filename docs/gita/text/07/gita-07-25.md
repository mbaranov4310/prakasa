```samhita
नाहं प्रकाशः सर्वस्य योगमायासमावृतः ।
मूढोऽयं नाभिजानाति लोको मामजमव्ययम् ॥
```

```translation
I am not manifest to everyone, wrapped as I am in the arranging of the guṇas, which is itself māyā. This deluded world does not know me as unborn and imperishable.
```

