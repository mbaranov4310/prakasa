```samhita
बलं बलवतां चाहं कामरागविवर्जितम् ।
धर्माविरुद्धो भूतेषु कामोऽस्मि भरतर्षभ ॥
```

```translation
And I am the strength of the strong, the strength free of desire and passion. I am the desire in beings that scripture does not forbid, O best of the Bharatas.
```
