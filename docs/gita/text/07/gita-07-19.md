```samhita
बहूनां जन्मनामन्ते ज्ञानवान्मां प्रपद्यते ।
वासुदेवः सर्वमिति स महात्मा सुदुर्लभः ॥
```

```translation
At the end of many births, one whose knowledge has ripened comes to me. He is the great self, very hard to find: he knows that Vāsudeva is everything.
```

```phrase
At the end of many births, one whose knowledge has ripened comes to me.
---
“Vāsudeva is everything”: he, the great self, is very hard to find.
```
