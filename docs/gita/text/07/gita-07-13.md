```samhita
त्रिभिर्गुणमयैर्भावैरेभिः सर्वमिदं जगत् ।
मोहितं नाभिजानाति मामेभ्यः परमव्ययम् ॥
```

```translation
Deluded by these three states made of the qualities, this whole world does not know me, who am beyond them and imperishable.
```

```phrase
By these three states made of the qualities, this whole world,
---
deluded, does not know me, beyond them and imperishable.
```
