```samhita
तेषां ज्ञानी नित्ययुक्त एकभक्तिर्विशिष्यते ।
प्रियो हि ज्ञानिनोऽत्यर्थमहं स च मम प्रियः ॥
```

```translation
Of those four, the knower stands apart: he is always steadfast, and his devotion is to one alone. For I am the Self of the knower, and so exceedingly dear, and he is my very Self, and so dear to me.
```

```phrase
Of them, the knower, always steadfast, whose devotion is to one alone, stands apart.
---
For I am the Self of the knower, and so exceedingly dear, and he is my very Self, and so dear to me.
```
