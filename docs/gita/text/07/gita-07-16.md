```samhita
चतुर्विधा भजन्ते मां जनाः सुकृतिनोऽर्जुन ।
आर्तो जिज्ञासुरर्थार्थी ज्ञानी च भरतर्षभ ॥
```

```translation
Four kinds of people of good deeds worship me, O Arjuna: the one in distress, the one who wishes to know, the one who seeks wealth, and the knower, O best of the Bharatas.
```

```phrase
Four kinds of people, those of good deeds, worship me, O Arjuna,
---
the afflicted, the one who wishes to know, the one who seeks wealth, and the knower, O best of the Bharatas.
```
