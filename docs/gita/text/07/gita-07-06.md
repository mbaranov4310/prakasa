```samhita
एतद्योनीनि भूतानि सर्वाणीत्युपधारय ।
अहं कृत्स्नस्य जगतः प्रभवः प्रलयस्तथा ॥
```

```translation
Understand that all beings have these two as their source. I am the origin of the whole world, and likewise its dissolution.
```

```phrase

I am the origin of the whole world, and likewise its dissolution.
```
