```samhita
इच्छाद्वेषसमुत्थेन द्वन्द्वमोहेन भारत ।
सर्वभूतानि संमोहं सर्गे यान्ति परंतप ॥
```

```translation
By the delusion caused by the pairs, risen from desire and aversion, all beings fall into delusion at their very birth, O Bhārata, O scorcher of the foe.
```

