```samhita
सदृशं चेष्टते स्वस्याः प्रकृतेर्ज्ञानवानपि ।
प्रकृतिं यान्ति भूतानि निग्रहः किं करिष्यति ॥
```

```translation
Even one who knows acts in keeping with his own nature. Beings follow their nature. What will restraint do?
```

