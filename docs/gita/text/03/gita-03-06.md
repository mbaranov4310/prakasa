```samhita
कर्मेन्द्रियाणि संयम्य य आस्ते मनसा स्मरन् ।
इन्द्रियार्थान्विमूढात्मा मिथ्याचारः स उच्यते ॥
```

```translation
He who holds back the organs of action and sits remembering the objects of the senses with his mind, that man, his mind bewildered, is called a man of false conduct.
```

```phrase
He who holds back the organs of action and sits remembering with his mind,
---
the objects of the senses: that man, his mind bewildered, is called a man of false conduct.
```
