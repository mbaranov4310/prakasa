```samhita
ये मे मतमिदं नित्यमनुतिष्ठन्ति मानवाः ।
श्रद्धावन्तोऽनसूयन्तो मुच्यन्ते तेऽपि कर्मभिः ॥
```

```translation
Men who always practice this teaching of mine, with faith and without finding fault, are released from actions.
```

```phrase
Men who always practice this teaching of mine,
---
who have faith and do not find fault, are released from actions.
```
