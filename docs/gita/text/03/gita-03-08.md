```samhita
नियतं कुरु कर्म त्वं कर्म ज्यायो ह्यकर्मणः ।
शरीरयात्रापि च ते न प्रसिध्येदकर्मणः ॥
```

```translation
Do the action that is laid down for you. Action is better than not acting, and without action even the keeping of your body would not come to pass.
```

```phrase

and without action even the keeping of your body would not come to pass.
```
