```samhita
श्रेयान्स्वधर्मो विगुणः परधर्मात्स्वनुष्ठितात् ।
स्वधर्मे निधनं श्रेयः परधर्मो भयावहः ॥
```

```translation
One's own duty, even without its good qualities, is better than another's duty well performed. Death in one's own duty is better. Another's duty brings danger.
```

```phrase

Death in one's own duty is better. Another's duty brings danger.
```
