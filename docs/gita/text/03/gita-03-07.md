```samhita
यस्त्विन्द्रियाणि मनसा नियम्यारभतेऽर्जुन ।
कर्मेन्द्रियैः कर्मयोगमसक्तः स विशिष्यते ॥
```

```translation
But he who controls the senses with the mind and, unattached, undertakes the yoga of action with the organs of action, O Arjuna: he excels.
```

```phrase
But he who controls the senses with the mind and sets to work, O Arjuna,
---
unattached, on the yoga of action, with the organs of action: he excels.
```
