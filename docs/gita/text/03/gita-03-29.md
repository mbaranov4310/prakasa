```samhita
प्रकृतेर्गुणसंमूढाः सज्जन्ते गुणकर्मसु ।
तानकृत्स्नविदो मन्दान्कृत्स्नविन्न विचालयेत् ॥
```

```translation
Those deluded by the qualities of nature cling to the actions of the qualities. He who knows the whole should not shake those dull people, who do not know it.
```
