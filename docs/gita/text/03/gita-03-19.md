```samhita
तस्मादसक्तः सततं कार्यं कर्म समाचर ।
असक्तो ह्याचरन्कर्म परमाप्नोति पूरुषः ॥
```

```translation
Therefore, unattached, always perform the action that is to be done. A man who performs action without attachment reaches the highest.
```

```phrase
Therefore, unattached, always perform the action that is to be done.
---
For a man who performs action without attachment reaches the highest.
```
