```samhita
यज्ञशिष्टाशिनः सन्तो मुच्यन्ते सर्वकिल्बिषैः ।
भुञ्जते ते त्वघं पापा ये पचन्त्यात्मकारणात् ॥
```

```translation
The good, who eat what is left from sacrifice, are released from every stain. But the wicked, who cook for their own sake, eat only evil.
```

```phrase
The good, who eat what is left from sacrifice, are released from every stain.
---
But the wicked, who cook for their own sake, eat only evil.
```
