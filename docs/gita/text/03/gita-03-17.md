```samhita
यस्त्वात्मरतिरेव स्यादात्मतृप्तश्च मानवः ।
आत्मन्येव च संतुष्टस्तस्य कार्यं न विद्यते ॥
```

```translation
But the man who delights in the self alone, who is content with the self and satisfied in the self alone: for him nothing remains to be done.
```

```phrase
But the man who delights in the self alone, and is content with the self,
---
and is satisfied in the self alone: for him nothing remains to be done.
```
