```samhita
व्यामिश्रेणेव वाक्येन बुद्धिं मोहयसीव मे ।
तदेकं वद निश्चित्य येन श्रेयोऽहमाप्नुयाम् ॥
```

```translation
With a speech that seems confused, you confuse my understanding, as it were. Decide, and tell me the one path by which I may reach the good.
```

