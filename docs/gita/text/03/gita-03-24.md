```samhita
उत्सीदेयुरिमे लोका न कुर्यां कर्म चेदहम् ।
संकरस्य च कर्ता स्यामुपहन्यामिमाः प्रजाः ॥
```

```translation
If I did not perform action, these worlds would perish. I would be the maker of the confusion of orders, and I would harm these creatures.
```

```phrase
These worlds would perish if I did not perform action.
---
I would be the maker of the confusion of orders, and I would harm these creatures.
```
