```samhita
अन्नाद्भवन्ति भूतानि पर्जन्यादन्नसंभवः ।
यज्ञाद्भवति पर्जन्यो यज्ञः कर्मसमुद्भवः ॥
```

```translation
From food, beings come to be, and from rain comes the arising of food. From sacrifice comes rain, and sacrifice arises from action.
```

```phrase
From food, beings come to be, and from rain comes the arising of food.
---
From sacrifice comes rain, and sacrifice arises from action.
```
