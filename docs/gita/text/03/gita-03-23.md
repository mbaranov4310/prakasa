```samhita
यदि ह्यहं न वर्तेय जातु कर्मण्यतन्द्रितः ।
मम वर्त्मानुवर्तन्ते मनुष्याः पार्थ सर्वशः ॥
```

```translation
If I did not tirelessly engage in action, O son of Pritha, men would follow my path in every way.
```

```phrase
If I ever failed to engage in action, unwearied,
---
men would follow my path in every way, O son of Pritha.
```
