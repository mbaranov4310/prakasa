```samhita
नैव तस्य कृतेनार्थो नाकृतेनेह कश्चन ।
न चास्य सर्वभूतेषु कश्चिदर्थव्यपाश्रयः ॥
```

```translation
He has no end to gain by what he does, and no harm here from what he does not do. Nor has he any end that depends on any being.
```

```phrase
He has no end to gain by what he does, and no harm here from what he does not do.
---
Nor has he any end that depends on any being.
```
