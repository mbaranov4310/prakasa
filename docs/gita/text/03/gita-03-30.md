```samhita
मयि सर्वाणि कर्माणि संन्यस्याध्यात्मचेतसा ।
निराशीर्निर्ममो भूत्वा युध्यस्व विगतज्वरः ॥
```

```translation
Laying every action on me with a mind turned to the self, and becoming free of hope and of the thought "mine," fight, free of the fever of grief.
```
