```samhita
सहयज्ञाः प्रजाः सृष्ट्वा पुरोवाच प्रजापतिः ।
अनेन प्रसविष्यध्वमेष वोऽस्त्विष्टकामधुक् ॥
```

```translation
Of old, having brought forth creatures together with sacrifice, the lord of creatures said: By this, bring forth increase. Let this be your yielder of the objects you wish.
```
