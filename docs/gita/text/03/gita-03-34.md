```samhita
इन्द्रियस्येन्द्रियस्यार्थे रागद्वेषौ व्यवस्थितौ ।
तयोर्न वशमागच्छेत्तौ ह्यस्य परिपन्थिनौ ॥
```

```translation
Attraction and aversion stand in the object of every sense. One should not come under their power. They are the ones who block his road.
```

```phrase
Attraction and aversion stand in the object of every sense.
---
One should not come under their power, for they block his road.
```
