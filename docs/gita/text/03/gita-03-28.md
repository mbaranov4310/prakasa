```samhita
तत्त्ववित्तु महाबाहो गुणकर्मविभागयोः ।
गुणा गुणेषु वर्तन्त इति मत्वा न सज्जते ॥
```

```translation
But he who knows the truth of how the qualities and actions are distinguished, O mighty-armed, understands that the qualities move among the qualities, and he is not attached.
```

```phrase
But he who knows the truth of the distinction between the qualities and actions, O mighty-armed,
---
understanding that the qualities move among the qualities, is not attached.
```
