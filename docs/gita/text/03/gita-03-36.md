```samhita
अर्जुन उवाच
अथ केन प्रयुक्तोऽयं पापं चरति पूरुषः ।
अनिच्छन्नपि वार्ष्णेय बलादिव नियोजितः ॥
```

```translation
Arjuna said: Driven by what does a man do evil even without wishing to, O descendant of Vrishni, as if compelled by force?
```
