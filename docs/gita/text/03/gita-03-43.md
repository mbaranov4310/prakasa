```samhita
एवं बुद्धेः परं बुद्ध्वा संस्तभ्यात्मानमात्मना ।
जहि शत्रुं महाबाहो कामरूपं दुरासदम् ॥
```

```translation
Thus knowing what is higher than the understanding, and steadying the self by the self, strike down that enemy, O mighty-armed: desire, hard to reach.
```

```phrase
Thus knowing what is higher than the understanding, and steadying the self by the self,
---
strike down the enemy in the form of desire, hard to reach, O mighty-armed.
```
