```samhita
प्रकृतेः क्रियमाणानि गुणैः कर्माणि सर्वशः ।
अहंकारविमूढात्मा कर्ताऽहमिति मन्यते ॥
```

```translation
Actions are done on every side by the qualities of nature. One whose mind is deluded by the thought "I" imagines, "I am the doer."
```
