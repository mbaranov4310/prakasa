```samhita
इन्द्रियाणि पराण्याहुरिन्द्रियेभ्यः परं मनः ।
मनसस्तु परा बुद्धिर्यो बुद्धेः परतस्तु सः ॥
```

```translation
They say the senses are higher, and the mind is higher than the senses. Higher than the mind is the understanding, and higher than the understanding is he.
```

```phrase
They say the senses are higher, and the mind is higher than the senses.
---
Higher than the mind is the understanding, and higher still than the understanding is he.
```
