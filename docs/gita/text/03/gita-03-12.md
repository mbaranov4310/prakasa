```samhita
इष्टान्भोगान्हि वो देवा दास्यन्ते यज्ञभाविताः ।
तैर्दत्तानप्रदायैभ्यो यो भुङ्क्ते स्तेन एव सः ॥
```

```translation
The gods, fostered by sacrifice, will give you the enjoyments you wish for. He who enjoys what they have given, without giving to them, is a thief.
```

```phrase
The gods, fostered by sacrifice, will give you the enjoyments you wish for.
---
He who enjoys what they have given, without giving to them, is a thief.
```
