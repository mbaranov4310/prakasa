```samhita
यद्यदाचरति श्रेष्ठस्तत्तदेवेतरो जनः ।
स यत्प्रमाणं कुरुते लोकस्तदनुवर्तते ॥
```

```translation
Whatever a leading man does, that very thing other people do. Whatever standard he sets, the world follows it.
```

```phrase
Whatever a leading man does, that very thing other people do.
---
Whatever standard he sets, the world follows it.
```
