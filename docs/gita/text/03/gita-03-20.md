```samhita
कर्मणैव हि संसिद्धिमास्थिता जनकादयः ।
लोकसंग्रहमेवापि संपश्यन्कर्तुमर्हसि ॥
```

```translation
By action alone, indeed, Janaka and the others reached perfection. You too should act, looking to the holding of the world to its course.
```

```phrase
By action alone, indeed, Janaka and the others reached perfection.
---
You too should act, looking just to the holding of the world to its course.
```
