```samhita
न कर्मणामनारम्भान्नैष्कर्म्यं पुरुषोऽश्नुते ।
न च संन्यसनादेव सिद्धिं समधिगच्छति ॥
```

```translation
A man does not reach freedom from action by not beginning actions, nor does he reach that perfection by renunciation alone.
```

```phrase
A man does not reach freedom from action by not beginning actions,
---
nor does he reach that perfection by renunciation alone.
```
