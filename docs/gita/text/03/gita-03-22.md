```samhita
न मे पार्थास्ति कर्तव्यं त्रिषु लोकेषु किंचन ।
नानवाप्तमवाप्तव्यं वर्त एव च कर्मणि ॥
```

```translation
There is nothing I must do in the three worlds, O son of Pritha, and nothing left unobtained that I still have to obtain. Even so, I engage in action.
```

```phrase
There is nothing I must do in the three worlds, O son of Pritha.
---
Nothing is unobtained, and nothing is still to be obtained; even so, I engage in action.
```
