```samhita
श्रीभगवानुवाच
काम एष क्रोध एष रजोगुणसमुद्भवः ।
महाशनो महापाप्मा विद्ध्येनमिह वैरिणम् ॥
```

```translation
The blessed Lord said: This is desire, and this is anger, born of the quality called rajas. Know it here as the enemy, of great appetite and a great evil.
```

```phrase
The blessed Lord said:
---
This is desire, and this is anger, born of the quality called rajas.
---
Know this, of great appetite and a great evil, as the enemy here.
```
