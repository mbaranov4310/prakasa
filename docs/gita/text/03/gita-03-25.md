```samhita
सक्ताः कर्मण्यविद्वांसो यथा कुर्वन्ति भारत ।
कुर्याद्विद्वांस्तथासक्तश्चिकीर्षुर्लोकसंग्रहम् ॥
```

```translation
As the unknowing, attached to the action, perform it, O Bharata, so should the one who knows perform it, unattached, wishing to hold the world together.
```

```phrase
As the unknowing, attached to the action, perform it, O Bharata,
---
so should the one who knows perform it, unattached, wishing to hold the world together.
```
