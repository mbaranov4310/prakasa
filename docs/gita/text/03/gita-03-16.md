```samhita
एवं प्रवर्तितं चक्रं नानुवर्तयतीह यः ।
अघायुरिन्द्रियारामो मोघं पार्थ स जीवति ॥
```

```translation
He who here does not keep turning the wheel set going in this way lives in vain, O son of Pritha: a man whose life is sin, taking his pleasure in the senses.
```
