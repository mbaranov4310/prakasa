```samhita
तस्मात्त्वमिन्द्रियाण्यादौ नियम्य भरतर्षभ ।
पाप्मानं प्रजहिह्येनं ज्ञानविज्ञाननाशनम् ॥
```

```translation
Therefore, O best of the Bharatas, first restrain the senses, and cast off this evil, the destroyer of knowledge and of knowing.
```

```phrase
Therefore first restrain the senses, O best of the Bharatas.
---
Cast off this evil, the destroyer of knowledge and of knowing.
```
