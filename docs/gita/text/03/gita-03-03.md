```samhita
श्रीभगवानुवाच
लोकेऽस्मिन्द्विविधा निष्ठा पुरा प्रोक्ता मयानघ ।
ज्ञानयोगेन सांख्यानां कर्मयोगेन योगिनाम् ॥
```

```translation
The blessed Lord said: In this world a twofold devotion was taught by me of old, O sinless one: by the yoga of knowledge for the Sankhyas, and by the yoga of action for the yogins.
```

