```samhita
न हि कश्चित्क्षणमपि जातु तिष्ठत्यकर्मकृत् ।
कार्यते ह्यवशः कर्म सर्वः प्रकृतिजैर्गुणैः ॥
```

```translation
For no one ever stays even a moment without doing some action, since everyone is driven to act, helpless, by the qualities born of nature.
```

```phrase

for everyone is driven to act, helpless, by the qualities born of nature.
```
