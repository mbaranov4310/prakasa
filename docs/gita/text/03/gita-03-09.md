```samhita
यज्ञार्थात्कर्मणोऽन्यत्र लोकोऽयं कर्मबन्धनः ।
तदर्थं कर्म कौन्तेय मुक्तसङ्गः समाचर ॥
```

```translation
Other than action done for sacrifice, this world is bound by action. Do action for that end, O son of Kunti, free of attachment.
```

