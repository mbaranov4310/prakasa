```samhita
इन्द्रियाणि मनो बुद्धिरस्याधिष्ठानमुच्यते ।
एतैर्विमोहयत्येष ज्ञानमावृत्य देहिनम् ॥
```

```translation
The senses, the mind, and the understanding are called its seat. Covering knowledge by means of these, it bewilders the embodied one.
```

```phrase
The senses, the mind, and the understanding are called its seat.
---
By these it bewilders the embodied one, covering knowledge.
```
