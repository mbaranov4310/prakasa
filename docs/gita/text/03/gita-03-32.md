```samhita
ये त्वेतदभ्यसूयन्तो नानुतिष्ठन्ति मे मतम् ।
सर्वज्ञानविमूढांस्तान् विद्धि नष्टानचेतसः ॥
```

```translation
But those who find fault with this and do not practice my teaching, know them to be lost, deluded about every kind of knowledge, and without judgment.
```

