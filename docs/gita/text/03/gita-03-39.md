```samhita
आवृतं ज्ञानमेतेन ज्ञानिनो नित्यवैरिणा ।
कामरूपेण कौन्तेय दुष्पूरेणानलेन च ॥
```

```translation
Knowledge is covered by this, the constant enemy of the one who knows, O son of Kunti: by desire, hard to fill, a fire that is never enough.
```

