```samhita
धूमेनाव्रियते वह्निर्यथादर्शो मलेन च ।
यथोल्बेनावृतो गर्भस्तथा तेनेदमावृतम् ॥
```

```translation
As fire is covered by smoke, and a mirror by dust, as an embryo is covered by the caul, so is this covered by that.
```

```phrase

as an embryo is covered by the caul, so is this covered by that.
```
