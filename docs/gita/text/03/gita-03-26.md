```samhita
न बुद्धिभेदं जनयेदज्ञानां कर्मसङ्गिनाम् ।
जोषयेत्सर्वकर्माणि विद्वान्युक्तः समाचरन् ॥
```

```translation
He should not shake the conviction of the unknowing who cling to action. The one who knows, himself engaged, should have them perform every action while he does the same.
```

```phrase
He should not cause a breach in the understanding of the unknowing who cling to action.
---
The one who knows should set them to every action, himself engaged and doing them.
```
