```samhita
क्लेशोऽधिकतरस्तेषामव्यक्तासक्तचेतसाम् ।
अव्यक्ता हि गतिर्दुःखं देहवद्भिरवाप्यते ॥
```

```translation
Greater is the trouble of those whose minds are set on the unmanifest, for the goal that is the unmanifest is reached with difficulty by those who identify with the body.
```

