```samhita
अभ्यासेऽप्यसमर्थोऽसि मत्कर्मपरमो भव ।
मदर्थमपि कर्माणि कुर्वन्सिद्धिमवाप्स्यसि ॥
```

```translation
If you are unable even to practise, be intent on work for me. Even by doing actions for my sake you will attain perfection.
```

```phrase
If you are unable even to practise, be intent on work for me.
---
Even by doing actions for my sake you will attain perfection.
```
