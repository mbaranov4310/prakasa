```samhita
ये त्वक्षरमनिर्देश्यमव्यक्तं पर्युपासते ।
सर्वत्रगमचिन्त्यं च कूटस्थमचलं ध्रुवम् ॥
```

```translation
But those who contemplate the Imperishable, which is indefinable, unmanifest, all-pervading, unthinkable, unchanging, immovable and eternal,
```

```phrase
But those who contemplate the Imperishable, indefinable and unmanifest,
---
all-pervading, unthinkable, unchanging, immovable, eternal,
```
