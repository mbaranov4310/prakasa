```samhita
समः शत्रौ च मित्रे च तथा मानापमानयोः ।
शीतोष्णसुखदुःखेषु समः सङ्गविवर्जितः ॥
```

```translation
The same toward enemy and friend, and likewise in honour and dishonour, the same in cold and heat, pleasure and pain, free from attachment,
```

```phrase

the same in cold and heat, pleasure and pain, free from attachment,
```
