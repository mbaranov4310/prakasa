```samhita
अथैतदप्यशक्तोऽसि कर्तुं मद्योगमाश्रितः ।
सर्वकर्मफलत्यागं ततः कुरु यतात्मवान् ॥
```

```translation
If you are unable to do even this, then, relying on my yoga and self-controlled, give up the fruit of all actions.
```

```phrase
If you are unable to do even this, then, relying on my yoga,
---
give up the fruit of all actions, self-controlled.
```
