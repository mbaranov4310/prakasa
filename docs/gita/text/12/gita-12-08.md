```samhita
मय्येव मन आधत्स्व मयि बुद्धिं निवेशय ।
निवसिष्यसि मय्येव अत ऊर्ध्वं न संशयः ॥
```

```translation
Fix the mind on me alone; settle the intellect in me. You will dwell in me alone hereafter. Of this there is no doubt.
```

