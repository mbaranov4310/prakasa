```samhita
तुल्यनिन्दास्तुतिर्मौनी संतुष्टो येन केनचित् ।
अनिकेतः स्थिरमतिर्भक्तिमान्मे प्रियो नरः ॥
```

```translation
to whom blame and praise are alike, silent, content with whatever comes, homeless, steady in understanding, full of devotion: that person is dear to me.
```

