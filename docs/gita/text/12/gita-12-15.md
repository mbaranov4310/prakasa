```samhita
यस्मान्नोद्विजते लोको लोकान्नोद्विजते च यः ।
हर्षामर्षभयोद्वेगैर्मुक्तो यः स च मे प्रियः ॥
```

```translation
He from whom the world does not shrink, and who does not shrink from the world, who is free from elation, intolerance, fear and agitation: he too is dear to me.
```

```phrase
He from whom the world does not shrink, and who does not shrink from the world,
---
who is free from elation, intolerance, fear and agitation: he too is dear to me.
```
