```samhita
तेषामहं समुद्धर्ता मृत्युसंसारसागरात् ।
भवामि न चिरात्पार्थ मय्यावेशितचेतसाम् ॥
```

```translation
for them, whose minds are fixed on me, O Pārtha, I soon become the deliverer from the ocean of mortal saṃsāra.
```

```phrase
For them I am the deliverer from the ocean of mortal saṃsāra;
---
I become so before long, O Pārtha, for those whose minds are fixed on me.
```
