```samhita
अर्जुन उवाच
एवं सततयुक्ता ये भक्तास्त्वां पर्युपासते ।
ये चाप्यक्षरमव्यक्तं तेषां के योगवित्तमाः ॥
```

```translation
Arjuna said: Some devotees, constantly engaged in this way, worship you; others worship the Imperishable, the unmanifest. Which of these know yoga best?
```

```phrase
Arjuna said:
---
Those devotees who, constantly engaged in this way, worship you,
---
and those who worship the Imperishable, the unmanifest: which of them know yoga best?
```
