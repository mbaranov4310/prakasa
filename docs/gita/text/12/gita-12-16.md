```samhita
अनपेक्षः शुचिर्दक्ष उदासीनो गतव्यथः ।
सर्वारम्भपरित्यागी यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः ॥
```

```translation
He who wants nothing, who is pure, capable, impartial and free from fear, who has given up every undertaking: he, my devotee, is dear to me.
```

```phrase
He who wants nothing, who is pure, capable, impartial and free from fear,
---
who has given up every undertaking: he, my devotee, is dear to me.
```
