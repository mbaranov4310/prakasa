```samhita
ये तु सर्वाणि कर्माणि मयि संन्यस्य मत्पराः ।
अनन्येनैव योगेन मां ध्यायन्त उपासते ॥
```

```translation
But those who give up all actions to me, hold me as the supreme, and worship me, meditating on me with a concentration that has no other object,
```

