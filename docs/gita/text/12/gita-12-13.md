```samhita
अद्वेष्टा सर्वभूतानां मैत्रः करुण एव च ।
निर्ममो निरहंकारः समदुःखसुखः क्षमी ॥
```

```translation
He who hates no being, who is friendly and compassionate, free from 'mine' and 'I', the same in pain and pleasure, forbearing,
```
