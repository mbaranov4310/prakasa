```samhita
संतुष्टः सततं योगी यतात्मा दृढनिश्चयः ।
मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः ॥
```

```translation
always content, a yogin, self-controlled, firm in conviction, with mind and intellect given over to me: he, my devotee, is dear to me.
```

```phrase
always content, a yogin, self-controlled, firm in conviction,
---
with mind and intellect given over to me: he, my devotee, is dear to me.
```
