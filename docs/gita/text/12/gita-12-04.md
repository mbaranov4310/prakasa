```samhita
संनियम्येन्द्रियग्रामं सर्वत्र समबुद्धयः ।
ते प्राप्नुवन्ति मामेव सर्वभूतहिते रताः ॥
```

```translation
having fully restrained all the senses, even-minded at all times, delighting in the good of all beings: they reach me alone.
```

```phrase
having fully restrained all the senses, even-minded at all times,
---
delighting in the good of all beings: they reach me alone.
```
