```samhita
श्रेयो हि ज्ञानमभ्यासाज्ज्ञानाद्ध्यानं विशिष्यते ।
ध्यानात्कर्मफलत्यागस्त्यागाच्छान्तिरनन्तरम् ॥
```

```translation
Knowledge is indeed better than practice; meditation is superior to knowledge; giving up the fruit of action is superior to meditation. From that giving up, peace follows at once.
```

```phrase
Knowledge is indeed better than practice; meditation is superior to knowledge;
---
giving up the fruit of action is superior to meditation. From that giving up, peace follows at once.
```
