```samhita
यो न हृष्यति न द्वेष्टि न शोचति न काङ्क्षति ।
शुभाशुभपरित्यागी भक्तिमान्यः स मे प्रियः ॥
```

```translation
He who neither rejoices nor hates, neither grieves nor longs, who has given up good and bad actions and is full of devotion: he is dear to me.
```
