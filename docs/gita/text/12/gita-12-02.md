```samhita
श्रीभगवानुवाच
मय्यावेश्य मनो ये मां नित्ययुक्ता उपासते ।
श्रद्धया परयोपेतास्ते मे युक्ततमा मताः ॥
```

```translation
The Blessed Lord said: Those who fix the mind on me and worship me, ever steadfast and endowed with supreme faith, I hold to be the best yogins.
```

```phrase
The Blessed Lord said:
---
Those who fix the mind on me and worship me, ever steadfast,
---
endowed with supreme faith: I hold them to be the best yogins.
```
