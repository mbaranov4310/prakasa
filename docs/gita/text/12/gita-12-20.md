```samhita
ये तु धर्म्यामृतमिदं यथोक्तं पर्युपासते ।
श्रद्दधाना मत्परमा भक्तास्तेऽतीव मे प्रियाः ॥
```

```translation
But those who follow this nectar of dharma as it has been taught, with faith, holding me as the highest goal: those devotees are exceedingly dear to me.
```
