```samhita
अथ चित्तं समाधातुं न शक्नोषि मयि स्थिरम् ।
अभ्यासयोगेन ततो मामिच्छाप्तुं धनंजय ॥
```

```translation
If you cannot fix the mind steadily on me, then seek to reach me by the yoga of practice, O Dhanaṃjaya.
```
