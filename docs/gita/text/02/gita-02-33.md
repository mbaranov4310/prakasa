```samhita
अथ चेत्त्वमिमं धर्म्यं संग्रामं न करिष्यसि ।
ततः स्वधर्मं कीर्तिं च हित्वा पापमवाप्स्यसि ॥
```

```translation
But if you will not fight this lawful battle, then you will abandon your own duty and your fame, and incur only guilt.
```

```phrase
But if you will not fight this lawful battle,

then, abandoning your own duty and your fame, you will incur guilt.
```
