```samhita
तानि सर्वाणि संयम्य युक्त आसीत मत्परः ।
वशे हि यस्येन्द्रियाणि तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता ॥
```

```translation
Let him sit, having brought all those senses under control, collected, intent on Me. For his knowledge is settled whose senses are in his control.
```

```phrase
Let him sit, having brought all of them under control, collected, intent on Me,
---
for his knowledge is settled whose senses are in his control.
```
