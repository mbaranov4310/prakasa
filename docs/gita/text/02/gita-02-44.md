```samhita
भोगैश्वर्यप्रसक्तानां तयापहृतचेतसाम् ।
व्यवसायात्मिका बुद्धिः समाधौ न विधीयते ॥
```

```translation
In those who are attached to pleasure and power, and whose minds that speech has carried off, the resolute understanding does not arise.
```

```phrase
In those attached to pleasure and power, whose minds that speech has carried off,
---
the resolute understanding does not arise in the mind.
```
