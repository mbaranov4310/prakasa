```samhita
इन्द्रियाणां हि चरतां यन्मनोऽनुविधीयते ।
तदस्य हरति प्रज्ञां वायुर्नावमिवाम्भसि ॥
```

```translation
For the mind that follows the senses as they wander carries off his knowledge, as the wind carries a boat on the water.
```

