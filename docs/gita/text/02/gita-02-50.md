```samhita
बुद्धियुक्तो जहातीह उभे सुकृतदुष्कृते ।
तस्माद्योगाय युज्यस्व योगः कर्मसु कौशलम् ॥
```

```translation
One endowed with that understanding casts off here both merit and demerit. Therefore apply yourself to yoga. Yoga is skill in action.
```

```phrase
One endowed with understanding casts off here both good and evil.
---
Therefore apply yourself to yoga. Yoga is skill in action.
```
