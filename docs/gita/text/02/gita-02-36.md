```samhita
अवाच्यवादांश्च बहून् वदिष्यन्ति तवाहिताः ।
निन्दन्तस्तव सामर्थ्यं ततो दुःखतरं नु किम् ॥
```

```translation
Your enemies will say many things that should not be said, belittling your prowess. What pain is worse than that?
```
