```samhita
कामात्मानः स्वर्गपरा जन्मकर्मफलप्रदाम् ।
क्रियाविशेषबहुलां भोगैश्वर्यगतिं प्रति ॥
```

```translation
they whose nature is desire and for whom heaven is the highest end: a speech that yields birth as the fruit of action, full of particular rites aimed at pleasure and power.
```

```phrase

full of particular rites, aimed at the attainment of pleasure and power.
```
