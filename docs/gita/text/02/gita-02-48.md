```samhita
योगस्थः कुरु कर्माणि
सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा धनंजय ।
सिद्ध्यसिद्ध्योः समो भूत्वा
समत्वं योग उच्यते ॥
```

```translation
Standing in yoga, do actions, Dhanaṃjaya, having given up attachment and become the same in success and failure. Evenness is called yoga.
```

```phrase
Standing in yoga, do actions,
---
having given up attachment, Dhanaṃjaya,
---
having become the same in success and failure.
---
Evenness is called yoga.
```
