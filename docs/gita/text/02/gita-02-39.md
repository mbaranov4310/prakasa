```samhita
एषा तेऽभिहिता सांख्ये बुद्धिर्योगे त्विमां शृणु ।
बुद्ध्या युक्तो यया पार्थ कर्मबन्धं प्रहास्यसि ॥
```

```translation
This understanding of Sāṃkhya has been declared to you. Hear now this understanding of yoga, endowed with which, son of Pṛthā, you will cast off the bond of action.
```

```phrase
This understanding of Sāṃkhya has been declared to you; hear now this, of yoga,
```
