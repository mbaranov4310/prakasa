```samhita
प्रसादे सर्वदुःखानां हानिरस्योपजायते ।
प्रसन्नचेतसो ह्याशु बुद्धिः पर्यवतिष्ठते ॥
```

```translation
In serenity an end of all his pains comes about, for the judgment of a man whose mind is clear soon settles on every side.
```

```phrase

for the judgment of one whose mind is clear soon settles on every side.
```
