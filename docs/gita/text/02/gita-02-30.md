```samhita
देही नित्यमवध्योऽयं देहे सर्वस्य भारत ।
तस्मात्सर्वाणि भूतानि न त्वं शोचितुमर्हसि ॥
```

```translation
The embodied one can never be killed, Bharata, though he stands in the body of every being. Therefore you should not grieve for any of these beings.
```

```phrase
The embodied one, in the body of every being, can never be killed, Bharata.
---
Therefore you should not grieve for all these beings.
```
