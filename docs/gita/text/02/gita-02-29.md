```samhita
आश्चर्यवत्पश्यति कश्चिदेन
माश्चर्यवद्वदति तथैव चान्यः ।
आश्चर्यवच्चैनमन्यः शृणोति
श्रुत्वाप्येनं वेद न चैव कश्चित् ॥
```

```translation
Someone sees him as a wonder. Another likewise speaks of him as a wonder, and another hears of him as a wonder. Even having heard, no one at all knows him.
```

```phrase
Someone sees him as a wonder.
---
Another likewise speaks of him as a wonder.
---
And another hears of him as a wonder.
---
Even having heard, no one at all knows him.
```
