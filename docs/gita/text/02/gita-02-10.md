```samhita
तमुवाच हृषीकेशः प्रहसन्निव भारत ।
सेनयोरुभयोर्मध्ये विषीदन्तमिदं वचः ॥
```

```translation
Hrishikesha, as if smiling, spoke this word to him as he sat sunk in grief between the two armies, O Bharata.
```

```phrase
Hrishikesha spoke to him, as if smiling, O Bharata,

this word, as he sat sunk in grief between the two armies.
```
