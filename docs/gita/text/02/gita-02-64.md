```samhita
रागद्वेषवियुक्तैस्तु विषयानिन्द्रियैश्चरन् ।
आत्मवश्यैर्विधेयात्मा प्रसादमधिगच्छति ॥
```

```translation
But a man whose mind is at his command attains serenity, moving among objects with senses freed from attraction and aversion and held in his own control.
```

