```samhita
यदृच्छया चोपपन्नं स्वर्गद्वारमपावृतम् ।
सुखिनः क्षत्रियाः पार्थ लभन्ते युद्धमीदृशम् ॥
```

```translation
Happy are the kshatriyas, son of Pritha, who come by chance upon such a battle, an open door to heaven.
```

```phrase
And come upon by chance, an open door to heaven:
---
happy are the kshatriyas, son of Pritha, who obtain a battle such as this.
```
