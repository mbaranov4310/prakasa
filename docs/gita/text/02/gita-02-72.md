```samhita
एषा ब्राह्मी स्थितिः पार्थ नैनां प्राप्य विमुह्यति ।
स्थित्वास्यामन्तकालेऽपि ब्रह्मनिर्वाणमृच्छति ॥
```

```translation
This, son of Pṛthā, is the state that belongs to Brahman. Reaching it, one is not confused. Standing in it even at the time of death, he attains the peace of Brahman.
```

```phrase
This is the state that belongs to Brahman, son of Pṛthā. Reaching it, one is not confused.
---
Standing in it even at the time of death, he attains the peace of Brahman.
```
