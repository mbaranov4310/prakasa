```samhita
यः सर्वत्रानभिस्नेहस्तत्तत्प्राप्य शुभाशुभम् ।
नाभिनन्दति न द्वेष्टि तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता ॥
```

```translation
Who has no clinging anywhere, and who neither rejoices nor hates when he meets the pleasant or the unpleasant: his knowledge is settled.
```

```phrase
Who has no clinging anywhere, and meeting the pleasant or the unpleasant,
---
neither rejoices nor hates: his knowledge is settled.
```
