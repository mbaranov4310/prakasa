```samhita
अर्जुन उवाच
कथं भीष्ममहं संख्ये द्रोणं च मधुसूदन ।
इषुभिः प्रतियोत्स्यामि पूजार्हावरिसूदन ॥
```

```translation
Arjuna said: How shall I fight with arrows in battle against Bhishma and Drona, Madhusudana? They are worthy of worship, slayer of enemies.
```

```phrase
Arjuna said:
---
How shall I, in battle, face Bhishma and Drona, Madhusudana?
---
How shall I fight them with arrows? They are worthy of worship, slayer of enemies.
```
