```samhita
न जायते म्रियते वा कदाचि
न्नायं भूत्वा भविता वा न भूयः ।
अजो नित्यः शाश्वतोऽयं पुराणो
न हन्यते हन्यमाने शरीरे ॥
```

```translation
This one is never born and never dies. Having come to be, he will not cease; nor, not having been, will he come to be again. Unborn, eternal, constant, and ancient, he is not killed when the body is killed.
```

```phrase
He is not born, nor does he ever die.
---
This one, having come to be, will not cease to be; nor, not having been, will he come to be again.
---
Unborn, eternal, constant, and ancient,
---
he is not killed when the body is killed.
```
