```samhita
क्लैब्यं मा स्म गमः पार्थ नैतत्त्वय्युपपद्यते ।
क्षुद्रं हृदयदौर्बल्यं त्यक्त्वोत्तिष्ठ परन्तप ॥
```

```translation
Do not give way to unmanliness, son of Pritha. It does not befit you. Cast off this petty weakness of heart and stand up, scorcher of the foe.
```

```phrase
Do not give way to unmanliness, son of Pritha. It does not befit you.
```
