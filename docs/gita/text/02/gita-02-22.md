```samhita
वासांसि जीर्णानि यथा विहाय
नवानि गृह्णाति नरोऽपराणि ।
तथा शरीराणि विहाय जीर्णा
न्यन्यानि संयाति नवानि देही ॥
```

```translation
As a man casts off worn-out clothes and takes other, new ones, so the embodied one casts off worn-out bodies and enters other, new ones.
```

```phrase
As a man, casting off worn-out clothes,
---
takes other, new ones,
---
so, casting off worn-out bodies,
---
the embodied one enters other, new ones.
```
