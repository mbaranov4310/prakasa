```samhita
कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि ॥
```

```translation
Your qualification is for action alone, never for the fruits. Do not let the fruit of action be your motive, nor let your attachment be to inaction.
```

```phrase
Your qualification is for action alone, never for the fruits.
---
Do not become one whose motive is the fruit of action, nor let your attachment be to inaction.
```
