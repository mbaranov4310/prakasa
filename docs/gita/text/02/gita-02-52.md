```samhita
यदा ते मोहकलिलं बुद्धिर्व्यतितरिष्यति ।
तदा गन्तासि निर्वेदं श्रोतव्यस्य श्रुतस्य च ॥
```

```translation
When your understanding crosses beyond the mire of delusion, then you will come to distaste for what is yet to be heard and for what has been heard.
```
