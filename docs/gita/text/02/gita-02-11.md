```samhita
श्रीभगवानुवाच
अशोच्यानन्वशोचस्त्वं प्रज्ञावादांश्च भाषसे ।
गतासूनगतासूंश्च नानुशोचन्ति पण्डिताः ॥
```

```translation
The blessed Lord said: You have grieved for those who should not be grieved for, and you speak the words of wise men as well. The wise, who know the self, grieve neither for the dead nor for the living.
```

```phrase
The blessed Lord said:
---
You have grieved for those who should not be grieved for, and you also speak words of the wise.
---
The wise grieve neither for the dead nor for the living.
```
