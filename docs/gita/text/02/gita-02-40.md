```samhita
नेहाभिक्रमनाशोऽस्ति प्रत्यवायो न विद्यते ।
स्वल्पमप्यस्य धर्मस्य त्रायते महतो भयात् ॥
```

```translation
On this path there is no loss of what is begun, and no contrary result follows. Even a little of this dharma protects one from great fear.
```

```phrase
On this path there is no loss of what is begun, and no contrary result is found.
---
Even a little of this dharma protects one from great fear.
```
