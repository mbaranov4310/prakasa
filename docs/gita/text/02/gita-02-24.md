```samhita
अच्छेद्योऽयमदाह्योऽयमक्लेद्योऽशोष्य एव च ।
नित्यः सर्वगतः स्थाणुरचलोऽयं सनातनः ॥
```

```translation
He cannot be cut or burned, wetted or dried. He is eternal, present everywhere, firm, unmoving, and from of old.
```

```phrase
He cannot be cut, he cannot be burned, he cannot be wetted, and he cannot be dried.
---
Eternal, present everywhere, firm, unmoving, he is from of old.
```
