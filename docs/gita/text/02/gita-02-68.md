```samhita
तस्माद्यस्य महाबाहो निगृहीतानि सर्वशः ।
इन्द्रियाणीन्द्रियार्थेभ्यस्तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता ॥
```

```translation
Therefore, mighty-armed one, his knowledge is settled whose senses are held in on every side from the objects of the senses.
```

```phrase
Therefore, mighty-armed one, whose senses are held in on every side
---
from the objects of the senses, his knowledge is settled.
```
