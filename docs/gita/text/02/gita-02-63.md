```samhita
क्रोधाद्भवति संमोहः संमोहात्स्मृतिविभ्रमः ।
स्मृतिभ्रंशाद्बुद्धिनाशो बुद्धिनाशात्प्रणश्यति ॥
```

```translation
From anger comes delusion, and from delusion the failure of memory. From the loss of memory comes the ruin of judgment, and from the ruin of judgment he is lost.
```

```phrase
From anger comes delusion, and from delusion the failure of memory.
---
From the loss of memory comes the ruin of judgment, and from that ruin he is lost.
```
