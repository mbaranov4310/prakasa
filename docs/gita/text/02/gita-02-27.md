```samhita
जातस्य हि ध्रुवो मृत्युर्ध्रुवं जन्म मृतस्य च ।
तस्मादपरिहार्येऽर्थे न त्वं शोचितुमर्हसि ॥
```

```translation
Death is certain for one who has been born, and birth is certain for one who has died. You should not grieve over a thing that cannot be turned aside.
```

```phrase
For one who is born, death is certain, and for one who is dead, birth is certain.
---
Therefore you should not grieve over what cannot be turned aside.
```
