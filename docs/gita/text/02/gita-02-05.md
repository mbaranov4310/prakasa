```samhita
गुरूनहत्वा हि महानुभावान्
श्रेयो भोक्तुं भैक्षमपीह लोके ।
हत्वार्थकामांस्तु गुरूनिहैव
भुञ्जीय भोगान्रुधिरप्रदिग्धान् ॥
```

```translation
It is better, here in this world, to eat even food got by begging than to kill these teachers, men of great character. If I killed them, teachers whose desire is gain, I would only enjoy pleasures smeared with blood.
```

```phrase
Than to kill these teachers, men of great character,
---
it is better to eat even food got by begging, here in this world.
---
But if I killed these teachers, whose desire is gain, here itself,
---
I would enjoy pleasures smeared with blood.
```
