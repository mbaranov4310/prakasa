```samhita
दूरेण ह्यवरं कर्म बुद्धियोगाद्धनंजय ।
बुद्धौ शरणमन्विच्छ कृपणाः फलहेतवः ॥
```

```translation
Action done for its fruit is far inferior to the yoga of understanding, Dhanaṃjaya. Seek refuge in understanding. Wretched are they whose motive is the fruit.
```

