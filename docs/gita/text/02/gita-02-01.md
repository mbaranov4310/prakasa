```samhita
संजय उवाच
तं तथा कृपयाविष्टमश्रुपूर्णाकुलेक्षणम् ।
विषीदन्तमिदं वाक्यमुवाच मधुसूदनः ॥
```

```translation
Sanjaya said: Madhusudana spoke this word to him as he sat thus, overcome by pity, his eyes filled with tears and troubled, sunk in grief.
```

