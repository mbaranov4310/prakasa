```samhita
न हि प्रपश्यामि ममापनुद्या
यच्छोकमुच्छोषणमिन्द्रियाणाम् ।
अवाप्य भूमावसपत्नमृद्धं
राज्यं सुराणामपि चाधिपत्यम् ॥
```

```translation
I do not see what would drive away this grief of mine that dries up the senses, even if I won a prosperous kingdom on earth with no rival, or lordship over the gods.
```

```phrase
I do not see what would drive away my
---
grief, which dries up the senses,
---
even having won on earth a prosperous kingdom with no rival,
---
or lordship even over the gods.
```
