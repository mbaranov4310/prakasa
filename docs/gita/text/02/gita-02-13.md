```samhita
देहिनोऽस्मिन्यथा देहे कौमारं यौवनं जरा ।
तथा देहान्तरप्राप्तिर्धीरस्तत्र न मुह्यति ॥
```

```translation
As childhood, youth, and old age come to the embodied one in this body, so does the passing into another body. The wise man is not bewildered at that.
```

