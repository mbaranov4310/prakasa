```samhita
भयाद्रणादुपरतं मंस्यन्ते त्वां महारथाः ।
येषां च त्वं बहुमतो भूत्वा यास्यसि लाघवम् ॥
```

```translation
The great warriors will think you withdrew from the battle out of fear. And among those who held you in high regard, you will come to be lightly thought of.
```

```phrase
The great warriors will think you withdrew from the battle out of fear.
---
And having been held in high regard by them, you will come to be lightly thought of.
```
