```samhita
अथ चैनं नित्यजातं नित्यं वा मन्यसे मृतम् ।
तथापि त्वं महाबाहो नैवं शोचितुमर्हसि ॥
```

```translation
Or suppose you think of him as born again and again, or as dead again and again. Even then, mighty-armed one, you should not grieve in this way.
```

```phrase
Or if you think of him as constantly born, or as constantly dead,
---
even so, mighty-armed one, you should not grieve in this way.
```
