```samhita
कार्पण्यदोषोपहतस्वभावः
पृच्छामि त्वां धर्मसंमूढचेताः ।
यच्छ्रेयः स्यान्निश्चितं ब्रूहि तन्मे
शिष्यस्तेऽहं शाधि मां त्वां प्रपन्नम् ॥
```

```translation
My nature is overcome by the fault of a weak spirit, and my mind is confused about what is right. I ask you: tell me for certain what is better. I am your student. Teach me, for I have come to you.
```

```phrase
My nature overcome by the fault of a weak spirit,
---
my mind confused about what is right, I ask you.
---
Tell me for certain what would be better. Tell that to me.
---
I am your student. Teach me, who have come to you for refuge.
```
