```samhita
कर्मजं बुद्धियुक्ता हि फलं त्यक्त्वा मनीषिणः ।
जन्मबन्धविनिर्मुक्ताः पदं गच्छन्त्यनामयम् ॥
```

```translation
Those endowed with understanding cast off the fruit born of action and become wise. Released from the bond of birth, they go to the state where there is no sorrow.
```

```phrase
For those endowed with understanding, having cast off the fruit born of action, become wise.
---
Released from the bond of birth, they go to the state where there is no sorrow.
```
