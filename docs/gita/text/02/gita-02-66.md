```samhita
नास्ति बुद्धिरयुक्तस्य न चायुक्तस्य भावना ।
न चाभावयतः शान्तिरशान्तस्य कुतः सुखम् ॥
```

```translation
The uncollected man has no knowledge, and no dwelling on that knowledge. One who does not dwell on it has no peace, and for one without peace, from where is happiness?
```

```phrase
There is no knowledge for the uncollected, and no dwelling on it for the uncollected.
---
For one who does not dwell on it there is no peace, and for one without peace, from where is happiness?
```
