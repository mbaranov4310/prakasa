```samhita
श्रीभगवानुवाच
कुतस्त्वा कश्मलमिदं विषमे समुपस्थितम् ।
अनार्यजुष्टमस्वर्ग्यमकीर्तिकरमर्जुन ॥
```

```translation
The blessed Lord said: From where has this faintness come upon you in a crisis, Arjuna? It is not the way of men who know what is right. It does not lead to heaven, and it brings disgrace.
```

