```samhita
यामिमां पुष्पितां वाचं प्रवदन्त्यविपश्चितः ।
वेदवादरताः पार्थ नान्यदस्तीति वादिनः ॥
```

```translation
The undiscerning, son of Pṛthā, who delight in the words of the Veda and declare that there is nothing else, utter this flowery speech,
```

```phrase
They utter this flowery speech, the undiscerning,
---
who delight in the words of the Veda and say there is nothing else, son of Pṛthā,
```
