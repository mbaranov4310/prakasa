```samhita
यदा संहरते चायं कूर्मोऽङ्गानीव सर्वशः ।
इन्द्रियाणीन्द्रियार्थेभ्यस्तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता ॥
```

```translation
When he draws the senses in from their objects on every side, as a tortoise draws in its limbs, his knowledge is settled.
```

```phrase
And when he draws them in altogether, as a tortoise its limbs,
---
the senses from the objects of the senses, his knowledge is settled.
```
