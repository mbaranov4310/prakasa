```samhita
ध्यायतो विषयान्पुंसः सङ्गस्तेषूपजायते ।
सङ्गात्संजायते कामः कामात्क्रोधोऽभिजायते ॥
```

```translation
In a man who dwells on objects, attachment to them arises. From attachment desire is born, and from desire, when it is checked, anger arises.
```

```phrase
In a man who dwells on objects, attachment to them arises.
---
From attachment desire is born, and from desire anger arises.
```
