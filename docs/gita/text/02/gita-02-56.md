```samhita
दुःखेष्वनुद्विग्नमनाः सुखेषु विगतस्पृहः ।
वीतरागभयक्रोधः स्थितधीर्मुनिरुच्यते ॥
```

```translation
He whose mind is unshaken in pain, whose longing has left him in pleasure, and from whom attraction, fear, and anger have gone, is called a sage of steady mind.
```

```phrase
Whose mind is unshaken in pains, and whose longing has gone in pleasures,
---
from whom attraction, fear, and anger have gone: he is called a sage of steady mind.
```
