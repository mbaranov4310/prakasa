```samhita
अव्यक्तोऽयमचिन्त्योऽयमविकार्योऽयमुच्यते ।
तस्मादेवं विदित्वैनं नानुशोचितुमर्हसि ॥
```

```translation
He is called unmanifest, unthinkable, and not subject to change. Therefore, having known him in this way, you should not grieve.
```

```phrase
He is called unmanifest, unthinkable, and not subject to change.
---
Therefore, having known him thus, you should not grieve.
```
