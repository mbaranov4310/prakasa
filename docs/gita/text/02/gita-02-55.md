```samhita
श्रीभगवानुवाच
प्रजहाति यदा कामान्सर्वान्पार्थ मनोगतान् ।
आत्मन्येवात्मना तुष्टः स्थितप्रज्ञस्तदोच्यते ॥
```

```translation
The blessed Lord said: When he casts off all desires that have entered the mind, son of Pṛthā, and is content in the Self alone by himself, then he is called a man of steady knowledge.
```

```phrase
The blessed Lord said:

content in the Self alone by himself, then he is called a man of steady knowledge.
```
