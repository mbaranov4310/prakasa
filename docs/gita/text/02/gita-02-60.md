```samhita
यततो ह्यपि कौन्तेय पुरुषस्य विपश्चितः ।
इन्द्रियाणि प्रमाथीनि हरन्ति प्रसभं मनः ॥
```

```translation
For even in a man who is striving, son of Kuntī, and who discerns, the turbulent senses carry the mind off by force.
```

```phrase
For even of a striving man, son of Kuntī, a man who discerns,
---
the turbulent senses carry the mind off by force.
```
