```samhita
अन्तवन्त इमे देहा नित्यस्योक्ताः शरीरिणः ।
अनाशिनोऽप्रमेयस्य तस्माद्युध्यस्व भारत ॥
```

```translation
These bodies of the eternal, indestructible, immeasurable embodied one are said to come to an end. Therefore fight, Bharata.
```

```phrase
These bodies of the eternal embodied one are said to come to an end.
---
Therefore fight, Bharata, for he is indestructible and cannot be measured.
```
