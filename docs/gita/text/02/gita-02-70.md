```samhita
आपूर्यमाणमचलप्रतिष्ठं
समुद्रमापः प्रविशन्ति यद्वत् ।
तद्वत्कामा यं प्रविशन्ति सर्वे
स शान्तिमाप्नोति न कामकामी ॥
```

```translation
As the waters enter the ocean, filled and yet standing unmoved, so the man whom all desires enter attains peace, and not the man who desires objects.
```

```phrase
Filled, and standing unmoved,
---
as the waters enter the ocean,
---
so all desires enter the man,
---
he attains peace, not the man who desires objects.
```
