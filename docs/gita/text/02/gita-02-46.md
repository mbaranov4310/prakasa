```samhita
यावानर्थ उदपाने सर्वतःसंप्लुतोदके ।
तावान् सर्वेषु वेदेषु ब्राह्मणस्य विजानतः ॥
```

```translation
As much use as a reservoir has when water is flooding on every side, that much use have all the Vedas for the brāhmaṇa who knows.
```

```phrase
As much use as a reservoir has when water floods on every side,

that much use have all the Vedas for the brāhmaṇa who knows.
```
