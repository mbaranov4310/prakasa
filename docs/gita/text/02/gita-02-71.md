```samhita
विहाय कामान्यः सर्वान्पुमांश्चरति निःस्पृहः ।
निर्ममो निरहंकारः स शान्तिमधिगच्छति ॥
```

```translation
The man who leaves every desire and moves about without longing, without the thought "mine" and without the thought "I", attains peace.
```

```phrase
The man who, leaving all desires, moves about without longing,
---
without "mine" and without "I", he attains peace.
```
