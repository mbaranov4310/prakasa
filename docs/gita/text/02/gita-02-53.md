```samhita
श्रुतिविप्रतिपन्ना ते यदा स्थास्यति निश्चला ।
समाधावचला बुद्धिस्तदा योगमवाप्स्यसि ॥
```

```translation
When your understanding, distracted by what you have heard, stands unmoving and unwavering in the Self, then you will reach yoga.
```

```phrase
When your understanding, pulled this way and that by what is heard, stands unmoving,
---
unwavering in the Self, then you will reach yoga.
```
