```samhita
सुखदुःखे समे कृत्वा लाभालाभौ जयाजयौ ।
ततो युद्धाय युज्यस्व नैवं पापमवाप्स्यसि ॥
```

```translation
Having made pleasure and pain, gain and loss, victory and defeat the same, set yourself to battle. Fighting in this way, you will not incur evil.
```

```phrase
Having made pleasure and pain, gain and loss, victory and defeat the same,
---
then set yourself to battle: fighting thus, you will not incur evil.
```
