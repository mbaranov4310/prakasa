```samhita
संजय उवाच
एवमुक्त्वा हृषीकेशं गुडाकेशः परंतपः ।
न योत्स्य इति गोविन्दमुक्त्वा तूष्णीं बभूव ह ॥
```

```translation
Sanjaya said: Having spoken thus to Hrishikesha, Gudakesha, scorcher of the foe, said to Govinda, "I will not fight," and fell silent.
```
