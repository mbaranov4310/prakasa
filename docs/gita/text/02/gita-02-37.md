```samhita
हतो वा प्राप्स्यसि स्वर्गं
जित्वा वा भोक्ष्यसे महीम् ।
तस्मादुत्तिष्ठ कौन्तेय
युद्धाय कृतनिश्चयः ॥
```

```translation
Slain, you will reach heaven; conquering, you will enjoy the earth. Therefore stand up, son of Kuntī, resolved for battle.
```

```phrase
Slain, you will reach heaven,
---
or, conquering, you will enjoy the earth.
---
Therefore stand up, son of Kuntī,
---
resolved for battle.
```
