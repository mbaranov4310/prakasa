```samhita
नासतो विद्यते भावो नाभावो विद्यते सतः ।
उभयोरपि दृष्टोऽन्तस्त्वनयोस्तत्त्वदर्शिभिः ॥
```

```translation
The unreal has no being, and the real does not cease to be. Those who see the truth have seen the certainty of both.
```

```phrase
The unreal has no being, and the real does not cease to be.
---
Those who see the truth have seen the certainty of both.
```
