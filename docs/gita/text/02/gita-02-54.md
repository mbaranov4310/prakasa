```samhita
अर्जुन उवाच
स्थितप्रज्ञस्य का भाषा समाधिस्थस्य केशव ।
स्थितधीः किं पृभाषेत किमासीत व्रजेत किम् ॥
```

```translation
Arjuna said: What is the description of the man of steady knowledge, established in samādhi, Keśava? What would the man of steady mind say, how would he sit, how would he move?
```

```phrase
Arjuna said:
---
What is the description of the man of steady knowledge, established in samādhi, Keśava?
---
What would the man of steady mind say, how would he sit, how would he move?
```
