```samhita
अकीर्तिं चापि भूतानि कथयिष्यन्ति तेऽव्ययाम् ।
संभावितस्य चाकीर्तिर्मरणादतिरिच्यते ॥
```

```translation
People will also tell of a lasting disgrace of yours, and for a man held in honor, disgrace is worse than death.
```

```phrase
And people will tell of your lasting disgrace.
---
For one who is held in honor, disgrace is worse than death.
```
