```samhita
अव्यक्तादीनि भूतानि व्यक्तमध्यानि भारत ।
अव्यक्तनिधनान्येव तत्र का परिदेवना ॥
```

```translation
Beings are unmanifest at the beginning, manifest in the middle, and unmanifest again at the end, Bharata. What lament is there in that?
```

```phrase
Beings are unmanifest at the beginning and manifest in the middle, Bharata.
---
They are unmanifest at the end as well. What lament is there in that?
```
