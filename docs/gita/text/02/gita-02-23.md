```samhita
नैनं छिन्दन्ति शस्त्राणि नैनं दहति पावकः ।
न चैनं क्लेदयन्त्यापो न शोषयति मारुतः ॥
```

```translation
Weapons do not cut him, fire does not burn him, water does not wet him, and the wind does not dry him.
```

```phrase
Weapons do not cut him, fire does not burn him,
---
water does not wet him, and the wind does not dry him.
```
