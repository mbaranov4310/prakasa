```samhita
विषया विनिवर्तन्ते निराहारस्य देहिनः ।
रसवर्जं रसोऽप्यस्य परं दृष्ट्वा निवर्तते ॥
```

```translation
Objects turn back from the embodied man who does not take them in, but the taste remains. Even the taste turns back when he has seen the highest.
```

```phrase
The objects turn back from the embodied man who does not feed on them, but not the taste.
---
Even his taste turns back when he has seen the highest.
```
