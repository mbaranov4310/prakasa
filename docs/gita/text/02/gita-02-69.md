```samhita
या निशा सर्वभूतानां
तस्यां जागर्ति संयमी ।
यस्यां जाग्रति भूतानि
सा निशा पश्यतो मुनेः ॥
```

```translation
What is night for all beings, in that the man of restraint is awake. Where beings are awake, that is night for the sage who sees.
```

```phrase
What is night for all beings,
---
in that the man of restraint is awake.
---
Where beings are awake,
---
that is night for the sage who sees.
```
