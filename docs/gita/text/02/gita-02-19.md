```samhita
य एनं वेत्ति हन्तारं यश्चैनं मन्यते हतम् ।
उभौ तौ न विजानीतो नायं हन्ति न हन्यते ॥
```

```translation
He who knows this one as the killer, and he who thinks this one is killed, neither of them understands. This one does not kill, and is not killed.
```

