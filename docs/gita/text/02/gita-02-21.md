```samhita
वेदाविनाशिनं नित्यं य एनमजमव्ययम् ।
कथं स पुरुषः पार्थ कं घातयति हन्ति कम् ॥
```

```translation
He who knows this one as indestructible, eternal, unborn, and unchanging: how, son of Pritha, does that man kill anyone, or cause anyone to be killed?
```

