```samhita
न त्वेवाहं जातु नासं न त्वं नेमे जनाधिपाः ।
न चैव न भविष्यामः सर्वे वयमतः परम् ॥
```

```translation
It is not that I ever failed to exist, nor you, nor these lords of men. Nor shall all of us cease to be hereafter. Through all three times we remain, as the self.
```

```phrase
It is not that I ever failed to exist, nor you, nor these lords of men.
---
Nor is it that all of us shall cease to be hereafter.
```
