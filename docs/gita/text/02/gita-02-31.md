```samhita
स्वधर्ममपि चावेक्ष्य न विकम्पितुमर्हसि ।
धर्म्याद्धि युद्धाच्छ्रेयोऽन्यत्क्षत्रियस्य न विद्यते ॥
```

```translation
Looking to your own duty as well, you should not waver. For a kshatriya there is nothing better than a lawful battle.
```

```phrase
Looking to your own duty as well, you should not waver.
---
For a kshatriya there is indeed nothing better than a lawful battle.
```
