```samhita
यं हि न व्यथयन्त्येते पुरुषं पुरुषर्षभ ।
समदुःखसुखं धीरं सोऽमृतत्वाय कल्पते ॥
```

```translation
The man whom these do not shake, O best of men, the wise man who is steady in pain and pleasure, is fit for release.
```
