```samhita
मात्रास्पर्शास्तु कौन्तेय शीतोष्णसुखदुःखदाः ।
आगमापायिनोऽनित्यास्तांस्तितिक्षस्व भारत ॥
```

```translation
Contacts of the senses, son of Kunti, give cold and heat, pleasure and pain. They come and go, so they do not last. Endure them, Bharata, and do not be lifted up or cast down.
```

```phrase
But the contacts of the senses, son of Kunti, give cold and heat, pleasure and pain.
---
They come and go, and so do not last. Endure them, Bharata.
```
