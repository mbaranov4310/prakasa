```samhita
त्रैगुण्यविषया वेदा निस्त्रैगुण्यो भवार्जुन ।
निर्द्वन्द्वो नित्यसत्त्वस्थो निर्योगक्षेम आत्मवान् ॥
```

```translation
The Vedas have the three guṇas as their subject. Be free of the three guṇas, Arjuna: free of the pairs, ever established in sattva, free of getting and keeping, and self-possessed.
```

```phrase

---
free of the pairs, ever established in sattva, free of getting and keeping, self-possessed.
```
