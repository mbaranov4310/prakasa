```samhita
व्यवसायात्मिका बुद्धिरेकेह कुरुनन्दन ।
बहुशाखा ह्यनन्ताश्च बुद्धयोऽव्यवसायिनाम् ॥
```

```translation
Here, joy of the Kurus, the resolute understanding is one. Many-branched and endless are the thoughts of those who lack resolve.
```

```phrase
Here, joy of the Kurus, the understanding whose nature is resolve is one.
---
Many-branched and endless are the thoughts of those without resolve.
```
