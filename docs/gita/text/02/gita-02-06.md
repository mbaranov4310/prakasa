```samhita
न चैतद्विद्मः कतरन्नो गरीयो
यद्वा जयेम यदि वा नो जयेयुः ।
यानेव हत्वा न जिजीविषाम्
स्तेऽवस्थिताः प्रमुखे धार्तराष्ट्राः ॥
```

```translation
We do not know which is the weightier for us: that we should conquer, or that they should conquer us. The sons of Dhritarashtra stand before us, and having killed them we would not wish to live.
```

```phrase
And we do not know which of the two is the weightier for us:
---
whether we should conquer, or they should conquer us.
---
Having killed these very men, we would not wish to live.
---
They stand before us, the sons of Dhritarashtra.
```
