```samhita
किं पुनर्ब्राह्मणाः पुण्या भक्ता राजर्षयस्तथा ।
अनित्यमसुखं लोकमिमं प्राप्य भजस्व माम् ॥
```

```translation
How much more, then, brāhmaṇas of meritorious birth, devotees, and royal seers. Having reached this impermanent, joyless world, worship me.
```

