```samhita
राजविद्या राजगुह्यं पवित्रमिदमुत्तमम् ।
प्रत्यक्षावगमं धर्म्यं सुसुखं कर्तुमव्ययम् ॥
```

```translation
It is the king of knowledges and the king of secrets, this highest purifier, grasped directly, righteous, very easy to practice, and imperishable.
```

```phrase
The king of knowledges, the king of secrets, this highest purifier,
---
grasped directly, righteous, very easy to practice, and imperishable.
```
