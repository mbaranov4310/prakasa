```samhita
श्रीभगवानुवाच
इदं तु ते गुह्यतमं प्रवक्ष्याम्यनसूयवे ।
ज्ञानं विज्ञानसहितं यज्ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसेऽशुभात् ॥
```

```translation
The blessed Lord said: I shall declare to you, who are free of spite, this most secret knowledge joined with realization. Knowing it, you will be released from what is impure.
```

```phrase
The blessed Lord said:
---
I shall declare to you, who are free of spite, this most secret thing,
---
the knowledge joined with realization; knowing it, you will be released from what is impure.
```
