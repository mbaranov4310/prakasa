```samhita
अहं क्रतुरहं यज्ञः स्वधाहमहमौषधम् ।
मन्त्रोऽहमहमेवाज्यमहमग्निरहं हुतम् ॥
```

```translation
I am the Vedic rite, I am the sacrifice, I am the offering to the ancestors, and I am the food all beings eat. I am the mantra, I alone am the offering of ghee, I am the fire, and I am the act of offering.
```

```phrase
I am the Vedic rite and I am the sacrifice; I am the offering to the ancestors, and I am the food.
---
I am the mantra; I alone am the ghee; I am the fire, and I am the offering.
```
