```samhita
पत्रं पुष्पं फलं तोयं यो मे भक्त्या प्रयच्छति ।
तदहं भक्त्युपहृतमश्नामि प्रयतात्मनः ॥
```

```translation
Whoever offers me a leaf, a flower, a fruit, or water with devotion, I accept that, brought with devotion, from the one whose mind is pure.
```

```phrase
Whoever offers me a leaf, a flower, a fruit, or water with devotion,
---
that, brought with devotion, I accept from the one whose mind is pure.
```
