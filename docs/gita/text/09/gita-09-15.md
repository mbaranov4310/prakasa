```samhita
ज्ञानयज्ञेन चाप्यन्ये यजन्तो मामुपासते ।
एकत्वेन पृथक्त्वेन बहुधा विश्वतोमुखम् ॥
```

```translation
Others, too, worship me by the sacrifice that is knowledge: as the one, as the many distinct forms, and in many ways as the all-faced.
```

```phrase
Others, too, honouring me, worship me by the sacrifice that is knowledge,
---
as one, as the distinct, and in many ways as the all-faced.
```
