```samhita
ते तं भुक्त्वा स्वर्गलोकं विशालं
क्षीणे पुण्ये मर्त्यलोकं विशन्ति ।
एवं त्रयीधर्ममनुप्रपन्ना
गतागतं कामकामा लभन्ते ॥
```

```translation
Having enjoyed that vast heaven, they enter the world of mortals when their merit is exhausted. Thus those who follow the rite of the three Vedas, desiring desires, get only the going and the coming.
```

```phrase
They, having enjoyed that vast world of heaven,
---
enter the world of mortals when their merit is exhausted.
---
Thus, having followed the rite of the three Vedas,
---
those who desire desires get the going and the coming.
```
