```samhita
मां हि पार्थ व्यपाश्रित्य येऽपि स्युः पापयोनयः ।
स्त्रियो वैश्यास्तथा शूद्रास्तेऽपि यान्ति परां गतिम् ॥
```

```translation
For even those of low birth who take refuge in me, O Pārtha — women, vaiśyas, and śūdras — they too go to the highest goal.
```

```phrase
For even those of low birth who take refuge in me, O Pārtha,
---
women, vaiśyas, and śūdras, they too go to the highest goal.
```
