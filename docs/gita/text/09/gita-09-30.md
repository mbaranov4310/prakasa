```samhita
अपि चेत्सुदुराचारो भजते मामनन्यभाक् ।
साधुरेव स मन्तव्यः सम्यग्व्यवसितो हि सः ॥
```

```translation
Even if a man of very vile conduct worships me with no other devotion, he is to be held a man of right conduct, for his resolve is set on the good.
```

```phrase
Even if a man of very vile conduct worships me, devoted to no other,
---
he is to be held a man of right conduct indeed, for he is rightly resolved.
```
