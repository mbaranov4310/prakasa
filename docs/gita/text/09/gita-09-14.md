```samhita
सततं कीर्तयन्तो मां यतन्तश्च दृढव्रताः ।
नमस्यन्तश्च मां भक्त्या नित्ययुक्ता उपासते ॥
```

```translation
Always praising me, striving, and firm in their vow, and bowing to me with devotion, those who are constantly engaged worship me.
```

```phrase
Always praising me, and striving, firm in their vow,
---
and bowing to me with devotion, the constantly engaged worship me.
```
