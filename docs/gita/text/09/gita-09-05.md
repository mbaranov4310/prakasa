```samhita
न च मत्स्थानि भूतानि पश्य मे योगमैश्वरम् ।
भूतभृन्न च भूतस्थो ममात्मा भूतभावनः ॥
```

```translation
Nor do beings abide in me. See my divine yoga. Supporting beings, and not standing in them, my self brings beings forth.
```

```phrase
Nor do beings abide in me. See my divine yoga.

Supporting beings, and not standing in them, my self brings beings forth.
```
