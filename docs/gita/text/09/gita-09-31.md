```samhita
क्षिप्रं भवति धर्मात्मा शश्वच्छान्तिं निगच्छति ।
कौन्तेय प्रतिजानीहि न मे भक्तः प्रणश्यति ॥
```

```translation
He quickly becomes one whose mind is dharma, and he reaches lasting peace. Promise this, O son of Kuntī: my devotee does not perish.
```

```phrase
Quickly he becomes one whose mind is dharma, and he reaches lasting peace.
---
Promise this, O son of Kuntī: my devotee does not perish.
```
