```samhita
प्रकृतिं स्वामवष्टभ्य विसृजामि पुनः पुनः ।
भूतग्राममिमं कृत्स्नमवशं प्रकृतेर्वशात् ॥
```

```translation
Taking hold of my own nature, I send forth again and again this whole host of beings, which is not its own master, under the sway of nature.
```

