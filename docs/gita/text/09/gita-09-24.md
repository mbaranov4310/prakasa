```samhita
अहं हि सर्वयज्ञानां भोक्ता च प्रभुरेव च ।
न तु मामभिजानन्ति तत्त्वेनातश्च्यवन्ति ते ॥
```

```translation
For I am the enjoyer of every sacrifice, and its lord. But they do not know me as I really am, and so they fall from the fruit of the sacrifice.
```

```phrase
For I am the enjoyer of all sacrifices, and their lord.
---
But they do not know me as I really am, and so they fall from the fruit.
```
