```samhita
तपाम्यहमहं वर्षं निगृह्णाम्युत्सृजामि च ।
अमृतं चैव मृत्युश्च सदसच्चाहमर्जुन ॥
```

```translation
I heat, and I hold the rain back and release it. I am the deathless, and death as well, and I am what is and what is not, O Arjuna.
```

