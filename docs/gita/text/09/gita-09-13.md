```samhita
महात्मानस्तु मां पार्थ दैवीं प्रकृतिमाश्रिताः ।
भजन्त्यनन्यमनसो ज्ञात्वा भूतादिमव्ययम् ॥
```

```translation
But the great-souled, O Pārtha, having taken to a divine nature, worship me with their minds on no other, knowing me as the imperishable source of beings.
```

