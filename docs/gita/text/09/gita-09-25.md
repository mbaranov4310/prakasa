```samhita
यान्ति देवव्रता देवान्पितॄन्यान्ति पितृव्रताः ।
भूतानि यान्ति भूतेज्या यान्ति मद्याजिनोऽपि माम् ॥
```

```translation
Those whose vow is the gods go to the gods, and those whose vow is the fathers go to the fathers. Worshippers of the spirits go to the spirits, and those who worship me come to me.
```

```phrase
Those whose vow is the gods go to the gods; those whose vow is the fathers go to the fathers.
---
Worshippers of the spirits go to the spirits, and those who worship me come to me as well.
```
