```samhita
मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु ।
मामेवैष्यसि युक्त्वैवमात्मानं मत्परायणः ॥
```

```translation
Be one whose mind is on me, my devotee, one who worships me, and bow to me. Having yoked the self thus, and holding me as your highest resort, you will come to me alone.
```

```phrase
Be one whose mind is on me, my devotee, one who worships me; bow to me.
---
You will come to me alone, having yoked the self thus, holding me as your highest resort.
```
