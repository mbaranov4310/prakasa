```samhita
अनन्याश्चिन्तयन्तो मां ये जनाः पर्युपासते ।
तेषां नित्याभियुक्तानां योगक्षेमं वहाम्यहम् ॥
```

```translation
Those men who are not other think of me and worship me wholly. For them, constantly engaged, I myself carry what they have yet to gain and the keeping of it.
```

```phrase
Those men who are not other think of me and worship me wholly,
---
for them, the constantly engaged, I carry the gaining and the keeping.
```
