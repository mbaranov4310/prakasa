```samhita
गतिर्भर्ता प्रभुः साक्षी निवासः शरणं सुहृत् ।
प्रभवः प्रलयः स्थानं निधानं बीजमव्ययम् ॥
```

```translation
I am the fruit of action, the nourisher, the master, the witness, the dwelling, the refuge, and the friend. I am the arising, the dissolution, the abiding, the store, and the imperishable seed.
```

```phrase
The fruit of action, the nourisher, the master, the witness, the dwelling, the refuge, and the friend.
---
The arising, the dissolution, the abiding, the store, and the imperishable seed.
```
