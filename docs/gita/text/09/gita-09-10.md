```samhita
मयाध्यक्षेण प्रकृतिः सूयते सचराचरम् ।
हेतुनानेन कौन्तेय जगद्विपरिवर्तते ॥
```

```translation
With me as its witness, nature brings forth what moves and what is still. By this cause, O son of Kuntī, the world turns through its changes.
```
