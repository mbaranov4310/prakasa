```samhita
अवजानन्ति मां मूढा मानुषीं तनुमाश्रितम् ।
परं भावमजानन्तो मम भूतमहेश्वरम् ॥
```

```translation
The deluded scorn me when I have taken a human body. They do not know my supreme state, the great Lord of beings.
```

```phrase
The deluded scorn me, who have taken a human body.
---
They do not know my supreme state, the great Lord of beings.
```
