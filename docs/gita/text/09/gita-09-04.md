```samhita
मया ततमिदं सर्वं जगदव्यक्तमूर्तिना ।
मत्स्थानि सर्वभूतानि न चाहं तेष्ववस्थितः ॥
```

```translation
By me, whose form is unmanifest, this whole world is pervaded. All beings abide in me, and I am not situated in them.
```

```phrase

All beings abide in me, and I am not situated in them.
```
