```samhita
यथाकाशस्थितो नित्यं वायुः सर्वत्रगो महान् ।
तथा सर्वाणि भूतानि मत्स्थानीत्युपधारय ॥
```

```translation
As the vast wind, going everywhere, always stands in the sky, so understand that all beings abide in me.
```

```phrase

so understand that all beings abide in me.
```
