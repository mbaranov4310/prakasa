```samhita
शुभाशुभफलैरेवं मोक्ष्यसे कर्मबन्धनैः ।
संन्यासयोगयुक्तात्मा विमुक्तो मामुपैष्यसि ॥
```

```translation
Offering them in this way, you will be freed from actions whose fruits are wanted and unwanted, the very bonds. With your mind yoked to that yoga of renunciation, and freed, you will come to me.
```

```phrase
Thus you will be freed from actions whose fruits are wanted and unwanted, the bonds themselves.
---
With your mind yoked to the yoga of renunciation, freed, you will come to me.
```
