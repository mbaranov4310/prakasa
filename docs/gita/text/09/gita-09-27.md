```samhita
यत्करोषि यदश्नासि यज्जुहोषि ददासि यत् ।
यत्तपस्यसि कौन्तेय तत्कुरुष्व मदर्पणम् ॥
```

```translation
Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer, whatever you give, and whatever austerity you practice, O son of Kuntī, make that an offering to me.
```

```phrase
Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer, whatever you give,
---
whatever austerity you practice, O son of Kuntī, make that an offering to me.
```
