```samhita
न च मां तानि कर्माणि निबध्नन्ति धनंजय ।
उदासीनवदासीनमसक्तं तेषु कर्मसु ॥
```

```translation
And those actions do not bind me, O winner of wealth, as I sit like one indifferent, unattached among those actions.
```

