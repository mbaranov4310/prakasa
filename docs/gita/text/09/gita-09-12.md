```samhita
मोघाशा मोघकर्माणो मोघज्ञाना विचेतसः ।
राक्षसीमासुरीं चैव प्रकृतिं मोहिनीं श्रिताः ॥
```

```translation
Their hopes are vain, their works are vain, their knowledge is vain, and their judgment is gone. They have taken to a deluding nature, the nature of rākṣasas and of asuras.
```

```phrase
Those whose hopes, works, and knowledge are vain, and whose judgment is gone,
---
have taken to a deluding nature, the nature of rākṣasas and of asuras.
```
