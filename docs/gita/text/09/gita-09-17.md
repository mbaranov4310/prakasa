```samhita
पिताहमस्य जगतो माता धाता पितामहः ।
वेद्यं पवित्रमोंकार ऋक्साम यजुरेव च ॥
```

```translation
I am the father of this world, the mother, the ordainer, and the grandfather. I am what is to be known, the purifier, the syllable oṃ, and the Ṛg, the Sāman, and the Yajus.
```

