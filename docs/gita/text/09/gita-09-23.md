```samhita
येऽप्यन्यदेवताभक्ता यजन्ते श्रद्धयान्विताः ।
तेऽपि मामेव कौन्तेय यजन्त्यविधिपूर्वकम् ॥
```

```translation
Even those who are devoted to other gods and worship them with faith worship me alone, O son of Kuntī, though ignorance goes before that worship.
```

```phrase
Even those devoted to other gods worship them, attended by faith.
---
They too worship me alone, O son of Kuntī, with ignorance going before.
```
