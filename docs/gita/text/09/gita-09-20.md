```samhita
त्रैविद्या मां सोमपाः पूतपापा
यज्ञैरिष्ट्वा स्वर्गतिं प्रार्थयन्ते ।
ते पुण्यमासाद्य सुरेन्द्रलोक
मश्नन्ति दिव्यान्दिवि देवभोगान् ॥
```

```translation
Knowers of the three Vedas, the soma-drinkers, whose sins are cleansed, worship me with sacrifices and pray for the way to heaven. Reaching the fruit of their merit, they enjoy divine pleasures in heaven, in Indra's world.
```

```phrase
Knowers of the three Vedas, soma-drinkers, whose sins are cleansed,
---
having worshipped me with sacrifices, pray for the way to heaven.
---
They, having reached the fruit of their merit, Indra's world,
---
enjoy the divine enjoyments of the gods in heaven.
```
