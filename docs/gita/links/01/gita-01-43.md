# gita-01-43

## same
from: gita-01-43-w2
to: gita-01-43-w1
judgment: obvious
note: etaiḥ and doṣaiḥ stand in the same case: by these evils.

## complement
from: gita-01-43-w1
to: gita-01-43-w5
judgment: obvious
note: With a passive verb the instrumental is the doer. The evils uproot the duties.

## complement
from: gita-01-43-w3

note: Genitive of whose evils they are.

## subject
from: gita-01-43-w5
to: gita-01-43-w6

note: The verb is passive, so its nominative is what is uprooted. The duties of the family are uprooted too.

## subject
