# gita-06-22

## yam
ids: gita-06-22-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Masculine accusative of yad, the gain of the self. Written yaṃ before labdhvā.
meaning: which (this gain of the self)

## labdhvā
ids: gita-06-22-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: labh
note: Absolutive of labh.
meaning: having gained

## ca
ids: gita-06-22-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written cā; the a joins the a of aparam.
meaning: and

## aparam
ids: gita-06-22-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: apara
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The a is written on ca. It agrees with lābham, so the -am is masculine accusative.
meaning: another

## lābham
ids: gita-06-22-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: lābha
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: labh
note: Written lābhaṃ before manyate.
meaning: gain

## manyate
ids: gita-06-22-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: man
class: div
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The subject is the yogin, not named in this verse.
meaning: he thinks

## na
ids: gita-06-22-w7, gita-06-22-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The first is written nā; the a joins the a of adhikam.
meaning: not

## adhikam
ids: gita-06-22-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: adhika
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The a is written on na. It agrees with lābham, so the -am is masculine accusative; with tataḥ it means greater than that.
meaning: greater

## tataḥ
ids: gita-06-22-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: tad with the suffix -tas, in the ablative sense: than that.
meaning: than that

## yasmin
ids: gita-06-22-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
note: Locative singular of yad, masculine or neuter in form. It is the truth of the self, which is neuter, so it is neuter.
meaning: in which (in this truth of the self)

## sthitaḥ
ids: gita-06-22-w11
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written sthito before na. Masculine of the yogin, the one who is not shaken.
meaning: established

## duḥkhena
ids: gita-06-22-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: duḥkha
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
meaning: by pain (even such as the fall of a weapon)

## guruṇā
ids: gita-06-22-w14
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: guru
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: It agrees with duḥkhena.
meaning: heavy

## api
ids: gita-06-22-w15
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The a joins the ā of guruṇā. It emphasizes that heavy pain.
meaning: even

## vicālyate
ids: gita-06-22-w16
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: cal
prefix: vi
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Causative of cal, then passive: he is made to move.
meaning: is he shaken
