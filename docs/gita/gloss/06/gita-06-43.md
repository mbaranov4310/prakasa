# gita-06-43

## tatra
ids: gita-06-43-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious

## tam
ids: gita-06-43-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with buddhi-saṃyogam.
meaning: that

## buddhi-saṃyogam
ids: gita-06-43-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: buddhi-saṃyoga
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: yuj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Union (saṃyoga) with the understanding (buddhi). saṃyoga is from yuj, with sam.
meaning: union with the understanding

## tataḥ
ids: gita-06-43-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: singular
note: Written tato before bhūyaḥ. The ablative is masculine or neuter; it is the former impression he strives from, so it is masculine.
meaning: from that (from the former impression)

## bhūyaḥ
ids: gita-06-43-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The neuter accusative singular of bhūyas, “more,” used as an adverb. Comparative of bahu.
meaning: still more

## kuru-nandana

judgment: obvious

gender: masculine
