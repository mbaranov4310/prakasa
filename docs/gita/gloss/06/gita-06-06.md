# gita-06-06

## bandhuḥ
ids: gita-06-06-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bandhu
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written bandhurā: the visarga becomes r, and the ā of ātmā joins.
meaning: the friend

## ātmā
ids: gita-06-06-w2, gita-06-06-w14
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The one after bandhuḥ is written tmā, and its ā takes the ā of ātmanaḥ. The one after varteta is written tmai, the e of eva joining as ai.
meaning: the self

## ātmanaḥ
ids: gita-06-06-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Genitive, not ablative: the friend of the self. The initial ā joins the preceding ātmā, and the visarga becomes s before tasya.
meaning: of the self

## tasya
ids: gita-06-06-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
meaning: of him

## yena
ids: gita-06-06-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: Masculine, not neuter: it answers tasya. Written yenā, a plus ā becoming ā.
meaning: by whom

## ātmā
ids: gita-06-06-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The body and the organs, the thing that has been mastered. Written tmai: its ā joins yenā, and the e of eva joins as ai.
meaning: the self (body and organs)

## eva
ids: gita-06-06-w7, gita-06-06-w15
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The first is written vā before ātmanā, a plus ā becoming ā. In both, the e joins ātmā as ai.
meaning: itself

## ātmanā
ids: gita-06-06-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: The initial ā is written on eva, as vā.
meaning: by the self

## jitaḥ
ids: gita-06-06-w9
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: ji
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Passive in sense, so the nominative is what has been mastered. It agrees with the self that is body and organs.
meaning: mastered

## anātmanaḥ
ids: gita-06-06-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: anātman
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Genitive, not ablative: the man whose self is unmastered. Written anātmanas before tu.
meaning: of one who has not mastered the self
