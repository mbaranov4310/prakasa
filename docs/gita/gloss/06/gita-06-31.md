# gita-06-31

## sarva-bhūta-sthitam
ids: gita-06-31-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-bhūta-sthita
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: sthā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: sthita is the past participle of sthā. Locative tatpuruṣa: abiding in every being. It agrees with mām. Written sarvabhūtasthitaṃ before yaḥ.
meaning: abiding in every being

## yaḥ
ids: gita-06-31-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written yo before mām.
meaning: who

## mām
ids: gita-06-31-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māṃ before bhajati.
meaning: me

## bhajati
ids: gita-06-31-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhaj
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Written bhajatye: i becomes y, and the e of ekatvam joins it.
meaning: worships

## ekatvam
ids: gita-06-31-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ekatva
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Abstract noun, eka with the suffix -tva. The initial e is written with bhajati. The final m meets the ā of āsthitaḥ, so the slice is katvamā. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he has taken his stand in, so it is accusative.
meaning: oneness

## āsthitaḥ
ids: gita-06-31-w6
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: ā
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Past participle of sthā with ā, agreeing with yaḥ. The initial ā is written with ekatvam, so the slice is sthitaḥ.
meaning: having taken his stand in

## sarvathā
ids: gita-06-31-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: sarva with the suffix -thā, “in every manner.”
meaning: in every way

## vartamānaḥ
ids: gita-06-31-w8
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: vṛt
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Present participle of vṛt, ātmanepada, agreeing with the yogin. Written vartamāno before the a of api, and that a is dropped.
meaning: living

## api
ids: gita-06-31-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written pi. The initial a is dropped after vartamānaḥ.
meaning: even

## saḥ

number: singular
