# gita-06-39

## etat
ids: gita-06-39-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written etan before me. etat is nominative or accusative, and this neuter form is the doubt he should cut, so it is accusative.
meaning: this

## me
ids: gita-06-39-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. me is genitive or dative; it is his doubt, so it is genitive.
meaning: my

## saṃśayam
ids: gita-06-39-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃśaya
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: śī
note: From sam and śī. Written saṃśayaṃ before kṛṣṇa.
meaning: doubt

## kṛṣṇa
ids: gita-06-39-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kṛṣṇa
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
meaning: O Krishna

## chettum
ids: gita-06-39-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: chid
note: Infinitive of chid. The slice writes च्छ after kṛṣṇa, while the pada has the single छ, and its final m carries the initial a of arhasi.
meaning: to cut away (to remove it)

## arhasi
ids: gita-06-39-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: arh
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is the unstated you. The initial a sits on the म of chettum, and i merges with the a of aśeṣataḥ as ya.
meaning: you should

## aśeṣataḥ
ids: gita-06-39-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: aśeṣa with the suffix -tas, with nothing left. The initial a is merged in the ya of arhasi.
meaning: completely (with nothing left)

## tvat-anyaḥ
ids: gita-06-39-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: tvat-anya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: tvat is the ablative of yuṣmad, and anya is other than that. The adjective stands as the person.
meaning: other than you

ids: gita-06-39-w9

judgment: obvious
stem: saṃśaya
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
root: śī
note: From sam and śī. Written saṃśayasyā, the final a merging with the a of asya.
meaning: of the doubt

## asya
ids: gita-06-39-w10
kind: pronoun

stem: idam

number: singular

## chettā

stem: chettṛ
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular

note: Agent noun from chid. The slice writes च्छ after asya; the pada has the single छ.

## na

## hi

judgment: obvious
