# gita-06-17

## yukta-āhāra-vihārasya
ids: gita-06-17-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yukta-āhāra-vihāra
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose food and walking are both kept to a fixed amount. Food and walking are a dvandva inside the bahuvrīhi, and Śaṅkara supplies yoginaḥ for this genitive.
meaning: whose food and walking are kept in measure

kind: adjective

stem: yukta-ceṣṭa
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular

meaning: whose effort is kept in measure

## yukta-svapna-avabodhasya
ids: gita-06-17-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yukta-svapna-avabodha
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose sleep and waking are both kept to fixed times. In the line, svapna and avabodha join as svapnā.
meaning: whose sleep and waking are kept to their times
