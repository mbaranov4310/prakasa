# gita-06-07

## jita-ātmanaḥ
ids: gita-06-07-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jita-ātman
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: jita is the past participle of ji: one by whom the self has been mastered. Genitive, not ablative, agreeing with praśāntasya.
meaning: of one who has mastered the self (body and organs)

## praśāntasya
ids: gita-06-07-w2
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: śam
prefix: pra
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: It agrees with jita-ātmanaḥ, the same man.
meaning: of one whose mind is at peace (the renouncer)

## parama-ātmā
ids: gita-06-07-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: parama-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: parama and ātman, the supreme self. In the line, a plus ā becomes ā.
meaning: the supreme Self

## samāhitaḥ
ids: gita-06-07-w4
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: dhā
prefix: sam-ā
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Past participle of sam-ā-dhā, agreeing with parama-ātmā. vartate, “is present,” is understood.
meaning: present (directly, as the self itself)

## śīta-uṣṇa-sukha-duḥkheṣu
ids: gita-06-07-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śīta-uṣṇa-sukha-duḥkha
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: plural
compound: dvandva
note: Written śītoṣṇa: a plus u becomes o. The locative goes with samaḥ, “the same,” which is understood.
meaning: in cold and heat, pleasure and pain

## tathā
ids: gita-06-07-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It joins honor and disgrace to the pairs before. samaḥ, “the same,” is understood with both locatives.
meaning: and

## māna-apamānayoḥ
ids: gita-06-07-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: māna-apamāna
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: dual
compound: dvandva
root: man
note: Written mānāpamānayoḥ: a plus a becomes ā. The -yoḥ is locative, not genitive, and it goes with the same understood samaḥ.
meaning: in honor and disgrace
