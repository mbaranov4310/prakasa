## na
ids: gita-06-40-w4, gita-06-40-w7, gita-06-40-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The first is written nai, a merging with the e of eva. The second is written nā, a merging with the a of amutra.
meaning: not

## eva
ids: gita-06-40-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written ve. The e has gone into nai, and a merges with the i of iha.
meaning: indeed

## iha

judgment: obvious
note: The initial i is merged in ve.
meaning: here (in this world)

## amutra
ids: gita-06-40-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The initial a is merged in nā.
meaning: in the next
