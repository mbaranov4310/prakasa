# gita-06-16

## na
ids: gita-06-16-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written nā before ati-aśnataḥ. The a of ati stays with na.
meaning: not

## ati-aśnataḥ
ids: gita-06-16-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ati-aśnat
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
root: aś
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: aśnat is the present participle of aś, with ati “too,” and it stands as the person. The a of ati is written with na, i before a becomes y, and the visarga is written s before tu.
meaning: of one who eats too much

## tu
ids: gita-06-16-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## yogaḥ
ids: gita-06-16-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
note: Written yogo before asti, and the a of asti is dropped. The avagraha in the slice marks that a.
meaning: yoga

## asti
ids: gita-06-16-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The initial a is dropped after yogaḥ.
meaning: is

## na
ids: gita-06-16-w6, gita-06-16-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## ca
ids: gita-06-16-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written cai before ekāntam: a and e combine as ai, and that ai stays with ca.
meaning: and

## ekāntam
ids: gita-06-16-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The neuter accusative singular of ekānta, used as an adverb. The initial e is written at the end of ca, and the following a of anaśnataḥ stays with this word.
meaning: altogether

## anaśnataḥ
ids: gita-06-16-w9
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: aś
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Negated, and it stands as the person. The initial a is written at the end of ekāntam.
meaning: of one who does not eat

## ca
ids: gita-06-16-w11

judgment: obvious
note: Written cā before ati-svapna-śīlasya. The a of ati stays with ca.
meaning: and

## ati-svapna-śīlasya
ids: gita-06-16-w12
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ati-svapna-śīla
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose habit (śīla) is too much sleep,” standing as that person. The a of ati is written at the end of ca.
meaning: of one given to too much sleep

## jāgrataḥ
ids: gita-06-16-w13
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: jāgṛ
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: It stands as the person. The form is genitive or ablative, and it matches the other genitives, so it is genitive; written jāgrato before na.
meaning: of one who stays awake (too long)
