# gita-06-47

## yoginām
ids: gita-06-47-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yogin
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
root: yuj
note: Written yogināma before api, and that a is dropped.
meaning: of yogins (even those set on Rudra and the sun)

## api
ids: gita-06-47-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The initial a is joined to yoginām.
meaning: even

## sarveṣām
ids: gita-06-47-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
note: Masculine, to agree with yoginām.
meaning: of all

## mat-gatena
ids: gita-06-47-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mad-gata
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: gam
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Gone (gata, past participle of gam) to me; the pada writes mat- before the voiced g. It agrees with antara-ātmanā, and its final a absorbs the next a.
meaning: gone to me (fixed on me)

## antara-ātmanā
ids: gita-06-47-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: antar-ātman
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: The inner (antar) self, instrumental singular of the -an stem. The pada writes antara- before ātmanā, and the initial a is joined to mat-gatena.
meaning: by the inner self (by the mind)

## śraddhāvān
ids: gita-06-47-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śraddhāvat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: śraddhā with the suffix -vat, “possessing faith,” agreeing with yaḥ. śrad in śraddhā is not a prefix.
meaning: full of faith

## bhajate
ids: gita-06-47-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhaj
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
meaning: worships

## yaḥ
ids: gita-06-47-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written yo before mām.
meaning: who

## mām
ids: gita-06-47-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender.
meaning: me

## saḥ
ids: gita-06-47-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The visarga is dropped before me.
meaning: he

## me
ids: gita-06-47-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The enclitic me is genitive or dative; he is deemed by the speaker, so it is genitive.
meaning: by me

## yukta-tamaḥ
ids: gita-06-47-w12
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yukta-tama
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
note: Superlative, with -tama, of yukta, the past participle of yuj. Written yuktatamo before mataḥ.
meaning: the most yoked

## mataḥ
ids: gita-06-47-w13
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: man
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Past participle of man, “deemed.” It agrees with saḥ.
meaning: deemed
