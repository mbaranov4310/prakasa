# gita-06-09

## suhṛt-mitra-ari-udāsīna-madhyastha-dveṣya-bandhuṣu
ids: gita-06-09-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: suhṛt-mitra-ari-udāsīna-madhyastha-dveṣya-bandhu
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: plural
compound: dvandva
note: One word, a dvandva of the seven persons. The locative plural ending sits on bandhu, the last member.
meaning: toward well-wisher, friend, foe, the indifferent, the neutral, the disliked, and the kinsman

## sādhuṣu
ids: gita-06-09-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sādhu
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: plural
note: Written sādhuṣv before api, the u of ṣu appearing as v. The locative plural is the same for masculine and neuter. It names people who follow scripture, so it is masculine. The adjective stands as a noun.
meaning: toward the good (those who follow scripture)

## pāpeṣu
ids: gita-06-09-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pāpa
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: plural
note: The locative plural is the same for masculine and neuter. It names people who do what is forbidden, so it is masculine. The adjective stands as a noun.
meaning: toward the wicked (those who do what is forbidden)

## sama-buddhiḥ
ids: gita-06-09-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sama-buddhi
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose mind (buddhi) is even (sama).” It stands as the man, so the gender is masculine. Written samabuddhir before viśiṣyate.
meaning: one whose mind is even (not asking who did what)
