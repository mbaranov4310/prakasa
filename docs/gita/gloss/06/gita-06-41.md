ids: gita-06-41-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: āp
prefix: pra
note: Absolutive of pra-āp, “having reached.”
meaning: having reached

## puṇya-kṛtām
ids: gita-06-41-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: puṇya-kṛt
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
root: kṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: kṛt is the agent noun from kṛ. Doers of merit (puṇya).
meaning: of those who have done merit (the horse-sacrifice and rites like it)
