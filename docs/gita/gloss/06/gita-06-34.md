# gita-06-34

## cañcalam
ids: gita-06-34-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: cañcala
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written cañcalaṃ before hi. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative; it agrees with manaḥ, so it is nominative.
meaning: fickle (exceedingly so)

## hi
ids: gita-06-34-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Śaṅkara reads it as yasmāt, the reason a steady state is hard.
meaning: for

## manaḥ
ids: gita-06-34-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: manas
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: man
note: manaḥ is nominative or accusative. The adjectives describe it and there is no verb, so it is nominative.
meaning: the mind

## kṛṣṇa
ids: gita-06-34-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kṛṣṇa
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
meaning: O Krishna

## pramāthi
ids: gita-06-34-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: pramāthin
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: This form of the -in stem is nominative or accusative. It agrees with manaḥ, so it is nominative.
meaning: agitating (it throws the body and the senses about)

## balavat
ids: gita-06-34-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: balavat
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: bala with the suffix -vat, written balavad before dṛḍham. This form is nominative or accusative; it agrees with manaḥ, so it is nominative.
meaning: strong (nothing can hold it back)

## dṛḍham
ids: gita-06-34-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: dṛḍha
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It agrees with manaḥ, so it is nominative.
meaning: firm (uncuttable, like a cord)

## tasya
ids: gita-06-34-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Written tasyā before aham, and that a is dropped. It takes up manaḥ.
meaning: of that (such a mind)

## aham
ids: gita-06-34-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The initial a is dropped after tasya.
meaning: I

## nigraham
ids: gita-06-34-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: nigraha
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: grah
note: From ni and grah. Written nigrahaṃ before manye.
meaning: the restraining

## manye
ids: gita-06-34-w11
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: man
class: div
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: In the sense of thinking, man is div class, and the present takes -ya-.
meaning: think

## vāyoḥ
ids: gita-06-34-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: vāyu
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Written vāyor before iva. vāyoḥ is genitive or ablative; it is the wind’s restraint in the comparison, so it is genitive.
meaning: of the wind

## iva
ids: gita-06-34-w13
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The i joins vāyoḥ in the slice.
meaning: as

## suduṣkaram
ids: gita-06-34-w14
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: suduṣkara
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: su intensifies duṣkara, hard to do. The ending -am agrees with nigraham, so it is masculine accusative.
meaning: very hard (harder even than that)
