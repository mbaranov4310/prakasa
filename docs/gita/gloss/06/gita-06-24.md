# gita-06-24

## saṃkalpa-prabhavān
ids: gita-06-24-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃkalpa-prabhava
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose origin (prabhava) is imagination (saṃkalpa); the pada keeps the anusvāra. It agrees with kāmān, so -ān is accusative plural.
meaning: born of imagination

## kāmān
ids: gita-06-24-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kāma
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
root: kam
note: Written kāmāṃs before tyaktvā.
meaning: the desires

## tyaktvā
ids: gita-06-24-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: tyaj
note: Absolutive of tyaj.
meaning: having left

## sarvān
ids: gita-06-24-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: The a of aśeṣataḥ is the vowel on this n.
meaning: all of them

## aśeṣataḥ
ids: gita-06-24-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: aśeṣa, “with no remainder,” and the suffix -tas. The a is written on sarvān.
meaning: with nothing remaining

## manasā
ids: gita-06-24-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: manas
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: man
note: Written manasai; ā and the e of eva join as ai. The discriminating quality is not a separate word.
meaning: by the mind (the mind that can tell one thing from another)

## eva
ids: gita-06-24-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e is written on manasā, and the a joins the i of indriya-grāmam. It singles out manasā.
meaning: alone

## indriya-grāmam
ids: gita-06-24-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya-grāma
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The host (grāma) of the senses. The i is written into the e of eva.
meaning: the host of the senses

## viniyamya
ids: gita-06-24-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: yam
prefix: vi-ni
note: Absolutive of yam with vi and ni.
meaning: having drawn them in

## samantataḥ
ids: gita-06-24-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: samanta with the suffix -tas: from every side.
meaning: from every side
