# gita-06-29

## sarva-bhūta-stham
ids: gita-06-29-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-bhūta-stha
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: sthā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Locative tatpuruṣa: standing (stha, from sthā) in every being. Written sarvabhūtasthamā: the ā of ātmānam joins it.
meaning: standing in every being

## ātmānam
ids: gita-06-29-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The initial ā joins the preceding word, so the slice is tmānaṃ.
meaning: his own self

## sarva-bhūtāni
ids: gita-06-29-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-bhūta
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
root: bhū
compound: karmadhāraya
note: All (sarva) beings (bhūta). bhūta is the past participle of bhū. The neuter plural -āni is nominative or accusative. It is what he sees in the self, so it is accusative.
meaning: all beings (from Brahmā down to a blade of grass)

## ca
ids: gita-06-29-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written cā before the ā of ātmani.
meaning: and

## ātmani
ids: gita-06-29-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: The initial ā joins ca, so the slice is tmani.
meaning: in the self

## īkṣate
ids: gita-06-29-w6

root: īkṣ

person: 3rd
number: singular

## yoga-yukta-ātmā
ids: gita-06-29-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga-yukta-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: The bahuvrīhi stands as a noun: one whose mind (ātman) is joined (yukta) in yoga. yukta is the past participle of yuj. The nominative singular of ātman is ātmā.
meaning: his mind joined in yoga (his inner organ collected)

## sarvatra
ids: gita-06-29-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: sarva with the suffix -tra, “in every place.”
meaning: everywhere

## sama-darśanaḥ
ids: gita-06-29-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sama-darśana
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose seeing (darśana, from dṛś) is the same (sama), with no distinctions.
meaning: seeing the same (one Brahman, with no distinctions)
