# gita-06-23

## tam
ids: gita-06-23-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written taṃ before vidyāt. Masculine accusative of tad, the state one should know.
meaning: that state

## vidyāt
ids: gita-06-23-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: ad
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Written vidyād before duḥkha-saṃyoga-viyogam. The subject, the one who should know, is not named in the verse.
meaning: one should know

## duḥkha-saṃyoga-viyogam
ids: gita-06-23-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: duḥkha-saṃyoga-viyoga
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: yuj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Severance (viyoga) from union (saṃyoga) with pain; both are from yuj. Written with anusvāra before yoga-saṃjñitam, and the -am agrees with tam, so it is accusative.
meaning: as severance from union with pain

## yoga-saṃjñitam
ids: gita-06-23-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga-saṃjñita
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: jñā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: saṃjñita is the past participle of jñā with sam: named yoga. It agrees with tam, so the -am is masculine accusative.
meaning: as what is called yoga

## saḥ
ids: gita-06-23-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: saḥ drops its visarga before a consonant and does not become o. Written sa before niścayena.
meaning: that

## niścayena
ids: gita-06-23-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: niścaya
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: ci
note: From ci with nis: a settled resolve.
meaning: with resolve

## yoktavyaḥ
ids: gita-06-23-w7
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: yuj
participle: potential (-tavya)
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written yoktavyo before yogaḥ. Passive in sense: yoga is to be practiced.

## yogaḥ
ids: gita-06-23-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
note: Written yogo before anirviṇṇa-cetasā, and that a is dropped.
meaning: yoga

## anirviṇṇa-cetasā
ids: gita-06-23-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: anirviṇṇa-cetas
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: The a is dropped after yogaḥ. It is the mind itself, an unwearied cetas, so the gender is neuter; anirviṇṇa is the negated past participle of vid.
meaning: by a mind that does not weary
