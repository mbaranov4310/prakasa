# gita-06-02

## yam
ids: gita-06-02-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written yaṃ before saṃnyāsam.
meaning: what

## saṃnyāsam
ids: gita-06-02-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃnyāsa
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: as
note: From sam-ni-as. Written saṃnyāsami, the i of iti joining the final m.
meaning: renunciation (giving up all action and its fruit)

## iti
ids: gita-06-02-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The i is written at the end of saṃnyāsam. iti marks that word as the name they give.
meaning: as

## prāhuḥ
ids: gita-06-02-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: ah
prefix: pra
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: pra with āhuḥ, the perfect of saying. Written prāhur before yogam, and the callers are not named in the verse.
meaning: they call

## asaṃnyasta-saṃkalpaḥ
ids: gita-06-02-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: asaṃnyasta-saṃkalpa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One by whom the aim has not been given up. The initial a is taken into hy, and the visarga becomes o before yogī.
meaning: one who has not renounced the aim (at the fruit)
