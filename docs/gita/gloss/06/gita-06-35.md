## asaṃśayam
ids: gita-06-35-w3

judgment: obvious

meaning: no doubt (there is no question of it)

gender: masculine

number: singular

## manaḥ
ids: gita-06-35-w5

stem: manas
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: man

number: singular
