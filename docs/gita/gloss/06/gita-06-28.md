# gita-06-28

## yuñjan
ids: gita-06-28-w1
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: yuj
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Present participle of yuj, agreeing with the yogin. Written yuñjanne: the e of evam joins, and n is doubled.
meaning: practicing

## evam
ids: gita-06-28-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written vam. The initial e joins yuñjan.
meaning: in this way (by the course just taught)

## sadā
ids: gita-06-28-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Joined with ātmānam. The line writes sadātmānaṃ with no avagraha.
meaning: always

## ātmānam
ids: gita-06-28-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The initial ā joins sadā, so the slice is tmānaṃ. There is no avagraha.
meaning: on the self

## yogī
ids: gita-06-28-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yogin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
note: Agent noun from yuj.
meaning: the yogin (clear of what blocks the practice)

## vigata-kalmaṣaḥ
ids: gita-06-28-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vigata-kalmaṣa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose stain (kalmaṣa) has gone (vigata).” vigata is the past participle of gam with vi.
meaning: whose stain is gone

## sukhena
ids: gita-06-28-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The instrumental singular of sukha, used as an adverb.
meaning: easily (with no strain)

## brahma-saṃsparśam
ids: gita-06-28-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: brahma-saṃsparśa
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Joy whose contact (saṃsparśa) is with Brahman.” saṃsparśa is from spṛś with sam. Written brahmasaṃsparśam before the a of atyantam, and that a is dropped. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It describes the joy he reaches, so it is accusative.
meaning: of contact with Brahman (with the supreme Brahman)

## atyantam
ids: gita-06-28-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: atyanta
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: ati + anta, past every limit. The initial a is dropped after brahma-saṃsparśam. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It describes the joy, so it is accusative.
meaning: endless (past every limit)

## sukham
ids: gita-06-28-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sukha
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written sukham before the a of aśnute, and that a is dropped. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he reaches, so it is accusative.
meaning: the joy

## aśnute
ids: gita-06-28-w11
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: aś
class: su
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The initial a is dropped after sukham.
meaning: he reaches
