# gita-06-12

## tatra
ids: gita-06-12-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It points to the seat just set down. Written tatrai before ekāgram: a and the following e combine as ai, and that ai stays with this word.
meaning: there

## ekāgram
ids: gita-06-12-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ekāgra
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Whose point (agra) is one (eka).” The initial e is written at the end of tatra. The neuter -am agrees with manaḥ, so it is accusative.
meaning: one-pointed (drawn back from every object)

## manaḥ
ids: gita-06-12-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: manas
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: man
note: The neuter -aḥ of manas is nominative or accusative. It is what he makes one-pointed, so it is accusative.
meaning: the mind

## kṛtvā
ids: gita-06-12-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
note: Absolutive, “having made.”
meaning: having made

## yata-citta-indriya-kriyaḥ
ids: gita-06-12-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yata-citta-indriya-kriya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose acts (kriyā) of mind (citta) and senses (indriya) are held in (yata).” yata is the past participle of yam. It stands as the man who practices.
meaning: with the acts of mind and senses held in

## upaviśya
ids: gita-06-12-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: viś
prefix: upa
note: Absolutive, “having sat down.” Written upaviśyā before āsane: a and the following ā combine, and that ā stays with this word.
meaning: sitting

## āsane
ids: gita-06-12-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: āsana
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
root: ās
note: The initial ā is written at the end of upaviśya.
meaning: on the seat

## yuñjyāt
ids: gita-06-12-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: yuj
class: rudh
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Written yuñjyād before yogam.
meaning: should practice

## yogam
ids: gita-06-12-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: yuj
note: Written yogamā before ātma-viśuddhaye: m and the following ā combine, and that ā stays with this word.
meaning: yoga

## ātma-viśuddhaye
ids: gita-06-12-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātma-viśuddhi
gender: feminine
case: dative
number: singular
root: śudh
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Dative of purpose, the cleansing of the mind. viśuddhi is from vi-śudh. The initial ā is written at the end of yogam.
meaning: for the cleansing of the mind
