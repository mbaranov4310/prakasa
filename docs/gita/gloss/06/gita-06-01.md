# gita-06-01

## śrī-bhagavān
ids: gita-06-01-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śrī-bhagavat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: The u of uvāca is written on the end of śrī-bhagavān.
meaning: the blessed Lord

## uvāca
ids: gita-06-01-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The opening u is written at the end of śrī-bhagavān.
meaning: said

## anāśritaḥ
ids: gita-06-01-w3
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: śri
prefix: ā
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The a- negates āśrita, “not depending.” It agrees with yaḥ.
meaning: not depending (free of longing for the fruit)

## karma-phalam
ids: gita-06-01-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karma-phala
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Written karma-phalaṃ before kāryam. The neuter -am is what he does not depend on, so it is accusative.
meaning: the fruit of action

## kāryam
ids: gita-06-01-w5
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
participle: potential (-ya)
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written kāryaṃ before karma. The neuter -am agrees with karma, so it is accusative.
meaning: what is to be done (the standing rite)

## karma
ids: gita-06-01-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: The neuter ending is nominative or accusative. It is what he performs, so it is accusative.
meaning: action (the fire-rite and the rest)

## niragniḥ
ids: gita-06-01-w15

gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Bahuvrīhi standing as a noun: one from whom the ritual fires have gone. Written niragnir before na.

## akriyaḥ
ids: gita-06-01-w18
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: akriya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One in whom the rites are absent. The adjective stands as a noun, and the initial a is dropped after cā.
meaning: one without rites (austerity, giving, and the like)
