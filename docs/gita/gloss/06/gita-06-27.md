# gita-06-27

## praśānta-manasam
ids: gita-06-27-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: praśānta-manas
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose mind (manas) is deeply quiet (praśānta).” praśānta is the past participle of śam with pra. Written praśāntamanasaṃ before hi.
meaning: whose mind has grown deeply quiet

## hi
ids: gita-06-27-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written hy before the e of enam.
meaning: for

## enam
ids: gita-06-27-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: ena
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: ena is the enclitic pronoun “this.” The initial e joins hi. Written naṃ before yoginam.
meaning: this

## yoginam
ids: gita-06-27-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yogin
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: yuj
note: Agent noun from yuj. Written yoginaṃ before sukham.
meaning: yogin

## sukham
ids: gita-06-27-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sukha
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The u of uttamam is written with this word. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what comes to the yogin, so it is nominative.
meaning: joy

## uttamam
ids: gita-06-27-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: uttama
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Superlative, with -tama. The initial u is written with sukham. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It describes the joy that comes, so it is nominative.
meaning: the highest (with nothing beyond it)

## upaiti
ids: gita-06-27-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: i
prefix: upa
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
meaning: comes

## śānta-rajasam
ids: gita-06-27-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śānta-rajas
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose passion (rajas) has grown quiet (śānta).” śānta is the past participle of śam. Written śāntarajasaṃ before brahma-bhūtam.
meaning: whose passion has died down (delusion and the other taints have faded)

## brahma-bhūtam
ids: gita-06-27-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: brahma-bhūta
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: bhū
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: bhūta is the past participle of bhū. He has become Brahman. Written brahmabhūtam before the a of akalmaṣam, and that a is dropped.
meaning: who has become Brahman (sure that all this is Brahman)

## akalmaṣam
ids: gita-06-27-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: akalmaṣa
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: a + kalmaṣa, “without stain.” The initial a is dropped after brahma-bhūtam. It agrees with the yogin, so the -am is masculine accusative.
meaning: free of stain (free of merit and of demerit)
