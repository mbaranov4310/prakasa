# gita-06-05

## uddharet
ids: gita-06-05-w1
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: hṛ
prefix: ud
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Written uddharedā: final t is voiced, and the ā of ātmanā joins. The one who should lift is not named.
meaning: should lift up (to the state of one established in yoga)

## ātmanā
ids: gita-06-05-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: The initial ā is written at the end of uddharet.
meaning: by the self

## ātmānam
ids: gita-06-05-w3, gita-06-05-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The first is written ātmānaṃ before na. The second loses its ā to nā, and the a of avasādayet is written on its final m.
meaning: the self

## na
ids: gita-06-05-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written nā before the second ātmānam. a plus ā becomes ā.
meaning: not

## avasādayet
ids: gita-06-05-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: sad
prefix: ava
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Causative of sad. The initial a is written on ātmānam, and the one who should drag down is not named.
meaning: should drag down

## ātmā
ids: gita-06-05-w7, gita-06-05-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The e of eva joins each as ai. The second loses its ā to bandhurā and is written tmai.
meaning: the self

## eva
ids: gita-06-05-w8, gita-06-05-w13
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Each is written va. The e joins the preceding ātmā as ai.
meaning: alone

## hi
ids: gita-06-05-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written hyā before ātmanaḥ. i plus ā becomes yā.
meaning: for

## ātmanaḥ
ids: gita-06-05-w10, gita-06-05-w15
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Genitive, not ablative: whose friend and whose enemy. The first is written ātmano, and both lose the initial ā to the word before.
meaning: of the self

## bandhuḥ
ids: gita-06-05-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bandhu
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written bandhurā: the visarga becomes r, and the ā of ātmā joins.
meaning: the friend

## ripuḥ
ids: gita-06-05-w14
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ripu
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written ripurā: the visarga becomes r, and the ā of ātmanaḥ joins.
meaning: the enemy
