# gita-06-15

## yuñjan
ids: gita-06-15-w1
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: yuj
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written yuñjanne before evam: n doubles before the vowel, and the e of evam stays with this word.
meaning: practicing

## evam
ids: gita-06-15-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The initial e is written at the end of yuñjan.
meaning: in this way (by the rule just taught)

## sadā
ids: gita-06-15-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: a and the following ā combine as one ā. The print is sadātmānaṃ, with no avagraha.
meaning: always

## ātmānam
ids: gita-06-15-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The initial ā is not written again after sadā. The slice begins at tm. It is what he practices, so it is accusative.
meaning: the self

## yogī
ids: gita-06-15-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yogin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
note: Nominative singular of the in-stem yogin.
meaning: the yogin

## niyata-mānasaḥ
ids: gita-06-15-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: niyata-mānasa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose mind (mānasa) is held in (niyata).” niyata is the past participle of ni-yam.
meaning: with the mind held in

## śāntim
ids: gita-06-15-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śānti
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: śam
meaning: peace (the quiet of cessation)

## nirvāṇa-paramām
ids: gita-06-15-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nirvāṇa-parama
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Peace whose highest goal (paramā) is release (nirvāṇa).” It agrees with śāntim.
meaning: whose highest end is release

## mat-saṃsthām
ids: gita-06-15-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mat-saṃstha
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: sthā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: saṃstha is “abiding,” from sam-sthā: the peace rests on me. Written mat-saṃsthāma before adhigacchati, and the a of that verb stays with this word.
meaning: resting on me

## adhigacchati
ids: gita-06-15-w10
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: gam
prefix: adhi
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The initial a is written at the end of mat-saṃsthām.
meaning: reaches
