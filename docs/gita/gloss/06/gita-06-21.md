# gita-06-21

## sukham
ids: gita-06-21-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sukha
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The ā of ātyantikam is written on sukham. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative; it is what he knows, so it is accusative.
meaning: joy

## ātyantikam
ids: gita-06-21-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ātyantika
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The ā is written on sukham, and the word is written ātyantikaṃ before yat. From atyanta, “without end”; the neuter -am agrees with sukham, so it is accusative.
meaning: endless

## yat
ids: gita-06-21-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter ending is nominative or accusative; it agrees with sukham, so it is accusative.
meaning: which

## tat
ids: gita-06-21-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written tad before buddhi-grāhyam. The neuter ending agrees with sukham, so it is accusative.
meaning: that

## buddhi-grāhyam
ids: gita-06-21-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: buddhi-grāhya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: grah
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: grāhya is the potential participle of grah, grasped by the intellect. The a of atīndriyam is written on it, and the neuter -am agrees with sukham, so it is accusative.
meaning: grasped by the intellect (with no help from the senses)

## atīndriyam
ids: gita-06-21-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: atīndriya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The a is written on buddhi-grāhyam. Beyond the senses; the neuter -am agrees with sukham, so it is accusative.
meaning: beyond the senses (not born of their objects)

## vetti
ids: gita-06-21-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The subject is the yogin carried from the previous verse. ayam is the subject of calati.
meaning: he knows

## yatra
ids: gita-06-21-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: at that time

## na
ids: gita-06-21-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## ca
ids: gita-06-21-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written cai; the a joins the e of eva.
meaning: and

## eva
ids: gita-06-21-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e is written on ca, and the a joins the a of ayam.
meaning: at all

## ayam
ids: gita-06-21-w12
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The a is written on eva. Written ayaṃ before sthitaḥ.
meaning: this man (the one who knows)

## sthitaḥ
ids: gita-06-21-w13
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written sthitaś before calati. He is established in his own nature; that place is not a separate word in the verse.
meaning: established (in his own true nature)

## calati
ids: gita-06-21-w14
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: cal
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: na and eva limit it: he does not slip at all.
meaning: does he slip

## tattvataḥ
ids: gita-06-21-w15
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: tattva with the suffix -tas, in the ablative sense: from the true nature.
meaning: from the truth
