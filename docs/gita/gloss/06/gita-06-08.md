# gita-06-08

## jñāna-vijñāna-tṛpta-ātmā
ids: gita-06-08-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna-vijñāna-tṛpta-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose mind is content; tṛpta is the past participle of tṛp. In the line, a plus ā becomes ā.
meaning: one whose mind is content through knowledge and through direct realization

## kūṭasthaḥ
ids: gita-06-08-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kūṭastha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: sthā
note: From sthā, standing firm, here unshaken. Written kūṭastho before vijita-indriyaḥ.
meaning: unshaken

## vijita-indriyaḥ
ids: gita-06-08-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vijita-indriya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose senses have been mastered. Written vijitendriyaḥ, a plus i becoming e.
meaning: with the senses mastered
