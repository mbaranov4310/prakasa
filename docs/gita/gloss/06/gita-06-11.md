# gita-06-11

## śucau
ids: gita-06-11-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śuci
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: It agrees with deśe.
meaning: in a clean place (pure, and secluded)

## deśe
ids: gita-06-11-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: deśa
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
meaning: on a spot

## pratiṣṭhāpya
ids: gita-06-11-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: prati
note: Causative absolutive of sthā, “having set the seat down.” It leans on the main verb in the next verse.
meaning: setting down

## sthiram
ids: gita-06-11-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sthira
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written sthiramā before āsanam: m and the following ā combine, and that ā stays with this word. The neuter -am agrees with āsanam, so it is accusative.
meaning: firm

## āsanam
ids: gita-06-11-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: āsana
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: ās
note: The initial ā is written at the end of sthiram. Written āsanamā before ātmanaḥ, and that ā stays with this word. The neuter -am is what he sets down, so it is accusative.
meaning: his seat

## ātmanaḥ
ids: gita-06-11-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: The initial ā is written at the end of āsanam.
meaning: his own

## na
ids: gita-06-11-w7, gita-06-11-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written nā before ati-ucchritam and before ati-nīcam. The a of ati stays with na.
meaning: not

## ati-ucchritam
ids: gita-06-11-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ati-ucchrita
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: śri
prefix: ud
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: ucchrita is the past participle of ud-śri, with ati “too.” The a of ati is written with na, i before u becomes y, and the neuter -am agrees with āsanam, so it is accusative.
meaning: too high

## ati-nīcam
ids: gita-06-11-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ati-nīca
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The a of ati is written with the preceding na. The neuter -am agrees with āsanam, so it is accusative.
meaning: too low

## caila-ajina-kuśa-uttaram
ids: gita-06-11-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: caila-ajina-kuśa-uttara
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “The seat on which cloth, deerskin, and kuśa grass are laid.” The three are named cloth, skin, grass, and they are laid in the opposite order, grass at the bottom. The neuter -am agrees with āsanam, so it is accusative.
meaning: with cloth, deerskin, and kuśa grass, the grass underneath
