# gita-06-10

## yogī
ids: gita-06-10-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yogin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
note: Nominative singular of the in-stem yogin.
meaning: the yogin (one who meditates)

## yuñjīta
ids: gita-06-10-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: yuj
class: rudh
tense: potential
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
meaning: should fix

## satatam
ids: gita-06-10-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The neuter accusative singular of satata, used as an adverb. Written satatamā before ātmānam: m and the following ā combine, and that ā stays with this word.
meaning: constantly

## ātmānam
ids: gita-06-10-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The initial ā is written at the end of satatam. Here ātman is the mind, what he fixes.
meaning: the mind

## ekākī
ids: gita-06-10-w7
kind: adjective

stem: ekākin
gender: masculine

## yata-citta-ātmā
ids: gita-06-10-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yata-citta-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose mind (citta) and body (ātman) are held in (yata).” yata is the past participle of yam. In this compound ātman is the body.
meaning: with mind and body held in

## nirāśīḥ
ids: gita-06-10-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nirāśis
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One in whom longing (āśis) is absent.” The same nominative could be a feminine noun. It agrees with yogī, so it is masculine. Written nirāśīr before aparigrahaḥ, and that a is dropped.
meaning: free of longing

## aparigrahaḥ
ids: gita-06-10-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: aparigraha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One who has no gathering of possessions (parigraha).” The initial a is not in the slice.
meaning: owning nothing
