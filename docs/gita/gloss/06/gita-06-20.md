# gita-06-20

## yatra
ids: gita-06-20-w1, gita-06-20-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The first is written yatro; a and the u of uparamate join as o.
meaning: when

## uparamate
ids: gita-06-20-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: ram
prefix: upa
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The u is written on yatra.
meaning: comes to rest

## cittam
ids: gita-06-20-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: citta
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: cit
note: Written cittaṃ before niruddham. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative; it is what comes to rest, so it is nominative.
meaning: the mind

## niruddham
ids: gita-06-20-w4
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: rudh
prefix: ni
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written niruddhaṃ before yoga-sevayā. The neuter -am agrees with cittam, so it is nominative.
meaning: checked (its movement stopped on every side)

## yoga-sevayā
ids: gita-06-20-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga-sevā
gender: feminine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: sev
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The practice of yoga.
meaning: by the practice of yoga

## ca
ids: gita-06-20-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written cai; the a joins the e of eva.
meaning: and

## eva
ids: gita-06-20-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e is written on ca, and the a joins the ā of ātmanā.
meaning: also

## ātmanā
ids: gita-06-20-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: The ā is written on eva.
meaning: by the self (by the mind made pure in concentration)

## ātmānam
ids: gita-06-20-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Its ā joins the final ā of ātmanā. The line writes ātmanātmānaṃ and shows no avagraha.
meaning: the self (consciousness, whose nature is light)

## paśyan
ids: gita-06-20-w11
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: dṛś
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Present participle of dṛś; the present stem is paśya. Written paśyannā, with the ā of ātmani.
meaning: seeing

## ātmani
ids: gita-06-20-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: The ā is written on paśyan.
meaning: in the self

## tuṣyati
ids: gita-06-20-w13
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: tuṣ
class: div
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The subject is the yogin, not named in this verse.
meaning: he rejoices
