# gita-06-32

## ātma-aupamyena
ids: gita-06-32-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātma-aupamya
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Genitive tatpuruṣa: likeness (aupamya) of the self. aupamya is the state of a comparison (upamā). The ā of ātma and the au of aupamya are written together as au.
meaning: by likeness with himself (judging others by what he finds in himself)

## sarvatra
ids: gita-06-32-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: sarva with the suffix -tra, “in every place.”
meaning: everywhere (in every being)

## samam
ids: gita-06-32-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The neuter accusative singular of sama, used as an adverb. Written samaṃ before paśyati. Śaṅkara: seeing pleasure and pain as the same, he harms no one.
meaning: as the same

## paśyati
ids: gita-06-32-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dṛś
class: div
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The present stem paśya stands for dṛś.
meaning: sees

## yaḥ
ids: gita-06-32-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written yo before the a of arjuna, and that a is dropped.
meaning: who

## arjuna
ids: gita-06-32-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: arjuna
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
note: Vocative. The initial a is dropped after yaḥ, so the slice is rjuna.
meaning: Arjuna

## sukham
ids: gita-06-32-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sukha
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written sukhaṃ before vā. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he sees as the same, so it is accusative.
meaning: pleasure (what suits him)

## vā
ids: gita-06-32-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Here vā joins the two things he sees, in the sense of and.
meaning: and

## yadi
ids: gita-06-32-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: With the second vā. Not a condition; there is no second verb.
meaning: likewise

## vā
ids: gita-06-32-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: With yadi: and also.
meaning: and also

## duḥkham
ids: gita-06-32-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: duḥkha
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written duḥkhaṃ before saḥ. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he sees as the same, so it is accusative.
meaning: pain (what goes against him)

## paramaḥ

judgment: obvious

gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular

## mataḥ
ids: gita-06-32-w15
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: man
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Past participle of man, “held, considered.” There is no finite verb. It agrees with saḥ yogī.
meaning: is held to be
