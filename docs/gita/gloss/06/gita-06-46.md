# gita-06-46

## tapasvibhyaḥ
ids: gita-06-46-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: tapasvin
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: plural
note: tapas with the suffix -vin, “one who has austerity.” The adjective stands as a noun, written tapasvibhyo before adhikaḥ, and that a is dropped.
meaning: than ascetics

## adhikaḥ
ids: gita-06-46-w2, gita-06-46-w7, gita-06-46-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: adhika
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The first and the third are written adhiko, and their a is joined to the word before. The second keeps the visarga, and its a is dropped after mato.
meaning: higher

## yogī
ids: gita-06-46-w3, gita-06-46-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yogin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
note: Nominative singular of yogin is yogī.
meaning: the yogin

## jñānibhyaḥ
ids: gita-06-46-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñānin
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: plural
note: jñāna with the suffix -in. The adjective stands as a noun, written jñānibhyo before api, and that a is dropped.
meaning: than the learned (men of mere scriptural learning)

## api
ids: gita-06-46-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The initial a is dropped after jñānibhyo.
meaning: also

## mataḥ
ids: gita-06-46-w6
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: man
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Past participle of man, “deemed,” agreeing with the yogin of the first line. Written mato before adhikaḥ, and that a is dropped.
meaning: deemed

## karmibhyaḥ
ids: gita-06-46-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karmin
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: plural
note: karman with the suffix -in, “one who performs rites.” The adjective stands as a noun, written karmibhyaś before ca.
meaning: than ritualists

## ca
ids: gita-06-46-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written cā before adhikaḥ, and that a is dropped.
meaning: and

## tasmāt
ids: gita-06-46-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Ablative of tad, used as an adverb. Written tasmād before yogī.
meaning: therefore

## yogī
ids: gita-06-46-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yogin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
note: Predicate of bhava, whose subject is the unstated you. Nominative singular of yogin is yogī.
meaning: a yogin

## bhava
ids: gita-06-46-w14
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is the unstated you. Written bhavā before arjuna, and that a is dropped.
meaning: become

## arjuna
ids: gita-06-46-w15
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: arjuna
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
