# gita-06-03

## ārurukṣoḥ
ids: gita-06-03-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ārurukṣu
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
root: ruh
note: Desiderative of ā-ruh, one who wishes to climb. Written ārurukṣor before muneḥ.
meaning: of one who wishes to climb (not yet able to stay in meditation)

## muneḥ
ids: gita-06-03-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: muni
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Written muner before yogam.
meaning: of the sage (one who has renounced the fruit of action)

## yogam
ids: gita-06-03-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: yuj
note: Written yogaṃ before karma. It is what he wishes to climb to.
meaning: to yoga

## karma
ids: gita-06-03-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: The neuter ending is nominative or accusative. It is what is said, so it is nominative.
meaning: action

## kāraṇam
ids: gita-06-03-w5, gita-06-03-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kāraṇa
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: Written kāraṇamu, the u of ucyate joining the final m. The neuter -am is the predicate, so it is nominative.
meaning: the means

## ucyate
ids: gita-06-03-w6, gita-06-03-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Passive of vac. The u is written at the end of kāraṇam.
meaning: is said to be

## yoga-ārūḍhasya
ids: gita-06-03-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga-ārūḍha
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
root: ruh
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: ārūḍha is the past participle of ā-ruh. One who has climbed to yoga, agreeing with tasya.
meaning: of one who has climbed to yoga

## tasya
ids: gita-06-03-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Written tasyai before va. a plus e becomes ai.
meaning: of him

## eva
ids: gita-06-03-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e joins tasya as ai, and the slice keeps va.
meaning: the same

## śamaḥ
ids: gita-06-03-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śama
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
