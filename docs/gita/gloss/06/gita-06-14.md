# gita-06-14

## praśānta-ātmā
ids: gita-06-14-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: praśānta-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose mind (ātman) is deeply at peace (praśānta).” praśānta is the past participle of pra-śam. It stands as the man who should sit.
meaning: one whose mind is deeply at peace
