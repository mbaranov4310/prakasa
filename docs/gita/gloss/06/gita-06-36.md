# gita-06-36

## asaṃyata-ātmanā
ids: gita-06-36-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: asaṃyata-ātman
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose mind (ātman) is not held in check (asaṃyata, from sam and yam). The bahuvrīhi stands as a noun.
meaning: by one whose mind is not under control

judgment: obvious
