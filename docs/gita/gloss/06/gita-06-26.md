# gita-06-26

## yataḥ
ids: gita-06-26-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: Written yato before the next yataḥ. Masculine and neuter ablative singular look the same. It is from whatever cause the mind runs out, and a cause is neuter.
meaning: from whatever

## yataḥ
ids: gita-06-26-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: The same ablative again. Written yato before niścarati.
meaning: from whatever (from sound, or from anything else that draws it)

## niścarati
ids: gita-06-26-w3
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: car
prefix: nis
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The s of nis is written ś before c.
meaning: it runs out

## manaḥ
ids: gita-06-26-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: manas
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: man
note: Written manaś before cañcalam. The neuter ending is nominative or accusative. It is what runs out, so it is nominative.
meaning: the mind

## cañcalam
ids: gita-06-26-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: cañcala
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written cañcalam before the a of asthiram, and that a is dropped. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It describes the mind that runs out, so it is nominative.
meaning: restless (restless from its own fault)

## asthiram
ids: gita-06-26-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: asthira
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: a + sthira, “not steady.” The initial a is dropped after cañcalam. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It describes the mind, so it is nominative.
meaning: and so unsteady

## tataḥ
ids: gita-06-26-w7, gita-06-26-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: The answer to yataḥ. The first is written tatas before the second; the second is written tato before niyamya.
meaning: from that

## niyamya
ids: gita-06-26-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: yam
prefix: ni
note: Absolutive of yam with ni, “having checked.” Written niamyai: the final a joins the e of etat.
meaning: having checked it (by seeing the object as it is, and by letting go)

## etat
ids: gita-06-26-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The initial e is written with niyamya. The final t meets the ā of ātmani, so the slice is tadā. The neuter form is nominative or accusative. It is what one brings under control, so it is accusative.
meaning: this mind

## ātmani
ids: gita-06-26-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: The initial ā is written with etat. The final i joins the e of eva, so the slice is tmanye.
meaning: in the self

## eva
ids: gita-06-26-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written va. The initial e joins ātmani.
meaning: alone

case: accusative
number: singular
root: vaś
note: Written vaśaṃ before nayet.
meaning: under control
