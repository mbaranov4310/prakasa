# gita-06-18

## yadā
ids: gita-06-18-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: when

## viniyatam
ids: gita-06-18-w2
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: yam
prefix: vi-ni
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written viniyataṃ before cittam. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It describes the mind, which is what comes to rest, so it is nominative.
meaning: held to one point

## cittam
ids: gita-06-18-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: citta
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: cit
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what comes to rest, so it is nominative. The ā of ātmani is written on cittam.
meaning: the mind

## ātmani
ids: gita-06-18-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: The ā is written on cittam, and the i joins the e of eva as ye.
meaning: in the self

## eva
ids: gita-06-18-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e is written on ātmani, and the a joins the a of avatiṣṭhate. It singles out ātmani.
meaning: alone

## avatiṣṭhate
ids: gita-06-18-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: ava
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The a of ava is written on eva.
meaning: comes to rest (it has left thought of outer things)

## niḥspṛhaḥ
ids: gita-06-18-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: niḥspṛha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose craving has gone out. The bahuvrīhi stands as the yogin Śaṅkara supplies, the one who is called concentrated.
meaning: free of craving (longing for the seen and the unseen has left him)

## sarva-kāmebhyaḥ
ids: gita-06-18-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-kāma
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: plural
root: kam
compound: karmadhāraya
note: Written sarva-kāmebhyo before yuktaḥ. The ending -ebhyaḥ is dative or ablative. Craving has left those objects, so it is ablative.
meaning: from every object of desire

## yuktaḥ
ids: gita-06-18-w9
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: yuj
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The visarga drops before the i of iti. Śaṅkara glosses it as samāhita, settled.
meaning: concentrated

## iti
ids: gita-06-18-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written ityu; the i joins the u of ucyate.
meaning: thus

## ucyate
ids: gita-06-18-w11
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The u is written on iti. Passive of vac; the root vowel becomes u.
meaning: he is called

## tadā
ids: gita-06-18-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: then
