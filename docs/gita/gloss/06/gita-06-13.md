# gita-06-13

## samam
ids: gita-06-13-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sama
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am agrees with kāya-śiras-grīvam, so it is accusative.
meaning: even

## kāya-śiras-grīvam
ids: gita-06-13-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kāya-śiras-grīva
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: dvandva
note: A collective dvandva: body, head, and neck taken as one. The neuter -am is what he holds, so it is accusative.
meaning: body, head, and neck

## dhārayan
ids: gita-06-13-w3
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: dhṛ
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Causative of dhṛ. Written dhārayann before acalam, and the a of acalam is dropped.
meaning: holding

## acalam
ids: gita-06-13-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: acala
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The initial a is not in the slice. It agrees with the body, head, and neck: what is held even is also unmoving, and the neuter -am is accusative.
meaning: still

## sthiraḥ
ids: gita-06-13-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sthira
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It names the man’s own steadiness as he holds the posture.
meaning: steady

## saṃprekṣya
ids: gita-06-13-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: īkṣ
prefix: sam-pra
note: Absolutive, “having let the gaze rest.” An “as it were” is understood: the eyes settle, and the mind is not fastened on the nose.
meaning: looking (the eyes resting, as it were)

## nāsikā-agram
ids: gita-06-13-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: nāsikā-agra
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The neuter -am is what the gaze rests toward, so it is accusative.
meaning: the tip of the nose

## svam
ids: gita-06-13-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sva
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am agrees with nāsikā-agram, so it is accusative.
meaning: his own

## diśaḥ
ids: gita-06-13-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: diś
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: diśaḥ is nominative or accusative plural. It is what he does not look about at, so it is accusative. Written diśaś before ca.
meaning: the directions

## ca
ids: gita-06-13-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written cā before anavalokayan. The a of that word stays with ca.
meaning: and

## anavalokayan
ids: gita-06-13-w11
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: lok
prefix: ava
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Causative of lok, and negated. The initial a is written at the end of ca.
meaning: not looking about
