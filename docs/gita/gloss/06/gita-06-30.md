# gita-06-30

## yaḥ
ids: gita-06-30-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written yo before mām.
meaning: who

## mām
ids: gita-06-30-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māṃ before paśyati.
meaning: me (Vāsudeva, the self of all)

## paśyati
ids: gita-06-30-w3, gita-06-30-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dṛś
class: div
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The present stem paśya stands for dṛś.
meaning: sees

## sarvatra
ids: gita-06-30-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: sarva with the suffix -tra, “in every place.”
meaning: everywhere (in every being)

## sarvam
ids: gita-06-30-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written sarvaṃ before ca. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he sees in me, so it is accusative.
meaning: everything (every being, from Brahmā on)

## ca
ids: gita-06-30-w6, gita-06-30-w14
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## mayi
ids: gita-06-30-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: locative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender.
meaning: in me (in the self of all)

## tasya
ids: gita-06-30-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Written tasyā before aham.
meaning: for him

## aham
ids: gita-06-30-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The initial a joins tasya, so the slice is ham.
meaning: I (the Lord)

## na
