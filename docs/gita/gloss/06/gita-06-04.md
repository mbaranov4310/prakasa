## hi
ids: gita-06-04-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: indeed

## na
ids: gita-06-04-w3, gita-06-04-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The first is written ne before indriya-artheṣu. a plus i becomes e.
meaning: not

## indriya-artheṣu
ids: gita-06-04-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya-artha
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: plural
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The objects of the senses. The initial i is taken into ne.
meaning: to the objects of the senses

## karmasu
ids: gita-06-04-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: plural
root: kṛ
note: Written karmasva before anuṣajjate. u plus a becomes va.
meaning: to actions (enjoined, occasional, desired, or forbidden)

## anuṣajjate
ids: gita-06-04-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: sañj
prefix: anu
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The initial a is written on karmasu. The man who does not cling is the yogin of this passage, not named in the verse.
meaning: clings (takes them as his to do)

## sarva-saṃkalpa-saṃnyāsī
ids: gita-06-04-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-saṃkalpa-saṃnyāsin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose habit is to renounce every resolve. The stem ends in saṃnyāsin.
meaning: one who renounces every resolve (the aims at a result)

## yoga-ārūḍhaḥ
ids: gita-06-04-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga-ārūḍha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: ruh
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: ārūḍha is the past participle of ā-ruh. Written yoga-ārūḍhas before tadā.
meaning: one who has climbed to yoga

## tadā
ids: gita-06-04-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written tado before ucyate. ā plus u becomes o.
meaning: then

## ucyate
ids: gita-06-04-w11
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Passive of vac. The u is written at the end of tadā.
