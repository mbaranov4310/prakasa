# gita-06-38

## kaccit
ids: gita-06-38-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: A question particle. Written kaccin before na, where t becomes n.
meaning: does he

## na
ids: gita-06-38-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written no: a merges with the u of ubhaya-vibhraṣṭaḥ.
meaning: not

## ubhaya-vibhraṣṭaḥ
ids: gita-06-38-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ubhaya-vibhraṣṭa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhraṃś
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: vibhraṣṭa is the past participle of vi and bhraṃś. The initial u is merged in no, and the visarga is written ś before chinna-abhram.
meaning: fallen from both (from works and from yoga)

## chinna-abhram
ids: gita-06-38-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: chinna-abhra
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: A cloud (abhra) that is torn (chinna, from chid). The neuter -am is the cloud that perishes with iva, so it is nominative, and the i of iva joins this word in the slice.
meaning: a torn cloud

## iva
ids: gita-06-38-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The i joins chinna-abhram in the slice.
meaning: like

## naśyati
ids: gita-06-38-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: naś
class: div
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
meaning: perish

## apratiṣṭhaḥ
ids: gita-06-38-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: apratiṣṭha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: sthā
note: Negation of pratiṣṭha, from prati and sthā. Written apratiṣṭho before mahā-bāho.
meaning: with no footing

## mahā-bāho
ids: gita-06-38-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mahā-bāhu
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose arms are great. The bahuvrīhi stands as a form of address.
meaning: O mighty-armed

## vimūḍhaḥ
ids: gita-06-38-w9
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: muh
prefix: vi
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written vimūḍho before brahmaṇaḥ.
meaning: bewildered

## brahmaṇaḥ
ids: gita-06-38-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahman
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: singular
note: brahmaṇaḥ is genitive or ablative. It is the path of Brahman, so it is genitive.
meaning: of Brahman

## pathi
ids: gita-06-38-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pathin
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
meaning: on the path
