# gita-07-11

## balam
ids: gita-07-11-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bala
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written balaṃ before bala-vatām. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative, and it is what he says he is, so it is nominative.
meaning: strength (capacity and vigor)

## bala-vatām
ids: gita-07-11-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bala-vat
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
note: bala with the suffix -vat, “possessing strength.” It stands as a noun, and the line writes balavatāṃ before ca.
meaning: of the strong

## ca
ids: gita-07-11-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written cā, joined with the a of aham.
meaning: and

## aham
ids: gita-07-11-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written haṃ: the initial a joins ca, and the m is an anusvara before kāma-rāga-vivarjitam.
meaning: I

## kāma-rāga-vivarjitam
ids: gita-07-11-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kāma-rāga-vivarjita
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: vṛj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Free from desire and passion: vivarjita is the past participle of vṛj with the prefix vi. The neuter -am agrees with balam, so it is nominative.
meaning: free of desire and passion

## dharma-aviruddhaḥ
ids: gita-07-11-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: dharma-aviruddha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: rudh
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Written dharmāviruddho before bhūteṣu. aviruddha negates the past participle of rudh with the prefix vi: not opposed by scripture.
meaning: not opposed by scripture

## bhūteṣu
ids: gita-07-11-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūta
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: plural
root: bhū
note: The past participle of bhū, used as a noun, “a being.”
meaning: in beings

## kāmaḥ
ids: gita-07-11-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kāma
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kam
note: Written kāmo, and the a of asmi is dropped. It is what he says he is, and the subject “I” is in the verb.
meaning: desire

## asmi
ids: gita-07-11-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: The initial a is dropped after kāmaḥ, as the avagraha in kāmo' shows. The subject “I” is in the ending.
meaning: am

## bharata-ṛṣabha
ids: gita-07-11-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bharata-ṛṣabha
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Written bharatarṣabha, the vowels of the two members joining. “Bull of the Bharatas,” the best of them.
meaning: O best of the Bharatas
