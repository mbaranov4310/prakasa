# gita-07-13

## tribhiḥ
ids: gita-07-13-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: tri
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: Written tribhir before guṇa-mayaiḥ. It agrees with the states, so it is masculine.
meaning: by three

## guṇa-mayaiḥ
ids: gita-07-13-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: guṇa-maya
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: Written guṇamayair before bhāvaiḥ. guṇa with the suffix -maya, “made of,” and it agrees with the states, so it is masculine.
meaning: made of the qualities

## bhāvaiḥ
ids: gita-07-13-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhāva
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
root: bhū
note: The slice bhāvaire keeps the e of ebhiḥ.
meaning: by the states (attachment, aversion, delusion)

## ebhiḥ
ids: gita-07-13-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: Written bhiḥ: the initial e joins the r of bhāvaiḥ. Masculine, with the states.
meaning: by these

## sarvam
ids: gita-07-13-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The slice sarvami keeps the i of idam. The neuter -am agrees with jagat, so it is nominative.
meaning: all

## idam
ids: gita-07-13-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written daṃ: the i joins sarvam, and the m is an anusvara before jagat. The neuter -am agrees with jagat, so it is nominative.
meaning: this

## jagat
ids: gita-07-13-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jagat
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: gam
note: The present participle of gam, used as a noun. It is what does not know, so the form, which is also accusative, is nominative.
meaning: the world

## mohitam
ids: gita-07-13-w8
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: muh
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written mohitaṃ. The neuter -am describes the world, the subject, so it is nominative.
meaning: deluded

## na
ids: gita-07-13-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written nā, joined with the a of abhijānāti.
meaning: not

## abhijānāti
ids: gita-07-13-w10
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: jñā
prefix: abhi
class: krī
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Written bhijānāti: the initial a joins na.
meaning: knows

## mām
ids: gita-07-13-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The slice māme keeps the e of ebhyaḥ.
meaning: me

## ebhyaḥ
ids: gita-07-13-w12
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: plural
note: Written bhyaḥ: the e is on the m of mām. The form is shared with etad and is dative or ablative; with ebhiḥ and with param it is the ablative of idam.
meaning: than these

## param
ids: gita-07-13-w13
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: para
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The slice parama keeps the a of avyayam. The accusative -am is masculine or neuter, and it is said of him, so it is masculine.
meaning: beyond

## avyayam
ids: gita-07-13-w14
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: avyaya
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: a and vyaya, “not subject to decay.” Written vyayam: the initial a is on the m of param.
meaning: imperishable (free of birth and the other changes)
