## tasyāḥ
ids: gita-07-22-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: feminine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: The ending is genitive or ablative. It is the worship of that form, so it is genitive. The s at the start of the slice is the visarga of yuktaḥ. Written stasyā. The visarga is dropped before rādhanam, and a space stands between them.
meaning: of that (deity's form)

## rādhanam
ids: gita-07-22-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: rādhana
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: rādh
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he pursues, so it is accusative. Written rādhanamī. The ī of īhate is the vowel on the final m.
meaning: the worship

gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: Ordained, fashioned. Śaṅkara rejects a cut that would make the desires wholesome: desires are not wholesome for anyone. Joined to hi with no space. The printed join is vihitānhi.
meaning: ordained
