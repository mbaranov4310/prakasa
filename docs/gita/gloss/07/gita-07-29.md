# gita-07-29

## jarā-maraṇa-mokṣāya
ids: gita-07-29-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jarā-maraṇa-mokṣa
gender: masculine
case: dative
number: singular
root: muc
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Old age and death are a dvandva; release from them. mokṣa is from muc.
meaning: for release from old age and death

## mām
ids: gita-07-29-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māma; the m joins the ā of āśritya.
meaning: me

## āśritya
ids: gita-07-29-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: śri
prefix: ā
note: Absolutive. The ā is joined to the m of mām.
meaning: having resorted to

## kṛtsnam
ids: gita-07-29-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kṛtsna
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It agrees with adhyātmam, so it is accusative. Śaṅkara: samastam, the entire thing concerning the inner self. Written kṛtsna at the line break; the m joins the a of adhyātmam.
meaning: entire

number: singular
note: The reality whose concern is the inner self. The neuter -am is accusative, as what they know. The a is joined to the m of kṛtsnam.
meaning: what concerns the self
