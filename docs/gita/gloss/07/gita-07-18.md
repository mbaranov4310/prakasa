# gita-07-18

## udārāḥ
ids: gita-07-18-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: udāra
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
meaning: noble (the best of their kind)

## sarve
ids: gita-07-18-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The final e merges with the e of eva. Written sarva before it.
meaning: all

## eva
ids: gita-07-18-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written evai. The a merges with the e of ete.
meaning: indeed

## ete
ids: gita-07-18-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The initial e is merged into the ai of evai. Written te, and the line breaks after it.
meaning: these

## jñānī
ids: gita-07-18-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñānin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: jñānin, “one who has knowledge.”
meaning: the knower

## tu
ids: gita-07-18-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written tvā. The u merges with the ā of ātmā.
meaning: but

## ātmā
ids: gita-07-18-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The ā is written with tu as vā, and with eva as ai. The printed slice is tmai.
meaning: the self

## eva
ids: gita-07-18-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e merges with the ā of ātmā. The printed slice is va.
meaning: itself

## me
ids: gita-07-18-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. me is genitive or dative. Śaṅkara glosses it mama, so it is genitive.
meaning: my

## matam
ids: gita-07-18-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mata
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: man
note: Past participle of man, standing as a noun. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. Śaṅkara takes it as the view that is stated, so it is nominative. The preceding clause is what is viewed.
meaning: settled view

## āsthitaḥ
ids: gita-07-18-w11
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: ā
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Has resorted to, set out toward.
meaning: has set out (turned toward that goal)

## saḥ
ids: gita-07-18-w12
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The visarga is dropped before hi. Written sa.

## hi
ids: gita-07-18-w13
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Śaṅkara takes hi as since.
meaning: for

## yukta-ātmā
ids: gita-07-18-w14
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yukta-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose self is steadied. a and ā are written ā. The line breaks after it.
meaning: his mind steadied

## mām
ids: gita-07-18-w15
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māme. The e of eva is joined to the final m.
meaning: me

## eva
ids: gita-07-18-w16
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e is joined to mām. The a merges with the a of anuttamām, written vā.
meaning: alone

## anuttamām
ids: gita-07-18-w17
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: anuttama
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Nothing is higher than it, so it is the highest. Feminine with gatim. The initial a is merged into the preceding ā. Written nuttamāṃ.
meaning: the highest (with nothing beyond it)
