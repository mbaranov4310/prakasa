# gita-07-08

## rasaḥ
ids: gita-07-08-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: rasa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: ras
note: Written raso': the visarga becomes o before aham, and that a is dropped.
meaning: the taste (the essence)

## aham
ids: gita-07-08-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The a is dropped after raso'. The slice is hama: m takes the a of apsu.
meaning: I

## apsu
ids: gita-07-08-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ap
gender: feminine
case: locative
number: plural
note: The a stays on hama; the slice is psu.
meaning: in water
