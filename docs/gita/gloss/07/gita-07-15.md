## na
ids: gita-07-15-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## mām
ids: gita-07-15-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māṃ.
meaning: me

## duṣkṛtinaḥ
ids: gita-07-15-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: duṣkṛtin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Written duṣkṛtino before mūḍhāḥ. The suffix is -in, “one who does evil,” and the plural ending is nominative or accusative; they are who do not take refuge, so it is nominative.
meaning: evildoers

ids: gita-07-15-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mūḍha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: muh
note: The past participle of muh, used as a noun.
meaning: the deluded

root: pad

## nara-adhamāḥ
ids: gita-07-15-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: nara-adhama
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The lowest among men. Written narādhamāḥ, the vowels of the two members joining.
meaning: the lowest of men

## māyayā
ids: gita-07-15-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: māyā
gender: feminine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: mā
note: Its ā absorbs the initial a of the next word.
meaning: by the power of appearance

## apahṛta-jñānāḥ
ids: gita-07-15-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: apahṛta-jñāna
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Whose knowledge is stolen.” The initial a is absorbed into the ā of māyayā, with no avagraha, and the line writes pahṛtajñānā before āsuram.
meaning: those whose knowledge is stolen

## āsuram
ids: gita-07-15-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: āsura
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written āsuraṃ. The -am is masculine or neuter, and it is said of the nature, so it is masculine accusative.
meaning: demonic (harm, untruth, and the like)

judgment: obvious
stem: bhāva
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: bhū
note: The slice bhāvamā keeps the ā of āśritāḥ.
meaning: a nature

## āśritāḥ
ids: gita-07-15-w11

judgment: obvious
