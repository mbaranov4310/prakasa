# gita-07-25

## na
ids: gita-07-25-w1, gita-07-25-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Both are written nā. The first joins the a of aham, the second the a of abhijānāti.
meaning: not

## aham
ids: gita-07-25-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The a is joined to na.
meaning: I

## prakāśaḥ
ids: gita-07-25-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: prakāśa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kāś
note: From kāś with the prefix pra.
meaning: manifest

## sarvasya
ids: gita-07-25-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: The -asya is masculine or neuter. Śaṅkara reads it of the world, so it is masculine.
meaning: to everyone

## yoga-māyā-samāvṛtaḥ
ids: gita-07-25-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga-māyā-samāvṛta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: vṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Covered by yoga-māyā,” the arranging of the guṇas, which is itself māyā. samāvṛta is the past participle of vṛ with the prefixes sam and ā. It agrees with aham.
meaning: wrapped in the arranging of the guṇas (that arranging is itself māyā)

## mūḍhaḥ
ids: gita-07-25-w6
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: muh
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written mūḍho; the visarga becomes o and the a of ayam is dropped.
meaning: deluded

## ayam
ids: gita-07-25-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The a is dropped after mūḍhaḥ.
meaning: this

## abhijānāti
ids: gita-07-25-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: jñā
prefix: abhi
class: krī
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The a is joined to na.
meaning: knows

## lokaḥ
ids: gita-07-25-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: loka
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written loko before mām.
meaning: the world

## mām
ids: gita-07-25-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māma; the m joins the a of ajam.
meaning: me

## ajam
ids: gita-07-25-w12
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: aja
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: jan
note: Negated, “not born.” It agrees with mām, so the -am is masculine accusative.
meaning: unborn

## avyayam
ids: gita-07-25-w13
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: avyaya
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Without decay. It agrees with mām, so the -am is masculine accusative. The a is joined to the m of ajam.
meaning: imperishable
