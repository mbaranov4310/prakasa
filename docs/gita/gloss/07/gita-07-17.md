# gita-07-17

## teṣām
ids: gita-07-17-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
note: Written teṣāṃ. The four are the four kinds named in the previous verse.
meaning: of those four

## jñānī
ids: gita-07-17-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñānin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: jñānin, “one who has knowledge.”
meaning: the knower

## nitya-yuktaḥ
ids: gita-07-17-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nitya-yukta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: yukta is the past participle of yuj. The visarga is dropped before eka-bhaktiḥ.
meaning: always steadfast

## eka-bhaktiḥ
ids: gita-07-17-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: eka-bhakti
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose devotion (bhakti) is to one (eka) alone. The same form could be a feminine noun, “devotion to one.” Śaṅkara takes the man, masculine with the knower. The visarga becomes r before viśiṣyate.
meaning: whose devotion is to one alone (he sees no other to worship)

## viśiṣyate
ids: gita-07-17-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: śiṣ
prefix: vi
class: rudh
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: 7th class.
meaning: stands apart

## priyaḥ
ids: gita-07-17-w6, gita-07-17-w14
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: priya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: prī
note: The first is written priyo before hi. The second stands as priyaḥ.
meaning: dear

## hi
ids: gita-07-17-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Śaṅkara takes hi as since.
meaning: for

## jñāninaḥ
ids: gita-07-17-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñānin
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: The ending is genitive or ablative. It is the knower’s, so it is genitive. Written jñānino before the a of atyartham, and that a is dropped.
meaning: of the knower

## atyartham
ids: gita-07-17-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The neuter accusative singular of atyartha, used as an adverb. The initial a is dropped after jñānino.
meaning: exceedingly

## aham
ids: gita-07-17-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The initial a is written on the preceding m. Written ahaṃ before saḥ.
meaning: I

## saḥ
ids: gita-07-17-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The visarga is dropped before ca.
meaning: he

## ca
ids: gita-07-17-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## mama
ids: gita-07-17-w13
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Śaṅkara supplies ātmā: he is my very Self, and so dear to me.
meaning: of me
