# gita-07-10

## bījam
ids: gita-07-10-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bīja
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written bījaṃ before mām. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative, and it is what he is to be known as, so it is accusative.
meaning: the seed (the cause of sprouting)

## mām
ids: gita-07-10-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māṃ before sarva-bhūtānām.
meaning: me

## sarva-bhūtānām
ids: gita-07-10-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-bhūta
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: plural
root: bhū
compound: karmadhāraya
note: “All beings.” bhūta is the past participle of bhū. Written sarvabhūtānāṃ before viddhi.
meaning: of all beings

## viddhi
ids: gita-07-10-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: ad
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The 2nd class verb, “to know.” The subject “you” is not a separate word.
meaning: know

## pārtha
ids: gita-07-10-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pārtha
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
meaning: O Pārtha

## sanātanam
ids: gita-07-10-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sanātana
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The accusative -am is masculine or neuter. It is said of the seed, so it is neuter.
meaning: ancient

## buddhiḥ
ids: gita-07-10-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: buddhi
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: budh
note: Written buddhir before buddhi-matām. It is what he says he is, and the subject “I” is in asmi.
meaning: the intellect (the power of discernment)

## buddhi-matām
ids: gita-07-10-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: buddhi-mat
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
note: buddhi with the suffix -mat, “possessing intellect.” It stands as a noun, and the slice buddhimatāma keeps the a of asmi.
meaning: of those who have it

## asmi
ids: gita-07-10-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: Written smi. The initial a is on the m of buddhi-matām, and the subject “I” is in the ending.
meaning: am

## tejaḥ
ids: gita-07-10-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: tejas
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: tij
note: Written tejas before tejas-vinām. The ending is nominative or accusative, and it is what he says he is, so it is nominative.
meaning: boldness

## tejas-vinām
ids: gita-07-10-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: tejas-vin
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
note: tejas with the suffix -vin, “having boldness.” It stands as a noun, and the slice tejasvināma keeps the a of aham.
meaning: of those who have it

## aham
ids: gita-07-10-w12
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written ham. The initial a is on the m of tejas-vinām.
meaning: I
