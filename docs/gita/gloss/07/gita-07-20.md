# gita-07-20

## kāmaiḥ
ids: gita-07-20-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kāma
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
root: kam
note: The visarga becomes s before taiḥ. Written kāmais.
meaning: by desires

## taiḥ
ids: gita-07-20-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: This ending is masculine or neuter. It agrees with kāmaiḥ, so it is masculine. The visarga becomes s before the next taiḥ. Written tais.
meaning: by those

## taiḥ
ids: gita-07-20-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: This ending is masculine or neuter. It agrees with kāmaiḥ, so it is masculine. The visarga becomes r before hṛta-jñānāḥ. Written tair.
meaning: by those (sons, cattle, heaven, and the like)

## hṛta-jñānāḥ
ids: gita-07-20-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: hṛta-jñāna
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose knowledge has been seized. It stands as the people who resort to other deities.
meaning: whose knowledge has been seized

## prapadyante
ids: gita-07-20-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: pad
prefix: pra
class: div
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: 4th class. The -ya- is the class stem, not the passive. The a of anya-devatāḥ is dropped after it.
meaning: resort

## anya-devatāḥ
ids: gita-07-20-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: anya-devatā
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: plural
compound: karmadhāraya
note: The initial a is dropped after prapadyante. The ending -āḥ is nominative or accusative. It is what they resort to, so it is accusative.
meaning: to other deities
