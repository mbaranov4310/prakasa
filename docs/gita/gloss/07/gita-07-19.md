# gita-07-19

## bahūnām
ids: gita-07-19-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: bahu
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: plural
note: This ending is masculine or neuter. It describes births, so it is neuter. Written bahūnāṃ.
meaning: of many

## janmanām
ids: gita-07-19-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: janman
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: plural
root: jan
note: Joined to ante. Written janmanāma, and that a is the initial a of ante.
meaning: of births

## ante
ids: gita-07-19-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: anta
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: The initial a is written as the vowel of the preceding m. The printed slice is nte.
meaning: at the end

## jñānavān
ids: gita-07-19-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñānavat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: jñāna with the suffix -vat, “possessing knowledge.” Śaṅkara: the knowledge has reached ripeness. The adjective stands as a noun.
meaning: one whose knowledge has ripened

## mām
ids: gita-07-19-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māṃ.
meaning: me (Vāsudeva, the inner self)

## prapadyate
ids: gita-07-19-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: pad
prefix: pra
class: div
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: 4th class. The -ya- is the class stem, not the passive.
meaning: comes to

number: singular

## sarvam
ids: gita-07-19-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is the predicate “everything,” so it is nominative. Written sarvami: the i of iti joins the final m.
meaning: everything

## iti
ids: gita-07-19-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The initial i is joined to sarvam. Written ti.
meaning: thus

gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
