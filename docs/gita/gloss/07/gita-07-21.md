# gita-07-21

## yaḥ
ids: gita-07-21-w1, gita-07-21-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Each is written yo. The visarga becomes o before y.
meaning: whoever

## yām
ids: gita-07-21-w3, gita-07-21-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written yāṃ.
meaning: whichever

## tanum
ids: gita-07-21-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: tanu
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: tan
note: Written tanuṃ. yām is feminine, so this form is feminine. Śaṅkara: a deity’s form.
meaning: form (of a deity)

## bhaktaḥ
ids: gita-07-21-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhakta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhaj
note: Past participle of bhaj, standing as a noun.
meaning: a devotee

## śraddhayā
ids: gita-07-21-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śraddhā
gender: feminine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: dhā
note: śrad is not a prefix. The a of arcitum merges into the final ā.
meaning: with faith

## arcitum
ids: gita-07-21-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: arc
note: Infinitive, “to worship.” The initial a merges into the ā of śraddhayā. The i of icchati joins the final m. The printed slice is rcitumi.
meaning: to worship

## icchati
ids: gita-07-21-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: iṣ
class: tud
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: 6th class. The initial i is written on the preceding m. The printed slice is cchati.
meaning: wishes

## tasya
ids: gita-07-21-w10, gita-07-21-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: The ending is masculine or neuter. It is the person who wishes, so it is masculine. The second is written tasyā: its a merges with the a of acalām.
meaning: of that person

## acalām
ids: gita-07-21-w12
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: acala
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: cal
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Not moving, so unwavering. The initial a is merged into the ā of tasyā. Written calāṃ.
meaning: unwavering

## śraddhām
ids: gita-07-21-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śraddhā
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: dhā
note: śrad is not a prefix. Written śraddhāṃ.

## tām
ids: gita-07-21-w14
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written tāme. The e of eva joins the final m.
meaning: that

## eva
ids: gita-07-21-w15
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e is joined to tām. The printed slice is va.
meaning: very

## vidadhāmi
ids: gita-07-21-w16
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dhā
prefix: vi
class: hu
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: 3rd class. The i becomes y before aham. The printed slice is vidadhāmya.
meaning: I make firm

## aham
ids: gita-07-21-w17
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The initial a is the vowel on the preceding y. The printed slice is ham.
meaning: I
