# gita-07-27

## icchā-dveṣa-samutthena
ids: gita-07-27-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: icchā-dveṣa-samuttha
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: sthā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Risen from the dvandva of desire and aversion. samuttha is from sthā with the prefixes sam and ud. The instrumental -ena is masculine or neuter; it agrees with dvandva-mohena, so it is masculine.
meaning: risen from desire and aversion
