# gita-07-28

## yeṣām
ids: gita-07-28-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
note: The genitive plural is masculine or neuter. These are the people, so it is masculine.
meaning: whose

## tu
ids: gita-07-28-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written tv; the u becomes v before the a of anta-gatam.
meaning: but

## anta-gatam
ids: gita-07-28-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: anta-gata
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: gam
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: gata is the past participle of gam, gone to the end. The neuter -am agrees with pāpam, so it is nominative. The a is joined to tu.
meaning: nearly spent

## pāpam
ids: gita-07-28-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pāpa
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what is nearly spent, so it is nominative.
meaning: evil

## janānām
ids: gita-07-28-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jana
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
meaning: of people

## puṇya-karmaṇām
ids: gita-07-28-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: puṇya-karman
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those whose deeds (karman) are good (puṇya).” Masculine, agreeing with janānām.
meaning: whose deeds are good

## te
ids: gita-07-28-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: te is masculine or feminine. It is the same people, with janānām, so it is masculine.
meaning: they

## dvandva-moha-nirmuktāḥ
ids: gita-07-28-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: dvandva-moha-nirmukta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: muc
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: nirmukta is the past participle of muc with the prefix nis. It agrees with te. The visarga is not shown at the line break.
meaning: freed from the delusion of the pairs

## bhajante
ids: gita-07-28-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhaj
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: plural

## mām
ids: gita-07-28-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular

## dṛḍha-vratāḥ
ids: gita-07-28-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: dṛḍha-vrata
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those whose vow (vrata) is firm (dṛḍha).” It agrees with te.
