## anyat
ids: gita-07-07-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: anya
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The a is taken into nā; the slice is nyat. The form anyat is nominative or accusative. It describes kiṃcit, so it is nominative.

## kiṃcit
ids: gita-07-07-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: kim
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: kim with the particle cit. The form is nominative or accusative. It is what is not, so it is nominative. Written kiṃcida: t becomes d, and the a of asti stays on this slice.
meaning: anything
