# gita-07-06

## etat-yonīni
ids: gita-07-06-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: etat-yoni
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose source (yoni) is these (etat): the lower nature and the higher. Agrees with bhūtāni.
meaning: having these as their source (the two natures)

## bhūtāni
ids: gita-07-06-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūta
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: bhū
note: Past participle of bhū, standing as a noun. The plural -āni is nominative or accusative. The iti clause names them, so it is nominative.
meaning: beings

## sarvāṇi
ids: gita-07-06-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Agrees with bhūtāni, so the plural -āni is nominative. Written sarvāṇī, joining the i of iti.
meaning: all

## iti
ids: gita-07-06-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The i is taken into sarvāṇī. Written tyu, joining the u of upadhāraya.
meaning: thus

## upadhāraya
ids: gita-07-06-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dhṛ
prefix: upa
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: Causative of dhṛ. The subject is you, shown by the person, and not named; the slice is padhāraya.
meaning: understand

## aham
ids: gita-07-06-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written ahaṃ.
meaning: I

## kṛtsnasya
ids: gita-07-06-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kṛtsna
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Agrees with jagataḥ.
meaning: of the whole

## jagataḥ
ids: gita-07-06-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jagat
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: singular
root: gam
note: jagataḥ is genitive or ablative. It is the world of which I am the origin, so it is genitive.
meaning: world

## prabhavaḥ
ids: gita-07-06-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prabhava
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhū
note: From pra and bhū, the arising.
meaning: the origin

## pralayaḥ
ids: gita-07-06-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pralaya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: lī
note: From pra and lī, the dissolution. Written pralayas before tathā.
meaning: the dissolution

## tathā
ids: gita-07-06-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It sets the dissolution beside the origin.
meaning: likewise
