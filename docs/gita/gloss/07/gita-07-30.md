# gita-07-30

## sa-adhibhūta-adhidaivam
ids: gita-07-30-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sa-adhibhūta-adhidaiva
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: sa- means together with the dvandva of the two things named here. The -am agrees with mām, so it is masculine accusative. sa joins the a of adhi.
meaning: together with what pertains to creatures and to the gods

## mām
ids: gita-07-30-w2, gita-07-30-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Both are written māṃ.
meaning: me

## sa-adhiyajñam
ids: gita-07-30-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sa-adhiyajña
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Together with what pertains to sacrifice.” The -am agrees with mām, so it is masculine accusative. sa joins the a of adhi.
meaning: together with what pertains to sacrifice

## ca
ids: gita-07-30-w4, gita-07-30-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## ye
ids: gita-07-30-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: ye is masculine or feminine. These are the people, so it is masculine.
meaning: who

## viduḥ
ids: gita-07-30-w6, gita-07-30-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: The present of vid has the endings of the perfect. The second is written vidur before yukta-cetasaḥ.
meaning: know

## prayāṇa-kāle
ids: gita-07-30-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prayāṇa-kāla
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The time of going forth. Śaṅkara reads it as the time of death. The a of api is dropped after it.
meaning: at the time of departure (at death)

## api
ids: gita-07-30-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The a is dropped after prayāṇa-kāle.
meaning: even

## te
ids: gita-07-30-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: te is masculine or feminine. It answers ye, the same people, so it is masculine.
meaning: they

## yukta-cetasaḥ
ids: gita-07-30-w13
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yukta-cetas
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those whose mind (cetas) is steadied (yukta).” The ending -asaḥ agrees with te, so it is nominative plural.
meaning: whose minds are steadied
