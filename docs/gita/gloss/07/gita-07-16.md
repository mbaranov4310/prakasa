# gita-07-16

## catur-vidhāḥ
ids: gita-07-16-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: catur-vidha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Of four kinds: whose sorts are four. The visarga is dropped before bhajante.
meaning: of four kinds

## bhajante
ids: gita-07-16-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhaj
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: plural
meaning: worship

ids: gita-07-16-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māṃ.
meaning: me

## janāḥ
ids: gita-07-16-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jana
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: jan

## su-kṛtinaḥ
ids: gita-07-16-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: su-kṛtin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: karmadhāraya
note: su “good” with kṛtin “one who acts.” Written sukṛtino before the a of arjuna, and that a is dropped.
meaning: those of good deeds

## arjuna
ids: gita-07-16-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: arjuna
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
note: The initial a is dropped after sukṛtino, and the word is written joined to it.
meaning: O Arjuna

## ārtaḥ
ids: gita-07-16-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ārta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: ṛ
note: Past participle of ṛ with ā, “fallen into distress,” standing as a noun for one of the four kinds. Written ārto before jijñāsuḥ.
meaning: the afflicted (seized by distress)

## jijñāsuḥ
ids: gita-07-16-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jijñāsu
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: jñā
note: Desiderative noun from jñā, “one who wishes to know.” It names one of the four kinds. Written jijñāsur before artha-arthī.
meaning: one who wishes to know (the Lord's truth)

## artha-arthī
ids: gita-07-16-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: artha-arthin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Seeker (arthin) of wealth (artha). It names one of the four kinds.
meaning: one who seeks wealth

## jñānī
ids: gita-07-16-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñānin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: jñānin, “one who has knowledge.” It names one of the four kinds.
meaning: the knower (of the Lord's truth)

## ca
ids: gita-07-16-w11

judgment: obvious

## bharata-ṛṣabha
ids: gita-07-16-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bharata-ṛṣabha
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
