# gita-07-02

## jñānam
ids: gita-07-02-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: jñā
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what I shall declare, so it is accusative.
meaning: knowledge

## te
ids: gita-07-02-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: dative
number: singular
note: te is genitive or dative. It is who I declare this to, so it is dative, and yuṣmad has no gender. Written te' before aham, and that a is dropped.
meaning: to you

## aham
ids: gita-07-02-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The a is dropped after te; the slice is haṃ.
meaning: I

## sa-vijñānam
ids: gita-07-02-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sa-vijñāna
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Together with vijñāna, direct experience. The neuter -am agrees with jñānam, so it is accusative. Written savijñānami, joining the i of idam.
meaning: together with direct experience

## idam
ids: gita-07-02-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The i joins the m of sa-vijñānam; the slice is daṃ. It agrees with jñānam, so the neuter -am is accusative.
meaning: this

## vakṣyāmi
ids: gita-07-02-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
class: ad
tense: simple future
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: The i becomes y before aśeṣataḥ, written vakṣyāmy.
meaning: shall declare

## aśeṣataḥ
ids: gita-07-02-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: a- negates śeṣa, remainder, and -tas makes the adverb. The a is taken into vakṣyāmy; the slice is śeṣataḥ.
meaning: in full

## yat
ids: gita-07-02-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written yaj before jñātvā. The form is nominative or accusative. It is what you have known, so it is accusative.
meaning: which

## jñātvā
ids: gita-07-02-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: jñā
note: Absolutive of jñā.
meaning: having known

## na
ids: gita-07-02-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written ne before iha.
meaning: not

## iha
ids: gita-07-02-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The i joins na; the slice is ha.
meaning: here

## bhūyaḥ
ids: gita-07-02-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Adverb, further, again. Written bhūyo' before anyat, and that a is dropped.
meaning: further

## anyat
ids: gita-07-02-w13
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: anya
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Another thing. The form anyat is nominative or accusative. It is what remains, so it is nominative. The a is dropped after bhūyaḥ, and t becomes j before jñātavyam; the slice is nyaj.
meaning: other

## jñātavyam
ids: gita-07-02-w14
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: jñā
participle: potential (-tavya)
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It describes anyat, what remains, so it is nominative. Written jñātavyama, taking the a of avaśiṣyate.
meaning: to be known

## avaśiṣyate
ids: gita-07-02-w15
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: śiṣ
prefix: ava
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Passive of śiṣ with ava: it is left, and so it remains. The slice is vaśiṣyate.
meaning: remains
