# gita-07-09

## puṇyaḥ
ids: gita-07-09-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: puṇya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written puṇyo before gandhaḥ.

## gandhaḥ
ids: gita-07-09-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: gandha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: gandh
note: It is what he says he is. “Am” is understood, and the subject “I” is not a separate word.
meaning: the smell
