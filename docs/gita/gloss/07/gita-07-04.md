# gita-07-04

## bhūmiḥ
ids: gita-07-04-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūmi
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written bhūmirā: the visarga becomes r before āpaḥ.
meaning: earth (the subtle element, not the gross)

## āpaḥ
ids: gita-07-04-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ap
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Nominative plural of ap. The ā joins bhūmiḥ. Written po': the visarga becomes o before analaḥ, and that a is dropped.
meaning: water (the subtle element, not the gross)

## analaḥ
ids: gita-07-04-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: anala
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The a is dropped after āpaḥ. The slice is nalo, the visarga becoming o before vāyuḥ.
meaning: fire (the subtle element, not the gross)

## vāyuḥ
ids: gita-07-04-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: vāyu
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: vā
meaning: air (the subtle element, not the gross)

## kham
ids: gita-07-04-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kha
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is named in the list of eight, so it is nominative. Written khaṃ.
meaning: space (the subtle element, not the gross)

## manaḥ
ids: gita-07-04-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: manas
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: man
note: The form manaḥ is nominative or accusative. It is named in the list, so it is nominative. Written mano before buddhiḥ.
meaning: mind (standing for the I-sense, its cause)

## buddhiḥ
ids: gita-07-04-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: buddhi
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: budh
note: Written buddhire: the visarga becomes r, and the e of eva stays on this slice.
meaning: intellect (standing for the great principle, its cause)

## eva

judgment: obvious
note: The e joins buddhiḥ; the slice is va.

## ca
ids: gita-07-04-w9

## ahaṃkāraḥ
ids: gita-07-04-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ahaṃkāra
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: aham, I, and kāra, making. The visarga is dropped before iti; the slice is ahaṃkāra.
meaning: the thought "I" (the unmanifest, joined with ignorance)

## iti
ids: gita-07-04-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Closes the list. Written itī, joining the i of iyam.
meaning: thus

## iyam
ids: gita-07-04-w12
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Feminine of idam, agreeing with prakṛtiḥ. The i joins iti; the slice is yaṃ.
meaning: this

## me
ids: gita-07-04-w13
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: me is genitive or dative. It is whose nature this is, so it is genitive, and asmad has no gender.
meaning: my

## bhinnā
ids: gita-07-04-w14
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: bhid
participle: past
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Agrees with prakṛtiḥ.

## prakṛtiḥ
ids: gita-07-04-w15
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prakṛti
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: From pra and kṛ. Written prakṛtira: the visarga becomes r before aṣṭadhā, and the next slice is ṣṭadhā.
meaning: nature (my power of appearance)

## aṣṭadhā
ids: gita-07-04-w16
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: In eight ways, with the suffix -dhā. The a stays on prakṛtira; the slice is ṣṭadhā.
meaning: eightfold
