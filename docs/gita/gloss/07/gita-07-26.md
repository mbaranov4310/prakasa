# gita-07-26

## veda
ids: gita-07-26-w1
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: The present of vid has the endings of the perfect. veda is 1st or 3rd singular; aham is the subject, so it is 1st. Śaṅkara glosses jāne. Written vedā before aham.
meaning: I know

## aham
ids: gita-07-26-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The a is joined to veda.
meaning: I

## sam-atītāni
ids: gita-07-26-w3
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: i
prefix: sam-ati
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
note: The neuter -āni is nominative or accusative. It agrees with bhūtāni, so it is accusative.
meaning: those that have passed
