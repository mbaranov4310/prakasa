# gita-07-05

## aparā
ids: gita-07-05-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: apara
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Agrees with iyam. Written apare: ā and the i of iyam join as e.
meaning: lower (impure, the nature of bondage)

## iyam
ids: gita-07-05-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The i is taken into apare. Written yami, joining the i of itaḥ.
meaning: this

## itaḥ
ids: gita-07-05-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Than this lower nature. The i joins iyam, and the visarga becomes s before tu; the slice is tas.
meaning: than this

## tu
ids: gita-07-05-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The u joins the a of anyām; the slice is tva.
meaning: but

## anyām
ids: gita-07-05-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: anya
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Agrees with prakṛtim. The a is taken into tva; the slice is nyāṃ.
meaning: other

## prakṛtim
ids: gita-07-05-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prakṛti
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: From pra and kṛ. Written prakṛtiṃ.
meaning: nature

## viddhi
ids: gita-07-05-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: ad
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is you, shown by the person, and not named in the verse.
meaning: know

## me
ids: gita-07-05-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: me is genitive or dative. It is whose nature this is, so it is genitive, and asmad has no gender.
meaning: my

## parām
ids: gita-07-05-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: para
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Agrees with prakṛtim.
meaning: higher (pure, my own self)

## jīva-bhūtām
ids: gita-07-05-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: jīva-bhūta
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: bhū
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Having become the jīva. Agrees with prakṛtim. Written jīvabhūtāṃ.
meaning: become the life-principle (the knower of the field)

## mahā-bāho

judgment: obvious
stem: mahā-bāhu
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: mahā, great, and bāhu, arm.
meaning: O mighty-armed

## yayā
ids: gita-07-05-w12
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: feminine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: Written yaye: ā and the i of idam join as e.

## idam
ids: gita-07-05-w13
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular

## dhāryate
ids: gita-07-05-w14
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dhṛ
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Causative of dhṛ, in the passive.
meaning: is held up

## jagat
ids: gita-07-05-w15
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jagat
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: gam
note: From gam, what moves. The form jagat is nominative or accusative. It is what is held, so it is nominative.
meaning: the world
