# gita-01-23

## yotsyamānān
ids: gita-01-23-w1
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: yudh
participle: future
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: Future participle, middle, of yudh, “going to fight.” The future stem is yotsya-. It stands as the men he will look at. The n joins avekṣe.
meaning: about to fight

## avekṣe
ids: gita-01-23-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: īkṣ
prefix: ava
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: The a of ava joins the n of yotsyamānān. The e drops the a of aham. Present in form, of what he will do once the chariot is placed.
meaning: I will look at

## aham
ids: gita-01-23-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The a is dropped after avekṣe.
meaning: I

## ye
ids: gita-01-23-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Before the e of ete, the e of ye becomes a, so the line shows ya.
meaning: those who

## ete
ids: gita-01-23-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: ete and atra join, and the a of atra is dropped.
meaning: these

## atra
ids: gita-01-23-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The a is dropped after ete.
meaning: here

## samāgatāḥ
ids: gita-01-23-w7
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: gam
prefix: sam-ā
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: “Come together.” There is no finite verb in the relative clause. “Are” is understood.
meaning: have come

## durbuddheḥ
ids: gita-01-23-w9

gender: masculine

number: singular

## priya-cikīrṣavaḥ
ids: gita-01-23-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: priya-cikīrṣu
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: kṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: cikīrṣu is the desiderative adjective of kṛ, “wishing to do.” With priya, “wishing to do what is dear,” and so “wishing to please.” Nominative plural of the stem in -u.
meaning: wishing to please
