# gita-01-37

## tasmāt
ids: gita-01-37-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: The ablative singular is the same in masculine and neuter. It points back to what he has just said, so it is neuter. “From that” is “therefore.” Written tasmān before na.
meaning: therefore

## na
ids: gita-01-37-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Its a and the a of arhāḥ join into ā: nārhāḥ.
meaning: not

## arhāḥ
ids: gita-01-37-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: arha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: arh
note: arha means “fit.” There is no finite verb in this line. “Are” is understood: we are not fit to kill. The initial a joins na.
meaning: we should

## vayam
ids: gita-01-37-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: plural
note: asmad has no gender. The same we is the subject of syāma in the next line.
meaning: we

## hantum
ids: gita-01-37-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: han
note: An infinitive in -tum.
meaning: kill

## sa-bāndhavān
ids: gita-01-37-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sa-bāndhava
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi

meaning: along with their kinsmen
