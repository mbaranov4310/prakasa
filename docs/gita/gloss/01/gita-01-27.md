## suhṛdaḥ
ids: gita-01-27-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: suhṛd
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural

## senayoḥ
ids: gita-01-27-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: senā
gender: feminine
case: locative
number: dual
note: senayoḥ is genitive or locative dual. The line places the men in both armies, so it is locative. Written senayor before ubhayoḥ: the visarga after o becomes r before a vowel.
meaning: of the armies
