# gita-01-33

## yeṣām
ids: gita-01-33-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
note: Written yeṣām before arthe: the m meets the following a.
meaning: those for whose

## arthe
ids: gita-01-33-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: artha
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: The locative of artha, “for the sake of.” Its initial a joins the m of yeṣām.
meaning: sake

## kāṅkṣitam
ids: gita-01-33-w3
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: kāṅkṣ
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It agrees with rājyam, the thing that was desired, so it is nominative. bhogāḥ and sukhāni are desired too; the participle stays neuter singular with rājyam. There is no finite verb.
meaning: was desired

## naḥ
ids: gita-01-33-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: plural
note: naḥ is dative or genitive, not instrumental. With the past participle the genitive is the doer: desired by us. asmad has no gender. Written no before rājyam.
meaning: by us

## rājyam
ids: gita-01-33-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: rājya
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what was desired, so it is nominative. Written rājyaṃ before bhogāḥ.
meaning: a kingdom

## bhogāḥ
ids: gita-01-33-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhoga
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: bhuj
note: Nominative, a further thing that was desired. The participle kāṅkṣitam stays neuter singular with rājyam. “Enjoyment,” from bhuj.
meaning: enjoyments

## sukhāni
ids: gita-01-33-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sukha
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The neuter plural -āṇi is nominative or accusative. They are among what was desired, with rājyam, so it is nominative.
meaning: pleasures
