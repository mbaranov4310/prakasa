# gita-01-45

## aho
ids: gita-01-45-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: An exclamation.
meaning: alas

## bata
ids: gita-01-45-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: An exclamation.
meaning: ah

## mahat
ids: gita-01-45-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mahat
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter nominative and accusative singular of mahat is mahat. It agrees with pāpam, what they are resolved to commit, so it is accusative. Written mahat before pāpam.
meaning: great

## pāpam
ids: gita-01-45-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pāpa
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what they are resolved to commit, so it is accusative. Written pāpaṃ before kartum.
meaning: a sin

## kartum
ids: gita-01-45-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
note: An infinitive in -tum.
meaning: to commit

## vyavasitāḥ
ids: gita-01-45-w6
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: so
prefix: vi-ava
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Past participle of so with vi-ava, “resolved.” It agrees with vayam. There is no finite verb. The visarga is not written before vayam.
meaning: resolved

## vayam
ids: gita-01-45-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: plural
note: asmad has no gender.
meaning: we

## yat
ids: gita-01-45-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The neuter accusative of yad, used as an indeclinable, “inasmuch as.” Written yad before rājya: final t becomes d before the voiced r.
meaning: in that

## rājya-sukha-lobhena
ids: gita-01-45-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: rājya-sukha-lobha
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: lubh
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Greed (lobha, from lubh) for the joy (sukha) of a kingdom (rājya).” Its initial r joins the d of yat.
meaning: by greed for the joys of a kingdom

## hantum
ids: gita-01-45-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: han
note: An infinitive in -tum.
meaning: to kill

## svajanam
ids: gita-01-45-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: svajana
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: sva, “one’s own,” and jana, “people.” Written svajanam before udyatāḥ: the m meets the u.
meaning: our own people

## udyatāḥ
ids: gita-01-45-w12
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: yam
prefix: ut
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Past participle of yam with ut, “raised to an act,” so “set.” It agrees with vayam. Its initial u joins the m of svajanam.
meaning: set
