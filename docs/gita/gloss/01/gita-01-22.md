# gita-01-22

## yāvat
ids: gita-01-22-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written yāvad before etān. “While.” The clause says why the chariot is to be placed in 1.21.
meaning: while

## etān
ids: gita-01-22-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: The e joins yāvat, so the line shows yāvad etān.
meaning: these men

## nirīkṣe
ids: gita-01-22-w3
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: īkṣ
prefix: nis
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: nis before īkṣ becomes nir. The e drops the a of aham.
meaning: I look over

## aham
ids: gita-01-22-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The a is dropped after nirīkṣe.
meaning: I

## yoddhu-kāmān
ids: gita-01-22-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yoddhu-kāma
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: yoddhum, the infinitive of yudh, with kāma, “desire”: “whose desire is to fight.” The n joins avasthitān.
meaning: eager to fight

## avasthitān
ids: gita-01-22-w6
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: ava
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: “Standing.” The a of ava joins yoddhu-kāmān. It agrees with etān.
meaning: standing

## kaiḥ
ids: gita-01-22-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: kim
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: The men he would fight, so masculine. Written kair before mayā. Instrumental with saha.
meaning: with whom

## mayā
ids: gita-01-22-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. With the potential participle, the instrumental is the doer.
meaning: by me

## saha
ids: gita-01-22-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: together

## yoddhavyam
ids: gita-01-22-w10
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: yudh
participle: potential (-tavya)
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Impersonal. The neuter -am is nominative. “It is to be fought.” There is no separate subject. Written yoddhavyam before asmin.
meaning: it is to be fought

## asmin
ids: gita-01-22-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: The a joins yoddhavyam and is not written again. Masculine, with raṇa-samudyame.
meaning: in this

## raṇa-samudyame
ids: gita-01-22-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: raṇa-samudyama
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
root: yam
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: samudyama, “undertaking,” is from sam-ud-yam. “The undertaking of battle.”
meaning: undertaking of battle
