## vākyam
ids: gita-01-21-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: vākya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: vac
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he spoke, so it is accusative. The i of idam joins it.
meaning: word

## idam
ids: gita-01-21-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The i stands with vākyam, and the ā of āha joins the m. It agrees with vākyam.
meaning: this

## āha
ids: gita-01-21-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: ah
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: āha is the perfect of ah, “to say,” a root used in the perfect. The ā joins idam, and only ha is left in the line.
meaning: he spoke

## mahīpate
ids: gita-01-21-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mahīpati
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: mahī, “earth,” with pati, “lord.” Vocative. The pada is not hyphenated.
meaning: O lord of the earth

## senayoḥ
ids: gita-01-21-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: senā
gender: feminine
case: genitive
number: dual
note: The dual -ayoḥ is genitive or locative. madhye takes the genitive, “in the middle of,” so it is genitive. The ḥ becomes r before ubhayoḥ, and the u of ubhaya joins it.
meaning: of the two armies

## ubhayoḥ
ids: gita-01-21-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ubhaya
gender: feminine
case: genitive
number: dual
note: The dual -ayoḥ is genitive or locative. It agrees with senayoḥ, so it is genitive. The u joins senayoḥ. Written ubhayor before madhye.
meaning: of both
