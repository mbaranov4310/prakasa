# gita-01-31

## nimittāni
ids: gita-01-31-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: nimitta
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
note: The neuter plural -āṇi is nominative or accusative. The omens are what he sees, so it is accusative.
meaning: omens

## ca
ids: gita-01-31-w2, gita-01-31-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## paśyāmi
ids: gita-01-31-w3
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dṛś
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: The subject is the unstated “I.”
meaning: I see

## viparītāni
ids: gita-01-31-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: viparīta
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
root: i
prefix: vi-pari
note: The past participle of i with vi-pari, “turned the wrong way,” here “adverse.” It agrees with nimittāni.
meaning: of disaster

## śreyaḥ
ids: gita-01-31-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śreyas
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: A comparative in -īyas, “the better thing,” standing as a noun. The neuter śreyaḥ is nominative or accusative. It is what he does not see, so it is accusative. Written śreyo before the a of anupaśyāmi, and that a is dropped.
meaning: any good

## anupaśyāmi
ids: gita-01-31-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dṛś
prefix: anu
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: The a of anu is dropped after śreyo. The subject is the unstated “I.”
meaning: I see

## hatvā
ids: gita-01-31-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: han
note: An absolutive in -tvā, “having killed,” the act in which he sees no good.
meaning: in killing

## svajanam
ids: gita-01-31-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: svajana
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: sva, “one’s own,” and jana, “people.” Written svajanam before āhave: the m meets that ā.
meaning: my own people

## āhave
ids: gita-01-31-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: āhava
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
root: hve
prefix: ā
note: “A call to fight,” from hve with ā. Its initial ā joins the m of svajanam.
meaning: in battle
