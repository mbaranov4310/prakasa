# gita-01-30

## gāṇḍīvam
ids: gita-01-30-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: gāṇḍīva
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. The bow is what slips, so it is nominative. Written gāṇḍīvaṃ before sraṃsate.
meaning: the Gandiva
