# gita-01-24

## saṃjaya
ids: gita-01-24-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃjaya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The visarga of the nominative drops before the u of uvāca. The print spells the name saṃjaya, with anusvāra.
meaning: Sanjaya

## uvāca
ids: gita-01-24-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
meaning: said

## evam
ids: gita-01-24-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The u of uktaḥ joins it: evamuktaḥ.
meaning: in this way

## uktaḥ
ids: gita-01-24-w4
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: vac
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: “Addressed.” The u joins evam, and the visarga becomes o before hṛṣīkeśaḥ.
meaning: addressed

## hṛṣīkeśaḥ
ids: gita-01-24-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: hṛṣīkeśa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: He drew the chariot up and is the subject of uvāca in 1.25. Written hṛṣīkeśo before guḍākeśena.

number: singular

## senayoḥ
ids: gita-01-24-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: senā
gender: feminine
case: genitive
number: dual
note: The dual -ayoḥ is genitive or locative. madhye takes the genitive, “in the middle of,” so it is genitive. The ḥ becomes r before ubhayoḥ, and the u of ubhaya joins it.
meaning: of the two armies

## ubhayoḥ
ids: gita-01-24-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ubhaya
gender: feminine
case: genitive
number: dual
note: The dual -ayoḥ is genitive or locative. It agrees with senayoḥ, so it is genitive. The u joins senayoḥ. Written ubhayor before madhye.
meaning: of both

## madhye
ids: gita-01-24-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: madhya
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
note: “In the middle,” and so between.
meaning: between

## sthāpayitvā
ids: gita-01-24-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
note: Causative of sthā. An absolutive in -tvā. It leans on uvāca in 1.25.
meaning: having drawn up

## ratha-uttamam
ids: gita-01-24-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ratha-uttama
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “The best (uttama) of chariots (ratha).” Accusative, what he drew up.
meaning: that finest of chariots
