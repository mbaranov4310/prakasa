# gita-01-36

## nihatya
ids: gita-01-36-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: han
prefix: ni
note: An absolutive in -ya, “having killed,” the act in which the joy would lie.
meaning: in killing

## dhārtarāṣṭrān
ids: gita-01-36-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dhārtarāṣṭra
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: Patronymic, “son of Dhṛtarāṣṭra.” Written dhārtarāṣṭrān before naḥ.
meaning: Dhritarashtra's sons

## naḥ
ids: gita-01-36-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: dative
number: plural
note: naḥ is dative or genitive. With prītiḥ the sense is “for us,” so it is dative. asmad has no gender.
meaning: for us

## kā
ids: gita-01-36-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious

gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with prītiḥ: what joy.
meaning: what

## pāpam
ids: gita-01-36-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pāpa
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. Sin is what would come upon us, so it is nominative. Written pāpam before eva.
meaning: sin

## eva
ids: gita-01-36-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Its final a and the ā of āśrayet join into ā: evāśrayet.
meaning: alone

## āśrayet
ids: gita-01-36-w10
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: śri
prefix: ā
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Potential of śri with ā, “would resort to,” and so “would come upon.” Its ā joins the final a of eva. Written āśrayed before asmān: final t becomes d before the vowel.
meaning: would come upon

## hatvā

## etān

stem: etad

## ātatāyinaḥ
ids: gita-01-36-w14
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātatāyin
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: A stem in -in. The accusative plural has the same shape as the nominative plural. They are the men who would be killed, so it is accusative.
meaning: attackers
