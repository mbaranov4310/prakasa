# gita-01-35

## etān
ids: gita-01-35-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: Written etān before na.
meaning: these men

## na
ids: gita-01-35-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## hantum
ids: gita-01-35-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: han
note: An infinitive in -tum. Written hantum before icchāmi: the i of icchāmi joins the m.
meaning: to kill

## icchāmi
ids: gita-01-35-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: iṣ
class: tud
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: The subject is the unstated “I.” Its i joins the m of hantum.
meaning: I want

## ghnataḥ
ids: gita-01-35-w5
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: han
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: Present participle of han. ghnataḥ is accusative plural or genitive singular. It agrees with the plural etān, the men he will not kill even while they kill, so it is accusative plural. The one they would kill is not a word in the verse. Written ghnato before the a of api, and that a is dropped.
meaning: even if they kill me

## api
ids: gita-01-35-w6, gita-01-35-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The first has lost its a after ghnato.
meaning: even

## hetoḥ
ids: gita-01-35-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: hetu
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: hetoḥ is the genitive of hetu, and the same form is the ablative. With the genitive trailokya-rājyasya it is the genitive “for the sake of.”
meaning: for the sake

## kim
ids: gita-01-35-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: kim
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: kim nu with a “for the sake of” phrase means “how much less.” The neuter kim is nominative or accusative. It is the question, so it is nominative. There is no verb. The wish not to kill is understood from the first line. Written kiṃ before nu.
meaning: how much less

## nu
ids: gita-01-35-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: indeed

## mahī-kṛte
ids: gita-01-35-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mahī-kṛta
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
root: kṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: kṛte, the locative of kṛta, at the end of a compound means “for the sake of.” It answers the kim of this line.
meaning: for the sake of the earth
