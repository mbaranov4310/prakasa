# gita-01-20

## atha
ids: gita-01-20-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: atha opens what he did next.
meaning: then

## vyavasthitān
ids: gita-01-20-w2
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: vi-ava
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: “Stationed, drawn up.” It agrees with dhārtarāṣṭrān.
meaning: drawn up

## dṛṣṭvā
ids: gita-01-20-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: dṛś
note: An absolutive in -tvā.
meaning: seeing

## dhārtarāṣṭrān
ids: gita-01-20-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dhārtarāṣṭra
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: The n joins kapi-dhvajaḥ.
meaning: Dhritarashtra's men

## kapi-dhvajaḥ
ids: gita-01-20-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kapi-dhvaja
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “He whose banner (dhvaja) is a monkey (kapi).” The same man as pāṇḍavaḥ.
meaning: he whose banner is a monkey

## pravṛtte
ids: gita-01-20-w6
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: vṛt
prefix: pra
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: “Begun.” Locative, with śastra-saṃpāte. The two together are a locative absolute.
meaning: when it had begun

## śastra-saṃpāte
ids: gita-01-20-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śastra-saṃpāta
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
root: pat
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “The clash (saṃpāta) of weapons (śastra).” saṃpāta is from sam-pat. Locative absolute: when the clash had begun.
meaning: the clash of weapons

## dhanuḥ
ids: gita-01-20-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dhanus
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -uḥ of dhanus is nominative or accusative. It is what he took up, so it is accusative. Written dhanur before udyamya.
meaning: his bow

## udyamya
ids: gita-01-20-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: yam
prefix: ud
note: An absolutive in -ya. The u joins dhanuḥ.
meaning: having taken up

## pāṇḍavaḥ
ids: gita-01-20-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pāṇḍava
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: He is the one who saw, took up the bow, and spoke the word in 1.21.
meaning: the son of Pandu
