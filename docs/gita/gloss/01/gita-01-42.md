# gita-01-42

## saṃkaraḥ
ids: gita-01-42-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃkara
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṛ
prefix: sam
note: “A mixing,” from kṛ with sam. It is the mixing of the orders in 1.41. The print writes saṃ with anusvāra. Written saṃkaro before narakāya.
meaning: the mixing

## narakāya
ids: gita-01-42-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: naraka
gender: masculine
case: dative
number: singular
note: Dative of the hell the mixing leads to. There is no verb in this line. “Is” is understood. Its ā and the e of eva join into ai: narakāyaiva.
meaning: to hell

## eva
ids: gita-01-42-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Its e joins the ā of narakāya.
meaning: indeed

## kula-ghnānām
ids: gita-01-42-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kula-ghna
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
root: han
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: ghna, from han, “killing.” “Those who destroy (ghna) the family (kula).” The ending -ānām is genitive plural. It names the people for whom the mixing means hell.
meaning: for the destroyers of the family

## kulasya
ids: gita-01-42-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kula
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: singular
note: The family as well, joined to the destroyers by ca.
meaning: for the family

## lupta-piṇḍa-udaka-kriyāḥ
ids: gita-01-42-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: lupta-piṇḍa-udaka-kriya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those for whom the rites (kriyā) of the rice-ball (piṇḍa) and of water (udaka) have lapsed (lupta, past participle of lup).” piṇḍa and udaka are a dvandva inside it. The last member is feminine kriyā; in the masculine the stem ends in -a, and the nominative plural is -āḥ. It agrees with pitaraḥ.
meaning: their offerings of rice and water ceased
