# gita-01-39

## katham
ids: gita-01-39-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written kathaṃ before na.
meaning: why

## na
ids: gita-01-39-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## jñeyam
ids: gita-01-39-w3
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: jñā
participle: potential (-ya)
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The potential participle in -ya, “to be known.” The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. The sentence is impersonal, “it is to be known,” so it is nominative. Written jñeyam before asmābhiḥ.
meaning: to be known

## asmābhiḥ
ids: gita-01-39-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: asmad has no gender. With the potential participle the instrumental is the one who should know.
meaning: by us

## pāpāt
ids: gita-01-39-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pāpa
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: Written pāpād before asmān.
meaning: from this sin

## asmān
ids: gita-01-39-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: plural
note: asmad has no gender. Written asmān before nivartitum.
meaning: ourselves

## nivartitum
ids: gita-01-39-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: vṛt
prefix: ni
note: An infinitive in -tum, “to turn back.” With asmān, “to turn ourselves back.”
meaning: to turn back

## kula-kṣaya-kṛtam
ids: gita-01-39-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kula-kṣaya-kṛta
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Done (kṛta, past participle of kṛ) by the destruction (kṣaya, from kṣi) of a family (kula).” The ending -am agrees with doṣam, so it is masculine accusative.
meaning: done by destroying a family

## doṣam
ids: gita-01-39-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: doṣa
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: duṣ
note: Written doṣaṃ before prapaśyadbhiḥ.
meaning: the evil

## prapaśyadbhiḥ
ids: gita-01-39-w10
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: dṛś
prefix: pra
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: Present participle of dṛś with pra. It agrees with asmābhiḥ: by us, who see. Written prapaśyadbhir before janārdana.
meaning: by us who see
