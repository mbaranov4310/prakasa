# gita-01-03

## paśya
ids: gita-01-03-w1
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dṛś
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The present stem of dṛś is paśya. paśya and the e of etām join into ai.
meaning: look

## etām
ids: gita-01-03-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Feminine, with camūm.
meaning: at this

## pāṇḍu-putrāṇām
ids: gita-01-03-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pāṇḍu-putra
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “The sons of Pāṇḍu.” The final ā and the ā of ācārya join.
meaning: of the sons of Pandu

## ācārya
ids: gita-01-03-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ācārya
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
note: Vocative. The initial ā joins the preceding genitive.
meaning: O teacher
