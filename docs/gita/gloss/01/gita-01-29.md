# gita-01-29

## sīdanti
ids: gita-01-29-w1
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: sad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: The present stem of sad is sīda-.
meaning: sink

## mama
ids: gita-01-29-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender.
meaning: my

## gātrāṇi
ids: gita-01-29-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: gātra
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The neuter plural -āṇi is nominative or accusative. The limbs are what sink, so it is nominative.
meaning: limbs

## mukham
ids: gita-01-29-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mukha
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. The mouth is what dries, so it is nominative. Written mukhaṃ before ca.
meaning: my mouth

## ca
ids: gita-01-29-w5, gita-01-29-w8, gita-01-29-w12

judgment: obvious
meaning: and
