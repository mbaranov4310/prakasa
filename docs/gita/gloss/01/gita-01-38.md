# gita-01-38

## yadi
ids: gita-01-38-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written yady before api: final i becomes y before the vowel. The main question is in 1.39.
meaning: if

## api
ids: gita-01-38-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written pye: the a is lost after yadi, and the i joins the e of ete.
meaning: even

## ete
ids: gita-01-38-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Its e joins the i of api. These are Dhṛtarāṣṭra’s sons, named in 1.37.
meaning: these men

## na
ids: gita-01-38-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## paśyanti
ids: gita-01-38-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dṛś
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
meaning: see

## lobha-upahata-cetasaḥ
ids: gita-01-38-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: lobha-upahata-cetas
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those whose mind (cetas) is struck (upahata, past participle of han with upa) by greed (lobha).” The ending -cetasaḥ is nominative plural or genitive singular. It agrees with ete, so it is nominative plural.
meaning: their minds overcome by greed

## kula-kṣaya-kṛtam
ids: gita-01-38-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kula-kṣaya-kṛta
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Done (kṛta, past participle of kṛ) by the destruction (kṣaya, from kṣi) of a family (kula).” The ending -am agrees with doṣam, so it is masculine accusative.
meaning: done by destroying a family
