# gita-01-13

## tataḥ
ids: gita-01-13-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: “From that,” with the ending -tas, and so “then.”
meaning: then

## śaṅkhāḥ
ids: gita-01-13-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śaṅkha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Written śaṅkhāś before ca.
meaning: conches

## ca
ids: gita-01-13-w3, gita-01-13-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## bheryaḥ
ids: gita-01-13-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bherī
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Feminine. Nominative plural of bherī. Written bheryaś before ca.
meaning: kettledrums

## paṇava-ānaka-gomukhāḥ
ids: gita-01-13-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: paṇava-ānaka-gomukha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: dvandva
note: A dvandva of the three instruments. The gender is the gender of the last, masculine.
meaning: tabors, drums, and cow-horns

## sahasā
ids: gita-01-13-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Adverb, “suddenly, all at once.” sahasā and eva join into sahasaiva.
meaning: suddenly

## eva
ids: gita-01-13-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The a of abhyahanyanta joins it.
meaning: indeed

## abhyahanyanta
ids: gita-01-13-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: han
prefix: abhi
tense: ordinary past
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: Imperfect passive of abhi-han, “were struck,” and so “were sounded.” The a of abhi joins eva.
meaning: were sounded

## saḥ
ids: gita-01-13-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written sa before śabdaḥ.
meaning: that

## śabdaḥ
ids: gita-01-13-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śabda
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written śabdas before tumulaḥ.
meaning: sound

## tumulaḥ
ids: gita-01-13-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: tumula
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Predicate of abhavat. Written tumulo, and the a of abhavat is dropped.
meaning: a tumult

## abhavat
ids: gita-01-13-w13
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
tense: ordinary past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The initial a is dropped after tumulaḥ.
meaning: became
