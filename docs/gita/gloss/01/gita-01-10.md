# gita-01-10

## aparyāptam
ids: gita-01-10-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: aparyāpta
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: āp
prefix: pari
note: a- (“not”) with paryāpta, the past participle of pari-āp, “adequate.” The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is the predicate of balam, so it is nominative.
meaning: inadequate

## tat
ids: gita-01-10-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written tad before asmākam.
meaning: that

## asmākam
ids: gita-01-10-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: plural
note: asmad has no gender. The initial a is joined after tat and is not written again.
meaning: of ours

## balam
ids: gita-01-10-w4, gita-01-10-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bala
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. Each is the army that is adequate or not, so it is nominative. Both are written balaṃ.
meaning: army

## bhīṣma-abhirakṣitam
ids: gita-01-10-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: bhīṣma-abhirakṣita
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: rakṣ
prefix: abhi
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: abhirakṣita is the past participle of abhi-rakṣ. “Guarded by Bhishma.” It agrees with balam.
meaning: guarded by Bhishma

## paryāptam
ids: gita-01-10-w6
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: āp
prefix: pari
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: “Adequate.” The neuter -am is the predicate of the second balam, so it is nominative.
meaning: adequate

## tu
ids: gita-01-10-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: tu and the i of idam join: tvidam.
meaning: while

## idam
ids: gita-01-10-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The i stands with tu. The e of eteṣām joins the m.
meaning: this

## eteṣām
ids: gita-01-10-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
note: The men, so masculine. The initial e joins the m of idam.
meaning: of theirs
