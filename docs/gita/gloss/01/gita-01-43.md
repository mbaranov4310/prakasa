# gita-01-43

## doṣaiḥ
ids: gita-01-43-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: doṣa
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
root: duṣ
note: Written doṣair before etaiḥ: the visarga becomes r before the vowel.
meaning: by these evils

## etaiḥ
ids: gita-01-43-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: It stands with doṣaiḥ: by these evils.
meaning: these

## kula-ghnānām
ids: gita-01-43-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kula-ghna
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
root: han
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: ghna, from han, “killing.” “Those who destroy (ghna) the family (kula).”
meaning: of the destroyers of a family

## varṇa-saṃkara-kārakaiḥ
ids: gita-01-43-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: varṇa-saṃkara-kāraka
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
root: kṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: kāraka, from kṛ, “causing.” “Causing (kāraka) a mixing of the orders (varṇa-saṃkara).” It agrees with doṣaiḥ. The print writes saṃ with anusvāra.
meaning: which bring about a mixing of the orders

## utsādyante
ids: gita-01-43-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: sad
prefix: ut
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: Causative of sad. The passive means “are caused to sink away,” so “are uprooted.”
meaning: are uprooted
