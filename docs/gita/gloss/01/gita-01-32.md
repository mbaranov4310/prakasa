# gita-01-32

## na
ids: gita-01-32-w1, gita-01-32-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## kāṅkṣe
ids: gita-01-32-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: kāṅkṣ
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: The subject is the unstated “I.” The same verb covers the kingdom and the pleasures, with na ca.
meaning: I desire

## vijayam
ids: gita-01-32-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: vijaya
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: ji
note: “Conquest,” from ji.
meaning: victory

## kṛṣṇa
ids: gita-01-32-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kṛṣṇa
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
meaning: O Krishna

## ca
ids: gita-01-32-w6, gita-01-32-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## rājyam
ids: gita-01-32-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: rājya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is another thing he does not desire, so it is accusative. The verb is the kāṅkṣe above.
meaning: a kingdom

## sukhāni
ids: gita-01-32-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sukha
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
note: The neuter plural -āni is nominative or accusative. They are further things he does not desire, so it is accusative.
meaning: pleasures

## kim
ids: gita-01-32-w10, gita-01-32-w14
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: kim
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter kim is nominative or accusative. It is the question “what,” and nothing acts on it, so it is nominative. There is no verb. “Is” is understood: what use is it.
meaning: what

## naḥ
ids: gita-01-32-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: dative
number: plural
note: naḥ is the short form of asmad for the dative or the genitive. The sense is “to us,” so it is dative. asmad has no gender. Written no before rājyena.
meaning: to us

## rājyena
ids: gita-01-32-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: rājya
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: Instrumental with kim: what use is a kingdom.
meaning: with a kingdom

## govinda
ids: gita-01-32-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: govinda

number: singular
meaning: O Govinda

## bhogaiḥ
ids: gita-01-32-w15
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhoga
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
root: bhuj
note: “Enjoyment,” from bhuj. Written bhogair before jīvitena. It goes with the second kim.
meaning: with enjoyments

## jīvitena
ids: gita-01-32-w16
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jīvita
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: jīv
note: jīvita is the past participle of jīv, used as a noun, “life.” It goes with the second kim. vā joins it to the enjoyments.
meaning: with life

ids: gita-01-32-w17
