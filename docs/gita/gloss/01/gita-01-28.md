number: singular
root: kṛp
meaning: by pity

## parayā
ids: gita-01-28-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: para
gender: feminine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: It agrees with kṛpayā. para, “extreme,” here “deep.” Its ā joins the ā of āviṣṭaḥ.
meaning: deep

## āviṣṭaḥ
ids: gita-01-28-w5
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: viś
prefix: ā
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Past participle of viś with ā, “entered,” and so “overcome.” The initial ā joins the ā of parayā. Written -viṣṭo before viṣīdan.
meaning: overcome

## viṣīdan
ids: gita-01-28-w6
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sad
prefix: vi
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Present participle of sad with vi. The present stem is sīda-. Written viṣīdann before idam: final n doubles before the vowel.
meaning: despairing

## idam
ids: gita-01-28-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter idam is nominative or accusative. It is what he spoke, the speech that follows, so it is accusative.
meaning: this

## abravīt
ids: gita-01-28-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: brū
class: ad
tense: ordinary past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The imperfect of brū.
meaning: he spoke

## dṛṣṭvā
ids: gita-01-28-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: dṛś
note: An absolutive in -tvā. Its ā and the i of imān join into e: dṛṣṭvemān.
meaning: seeing

## imān
ids: gita-01-28-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: Accusative plural. This line is imān svajanān yuyutsūn samupasthitān, all plural. Its i joins the ā of dṛṣṭvā.
meaning: these

## svajanān
ids: gita-01-28-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: svajana
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: sva, “one’s own,” and jana, “people.” Accusative plural, with imān, yuyutsūn, and samupasthitān.
meaning: kinsmen
