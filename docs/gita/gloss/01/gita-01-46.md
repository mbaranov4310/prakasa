# gita-01-46

## yadi
ids: gita-01-46-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: yadi marks the condition. The main clause is tat ... bhavet.
meaning: if

## mām
ids: gita-01-46-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written mam before apratīkāram: the anusvāra becomes m before the vowel.
meaning: me

## apratīkāram
ids: gita-01-46-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: apratīkāra
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: a- (“not”) with pratīkāra, “resistance.” It agrees with mām. asmad has no gender; the adjective is masculine, for the speaker.
meaning: offering no resistance

## aśastram
ids: gita-01-46-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: aśastra
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: a- (“not”) with śastra, “a weapon.” It agrees with mām. Written aśastraṃ before śastra-pāṇayaḥ.
meaning: unarmed

## śastra-pāṇayaḥ
ids: gita-01-46-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śastra-pāṇi
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those in whose hand (pāṇi) a weapon (śastra) is.” The nominative plural of the bahuvrīhi in -i is -ayaḥ. It agrees with dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ.
meaning: with weapons in hand

## dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ
ids: gita-01-46-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dhārtarāṣṭra
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Patronymic, “son of Dhṛtarāṣṭra.” The visarga is not written before raṇe.
meaning: the sons of Dhritarashtra

## raṇe
ids: gita-01-46-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: raṇa
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
root: raṇ
meaning: in battle

## hanyuḥ
ids: gita-01-46-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: han
class: ad
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: Potential of han. Written hanyus before tat.
meaning: should kill

## tat
ids: gita-01-46-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter tat is nominative or accusative. It takes up the killing in the if-clause, and it is what would be better, so it is nominative. Written tan before me.
meaning: that

## me
ids: gita-01-46-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: dative
number: singular
note: me is dative or genitive. With kṣema-taram the sense is “for me,” so it is dative. asmad has no gender.
meaning: for me

## kṣema-taram
ids: gita-01-46-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kṣema-tara
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Comparative, with -tara, of kṣema, “safe, well.” The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is the predicate of bhavet, agreeing with tat, so it is nominative.
meaning: better

## bhavet
ids: gita-01-46-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Potential of bhū, “would be.”
meaning: would be
