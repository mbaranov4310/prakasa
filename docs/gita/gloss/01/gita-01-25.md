# gita-01-25

## bhīṣma-droṇa-pramukhataḥ
ids: gita-01-25-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: pramukha, “front,” with the suffix -tas, “in front of.” Bhīṣma and Droṇa are a dvandva, the people in front. The kings in the genitive are counted with them.
meaning: in front of Bhishma and Drona

## sarveṣām
ids: gita-01-25-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
note: It agrees with mahī-kṣitām. Written sarveṣāṃ before ca.
meaning: of all

## ca
ids: gita-01-25-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## mahī-kṣitām
ids: gita-01-25-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mahī-kṣit
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
root: kṣi
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Ruler (kṣit, from kṣi, to rule) of the earth (mahī).” Genitive plural of the consonant stem.
meaning: the kings
