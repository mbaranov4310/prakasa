# gita-01-44

## utsanna-kula-dharmāṇām
ids: gita-01-44-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: utsanna-kula-dharma
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those whose family duties (kula-dharma) are destroyed (utsanna, past participle of sad with ut).” It agrees with manuṣyāṇām.
meaning: whose family duties are destroyed

## manuṣyāṇām
ids: gita-01-44-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: manuṣya
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
note: Genitive of the men whose dwelling in hell is certain. The sense of the line is “for men.”
meaning: for men

## niyatam
ids: gita-01-44-w5
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: yam
prefix: ni
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Past participle of yam with ni, “fixed,” so “certain.” A second word, after narake. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. vāsaḥ is masculine, and a matching nominative would be niyataḥ. The print has niyatam, and the line says the dwelling is certain, so the neuter is the predicate.
meaning: certain

stem: vāsa

number: singular
root: vas
note: “A dwelling,” from vas. Written vāso before bhavati.
meaning: a dwelling

## bhavati
ids: gita-01-44-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Its final i and the i of iti join into ī: bhavatīti.
meaning: is

## iti
ids: gita-01-44-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written ty: its i joins the i of bhavati, and that i becomes y before anuśuśruma.
meaning: so

## anuśuśruma
ids: gita-01-44-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: śru
prefix: anu
class: su
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: plural
note: The perfect of śru, “we have heard.” The present is śṛṇoti, class 5; the perfect stem does not show that. The subject is the unstated “we.” Its a joins the y of iti.
meaning: we have heard
