# gita-02-22

## vāsāṃsi
ids: gita-02-22-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: vāsas
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
root: vas
note: Neuter plural of vāsas, “cloth,” from vas “to wear.” The plural -āṃsi is nominative or accusative. It is what he casts off, so it is accusative.
meaning: clothes

root: jṝ
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
note: Past participle of jṝ, “worn out.” It agrees with vāsāṃsi.
meaning: worn out
