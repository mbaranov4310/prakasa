# gita-02-50

## buddhi-yuktaḥ
ids: gita-02-50-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: buddhi-yukta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: yukta is the past participle of yuj. “Joined with understanding.” Written buddhiyukto before jahāti.
meaning: one endowed with understanding (evenness of mind)

## jahāti
ids: gita-02-50-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: hā
class: hu
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Its i and the i of iha join into ī: jahātīha.
meaning: casts off

## iha
ids: gita-02-50-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The i joins the final i of jahāti.
meaning: here (in this world)

## ubhe
ids: gita-02-50-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ubha
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: dual
note: The dual -e is nominative or accusative. It goes with what is cast off, so it is accusative.
meaning: both

## sukṛta-duṣkṛte
ids: gita-02-50-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sukṛta-duṣkṛta
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: dual
root: kṛ
compound: dvandva
note: A dvandva of the two, merit and demerit, both past participles of kṛ. The dual -e is nominative or accusative. It is what he casts off, so it is accusative.
meaning: merit and demerit

## tasmāt
ids: gita-02-50-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: The ablative singular is the same in masculine and neuter. It points back to what was just said, so it is neuter. “From that” is “therefore.” Written tasmād before yogāya.
meaning: therefore

## kauśalam
ids: gita-02-50-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious

gender: neuter

number: singular
