## pūjā-arhau
ids: gita-02-04-w12
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: pūjā-arha
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: dual
root: arh
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Worthy (arha) of worship (pūjā).” The dual -au is nominative or accusative. It names Bhīṣma and Droṇa, who are accusative, so it is accusative. Written pūjārhāv before arisūdana.
meaning: the two who are worthy of worship
