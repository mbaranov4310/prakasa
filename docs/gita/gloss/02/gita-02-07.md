# gita-02-07

## kārpaṇya-doṣa-upahata-svabhāvaḥ
ids: gita-02-07-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kārpaṇya-doṣa-upahata-svabhāva
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Whose nature (svabhāva) is struck (upahata, past participle of upa-han) by the fault (doṣa) of a weak spirit (kārpaṇya).” It agrees with aham.
meaning: my nature overcome by the fault of a weak spirit

## dharma-saṃmūḍha-cetāḥ
ids: gita-02-07-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: dharma-saṃmūḍha-cetas
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Whose mind (cetas) is confused (saṃmūḍha, past participle of sam-muh) about what is right (dharma).” The nominative singular of a bahuvrīhi in -cetas is cetāḥ. It agrees with aham.
meaning: my mind confused about what is right

## śreyaḥ
