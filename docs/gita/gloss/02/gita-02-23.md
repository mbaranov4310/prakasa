# gita-02-23

## na
ids: gita-02-23-w1, gita-02-23-w5, gita-02-23-w9, gita-02-23-w14
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The first two join enam: nainam.
meaning: not

## enam
ids: gita-02-23-w2, gita-02-23-w6, gita-02-23-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: ena
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Accusative of the enclitic demonstrative ena. Śaṅkara takes it as the embodied one. The first two lose their e after na. The third loses it after ca.
meaning: him

## chindanti
ids: gita-02-23-w3
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: chid
class: rudh
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: chid is class 7.
meaning: cut

## śastrāṇi
ids: gita-02-23-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śastra
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: śas
note: The neuter plural is nominative or accusative. Weapons are what cut, so it is nominative.
meaning: weapons
