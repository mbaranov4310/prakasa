# gita-02-61

## tāni
ids: gita-02-61-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
note: The neuter plural -āni is nominative or accusative. It is what he brings under control, so it is accusative. It takes up the senses of the previous verse.
meaning: those (the senses)

## sarvāṇi
ids: gita-02-61-w2

gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
note: It agrees with tāni.
meaning: all of them

## mat-paraḥ
ids: gita-02-61-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mat-para
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One for whom I (mat) am the highest (para).” mat is the form asmad takes at the front of a compound. It agrees with the man who is to sit, who is not named here. Śaṅkara: I, the Self of all, am the one he holds highest.
meaning: intent on Me (the Self of all)
