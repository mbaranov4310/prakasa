# gita-02-45

## traiguṇya-viṣayāḥ
ids: gita-02-45-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: traiguṇya-viṣaya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those whose subject (viṣaya) is the three guṇas (traiguṇya).” It agrees with vedāḥ. The visarga drops before vedāḥ.
meaning: have the three guṇas as their subject

## vedāḥ
ids: gita-02-45-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: veda
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: vid
note: The visarga drops before nistraiguṇyaḥ. There is no finite verb. “Are” is understood.
meaning: the Vedas

## nistraiguṇyaḥ
ids: gita-02-45-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nistraiguṇya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: nis-, “out, without,” with traiguṇya: free of the three guṇas. It agrees with the unstated “you” of bhava. Written nistraiguṇyo before bhava.
meaning: free of the three guṇas (free of desire)

## bhava
ids: gita-02-45-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject “you” is not spoken. Written bhavā before rjuna: a joins arjuna.
meaning: be

## arjuna
ids: gita-02-45-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: arjuna
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
note: Vocative. Its initial a joins bhava.
meaning: Arjuna

## nirdvandvaḥ
ids: gita-02-45-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nirdvandva
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: nis- with dvandva, “a pair”: free of the pairs. It agrees with the unstated “you.” Written nirdvandvo before nitya-.
meaning: free of the pairs (of pleasure and pain)

## nitya-sattva-sthaḥ
ids: gita-02-45-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nitya-sattva-stha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: sthā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Standing (stha, from sthā) always (nitya) in sattva.” It agrees with the unstated “you.” Written -stho before niryoga-.
meaning: ever established in sattva

## niryoga-kṣemaḥ
ids: gita-02-45-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: niryoga-kṣema
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: nis- with yoga-kṣema. yoga is getting what one lacks, kṣema is keeping what one has. It agrees with the unstated “you.” The visarga drops before ātmavān.
meaning: free of getting and keeping

## ātmavān
ids: gita-02-45-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ātmavat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: ātman with the suffix -vat, “possessing the self,” so self-possessed. The nominative singular is -vān. It agrees with the unstated “you.”
meaning: self-possessed (vigilant)
