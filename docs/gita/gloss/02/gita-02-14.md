# gita-02-14

## mātrā-sparśāḥ
ids: gita-02-14-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mātrā-sparśa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Contacts (sparśa) of the senses (mātrā).” Śaṅkara glosses mātrā as the senses, by which sound and the rest are measured. Written -sparśās before tu.
meaning: contacts of the senses with their objects

## tu
ids: gita-02-14-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: but

## śīta-uṣṇa-sukha-duḥkha-dāḥ
ids: gita-02-14-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śīta-uṣṇa-sukha-duḥkha-da
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: dā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Giving (da, from dā) cold, heat, pleasure, and pain.” It agrees with mātrā-sparśāḥ.
meaning: giving cold and heat, pleasure and pain

## āgama-apāyinaḥ
ids: gita-02-14-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: āgama-apāyin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Whose way is coming (āgama) and going (apāya).” Śaṅkara glosses it āgamāpāya-śīlāḥ. The ending -inaḥ is nominative plural or genitive singular. It agrees with anityāḥ, so it is nominative plural. Written -apāyino before anityāḥ, and that a is dropped.
meaning: coming and going

## anityāḥ
ids: gita-02-14-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: anitya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: It agrees with mātrā-sparśāḥ. The a is dropped after āgama-apāyinaḥ. Written anityās before tān.
meaning: not lasting
