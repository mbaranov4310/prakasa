# gita-02-32

## yadṛcchayā
ids: gita-02-32-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yadṛcchā
gender: feminine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: “By chance,” of itself, without being sought.

## ca
ids: gita-02-32-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written co before papannam: a joins the u of upapannam.
meaning: and

## upapannam
ids: gita-02-32-w3
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: pad
prefix: upa
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: “Come upon, arrived.” The neuter -am agrees with yuddham, so it is accusative. Its u joins ca.
meaning: come upon

## svarga-dvāram
ids: gita-02-32-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: svarga-dvāra
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa

meaning: a door to heaven
