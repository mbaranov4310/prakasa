# gita-02-08

## na
ids: gita-02-08-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## hi
ids: gita-02-08-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: indeed

## prapaśyāmi
ids: gita-02-08-w3
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dṛś
prefix: pra
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: The subject “I” is not spoken. What he does not see is the yat clause, the thing that would drive the grief away.
meaning: I see

## mama
ids: gita-02-08-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. It joins apanudyāt.
meaning: my

## apanudyāt
ids: gita-02-08-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: nud
prefix: apa
class: tud
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Optative of nud, class 6. The a is joined to mama. The line breaks before yat.
meaning: that would drive away

## yat
ids: gita-02-08-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It is what would drive the grief away, and so what he does not see. It joins śokam.
meaning: which

## śokam
ids: gita-02-08-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śoka
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: śuc
note: The ś is joined to yat. The m joins ucchoṣaṇam.
meaning: grief

## ucchoṣaṇam
ids: gita-02-08-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ucchoṣaṇa
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: śuṣ
prefix: ud
note: “Drying up,” from ud-śuṣ. It agrees with śokam, so the -am is masculine accusative. The u is joined to śokam. The m joins indriyāṇām.
meaning: drying up

## indriyāṇām
ids: gita-02-08-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: plural
note: The i is joined to ucchoṣaṇam.
meaning: of the senses

## avāpya
ids: gita-02-08-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: āp
prefix: ava
note: An absolutive in -ya.
meaning: having obtained

## bhūmau
ids: gita-02-08-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūmi
gender: feminine
case: locative
number: singular
note: It joins asapatnam.
meaning: on earth

## asapatnam
ids: gita-02-08-w12
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: asapatna
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Whose rival (sapatna) is none.” The pada is not hyphenated. It agrees with rājyam. The a is joined to bhūmau. The m joins ṛddham.
meaning: with no rival

## ṛddham
ids: gita-02-08-w13
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: ṛdh
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Past participle of ṛdh, “thriven,” and so prosperous. It agrees with rājyam. The ṛ is joined to asapatnam.
meaning: prosperous

## rājyam
ids: gita-02-08-w14
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: rājya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he would have obtained, so it is accusative.
meaning: a kingdom

## surāṇām
ids: gita-02-08-w15
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sura
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
note: The m joins api.
meaning: of the gods

## api
ids: gita-02-08-w16
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The a is joined to surāṇām.
meaning: even

## ca
ids: gita-02-08-w17
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It joins ādhipatyam.
meaning: and

## ādhipatyam
ids: gita-02-08-w18
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ādhipatya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: “Lordship,” from adhipati. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is the other thing he would have obtained, so it is accusative. The ā is joined to ca.
meaning: lordship
