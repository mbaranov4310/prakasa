# gita-02-41

## vyavasāya-ātmikā
ids: gita-02-41-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vyavasāya-ātmika
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Whose nature (ātman, with the suffix -ika) is resolve (vyavasāya).” Feminine to agree with buddhiḥ.
meaning: whose nature is resolve

## buddhiḥ
ids: gita-02-41-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: buddhi
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: budh
note: Written buddhire before ke: the visarga becomes r, and the e of ekā is joined to it. There is no finite verb. “Is” is understood.
meaning: the understanding

## ekā
ids: gita-02-41-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: eka
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Feminine to agree with buddhiḥ. Its e stands with buddhiḥ, and its ā joins iha into e, so the slice is ke.
meaning: one alone

## iha
ids: gita-02-41-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Its i joins the ā of ekā, so the slice is ha.
meaning: here (on the path of the good)

## kuru-nandana
ids: gita-02-41-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kuru-nandana
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
root: nand
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Joy (nandana, from nand) of the Kurus.” Vocative.
meaning: joy of the Kurus

## bahu-śākhāḥ
ids: gita-02-41-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: bahu-śākhā
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those of which the branches (śākhā) are many (bahu).” Feminine to agree with buddhayaḥ. The visarga drops before hi.
meaning: many-branched

## hi
ids: gita-02-41-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written hy before anantāḥ.
meaning: for

## anantāḥ
ids: gita-02-41-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ananta
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: a- (“not”) with anta, “end.” Feminine to agree with buddhayaḥ. Written anantāś before ca.
meaning: endless

## ca
ids: gita-02-41-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## buddhayaḥ
ids: gita-02-41-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: buddhi
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: budh
note: Nominative plural of buddhi. Written buddhayo before avyavasāyinām, and that a is dropped. There is no finite verb. “Are” is understood.
meaning: the thoughts

## avyavasāyinām
ids: gita-02-41-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: avyavasāyin
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: a- (“not”) with vyavasāyin, a stem in -in, “one who has resolve.” The genitive plural is -inām. It stands as a noun. Its initial a is dropped after buddhayaḥ.
meaning: of those without resolve
