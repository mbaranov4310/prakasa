# gita-02-47

## karmaṇi
ids: gita-02-47-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: Written karmaṇye before vā: i joins the e of eva.
meaning: in action

## eva
ids: gita-02-47-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Its e joins karmaṇi, and its a joins adhikāraḥ into ā, so the slice is vā.
meaning: alone

## adhikāraḥ
ids: gita-02-47-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: adhikāra
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṛ
prefix: adhi
note: Its initial a joins eva. Written -dhikāras before te: the visarga becomes s. There is no finite verb in this clause. “Is” is understood.
meaning: the qualification

## te
ids: gita-02-47-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: te is dative or genitive. With adhikāraḥ it is genitive, “yours.” yuṣmad has no gender.
meaning: yours

## mā
ids: gita-02-47-w5, gita-02-47-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Prohibitive mā, “let there not be.” With the first, astu is supplied. The second stands with astu.
meaning: let there not be

## phaleṣu
ids: gita-02-47-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: phala
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: plural
meaning: in the fruits

## kadācana
ids: gita-02-47-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: kadā, “when,” with cana: “at any time.”
meaning: at any time

## mā
ids: gita-02-47-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Prohibitive mā with bhūḥ: “do not.”
meaning: do not

## karma-phala-hetuḥ
ids: gita-02-47-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: karma-phala-hetu
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose motive (hetu) is the fruit (phala) of action (karma).” It agrees with the unstated “you” of bhūḥ. Written -hetur before bhūḥ.
meaning: one whose motive is the fruit of action

## bhūḥ
ids: gita-02-47-w10
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: With mā, the aorist of bhū without the augment: “do not become.” It has the force of a command not to. The imperative would be bhava. The subject “you” is not spoken. Written bhūr before mā.
meaning: become

## te
ids: gita-02-47-w12
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: te is dative or genitive. With saṅgaḥ it is genitive, “your.” yuṣmad has no gender.
meaning: your

## saṅgaḥ
ids: gita-02-47-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṅga
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: sañj
prefix: sam
note: Written saṅgo before astu, and that a is dropped.
meaning: attachment

## astu
ids: gita-02-47-w14
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Command of as, “let it be,” with mā: “let there not be.” Written stva before karmaṇi: u joins the a of akarmaṇi into va.
meaning: be

## akarmaṇi
ids: gita-02-47-w15
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: akarman
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: a- (“not”) with karman: inaction. Its initial a joins the u of astu, so the slice is karmaṇi.
meaning: to inaction
