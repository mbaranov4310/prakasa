# gita-02-40

## na
ids: gita-02-40-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written ne before hā: a joins the i of iha.
meaning: not

## iha
ids: gita-02-40-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Its i joins na, and its a joins abhikrama into ā, so the slice is hā.
meaning: here (in karma-yoga)

## abhikrama-nāśaḥ
ids: gita-02-40-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: abhikrama-nāśa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: naś
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “The loss (nāśa, from naś) of an undertaking (abhikrama).” Its initial a joins iha. Written -nāśo before asti, and that a is dropped.
meaning: loss of what is begun

## asti
ids: gita-02-40-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Its initial a is dropped after nāśaḥ.
meaning: there is

## pratyavāyaḥ
ids: gita-02-40-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pratyavāya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: i
prefix: prati-ava
note: “A going (aya, from i) against (prati-ava),” a setback. Written pratyavāyo before na.
meaning: a contrary result (as from a failed cure)

## na
ids: gita-02-40-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## vidyate
ids: gita-02-40-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: div
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: This vid means “to be found,” not “to know.” The present vidyate is class 4.
meaning: is found

## svalpam
ids: gita-02-40-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: svalpa
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what protects, the subject of trāyate, so it is nominative. The adjective stands as a noun, “a little.” Written svalpam before api.
meaning: even a little

## api
ids: gita-02-40-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written py before asya: i joins the a of asya into ya.
meaning: also

## asya
ids: gita-02-40-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Masculine, agreeing with dharmasya. Its a joins api, so the slice is sya.
meaning: of this

## dharmasya
ids: gita-02-40-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dharma
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
meaning: dharma (the yoga)

## trāyate
ids: gita-02-40-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: trai
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The person who is protected is not named in the verse.
meaning: protects one

## mahataḥ
ids: gita-02-40-w13
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mahat
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: Ablative singular of mahat, agreeing with bhayāt. The “from” in the meaning is that ablative. Written mahato before bhayāt.
meaning: from great
