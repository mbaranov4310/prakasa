# gita-02-02

## śrī-bhagavān
ids: gita-02-02-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śrī-bhagavat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: śrī with bhagavat. The nominative is bhagavān, and that n joins the u of uvāca.
meaning: the blessed Lord

## uvāca
ids: gita-02-02-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The u is joined to śrī-bhagavān.
meaning: said

## kutaḥ
ids: gita-02-02-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: “From where,” from kim with the suffix -tas. Written kutas before tvā.
meaning: from where

## tvā
ids: gita-02-02-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: tvā is the enclitic accusative of yuṣmad. yuṣmad has no gender.
meaning: upon you

## kaśmalam
ids: gita-02-02-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kaśmala
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what has come upon him, so it is nominative. The m joins idam.
meaning: faintness

## idam
ids: gita-02-02-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with kaśmalam. The i is joined to kaśmalam.
meaning: this

## viṣame
ids: gita-02-02-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: viṣama
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
meaning: in a crisis

## samupasthitam
ids: gita-02-02-w8
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: sam-upa
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Past participle of sam-upa-sthā, “come upon.” It agrees with kaśmalam. There is no finite verb.
meaning: has come

## anārya-juṣṭam
ids: gita-02-02-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: anārya-juṣṭa
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: juṣ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Practiced (juṣṭa, past participle of juṣ) by those who are not noble (anārya).” It agrees with kaśmalam. The m joins asvargyam.
meaning: not the way of men who know what is right

## asvargyam
ids: gita-02-02-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: asvargya
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: “Not leading to heaven,” a-svargya. It agrees with kaśmalam. The a is joined to anārya-juṣṭam, and the m joins akīrti-karam.
meaning: not leading to heaven

## akīrti-karam
ids: gita-02-02-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: akīrti-kara
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Making (kara, from kṛ) disgrace (akīrti).” It agrees with kaśmalam. The a is joined to asvargyam.
meaning: bringing disgrace

## arjuna
ids: gita-02-02-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: arjuna
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
note: The vocative. The a is joined to akīrti-karam.
meaning: Arjuna
