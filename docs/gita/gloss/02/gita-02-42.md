# gita-02-42

## yām
ids: gita-02-42-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Feminine, with vācam. Written yāmi before mām: the i of imām is joined to it.
meaning: which

## imām
ids: gita-02-42-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Feminine, with vācam. Its i stands with yām, so the slice is mām.
meaning: this

## puṣpitām
ids: gita-02-42-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: puṣpita
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: “Flowered,” from puṣpa, “a flower,” so pleasant to hear. It agrees with vācam.
meaning: flowery (pleasant to hear)

## vācam
ids: gita-02-42-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: vāc
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: vac
meaning: speech

## pravadanti
ids: gita-02-42-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vad
prefix: pra
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: Written pravadanty before avipaścitaḥ.
meaning: utter

## avipaścitaḥ
ids: gita-02-42-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: avipaścit
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: a- (“not”) with vipaścit, “discerning.” Nominative plural of the stem in -t. Its initial a joins pravadanti.
meaning: the undiscerning

## veda-vāda-ratāḥ
ids: gita-02-42-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: veda-vāda-rata
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: ram
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: rata is the past participle of ram. “Delighting (rata) in the sayings (vāda) of the Veda.” It agrees with avipaścitaḥ.
meaning: who delight in the words of the Veda

## pārtha
ids: gita-02-42-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pārtha
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
note: Vocative, “son of Pṛthā.”
meaning: son of Pṛthā

## na
ids: gita-02-42-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written nā before nyad: a joins the a of anyat.
meaning: not

## anyat
ids: gita-02-42-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: anya
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter anyat is nominative or accusative. It is the subject of asti, so it is nominative. Its a joins na, and t becomes d before asti, so the slice is nyad.
meaning: anything else (than these rites and their fruits)

## asti
ids: gita-02-42-w11
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Written stī before ti: its a is read with anyat, and its i joins iti.
meaning: there is

## iti
ids: gita-02-42-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Its i joins asti, so the slice is ti. iti marks the words na anyad asti.
meaning: thus

## vādinaḥ
ids: gita-02-42-w13
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vādin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: A stem in -in, from vad, “saying.” The nominative plural is -inaḥ. It agrees with avipaścitaḥ.
meaning: declaring
