# gita-02-43

## kāma-ātmānaḥ
ids: gita-02-43-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kāma-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those whose self (ātman) is desire (kāma).” Nominative plural of the stem in -an. It agrees with avipaścitaḥ in 2.42.
meaning: whose nature is desire

## svarga-parāḥ
ids: gita-02-43-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: svarga-para
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those for whom heaven (svarga) is the highest end (para).” The visarga drops before janma-. It agrees with the same people.
meaning: for whom heaven is the highest end

## janma-karma-phala-pradām
ids: gita-02-43-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: janma-karma-phala-prada
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: dā
prefix: pra
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Giving (prada, from dā with pra) birth (janma) as the fruit (phala) of action (karma).” The fruit of action here is rebirth. Feminine accusative, agreeing with vācam in 2.42.
meaning: which yields birth as the fruit of action

## kriyā-viśeṣa-bahulām
ids: gita-02-43-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kriyā-viśeṣa-bahula
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “That in which particular rites (kriyā-viśeṣa) are abundant (bahula).” Feminine accusative, agreeing with vācam in 2.42.
meaning: full of particular rites

## bhoga-aiśvarya-gatim
ids: gita-02-43-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhoga-aiśvarya-gati
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: gam
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “The reaching (gati, from gam) of pleasure and power.” bhoga and aiśvarya are a pair inside it. Accusative with prati.
meaning: the attainment of pleasure and power

## prati
ids: gita-02-43-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: A preposition with the accusative, “toward, aimed at.”
meaning: aimed at
