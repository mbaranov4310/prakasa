# gita-02-12

## na
ids: gita-02-12-w1, gita-02-12-w6, gita-02-12-w8, gita-02-12-w10, gita-02-12-w13, gita-02-12-w16
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The na before āsam joins it: nāsam. The na before ime joins it: neme.
meaning: not

## tu
ids: gita-02-12-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It joins eva.
meaning: but

## eva
ids: gita-02-12-w3, gita-02-12-w15
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The first is joined to tu and to aham: tvevāham. The second is joined to ca: caiva.
meaning: indeed

## aham
ids: gita-02-12-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The a is joined to eva.
meaning: I

## jātu
ids: gita-02-12-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: ever

## āsam
ids: gita-02-12-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: ordinary past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: Imperfect of as. With the two na, Śaṅkara reads “it is not that I did not exist.” The ā is joined to na.
meaning: did exist

## tvam
ids: gita-02-12-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: yuṣmad has no gender.
meaning: you

## ime
ids: gita-02-12-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: It agrees with jana-adhipāḥ. The i is joined to na.
meaning: these

## jana-adhipāḥ
ids: gita-02-12-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jana-adhipa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Lords (adhipa) of men (jana).”
meaning: lords of men

## ca
ids: gita-02-12-w14
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It joins eva.
meaning: and

## bhaviṣyāmaḥ
ids: gita-02-12-w17
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
tense: simple future
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: plural
note: Future of bhū. With the two na, Śaṅkara reads “it is not that we shall not be.”
meaning: we shall be

## sarve
ids: gita-02-12-w18
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: It agrees with vayam.
meaning: all

## vayam
ids: gita-02-12-w19
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: plural
note: asmad has no gender. The m takes the a of ataḥ as its vowel.
meaning: we

## ataḥ
ids: gita-02-12-w20
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: “From this,” with the ending -tas. Śaṅkara takes “this” as the loss of the body. Its a is the vowel of the m of vayam.
meaning: from this

## param
ids: gita-02-12-w21
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The neuter accusative singular of para, used as an adverb, “after, hereafter.”
meaning: hereafter
