# gita-02-13

## dehinaḥ
ids: gita-02-13-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dehin
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: “The one who has a body.” The adjective stands as a noun. dehinaḥ is genitive or ablative. Śaṅkara takes the genitive, the one whose body this is. Written dehino before asmin, and that a is dropped.
meaning: of the embodied one

## asmin
ids: gita-02-13-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: It agrees with dehe. The a is dropped after dehino, and the y of yathā is written with it.
meaning: in this

## yathā
ids: gita-02-13-w3

note: Its y is written at the end of asmin.
