kind: verb

## puruṣarṣabha
ids: gita-02-15-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: puruṣarṣabha
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Bull (ṛṣabha) among men,” so the best of men. The pada is not hyphenated.
meaning: O best of men

## sama-duḥkha-sukham
ids: gita-02-15-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sama-duḥkha-sukha
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “He for whom pain and pleasure are the same.” Śaṅkara: same duḥkha-sukhe yasya. The masculine accusative ends in -am. It agrees with puruṣam.
meaning: steady in pain and pleasure

## dhīram
ids: gita-02-15-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: dhīra
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with puruṣam. Śaṅkara glosses it dhīmantam, “wise.”
meaning: wise
