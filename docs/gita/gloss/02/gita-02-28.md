# gita-02-28

## avyakta-ādīni
ids: gita-02-28-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: avyakta-ādi
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those whose beginning (ādi) is the unmanifest (avyakta).” Neuter plural to agree with bhūtāni. The nominative plural of the stem in -i is -īni.
meaning: unmanifest at the beginning

## bhūtāni
ids: gita-02-28-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūta
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: bhū
note: bhūta is the past participle of bhū, used as a noun. The neuter plural -āni is nominative or accusative. Nothing acts on the beings, so it is nominative. There is no finite verb. “Are” is understood.
meaning: beings

## vyakta-madhyāni
ids: gita-02-28-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vyakta-madhya
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those whose middle (madhya) is manifest (vyakta).” It agrees with bhūtāni.
meaning: manifest in the middle

## bhārata
ids: gita-02-28-w4

stem: bhārata
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular

meaning: O Bharata

## avyakta-nidhanāni
ids: gita-02-28-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: avyakta-nidhana
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those whose end (nidhana) is the unmanifest (avyakta).” It agrees with bhūtāni. Written -nidhanāny before eva.
meaning: unmanifest at the end

## eva
ids: gita-02-28-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The y of nidhanāni joins it, so the slice is va.
meaning: as well

## tatra
