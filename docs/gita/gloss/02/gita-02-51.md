# gita-02-51

## karma-jam
ids: gita-02-51-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: karma-ja
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: jan
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: ja is from jan, “born.” The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It describes phalam, so it is accusative. Śaṅkara joins it to phalam across the words between them.
meaning: born of action

## buddhi-yuktāḥ
ids: gita-02-51-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: buddhi-yukta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: yuj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: yukta is the past participle of yuj. The visarga drops before hi.
meaning: those endowed with understanding

## hi
ids: gita-02-51-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: for

## phalam
ids: gita-02-51-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: phala
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what they cast off, so it is accusative.
meaning: the fruit

## tyaktvā
ids: gita-02-51-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: tyaj
note: An absolutive in -tvā.
meaning: having cast off

## manīṣiṇaḥ
ids: gita-02-51-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: manīṣin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: manīṣā, “wisdom,” with the suffix -in. manīṣiṇaḥ is nominative or accusative plural. Śaṅkara supplies bhūtvā, “having become wise,” the same people who go, so it is nominative.
meaning: the wise

## janma-bandha-vinirmuktāḥ
ids: gita-02-51-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: janma-bandha-vinirmukta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: muc
prefix: vi-nir
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: vinirmukta is the past participle of vi-nir-muc. Śaṅkara takes janma-bandha as the bond that is birth, and the compound as freed from that.
meaning: released from the bond that is birth

## padam
ids: gita-02-51-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pada
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: pad
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what they go to, so it is accusative.
meaning: the state (the supreme abode, called liberation)

## gacchanti
ids: gita-02-51-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: gam
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: Written gacchanty before anāmayam.
meaning: they go to

## anāmayam
ids: gita-02-51-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: anāmaya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: a- (“not”) with āmaya, “illness”: “where there is no sorrow.” It agrees with padam. The pada is not hyphenated. The a joins the i of gacchanti.
meaning: where there is no sorrow
