# gita-02-16

## na
ids: gita-02-16-w1, gita-02-16-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Each joins the next vowel: nāsataḥ, nābhāvaḥ.
meaning: not

## asataḥ
ids: gita-02-16-w2
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: as
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: The negative of the present participle sat, standing as a noun, “the unreal.” Śaṅkara sets it beside the self, so it is masculine. The genitive singular does not by itself show masculine against neuter. The a is joined to na.
meaning: of the unreal

## vidyate
ids: gita-02-16-w3, gita-02-16-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: div
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Class 4 vidyate means “there is.” Śaṅkara glosses it with nāsti.
meaning: there is

## bhāvaḥ
ids: gita-02-16-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhāva
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhū
note: Written bhāvo before na.
meaning: being

## abhāvaḥ
ids: gita-02-16-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: abhāva
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhū
note: Negation of bhāva, “ceasing to be.” The a is joined to na. Written abhāvo before vidyate.
meaning: ceasing to be

## sataḥ
ids: gita-02-16-w8
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: as
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Present participle of as, standing as a noun, “the real.” Śaṅkara glosses it as the self, so it is masculine. The genitive singular does not by itself show masculine against neuter.
meaning: of the real

## ubhayoḥ
ids: gita-02-16-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ubhaya
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: dual
note: The dual -ayoḥ is genitive or locative. It is the pair the conclusion is about, the self and what is not the self, so it is genitive and masculine. It joins api.
meaning: of both

## api
ids: gita-02-16-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The a is joined to ubhayoḥ.
meaning: also

## dṛṣṭaḥ
ids: gita-02-16-w11
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: dṛś
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Past participle of dṛś, “seen.” It agrees with antaḥ. Written dṛṣṭo before antaḥ, and that a is dropped.
meaning: seen

## antaḥ
ids: gita-02-16-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: anta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Śaṅkara takes anta as the settled conclusion, nirṇaya. The a is dropped after dṛṣṭo. Written antas before tu.
meaning: the certainty

## tu
ids: gita-02-16-w13
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It joins anayoḥ.
meaning: and

## anayoḥ
ids: gita-02-16-w14
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: dual
note: The dual -ayoḥ is genitive or locative. It is the same pair as ubhayoḥ, so genitive masculine. The a is joined to tu. Written anayos before tattva.
meaning: of these two

## tattva-darśibhiḥ
ids: gita-02-16-w15
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: tattva-darśin
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
root: dṛś
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Those whose way is to see (darśin, from dṛś) the truth (tattva).” The adjective stands as a noun.
meaning: by those who see the truth
