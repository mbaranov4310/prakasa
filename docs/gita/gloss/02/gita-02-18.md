# gita-02-18

## antavantaḥ
ids: gita-02-18-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: antavat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: anta, “an end,” with -vat, “having.” It agrees with dehāḥ. The visarga drops before ime.
meaning: coming to an end

## ime
ids: gita-02-18-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: It agrees with dehāḥ.
meaning: these

## dehāḥ
ids: gita-02-18-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: deha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The visarga drops before nityasya.
meaning: bodies

## nityasya
ids: gita-02-18-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nitya
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: It agrees with śarīriṇaḥ. It joins the u of uktāḥ: nityasyoktāḥ.
meaning: of the eternal

## uktāḥ
ids: gita-02-18-w5
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: vac
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Past participle of vac, “said,” and so “are said.” It agrees with dehāḥ. The u is joined to nityasya.
meaning: are said

## śarīriṇaḥ
ids: gita-02-18-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śarīrin
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: “The one who has a body.” The adjective stands as a noun. Genitive singular.
meaning: of the embodied one

## anāśinaḥ
ids: gita-02-18-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: anāśin
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: “Not subject to destruction.” anāśinaḥ is nominative plural or genitive singular. Śaṅkara takes it with nityasya and aprameyasya, so genitive singular. Written anāśino before aprameyasya, and that a is dropped.
meaning: of the indestructible

## aprameyasya
ids: gita-02-18-w8
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: mā
prefix: pra
participle: potential (-ya)
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Negated potential participle of pra-mā, “not to be measured.” It agrees with śarīriṇaḥ. The a is dropped after anāśino.
meaning: of what cannot be measured

## tasmāt
ids: gita-02-18-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: Ablative of tad, “from that,” and so “therefore.” Written tasmād before yudhyasva.
meaning: therefore

## yudhyasva
ids: gita-02-18-w10
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: yudh
class: div
tense: command
voice: ātmanepada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: Imperative of yudh, class 4. The subject “you” is not spoken.
meaning: fight

## bhārata
ids: gita-02-18-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhārata
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
meaning: O Bharata
