# gita-02-05

## gurūn
ids: gita-02-05-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: guru
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: It joins ahatvā.
meaning: the teachers

## ahatvā
ids: gita-02-05-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: han
note: A negative absolutive, a-hatvā, “without killing.” With śreyaḥ it is what the eating is better than. The a is the inherent vowel of the n of gurūn.
meaning: without killing

## hi
ids: gita-02-05-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: indeed

## mahā-anubhāvān
ids: gita-02-05-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mahā-anubhāva
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Whose dignity (anubhāva) is great (mahā).” It agrees with gurūn.
meaning: men of great character

## śreyaḥ
ids: gita-02-05-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śreyas
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Comparative of praśasya, “better.” The masculine would be śreyān, so this is neuter. There is no finite verb. Written śreyo before bhoktum.
meaning: better

## bhoktum
ids: gita-02-05-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: bhuj
note: An infinitive in -tum. It is what is better.
meaning: to eat

## bhaikṣam
ids: gita-02-05-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhaikṣa
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: From bhikṣā. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what one would eat, so it is accusative. The m joins api.
meaning: food got by begging

## api
ids: gita-02-05-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It joins iha: apīha.
meaning: even

## iha
ids: gita-02-05-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The i is joined to api.
meaning: here

## loke
ids: gita-02-05-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: loka
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
meaning: in this world

## hatvā
ids: gita-02-05-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: han
note: An absolutive in -tvā. It joins artha: hatvārtha.
meaning: having killed

## artha-kāmān
ids: gita-02-05-w12
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: artha-kāma
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Whose desire (kāma) is gain (artha).” It agrees with the second gurūn. The a is joined to hatvā. Written -kāmāṃs before tu.
meaning: men whose desire is gain

## tu
ids: gita-02-05-w13
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: but

## gurūn
ids: gita-02-05-w14

judgment: obvious
stem: guru

note: The n joins the i of iha.

## iha
ids: gita-02-05-w15
kind: indeclinable

## eva

## bhuñjīya

kind: verb
judgment: obvious

class: rudh
tense: potential

person: 1st
number: singular

ids: gita-02-05-w18

judgment: obvious
stem: bhoga
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural

## rudhira-pradigdhān
ids: gita-02-05-w19
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: rudhira-pradigdha
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
root: dih
