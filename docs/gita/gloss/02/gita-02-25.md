# gita-02-25

## avyaktaḥ
ids: gita-02-25-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: avyakta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: añj
prefix: vi
note: a- (“not”) with vyakta, the past participle of añj with vi, “manifest.” Written avyakto before the a of ayam, and that a is dropped.
meaning: unmanifest

## ayam
ids: gita-02-25-w2, gita-02-25-w4, gita-02-25-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The initial a of each is dropped after the preceding o, and the print marks that with an avagraha. The third is written yamu before cyate.
meaning: he

## acintyaḥ
ids: gita-02-25-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: acintya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: cint
note: a- (“not”) with cintya, the potential participle in -ya of cint. Written acintyo before the a of ayam, and that a is dropped.
meaning: unthinkable

## ucyate
ids: gita-02-25-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Passive of vac, “is called.”

## tasmāt
ids: gita-02-25-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: The ablative singular is the same in masculine and neuter. It points back to what was just said, so it is neuter. “From that” is “therefore.” Written tasmāde before vam: t becomes d, and the e of evam is joined to it.
meaning: therefore

## evam
ids: gita-02-25-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Its e stands with tasmāt, so the slice is vam.
meaning: in this way

## viditvā
ids: gita-02-25-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: vid
note: An absolutive in -tvā of vid, “to know.” Written viditvai before nam: ā joins the e of enam.
meaning: having known

## enam
ids: gita-02-25-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: enam is the accusative idam takes for someone already spoken of, here the self. Its e joins viditvā.
meaning: him

## na
ids: gita-02-25-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Its a joins anuśocitum: nānuśocitum.
meaning: not

## anuśocitum
ids: gita-02-25-w13
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: śuc
prefix: anu
note: An infinitive in -tum. Its initial a joins na.
meaning: to grieve

## arhasi
ids: gita-02-25-w14
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: arh

voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
