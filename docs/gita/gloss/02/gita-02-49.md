## dūreṇa
ids: gita-02-49-w1

meaning: by far

judgment: obvious

case: nominative

## karma
ids: gita-02-49-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: The neuter form karma is nominative or accusative. It is what is inferior, so it is nominative.
