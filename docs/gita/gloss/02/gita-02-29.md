# gita-02-29

## āścaryavat
ids: gita-02-29-w1, gita-02-29-w5, gita-02-29-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Adverb in -vat, “like a wonder,” from āścarya. The second is written -vad before vadati. The third is written āścaryavac before ca.
meaning: as a wonder

## paśyati
ids: gita-02-29-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dṛś
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The present stem of dṛś is paśya.
meaning: sees

## kaścit
ids: gita-02-29-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: kim
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: kim with cit. kaḥ + cit becomes kaścit. Written kaścide before na: the e of enam is joined to it.
meaning: someone

## enam
ids: gita-02-29-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: enam is the accusative idam takes for the self already spoken of. The line breaks after na, and the m is written at the start of the next line.
meaning: him
