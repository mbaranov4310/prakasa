# gita-02-19

## yaḥ
ids: gita-02-19-w1, gita-02-19-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The first loses its visarga before enam. The second is written yaś before ca.
meaning: he who

## enam
ids: gita-02-19-w2, gita-02-19-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: ena
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: enam is the accusative of the enclitic demonstrative ena, which has no nominative. Śaṅkara takes it as the embodied one already in question. The second loses its e after ca: cainam.
meaning: this one

## vetti
ids: gita-02-19-w3
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Present of vid, class 2, “knows.”
meaning: knows

## hantāram
ids: gita-02-19-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: hantṛ
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: han
note: Agent noun from han, “killer.” It agrees with enam: he knows him as the killer.
meaning: as the killer

## ca
ids: gita-02-19-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It joins the second enam: cainam.
meaning: and

## manyate
ids: gita-02-19-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: man
class: div
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: man, class 4, “thinks.”
meaning: thinks

## hatam
ids: gita-02-19-w9
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: han
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Past participle of han, “killed.” It agrees with enam: he thinks him killed.
meaning: killed

## ubhau
ids: gita-02-19-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ubha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: dual
note: The dual ubhau is nominative or accusative. They are the ones who do not understand, so it is nominative. It agrees with tau.
meaning: both

## tau
ids: gita-02-19-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: dual
note: tau is nominative or accusative dual. It is the subject of vijānītaḥ, so it is nominative.
meaning: those two

## na
ids: gita-02-19-w12, gita-02-19-w14, gita-02-19-w17
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The second joins ayam: nāyam.
meaning: not

## vijānītaḥ
ids: gita-02-19-w13
kind: verb
judgment: obvious

class: krī
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: dual
note: jñā is class 9. The dual ending -ītaḥ is parasmaipada. Written vijānīto before na.
meaning: understand
