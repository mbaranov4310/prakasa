# gita-02-37

## hataḥ
ids: gita-02-37-w1
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: han
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with the unstated “you” of prāpsyasi. Written hato before vā.
meaning: slain

## vā
ids: gita-02-37-w2, gita-02-37-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: or

## prāpsyasi
ids: gita-02-37-w3
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: āp
prefix: pra
class: su
tense: simple future
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: Future of āp with pra. The present is āpnoti, class 5. The future stem does not show the class. The subject “you” is not spoken.
meaning: you will reach

## tasmāt
ids: gita-02-37-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: The ablative singular is the same in masculine and neuter. It points back to the two outcomes, so it is neuter. Written tasmādu before ttiṣṭha: t becomes d, and the u of uttiṣṭha is joined to it.
meaning: therefore

ids: gita-02-37-w10

person: 2nd
number: singular
note: Command of sthā with ut. The present stem is tiṣṭha. The subject “you” is not spoken. Its u stands with tasmāt.
meaning: stand up

## kṛta-niścayaḥ
ids: gita-02-37-w13
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kṛta-niścaya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose resolve (niścaya) is made (kṛta, past participle of kṛ).” It agrees with the unstated “you” of uttiṣṭha. The resolve is to conquer, or to die.
meaning: resolved (to conquer, or to die)
