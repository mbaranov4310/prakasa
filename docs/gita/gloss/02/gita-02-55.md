# gita-02-55

## śrī-bhagavān
ids: gita-02-55-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śrī-bhagavat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: bhagavat is bhaga (“fortune, lordship”) with the suffix -vat, “having.” śrī is a title of honour set before it. Written śrībhagavānu: the u of uvāca joins it.
meaning: the blessed Lord

## uvāca
ids: gita-02-55-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Its u is written on śrī-bhagavān.
meaning: said

number: singular
