stem: sthiti

case: nominative
number: singular

## pārtha
ids: gita-02-72-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pārtha
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular

## na
ids: gita-02-72-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written nai: its a and the e of enām join into ai.
meaning: not

## enām
ids: gita-02-72-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: enām is the feminine accusative of ena, the form etad takes when it points back. It takes up eṣā sthitiḥ. The e joins na.
meaning: this

## prāpya
ids: gita-02-72-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: āp
prefix: pra
note: An absolutive in -ya.
meaning: reaching

## vimuhyati
ids: gita-02-72-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: muh
prefix: vi
class: div
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The subject is unstated. na negates it: reaching this state, one is not confused.
meaning: one is confused

## sthitvā
ids: gita-02-72-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
note: An absolutive in -tvā. Its ā and the a of asyām join into ā.
meaning: standing

## asyām
ids: gita-02-72-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: feminine
case: locative
number: singular
note: Feminine, taking up the state. The a joins sthitvā.
meaning: in it

## anta-kāle
ids: gita-02-72-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: anta-kāla
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “The time (kāla) of the end (anta).” The a joins asyām. Written -kāle before api, and the a of api is dropped.
meaning: at the time of death

## api
ids: gita-02-72-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The a is dropped after anta-kāle.
meaning: even

## brahma-nirvāṇam
ids: gita-02-72-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahma-nirvāṇa
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: vā
prefix: nir
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “The peace (nirvāṇa, from nir-vā) of Brahman.” Śaṅkara takes it as liberation. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he attains, so it is accusative. The ṛ of ṛcchati is written on it.
meaning: the peace of Brahman

## ṛcchati
ids: gita-02-72-w14
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: ṛ
class: tud
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The ṛ is written on brahma-nirvāṇam. The subject is the one who stands in the state. He is not named here.
meaning: he attains
