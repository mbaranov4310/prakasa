## yadā

kind: indeclinable

## te
ids: gita-02-52-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: te is dative or genitive. With buddhiḥ it is “your,” so it is genitive. yuṣmad has no gender.
meaning: your

## moha-kalilam
ids: gita-02-52-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: moha-kalila
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “The mire (kalila) of delusion (moha).” Śaṅkara takes it as the impurity that consists of delusion. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what the understanding crosses, so it is accusative.
meaning: the mire of delusion

number: singular

meaning: the understanding

## nirvedam

stem: nirveda
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
