## prasanna-cetasaḥ
ids: gita-02-65-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: prasanna-cetas
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose mind (cetas) is clear (prasanna).” prasanna is the past participle of pra-sad. The ending -cetasaḥ is genitive or ablative. It is the one whose judgment settles, so it is genitive. It stands as the noun. Written prasannacetaso before hi.
meaning: of one whose mind is clear
