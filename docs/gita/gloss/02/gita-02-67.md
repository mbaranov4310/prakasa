## yat
ids: gita-02-67-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter yat is nominative or accusative. It is the mind which follows, so it is nominative. Written yan before manaḥ.
meaning: which

## manaḥ
ids: gita-02-67-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: manas
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: man
note: The neuter manaḥ is nominative or accusative. It is the mind which follows, so it is nominative. Written mano before the a of anuvidhīyate, and that a is dropped.
meaning: mind

## anuvidhīyate
ids: gita-02-67-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dhā
prefix: anu-vi
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Passive of dhā, “to place,” with anu-vi: “is set to follow.” Śaṅkara glosses it anupravartate. The a is dropped after manaḥ.
meaning: follows along
