# gita-02-06

## na
ids: gita-02-06-w1, gita-02-06-w18
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## ca
ids: gita-02-06-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It joins etat: caitat.
meaning: and

## etat
ids: gita-02-06-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter etat is nominative or accusative. It is what we do not know, so it is accusative. The e is joined to ca.
meaning: this

## vidmaḥ
ids: gita-02-06-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: plural
note: vid “to know” is class 2. The subject “we” is not spoken.
meaning: we know

## katarat
ids: gita-02-06-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: katara
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: “Which of the two.” The neuter katarat is nominative or accusative. It is what is weightier, so it is nominative. The t joins naḥ.
meaning: which of the two

## naḥ
ids: gita-02-06-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: dative
number: singular
note: naḥ is accusative, dative, or genitive. With garīyaḥ it is “weightier for us,” so it is dative. asmad has no gender.
meaning: for us

## garīyaḥ
ids: gita-02-06-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: garīyas
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Comparative of guru, with the suffix -īyas. The masculine would be garīyān, so this is neuter. There is no verb. Written garīyo before yat.
meaning: is the weightier

## yat
ids: gita-02-06-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: With vā and yadi vā it means “whether … or.” It sets out the two things katarat asks about. The d joins vā.
meaning: whether

## vā
ids: gita-02-06-w9, gita-02-06-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: or

## jayema
ids: gita-02-06-w10
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: ji
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: plural
note: Optative of ji. The subject “we” is not spoken.
meaning: we should conquer

## yadi
ids: gita-02-06-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: if

number: singular

## yān

## eva

## hatvā

## jijīviṣāmaḥ
ids: gita-02-06-w19
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: jīv
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: plural
note: Desiderative of jīv, “wish to live.” The subject “we” is not spoken. The visarga becomes s before te, and the line breaks before that s.
meaning: we would wish to live

## te
ids: gita-02-06-w20
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The s of jijīviṣāmaḥ is written with it, and the a of avasthitāḥ is dropped after it.
meaning: they

## avasthitāḥ
ids: gita-02-06-w21
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: ava
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Past participle of ava-sthā, “standing.” It agrees with te. There is no finite verb. The a is dropped after te.
meaning: standing
