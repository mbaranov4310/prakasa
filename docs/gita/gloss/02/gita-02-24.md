# gita-02-24

## acchedyaḥ
ids: gita-02-24-w1
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: chid
participle: potential (-ya)
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Negated potential participle of chid, “not to be cut.” It agrees with ayam. Written acchedyo before ayam, and that a is dropped.
meaning: unable to be cut

## ayam
ids: gita-02-24-w2, gita-02-24-w4, gita-02-24-w13
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The a is dropped after a preceding o: acchedyo 'yam, adāhyo 'yam, acalo 'yam.
meaning: he

## adāhyaḥ
ids: gita-02-24-w3
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: dah
participle: potential (-ya)
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Negated potential participle of dah, “not to be burned.” It agrees with the second ayam. Its a is the vowel of the m of the first ayam. Written adāhyo before the next ayam, and that a is dropped.
meaning: unable to be burned

## akledyaḥ
ids: gita-02-24-w5
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: klid
participle: potential (-ya)
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Negated potential participle of klid, “not to be wetted.” It agrees with the second ayam. Its a is the vowel of the m of that ayam. Written akledyo before aśoṣyaḥ, and that a is dropped.
meaning: unable to be wetted

## aśoṣyaḥ
ids: gita-02-24-w6
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: śuṣ
participle: potential (-ya)
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Negated potential participle of śuṣ, “not to be dried.” The a is dropped after akledyo. The visarga drops before eva.
meaning: unable to be dried

## sarvagataḥ
