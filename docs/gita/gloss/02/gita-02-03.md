# gita-02-03

## klaibyam
ids: gita-02-03-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: klaibya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he should not give way to, so it is accusative.
meaning: unmanliness

## mā
ids: gita-02-03-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: do not

## sma
ids: gita-02-03-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: With mā it marks the prohibition.
meaning: (with मा)

## gamaḥ
ids: gita-02-03-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: gam
tense: recent past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: With mā and sma, the augmentless aorist is a prohibition. The subject “you” is not spoken.
meaning: give way

## pārtha
ids: gita-02-03-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pārtha
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
meaning: O son of Pritha

## na
ids: gita-02-03-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It joins etat: naitat.
meaning: not

## etat
ids: gita-02-03-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter etat is nominative or accusative. It is what does not befit him, so it is nominative. Joined to na: naitat. It joins tvayi.
meaning: this

## tvayi
ids: gita-02-03-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: locative
number: singular
note: yuṣmad has no gender. It joins the u of upapadyate.
meaning: in you

## upapadyate
ids: gita-02-03-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: pad
prefix: upa
class: div
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The u is joined to tvayi.
meaning: befits

## kṣudram
ids: gita-02-03-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kṣudra
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with hṛdaya-daurbalyam.
meaning: petty

## hṛdaya-daurbalyam
ids: gita-02-03-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: hṛdaya-daurbalya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Weakness (daurbalya) of the heart (hṛdaya).” The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he should cast off, so it is accusative.
meaning: weakness of heart

## tyaktvā
ids: gita-02-03-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: tyaj
note: An absolutive in -tvā. It joins uttiṣṭha: tyaktvottiṣṭha.
meaning: having cast off

## uttiṣṭha
ids: gita-02-03-w13
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: ud
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: Imperative of ud-sthā. The subject “you” is not spoken. The u is joined to tyaktvā.
meaning: stand up

## parantapa

judgment: obvious
stem: parantapa
gender: masculine

number: singular
