# gita-02-70

## āpūryamāṇam
ids: gita-02-70-w1
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: pṝ
prefix: ā
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Present participle, passive, of ā-pṝ, “being filled.” Masculine accusative, agreeing with samudram. The nominative would be āpūryamāṇaḥ. The m joins acala-pratiṣṭham.
meaning: being filled

## acala-pratiṣṭham
ids: gita-02-70-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: acala-pratiṣṭha
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Whose stance (pratiṣṭhā) is unmoving (acala).” Masculine accusative, agreeing with samudram. The nominative would be acala-pratiṣṭhaḥ. The a joins āpūryamāṇam.
meaning: standing unmoved

## samudram
ids: gita-02-70-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: samudra
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It is what the waters enter, so it is accusative. Written samudramā: the ā of āpaḥ joins it.
meaning: the ocean

## āpaḥ
ids: gita-02-70-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ap
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: āpaḥ is nominative or accusative plural of ap, “water.” The waters are what enter, so it is nominative. The ā is written on samudram.
meaning: the waters
