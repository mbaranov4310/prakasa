# gita-02-26

## atha
ids: gita-02-26-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: With ca, atha opens a supposition: grant the other view.
meaning: or else

## ca
ids: gita-02-26-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written cai before nam: a joins the e of enam.
meaning: and (granting this)

## enam
ids: gita-02-26-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: enam is the accusative idam takes for the self already spoken of. Its e joins ca.
meaning: him

## nitya-jātam
ids: gita-02-26-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nitya-jāta
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: jan
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: jāta is the past participle of jan. “Born (jāta) perpetually (nitya).” The ending -am agrees with enam, so it is masculine accusative.
meaning: constantly born

## nityam
ids: gita-02-26-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The neuter accusative singular of nitya, used as an adverb with mṛtam.
meaning: constantly

## vā
ids: gita-02-26-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: or

## manyase
ids: gita-02-26-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: man
class: div
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject “you” is not spoken. The present manyate is class 4.
meaning: you think

## mṛtam
ids: gita-02-26-w8
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: mṛ
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with enam: you think him dead.
meaning: dead

## tathā
ids: gita-02-26-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Its ā joins api: tathāpi.
meaning: so

## api
ids: gita-02-26-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Its a joins tathā. With tathā: “even so.”
meaning: even

## tvam
ids: gita-02-26-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: yuṣmad has no gender.
meaning: you

## mahābāho
ids: gita-02-26-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mahābāhu
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose arms (bāhu) are great (mahā).” Vocative singular of the stem in -u.
meaning: O mighty-armed

## na
ids: gita-02-26-w13
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written nai before vam: a joins the e of evam.
meaning: not

## evam
ids: gita-02-26-w14
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Its e joins na, so the slice is vam.
meaning: in this way

## śocitum
ids: gita-02-26-w15
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: śuc
note: An infinitive in -tum.
meaning: to grieve

## arhasi
ids: gita-02-26-w16
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: arh
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
meaning: you ought
