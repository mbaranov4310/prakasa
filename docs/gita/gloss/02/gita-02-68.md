# gita-02-68

## tasmāt
ids: gita-02-68-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: The ablative singular is the same in masculine and neuter. It points back to the argument just made, so it is neuter. “From that” is “therefore.” Written tasmād before yasya.
meaning: therefore

## yasya
ids: gita-02-68-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
meaning: whose

## mahābāho
ids: gita-02-68-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mahābāhu
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “O you whose arm (bāhu) is great (mahā).” Vocative. The pada is not hyphenated.
meaning: O mighty-armed

## nigṛhītāni
ids: gita-02-68-w4
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: grah
prefix: ni
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The past participle of ni-grah. The neuter plural -āni is nominative or accusative. It describes indriyāṇi, so it is nominative.
meaning: are held in

## sarvaśaḥ
ids: gita-02-68-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: sarva with the suffix -śas, “on every side.”
meaning: on every side

## indriyāṇi
ids: gita-02-68-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The neuter plural -āṇi is nominative or accusative. The senses are what are held in, so it is nominative. Its i and the i of indriya-arthebhyaḥ join into ī.
meaning: the senses

## indriya-arthebhyaḥ
ids: gita-02-68-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya-artha
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: plural
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “The objects (artha) of the senses (indriya).” The i joins indriyāṇi. Written -ebhyas before tasya.
meaning: from the objects of the senses
