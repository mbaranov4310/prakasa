# gita-02-56

## duḥkheṣu
ids: gita-02-56-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: duḥkha
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: plural
note: Written duḥkheṣv before anudvigna-manāḥ.
meaning: in pains

## anudvigna-manāḥ
ids: gita-02-56-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: anudvigna-manas
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose mind (manas) is unshaken (anudvigna).” anudvigna is a- with the past participle of ud-vij. The a joins the u of duḥkheṣu.
meaning: whose mind is unshaken

## vigata-spṛhaḥ
ids: gita-02-56-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vigata-spṛha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose longing (spṛhā) has gone (vigata).” vigata is the past participle of vi-gam.
meaning: whose longing has gone

## vīta-rāga-bhaya-krodhaḥ
ids: gita-02-56-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vīta-rāga-bhaya-krodha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One from whom attraction, fear, and anger have gone (vīta).” vīta is the past participle of vi-i. The three are a dvandva. Śaṅkara reads it that way.
meaning: from whom attraction, fear, and anger have gone

## sthita-dhīḥ
ids: gita-02-56-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sthita-dhī
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Whose mind (dhī) is steady (sthita).” sthita is the past participle of sthā. Written sthitadhīr before muniḥ.
meaning: of steady mind

## muniḥ
