# gita-02-20

## na
ids: gita-02-20-w1, gita-02-20-w6, gita-02-20-w11, gita-02-20-w18
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The na before ayam is written across the line break: kadācin nāyam.
meaning: not

## jāyate
ids: gita-02-20-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: jan
class: div
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Class 4 jāyate, “is born.”
meaning: is born

## mriyate
ids: gita-02-20-w3
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: mṛ
class: tud
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Class 6 mriyate, “dies.”
meaning: dies

## vā
ids: gita-02-20-w4, gita-02-20-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Śaṅkara takes vā in the sense of “and.”
meaning: or

## kadācit
ids: gita-02-20-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The line breaks before the n that sandhi writes with the next na.
meaning: at any time

## ayam
ids: gita-02-20-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The a is joined to na: nāyam.
meaning: this one

## bhūtvā
ids: gita-02-20-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
note: An absolutive in -tvā, “having come to be.” It leans on bhavitā.
meaning: having come to be

## bhavitā
ids: gita-02-20-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
tense: distant future
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Periphrastic future of bhū. With the two na and with vā, Śaṅkara reads it twice: having come to be, he will not cease, and, not having been, he will not come to be again.
meaning: will come to be

## bhūyaḥ
ids: gita-02-20-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The neuter accusative of bhūyas, “more, again,” used as an adverb.
meaning: again

## ajaḥ
ids: gita-02-20-w13
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: aja
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: jan
note: “Unborn,” a-ja, from jan. It agrees with the second ayam.
meaning: unborn

## nityaḥ
ids: gita-02-20-w14
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nitya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with the second ayam.
meaning: eternal

## śāśvataḥ
ids: gita-02-20-w15
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śāśvata
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Śaṅkara takes it as “not subject to dwindling,” śaśvad-bhāva. It agrees with the second ayam. Written śāśvato before ayam, and that a is dropped.
meaning: constant

## ayam
ids: gita-02-20-w16
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The a is dropped after śāśvato.
meaning: this

## purāṇaḥ
ids: gita-02-20-w17
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: purāṇa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Śaṅkara takes it as “not subject to growth”: ancient, and still as it was. It agrees with ayam.
meaning: ancient

## hanyate
ids: gita-02-20-w19
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: han
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Passive of han. Śaṅkara reads the root here as change of state, so that it does not repeat 2.19.
meaning: is killed
