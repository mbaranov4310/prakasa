# gita-02-44

## bhoga-aiśvarya-prasaktānām
ids: gita-02-44-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: bhoga-aiśvarya-prasakta
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
root: sañj
prefix: pra
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: prasakta is the past participle of sañj with pra, “clung to.” “Attached to pleasure and power.”
meaning: of those attached to pleasure and power

## tayā
ids: gita-02-44-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: feminine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: Feminine, taking up vācam, the flowery speech of 2.42. Written tayā before pahṛta-: the a of apahṛta is joined to it.
meaning: by that (speech)

## apahṛta-cetasām
ids: gita-02-44-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: apahṛta-cetas
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those whose mind (cetas) is carried off (apahṛta, past participle of hṛ with apa).” It agrees with bhoga-aiśvarya-prasaktānām. Its initial a joins tayā.
meaning: whose minds are carried off

## vyavasāya-ātmikā
ids: gita-02-44-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vyavasāya-ātmika
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Whose nature (ātman, with the suffix -ika) is resolve (vyavasāya).” Feminine to agree with buddhiḥ.
meaning: whose nature is resolve
