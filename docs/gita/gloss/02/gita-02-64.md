# gita-02-64

## rāga-dveṣa-viyuktaiḥ
ids: gita-02-64-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: rāga-dveṣa-viyukta
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: plural
root: yuj
prefix: vi
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: viyukta is the past participle of vi-yuj. “Freed from attraction and aversion,” a dvandva. The instrumental plural -aiḥ is the same for masculine and neuter. It agrees with the senses, so it is neuter. Written -viyuktais before tu.
meaning: freed from attraction and aversion

## tu
ids: gita-02-64-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: but

## viṣayān
ids: gita-02-64-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: viṣaya
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: The i of indriyaiḥ is written on it.
meaning: objects (those he cannot leave aside)

## indriyaiḥ
ids: gita-02-64-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: The instrumental plural -aiḥ is the same for masculine and neuter. These are the senses, so it is neuter. The i joins viṣayān. Written indriyaiś before caran.
meaning: with the senses

## caran
ids: gita-02-64-w5
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: car
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Present participle of car. It agrees with vidheya-ātmā.
meaning: moving among them

## ātma-vaśyaiḥ
ids: gita-02-64-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ātma-vaśya
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: plural
root: vaś
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: vaśya is the potential participle of vaś, “to be controlled.” “Under the control of the self (ātman).” It agrees with indriyaiḥ, so it is neuter. Written -vaśyair before vidheyātmā.
meaning: under his own control

## vidheya-ātmā
ids: gita-02-64-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vidheya-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose mind (ātman, the inner organ) is at his command (vidheya).” vidheya is the potential participle of vi-dhā. The nominative singular of the bahuvrīhi in -an ends in -ā.
meaning: whose mind is at his command
