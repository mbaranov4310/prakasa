## sarvatra
ids: gita-02-57-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: sarva with the suffix -tra, “in every place.” Written sarvatrā: its a and the a of anabhisnehaḥ join into ā.
meaning: anywhere (even toward body and life)

gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: a- (“not”) with abhisneha, “clinging.” The a joins sarvatra. Written anabhisnehas before tat.
meaning: without clinging

## tat
ids: gita-02-57-w4

judgment: obvious

gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular

## tat
ids: gita-02-57-w5
