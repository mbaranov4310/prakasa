# gita-02-39

## eṣā
ids: gita-02-39-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Feminine to agree with buddhiḥ.
meaning: this

## te
ids: gita-02-39-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: dative
number: singular
note: te is dative or genitive. It is the person the understanding was declared to, so it is dative. yuṣmad has no gender. Written te before the a of abhihitā, and that a is dropped.
meaning: to you

## abhihitā
ids: gita-02-39-w3
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: dhā
prefix: abhi
participle: past
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Passive in sense, “declared.” Its initial a is dropped after te. There is no finite verb. “Has been” is understood.
meaning: has been declared

## sāṃkhye
ids: gita-02-39-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sāṃkhya
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
note: Neuter, the Sāṃkhya teaching. The locative is the same for masculine.
meaning: in Sāṃkhya (knowledge of the highest reality)

ids: gita-02-39-w5
kind: noun

stem: buddhi
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: budh
note: Written buddhir before yoge.

## yoge
ids: gita-02-39-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga

number: singular
root: yuj
meaning: in yoga (the means of reaching that knowledge)

## tu
ids: gita-02-39-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written tvi before mām: u becomes v, and the i of imām is joined to it.
meaning: now

## imām
ids: gita-02-39-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Feminine, with the buddhi of yoga that is to be heard. Its i stands with tu, so the slice is mām.
meaning: this

## prahāsyasi
ids: gita-02-39-w15
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: hā
prefix: pra
class: hu
tense: simple future
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: Future of hā with pra, “to abandon.” The present is jahāti, class 3. The future stem does not show the class. The subject “you” is not spoken.
meaning: you will cast off
