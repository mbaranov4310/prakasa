# gita-02-17

## avināśi
ids: gita-02-17-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: avināśin
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: “Not of a nature to perish.” The neuter of a stem in -in is avināśi, nominative or accusative. It is what one should know tat to be, so it is accusative.
meaning: what does not perish

## tu
ids: gita-02-17-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Śaṅkara takes tu as setting this apart from the unreal.
meaning: by contrast

## tat
ids: gita-02-17-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter tat is nominative or accusative. It is what one should know, so it is accusative. It joins viddhi.
meaning: that

## viddhi
ids: gita-02-17-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: ad
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: Imperative of vid, class 2. The subject “you” is not spoken. The d is joined to tat.
meaning: know

## yena
ids: gita-02-17-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: Instrumental of yad, “by which.” It refers back to tat.
meaning: by which

## sarvam
ids: gita-02-17-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It agrees with idam, what is pervaded, so it is nominative. It joins idam.
meaning: all

## idam
ids: gita-02-17-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The i is joined to sarvam. It is what is pervaded.
meaning: this

## tatam
ids: gita-02-17-w8
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: tan
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Past participle of tan, “stretched,” and so pervaded. It agrees with idam. There is no finite verb.
meaning: pervaded

## vināśam
ids: gita-02-17-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: vināśa
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: naś
prefix: vi
note: “Destruction,” from vi-naś. It is what no one can bring about, so it is accusative. It joins avyayasya.
meaning: destruction

## avyayasya
ids: gita-02-17-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: avyaya
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: singular
note: “Not subject to decay.” It agrees with asya. The a is joined to vināśam.
meaning: of the unchanging

## asya
ids: gita-02-17-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Genitive of idam. The same unchanging one as tat.
meaning: of this

## na
ids: gita-02-17-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## kaścit
ids: gita-02-17-w13
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: kim
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The masculine nominative of kim with cit, “anyone.” It joins kartum.
meaning: anyone

## kartum
ids: gita-02-17-w14
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
note: An infinitive in -tum. The k is joined to kaścit, and the m joins arhati.
meaning: to bring about

## arhati
ids: gita-02-17-w15
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: arh
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The a is joined to kartum.
meaning: is able
