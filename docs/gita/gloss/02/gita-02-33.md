# gita-02-33

## atha
ids: gita-02-33-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: atha opens the other case, with cet.
meaning: but

## cet
ids: gita-02-33-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: “If.” Written cet before tvam.
meaning: if

## tvam
ids: gita-02-33-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: yuṣmad has no gender. Written tvami before mam: the i of imam is joined to it.
meaning: you

## imam
ids: gita-02-33-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Masculine, agreeing with saṃgrāmam. Its i stands with tvam, so the slice is mam.
meaning: this

## dharmyam
ids: gita-02-33-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: dharmya
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with saṃgrāmam.
meaning: lawful

## saṃgrāmam
ids: gita-02-33-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃgrāma
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
meaning: battle

## na
ids: gita-02-33-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## kariṣyasi
ids: gita-02-33-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
class: tan
tense: simple future
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: Future of kṛ. The present is karoti, class 8. The future stem does not show the class.
meaning: you will do

## tataḥ
ids: gita-02-33-w9

judgment: obvious
note: “Then,” with the ending -tas.
meaning: then

## pāpam
ids: gita-02-33-w14
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pāpa
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is what you will incur, so it is accusative. Written pāpam before avāpsyasi.
meaning: guilt

## avāpsyasi
ids: gita-02-33-w15
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: āp
prefix: ava
class: su
tense: simple future
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: Future of āp with ava. The present is āpnoti, class 5. The future stem does not show the class. Its initial a joins pāpam.
meaning: you will incur
