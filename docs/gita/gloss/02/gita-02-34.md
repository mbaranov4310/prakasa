# gita-02-34

## akīrtim
ids: gita-02-34-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: akīrti
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṝ
note: a- (“not”) with kīrti, “fame.”
meaning: disgrace

## ca
ids: gita-02-34-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written cā before pi.
meaning: and

## api
ids: gita-02-34-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Its a joins ca, so the slice is pi.
meaning: also

## bhūtāni
ids: gita-02-34-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūta
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: bhū
note: bhūta is the past participle of bhū, used as a noun, here the people who will talk. The neuter plural -āni is nominative or accusative. It is what does the telling, so it is nominative.
meaning: people

## kathayiṣyanti
ids: gita-02-34-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: kath
tense: simple future
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: Causative of kath, “to tell.”
meaning: will tell

## te
ids: gita-02-34-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: te is the short form of yuṣmad for the dative or the genitive. With akīrtim it is genitive, “your.” yuṣmad has no gender. Written te before the a of avyayām, and that a is dropped.
meaning: your

## avyayām
ids: gita-02-34-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: avyaya
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: a- (“not”) with vyaya, “passing away”: lasting. Feminine to agree with akīrtim. Its initial a is dropped after te.
meaning: lasting

## saṃbhāvitasya
ids: gita-02-34-w8
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
prefix: sam
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Causative of bhū with sam, “held in honor.” Standing as a noun.
meaning: for one held in honor

## ca
ids: gita-02-34-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written cā before kīrtiḥ: a joins the a of akīrtiḥ.
meaning: and

## akīrtiḥ
ids: gita-02-34-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: akīrti
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṝ
note: a- (“not”) with kīrti. Written akīrtir before maraṇāt. Its initial a joins ca.
meaning: disgrace

## maraṇāt
ids: gita-02-34-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: maraṇa
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
root: mṛ
note: Ablative of comparison, “than.” Written maraṇād before atiricyate.
meaning: than death

## atiricyate
ids: gita-02-34-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: ric
prefix: ati
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Passive of ric with ati, “is left over,” and so “goes beyond.” What it surpasses stands in the ablative.
meaning: goes beyond
