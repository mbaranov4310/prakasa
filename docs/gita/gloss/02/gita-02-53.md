# gita-02-53

## śruti-vipratipannā
ids: gita-02-53-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śruti-vipratipanna
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: pad
prefix: vi-prati
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: vipratipanna is the past participle of vi-prati-pad. “Pulled in many directions by what is heard (śruti).” Feminine to agree with buddhiḥ.
meaning: distracted by what is heard

## te
ids: gita-02-53-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: te is dative or genitive. With buddhiḥ it is “your,” so it is genitive. yuṣmad has no gender.
meaning: your

## samādhau
ids: gita-02-53-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: samādhi
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
root: dhā
prefix: sam-ā
note: Śaṅkara glosses samādhi as the Self, that in which the mind is collected. Written samādhāv before acalā: au before a becomes āv.
meaning: in the Self
