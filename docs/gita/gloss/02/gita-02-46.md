# gita-02-46

## yāvān
ids: gita-02-46-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yāvat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: “As much,” answered by tāvān. The nominative singular of the stem in -vant is -vān. It agrees with arthaḥ. Written yāvān before arthaḥ; the a of arthaḥ is read with the n.
meaning: as much

## arthaḥ
ids: gita-02-46-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: artha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Its initial a is the inherent a of the n in yāvān, so the slice starts at rtha. There is no finite verb. “Is” is understood.
meaning: use (bathing, drinking, and the like)

## udapāne
ids: gita-02-46-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: udapāna
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “A place for drinking (pāna) water (uda),” a reservoir.
meaning: in a reservoir

## sarvataḥ
ids: gita-02-46-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Adverb in -tas, “on every side.”
meaning: on every side

## saṃpluta-udake
ids: gita-02-46-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃpluta-udaka
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
root: plu
prefix: sam
compound: karmadhāraya
note: saṃpluta is the past participle of plu with sam, “flooded,” with udaka, “water.” Locative of the circumstance: when the water is a flood. There is no finite verb.
meaning: when the water is a flood

## tāvān
ids: gita-02-46-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: tāvat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: “That much,” answering yāvān. The use it agrees with is the arthaḥ of the first line, carried over. There is no finite verb. “Is” is understood.
meaning: that much
