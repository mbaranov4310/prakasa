# gita-02-35

## bhayāt
ids: gita-02-35-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhaya
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
root: bhī
note: Ablative of cause, “from, out of.” Written bhayād before raṇāt.
meaning: from fear

## raṇāt
ids: gita-02-35-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: raṇa
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: singular
root: raṇ
note: Ablative of the place left. Written raṇādu before paratam: the u of uparatam is joined to it.
meaning: from the battle

## uparatam
ids: gita-02-35-w3
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: ram
prefix: upa
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: “Withdrawn, ceased.” It agrees with tvām. yuṣmad has no gender; the participle is masculine, for Arjuna. The ending -am is masculine accusative. Its u stands with raṇāt.
meaning: withdrawn

## maṃsyante
ids: gita-02-35-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: man
class: div
tense: simple future
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: Future of man, “they will think.” The present manyate is class 4. The future stem does not show the class.
meaning: they will think

## tvām
