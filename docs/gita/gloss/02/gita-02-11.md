# gita-02-11

## śrī-bhagavān
ids: gita-02-11-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śrī-bhagavat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: śrī with bhagavat. The nominative is bhagavān, and that n joins the u of uvāca.
meaning: the blessed Lord

## uvāca
ids: gita-02-11-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The u is joined to śrī-bhagavān.
meaning: said

## aśocyān
ids: gita-02-11-w3
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: śuc
participle: potential (-ya)
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: Negated potential participle, “not to be grieved for,” standing as the people one should not grieve for. It joins anvaśocaḥ.
meaning: those who should not be grieved for

## anvaśocaḥ
ids: gita-02-11-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: śuc
prefix: anu
tense: ordinary past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: Imperfect of anu-śuc. The augment joins anu: anvaśocaḥ. Written anvaśocas before tvam.
meaning: you have grieved

## tvam
ids: gita-02-11-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: yuṣmad has no gender.
meaning: you

## prajñā-vādān
ids: gita-02-11-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prajñā-vāda
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
root: vad
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “Words (vāda, from vad) of the wise (prajñā).” Śaṅkara takes prajñā as men of wisdom. Written -vādāṃś before ca.
meaning: words of wise men

## ca
ids: gita-02-11-w7, gita-02-11-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## bhāṣase
ids: gita-02-11-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhāṣ
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 2nd
number: singular
meaning: you speak

## gatāsūn
ids: gita-02-11-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: gatāsu
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those whose breath (asu) has gone (gata).” gata is the past participle of gam. The adjective stands as a noun, the dead. It joins agatāsūn.
meaning: the dead

## agatāsūn
ids: gita-02-11-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: agatāsu
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those whose breath has not gone,” the living. The a is joined to gatāsūn. Written -sūṃś before ca.
meaning: the living

## na
ids: gita-02-11-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It joins anuśocanti.
meaning: not

## anuśocanti
ids: gita-02-11-w13
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: śuc
prefix: anu
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: The a is joined to na.
meaning: grieve

## paṇḍitāḥ
ids: gita-02-11-w14
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: paṇḍita
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Śaṅkara takes the wise as those who know the self.
meaning: the wise (those who know the self)
