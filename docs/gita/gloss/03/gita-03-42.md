# gita-03-42

## indriyāṇi
ids: gita-03-42-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The neuter plural -āni is nominative or accusative. They are what is said to be higher, so it is nominative.
meaning: the senses

## parāṇi
ids: gita-03-42-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: para
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The neuter plural -āni is nominative or accusative. It is said of the senses, so it is nominative; the comparison is with the body, which Śaṅkara supplies, and the final i joins with the ā of āhuḥ as yā.
meaning: higher (than the body)

## āhuḥ
ids: gita-03-42-w3
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: brū
class: ad
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: āhuḥ is the perfect that stands for brū, and it means they say. Śaṅkara's subject, the wise, is not a word in the verse; the ā joins the final i of parāṇi as yā, and it is written āhur before indriyebhyaḥ.
meaning: they say

## indriyebhyaḥ
ids: gita-03-42-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: plural
note: indriyebhyaḥ is dative or ablative. The mind is higher than the senses, so it is ablative.
meaning: than the senses

## param
ids: gita-03-42-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: para
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is said of the mind, so it is nominative, and it is written paraṃ before manaḥ.
meaning: higher
