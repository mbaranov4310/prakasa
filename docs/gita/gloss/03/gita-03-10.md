# gita-03-10

## saha-yajñāḥ
ids: gita-03-10-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: saha-yajñā
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Sacrifice is with them.” Śaṅkara glosses yajña-sahitāḥ. The plural -āḥ is nominative or accusative. They are what he brought forth, so it is accusative. It agrees with prajāḥ.
meaning: together with sacrifice

## prajāḥ
ids: gita-03-10-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prajā
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: plural
root: jan
note: From jan with pra. The plural -āḥ is nominative or accusative. They are what he brought forth, so it is accusative. Śaṅkara glosses the three classes.
meaning: creatures

## sṛṣṭvā
ids: gita-03-10-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: sṛj
note: Absolutive, “having brought forth.” Śaṅkara glosses utpādya.
meaning: having brought forth

## purā
ids: gita-03-10-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written puro. The ā joins the u of uvāca and becomes o. Śaṅkara glosses pūrvam, at the beginning of creation.
meaning: of old

## uvāca
ids: gita-03-10-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The initial u is written in the o of puro. Śaṅkara glosses uktavān.
meaning: said

## prajā-patiḥ
ids: gita-03-10-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prajā-pati
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Lord of creatures. Śaṅkara glosses the creator of creatures.
meaning: the lord of creatures

## anena
ids: gita-03-10-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: anena is masculine or neuter. Śaṅkara glosses anena yajñena, by this sacrifice, so it is masculine.
meaning: by this

## prasaviṣyadhvam
ids: gita-03-10-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: sū
prefix: pra
class: ad
tense: command
voice: ātmanepada
person: 2nd
number: plural
note: Command of sū with pra. The stem saviṣya is the future stem, and the ending -dhvam is the 2nd plural command. Śaṅkara glosses kurudhvam: bring about increase. Written prasaviṣyadhvame. The m joins the e of eṣaḥ.
meaning: bring forth increase

## eṣaḥ
ids: gita-03-10-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The initial e is written in prasaviṣyadhvame. The visarga drops before vaḥ, and the slice is ṣa. Śaṅkara glosses eṣa yajñaḥ, this sacrifice.
meaning: this

## vaḥ
ids: gita-03-10-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: genitive
number: plural
note: yuṣmad has no gender. vaḥ is accusative, dative, or genitive, dual or plural. The command is plural, Śaṅkara glosses yuṣmākam, and the sense is “your,” so it is genitive plural. Written vo' before astu.
meaning: your

## astu
ids: gita-03-10-w11
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Śaṅkara glosses bhavatu. The initial a is dropped after o, marked by the avagraha. Written stvi; the u joins the i of iṣṭa.
meaning: let it be

## iṣṭa-kāma-dhuk
ids: gita-03-10-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: iṣṭa-kāma-duh
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: duh
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Agent noun from duh, “it milks.” iṣṭa is the past participle of iṣ, “desired.” It agrees with eṣaḥ, so it is masculine. The initial i is written in stvi. Śaṅkara glosses one that yields the wished-for fruits.
meaning: the yielder of the objects you wish
