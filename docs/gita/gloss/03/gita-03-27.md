# gita-03-27

## prakṛteḥ
ids: gita-03-27-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prakṛti
gender: feminine
case: genitive
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: Built from pra and kṛ. The ending -eḥ is genitive or ablative. It names whose qualities do the actions, so it is genitive.
meaning: of nature

## kriyamāṇāni
ids: gita-03-27-w2
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
participle: present
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Passive. The neuter -āni is nominative or accusative. It agrees with karmāṇi, so it is nominative.
meaning: being done

## guṇaiḥ
ids: gita-03-27-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: guṇa
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
meaning: by the qualities

## karmāṇi
ids: gita-03-27-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: kṛ
note: The neuter -āṇi is nominative or accusative. The actions are what is being done, so it is nominative.
meaning: actions

## sarvaśaḥ
ids: gita-03-27-w5

judgment: obvious
note: An adverb in -śas.

## ahaṃkāra-vimūḍha-ātmā
ids: gita-03-27-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ahaṃkāra-vimūḍha-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: A bahuvrīhi standing as a noun, one whose mind is deluded by the thought "I". vimūḍha is the past participle of muh with vi.
meaning: one whose mind is deluded by the thought "I"
