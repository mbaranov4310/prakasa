# gita-03-35

## śreyān
ids: gita-03-35-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śreyas
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Comparative, “better.” Śaṅkara glosses it praśasyataraḥ. It agrees with sva-dharmaḥ.
meaning: better

## sva-dharmaḥ
ids: gita-03-35-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sva-dharma
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: dhṛ
note: sva and dharma; dharma is from dhṛ. Śaṅkara unpacks it as svo dharmaḥ. Written svadharmo before viguṇaḥ; the visarga becomes o.
meaning: one's own duty

## viguṇaḥ
ids: gita-03-35-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: viguṇa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Śaṅkara glosses vigata-guṇaḥ, whose good qualities are absent. It agrees with sva-dharmaḥ.
meaning: even without its good qualities

## para-dharmāt
ids: gita-03-35-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: para-dharma
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: singular
root: dhṛ
note: para and dharma; dharma is from dhṛ. Ablative of comparison with śreyān.
meaning: than another's duty

## svanuṣṭhitāt
ids: gita-03-35-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: su-anuṣṭhita
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: singular
root: sthā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: su, “well,” with anuṣṭhita, the past participle of anu-sthā. u + a is written va. It agrees with para-dharmāt.
meaning: well performed

## sva-dharme

kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sva-dharma
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
root: dhṛ
note: sva and dharma; dharma is from dhṛ. Locative: death in one's own duty.
meaning: in one's own duty

## nidhanam
ids: gita-03-35-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: nidhana
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what is better, so it is nominative. Written nidhanaṃ before śreyaḥ.
meaning: death

## śreyaḥ
ids: gita-03-35-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śreyas
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Comparative, agreeing with nidhanam. The neuter śreyaḥ is nominative or accusative. It agrees with nidhanam, so it is nominative. Śaṅkara supplies the comparison, than life in another's duty. That ablative is not in the verse.
meaning: is better

number: singular
root: dhṛ
note: para and dharma; dharma is from dhṛ. Written paradharmo before bhayāvahaḥ; the visarga becomes o.

ids: gita-03-35-w10
