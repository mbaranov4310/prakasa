# gita-03-03

## śrī-bhagavān
ids: gita-03-03-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śrī-bhagavat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: The blessed Lord. Written śrībhagavānu; the visarga is dropped and the u of uvāca is joined on.
meaning: the blessed Lord

## uvāca
ids: gita-03-03-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Written vāca; the u is joined to bhagavān.
meaning: said

## loke
ids: gita-03-03-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: loka
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: Locative with asmin. Written loke before asmin, and the a of asmin is dropped.
meaning: in the world

## asmin
ids: gita-03-03-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: asmin is masculine or neuter. It agrees with loke, so it is masculine. Written smin after loke.
meaning: this

## dvividhā
ids: gita-03-03-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: dvi-vidha
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Of two kinds. It agrees with niṣṭhā. Śaṅkara glosses dvi-prakārā.
meaning: twofold

## niṣṭhā
ids: gita-03-03-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: niṣṭhā
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: sthā
note: From ni and sthā, steadfastness in a practice. Śaṅkara glosses sthiti.
meaning: devotion

## purā
ids: gita-03-03-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Śaṅkara glosses pūrvam, formerly.
meaning: of old

## proktā
ids: gita-03-03-w8
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: vac
prefix: pra
participle: past
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with niṣṭhā. Passive: the devotion was taught.
meaning: was taught

## mayā
ids: gita-03-03-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written mayā before anagha, and the a of anagha is dropped.
meaning: by me

## anagha
ids: gita-03-03-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: an-agha
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Vocative, “one without sin.” Written nagha; the a is dropped after mayā.
meaning: O sinless one

## jñāna-yogena
ids: gita-03-03-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna-yoga
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: yuj
compound: karmadhāraya
note: Śaṅkara glosses jñānam eva yogaḥ, knowledge itself as the yoga.
meaning: by the yoga of knowledge

## sāṃkhyānām
ids: gita-03-03-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sāṃkhya
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
note: Written sāṃkhyānāṃ before karma-yogena.
meaning: of the Sankhyas (those who know the self and renounce)

## karma-yogena
ids: gita-03-03-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karma-yoga
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: yuj
compound: karmadhāraya
note: Śaṅkara glosses karma eva yogaḥ, action itself as the yoga. The second devotion, with niṣṭhā understood again.
meaning: by the yoga of action

## yoginām
ids: gita-03-03-w14
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yogin
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
root: yuj
note: Śaṅkara glosses karmiṇām, those given to action. The other devotion, with niṣṭhā understood again.
meaning: of the yogins (those given to action)
