# gita-03-06

## karma-indriyāṇi
ids: gita-03-06-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karma-indriya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The neuter -āni is nominative or accusative. They are what he holds back, so they are accusative. The organs of action, the hands and the rest.
meaning: the organs of action

## saṃyamya
ids: gita-03-06-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: yam
prefix: sam
note: Absolutive, “having held back.” Śaṅkara glosses saṃhṛtya.
meaning: holding back

## indriya-arthān
ids: gita-03-06-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya-artha
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The objects of the senses. Written joined to vimūḍhātmā. Śaṅkara glosses viṣayān.
meaning: the objects of the senses

## vimūḍha-ātmā
ids: gita-03-06-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vimūḍha-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Whose inner organ is bewildered.” vimūḍha is the past participle of muh with vi. Śaṅkara glosses it as the inner organ. It agrees with saḥ.
meaning: whose mind is bewildered

## mithyā-ācāraḥ
ids: gita-03-06-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mithyā-ācāra
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose conduct is false.” It is what he is called. Śaṅkara glosses mṛṣācāraḥ and pāpācāraḥ.
meaning: a man of false conduct
