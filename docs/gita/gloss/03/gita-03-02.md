# gita-03-02

## vyāmiśreṇa
ids: gita-03-02-w1
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: miśr
prefix: vi-ā
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: It agrees with vākyena. The speech is as it were mixed, confused. Written vyāmiśreṇe before iva, and the i of iva is dropped.
meaning: with a confused one

## iva
ids: gita-03-02-w2, gita-03-02-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written va both times; the i is dropped after the preceding vowel.
meaning: as it were

## vākyena
ids: gita-03-02-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: vākya
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: vac
note: Instrumental of the means with mohayasi.
meaning: with a speech

## buddhim
ids: gita-03-02-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: buddhi
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: budh
note: Written buddhiṃ before mohayasi.
meaning: understanding

## mohayasi
ids: gita-03-02-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: muh
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: Causative of muh. Written mohayasī before iva, and the i of iva is dropped.
meaning: you confuse

## me
ids: gita-03-02-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. me is genitive or dative. Śaṅkara glosses mama with buddhim, so it is genitive.
meaning: my

## tat
ids: gita-03-02-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter tat is nominative or accusative. It agrees with ekam, the one path he should tell, so it is accusative. Written tade before ekam, and the e of ekam is dropped.
meaning: that

## ekam
ids: gita-03-02-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: eka
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It stands as a noun, the one of the two paths, knowledge or action. ekam is masculine or neuter, and nominative or accusative. It is what he should tell, so it is neuter accusative. Written kaṃ after tade.
meaning: one (of the two paths)

## vada
ids: gita-03-02-w10
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vad
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
meaning: tell

## niścitya
ids: gita-03-02-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: ci
prefix: nis
note: Absolutive of ci, “having decided.” nis is written niś before c.
meaning: having decided

## yena
ids: gita-03-02-w12
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: yena is masculine or neuter. Śaṅkara supplies jñānena or karmaṇā, the one path, so it is neuter.
meaning: by which

## śreyaḥ
ids: gita-03-02-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śreyas
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Neuter of the comparative, “the good.” śreyaḥ is nominative or accusative. It is what he would reach, so it is accusative. Written śreyo before aham, and the a of aham is dropped.
meaning: the good

## aham
ids: gita-03-02-w14
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written hama; the a is dropped after śreyo, and the ā of āpnuyām is joined to it.
meaning: I

## āpnuyām
ids: gita-03-02-w15
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: āp
class: su
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: Written pnuyām; the ā is joined to aham.
