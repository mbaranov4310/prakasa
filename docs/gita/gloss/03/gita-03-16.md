# gita-03-16

## evam
ids: gita-03-16-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: in this way

## pravartitam
ids: gita-03-16-w2
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: vṛt
prefix: pra
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Causative of vṛt. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It agrees with cakram, so it is accusative.
meaning: set going

## cakram
ids: gita-03-16-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: cakra
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he does not keep turning, so it is accusative.
meaning: the wheel (of the world, through the Veda and sacrifice)

## na
ids: gita-03-16-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Joined to anuvartayati. a + a is written ā in nānuvartayati.
meaning: not

## anuvartayati
ids: gita-03-16-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vṛt
prefix: anu
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Causative of vṛt. The initial a is swallowed in nānuvartayati, and the final i lengthens before iha.
meaning: keeps turning

## iha
ids: gita-03-16-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The i of iha is absorbed into anuvartayatīha.
meaning: here

## yaḥ
ids: gita-03-16-w7

stem: yad

case: nominative
number: singular

## agha-āyuḥ
ids: gita-03-16-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: agha-āyus
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi

## indriya-ārāmaḥ
ids: gita-03-16-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya-ārāma
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose delight is through the senses. The initial i stands on the previous slice. Written indriyārāmo before mogham.
meaning: taking his pleasure in the senses

## mogham
ids: gita-03-16-w10
