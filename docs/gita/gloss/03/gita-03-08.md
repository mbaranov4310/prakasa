# gita-03-08

## niyatam
ids: gita-03-08-w1
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: yam
prefix: ni
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It describes the action he is told to do, so it is accusative. Written niyataṃ before kuru. Śaṅkara glosses what scripture lays down, and not for a fruit.
meaning: laid down (enjoined, and not for a fruit)

## akarmaṇaḥ
ids: gita-03-08-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: a-karman
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
root: kṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The genitive and the ablative of karman are both karmaṇaḥ. Action is better than not acting, so it is ablative. The initial a is the vowel of hya. Śaṅkara glosses akaraṇāt, not doing.
meaning: than not acting
