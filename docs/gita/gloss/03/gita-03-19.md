# gita-03-19

## tasmāt
ids: gita-03-19-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: Written tasmād before the a of asaktaḥ. Ablative of tad in the sense therefore.
meaning: therefore

## asaktaḥ
ids: gita-03-19-w2, gita-03-19-w7
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sañj
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Negated past participle of sañj; the a- is not a prefix. The first agrees with the unstated you of the command, and the second is written asakto before hi and agrees with pūruṣaḥ.
meaning: unattached

## satatam
ids: gita-03-19-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Neuter accusative of satata, used as an adverb. Written satataṃ before kāryam.
meaning: always

## kāryam
ids: gita-03-19-w4
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
participle: potential (-ya)
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative; it agrees with karma, so it is accusative. Written kāryaṃ before karma.
meaning: what is to be done

## karma
ids: gita-03-19-w5, gita-03-19-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: The singular of karman is nominative or accusative. It is what is performed, so it is accusative.
meaning: action

## samācara
ids: gita-03-19-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: car
prefix: sam-ā
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The command is to the listener. You is not a word in the verse.
meaning: perform

## hi
ids: gita-03-19-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written hy before ācaran.
meaning: for

## ācaran
ids: gita-03-19-w9
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: car
prefix: ā
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with pūruṣaḥ. The initial ā is joined in hyācaran.
meaning: performing

## param
ids: gita-03-19-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: para
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It stands alone as a noun. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative; it is what the man reaches, so it is accusative, and its m joins the ā of āpnoti.
meaning: the highest (liberation)
