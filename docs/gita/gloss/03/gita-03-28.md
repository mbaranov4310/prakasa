# gita-03-28

## tattva-vit
ids: gita-03-28-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: tattva-vid
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: vid
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Agent compound, one who knows the truth. vit is the nominative of the root noun vid, and that ending does not show gender. He is the one who is not attached, so it is masculine. Joined with tu: the verse prints tattvavittu.
meaning: the knower of the truth

## guṇa-karma-vibhāgayoḥ
ids: gita-03-28-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: guṇa-karma-vibhāga
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: dual
root: bhaj
compound: dvandva
note: The two distinctions, that of the qualities and that of actions. vibhāga is from vi and bhaj. The ending -yoḥ is genitive or locative. It names what he knows the truth of, so it is genitive.
meaning: of the distinction between the qualities and actions
