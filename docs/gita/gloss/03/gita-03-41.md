# gita-03-41

## tasmāt
ids: gita-03-41-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The ablative of tad, used as therefore. The final t meets the t of tvam.
meaning: therefore

## tvam
ids: gita-03-41-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: yuṣmad has no gender. The m stands before the i of indriyāṇi.
meaning: you

## indriyāṇi
ids: gita-03-41-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
note: The neuter plural -āni is nominative or accusative. They are what is restrained, so it is accusative, and the final i joins with the ā of ādau as yā.
meaning: the senses

## ādau
ids: gita-03-41-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ādi
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: Locative of ādi, at the beginning, and so it means first. The ā is joined to the final i of indriyāṇi as yā.
meaning: first

## niyamya
ids: gita-03-41-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: yam
prefix: ni
note: Absolutive of ni and yam, having restrained.
meaning: having restrained

## bharatarṣabha
ids: gita-03-41-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bharata-ṛṣabha
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: bharata and ṛṣabha: the bull of the Bharatas, so the best. The a and ṛ join as ar.
meaning: O best of the Bharatas

## pāpmānam
ids: gita-03-41-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pāpman
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Śaṅkara reads it as kāma, the evil-doer. Written pāpmānaṃ before prajahihi.
meaning: the evil one (this desire)

## prajahihi
ids: gita-03-41-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: hā
prefix: pra
class: hu
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The reduplicated imperative of hā, abandon. The final i joins with the e of enam as ye.
meaning: cast off

## enam
ids: gita-03-41-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: enam is the accusative idam takes when it follows another word. The e joins with the final i of prajahihi as ye.
meaning: this

## jñāna-vijñāna-nāśanam
ids: gita-03-41-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna-vijñāna-nāśana
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: naś
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The first two members are the pair, and nāśana, from the causative of naś, is the destroyer. The -am matches a neuter, but it names pāpmānam, so it is masculine accusative.
meaning: the destroyer of knowledge and of knowing
