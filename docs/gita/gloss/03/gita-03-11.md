# gita-03-11

## devān
ids: gita-03-11-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: deva
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
meaning: the gods

## bhāvayata
ids: gita-03-11-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: plural
note: Causative of bhū. The subject “you” is not spoken. Written bhāvayatā before anena: the final a and the a of anena join into ā.
meaning: foster

## anena
ids: gita-03-11-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: anena is masculine or neuter. It is the sacrifice of the previous verse, so it is masculine. Its initial a is joined into the ā of bhāvayatā.
meaning: with this

## te
ids: gita-03-11-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
meaning: those

## devāḥ
ids: gita-03-11-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: deva
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The visarga drops before bhāvayantu.
meaning: gods

## bhāvayantu
ids: gita-03-11-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: Causative of bhū. The third-person command is a wish: may they foster.
meaning: may they foster

## vaḥ
ids: gita-03-11-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: accusative
number: plural
note: vaḥ is accusative, dative, or genitive. It is whom the gods foster, so it is accusative. yuṣmad has no gender.
meaning: you

## parasparam
ids: gita-03-11-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: paraspara
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The reciprocal “one another.” The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what they foster, so it is accusative.
meaning: one another

## bhāvayantaḥ
ids: gita-03-11-w9
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Causative of bhū. It agrees with the unstated “you” of avāpsyatha.
meaning: fostering

## śreyaḥ
ids: gita-03-11-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śreyas
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Comparative of praśasya, standing as a noun, “the good.” The neuter śreyaḥ is nominative or accusative. It is what you will reach, so it is accusative.
meaning: the good

## param
ids: gita-03-11-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: para
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It agrees with śreyaḥ, so it is accusative. Written parama before avāpsyatha; the initial a of ava is the vowel of that ma.
meaning: the highest (liberation)

## avāpsyatha
ids: gita-03-11-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: āp
prefix: ava
class: su
tense: simple future
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: plural
note: Future of āp with ava. The present is āpnoti, class 5. The future stem does not show the class. The subject “you” is not spoken. Its initial a is the vowel of the ma of param.
meaning: you will reach
