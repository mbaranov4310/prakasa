# gita-03-18

## na
ids: gita-03-18-w1, gita-03-18-w6, gita-03-18-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The first joins eva, written nai, and the e of eva stands at the end of that slice. The second joins akṛtena, written nā, and the initial a of akṛtena stands at the end of that slice. The third stands before ca.
meaning: not

## eva
ids: gita-03-18-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e stands on the previous slice. With the first na it means not at all.
meaning: at all

## tasya
ids: gita-03-18-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
meaning: for him

## kṛtena
ids: gita-03-18-w4
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: Used as a noun, what is done. The instrumental -ena is masculine or neuter. It is the deed, so it is neuter. Written kṛtenā before artha: a + a becomes ā, and the ā of artha stands at the end of this slice.
meaning: by what is done

## arthaḥ
ids: gita-03-18-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: artha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The initial a stands on the previous slice. Written artho before na. Subject of an understood asti.
meaning: an end

## akṛtena
ids: gita-03-18-w7
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: Negated past participle of kṛ, what is not done. The initial a stands on the previous slice. Written akṛtene before iha: a + i becomes e, and the i of iha stands at the end of this slice. The instrumental -ena is masculine or neuter. It is the not-doing, so it is neuter.
meaning: by what is not done

## iha
ids: gita-03-18-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The i stands on the previous slice.
meaning: here

## kaścana
ids: gita-03-18-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: kim
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: kaḥ with cana. It stands as the harm that is absent, subject of an understood asti.
meaning: any (harm)

## ca
ids: gita-03-18-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written cā before asya. a + a becomes ā, and the a of asya stands at the end of this slice.
meaning: and

ids: gita-03-18-w12
kind: pronoun

stem: idam
gender: masculine

note: The initial a stands on the previous slice.
meaning: for this man

## sarva-bhūteṣu
ids: gita-03-18-w13

judgment: obvious

gender: neuter
case: locative
number: plural

stem: kim

case: nominative
number: singular

## artha-vyapāśrayaḥ
ids: gita-03-18-w15
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: artha-vyapāśraya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: śri
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: vyapāśraya, from śri with vi, apa, and ā, is the recourse; artha is the end that recourse would serve. The initial a stands on the previous slice. Subject of an understood asti.
meaning: an end to be gained by relying on something
