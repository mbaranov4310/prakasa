ids: gita-03-14-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: anna
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular

note: From ad, “what is eaten.” Written annād before bhavanti.

## bhūtāni
ids: gita-03-14-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūta
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: bhū
note: Past participle of bhū, standing as a noun. The neuter plural -āni is nominative or accusative. They are what come to be, so it is nominative.
meaning: beings

## anna-saṃbhavaḥ
ids: gita-03-14-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: anna-saṃbhava
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhū
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “The arising (saṃbhava, from sam-bhū) of food (anna).” Nominative or vocative. It is what comes from rain, so it is nominative. The initial a is the vowel of the d of parjanyād.
meaning: the arising of food
