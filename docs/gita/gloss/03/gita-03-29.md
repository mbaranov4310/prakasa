# gita-03-29

## prakṛteḥ
ids: gita-03-29-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prakṛti
gender: feminine
case: genitive
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: Built from pra and kṛ. Written prakṛter before guṇa: the visarga becomes r before the voiced g. The ending -eḥ is genitive or ablative. It names whose qualities delude them, so it is genitive.
meaning: of nature

## guṇa-saṃmūḍhāḥ
ids: gita-03-29-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: guṇa-saṃmūḍha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: muh
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: A tatpuruṣa standing as a noun. saṃmūḍha is the past participle of muh with sam: wholly deluded by the qualities.
meaning: those wholly deluded by the qualities

number: plural

## akṛtsna-vidaḥ
ids: gita-03-29-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: akṛtsna-vid
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
root: vid
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Negation of kṛtsna-vid, one who does not know the whole. The a joins tān, and the visarga becomes o before mandān. vidaḥ is nominative or accusative plural. It agrees with tān, so it is accusative.
meaning: who do not know the whole

ids: gita-03-29-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: manda
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: Joined with kṛtsna-vit. The verse prints mandānkṛtsnavin.
meaning: the dull

## kṛtsna-vit
ids: gita-03-29-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kṛtsna-vid
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: vid
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Agent compound, one who knows the whole. vit is the nominative of the root noun vid, and that ending does not show gender. He is the one who should not shake them, so it is masculine. The t becomes n before na. The verse prints kṛtsnavinna.
meaning: he who knows the whole
