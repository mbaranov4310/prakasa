# gita-03-25

## saktāḥ
ids: gita-03-25-w1
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sañj
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: It agrees with avidvāṃsaḥ. Śaṅkara reads the thought “the fruit of this action will be mine.”
meaning: attached (thinking the fruit will be theirs)

## karmaṇi
ids: gita-03-25-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: The i becomes y before avidvāṃsaḥ.
meaning: to the action

## avidvāṃsaḥ
ids: gita-03-25-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: a-vidvāṃs
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: vid
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: a- negates vidvāṃs, the perfect participle of vid. Śaṅkara glosses kecid. The initial a joins karmaṇi. Before yathā the visarga becomes o, so the slice is vidvāṃso.
meaning: the unknowing

## yathā
ids: gita-03-25-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: tathā on the next line answers it.
meaning: as

## kurvanti
ids: gita-03-25-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
class: tan
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: The action they perform is the one in karmaṇi. It is not a separate word.
meaning: they perform

## bhārata
ids: gita-03-25-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhārata
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
meaning: O Bharata

## kuryāt
ids: gita-03-25-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
class: tan
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Written kuryād before vidvān. t becomes d. The action is the same one the unknowing perform, not named again.
meaning: should perform

## vidvān
ids: gita-03-25-w8
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: vid
participle: perfect
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Used as a noun, the one who knows. Śaṅkara glosses ātmavit. Before tathā, n is written anusvāra plus s, so the slice is vidvāṃs.
meaning: the one who knows

## tathā
ids: gita-03-25-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It answers yathā. The a of asaktaḥ joins the final ā.
meaning: so

## asaktaḥ
ids: gita-03-25-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: a-sakta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: sañj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: a- negates sakta, the past participle of sañj. It agrees with vidvān. Śaṅkara supplies san. The initial a joins tathā. Before cikīrṣuḥ the visarga becomes ś, so the slice is saktaś.
meaning: unattached

## cikīrṣuḥ
ids: gita-03-25-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: cikīrṣu
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: Desiderative of kṛ. Śaṅkara glosses kartum icchuḥ and reads it as the purpose of the action. It agrees with vidvān. The visarga becomes r before loka-saṃgraham.
meaning: wishing to bring about

## loka-saṃgraham
ids: gita-03-25-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: loka-saṃgraha
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: grah
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: saṃgraha is from sam and grah. It is what he wishes to bring about.
meaning: the holding together of the world
