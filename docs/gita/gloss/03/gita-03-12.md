# gita-03-12

## iṣṭān
ids: gita-03-12-w1
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: iṣ
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: Past participle of iṣ, “wished for.” iṣṭa is also the participle of yaj, “sacrificed.” It is what they wish for, so it is from iṣ. It agrees with bhogān.
meaning: wished for

## bhogān
ids: gita-03-12-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhoga
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
root: bhuj
meaning: enjoyments

## hi
ids: gita-03-12-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: indeed

## vaḥ
ids: gita-03-12-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: dative
number: plural
note: vaḥ is accusative, dative, or genitive. It is the person the gods give to, so it is dative. yuṣmad has no gender. Written vo before devāḥ.
meaning: to you

## devāḥ
ids: gita-03-12-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: deva
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: devāḥ is nominative or vocative. They are the ones who will give, so it is nominative. The visarga drops before dāsyante.
meaning: the gods

## dāsyante
ids: gita-03-12-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dā
class: hu
tense: simple future
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: Future of dā, “to give.” The present is dadāti, class 3. The future stem does not show the class.
meaning: will give

## yajña-bhāvitāḥ
ids: gita-03-12-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yajña-bhāvita
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: bhū
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: bhāvita is the causative past participle of bhū, “fostered.” Fostered by the sacrifice. The ending -āḥ is masculine or feminine. It agrees with devāḥ, so it is masculine.
meaning: fostered by sacrifice

## taiḥ
ids: gita-03-12-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: Written tair before dattān. The givers are the gods.
meaning: by them

## dattān
ids: gita-03-12-w9
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: dā
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: Past participle of dā, “given.” It agrees with bhogān. Written dattāna before apradāya; the initial a of apradāya is the vowel of that na.
meaning: given

## apradāya
ids: gita-03-12-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: dā
prefix: pra
note: Negative a- with the absolutive in -ya of dā with pra: without giving. The initial a is the vowel of the na of dattān. The final a and the e of ebhyaḥ join into ai.
meaning: without giving

## ebhyaḥ
ids: gita-03-12-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: masculine
case: dative
number: plural
note: ebhyaḥ is masculine or neuter. It is these gods, so it is masculine. Written bhyo: the e is in the ai of pradāyai, and the visarga becomes o before yaḥ.
meaning: to these
