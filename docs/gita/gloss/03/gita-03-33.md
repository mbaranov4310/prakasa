# gita-03-33

## sadṛśam
ids: gita-03-33-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Neuter accusative of sadṛśa, used as an adverb. Written sadṛśaṃ before ceṣṭate.
meaning: in keeping

## ceṣṭate
ids: gita-03-33-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: ceṣṭ
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
meaning: he acts

## svasyāḥ
ids: gita-03-33-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sva
gender: feminine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: It agrees with prakṛteḥ. The genitive is the standard of comparison, which the meaning renders as “with.”
meaning: with his own

## prakṛteḥ
ids: gita-03-33-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prakṛti
gender: feminine
case: genitive
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: From pra-kṛ. Written prakṛter before jñānavān; the visarga becomes r.
meaning: nature (the tendencies of past action, present from birth)

## jñānavān
ids: gita-03-33-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñānavat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It stands alone as the one who acts. The a of api joins its n.
meaning: one who knows

## api
ids: gita-03-33-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Joined to jñānavān, so only pi shows in the line.
meaning: even

## prakṛtim
ids: gita-03-33-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prakṛti
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: From pra-kṛ. Written prakṛtiṃ before yānti.
meaning: to nature

## yānti
ids: gita-03-33-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: yā
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
meaning: they go

## bhūtāni
ids: gita-03-33-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūta
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: bhū
note: Past participle of bhū, standing as the noun “beings.” The neuter plural is nominative or accusative. They are what go, so it is nominative.
meaning: beings

## nigrahaḥ
ids: gita-03-33-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: nigraha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: grah
note: From ni-grah.
meaning: restraint

## kim
ids: gita-03-33-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: kim
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter kim is nominative or accusative. It is what restraint will do, so it is accusative. Written kiṃ before kariṣyati.
meaning: what
