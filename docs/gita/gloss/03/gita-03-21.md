# gita-03-21

## yat
ids: gita-03-21-w1, gita-03-21-w2, gita-03-21-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter yat is nominative or accusative; it is what is done or set, so it is accusative. The first is written yad before the second, and the second yadā, with t becoming d before ācarati.
meaning: whatever

## ācarati
ids: gita-03-21-w3
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: car
prefix: ā
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The ā is joined to the d of the second yat. The second clause borrows this verb for what other people do.
meaning: does

## śreṣṭhaḥ
ids: gita-03-21-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śreṣṭha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Superlative standing alone as a noun, the leading man. Written śreṣṭhas before tat.
meaning: a leading man

## tat
ids: gita-03-21-w5, gita-03-21-w6, gita-03-21-w15
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter tat is nominative or accusative; each is what a verb acts on, so it is accusative. The second is written tade before eva, and the last tad before anuvartate.
meaning: that

## eva
ids: gita-03-21-w7

judgment: obvious
note: The a joins the i of itaraḥ as e.
meaning: very
