# gita-03-31

## ye
ids: gita-03-31-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
meaning: those who

## me
ids: gita-03-31-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. me is genitive or dative. It names whose teaching it is, so it is genitive.
meaning: my

## matam
ids: gita-03-31-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mata
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: man
note: Past participle of man, standing as a noun. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what they practice, so it is accusative. The i of idam is written on this slice. The verse prints matamidaṃ.
meaning: teaching

## idam
ids: gita-03-31-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with matam. The i is written on the slice of matam, and the m is anusvāra before nityam.
meaning: this

## nityam
ids: gita-03-31-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Neuter accusative of nitya used as an adverb. The m joins the a of anutiṣṭhanti.
meaning: always

## anutiṣṭhanti
ids: gita-03-31-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: anu
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: The present of sthā reduplicates. The initial a joins nityam.
meaning: practice

## mānavāḥ
ids: gita-03-31-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mānava
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
meaning: men

## śraddhāvantaḥ
ids: gita-03-31-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śraddhāvant
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: śrad in śraddhā is not a prefix. The suffix is -vant. It agrees with ye. Written śraddhāvanto before anasūyantaḥ: the visarga becomes o, and the following a drops.
meaning: who have faith

## anasūyantaḥ
ids: gita-03-31-w9
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: asūy
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Denominative of asūyā. The a negates it. It agrees with ye. The initial a joins śraddhāvantaḥ, and the visarga becomes o before mucyante.
meaning: who do not find fault

## mucyante
ids: gita-03-31-w10
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: muc
class: tud
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: Passive.
meaning: are released

## te
ids: gita-03-31-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The a of api drops after e. The slice keeps the avagraha.
meaning: they

## api
ids: gita-03-31-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The a drops after te. The verse prints te 'pi.
meaning: also

## karmabhiḥ
ids: gita-03-31-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: plural
root: kṛ
note: With muc the instrumental names what they are released from.
meaning: from actions
