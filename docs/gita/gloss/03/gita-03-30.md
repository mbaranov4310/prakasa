# gita-03-30

## mayi
ids: gita-03-30-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: locative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender.
meaning: on me (on the Lord)

## sarvāṇi
ids: gita-03-30-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
note: It agrees with karmāṇi. The neuter -āṇi is nominative or accusative. The actions are what one lays down, so it is accusative.
meaning: all

## karmāṇi
ids: gita-03-30-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
root: kṛ
note: The neuter -āṇi is nominative or accusative. They are what one lays down, so it is accusative.
meaning: actions

## saṃnyasya
ids: gita-03-30-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: as
prefix: sam-ni
note: Absolutive of as, to throw. The final a joins the a of adhyātma, and the verse prints saṃnyasyādhyātma.
meaning: having laid down

## adhyātma-cetasā
ids: gita-03-30-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: adhyātma-cetas
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: cit
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: A mind turned to the self. Śaṅkara takes it as the discriminating thought, “I am the doer, and I act for the Lord as a servant.” The Lord is mayi. cetas is neuter. The initial a joins saṃnyasya.
meaning: with a mind turned to the self

## nirāśīḥ
ids: gita-03-30-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nirāśis
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Bahuvrīhi, one from whom hope has gone. It agrees with the you of yudhyasva. Written nirāśīr before nirmamaḥ: the visarga becomes r.
meaning: free of hope

## nirmamaḥ
ids: gita-03-30-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nirmama
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Bahuvrīhi, one from whom the thought "mine" has gone. It agrees with the you of yudhyasva. Written nirmamo before bhūtvā: the visarga becomes o before the voiced b.
meaning: free of the thought "mine"

## bhūtvā
ids: gita-03-30-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
note: Absolutive of bhū.
meaning: having become

## yudhyasva
ids: gita-03-30-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: yudh
class: div
tense: command
voice: ātmanepada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is the unstated you.
meaning: fight

## vigata-jvaraḥ
ids: gita-03-30-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vigata-jvara
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Bahuvrīhi, one whose fever has gone. vigata is the past participle of gam with vi. It agrees with the you of yudhyasva.
meaning: free of the fever of grief
