# gita-03-24

## utsīdeyuḥ
ids: gita-03-24-w1
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: sad
prefix: ut
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: The present stem of sad is sīd. Śaṅkara glosses it vinaśyeyuḥ. The visarga becomes r before ime, and the i of ime stands in this slice.
meaning: would perish

## ime
ids: gita-03-24-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: It agrees with lokāḥ. The i stands in the slice of utsīdeyuḥ, so the slice is me.
meaning: these

## lokāḥ
ids: gita-03-24-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: loka
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The visarga drops before na.
meaning: worlds

## na
ids: gita-03-24-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## kuryām
ids: gita-03-24-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
class: tan
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: The m is anusvāra before karma.
meaning: I would perform

## karma
ids: gita-03-24-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: The singular nominative and accusative of karman are both karma. It is what I would perform, so it is accusative.
meaning: action

## cet
ids: gita-03-24-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written ced before aham. t becomes d, and the a of aham is the vowel of that d.
meaning: if

## aham
ids: gita-03-24-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The initial a is the vowel of ced, so the slice is ham.
meaning: I

## saṃkarasya
ids: gita-03-24-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃkara
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
root: kṝ
note: From sam and kṝ, a mixing of orders.
meaning: of the confusion of orders

## ca
ids: gita-03-24-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Śaṅkara reads kiṃ ca, and then tena kāraṇena. The harm follows on being that maker.
meaning: and

## kartā
ids: gita-03-24-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kartṛ
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: Agent noun of kṛ. With syām it is the predicate, so it agrees with aham.
meaning: the maker

## syām
ids: gita-03-24-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: Written syāmu. The u of upahanyām stands in this slice.
meaning: I would be

## upahanyām
ids: gita-03-24-w13
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: han
prefix: upa
class: ad
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: Śaṅkara paraphrases it upahatiṃ kuryām. The initial u stands in the slice of syām. The final m takes the i of imāḥ, so the slice is pahanyāmi.
meaning: I would harm

## imāḥ
ids: gita-03-24-w14
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: It agrees with prajāḥ. The feminine plural -āḥ is nominative or accusative. prajāḥ is what I would harm, so imāḥ is accusative. The i stands in the slice of upahanyām, so the slice is māḥ.
meaning: these

## prajāḥ
ids: gita-03-24-w15
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prajā
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: plural
root: jan
note: From pra and jan. Nominative and accusative plural are both prajāḥ. It is what I would harm, so it is accusative.
meaning: creatures
