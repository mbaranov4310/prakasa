# gita-03-04

## na
ids: gita-03-04-w1, gita-03-04-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## karmaṇām
ids: gita-03-04-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: plural
root: kṛ
note: Genitive with anārambha, the beginning of actions. The a of anārambhāt is joined to the m.
meaning: of actions

## anārambhāt
ids: gita-03-04-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: an-ārambha
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: singular
root: rabh
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Negative of ārambha, from ā and rabh, “not beginning.” Written nārambhāt; the initial a is joined to karmaṇām. Before naiṣkarmyam the final t is written n.
meaning: by not beginning

## naiṣkarmyam
ids: gita-03-04-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: naiṣkarmya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Abstract from niṣkarman, the state of being without action. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what the man does not reach, so it is accusative. Written naiṣkarmyaṃ before puruṣaḥ. Śaṅkara glosses the steadfastness that is jñāna-yoga.
meaning: freedom from action

## puruṣaḥ
ids: gita-03-04-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: puruṣa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written puruṣo before aśnute, and the a of aśnute is dropped.
meaning: a man

## saṃnyasanāt
ids: gita-03-04-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃnyasana
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
root: as
note: From sam, ni, and as, the act of renouncing. Written saṃnyasanāde before eva, and the e of eva is dropped.
meaning: by renunciation

## eva
ids: gita-03-04-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written va; the e is dropped after saṃnyasanāde. Śaṅkara glosses kevalāt, renunciation alone, without knowledge.
meaning: alone
