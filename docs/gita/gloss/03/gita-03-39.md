# gita-03-39

## āvṛtam
ids: gita-03-39-w1
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: vṛ
prefix: ā
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written āvṛtaṃ before jñānam. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative, and it agrees with jñānam, so it is nominative.
meaning: covered

## jñānam
ids: gita-03-39-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: jñā
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what is covered, so it is nominative, and the m joins the e of etena.
meaning: knowledge

## etena
ids: gita-03-39-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: Masculine and neuter share this instrumental. It agrees with nitya-vairiṇā, so it is masculine.
meaning: by this

## jñāninaḥ
ids: gita-03-39-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñānin
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: jñāninaḥ can be genitive singular or nominative plural. Śaṅkara pairs it with mūrkhasya, of one knower, so it is genitive singular, and it is written jñānino before nitya-vairiṇā.
meaning: of the one who knows

## nitya-vairiṇā
ids: gita-03-39-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: nitya-vairin
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: The constant enemy: nitya and vairin. It names etena.
meaning: by the constant enemy

## kāma-rūpeṇa
ids: gita-03-39-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kāma-rūpa
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Desire is its form. It agrees with etena, so the ending is masculine.
meaning: by that which has the form of desire

## kaunteya
ids: gita-03-39-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kaunteya
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
meaning: O son of Kunti

## duṣpūreṇa
ids: gita-03-39-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: duṣpūra
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: pṝ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: dus and pūra, from pṝ: hard to fill. It agrees with etena, and the final a joins with the a of analena.
meaning: by what is hard to fill

## analena
ids: gita-03-39-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: anala
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: anala is fire, and Śaṅkara also reads na and alam: nothing is enough for it. The initial a is joined to the final a of duṣpūreṇa.
meaning: by a fire that is never enough

## ca
ids: gita-03-39-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and
