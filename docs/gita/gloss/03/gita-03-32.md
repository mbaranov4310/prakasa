# gita-03-32

## ye
ids: gita-03-32-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
meaning: those who

## tu
ids: gita-03-32-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Joined with etat. The e of etat is written on this slice: the verse prints tvetad.
meaning: but

## etat
ids: gita-03-32-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter form is nominative or accusative. It agrees with matam, so it is accusative. The e is written on the slice of tu. The final t becomes d before abhyasūyantaḥ, and the a of abhi is the vowel on that d.
meaning: this

## abhyasūyantaḥ
ids: gita-03-32-w4
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: asūy
prefix: abhi
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Denominative of asūyā. It agrees with ye. The initial a is written on the slice of etat, and the visarga becomes o before na.
meaning: find fault with

## na
ids: gita-03-32-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The a joins the a of anutiṣṭhanti. The verse prints nānutiṣṭhanti.
meaning: not

## anutiṣṭhanti
ids: gita-03-32-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: anu
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: The present of sthā reduplicates. The initial a joins na.
meaning: practice

## me
ids: gita-03-32-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. me is genitive or dative. It names whose teaching it is, so it is genitive.
meaning: my

## matam
ids: gita-03-32-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mata
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: man
note: Past participle of man, standing as a noun. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what they find fault with and do not practice, so it is accusative.
meaning: teaching

## sarva-jñāna-vimūḍhān
ids: gita-03-32-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-jñāna-vimūḍha
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
root: muh
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: vimūḍha is the past participle of muh with vi: deluded about every kind of knowledge. It agrees with tān. The final n becomes anusvāra plus s before tān.
meaning: deluded about every kind of knowledge

## tān
ids: gita-03-32-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
meaning: them

## viddhi
ids: gita-03-32-w11
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: ad
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is the unstated you.
meaning: know

## naṣṭān
ids: gita-03-32-w12
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: naś
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: It agrees with tān. Joined with acetasaḥ. The verse prints naṣṭānacetasaḥ.
meaning: lost

## acetasaḥ
ids: gita-03-32-w13
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: acetas
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Bahuvrīhi, one whose judgment is gone. acetasaḥ is nominative plural, accusative plural, or genitive singular. It agrees with tān, so it is accusative plural. The initial a joins naṣṭān.
meaning: without judgment
