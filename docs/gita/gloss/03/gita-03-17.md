# gita-03-17

## yaḥ
ids: gita-03-17-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written yas before tu. The visarga becomes s.
meaning: who

## tu
ids: gita-03-17-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written tvā. The u joins the ā of ātma, and that ā stands at the end of this slice.
meaning: but

## ātma-ratiḥ
ids: gita-03-17-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ātma-rati
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose delight is in the self. The initial ā stands on the previous slice. Written ātma-ratir before eva, and the e of eva stands at the end of this slice.
meaning: delighting in the self

## eva
ids: gita-03-17-w4, gita-03-17-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Each e stands on the previous slice. The first restricts ātma-ratiḥ, the second ātmani: in the self alone.
meaning: alone

## syāt
ids: gita-03-17-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Written syād before ātma. The ā of ātma stands at the end of this slice.
meaning: is

## ātma-tṛptaḥ
ids: gita-03-17-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ātma-tṛpta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: tṛp
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: tṛpta is the past participle of tṛp. Satisfied by the self. The initial ā stands on the previous slice. Written ātma-tṛptaś before ca.
meaning: content with the self
