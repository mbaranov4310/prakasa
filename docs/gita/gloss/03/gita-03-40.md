# gita-03-40

## indriyāṇi
ids: gita-03-40-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The neuter plural -āni is nominative or accusative. They are what is called the seat, so it is nominative.
meaning: the senses

## manaḥ
ids: gita-03-40-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: manas
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: man
note: manaḥ is nominative or accusative. It is what is called the seat, so it is nominative, and it is written mano before buddhiḥ.
meaning: the mind

## buddhiḥ
ids: gita-03-40-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: buddhi
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: budh
note: Written buddhir before asya.
meaning: the understanding

## asya
ids: gita-03-40-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asya is masculine or neuter. Śaṅkara takes it as kāmasya, of desire, so it is masculine, the a is joined to buddhir, and the final a joins with the a of adhiṣṭhānam.
meaning: its

## adhiṣṭhānam
ids: gita-03-40-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: adhiṣṭhāna
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: sthā
note: From adhi and sthā. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative, and it is what they are called, so it is nominative; the m joins the u of ucyate.
meaning: seat

## ucyate
ids: gita-03-40-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
class: ad
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The passive of vac is ucyate.
meaning: is called

## etaiḥ
ids: gita-03-40-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: etaiḥ is masculine or neuter. Śaṅkara takes it as the seats, āśrayaiḥ, so it is masculine, and it is written etair before vimohayati.
meaning: by these

## vimohayati
ids: gita-03-40-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: muh
prefix: vi
class: div
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Causative of muh. The final i joins with the e of eṣaḥ as ye.
meaning: it bewilders

## eṣaḥ
ids: gita-03-40-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The e joins with the final i of vimohayati as ye, and the visarga drops before jñānam. It is kāma, the enemy named before.
meaning: this

## jñānam
ids: gita-03-40-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: jñā
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what is covered, so it is accusative, and the a joins with the ā of āvṛtya.
meaning: knowledge

## āvṛtya
ids: gita-03-40-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: vṛ
prefix: ā
note: Absolutive of ā and vṛ. The ā is joined to jñānam.
meaning: having covered

## dehinam
ids: gita-03-40-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dehin
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It stands as a noun, the embodied one.
meaning: the embodied one
