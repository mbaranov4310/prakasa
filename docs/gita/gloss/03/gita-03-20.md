# gita-03-20

## karmaṇā
ids: gita-03-20-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: Instrumental of means. Written karmaṇai; the ā joins the e of eva.
meaning: by action

## eva
ids: gita-03-20-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e joins the ā of karmaṇā; va stands before hi.
meaning: alone

## hi
ids: gita-03-20-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: indeed

## saṃsiddhim
ids: gita-03-20-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃsiddhi
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: sidh
note: From sidh, with the prefix saṃ. It is what they reached, and its m joins the ā of āsthitāḥ.
meaning: perfection (liberation)

## āsthitāḥ
ids: gita-03-20-w5
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: ā
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: It agrees with janaka-ādayaḥ, so the plural -āḥ is masculine. The visarga drops before janaka-ādayaḥ.
meaning: reached

## janaka-ādayaḥ
ids: gita-03-20-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: janaka-ādi
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Janaka stands first in the group: Janaka and the others. Written janakādayaḥ; the a and the ā combine.
meaning: Janaka and the others

## loka-saṃgraham
ids: gita-03-20-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: loka-saṃgraha
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: grah
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Tatpuruṣa: the holding of the world, from grah with saṃ. It is what he looks to, so it is accusative, and its m joins the e of eva.
meaning: the holding of the world to its course

## eva
ids: gita-03-20-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e joins the m of loka-saṃgraham, and the a joins api.
meaning: just

## api
ids: gita-03-20-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The a is joined into the vā of eva.
meaning: also

## saṃpaśyan
ids: gita-03-20-w10
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: dṛś
prefix: sam
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The present stem is paśya. It agrees with the unstated you of arhasi.
meaning: looking to

## kartum
ids: gita-03-20-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
note: Infinitive of kṛ. Its m joins the a of arhasi.
meaning: to act

## arhasi
ids: gita-03-20-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: arh
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: Present of arh with an infinitive means ought. You is not a word in the verse.
meaning: you should
