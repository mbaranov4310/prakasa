# gita-03-13

## yajña-śiṣṭa-āśinaḥ
ids: gita-03-13-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yajña-śiṣṭa-āśin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Eaters (āśin, from aś) of what is left (śiṣṭa, past participle of śiṣ) of the sacrifice. The -in has a habitual sense. It agrees with santaḥ.
meaning: who eat what is left from sacrifice

## santaḥ
ids: gita-03-13-w2
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: as
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Present participle of as, standing as a noun, “the good.” Written santo before mucyante.
meaning: the good

## mucyante
ids: gita-03-13-w3
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: muc
class: tud
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: Passive of muc, “to release.” The present is muñcati, class 6. The passive stem does not show the class.
meaning: are released

## sarva-kilbiṣaiḥ
ids: gita-03-13-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-kilbiṣa
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: plural
compound: karmadhāraya
note: “Every (sarva) stain (kilbiṣa).” Instrumental of that from which one is released.
meaning: from every stain (harm a household cannot avoid, and the rest)

judgment: obvious
root: bhuj
class: rudh
tense: present

person: 3rd
number: plural

## te
ids: gita-03-13-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: te of tad is nominative plural, or nominative dual. The wicked are many, so it is plural.
meaning: they

## tu
ids: gita-03-13-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written tva before agham: u and a join into va.
meaning: but

## agham
ids: gita-03-13-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: agha
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what they eat, so it is accusative. The initial a is joined into the va of tva.
meaning: evil

number: plural

## ātma-kāraṇāt
ids: gita-03-13-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātma-kāraṇa
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
root: kṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “For the sake (kāraṇa, from kṛ) of oneself (ātman).” Ablative of cause. The initial ā is in the yā of pacantyā.
meaning: for their own sake
