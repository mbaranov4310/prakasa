# gita-03-05

## na
ids: gita-03-05-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## hi
ids: gita-03-05-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: na hi opens the reason for the last verse. Śaṅkara glosses this hi as yasmāt.
meaning: because

## kaścit
ids: gita-03-05-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: kim
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: kaḥ with cit, “anyone.” The visarga of kaḥ becomes ś before c.
meaning: anyone

## kṣaṇam
ids: gita-03-05-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kṣaṇa
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Accusative of duration with tiṣṭhati: stays for a moment. Śaṅkara glosses kālam. The a of api is joined to the m.
meaning: a moment

## api
ids: gita-03-05-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written pi; the a is joined to kṣaṇam. It limits kṣaṇam, “even a moment.”
meaning: even

## jātu
ids: gita-03-05-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Śaṅkara glosses kadācit.
meaning: ever

## tiṣṭhati
ids: gita-03-05-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Written tiṣṭhatya; the a of akarma-kṛt is joined to the y.
meaning: stays

## akarma-kṛt
ids: gita-03-05-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: akarma-kṛt
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Agent noun, “one who does no action.” The nominative of kṛt has this bare form. It agrees with kaścit, so it is nominative. Written karmakṛt; the initial a is joined to tiṣṭhati.
meaning: doing no action

## kāryate
ids: gita-03-05-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Causative of kṛ, in the passive. Śaṅkara glosses pravartyate, “is set in motion.”
meaning: is driven to act

## hi
ids: gita-03-05-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Śaṅkara glosses yasmāt, the reason no one stays without acting. Written hya before avaśaḥ, and the a of avaśaḥ is dropped.
meaning: for

## avaśaḥ
ids: gita-03-05-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: a-vaśa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Negative of vaśa, “not one’s own master.” It agrees with sarvaḥ. Śaṅkara glosses asvatantra. Written vaśaḥ; the a is dropped after hya.
meaning: helpless

## karma
ids: gita-03-05-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karma
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: The neuter karma is nominative or accusative. It is the action he is made to do, so it is accusative.
meaning: action

## sarvaḥ
ids: gita-03-05-w13
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It stands as a noun, everyone. Śaṅkara supplies prāṇī, and ajña, the one who does not know.
meaning: everyone (who does not know the self)

## prakṛti-jaiḥ
ids: gita-03-05-w14
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: prakṛti-ja
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
root: jan
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Born from prakṛti. It agrees with guṇaiḥ. Śaṅkara glosses prakṛtito jātaiḥ, sattva, rajas, and tamas. Written prakṛtijair before guṇaiḥ.
meaning: born of nature

## guṇaiḥ
ids: gita-03-05-w15
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: guṇa
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
meaning: by the qualities
