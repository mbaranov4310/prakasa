# gita-03-07

## yaḥ
ids: gita-03-07-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written yas before tu.
meaning: who

## tu
ids: gita-03-07-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written tvi. The u joins the i of indriyāṇi. Śaṅkara glosses punaḥ.
meaning: but

## indriyāṇi
ids: gita-03-07-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
note: The initial i is written in tvi. The neuter -āni is nominative or accusative. They are what he controls, so they are accusative. Śaṅkara glosses the organs of knowledge.
meaning: the senses (the organs of knowledge)

## manasā
ids: gita-03-07-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: manas
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: man
meaning: with the mind

## niyamya
ids: gita-03-07-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: yam
prefix: ni
note: Absolutive, “having controlled.” Written niyamyā. The final a joins the ā of ārabhate.
meaning: having controlled

## ārabhate
ids: gita-03-07-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: rabh
prefix: ā
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The initial ā is written in niyamyā. Written rabhate' before arjuna.
meaning: undertakes

## arjuna
ids: gita-03-07-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: arjuna
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
note: The initial a is dropped after e, marked by the avagraha.
meaning: O Arjuna

## karma-indriyaiḥ
ids: gita-03-07-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karma-indriya
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: plural
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The organs of action. Śaṅkara glosses speech, hands, and the rest.
meaning: with the organs of action

## karma-yogam
ids: gita-03-07-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karma-yoga
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: yuj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The yoga of action, what he undertakes. Written yogam before asaktaḥ.
meaning: the yoga of action

## asaktaḥ
ids: gita-03-07-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: a-sakta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: sañj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: sakta is the past participle of sañj. The a- negates it. Śaṅkara glosses one who has given up aiming at the fruit. The initial a is written in the m of yogam.
meaning: unattached (not looking to the fruit)

## saḥ
ids: gita-03-07-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The visarga drops before viśiṣyate.
meaning: he

## viśiṣyate
ids: gita-03-07-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: śiṣ
prefix: vi
class: rudh
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Passive of śiṣ, “is distinguished,” so “excels.” Śaṅkara supplies the ablative of comparison, over the man of false conduct in the previous verse.
meaning: excels (that man of false conduct)
