# gita-03-34

## indriyasya
ids: gita-03-34-w1, gita-03-34-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Repeated, it means each sense. The first is written with the i of the second (a + i becomes e). The second loses that i, and its final a joins arthe (a + a becomes ā).
meaning: of each sense

## arthe
ids: gita-03-34-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: artha
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: The a joins the preceding a, so the line shows rthe.
meaning: in the object

## rāga-dveṣau
ids: gita-03-34-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: rāga-dveṣa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: dual
compound: dvandva
note: rāga is from rañj and dveṣa from dviṣ. The dual -au is nominative or accusative. They are what stand, so it is nominative.
meaning: attraction and aversion

## vyavasthitau
ids: gita-03-34-w5
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: vi-ava
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: dual
note: It agrees with rāga-dveṣau.
meaning: stand

## tayoḥ
ids: gita-03-34-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: dual
note: It takes up rāga-dveṣau. Written tayor before na; the visarga becomes r.
meaning: of those two

## na
ids: gita-03-34-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## vaśam
ids: gita-03-34-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: vaśa
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: vaś
note: Accusative goal of āgam, what one should not come under. Written vaśamā; the ā of āgacchet joins it.
meaning: under the power

## āgacchet
ids: gita-03-34-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: gam
prefix: ā
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The ā is written on vaśam, so the line shows gacchet. The one who should not come is not named in the verse.
meaning: one should come

## tau
ids: gita-03-34-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: dual
note: It takes up rāga-dveṣau. The dual -au is nominative or accusative. paripanthinau is said of them, so it is nominative.
meaning: those two

## hi
ids: gita-03-34-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written hy before asya; i + a becomes y.
meaning: for

## asya
ids: gita-03-34-w12
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Śaṅkara reads it as the person. The a joins hi, so only sya shows.
meaning: his

## paripanthinau
ids: gita-03-34-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: paripanthin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: dual
note: pari with pathin, one who blocks the road. The dual -au is nominative or accusative. It is said of tau, so it is nominative.
meaning: block the road
