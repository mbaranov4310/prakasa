# gita-03-43

## evam
ids: gita-03-43-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written evaṃ before buddheḥ.
meaning: thus

## buddheḥ
ids: gita-03-43-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: buddhi
gender: feminine
case: ablative
number: singular
root: budh
note: buddheḥ is genitive or ablative. The self is higher than the understanding, so it is ablative.
meaning: than the understanding

## param
ids: gita-03-43-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: para
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It stands as a noun, the higher self, and Śaṅkara glosses it ātmānam. Masculine and neuter share this -am; it is what one knows, so it is masculine accusative, and it is written paraṃ before buddhvā.
meaning: what is higher

## buddhvā
ids: gita-03-43-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: budh
note: Absolutive of budh, having known.
meaning: having known

## saṃstabhya
ids: gita-03-43-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: stambh
prefix: sam
note: Absolutive of sam and stambh, having steadied. The final a joins with the ā of ātmānam.
meaning: having steadied

## ātmānam
ids: gita-03-43-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The self one steadies. The ā is joined to the final a of saṃstabhya, and the m joins the ā of ātmanā.
meaning: the self

## ātmanā
ids: gita-03-43-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: By the self: Śaṅkara reads the prepared mind. The ā is joined to the m of ātmānam.
meaning: by the self

## jahi
ids: gita-03-43-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: han
class: ad
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The imperative of han is jahi. The you is unstated.
meaning: strike down

## śatrum
ids: gita-03-43-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śatru
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written śatruṃ before mahā-bāho.
meaning: the enemy

## mahā-bāho
ids: gita-03-43-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mahā-bāhu
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose arm is great. The vocative of bāhu is bāho, and it stands as the one addressed.
meaning: O mighty-armed

## kāma-rūpam
ids: gita-03-43-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kāma-rūpa
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Desire is its form. It agrees with śatrum, so this -am is masculine accusative, and it is written kāma-rūpaṃ before durāsadam.
meaning: in the form of desire

## durāsadam
ids: gita-03-43-w12
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: durāsada
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose approach is with difficulty. It agrees with śatrum, so this -am is masculine accusative.
meaning: hard to reach
