# gita-03-23

## yadi
ids: gita-03-23-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: if

## hi
ids: gita-03-23-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The i becomes y before aham, so the slice is hy.
meaning: indeed

## aham
ids: gita-03-23-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The initial a joins hi, so the slice is haṃ. The m is anusvāra before na.
meaning: I

## na
ids: gita-03-23-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## varteya
ids: gita-03-23-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vṛt
tense: potential
voice: ātmanepada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: The ātmanepada potential ends in eya, so the form is varteya with no anusvāra. Śaṅkara reads na varteya.
meaning: were to engage

## jātu
ids: gita-03-23-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: With na, not at any time. Śaṅkara glosses it kadācit.
meaning: ever

## karmaṇi
ids: gita-03-23-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: The i becomes y before atandritaḥ.
meaning: in action

## atandritaḥ
ids: gita-03-23-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: a-tandrita
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: a- negates tandrita, wearied. Śaṅkara glosses analasaḥ san, not lazy. It agrees with aham. The initial a joins karmaṇi.
meaning: unwearied

## mama
ids: gita-03-23-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender.
meaning: my

## vartma
ids: gita-03-23-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: vartman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: vṛt
note: The singular nominative and accusative of vartman are both vartma. It is what they follow, and Śaṅkara glosses it mārgam, so it is accusative. The final a and the a of anuvartante combine as ā.
meaning: path

## anuvartante
ids: gita-03-23-w11
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vṛt
prefix: anu
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: The initial a joins vartma, so the slice is nuvartante. The form is present. With yadi and varteya it says what men would do.
meaning: they follow

## manuṣyāḥ
ids: gita-03-23-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: manuṣya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
meaning: men

## pārtha
ids: gita-03-23-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pārtha
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
meaning: O son of Pritha

## sarvaśaḥ
ids: gita-03-23-w14
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Adverb in -śas. Śaṅkara glosses it sarva-prakāraiḥ.
meaning: in every way
