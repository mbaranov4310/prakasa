ids: gita-03-01-w3

gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular

## cet
ids: gita-03-01-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: if

## karmaṇaḥ
ids: gita-03-01-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: karmaṇaḥ is genitive or ablative. It is the comparison with jyāyasī, better than action, so it is ablative. Written karmaṇas before te.
meaning: than action

## te
ids: gita-03-01-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: yuṣmad has no gender. te is genitive or dative. Śaṅkara glosses tava, “your,” with matā, so it is genitive.
meaning: your

## matā
ids: gita-03-01-w7
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: man
participle: past
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with buddhiḥ. Śaṅkara glosses abhipretā, the view that is accepted.
meaning: held
