# gita-03-26

## na
ids: gita-03-26-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It denies janayet. joṣayet is what he should do instead.
meaning: not

## buddhi-bhedam
ids: gita-03-26-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: buddhi-bheda
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: bhid
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: A splitting of buddhi. Śaṅkara glosses bhedanam cālanam, of the conviction that the act and its fruit are theirs. It is what he should not cause. The m is anusvāra before janayet.
meaning: a breach in their understanding (that they must act and enjoy the fruit)

## janayet
ids: gita-03-26-w3
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: jan
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Causative of jan. Śaṅkara glosses utpādayet. Written janayed before ajñānām. t becomes d.
meaning: he should cause

## ajñānām
ids: gita-03-26-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: a-jña
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
root: jñā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: a- negates jña. Genitive plural, the people whose understanding it is. Śaṅkara glosses avivekinām, so it is masculine. The initial a is the vowel of janayed, so the slice is jñānāṃ.
meaning: of the unknowing

## karma-saṅginām
ids: gita-03-26-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: karma-saṅgin
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Clinging to action. It agrees with ajñānām. Śaṅkara glosses karmaṇi āsaktānām.
meaning: who cling to action

## joṣayet
ids: gita-03-26-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: juṣ
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Causative of juṣ. Śaṅkara glosses kārayet. The people he sets to work are the unknowing of the first line. Joined in writing to sarva-karmāṇi.
meaning: he should have them do

## sarva-karmāṇi
ids: gita-03-26-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-karman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
root: kṛ
compound: karmadhāraya
note: Every action. The neuter plural -āni is nominative or accusative. It is what he should have them do, so it is accusative.
meaning: all actions

## vidvān
ids: gita-03-26-w8
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: vid
participle: perfect
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Used as a noun, the one who knows. Joined to yuktaḥ. n stays before y.
meaning: the one who knows

## yuktaḥ
ids: gita-03-26-w9
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: yuj
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with vidvān. Śaṅkara glosses abhiyuktaḥ. The “himself” is his svayam.
meaning: himself engaged

## samācaran
ids: gita-03-26-w10
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: car
prefix: sam-ā
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with vidvān. He himself does the actions he sets them to. Śaṅkara says svayaṃ tad eva.
meaning: doing them
