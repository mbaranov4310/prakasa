# gita-03-22

## na
ids: gita-03-22-w1, gita-03-22-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The first denies asti. The second denies the same verb on the next line, which Śaṅkara reads as the reason.
meaning: not

## me
ids: gita-03-22-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. me is genitive or dative. With the potential participle kartavyam the genitive is the doer, and Śaṅkara glosses it mama.
meaning: for me

## pārtha
ids: gita-03-22-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pārtha
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
note: Written pārthā before asti. The final a and the a of asti combine as ā.
meaning: O son of Pritha

## asti
ids: gita-03-22-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The initial a joins pārtha, so the slice is sti. Śaṅkara glosses it vidyate and carries it into the next line.
meaning: there is

## kartavyam
ids: gita-03-22-w5
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
participle: potential (-tavya)
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Used as a noun, a duty. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what there is or is not, so it is nominative. The m is anusvāra before triṣu.
meaning: a duty

## triṣu
ids: gita-03-22-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: tri
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: plural
note: It agrees with lokeṣu.
meaning: in the three

## lokeṣu
ids: gita-03-22-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: loka
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: plural
meaning: worlds

## kiṃcana
ids: gita-03-22-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: kim
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: cana makes kim indefinite. Śaṅkara glosses it kiṃcid api. The form is nominative or accusative. It stands with kartavyam as what does not exist, so it is nominative.
meaning: anything at all

## anavāptam
ids: gita-03-22-w10
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: āp
prefix: ava
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: an- negates avāpta. The a of the second na and the a of anavāptam join as ā. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is the subject of asti, carried forward, so it is nominative. The m joins avāptavyam.
meaning: unobtained

## avāptavyam
ids: gita-03-22-w11
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: āp
prefix: ava
participle: potential (-tavya)
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The initial a is the vowel of the preceding m, so the slice begins at vā. It agrees with anavāptam, so the neuter -am is nominative. The m is anusvāra before varta.
meaning: still to be obtained

## varte
ids: gita-03-22-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vṛt
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: The text writes varta eva, two words. Before eva the e of varte becomes a. Śaṅkara supplies aham, which is not a word in the verse.
meaning: I engage

## eva
ids: gita-03-22-w13
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Śaṅkara reads tathāpi varta eva, “even so, I do engage.”
meaning: even so

## ca
ids: gita-03-22-w14
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## karmaṇi
ids: gita-03-22-w15
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
root: kṛ
meaning: in action
