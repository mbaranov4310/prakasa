# gita-03-37

## śrī-bhagavān
ids: gita-03-37-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śrī-bhagavat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: śrī and bhagavat. The u of uvāca joins its n.
meaning: the blessed Lord

## uvāca
ids: gita-03-37-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The u is written on bhagavān, so the line shows vāca.
meaning: said

## krodhaḥ
ids: gita-03-37-w5

judgment: obvious
stem: krodha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: krudh
note: The visarga drops before the e of eṣaḥ.

## rajo-guṇa-samudbhavaḥ
ids: gita-03-37-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: rajo-guṇa-samudbhava
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhū
note: From sam-ud-bhū, agreeing with kāmaḥ. Śaṅkara allows a bahuvrīhi, born from rajo-guṇa, and a tatpuruṣa, the arising of rajo-guṇa.
meaning: born of the quality called rajas

## mahāśanaḥ
ids: gita-03-37-w8

gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular

note: Śaṅkara: mahad aśanam asya, whose food is great, said of kāmaḥ. Written mahāśano before mahā-pāpmā; the visarga becomes o.
meaning: of great appetite

## mahā-pāpmā

number: singular

## viddhi
ids: gita-03-37-w10
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: ad
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The you of the command is not named. Written viddhye; i + e of enam becomes ye.

## enam
ids: gita-03-37-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The e joins viddhi. The i of iha is written on the m, so the line shows nami.
meaning: this

## iha
ids: gita-03-37-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The i is written on enam, so only ha shows. Śaṅkara glosses it saṃsāre, in this world.
meaning: here
