# gita-04-09

## janma
ids: gita-04-09-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: janman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: jan
note: janman in the singular nominative or accusative is janma. It is what he knows, so it is accusative.
meaning: birth (brought about by maya)

## karma
ids: gita-04-09-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: karman in the singular nominative or accusative is karma. It is the second thing he knows, so it is accusative.
meaning: action (protecting the good, and the rest)

## ca
ids: gita-04-09-w3

## me
ids: gita-04-09-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The enclitic me is genitive or dative. It names whose birth and action, so it is genitive.
meaning: my

## divyam
ids: gita-04-09-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: divya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It agrees with janma and karma, so it is accusative. Written divyame where m meets the e of evam.
meaning: divine (the Lord's, not of this world)

## evam
ids: gita-04-09-w6

judgment: obvious
note: The e is written at the end of divyam. The line has divyamevaṃ.
meaning: thus
