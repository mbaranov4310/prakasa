# gita-04-26

## śrotra-ādīni
ids: gita-04-26-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śrotra-ādi
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Hearing and the rest, the senses whose first is hearing. It agrees with indriyāṇi. The neuter plural is nominative or accusative, and the senses are what they offer, so it is accusative. The final i joins the i of indriyāṇi as ī.
meaning: hearing and the other senses

## indriyāṇi
ids: gita-04-26-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
note: The neuter plural is nominative or accusative. It is what they offer, so it is accusative. The initial i joins the preceding i. The final i becomes y before anye, and the a of anye is written with that y.
meaning: the senses

## anye
ids: gita-04-26-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: anya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: anye is locative singular or the pronominal nominative plural. juhvati is plural, so it is nominative plural, some men. The initial a is written with the y of indriyāṇi, and the slice is nye.
meaning: some

## saṃyama-agniṣu
ids: gita-04-26-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃyama-agni
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: plural
compound: karmadhāraya
note: The restraints themselves are the fires, one for each sense.
meaning: into the fires of restraint (one for each sense)

## juhvati
ids: gita-04-26-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: hu
class: hu
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: Reduplicated present of hu. They offer the senses.
meaning: offer

## śabda-ādīn
ids: gita-04-26-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śabda-ādi
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Sound and the rest, the objects whose first is sound. It agrees with viṣayān.
meaning: sound and the other objects

## viṣayān
ids: gita-04-26-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: viṣaya
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: What the second offering puts into the senses. Written viṣayāna, taking the a of anyaḥ.
meaning: the objects

## anyaḥ
ids: gita-04-26-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: anya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Another man, subject of the second juhvati. That verb is plural. The initial a joins viṣayān. The visarga is dropped before the vowel of indriya-agniṣu, and the slice is nya.
meaning: another

## indriya-agniṣu
ids: gita-04-26-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya-agni
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: plural
compound: karmadhāraya
note: The senses themselves are the fires.
meaning: into the fires that are the senses

## juhvati
ids: gita-04-26-w10
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: hu
class: hu
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: The form is third person plural. The subject anyaḥ is singular. He offers by taking in what is not forbidden.
meaning: offers (taking in what is not forbidden)
