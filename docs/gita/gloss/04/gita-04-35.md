# gita-04-35

## yat
ids: gita-04-35-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written yaj before jñātvā. t becomes j before the palatal. The neuter yat is nominative or accusative. It is what you gain, so it is accusative.
meaning: which

## jñātvā
ids: gita-04-35-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: jñā
note: Absolutive of jñā.
meaning: having gained (the knowledge they teach)

## na
ids: gita-04-35-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## punaḥ
ids: gita-04-35-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written punar before moham. The visarga becomes r before the voiced m.
meaning: again

## moham
ids: gita-04-35-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: moha
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: muh
note: From muh. The e of evam is written on this slice, as mohame. The word is moham.
meaning: into confusion

## evam
ids: gita-04-35-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e is written with moham in mohamevaṃ. What remains is printed vaṃ, with anusvāra.
meaning: as you are now

root: yā

tense: simple future

number: singular

## yena

kind: pronoun

stem: yad

case: instrumental
number: singular

## bhūtāni
ids: gita-04-35-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūta
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
root: bhū
note: Past participle of bhū, used as a noun. The neuter plural -āni is nominative or accusative. They are what you see, so it is accusative. The i becomes y before aśeṣeṇa.
meaning: beings

## aśeṣeṇa
ids: gita-04-35-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: aśeṣa
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: Without a remainder, so every one of them. The instrumental is adverbial with the seeing. The initial a is lost in bhūtānyaśeṣeṇa. The slice that remains is śeṣeṇa.
meaning: every one of them, down to a clump of grass

## drakṣyasi
ids: gita-04-35-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dṛś
tense: simple future
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is the unstated you. The i becomes y and takes the ā of ātmani, so the slice is drakṣyasyā.
meaning: you will see

## ātmani
ids: gita-04-35-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: The ā is written in the yā of drakṣyasyā. The final i becomes y before atho, so this slice is tmany.
meaning: in the self

## atho
ids: gita-04-35-w14
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: atha with the particle u. The initial a is taken into the ya of ātmanyatho. The slice that remains is tho.
meaning: and also

## mayi
ids: gita-04-35-w15
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: locative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender.
meaning: in Me
