# gita-04-27

## sarvāṇi
ids: gita-04-27-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
note: It agrees with indriya-karmāṇi. The neuter plural is nominative or accusative. The actions are what they cast, so it is accusative. Written sarvāṇī, the i joining the i of indriya.
meaning: all

## indriya-karmāṇi
ids: gita-04-27-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya-karman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
root: kṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Actions of the senses. The neuter plural is nominative or accusative. It is what they cast, so it is accusative. The initial i joins sarvāṇi.
meaning: the actions of the senses

## prāṇa-karmāṇi
ids: gita-04-27-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prāṇa-karman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
root: kṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Actions of the vital breath, such as bending and stretching. Same case as indriya-karmāṇi, and they are cast too.
meaning: the actions of the vital breaths (bending, stretching)

## ca
ids: gita-04-27-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written cā before apare, and that a is dropped.
meaning: and

## apare
ids: gita-04-27-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: apara
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: apare is locative singular or the pronominal nominative plural. juhvati is plural, so it is nominative plural, the others. The initial a is dropped after ca.
meaning: others

## ātma-saṃyama-yoga-agnau
ids: gita-04-27-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātma-saṃyama-yoga-agni
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: Restraint in the self. That restraint is itself the yoga, and that yoga is the fire. Locative of where they cast the actions.
meaning: into the fire of the yoga that is restraint in the self

## juhvati
ids: gita-04-27-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: hu
class: hu
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: Reduplicated present of hu. They cast the actions in.
meaning: cast

## jñāna-dīpite
ids: gita-04-27-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna-dīpita
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
root: dīp
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Causative of dīp. dīpita is its past participle, kindled by knowledge. It agrees with the fire.
meaning: kindled by discriminating knowledge
