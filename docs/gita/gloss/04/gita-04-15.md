# gita-04-15

## evam
ids: gita-04-15-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written evaṃ before jñātvā.
meaning: thus

## jñātvā
ids: gita-04-15-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: jñā
note: Absolutive of jñā, "having known."
meaning: having known

## kṛtam
ids: gita-04-15-w3, gita-04-15-w15
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It predicates karma, so it is nominative. The first is written kṛtaṃ before karma.
meaning: done

## karma
ids: gita-04-15-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: The form karma is nominative or accusative. It is what was done, so it is nominative.
meaning: action

## pūrvaiḥ
ids: gita-04-15-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pūrva
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: An adjective standing alone as a noun, the earlier people. Written pūrvair before api.
meaning: by the earlier ones

## api
ids: gita-04-15-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: even

## mumukṣubhiḥ
ids: gita-04-15-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mumukṣu
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
root: muc
note: Desiderative of muc, one who wishes to be released.
meaning: by those who sought release

## kuru
ids: gita-04-15-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
class: tan
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
meaning: do

## karma
ids: gita-04-15-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: The form karma is nominative or accusative. It is what you are told to do, so it is accusative. Written karmai before eva.
meaning: action

## eva
ids: gita-04-15-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e joins karma, and the line reads karmaiva.
meaning: itself (not sitting silent, and not renunciation as the thing to do)

## tasmāt
ids: gita-04-15-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: Ablative of tad, "from that," and so "therefore." The neuter is the fact just stated.
meaning: therefore

## tvam
ids: gita-04-15-w12
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: yuṣmad has no gender. Written tvaṃ after tasmāt, with the t doubled.
meaning: you

## pūrvaiḥ
ids: gita-04-15-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pūrva
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: An adjective standing alone as a noun, the earlier people.
meaning: by the earlier ones (Janaka and the rest)

## pūrvataram
ids: gita-04-15-w14
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Comparative of pūrva, with -tara. The neuter accusative is used as an adverb. Written pūrvataraṃ.
meaning: long before
