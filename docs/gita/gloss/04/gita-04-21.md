# gita-04-21

## nirāśīḥ
ids: gita-04-21-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nir-āśis
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Wishes, āśis, have gone from him. Written nirāśīr before yata-citta-ātmā.
meaning: one from whom wishes have gone

## yata-citta-ātmā
ids: gita-04-21-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yata-citta-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: yata is the past participle of yam. The mind and the body are both held. The line writes yatacittātmā.
meaning: whose mind and body are held in check

## tyakta-sarva-parigrahaḥ
ids: gita-04-21-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: tyakta-sarva-parigraha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: tyakta is the past participle of tyaj. Every acquisition is given up by him. The line writes it as one word.
meaning: by whom every acquisition has been given up

## śārīram
ids: gita-04-21-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śārīra
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with karma. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. The action is what he does, so it is accusative. Written śārīraṃ.
meaning: of the body (only what keeps the body alive)

## kevalam
ids: gita-04-21-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kevala
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with karma. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It describes that action, so it is accusative. Written kevalaṃ.
meaning: only (and without the claim of being the doer)

## karma
ids: gita-04-21-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: The form karma is nominative or accusative. It is what he does, so it is accusative.
meaning: action

## kurvan
ids: gita-04-21-w7
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Present participle of kṛ. Joined with na in kurvannāpnoti.
meaning: doing

## na
ids: gita-04-21-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written nā, the a joining the ā of āpnoti.
meaning: not

## āpnoti
ids: gita-04-21-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: āp
class: su
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The ā is heard in nāpnoti.
meaning: incurs

## kilbiṣam
ids: gita-04-21-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kilbiṣa
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he does not incur, so it is accusative.
meaning: a stain (evil, and merit too, since both bind)
