# gita-04-39

## śraddhāvān
ids: gita-04-39-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śraddhāvant
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: śrad in śraddhā is not a prefix. The man of faith stands as this word. Written śraddhāvāṃ before labhate: the verse stores anusvāra for the printed candrabindu, and the l of labhate is doubled.
meaning: the man of faith

## labhate
ids: gita-04-39-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: labh
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The l is doubled after śraddhāvān.
meaning: obtains

## jñānam
ids: gita-04-39-w3, gita-04-39-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: jñā
note: jñāna is from jñā. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he obtains, and what he has gained, so it is accusative.
meaning: knowledge

## tat-paraḥ
ids: gita-04-39-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: tat-para
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Intent on that, the means of knowledge. It agrees with the man of faith.
meaning: intent on the means (going to a teacher, and the rest)

## saṃyata-indriyaḥ
ids: gita-04-39-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃyata-indriya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose senses are drawn back from their objects. saṃyata is the past participle of yam with sam. The verse joins a and i as e.
meaning: with the senses drawn back from their objects

## labdhvā
ids: gita-04-39-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: labh
note: Absolutive of labh.
meaning: having gained

## parām
ids: gita-04-39-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: para
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with śāntim.
meaning: the highest

## śāntim
ids: gita-04-39-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śānti
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: śam
note: From śam. Joined with acireṇa; the verse keeps that a on this word.
meaning: peace (release)

## acireṇa
ids: gita-04-39-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: acira
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: a- negatives cira. Instrumental of time, before long. The ending -ena is masculine or neuter; as an adverb of time it is neuter. The initial a is kept with śāntim. Joined with adhigacchati, a and a merge, and the verse keeps the ā on this word.
meaning: before long

## adhigacchati
ids: gita-04-39-w11
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: gam
prefix: adhi
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The initial a is dropped after acireṇa. The subject is the man of faith in the first line.
meaning: he reaches
