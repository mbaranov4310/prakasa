# gita-04-14

## na
ids: gita-04-14-w1, gita-04-14-w5, gita-04-14-w14
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## mām
ids: gita-04-14-w2, gita-04-14-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Both times it is written māṃ, before karmāṇi and before yaḥ.
meaning: me

## karmāṇi
ids: gita-04-14-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: kṛ
note: The neuter plural -āni is nominative or accusative. The actions are what stain, so it is nominative.
meaning: actions

## limpanti
ids: gita-04-14-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: lip
class: tud
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: lip in the tud class takes the nasal infix. Śaṅkara gives the reason they do not stain: there is no sense of “I” that would start a body.
meaning: stain

## me
ids: gita-04-14-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The enclitic me is genitive or dative. It names whose craving, so it is genitive.
meaning: my

## karma-phale
ids: gita-04-14-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karma-phala
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The fruit of action. karma is from kṛ. Locative of what the craving is for. Written as one word in the line.
meaning: for the fruit of action

## spṛhā
ids: gita-04-14-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: spṛhā
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: spṛh
note: From spṛh. There is no finite verb. “Is” is understood: there is no craving.
meaning: craving

## iti
ids: gita-04-14-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It closes the two statements just made.
meaning: thus

## yaḥ
ids: gita-04-14-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The visarga becomes o before abhijānāti, and the a of abhi is dropped.
meaning: who

## abhijānāti
ids: gita-04-14-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: jñā
prefix: abhi
class: krī
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: jñā is a ninth-class verb, jānāti. The a of abhi is dropped after yo.
meaning: knows (as himself: I do not act, and I do not crave the fruit)

## karmabhiḥ
ids: gita-04-14-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: plural
root: kṛ
note: The visarga becomes r before na.
meaning: by actions

## saḥ
ids: gita-04-14-w15
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: saḥ before a consonant is written sa.
meaning: he

## badhyate
ids: gita-04-14-w16
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bandh
class: krī
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Passive of bandh. The ya is the passive sign. bandh is a ninth-class root.
meaning: is bound
