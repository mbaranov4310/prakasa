# gita-04-41

## yoga-saṃnyasta-karmāṇam
ids: gita-04-41-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga-saṃnyasta-karman
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: He by whom actions have been laid down by yoga. saṃnyasta is the past participle of as, to throw, with sam and ni. It agrees with the attentive man, so the ending -āṇam is masculine accusative.
meaning: who has laid action down by the yoga of seeing the truth

## jñāna-saṃchinna-saṃśayam
ids: gita-04-41-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna-saṃchinna-saṃśaya
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose doubt has been cut by knowledge. saṃchinna is the past participle of chid with sam. It agrees with the attentive man, so it is masculine accusative.
meaning: whose doubt is cut by knowledge that the self and the Lord are one

## ātmavantam
ids: gita-04-41-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātmavant
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The attentive man stands as this word. It is whom the actions do not bind.
meaning: the attentive man (one who does not slip)

## na

## karmāṇi
ids: gita-04-41-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: kṛ
note: karman is from kṛ. The neuter plural -āni is nominative or accusative. The verb is plural and the man is singular, so actions are what bind, and it is nominative.
meaning: actions (seen as the play of the constituents)
