# gita-04-10

## vīta-rāga-bhaya-krodhāḥ
ids: gita-04-10-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vīta-rāga-bhaya-krodha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Passion, fear, and anger are a dvandva, and vīta says they have gone from these people. vīta is the past participle of i with vi. The plural -āḥ is nominative or vocative. It agrees with bahavaḥ, so it is nominative. The visarga drops before manmayāḥ.
meaning: those from whom passion, fear, and anger have gone

## manmayāḥ
ids: gita-04-10-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: manmaya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: From mat, “me,” and the suffix maya, “made of, full of.” The plural -āḥ is nominative or vocative. It agrees with bahavaḥ, so it is nominative. The visarga drops before mām.
meaning: absorbed in me (seeing no difference from the Lord)

## mām
ids: gita-04-10-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māmu, where m meets the u of upāśritāḥ.
meaning: me

## upāśritāḥ
ids: gita-04-10-w4
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: śri
prefix: upa-ā
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Past participle of śri with upa and ā. The u is written at the end of mām. The plural -āḥ is nominative or vocative. It agrees with bahavaḥ, so it is nominative.
meaning: who have taken refuge

## bahavaḥ
ids: gita-04-10-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bahu
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: An adjective standing as the head, “many.” The plural -aḥ is nominative or vocative. These are the ones who reach, so it is nominative. The visarga becomes o before jñāna-tapasā.
meaning: many

## jñāna-tapasā
ids: gita-04-10-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna-tapas
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: tap
compound: karmadhāraya
note: The austerity that is knowledge. jñāna is from jñā, and tapas is from tap. Instrumental of the means. Written as one word in the line.
meaning: by the austerity that is knowledge

## pūtāḥ
ids: gita-04-10-w7
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: pū
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Past participle of pū. The plural -āḥ is nominative or vocative. It agrees with bahavaḥ, so it is nominative. The visarga drops before mad-bhāvam.
meaning: made pure

## mad-bhāvam
ids: gita-04-10-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mad-bhāva
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: bhū
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: mad is the compound form of asmad, and bhāva is from bhū: my state. Written mad-bhāvamā, where m meets the ā of āgatāḥ.
meaning: my state (release)
