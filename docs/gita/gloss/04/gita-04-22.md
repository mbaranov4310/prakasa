# gita-04-22

## yadṛcchā-lābha-saṃtuṣṭaḥ
ids: gita-04-22-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yadṛcchā-lābha-saṃtuṣṭa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: tuṣ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Content with a gain that comes of itself. saṃtuṣṭa is the past participle of tuṣ with sam. The visarga is written o before the voiced d of dvandva-atītaḥ.
meaning: content with a gain that comes of itself, unasked

## dvandva-atītaḥ
ids: gita-04-22-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: dvandva-atīta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: i
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Gone beyond the pairs. atīta is the past participle of i with ati. The visarga is written o before the voiced v of vimatsaraḥ.
meaning: unshaken by the pairs (cold and heat, and the rest)

## vimatsaraḥ
ids: gita-04-22-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vimatsara
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: vi has the sense of vigata, one whose envy has gone. It names the same man as the nominatives beside it.
meaning: free of envy

## samaḥ
ids: gita-04-22-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sama
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Equal when that gain comes and when it does not. It names the same man.
meaning: the same

## siddhau
ids: gita-04-22-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: siddhi
gender: feminine
case: locative
number: singular
root: sidh
note: Locative of when, when that gain comes. Written siddhāv before the a of asiddhau, and that a is dropped.
meaning: when that gain comes

## asiddhau
ids: gita-04-22-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: asiddhi
gender: feminine
case: locative
number: singular
root: sidh
note: Negated siddhi, when that gain does not come. The initial a joins the v of siddhāv.
meaning: when it does not

## ca
ids: gita-04-22-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## kṛtvā
ids: gita-04-22-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
note: Absolutive of kṛ, having done. Śaṅkara takes the act as begging and the like, done only to keep the body. It leans on na nibadhyate.
meaning: having done

## api
ids: gita-04-22-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The particle api, even, with kṛtvā. The initial a is dropped after kṛtvā.
meaning: even

## na
ids: gita-04-22-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## nibadhyate
ids: gita-04-22-w11
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bandh
prefix: ni
class: krī
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The -ya- marks the passive of bandh.
meaning: is he bound (knowledge has already burned the act that binds)
