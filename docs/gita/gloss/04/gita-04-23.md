# gita-04-23

## gata-saṅgasya
ids: gita-04-23-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: gata-saṅga
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: A bahuvrīhi standing as the man whose attachment has gone. gata is the past participle of gam.
meaning: whose attachment has withdrawn on every side

## muktasya
ids: gita-04-23-w2
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: muc
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Past participle of muc, the man who is freed. It agrees with gata-saṅgasya.
meaning: free of the bond of merit and demerit

## jñāna-avasthita-cetasaḥ
ids: gita-04-23-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna-avasthita-cetas
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose mind rests in knowledge. avasthita, the middle member, is the past participle of sthā with ava. The bahuvrīhi is masculine of the man, so the genitive ends in -aḥ.
meaning: whose mind rests in knowledge

## yajñāya
ids: gita-04-23-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yajña
gender: masculine
case: dative
number: singular
root: yaj
note: Dative of purpose, for the sake of the sacrifice. Written yajñāyā before ācarataḥ, and that initial ā is dropped.
meaning: for the sake of sacrifice

## ācarataḥ
ids: gita-04-23-w5
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: car
prefix: ā
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Present participle of car with ā, of the man who acts. The ending -ataḥ is genitive or ablative. It stands with the other genitives, so it is genitive. The initial ā joins yajñāya.
meaning: who acts

## karma
ids: gita-04-23-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: The neuter karma is nominative or accusative. It is what dissolves, so it is nominative.
meaning: the action

## samagram
ids: gita-04-23-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: samagra
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: sa-agra, with its fruit. It agrees with karma. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative, and karma is nominative, so this is nominative. Written samagraṃ before pravilīyate.
meaning: together with its fruit

## pravilīyate
ids: gita-04-23-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: lī
prefix: pra-vi
class: div
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: lī is class 4, so the -ya- is the present stem. The action perishes. There is no one who dissolves it.
meaning: dissolves
