# gita-04-28

## dravya-yajñāḥ
ids: gita-04-28-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dravya-yajña
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: The form could name the sacrifice or the man whose sacrifice it is. It names the man: one whose sacrifice is the giving of wealth. The visarga is written s before tapas-yajñāḥ.
meaning: men whose sacrifice is a gift of wealth

## tapas-yajñāḥ
ids: gita-04-28-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: tapas-yajña
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose sacrifice is austerity. In the joined line tapas is written tapo, and the visarga is not shown before yoga-yajñāḥ.
meaning: men whose sacrifice is austerity

## yoga-yajñāḥ
ids: gita-04-28-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga-yajña
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose sacrifice is yoga, restraint of the breath and the rest. The visarga is written s before tathā.
meaning: men whose sacrifice is yoga (restraint of breath, and the rest)

## tathā
ids: gita-04-28-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: And likewise. The a of apare is dropped after it.
meaning: and

## apare
ids: gita-04-28-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: apara
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: apare is locative singular or the pronominal nominative plural. It stands with the nominative plural names of the men, so it is nominative plural, the others. The initial a is dropped after tathā.
meaning: others

## svādhyāya-jñāna-yajñāḥ
ids: gita-04-28-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: svādhyāya-jñāna-yajña
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Recitation and knowledge, the two sacrifices. The one word names both groups of men, those whose sacrifice is recitation and those whose sacrifice is knowledge of scripture. The visarga is written ś before ca.
meaning: men whose sacrifice is recitation and knowledge of scripture

## ca
ids: gita-04-28-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## yatayaḥ
ids: gita-04-28-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yati
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: yat
note: Men whose way is effort. There is no finite verb. It is said of the recitation and knowledge sacrificers.
meaning: men given to effort

## saṃśita-vratāḥ
ids: gita-04-28-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃśita-vrata
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Those whose vows are sharpened. śita is the past participle of śo, with sam. It agrees with yatayaḥ.
meaning: whose vows are sharpened
