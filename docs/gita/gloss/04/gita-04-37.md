# gita-04-37

## yathā
ids: gita-04-37-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written yathai before edhāṃsi; ā and e join as ai, kept on this word.
meaning: as

## edhāṃsi
ids: gita-04-37-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: edhas
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
note: The initial e is dropped after yathā. The neuter plural -āṃsi is nominative or accusative. The fire makes the fuel into ash, so it is accusative.
meaning: fuel

## samiddhaḥ
ids: gita-04-37-w3
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: indh
prefix: sam
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with agniḥ. Written samiddho before agniḥ; the visarga becomes o, and the a of agniḥ is dropped.
meaning: well kindled

## agniḥ
ids: gita-04-37-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: agni
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The initial a is dropped after samiddho. Written agnir before bhasmasāt.
meaning: a fire

## bhasmasāt
ids: gita-04-37-w5, gita-04-37-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: -sāt makes an indeclinable, “into ash,” with kurute.
meaning: to ash

## kurute
ids: gita-04-37-w6, gita-04-37-w11
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
class: tan
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Before arjuna the a of arjuna is dropped, and the verse writes kurute'.
meaning: makes

## arjuna
ids: gita-04-37-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: arjuna
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
note: The initial a is dropped after kurute.
meaning: O Arjuna

## jñāna-agniḥ
ids: gita-04-37-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna-agni
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: jñā
compound: karmadhāraya
note: jñāna is from jñā. Knowledge itself is the fire.
meaning: the fire of knowledge (right vision, which leaves action without seed)

## sarva-karmāṇi
ids: gita-04-37-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-karman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
root: kṛ
compound: karmadhāraya
note: karman is from kṛ. The neuter plural -āni is nominative or accusative. It is what the fire of knowledge makes into ash, so it is accusative.
meaning: all actions

## tathā
ids: gita-04-37-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It answers yathā.
meaning: so
