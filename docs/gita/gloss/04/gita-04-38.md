## jñānena

kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna

case: instrumental
number: singular

## sadṛśam
ids: gita-04-38-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sadṛśa
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It agrees with pavitram, the thing that is found, so it is nominative.
meaning: equal

## pavitram
ids: gita-04-38-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pavitra
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: pū
note: From pū. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what is found, so it is nominative. Joined with iha as pavitramiha; the verse keeps the i on this word.
meaning: a means of purification

## iha
ids: gita-04-38-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The initial i is kept with pavitram.
meaning: here

## vidyate
ids: gita-04-38-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: div
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The root is vid of the fourth class, to exist, to be found.
meaning: is found

## tat
ids: gita-04-38-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It takes up the knowledge of the first line. The neuter tat is nominative or accusative. It is what he finds, so it is accusative.
meaning: that

## svayam
ids: gita-04-38-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It names the seeker himself.
meaning: himself, the seeker

## yoga-saṃsiddhaḥ
ids: gita-04-38-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga-saṃsiddha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: sidh
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: saṃsiddha is the past participle of sidh with sam. Made fit by yoga. It is the subject of vindati; the seeker stands as this word.
meaning: made fit by yoga (the yoga of action and the yoga of composure)

number: singular
