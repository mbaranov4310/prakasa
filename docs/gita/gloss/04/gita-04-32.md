# gita-04-32

## evam
ids: gita-04-32-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Printed with anusvāra as evaṃ.
meaning: thus

## bahu-vidhāḥ
ids: gita-04-32-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: bahu-vidha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Of many kinds. The visarga of -āḥ is dropped before the voiced y of yajñāḥ.
meaning: of many kinds

## yajñāḥ
ids: gita-04-32-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yajña
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: yaj
note: The visarga of -āḥ is dropped before the voiced v of vitatāḥ.
meaning: sacrifices

## vitatāḥ
ids: gita-04-32-w4
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: tan
prefix: vi
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Past participle of tan with the prefix vi. The visarga of -āḥ is dropped before the voiced b of brahmaṇaḥ. There is no finite verb; “are” is understood.
meaning: are spread

## brahmaṇaḥ
ids: gita-04-32-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahman
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Here brahman is the Veda. brahmaṇaḥ is genitive or ablative. The door belongs to it, so it is genitive. Written brahmaṇo before mukhe; the visarga becomes o.
meaning: of the Veda

## mukhe
ids: gita-04-32-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mukha
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
note: The mouth is the door where the sacrifices are spread.
meaning: at the door

## karma-jān
ids: gita-04-32-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: karma-ja
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
root: jan
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Born from action. ja is from jan. It agrees with tān.
meaning: born of action (of body, speech, and mind, not of the self)

kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: ad
tense: command

person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is the unstated you.
meaning: know

## tān
ids: gita-04-32-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
meaning: them

## sarvān
ids: gita-04-32-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: The e of the following evam is written on this slice, as sarvāne. The word is sarvān.
meaning: all

## evam
ids: gita-04-32-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e is written with sarvān in sarvānevaṃ. What remains is printed vaṃ, with anusvāra.
meaning: in this way

## jñātvā
ids: gita-04-32-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: jñā
note: Absolutive of jñā.
meaning: knowing (these acts are not mine; I do nothing)

## vimokṣyase
ids: gita-04-32-w13
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: muc
prefix: vi
class: tud
tense: simple future
voice: ātmanepada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is the unstated you. The middle means you will be released.
meaning: you will be released from bondage
