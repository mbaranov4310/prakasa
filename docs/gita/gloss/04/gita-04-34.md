# gita-04-34

## tat
ids: gita-04-34-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written tad before viddhi; t becomes d before the voiced v. The neuter tat is nominative or accusative. It is what you learn, so it is accusative.
meaning: that

## viddhi
ids: gita-04-34-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: ad
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is the unstated you.
meaning: learn

## praṇipātena
ids: gita-04-34-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: praṇipāta
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: pat
note: From pat with the prefixes pra and ni.
meaning: by a full prostration

## paripraśnena
ids: gita-04-34-w4

stem: paripraśna

case: instrumental

## te
ids: gita-04-34-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: dative
number: singular
note: te is nominative plural of tad, or dative or genitive singular of yuṣmad. The knowers are the ones who teach, and te is the person taught, so it is dative singular. yuṣmad has no gender.
meaning: you

## jñānam
ids: gita-04-34-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: jñā
note: Printed with anusvāra as jñānaṃ. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what they will teach, so it is accusative.
meaning: the knowledge

## jñāninaḥ
ids: gita-04-34-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñānin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: jñāninaḥ is nominative plural, accusative plural, or genitive singular. These are the people who teach, so it is nominative plural. Written jñāninas before tattva; the visarga becomes s.
meaning: the knowers

## tattva-darśinaḥ
ids: gita-04-34-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: tattva-darśin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Those who see the truth. It agrees with jñāninaḥ, so this ending too is nominative plural.
meaning: who see the truth (a learned man who does not see cannot teach what does the work)
