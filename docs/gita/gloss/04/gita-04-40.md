# gita-04-40

## ajñaḥ
ids: gita-04-40-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ajña
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: jñā
note: a- negatives jña, the agent of jñā. The person stands as this word. Written ajñaś before ca.
meaning: one who does not know the self

## ca
ids: gita-04-40-w2, gita-04-40-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The first is written cā before aśraddadhānaḥ; a and a join, and the verse keeps the ā on this word.
meaning: and

## aśraddadhānaḥ
ids: gita-04-40-w3
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: dhā
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Present participle of dhā. śrad in śraddhā is not a prefix. a- is the negative. The initial a is kept with the preceding ca. Written aśraddadhānaś before ca.
meaning: one without faith in the teacher's word and in scripture

## saṃśaya-ātmā
ids: gita-04-40-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃśaya-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose mind is doubt. The person stands as this word. The verse joins a and ā as ā.
meaning: one whose mind is doubt (the worst of the three)

## vinaśyati
ids: gita-04-40-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: naś
prefix: vi
class: div
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
meaning: perishes

## na
ids: gita-04-40-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written nā before ayam; a and a join, and the verse keeps the ā on this word.
meaning: not

## ayam
ids: gita-04-40-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The initial a is dropped after nā. It agrees with lokaḥ.
meaning: this

## lokaḥ
ids: gita-04-40-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: loka
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written loko before asti; the visarga becomes o, and the a of asti is dropped.
meaning: common world

## asti
ids: gita-04-40-w10
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The initial a is dropped after loko. The person is the genitive saṃśayātmanaḥ, not part of this verb.
meaning: is

## na
ids: gita-04-40-w11, gita-04-40-w13
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: nor

## paraḥ
ids: gita-04-40-w12
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: para
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It stands for the next world. That lokaḥ is understood, and it is not the lokaḥ already named. Written paro before na.
meaning: the next

## sukham
ids: gita-04-40-w14
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sukha
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is a third thing that is not there for him, so it is nominative.
meaning: happiness

## saṃśaya-ātmanaḥ
ids: gita-04-40-w15
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃśaya-ātman
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose mind is doubt. The ending -manaḥ is genitive or ablative. It is the person for whom this world, the next, and happiness are absent, so it is genitive. The verse joins a and ā as ā.
meaning: for the man whose mind is doubt
