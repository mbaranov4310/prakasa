# gita-04-12

## kāṅkṣantaḥ
ids: gita-04-12-w1
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: kāṅkṣ
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Present participle of kāṅkṣ. There is no other nominative, so these are the ones who sacrifice. The plural -aḥ is nominative or vocative. The verb is third person, so it is nominative.
meaning: wanting

case: genitive
number: plural
root: kṛ

meaning: of actions

## devatāḥ
ids: gita-04-12-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: devatā
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: The feminine plural -āḥ is nominative or accusative. The sacrificers are the masculine kāṅkṣantaḥ, and the gods are what they sacrifice to, so it is accusative.
meaning: the gods (Indra, Agni, and the rest)
