# gita-04-24

## brahma
ids: gita-04-24-w1, gita-04-24-w3, gita-04-24-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahman
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter brahma is nominative or accusative, and each one is nominative. The offering is Brahman, the oblation is Brahman, and Brahman is what he reaches. The first is its own word, not a compound with arpaṇam, and it is written brahmā, the following a being dropped. The third joins eva as brahmai.
meaning: Brahman

## arpaṇam
ids: gita-04-24-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: arpaṇa
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: ṛ
note: Noun from the causative of ṛ, the offering. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what is said to be Brahman, so it is nominative. Written arpaṇaṃ before the next brahma. The initial a is dropped after the first brahma.
meaning: the offering

## haviḥ
ids: gita-04-24-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: havis
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: hu
note: The neuter haviḥ is nominative or accusative. It is what is said to be Brahman, and it is what is offered, so it is nominative. Written havir before the voiced b of brahma-agnau.
meaning: the oblation

## brahma-agnau
ids: gita-04-24-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahma-agni
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: The fire that is Brahman, where the oblation is offered.
meaning: into the fire that is Brahman

## brahmaṇā
ids: gita-04-24-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahman
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: Instrumental of the agent who offers. That agent is Brahman.
meaning: by Brahman, the agent

## hutam
ids: gita-04-24-w7
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: hu
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Past participle of hu. It agrees with haviḥ. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative, and haviḥ is nominative, so this is nominative.
meaning: offered

## eva
ids: gita-04-24-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It singles out the third brahma. brahma and eva join as brahmai, and the slice keeps v.
meaning: itself

## tena
ids: gita-04-24-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: tena is the same form in every gender. brahma-karma-samādhinā names a man, so it is masculine.
meaning: by him

## gantavyam
ids: gita-04-24-w11
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: gam
participle: potential (-tavya)
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: What is to be reached. It agrees with brahma. There is no finite verb. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative, and brahma is nominative, so this is nominative. Written gantavyaṃ before brahma-karma-samādhinā.
meaning: the end to be reached

## brahma-karma-samādhinā
ids: gita-04-24-w12
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: brahma-karma-samādhi
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: He whose meditation is that the action is Brahman. brahma-karma, the action that is Brahman, sits inside the bahuvrīhi. It agrees with tena.
meaning: whose meditation is that the action is Brahman
