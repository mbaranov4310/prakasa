# gita-04-29

## apāne
ids: gita-04-29-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: apāna
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
root: an
note: From an with the prefix apa. Śaṅkara: the downward breath. Offering the outgoing breath into it is the restraint called filling.
meaning: into the downward breath

## juhvati
ids: gita-04-29-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: hu
class: hu
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
meaning: they offer

## prāṇam
ids: gita-04-29-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prāṇa
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: an
note: From an with the prefix pra. Printed with anusvāra as prāṇaṃ. Śaṅkara: the breath whose path is the air going out through the mouth and nose.
meaning: the outgoing breath

## prāṇe
ids: gita-04-29-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prāṇa
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
root: an
note: From an with the prefix pra. Written prāṇe before the avagraha in prāṇe'pānam. Offering the downward breath into it is the restraint called emptying.
meaning: into the outgoing breath

## apānam
ids: gita-04-29-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: apāna
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: an
note: From an with the prefix apa. The avagraha in prāṇe'pānam stands for the dropped a of apānam, and the rest is printed pānaṃ with anusvāra. Śaṅkara: its path is the reverse of prāṇa's, going downward.
meaning: the downward breath

## tathā
ids: gita-04-29-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Printed in tathāpare with no avagraha. The ā of tathā and the a of apare combine as ā.
meaning: likewise

## apare
ids: gita-04-29-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: apara
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: It stands alone as a noun. apare is locative singular or the pronominal nominative plural. It names the people who offer, so it is nominative plural. The initial a is lost in tathāpare.
meaning: others

## prāṇa-apāna-gatī
ids: gita-04-29-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prāṇa-apāna-gati
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: dual
root: gam
compound: dvandva
note: Śaṅkara: prāṇa's path is the air going out through the mouth and nose, and apāna's path goes downward. gati is from gam. The feminine dual -ī is nominative or accusative. It is what they stop, so it is accusative.
meaning: the outgoing path and the downward path

## ruddhvā
ids: gita-04-29-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: rudh
note: Absolutive of rudh. Stopping the two paths is the restraint called holding.
meaning: having stopped

## prāṇāyāma-parāyaṇāḥ
ids: gita-04-29-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: prāṇāyāma-parāyaṇa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Those whose highest aim is restraint of the breath. It agrees with apare.
meaning: given over to restraint of the breath
