# gita-04-06

## ajaḥ
ids: gita-04-06-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: aja
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: jan
note: a-ja, not born. It agrees with san. Written ajo before api; the a of api is dropped.
meaning: unborn

## api
ids: gita-04-06-w2, gita-04-06-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The initial a of each is dropped after a visarga; the avagraha marks it.
meaning: though

## san
ids: gita-04-06-w3, gita-04-06-w8
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: as
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Present participle of as. The first joins avyaya-ātmā, and the n is doubled. The second stands before the daṇḍa.
meaning: being

## avyaya-ātmā
ids: gita-04-06-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: avyaya-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose nature does not waste. It agrees with the first san. The initial a is joined to the n of san.
meaning: of a nature that does not waste (knowledge and power stay)

## bhūtānām
ids: gita-04-06-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūta
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: plural
root: bhū
note: Past participle of bhū, used as a noun. The final m joins the ī of īśvaraḥ.
meaning: of beings (from Brahma down to a clump of grass)

## īśvaraḥ
ids: gita-04-06-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: īśvara
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: īś
note: It agrees with the second san. Written īśvaro before api; the a of api is dropped. The initial ī is joined to bhūtānām.
meaning: the lord

## prakṛtim
ids: gita-04-06-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prakṛti
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: From pra-kṛ. Written prakṛtiṃ.
meaning: nature (my maya, of the three qualities)

## svām
ids: gita-04-06-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sva
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with prakṛtim. The final m joins the a of adhiṣṭhāya.
meaning: my own

## adhiṣṭhāya
ids: gita-04-06-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: adhi
note: Absolutive of adhi-sthā. The initial a is joined to the m of svām.
meaning: having taken charge of

## saṃbhavāmi
ids: gita-04-06-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
prefix: sam
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: The subject I is in the ending. The final i joins the ā of ātma-māyayā.
meaning: I come to be (as if with a body)

## ātma-māyayā
ids: gita-04-06-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātma-māyā
gender: feminine
case: instrumental
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The māyā of the self. The initial ā is joined to the i of saṃbhavāmi.
meaning: by my own maya (not in the ultimate sense)
