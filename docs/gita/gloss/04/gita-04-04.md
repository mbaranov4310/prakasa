# gita-04-04

## arjunaḥ
ids: gita-04-04-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: arjuna
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The visarga drops before the u of uvāca.
meaning: Arjuna

## uvāca
ids: gita-04-04-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
class: ad
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
meaning: said

## aparam
ids: gita-04-04-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: apara
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with the first janma, so the -am is nominative. Written aparaṃ.
meaning: later (in Vasudeva's house)

## bhavataḥ
ids: gita-04-04-w4
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Present participle of bhū, used as the respectful you. bhavataḥ is genitive or ablative. It is whose birth, so it is genitive. Written bhavato before janma.
meaning: your

## janma
ids: gita-04-04-w5, gita-04-04-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: janman
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: jan
note: The form janma is nominative or accusative. Each is the subject of its own clause, so it is nominative.
meaning: birth

## param
ids: gita-04-04-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: para
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with the second janma, so the -am is nominative. Written paraṃ.
meaning: earlier (at the start of creation)

## vivasvataḥ
ids: gita-04-04-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: vivasvat
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: vivasvataḥ is genitive or ablative. It is whose birth, so it is genitive.

## katham
ids: gita-04-04-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The final m joins the e of etat.
meaning: how

## etat
ids: gita-04-04-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter form is nominative or accusative. It is what he would understand, so it is accusative. Written etad before vijānīyām.
meaning: this

## vijānīyām
ids: gita-04-04-w11
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: jñā
prefix: vi
class: krī
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: The subject I is in the ending. Written vijānīyāṃ before tvam.
meaning: am I to understand (without contradiction)

## tvam
ids: gita-04-04-w12
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: yuṣmad has no gender. The final m joins the ā of ādau.
meaning: you

## ādau
ids: gita-04-04-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ādi
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: The ā is joined to the m of tvam.
meaning: in the beginning

## proktavān
ids: gita-04-04-w14
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: vac
prefix: pra
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The active past participle in -vat. It agrees with tvam and is the predicate of the iti clause. The yoga it taught is not stated in this verse. The final n joins the i of iti.
meaning: taught

## iti
ids: gita-04-04-w15
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The i is joined to the n of proktavān.
meaning: that
