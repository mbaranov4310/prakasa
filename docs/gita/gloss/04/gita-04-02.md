# gita-04-02

## evam
ids: gita-04-02-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: thus

## paramparā-prāptam
ids: gita-04-02-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: paramparā-prāpta
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: āp
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: prāpta is the past participle of pra-āp. It agrees with imam, so the -am is masculine accusative. The final m joins the i of imam.
meaning: received through the succession (of kshatriyas)

## imam
ids: gita-04-02-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The i joins the m of paramparā-prāptam. Written imaṃ before rāja-ṛṣayaḥ.
meaning: this

## rāja-ṛṣayaḥ
ids: gita-04-02-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: rāja-ṛṣi
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: karmadhāraya
note: Kings who are seers. Written rājarṣayo before viduḥ.
meaning: royal sages (kings who are seers)

## viduḥ
ids: gita-04-02-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: ad
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: Perfect of vid, to know.
meaning: knew

## saḥ
ids: gita-04-02-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with yogaḥ. The visarga of saḥ drops before kālena.
meaning: that

## kālena
ids: gita-04-02-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kāla
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: The final a joins the i of iha.
meaning: by time

## iha
ids: gita-04-02-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The i is dropped after the e of kālene.
meaning: here (in this world)

## mahatā
ids: gita-04-02-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mahat
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: The instrumental mahatā is masculine or neuter. It agrees with kālena, so it is masculine.
meaning: long

## yogaḥ
ids: gita-04-02-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
note: Written yogo before naṣṭaḥ.

## naṣṭaḥ
ids: gita-04-02-w11
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: naś
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Past participle of naś. It is the predicate of yogaḥ.

## paraṃtapa
ids: gita-04-02-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: paraṃtapa
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
root: tap
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Vocative singular, so there is no visarga. He burns the foe.
meaning: O scorcher of the foe (he burns those who stand against him)
