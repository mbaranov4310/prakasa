# gita-04-25

## daivam
ids: gita-04-25-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: daiva
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The sacrifice by which the gods are worshipped. It agrees with the first yajñam. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative, and yajñam is accusative, so this is accusative. Written daivame, the e of eva joining it.
meaning: directed to the gods

## eva
ids: gita-04-25-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It singles out that sacrifice. The e joins daivam. The a joins the a of apare as ā, so the slice is vā, and apare loses its a.
meaning: only

## apare
ids: gita-04-25-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: apara
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: apare is locative singular or the pronominal nominative plural. The verb is plural, and yoginaḥ stands beside it, so it is nominative plural, some men. The initial a is dropped after eva.
meaning: some

## yajñam
ids: gita-04-25-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yajña
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: yaj
note: What they attend to. Written yajñaṃ before yoginaḥ.
meaning: the sacrifice

## yoginaḥ
ids: gita-04-25-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yogin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: yuj
note: yoginaḥ is nominative or accusative plural. It names the same men as apare, so it is nominative. Here the men of yoga are the men of rites.
meaning: ritualists (men of rites)

## paryupāsate
ids: gita-04-25-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: ās
prefix: pari-upa
class: ad
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: plural
meaning: attend to

## brahma-agnau
ids: gita-04-25-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahma-agni
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: The fire is Brahman, and it is where they offer. Written brahmāgnāv before apare, and that a is dropped.
meaning: into the fire of Brahman

## apare
ids: gita-04-25-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: apara
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: apare is locative singular or the pronominal nominative plural. upajuhvati is plural, so it is nominative plural, the others. The initial a is dropped after brahmāgnāv.
meaning: others (those who have renounced)

## yajñam
ids: gita-04-25-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yajña
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: yaj
note: What they offer. He takes this yajña as the self. Written yajñaṃ before yajñena.
meaning: the sacrifice (the self, so called)

## yajñena
ids: gita-04-25-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yajña
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: yaj
note: Instrumental of the means, by that same sacrifice. Written yajñenai, the a joining the e of eva.
meaning: by the sacrifice

## eva
ids: gita-04-25-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It singles out yajñena. The remaining a joins the u of upajuhvati as o, so the slice is vo.
meaning: itself

## upajuhvati
ids: gita-04-25-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: hu
prefix: upa
class: hu
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: The initial u is joined into the o of the preceding vo.
meaning: offer
