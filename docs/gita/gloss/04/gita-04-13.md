# gita-04-13

## cāturvarṇyam
ids: gita-04-13-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: cāturvarṇya
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The four orders taken as one. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what was brought forth, so it is nominative. Written cāturvarṇyaṃ before mayā.
meaning: the four orders

## mayā
ids: gita-04-13-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender.
meaning: by me

## sṛṣṭam
ids: gita-04-13-w3
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sṛj
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Past participle of sṛj, passive. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It agrees with cāturvarṇyam, so it is nominative. Written sṛṣṭaṃ before guṇa-karma-vibhāgaśaḥ.
meaning: brought forth

## guṇa-karma-vibhāgaśaḥ
ids: gita-04-13-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: A -śas adverb. guṇa and karma are taken together: according to the division of the qualities and of the actions. vibhāga is from vi and bhaj. Written as one word in the line.
meaning: according to the division of the qualities and of actions

## tasya
ids: gita-04-13-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: singular
note: tasya is the genitive for masculine or neuter. It points back to cāturvarṇyam, so it is neuter.
meaning: of that

## kartāram
ids: gita-04-13-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kartṛ
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: Agent noun from kṛ. It agrees with mām. Written kartārama, where m meets the a of api.
meaning: the maker (in the transactions of maya)

## api
ids: gita-04-13-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The a is written at the end of kartāram. It marks the concession, though he is the maker.
meaning: though

## mām
ids: gita-04-13-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māṃ before viddhi.
meaning: me

## viddhi
ids: gita-04-13-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: ad
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is the unstated you. The i becomes y before akartāram.
meaning: know

## akartāram
ids: gita-04-13-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: akartṛ
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: The negative of the agent noun kartṛ. It agrees with mām. The initial a is written as y on viddhi, and m meets the a of avyayam.
meaning: one who does not act (in the ultimate sense)

## avyayam
ids: gita-04-13-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: avyaya
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: a and vyaya, not subject to decay. The -am of a masculine adjective is accusative. It agrees with mām. The initial a is written at the end of akartāram.
meaning: unchanging (not caught in the round of birth)
