# gita-04-11

## ye
ids: gita-04-11-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
meaning: who

## yathā
ids: gita-04-11-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It is answered by tathā.
meaning: in whatever way (for whatever end)

## mām
ids: gita-04-11-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māṃ before prapadyante.
meaning: me

## prapadyante
ids: gita-04-11-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: pad
prefix: pra
class: div
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: A class-4 present and the passive look the same. They resort to me, so it is ātmanepada.
meaning: resort

## tān
ids: gita-04-11-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: Written tāṃs before tathā.
meaning: them

## tathā
ids: gita-04-11-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Joins eva. The line writes tathaiva. It answers yathā.
meaning: in that way

## eva
ids: gita-04-11-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e is written at the end of tathā.
meaning: that very

## bhajāmi
ids: gita-04-11-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhaj
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: The i becomes y before aham. The line writes bhajāmyaham.
meaning: I favor (by giving that fruit, not from partiality)

## aham
ids: gita-04-11-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The a is joined to bhajāmi, and the line writes bhajāmyaham.
meaning: I

## mama
ids: gita-04-11-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender.
meaning: my

## vartma
ids: gita-04-11-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: vartman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: vṛt
note: vartman in the singular nominative or accusative is vartma. It is what they follow, so it is accusative. The final a joins the a of anu. The line writes vartmānu.
meaning: path

## anuvartante
ids: gita-04-11-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vṛt
prefix: anu
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: The a of anu is written at the end of vartma.
meaning: follow

## manuṣyāḥ
ids: gita-04-11-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: manuṣya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The plural -āḥ is nominative or vocative. They are the ones who follow, so it is nominative.
meaning: men (those fit for an action and seeking its fruit)

## pārtha
ids: gita-04-11-w14
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pārtha
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
note: Vocative.
meaning: O son of Pritha

## sarvaśaḥ
ids: gita-04-11-w15
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: A -śas adverb from sarva.
