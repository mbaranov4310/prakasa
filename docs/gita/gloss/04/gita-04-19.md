# gita-04-19

## yasya
ids: gita-04-19-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: The genitive yasya is masculine or neuter. The one who sees is a man, so it is masculine.
meaning: whose (the one who sees in that way)

## sarve
ids: gita-04-19-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: It agrees with samārambhāḥ.
meaning: all

## samārambhāḥ
ids: gita-04-19-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: samārambha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: rabh
note: Noun from sam-ā-rabh, an undertaking.
meaning: undertakings

## kāma-saṃkalpa-varjitāḥ
ids: gita-04-19-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kāma-saṃkalpa-varjita
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: vṛj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: varjita is the past participle of vṛj. Free of desire and of resolve. The line writes it as one word.
meaning: free of desire and of the resolves that cause it

## jñāna-agni-dagdha-karmāṇam
ids: gita-04-19-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna-agni-dagdha-karman
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: His actions are burned, and the fire is knowledge. dagdha is the past participle of dah. It agrees with tam. The line writes jñānāgnidagdhakarmāṇaṃ.
meaning: one whose actions are burned by the fire of knowledge
