## cet

judgment: obvious

## asi
ids: gita-04-36-w3
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is the unstated you.
meaning: you are

## pāpebhyaḥ
ids: gita-04-36-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pāpa
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: plural
note: The ending -ebhyaḥ is masculine or neuter. It names the people the superlative is measured against, so it is masculine.
meaning: than those who do wrong

## sarvebhyaḥ
ids: gita-04-36-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: plural
note: It agrees with pāpebhyaḥ. The shared ending is ablative.
meaning: than all of them

## pāpa-kṛt-tamaḥ
ids: gita-04-36-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: pāpa-kṛt-tama
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Superlative, with -tama, of pāpa-kṛt, doer of wrong. It is the predicate of asi; the you is only in the verb.
meaning: the one who has done the most wrong

## sarvam
ids: gita-04-36-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It agrees with vṛjinam, the thing crossed, so it is accusative.
meaning: the whole

## jñāna-plavena
ids: gita-04-36-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna-plava
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: jñā
compound: karmadhāraya
note: jñāna is from jñā. Knowledge itself is the raft. Joined with eva as jñānaplavenaiva; the verse keeps the ai on this word.
meaning: by the raft of knowledge

## eva
ids: gita-04-36-w9

judgment: obvious
meaning: alone

## vṛjinam
ids: gita-04-36-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: vṛjina
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what you will cross, so it is accusative.
