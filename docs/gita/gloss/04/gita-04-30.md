# gita-04-30

## apare
ids: gita-04-30-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: apara
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: It stands alone as a noun. apare is locative singular or the pronominal nominative plural. It names the people who offer, so it is nominative plural.
meaning: others

## niyata-āhārāḥ
ids: gita-04-30-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: niyata-āhāra
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Those whose food is limited. niyata is the past participle of yam with the prefix ni. Written niyatāhārāḥ, where the a of niyata and the ā of āhāra join as ā. It agrees with apare.
meaning: who keep their food limited

## sarve
ids: gita-04-30-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Written sarve before the avagraha in sarve'pyete. The avagraha stands for the dropped a of api.
meaning: all

## api
ids: gita-04-30-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The a drops after sarve and is marked by the avagraha. The i becomes y before ete, so the print runs sarve'pyete.
meaning: indeed

## ete
ids: gita-04-30-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The e is written with the y of api. The slice that remains is te.
meaning: these

## yajña-vidaḥ
ids: gita-04-30-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yajña-vid
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: vid
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Agent noun from vid, one who knows the sacrifice. Written vido before yajña; the visarga becomes o.

## yajña-kṣapita-kalmaṣāḥ
ids: gita-04-30-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yajña-kṣapita-kalmaṣa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Those whose stain has been destroyed by the sacrifices. It agrees with ete.
meaning: their stain destroyed by the sacrifices just named
