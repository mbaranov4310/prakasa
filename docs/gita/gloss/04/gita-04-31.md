# gita-04-31

## yajña-śiṣṭa-amṛta-bhujaḥ
ids: gita-04-31-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yajña-śiṣṭa-amṛta-bhuj
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: bhuj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Those who eat the remainder of the sacrifice, and that remainder is the deathless food. bhuj is the agent noun. Written yajñaśiṣṭāmṛtabhujo: the a of śiṣṭa and the a of amṛta join as ā, and the visarga becomes o before yānti.
meaning: who eat the remainder of the sacrifice, called the deathless food

## yānti
ids: gita-04-31-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: yā
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
meaning: go (in time, if they seek release)

## brahma
ids: gita-04-31-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter brahma is nominative or accusative. It is where they go, so it is accusative.
meaning: to Brahman

## sanātanam
ids: gita-04-31-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sanātana
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It agrees with brahma, so it is accusative.
meaning: the eternal

## na
ids: gita-04-31-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Joined with ayam as nāyam. The two short a vowels combine as ā.
meaning: not

## ayam
ids: gita-04-31-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The initial a is lost in nāyam. What remains is printed yaṃ, with anusvāra.
meaning: this

## lokaḥ
ids: gita-04-31-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: loka
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written loko before the avagraha in loko'styayajñasya. The visarga becomes o, and the a of asti drops.
meaning: world

## asti
ids: gita-04-31-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The a is dropped after loko and marked by the avagraha. The i becomes y before ayajñasya, so the print runs loko'styayajñasya.
meaning: is

## ayajñasya
ids: gita-04-31-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: a-yajña
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: It stands alone as a noun, one for whom there is no sacrifice. The initial a is taken into the ya of loko'styayajñasya. The slice that remains is yajñasya.
meaning: for one who offers none of these

## kutaḥ
ids: gita-04-31-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Ablative of kim, used as an adverb. Written kuto before the avagraha in kuto'nyaḥ. The visarga becomes o, and the a of anyaḥ drops.
meaning: how

## anyaḥ
ids: gita-04-31-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: anya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It stands alone as a noun, the other end. The initial a is dropped after kuto and marked by the avagraha.
meaning: could another, higher, end

## kuru-sattama
ids: gita-04-31-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kuru-sattama
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
