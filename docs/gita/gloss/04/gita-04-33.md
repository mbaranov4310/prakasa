# gita-04-33

## śreyān
ids: gita-04-33-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śreyas
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Comparative of praśasya. It agrees with jñāna-yajñaḥ.
meaning: better (the material sacrifice starts a fruit; this one does not)

## dravya-mayāt
ids: gita-04-33-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: dravya-maya
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Made of material things. It agrees with yajñāt. Written dravyamayād before yajñāt; t becomes d before the voiced y.
meaning: than one made of material things

## yajñāt
ids: gita-04-33-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yajña
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: singular
root: yaj
note: Written yajñāj before jñāna. t becomes j before the palatal.
meaning: than the sacrifice

## jñāna-yajñaḥ
ids: gita-04-33-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna-yajña
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yaj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The sacrifice of knowledge. jñāna is from jñā, and yajña from yaj.
meaning: the sacrifice of knowledge

## paraṃtapa
ids: gita-04-33-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: paraṃtapa
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
root: tap
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Vocative. Scorcher of the foe. tapa is the agent noun from tap.
meaning: O scorcher of the foe

## sarvam
ids: gita-04-33-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Printed with anusvāra as sarvaṃ. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It agrees with karma, so it is nominative.
meaning: all

## karma
ids: gita-04-33-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: The neuter karma is nominative or accusative. It is what comes to rest, so with the passive verb it is nominative. The a joins the a of akhilam as ā, and the print is karmā.
meaning: action

## akhilam
ids: gita-04-33-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: akhila
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: With no gap left, so the whole of it. The initial a is lost in karmākhilaṃ, and the end is printed with anusvāra. It agrees with karma, so the neuter -am is nominative.
meaning: the whole of it, with nothing left out
