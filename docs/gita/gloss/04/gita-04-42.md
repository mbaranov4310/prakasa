# gita-04-42

## tasmāt
ids: gita-04-42-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: Used as therefore. The ablative is masculine or neuter; as therefore it is neuter. Written tasmād before ajñāna; t becomes d, and the a of ajñāna is the vowel of that d.
meaning: therefore

## ajñāna-saṃbhūtam
ids: gita-04-42-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ajñāna-saṃbhūta
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: bhū
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: saṃbhūta is the past participle of bhū with sam, born from ignorance. a- negatives jñāna, from jñā. It agrees with saṃśayam. The initial a is the vowel of d in tasmād.
meaning: born of ignorance (failure to discriminate)

## hṛt-stham
ids: gita-04-42-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: hṛt-stha
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: sthā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: stha, from sthā, means seated in the heart. It agrees with saṃśayam, so the masculine -am is accusative.
meaning: seated in the heart

## jñāna-asinā
ids: gita-04-42-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna-asi
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: jñā
compound: karmadhāraya
note: jñāna, from jñā, is itself the sword. Joined with ātmanaḥ as jñānāsinātmanaḥ; ā and ā merge, the verse writes no avagraha, and this slice keeps the ā.
meaning: with the sword of knowledge

## ātmanaḥ
ids: gita-04-42-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: The ending -manaḥ is genitive or ablative. It is one's own, with the sword of knowledge, so it is genitive. The initial ā is lost in that join, with no avagraha; the slice is tmanaḥ.
meaning: one's own (the doubt is about oneself)

## chittvā
ids: gita-04-42-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: chid
note: Absolutive of chid. Joined with enam as chittvainam; the verse keeps the ai on this word.
meaning: having cut

## enam
ids: gita-04-42-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with saṃśayam. The initial e is kept with chittvā.
meaning: this

## saṃśayam
ids: gita-04-42-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃśaya
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It is what he should cut. Masculine -am is accusative.
meaning: doubt

## yogam
ids: gita-04-42-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: yuj
note: From yuj. It is what he should stand in, the object of ātiṣṭha, so the masculine -am is accusative. Joined with ātiṣṭha; a and ā merge, and the verse keeps the ā on this word.
meaning: in yoga (the practice of action that leads to right vision)

## ātiṣṭha
ids: gita-04-42-w10
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: ā
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is the unstated you. The initial ā is kept with yogam. Joined with uttiṣṭha; a and u merge as o, kept on this word.
meaning: stand

## uttiṣṭha
ids: gita-04-42-w11

root: sthā

tense: command

person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is the unstated you. The initial u is that o of ātiṣṭho.
meaning: and rise (for the battle)
