# gita-04-03

## saḥ
ids: gita-04-03-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with yogaḥ and takes up the yoga of the previous verse. The visarga drops before eva.
meaning: that

## eva
ids: gita-04-03-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The final a joins the a of ayam.
meaning: very

## ayam
ids: gita-04-03-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with yogaḥ. The a is joined to eva. Written ayaṃ before mayā.
meaning: this

## mayā
ids: gita-04-03-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender.
meaning: by me

## te
ids: gita-04-03-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: dative
number: singular
note: yuṣmad has no gender. te is dative or genitive. It is the person taught, so it is dative. Before adya the following a is dropped; the avagraha marks it.
meaning: to you

## adya
ids: gita-04-03-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The initial a is dropped after te; the avagraha marks it.
meaning: now

## yogaḥ
ids: gita-04-03-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
meaning: yoga

## proktaḥ
ids: gita-04-03-w8
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: vac
prefix: pra
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Past participle of pra-vac. It is the predicate of yogaḥ.
meaning: taught

## purātanaḥ
ids: gita-04-03-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: purātana
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with yogaḥ.
meaning: ancient

## bhaktaḥ
ids: gita-04-03-w10
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: bhaj
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Past participle of bhaj. Written bhakto before asi; the a of asi is dropped. The subject you is not stated.
meaning: devoted

## asi
ids: gita-04-03-w11
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The initial a is dropped after bhaktaḥ; the avagraha marks it. The subject you is in the ending.
meaning: you are

## me
ids: gita-04-03-w12
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: dative
number: singular
note: me is dative or genitive. Śaṅkara reads bhakto 'si me, sakhā ca asi: you are devoted to me, and you are a friend. So me goes with bhaktaḥ, not with sakhā.
meaning: to me

## sakhā
ids: gita-04-03-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sakhi
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Nominative of sakhi. It is a second predicate, and asi is carried from the previous clause. The subject you is not stated.
meaning: a friend

## ca
ids: gita-04-03-w14
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written ce before iti; the a and i join as e.
meaning: and

## iti
ids: gita-04-03-w15
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The i joins the a of ca.
meaning: so

## rahasyam
ids: gita-04-03-w16
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: rahasya
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what “this” is, so it is nominative. Written rahasyaṃ before hi.
meaning: a secret

## hi
ids: gita-04-03-w17
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written hy before etat; the i is dropped.
meaning: for

## etat
ids: gita-04-03-w18
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter form is nominative or accusative. It is what the secret is said of, so it is nominative. The final d joins the u of uttamam.
meaning: this

## uttamam
ids: gita-04-03-w19
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: uttama
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with rahasyam, so the -am is nominative. The initial u is joined to the d of etat.
meaning: the highest (yoga, that is knowledge)
