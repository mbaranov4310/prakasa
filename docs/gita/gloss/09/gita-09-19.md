# gita-09-19

## tapāmi
ids: gita-09-19-w1
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: tap
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: Written tapāmy before aham. The i becomes y.
meaning: I heat

## aham
ids: gita-09-19-w2, gita-09-19-w3, gita-09-19-w16
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The initial a joins the word before it, and final m is an anusvāra before a consonant.
meaning: I

## varṣam
ids: gita-09-19-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: varṣa
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Masculine, so -am is accusative. Written varṣaṃ before nigṛhṇāmi.
meaning: the rain

## nigṛhṇāmi
ids: gita-09-19-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: grah
prefix: ni
class: krī
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: Written nigṛhṇāmy before utsṛjāmi. The i becomes y.
meaning: I hold back

## utsṛjāmi
ids: gita-09-19-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: sṛj
prefix: ut
class: tud
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: The initial u is written with nigṛhṇāmi.
meaning: I release

## ca
ids: gita-09-19-w7, gita-09-19-w9, gita-09-19-w12, gita-09-19-w15
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written cai before eva, and cā before aham.
meaning: and

## amṛtam
ids: gita-09-19-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: amṛta
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: mṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Negative of mṛta, the past participle of mṛ. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative, and it is what I am, so it is nominative.
meaning: the deathless (what belongs to the gods)

## eva
ids: gita-09-19-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written va. The e joins ca.
meaning: also

## mṛtyuḥ
ids: gita-09-19-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mṛtyu
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: mṛ
note: Written mṛtyuś before ca.
meaning: death (what belongs to mortals)

## sat
ids: gita-09-19-w13
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: as
participle: present
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It is what I am, so the neuter form is nominative. Written sad, joining the a of asat.
meaning: what is

## asat
ids: gita-09-19-w14
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: asat
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: as
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Negative of sat. It is what I am, so it is nominative, and it is written sac before ca.
meaning: what is not

## arjuna

stem: arjuna

case: vocative
number: singular
