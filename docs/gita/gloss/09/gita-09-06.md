# gita-09-06

## yathā
ids: gita-09-06-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: as

## ākāśa-sthitaḥ
ids: gita-09-06-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ākāśa-sthita
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: sthā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: sthita is the past participle of sthā, standing in the sky. The initial ā joins yathā, and the visarga becomes o before nityam.
meaning: standing in the sky

## nityam
ids: gita-09-06-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The neuter accusative singular of nitya, used as an adverb.
meaning: always

ids: gita-09-06-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: vāyu
gender: masculine

number: singular

## sarvatra-gaḥ
ids: gita-09-06-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sarvatra-ga
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: gam
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: ga, from gam, means going: one who goes everywhere. The visarga becomes o before mahān.
meaning: going everywhere

## sarvāṇi
ids: gita-09-06-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The neuter plural is nominative or accusative. It agrees with bhūtāni, so it is nominative.
meaning: all

## bhūtāni
ids: gita-09-06-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūta
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: bhū
note: The past participle of bhū stands as a noun. The neuter plural is nominative: these are what abide in me.
meaning: beings

## mat-sthāni
ids: gita-09-06-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mat-stha
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: sthā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: stha, from sthā, means standing in me, and it agrees with bhūtāni. The final i joins the i of iti, written ī.
meaning: abiding in me

## iti
ids: gita-09-06-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The initial i is joined to mat-sthāni. What remains is written tyu: i becomes y before upadhāraya.
meaning: thus

## upadhāraya
ids: gita-09-06-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dhṛ
prefix: upa
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: Causative of dhṛ. The initial u is joined to iti.
meaning: understand
