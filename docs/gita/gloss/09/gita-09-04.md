# gita-09-04

## mayā
ids: gita-09-04-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender.
meaning: by me

## tatam
ids: gita-09-04-w2
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: tan
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Past participle of tan, “stretched, pervaded.” Written tatami: the m joins the i of idam, and the neuter -am is nominative with jagat.
meaning: pervaded

## idam
ids: gita-09-04-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative; it agrees with jagat, so it is nominative. The initial i is joined to tatam.
meaning: this

## sarvam
ids: gita-09-04-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It agrees with jagat, so it is nominative.
meaning: whole

## jagat
ids: gita-09-04-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jagat
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: gam
note: The present participle of gam, “what goes,” stands as a noun. Written jagad before avyakta: t becomes d, and the following a joins it.
meaning: world

## avyakta-mūrtinā
ids: gita-09-04-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: avyakta-mūrti
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose form (mūrti) is unmanifest. Masculine instrumental, agreeing with mayā; the initial a is joined to jagat.
meaning: whose form is unmanifest (beyond the senses)

## mat-sthāni
ids: gita-09-04-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mat-stha
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: sthā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: stha, from sthā, means standing in me. It agrees with sarva-bhūtāni, so the neuter plural is nominative.
meaning: abiding in me

## sarva-bhūtāni
ids: gita-09-04-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-bhūta
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: bhū
compound: karmadhāraya
note: bhūta, the past participle of bhū, stands as a noun, “a being.” The neuter plural is nominative: these are what abide in me.
meaning: all beings

## na
ids: gita-09-04-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## ca
ids: gita-09-04-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The a of aham is joined to ca, written as ā.
meaning: and

## aham
ids: gita-09-04-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The initial a is joined to ca, and m is an anusvāra before teṣu.
meaning: I

## teṣu
ids: gita-09-04-w12
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: plural
note: Written teṣv before avasthitaḥ: u becomes v. teṣu is masculine or neuter; it takes up the beings, so it is neuter.
meaning: in them

## avasthitaḥ
ids: gita-09-04-w13
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: ava
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The initial a is joined to teṣu. Masculine nominative, said of the speaker.
meaning: situated (not in contact, as a shaped thing would be)
