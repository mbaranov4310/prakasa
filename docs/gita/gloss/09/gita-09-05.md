# gita-09-05

## na
ids: gita-09-05-w1, gita-09-05-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## ca
ids: gita-09-05-w2, gita-09-05-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## mat-sthāni
ids: gita-09-05-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mat-stha
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: sthā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: stha, from sthā, means standing in me. It agrees with bhūtāni, so the neuter plural is nominative.
meaning: abiding in me

## bhūtāni
ids: gita-09-05-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūta
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: bhū
note: The past participle of bhū stands as a noun. The neuter plural is nominative or accusative; it is the subject, so it is nominative.
meaning: beings

## paśya
ids: gita-09-05-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dṛś
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
meaning: see

## me
ids: gita-09-05-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. me is genitive or dative; it means my, so it is genitive.
meaning: my

## yogam
ids: gita-09-05-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: yuj
note: Written yogamai: the m joins the ai of aiśvaram.
meaning: yoga (how I am joined to the world)

## aiśvaram
ids: gita-09-05-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: aiśvara
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Belonging to the Lord. The initial ai is joined to yogam, and it agrees with yogam.
meaning: divine (the Lord's own)

## bhūta-bhṛt
ids: gita-09-05-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūta-bhṛt
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Agent noun from bhṛ, bearer of beings, masculine with ātmā. Written bhūta-bhṛn before na: t becomes n.
meaning: supporting beings

## bhūta-sthaḥ
ids: gita-09-05-w12
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūta-stha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: sthā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: stha, from sthā, means standing in beings. Written bhūta-stho before mama.
meaning: standing in beings

## mama
ids: gita-09-05-w13
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The final a joins the ā of ātmā, written mamā.
meaning: my

## ātmā
ids: gita-09-05-w14
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The initial ā is joined to mama.
meaning: self (said as people say it)

## bhūta-bhāvanaḥ
ids: gita-09-05-w15
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūta-bhāvana
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhū
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Causative of bhū. The agent who brings beings forth, said of the self.
meaning: who brings beings forth
