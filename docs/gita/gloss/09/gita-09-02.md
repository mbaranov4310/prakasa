# gita-09-02

## rāja-vidyā
ids: gita-09-02-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: rāja-vidyā
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: vid
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: King among knowledges. The last member vidyā keeps the predicate noun feminine.
meaning: the king of knowledges (it outshines every other)

## rāja-guhyam
ids: gita-09-02-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: rāja-guhya
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: guh
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: King among secrets. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative; it is predicated of idam, so it is nominative.
meaning: the king of secrets

## pavitram
ids: gita-09-02-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: pavitra
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: pū
note: From pū, “to purify.” Written pavitrami before idam, and the neuter -am is nominative with idam.
meaning: purifying

## idam
ids: gita-09-02-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative; it is what the other words name, so it is nominative. The initial i joins pavitram, and the m joins the u of uttamam.
meaning: this

## uttamam
ids: gita-09-02-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: uttama
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Superlative, “the highest.” The initial u is joined to idam, and the neuter -am is nominative with idam.
meaning: the highest (the finest of purifiers)

## pratyakṣa-avagamam
ids: gita-09-02-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: pratyakṣa-avagama
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose grasp (avagama) is direct (pratyakṣa). The neuter -am is nominative with idam.
meaning: grasped directly (as pleasure is)

## dharmyam
ids: gita-09-02-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: dharmya
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: In accord with dharma. The neuter -am is nominative with idam.
meaning: righteous (not opposed to dharma)

## su-sukham
ids: gita-09-02-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: su-sukha
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: su, “very,” with sukha, “easy.” The neuter -am is nominative with idam, and kartum completes it.
meaning: very easy

## kartum
ids: gita-09-02-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
note: Infinitive, “to practice.”
meaning: to practice

## avyayam
ids: gita-09-02-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: avyaya
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: A negative compound, a-vyaya, “not wasting away.” The initial a is dropped after kartum, and the neuter -am is nominative with idam.
meaning: imperishable (its fruit does not waste away)
