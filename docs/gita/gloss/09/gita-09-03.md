# gita-09-03

## aśraddadhānāḥ
ids: gita-09-03-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: aśraddadhāna
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: dhā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Negative of śraddadhāna, the middle present participle built on śraddhā. śrad in śraddhā is not a prefix.
meaning: without faith (in this knowledge and in its fruit)

## puruṣāḥ
ids: gita-09-03-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: puruṣa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The visarga drops before dharmasya.
meaning: men

## dharmasya
ids: gita-09-03-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dharma
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
root: dhṛ
note: The final a joins the a of asya, written as ā.
meaning: of the teaching

## asya
ids: gita-09-03-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asya is masculine or neuter. It agrees with dharmasya, so it is masculine. The initial a is joined to dharmasya.
meaning: this

## param-tapa
ids: gita-09-03-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: param-tapa
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
root: tap
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: param, “a foe,” with tapa, “one who scorches,” from tap. Written paraṃtapa: the m of param is an anusvāra before t.
meaning: O scorcher of foes

## aprāpya
ids: gita-09-03-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: āp
prefix: pra
note: Negative absolutive of pra-āp, “not reaching.” The opening a is the negative.
meaning: not reaching

## mām
ids: gita-09-03-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender.
meaning: me (the supreme Lord)

## nivartante
ids: gita-09-03-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vṛt
prefix: ni
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: plural

## mṛtyu-saṃsāra-vartmani
ids: gita-09-03-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mṛtyu-saṃsāra-vartman
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
root: vṛt
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The path (vartman) of the world that is joined with death.
meaning: on the path of the world that ends in death
