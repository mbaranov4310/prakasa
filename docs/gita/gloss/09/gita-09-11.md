# gita-09-11

## avajānanti
ids: gita-09-11-w1
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: jñā
prefix: ava
class: krī
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: ava with jñā means to think little of someone.
meaning: they scorn

## mām
ids: gita-09-11-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māṃ before mūḍhāḥ.
meaning: me

## mūḍhāḥ
ids: gita-09-11-w3
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: muh
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: It stands as a noun, the people who are deluded. Written mūḍhā before mānuṣīm.
meaning: the deluded (who do not discriminate)

## mānuṣīm
ids: gita-09-11-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mānuṣa
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Feminine to agree with tanum.
meaning: human

## tanum
ids: gita-09-11-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: tanu
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: tan
note: From the root tan. The ā of āśritam is written with this word.
meaning: a body

## āśritam
ids: gita-09-11-w6
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: śri
prefix: ā
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with mām, in the active sense of having taken something on. The ā is written with tanum.
meaning: having taken on (moving about in a human body)

## param
ids: gita-09-11-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: para
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with bhāvam.
meaning: supreme

## bhāvam
ids: gita-09-11-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhāva
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: bhū
note: From the root bhū. The a of ajānantaḥ is written with this word.
meaning: state (the truth of the self, finer than space)

## ajānantaḥ
ids: gita-09-11-w9
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: jñā
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Negative of jānantaḥ, agreeing with mūḍhāḥ. The a is written with bhāvam, and the word is ajānanto before mama.
meaning: not knowing

## mama
ids: gita-09-11-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender.
meaning: my

## bhūta-mahā-īśvaram
ids: gita-09-11-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūta-mahā-īśvara
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: īś
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The great (mahā) Lord (īśvara) of beings (bhūta).
meaning: the great Lord of beings (their own self)
