# gita-09-20

## traividyāḥ
ids: gita-09-20-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: traividya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Knowers of the three Vedas. The visarga is dropped before mām.
meaning: knowers of the three Vedas

## mām
ids: gita-09-20-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māṃ before somapāḥ.
meaning: me

## somapāḥ
ids: gita-09-20-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: somapa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: pā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Agent noun from pā, one who drinks soma.
meaning: soma-drinkers

## pūta-pāpāḥ
ids: gita-09-20-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: pūta-pāpa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Those whose sin (pāpa) is cleansed (pūta, from pū). The visarga is dropped at the line break.
meaning: whose sins are cleansed

## yajñaiḥ
ids: gita-09-20-w5

number: plural
root: yaj

## iṣṭvā
ids: gita-09-20-w6

judgment: obvious
root: yaj
note: Absolutive of yaj. The initial i is written with yajñaiḥ.

number: singular

## te

kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural

## puṇyam
ids: gita-09-20-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: puṇya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what they reach, so it is accusative, and the m joins the ā of āsādya.
meaning: the fruit of merit

## āsādya
