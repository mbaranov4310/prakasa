## sudurācāraḥ
ids: gita-09-30-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: su-dur-ācāra
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose conduct (ācāra) is very (su) bad (dur). It stands as the subject. Written sudurācāro before bhajate.
meaning: one of very vile conduct

person: 3rd
number: singular

## mām
ids: gita-09-30-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māma; the a of ananya joins the final m.

## ananya-bhāk
ids: gita-09-30-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ananya-bhāj
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhaj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: bhāj, from bhaj, is one who is devoted; ananya is no other. The nominative is bhāk, and its initial a is written with mām.
meaning: devoted to no other
