# gita-09-12

## mogha-āśāḥ
ids: gita-09-12-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mogha-āśa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Those whose hope (āśā) is vain (mogha). The bahuvrīhi stands as a noun, and it is written mogha-āśā before the next word.
meaning: whose hopes are vain

## mogha-karmāṇaḥ
ids: gita-09-12-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mogha-karman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Those whose work (karman) is fruitless. The bahuvrīhi stands as a noun, and it is written mogha-karmāṇo before the next word.
meaning: whose works are fruitless

## mogha-jñānāḥ
ids: gita-09-12-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mogha-jñāna
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Those whose knowledge (jñāna) is fruitless. The bahuvrīhi stands as a noun, and it is written mogha-jñānā before vicetasaḥ.
meaning: whose knowledge is fruitless

## vicetasaḥ
ids: gita-09-12-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: vicetas
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: A bahuvrīhi standing as a noun: those whose discrimination (cetas) is gone (vi). The ending -asaḥ is nominative plural or genitive singular; it stands with the other nominatives, so it is nominative plural.
meaning: whose discrimination is gone
