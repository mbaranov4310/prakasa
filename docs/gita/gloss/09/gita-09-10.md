# gita-09-10

## mayā
ids: gita-09-10-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The a of adhyakṣeṇa is written with this word.
meaning: by me

## adhyakṣeṇa
ids: gita-09-10-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: adhyakṣa
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: One who watches over (adhi and akṣa). The instrumental -eṇa is masculine or neuter; it agrees with the speaker, so it is masculine.
meaning: as the witness (mere seeing, itself unchanged)

## prakṛtiḥ
ids: gita-09-10-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prakṛti
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: From pra and the root kṛ.
meaning: nature (the māyā of the three qualities)

## sūyate
ids: gita-09-10-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: sū
class: div
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The present stem in -ya- is active here: nature brings the world forth.
meaning: brings forth

## sa-cara-acaram
ids: gita-09-10-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sa-cara-acara
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: With what moves (cara) and what is still (acara), standing as a noun. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative; it is what nature brings forth, so it is accusative.
meaning: what moves and what is still

## hetunā
ids: gita-09-10-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: hetu
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: hi
note: From the root hi. The a of anena is written with this word.
meaning: by the cause

## anena
ids: gita-09-10-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: The form anena is masculine or neuter. It agrees with hetunā, so it is masculine.
meaning: this (my being only the witness)

## kaunteya
ids: gita-09-10-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kaunteya
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
note: The vocative has no link.
meaning: O son of Kuntī

## jagat
ids: gita-09-10-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jagat
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: gam
note: From gam, what moves. The form jagat is nominative or accusative; it is what turns, so it is nominative, and it is written jagad before the verb.
meaning: the world

## viparivartate
ids: gita-09-10-w10
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vṛt
prefix: vi-pari
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
meaning: turns through its changes
