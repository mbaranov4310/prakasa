# gita-09-13

## mahātmānaḥ
ids: gita-09-13-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mahātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Those whose self (ātman) is great (mahā). The bahuvrīhi stands as a noun, and it is written mahātmānas before tu.
meaning: the great-souled

## tu
ids: gita-09-13-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: but

## mām
ids: gita-09-13-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māṃ before pārtha.
meaning: me

## pārtha
ids: gita-09-13-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pārtha
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
note: The vocative has no link.
meaning: O Pārtha

## daivīm
ids: gita-09-13-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: daiva
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Feminine to agree with prakṛtim. Written daivīṃ before prakṛtim.
meaning: divine (calm, restraint, kindness, and faith)

## prakṛtim
ids: gita-09-13-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prakṛti
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: From pra and the root kṛ. The ā of āśritāḥ is written with this word.
meaning: a nature

## āśritāḥ
ids: gita-09-13-w7
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: śri
prefix: ā
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Active in sense, it agrees with mahātmānaḥ. The ā is written with prakṛtim.
meaning: having resorted to

## bhajanti
ids: gita-09-13-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhaj
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: The i becomes y before na-anya-manasaḥ, and that word's first a is lost in the join.
meaning: they worship

## na-anya-manasaḥ
ids: gita-09-13-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: na-anya-manas
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose mind (manas) is on no other (na anya). The ending -asaḥ is nominative plural or genitive singular; it agrees with mahātmānaḥ, so it is nominative plural.
meaning: with minds on no other

## jñātvā
ids: gita-09-13-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: jñā
note: Absolutive of jñā.
meaning: having known

## bhūta-ādim
ids: gita-09-13-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūta-ādi
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The source (ādi) of beings (bhūta). The a of avyayam is written with this word.
meaning: the source of beings

## avyayam
ids: gita-09-13-w12
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: avyaya
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with bhūta-ādim. The a is written with that word.
meaning: imperishable
