## na

kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## ca
ids: gita-09-09-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## mām
ids: gita-09-09-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māṃ before tāni.
meaning: me

## tāni
ids: gita-09-09-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The neuter plural tāni is nominative or accusative. It agrees with karmāṇi, so it is nominative.
meaning: those

## karmāṇi
ids: gita-09-09-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: kṛ
note: From the root kṛ. The neuter plural -āni is nominative or accusative; the actions are what bind, so it is nominative.
meaning: actions (that make the creation uneven)

## nibadhnanti
ids: gita-09-09-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bandh
prefix: ni
class: krī
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
meaning: bind

## dhanaṃjaya
ids: gita-09-09-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dhanaṃjaya
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
root: ji
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Winner (jaya) of wealth (dhana). The vocative has no link.
meaning: O winner of wealth

## udāsīna-vat
ids: gita-09-09-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The suffix -vat means like, and the word does not decline. Written udāsīnavad before āsīnam, whose ā is kept here.
meaning: like one indifferent

## āsīnam
ids: gita-09-09-w9
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: ās
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The participle has an active sense, seated, and it agrees with mām. The ā is written with udāsīna-vat.
meaning: seated

## asaktam
ids: gita-09-09-w10
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sañj
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Negative of sakta, agreeing with mām. The a is written with āsīnam.
meaning: unattached (to the fruit, without the thought that I act)

## teṣu
ids: gita-09-09-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: plural
note: The locative plural teṣu is masculine or neuter. It agrees with karmasu, so it is neuter.
meaning: among those

## karmasu
ids: gita-09-09-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: plural
root: kṛ
note: From the root kṛ.
