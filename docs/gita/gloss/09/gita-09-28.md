# gita-09-28

## śubha-aśubha-phalaiḥ
ids: gita-09-28-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śubha-aśubha-phala
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Actions whose fruit is wanted and unwanted; the bahuvrīhi stands as a noun, and the ending -aiḥ is neuter because the actions are. Written -phalair before evam: the visarga becomes r, and the e of evam joins it.
meaning: from what has wanted and unwanted fruits

## evam
ids: gita-09-28-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e joins the preceding word, so only vam is left in the line.
meaning: thus

## mokṣyase
ids: gita-09-28-w3
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: muc
class: tud
tense: simple future
voice: passive
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is the unstated you, the one who is freed.
meaning: you will be freed

## karma-bandhanaiḥ
ids: gita-09-28-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karma-bandhana
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: plural
root: bandh
compound: karmadhāraya
note: A karmadhāraya: the actions themselves are the bonds. The ending -aiḥ is masculine or neuter, and the actions are neuter, so this is neuter.
meaning: from the bonds that actions are

## saṃnyāsa-yoga-yukta-ātmā
ids: gita-09-28-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃnyāsa-yoga-yukta-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose mind (ātman) is yoked to the yoga of renunciation, the offering of action. The nominative describes the unstated you.
meaning: whose mind is yoked to the yoga of renunciation

## vimuktaḥ
ids: gita-09-28-w6
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: muc
prefix: vi
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with the unstated you. Written vimukto before mām.

## mām

kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The u of upaiṣyasi joins it.
