# gita-09-01

## śrī-bhagavān
ids: gita-09-01-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śrī-bhagavat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: śrī, “blessed,” with bhagavat, “the Lord.” The final n joins the u of uvāca.
meaning: the blessed Lord

## uvāca
ids: gita-09-01-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
meaning: said

## idam
ids: gita-09-01-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he will declare, so it is accusative.
meaning: this (the knowledge of Brahman)

## tu
ids: gita-09-01-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: indeed (this one alone)

## te
ids: gita-09-01-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: dative
number: singular
note: yuṣmad has no gender. te is genitive or dative; Śaṅkara reads it as tubhyam, so it is dative.
meaning: to you

## guhyatamam
ids: gita-09-01-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: guhyatama
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Superlative, with -tama, of guhya. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative; it describes what he will declare, so it is accusative.
meaning: most secret

## pravakṣyāmi
ids: gita-09-01-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
prefix: pra
class: ad
tense: simple future
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: Written pravakṣyāmy before anasūyave.
meaning: I shall declare

## anasūyave
ids: gita-09-01-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: anasūyu
gender: masculine
case: dative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: A negative compound, an-asūyu, “without spite.” The initial a is joined to pravakṣyāmi and written as y.
meaning: who are free of spite

## jñānam
ids: gita-09-01-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: jñā
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It names what he will declare, so it is accusative.
meaning: knowledge

## vijñāna-sahitam
ids: gita-09-01-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vijñāna-sahita
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: sah
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: sahita is the past participle of sah, here “accompanied by.” It agrees with jñānam, so the neuter -am is accusative.
meaning: joined with realization

## yat
ids: gita-09-01-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written yaj before jñātvā. The neuter yat is nominative or accusative; it is what one knows, so it is accusative.
meaning: which

## jñātvā
ids: gita-09-01-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: jñā
note: Absolutive, “having known.”
meaning: having known (having reached it)

## mokṣyase
ids: gita-09-01-w13
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: muc
class: tud
tense: simple future
voice: ātmanepada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: Middle future of muc. The sense is that you yourself are set free.
meaning: you will be released

## aśubhāt
ids: gita-09-01-w14
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: aśubha
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: A negative compound, the adjective standing as a noun, “the impure.” The initial a is dropped after mokṣyase.
meaning: from the impure (the bondage of the world)
