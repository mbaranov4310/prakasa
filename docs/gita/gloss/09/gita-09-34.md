# gita-09-34

## mat-manāḥ
ids: gita-09-34-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mat-manas
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose mind (manas) is on me (mat), predicate of bhava, whose subject is the unstated you. Written manmanā before bhava.
meaning: with your mind on me

## bhava
ids: gita-09-34-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is the unstated you.
meaning: be

## mat-bhaktaḥ
ids: gita-09-34-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mat-bhakta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhaj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Devotee of me, a further predicate of bhava, whose subject is the unstated you. Written madbhakto: mat becomes mad before bh, and the visarga becomes o before madyājī.
meaning: my devotee

## mat-yājī
ids: gita-09-34-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mat-yājin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: One who worships me (yājin, from yaj), a further predicate of bhava, whose subject is the unstated you. Written madyājī; mat becomes mad before y.
meaning: one who worships me

## mām
ids: gita-09-34-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender.
meaning: me

## namaskuru
ids: gita-09-34-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
prefix: namas
class: tan
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: namas with the command of kṛ: do obeisance. The subject is the unstated you.
meaning: bow

## mām
ids: gita-09-34-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māme; the e of eva joins the final m.
meaning: to me

## eva
ids: gita-09-34-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It stresses that mām: to me alone. Written vai; the a of eva joins the e of eṣyasi as ai.
meaning: alone

## eṣyasi
ids: gita-09-34-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: i
class: ad
tense: simple future
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: Written ṣyasi; its e joins eva. The subject is the unstated you.
meaning: you will come

## yuktvā
ids: gita-09-34-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: yuj
note: Absolutive of yuj: having yoked. Written yuktvai; ā and the e of evam join as ai.
meaning: having yoked

## evam
ids: gita-09-34-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written vamā; its e joins yuktvā, and the ā of ātmānam joins the final m.
meaning: thus

## ātmānam
ids: gita-09-34-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The self here is me, the self of all beings. Written tmānam; its ā joins evam.
meaning: the self (me, the self of all beings)

## mat-parāyaṇaḥ
ids: gita-09-34-w13
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mat-parāyaṇa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: One whose highest resort (parāyaṇa) is me. It agrees with the unstated you of eṣyasi.
meaning: holding me as the highest resort
