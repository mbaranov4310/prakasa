# gita-09-07

## sarva-bhūtāni
ids: gita-09-07-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-bhūta
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: bhū
compound: karmadhāraya
note: bhūta, the past participle of bhū, stands as a noun, a being. The neuter plural is nominative or accusative; these are what enter, so it is nominative.
meaning: all beings
