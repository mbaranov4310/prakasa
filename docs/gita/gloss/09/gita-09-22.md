# gita-09-22

## ananyāḥ
ids: gita-09-22-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ananya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: an with anya, “not other.” Written ananyāś before cintayantaḥ.
meaning: not other (having reached Nārāyaṇa as the self)

## cintayantaḥ
ids: gita-09-22-w2
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: cint
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Present participle of cint, a tenth-class root, so the stem is cintaya. Written cintayanto before mām.
meaning: thinking

## yoga-kṣemam
ids: gita-09-22-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga-kṣema
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṣi
compound: dvandva
note: A dvandva: yoga is the gaining of what is not yet gained, and kṣema is its keeping. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative, and since it is what I carry it is accusative.
meaning: the gaining of what is not yet gained, and its keeping
