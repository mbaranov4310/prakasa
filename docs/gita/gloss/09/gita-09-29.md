# gita-09-29

## samaḥ
ids: gita-09-29-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sama
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written samo before aham, and the a of aham is dropped. It agrees with aham.
meaning: the same

## aham
ids: gita-09-29-w2, gita-09-29-w20
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: nominative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The second is written ham after api.
meaning: I

## sarva-bhūteṣu
ids: gita-09-29-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-bhūta
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: plural
root: bhū
compound: karmadhāraya
note: bhūta is the past participle of bhū, used as the noun “being.” sarva describes it: all beings.
meaning: in all beings

## me
ids: gita-09-29-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: dative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The enclitic me is genitive or dative. It is the person for whom no one is hateful or dear, so it is dative.
meaning: for me
