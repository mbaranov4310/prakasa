# gita-09-27

## yat
ids: gita-09-27-w1, gita-09-27-w3, gita-09-27-w5, gita-09-27-w8, gita-09-27-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter yat is nominative or accusative, and it is what you do, eat, offer, give, and practice, so it is accusative. Before aśnāsi it is written yad, and before juhoṣi the t becomes j.
meaning: whatever
