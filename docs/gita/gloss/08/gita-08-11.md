# gita-08-11

## yat
ids: gita-08-11-w1, gita-08-11-w6, gita-08-11-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Before a vowel the t is written d: yad before akṣaram and before yatayaḥ, yadi before icchantaḥ.
meaning: which

## akṣaram
ids: gita-08-11-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: akṣara
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what the knowers declare, so it is accusative. The a is written at the end of yat.
meaning: the Imperishable (what does not perish)

## veda-vidaḥ
ids: gita-08-11-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: veda-vid
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: vid
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Knowers (vid) of the Veda. The ending -aḥ is several cases. They are the ones who declare, so it is nominative plural. Written vido before vadanti.
meaning: knowers of the Veda

## yatayaḥ
ids: gita-08-11-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yati
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: yat
note: From yat, one who strives. Written yatayo before vīta-rāgāḥ.

## vīta-rāgāḥ
ids: gita-08-11-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vīta-rāga
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Those from whom passion (rāga) has gone (vīta).
meaning: from whom passion has gone

## icchantaḥ
ids: gita-08-11-w10
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: iṣ
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: iṣ is 6th class. It agrees with the they of caranti, who are not named in this line. The i is written with yat, and the visarga becomes o before brahma-caryam.
meaning: desiring (to know it)

## brahma-caryam
ids: gita-08-11-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahma-carya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: car
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: carya is the practice, from car. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what they practice, so it is accusative.
meaning: the student's discipline (under a teacher)
