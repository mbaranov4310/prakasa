# gita-08-08

## abhyāsa-yoga-yuktena
ids: gita-08-08-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: abhyāsa-yoga-yukta
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: yuj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: yukta is the past participle of yuj. The repeated practice is itself the yoga, and the mind is joined to it. It agrees with cetasā, so it is neuter.
meaning: joined to the yoga of practice
