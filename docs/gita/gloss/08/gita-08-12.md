# gita-08-12

## sarva-dvārāṇi
ids: gita-08-12-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-dvāra
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
compound: karmadhāraya
note: All the gates. The neuter plural -āṇi is nominative or accusative. They are what he restrains, so the case is accusative.
