# gita-08-06

## yam
ids: gita-08-06-w1, gita-08-06-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with bhāvam. The second repeats the first: whichever being.
meaning: whatever

## vā
ids: gita-08-06-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: or

## api
ids: gita-08-06-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: even

## smaran
ids: gita-08-06-w5
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: smṛ
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with the one who leaves, who is not named in the verse.
meaning: remembering

## bhāvam
ids: gita-08-06-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhāva
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: bhū
note: bhāva is masculine. The -am is accusative. It is what he remembers, a particular god.
meaning: a being (a particular god)

## tyajati
ids: gita-08-06-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: tyaj
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The subject is the one who leaves, shown by the person, and not named in the verse. The i becomes y, and the a of ante is dropped.
meaning: one leaves

## ante
ids: gita-08-06-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: anta
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: The initial a is dropped after tyajati. At death, when the breath leaves.
meaning: at the end (at death)

## kalevaram
ids: gita-08-06-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kalevara
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he leaves, so it is accusative.
meaning: the body

## tam
ids: gita-08-06-w10, gita-08-06-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It answers yam. The second repeats the first. On the second, the e of eva joins tam in the written line.
meaning: that

## eva
ids: gita-08-06-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The a of eva joins the e of eti.
meaning: very

## eti
ids: gita-08-06-w13
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: i
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The subject is the one who leaves, shown by the person, and not named in the verse. The initial e joins eva. He goes to that being and to no other.
meaning: he goes to

## tat-bhāva-bhāvitaḥ
ids: gita-08-06-w16
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: tat-bhāva-bhāvita
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhū
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Causative of bhū. bhāvita is the past participle: shaped by that (tat) state (bhāva), by dwelling on it. It describes the one who goes, who is not named in the verse.
meaning: shaped by that being (by dwelling on it)
