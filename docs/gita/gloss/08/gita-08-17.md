# gita-08-17

## sahasra-yuga-paryantam
ids: gita-08-17-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sahasra-yuga-paryanta
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose end (paryanta) is a thousand yugas. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It describes ahaḥ, which is what they know, so it is accusative.
meaning: ending at a thousand yugas

## ahaḥ
ids: gita-08-17-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ahan
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter ahaḥ is nominative or accusative. It is what they know, so it is accusative. Written ahar before yat: the a joins paryantam, and the visarga becomes r.
meaning: the day

## yat
ids: gita-08-17-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with ahaḥ. Written yad before brahmaṇaḥ.
meaning: which
