# gita-08-14

## na-anya-cetāḥ
ids: gita-08-14-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: na-anya-cetas
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose mind (cetas) is on no other object. It agrees with yaḥ.
meaning: whose mind is on no other object

## satatam
ids: gita-08-14-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The neuter accusative singular of satata, used as an adverb: without a break.
meaning: unceasingly (without a break)

number: singular

## mām

number: singular

## nityaśaḥ
ids: gita-08-14-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: An adverb in -śas. satatam is without a break, and nityaśaḥ is for the whole of life.
meaning: through life (as long as one lives)
