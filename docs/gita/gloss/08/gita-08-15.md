# gita-08-15

## mām
ids: gita-08-15-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māmu before upetya.
meaning: me

## upetya
ids: gita-08-15-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: i
prefix: upa
note: Absolutive of i, “having reached.” The initial u joins mām.
meaning: having reached (my own state)

## punarjanma
ids: gita-08-15-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: punar-janman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: jan
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Birth (janman) again (punar). The neuter ending is nominative or accusative. It is what they do not attain, so it is accusative.
meaning: rebirth

## duḥkha-ālayam
ids: gita-08-15-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: duḥkha-ālaya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: lī
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: An abode (ālaya) of sorrows (duḥkha). ālaya is from lī with ā, the place where they settle. It agrees with punarjanma. Written duḥkhālayama before a-śāśvatam, and that a is dropped.
meaning: an abode of sorrows

## a-śāśvatam
ids: gita-08-15-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: a-śāśvata
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: a- negates śāśvata, lasting. It agrees with punarjanma.
meaning: unstable

## na
ids: gita-08-15-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written nā before āpnuvanti.
meaning: not

## āpnuvanti
ids: gita-08-15-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: āp
class: su
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: The initial ā joins na.
meaning: they attain

## mahātmānaḥ
ids: gita-08-15-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mahā-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose self (ātman) is great (mahā). The adjective stands as a noun.
meaning: the great-souled (those who strive)

## saṃsiddhim
ids: gita-08-15-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃsiddhi
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: sidh
note: siddhi is from sidh, with the prefix sam. Written saṃsiddhiṃ before paramām.
meaning: perfection (release)

## paramām
ids: gita-08-15-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: parama
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with saṃsiddhim.
meaning: highest

## gatāḥ
ids: gita-08-15-w11
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: gam
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: It agrees with mahātmānaḥ.
meaning: having reached
