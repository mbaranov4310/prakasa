# gita-08-03

## śrī-bhagavān
ids: gita-08-03-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śrī-bhagavat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: śrī, blessed, and bhagavat, the Lord. Written śrībhagavānu: the final n joins the u of uvāca.
meaning: the blessed Lord

## uvāca
ids: gita-08-03-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
meaning: said

## akṣaram
ids: gita-08-03-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: akṣara
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is the subject, so it is nominative. What does not perish: the supreme Self.
meaning: the Imperishable (the supreme Self)

## brahma
ids: gita-08-03-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahman
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The nominative and accusative singular are both brahma. It is the predicate, so it is nominative.
meaning: Brahman

## paramam
ids: gita-08-03-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: parama
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It describes brahma, so it is nominative.
meaning: supreme (with nothing beyond it)

## svabhāvaḥ
ids: gita-08-03-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: svabhāva
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhū
compound: karmadhāraya
note: sva and bhāva: one's own being, the supreme Brahman as the inner self in the body. Written svabhāvo before adhyātmam, and that a is dropped.
meaning: one's own being (the inner self in the body)

## adhyātmam
ids: gita-08-03-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: adhyātma
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: adhi and ātman: what concerns the self. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It keeps its own gender as the name, so it is nominative. The initial a is dropped after svabhāvaḥ, and the u of ucyate joins the written line.
meaning: what concerns the self

## ucyate
ids: gita-08-03-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The initial u joins adhyātmam.
meaning: is called

## bhūta-bhāva-udbhava-karaḥ
ids: gita-08-03-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūta-bhāva-udbhava-kara
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: What makes (kara) the arising (udbhava) of the being (bhāva) of beings (bhūta). The visarga is written o before visargaḥ.
meaning: what brings beings to birth

## visargaḥ

kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: visarga
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: sṛj
note: The giving up of the offering to the gods. From vi and sṛj.
meaning: the offering (of sacrificial materials)

## karma-saṃjñitaḥ
