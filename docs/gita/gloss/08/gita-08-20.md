# gita-08-20

## paraḥ
ids: gita-08-20-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: para
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Other than, distinct. Written paras before tasmāt.
meaning: other

## tasmāt
ids: gita-08-20-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: The ablative is masculine or neuter. It points to the unmanifest of the previous verses, which is neuter, so it is neuter.
meaning: than that

## tu
ids: gita-08-20-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It marks the contrast with that unmanifest.
meaning: but

ids: gita-08-20-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhāva
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhū

## anyaḥ
ids: gita-08-20-w5
kind: adjective

stem: anya

number: singular

## avyaktaḥ
ids: gita-08-20-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: avyakta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: añj
note: a- negates vyakta, the past participle of añj with vi. Masculine, agreeing with bhāvaḥ. Written avyakto before avyaktāt.
meaning: unmanifest (beyond the senses)

## avyaktāt
ids: gita-08-20-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: avyakta
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
root: añj
note: a- negates vyakta, from añj with vi. The unmanifest that is the seed of the mass of beings. The ablative -āt is masculine or neuter. That unmanifest is neuter, so it is neuter. The adjective stands as a noun. The initial a is dropped after avyakto.
meaning: from the unmanifest
