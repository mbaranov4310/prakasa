# gita-08-27

## na
ids: gita-08-27-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written nai before ete.
meaning: not

## ete
ids: gita-08-27-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: dual
note: The e joins na. The dual ete is nominative or accusative, masculine or feminine. It is what he knows, the two paths, so it is feminine accusative.
meaning: these

ids: gita-08-27-w3

number: dual

meaning: the two paths
