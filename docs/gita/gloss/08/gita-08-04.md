# gita-08-04

## adhibhūtam
ids: gita-08-04-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: adhibhūta
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: adhi and bhūta: what concerns beings. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is the term being defined, so it is nominative.
meaning: what concerns beings

number: singular
