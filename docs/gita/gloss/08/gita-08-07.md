# gita-08-07

## tasmāt
ids: gita-08-07-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: It refers to the reason just given, so it is neuter. Ablative of tad: from that, therefore.
meaning: therefore

## sarveṣu
ids: gita-08-07-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: plural
note: The locative plural is masculine or neuter. It agrees with kāleṣu, so it is masculine.
meaning: at all

## kāleṣu
ids: gita-08-07-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kāla
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: plural
meaning: times

## mām
ids: gita-08-07-w4, gita-08-07-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. On the first, the a of anusmara is dropped. The second is written māme: the e of eva joins mām.
meaning: me

## anusmara
ids: gita-08-07-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: smṛ
prefix: anu
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is you, shown by the person, and not named in the verse. Remember as the teaching has it. The initial a is dropped after mām.
meaning: remember (as scripture teaches)

## yudhya
ids: gita-08-07-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: yudh
class: div
tense: command
voice: ātmanepada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is you, shown by the person, and not named in the verse. The root takes ātmanepada.
meaning: fight (do your own duty)

## ca
ids: gita-08-07-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## mayi
ids: gita-08-07-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: locative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The i becomes y before arpita, and that a is dropped.
meaning: to me

## arpita-manaḥ-buddhiḥ
ids: gita-08-07-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: arpita-manas-buddhi
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose mind (manas) and intellect (buddhi) are given over (arpita). The ending could follow buddhi and look feminine. It describes you, so it is masculine nominative. The visarga is written r before mām.
meaning: whose mind and intellect are given over

## eva
ids: gita-08-07-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The a of eva joins the e of eṣyasi.
meaning: alone

## eṣyasi
ids: gita-08-07-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: i
class: ad
tense: simple future
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The subject is you, shown by the person, and not named in the verse. Future of i: with mām as the goal, you will come. The initial e joins eva, and i becomes y before asaṃśayaḥ.
meaning: you will come

## asaṃśayaḥ
ids: gita-08-07-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: asaṃśaya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: a negates saṃśaya. It stands as its own clause: there is no doubt. Is is understood. The initial a is dropped after eṣyasi in the slice.
meaning: free from doubt
