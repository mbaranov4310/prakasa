# gita-08-16

## ā
ids: gita-08-16-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: With the ablative it names the near limit, and that world is included.
meaning: as far as

## brahma-bhuvanāt
ids: gita-08-16-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahma-bhuvana
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
root: bhū
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The world (bhuvana) of Brahmā. bhuvana is from bhū, where beings come to be. Written brahmabhuvanāl before lokāḥ.
meaning: from the world of Brahmā

## lokāḥ
ids: gita-08-16-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: loka
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
meaning: worlds

## punar-āvartinaḥ
ids: gita-08-16-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: punar-āvartin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: One who returns (āvartin) again (punar). āvartin is from vṛt with ā and the suffix -in. Written punarāvartino before arjuna, and that a is dropped.
meaning: bound to return

## arjuna
ids: gita-08-16-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: arjuna
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
note: The initial a is dropped after punarāvartino.
meaning: O Arjuna

## mām
ids: gita-08-16-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written māmu before upetya.
meaning: me

## upetya
ids: gita-08-16-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: i
prefix: upa
note: Absolutive of i, “having reached.” The initial u joins mām.
meaning: having reached

## tu
ids: gita-08-16-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It marks the contrast with the worlds that return.
meaning: but

## kaunteya

number: singular
meaning: O son of Kuntī

## punarjanma
ids: gita-08-16-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: punar-janman
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: jan
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Birth (janman) again (punar). The neuter ending is nominative or accusative. It is what is not found, so it is nominative.
meaning: rebirth
