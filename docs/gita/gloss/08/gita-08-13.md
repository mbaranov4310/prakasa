# gita-08-13

## om
ids: gita-08-13-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The syllable he utters. Written omi; m joins iti.
meaning: Om

## iti
ids: gita-08-13-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: iti quotes Om. Written ty before eka-akṣaram; i becomes y.
meaning: thus

## eka-akṣaram
ids: gita-08-13-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: eka-akṣara
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: It agrees with brahma, so the neuter -am is accusative: this is what he utters. The e is written with iti.
meaning: the one syllable

## brahma
ids: gita-08-13-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter ending of brahman is nominative or accusative. It is what he utters, so it is accusative.
meaning: Brahman (named by the syllable Om)
