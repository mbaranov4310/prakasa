# gita-08-18

## avyaktāt
ids: gita-08-18-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: avyakta
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
root: añj
note: a- negates vyakta, the past participle of añj with vi. The ablative -āt is masculine or neuter. It is Brahmā’s sleep, which is neuter, so it is neuter. The adjective stands as a noun. Written avyaktād before vyaktayaḥ.
meaning: from the unmanifest (his sleep)

## vyaktayaḥ
ids: gita-08-18-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: vyakti
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: añj
note: From añj with vi, a being that has become manifest.
meaning: manifest beings

## sarvāḥ
ids: gita-08-18-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Feminine, agreeing with vyaktayaḥ.
meaning: all

## prabhavanti
ids: gita-08-18-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
prefix: pra
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: Written prabhavanty before ahar-āgame, and that a is dropped.
meaning: come forth

## ahar-āgame
ids: gita-08-18-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ahar-āgama
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
root: gam
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The coming (āgama) of day (ahar). āgama is from gam with ā. Locative of time, Brahmā’s waking. The initial a is dropped after prabhavanty.
meaning: at the coming of day (his waking)

number: singular

## tatra
ids: gita-08-18-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written tatrai: the e of eva joins tatra.
meaning: there

## eva
ids: gita-08-18-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It reinforces tatra, that very place. Written vā, and the initial a of avyakta is dropped.
meaning: the very same

## avyakta-saṃjñake
ids: gita-08-18-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: avyakta-saṃjñaka
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose name (saṃjñā) is the unmanifest. The locative -e is masculine or neuter. It names that same neuter unmanifest, so it is neuter. The adjective stands as a noun.
meaning: in what is called the unmanifest
