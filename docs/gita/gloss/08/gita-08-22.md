## yasya

stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular

meaning: whose

## antaḥ-sthāni
ids: gita-08-22-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: antaḥ-stha
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: sthā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Standing (stha, from sthā) within (antaḥ). It agrees with bhūtāni. The neuter -āni is nominative or accusative. The beings are what stand within him, so it is nominative.
meaning: standing within

## bhūtāni
ids: gita-08-22-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūta
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: plural
root: bhū
note: The past participle of bhū, standing as a noun. The neuter -āni is nominative or accusative. They stand within him, so it is nominative.
meaning: beings

## sarvam

stem: sarva
