# gita-08-21

## avyaktaḥ
ids: gita-08-21-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: avyakta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: añj
note: a- negates vyakta, the past participle of añj with vi. Quoted, with akṣaraḥ, as what he is called. Masculine, the name of that being. The adjective stands as a noun. Written avyakto before akṣaraḥ, and that a is dropped.
meaning: the unmanifest

## akṣaraḥ
ids: gita-08-21-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: akṣara
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṣar
note: a- negates kṣara, from kṣar, perishing. The name beside avyaktaḥ. The initial a is dropped after avyakto. The visarga drops before iti.
meaning: the imperishable

## iti
ids: gita-08-21-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: It marks the two names. Written ity before uktaḥ.
meaning: as

## uktaḥ
ids: gita-08-21-w4
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: vac
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Past participle of vac. vac is 2nd class. The one so called is the being of the previous verse, not named in this verse. Written uktas before tam.
meaning: called

## tam
ids: gita-08-21-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: Written tama before āhuḥ. It resumes the one who is called by those names.
meaning: that

## āhuḥ
ids: gita-08-21-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: ah
class: ad
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: Perfect of ah, used in the sense of the present. The initial ā joins tam. The subject, people in general, is not named in the verse.
meaning: they call

## paramām

number: singular
