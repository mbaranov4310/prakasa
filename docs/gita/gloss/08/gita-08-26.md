# gita-08-26

## śukla-kṛṣṇe
ids: gita-08-26-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śukla-kṛṣṇa
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: dual
compound: dvandva
note: The bright one and the dark one. The ending -e is locative singular or nominative dual. It agrees with the two paths, so it is nominative dual.
meaning: the bright and the dark

## gatī
ids: gita-08-26-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: gati
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: dual
root: gam
note: The dual gatī is nominative or accusative. The paths are what are held to be eternal, so it is nominative.
meaning: the two paths

## jagataḥ
ids: gita-08-26-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jagat
gender: neuter

meaning: of the world (of those fit for knowledge and for rites)
