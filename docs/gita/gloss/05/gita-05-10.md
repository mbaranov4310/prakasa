# gita-05-10

## brahmaṇi
ids: gita-05-10-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahman
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
note: Written brahmaṇyā: i becomes y before ādhāya, and that ā stays in this slice. Śaṅkara reads brahman as the Lord.
meaning: on the Lord

## ādhāya
ids: gita-05-10-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: dhā
prefix: ā
note: Absolutive of dhā with ā. The initial ā is written with brahmaṇi, so the slice is dhāya.
meaning: having laid

## karmāṇi
ids: gita-05-10-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
root: kṛ
note: The neuter -āni is nominative or accusative. It is what he lays down and what he does, so it is accusative.
meaning: actions

## saṅgam
ids: gita-05-10-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṅga
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: sañj
note: Written saṅgaṃ before tyaktvā. The -am is nominative or accusative, and it is what he gives up, so it is accusative.
meaning: attachment

## tyaktvā
ids: gita-05-10-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: tyaj
note: Absolutive of tyaj. Śaṅkara takes the attachment as attachment to the fruit, liberation included.
meaning: having given up

## pāpena
ids: gita-05-10-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pāpa
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: pāpena is the same for masculine and neuter. The meaning is the evil itself, so it is neuter.
meaning: by evil

## padma-patram
ids: gita-05-10-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: padma-patra
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative, and it stands with saḥ in the comparison, so it is nominative. The i of iva is written in this slice.
meaning: a lotus leaf

## iva
ids: gita-05-10-w13
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The i is written with padma-patram, and a joins ambhasā, so the slice is vā.
meaning: as
