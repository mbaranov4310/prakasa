# gita-05-25

## labhante
ids: gita-05-25-w1
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: labh
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: plural
meaning: gain

## brahma-nirvāṇam
ids: gita-05-25-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahma-nirvāṇa
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: vā
prefix: nir
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what they gain, so it is accusative, and the slice keeps the ṛ of ṛṣayaḥ.
meaning: the peace of Brahman (release)

## ṛṣayaḥ
ids: gita-05-25-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ṛṣi
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: ṛṣ
note: The ṛ stands on the slice of brahma-nirvāṇam. Śaṅkara: those who see rightly and have renounced.
meaning: the seers (those who see rightly, and have renounced)

## kṣīṇa-kalmaṣāḥ
ids: gita-05-25-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kṣīṇa-kalmaṣa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose stain is worn away. kṣīṇa is the past participle of kṣi.
meaning: whose stain is worn away (pāpa gone)

## chinna-dvaidhāḥ
ids: gita-05-25-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: chinna-dvaidha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose doubt is cut. chinna is the past participle of chid, and the visarga drops before yata-ātmānaḥ.
meaning: whose doubt is cut

## yata-ātmānaḥ
ids: gita-05-25-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yata-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: ātman here is the senses, held in (yata, from yam). Śaṅkara glosses it saṃyatendriyāḥ.
meaning: whose senses are held in

## sarva-bhūta-hite
ids: gita-05-25-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-bhūta-hita
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
root: dhā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The good of every being. hita is from dhā, and Śaṅkara glosses it as what is favorable for them.
meaning: on the good of every being

## ratāḥ
ids: gita-05-25-w8
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: ram
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: It agrees with the seers. Śaṅkara adds that they do no harm.
meaning: intent (doing no harm)
