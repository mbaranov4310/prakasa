# gita-05-29

## bhoktāram
ids: gita-05-29-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhoktṛ
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: bhuj
note: Agent noun, the enjoyer. Śaṅkara: as the doer and as the deity. Written bhoktāraṃ.
meaning: the enjoyer (as the agent, and as the deity)

## yajña-tapasām
ids: gita-05-29-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yajña-tapas
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: plural
compound: dvandva
note: Of sacrifices and of austerities, from yaj and tap. The genitive plural follows tapas. Written yajñatapasāṃ.
meaning: of sacrifices and of austerities

## sarva-loka-maheśvaram
ids: gita-05-29-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-loka-maheśvara
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: īś
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The great lord of all worlds. mahā and īśvara join as maheśvara. Accusative, naming me.
meaning: the great lord of all worlds

## suhṛdam
ids: gita-05-29-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: suhṛd
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: su-hṛd, the friend who helps and looks for nothing back. Accusative, naming me. Written suhṛdaṃ.
meaning: the friend (who helps, and looks for nothing back)

## sarva-bhūtānām
ids: gita-05-29-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-bhūta
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: plural
root: bhū
compound: karmadhāraya
note: Of all beings. bhūta is the past participle of bhū. Śaṅkara: of all who breathe. Written sarvabhūtānāṃ.
meaning: of all beings

## mām
ids: gita-05-29-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Śaṅkara: Nārāyaṇa. Written māṃ.

## śāntim
ids: gita-05-29-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śānti
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: śam
note: Peace, the cessation of the whole round of rebirth. The slice keeps the ṛ of ṛcchati.
meaning: peace (the end of the whole round of rebirth)

## ṛcchati
ids: gita-05-29-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: ṛ
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The ṛ stands on the slice of śāntim. Śaṅkara glosses it prāpnoti, “reaches.”
meaning: reaches
