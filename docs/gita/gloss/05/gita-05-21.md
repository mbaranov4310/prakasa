# gita-05-21

## bāhya-sparśeṣu
ids: gita-05-21-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bāhya-sparśa
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: plural
root: spṛś
compound: karmadhāraya
note: bāhya and sparśa, from spṛś. Śaṅkara takes the contacts as sound and the other objects. sparśeṣu is masculine or neuter. He glosses sparśāḥ, so it is masculine. Written bāhya-sparśeṣv, and that v takes the a of asakta-ātmā.
meaning: amid external contacts (sound and the other objects)

## asakta-ātmā
ids: gita-05-21-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: asakta-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: He whose mind, the inner organ, is asakta, the negated past participle of sañj. No fondness for objects. The initial a joins sparśeṣu, written saktātmā.
meaning: he whose mind is unattached (no fondness for objects)

## vindati
ids: gita-05-21-w3
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vid
class: tud
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: vid in the sense he finds. Śaṅkara glosses labhate, and supplies tad as the object, the joy which is in the self. Written vindaty; i becomes y before ātmani.
meaning: finds

## ātmani
ids: gita-05-21-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: The initial ā joins vindati, written tmani.
meaning: in the self

## yat
ids: gita-05-21-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: yat is nominative or accusative. It agrees with the joy that is in the self, so it is nominative.
meaning: which

## sukham
ids: gita-05-21-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sukha
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is the joy which is in the self, so it is nominative. Śaṅkara supplies tad as what he finds, and that tad is not a word in the verse.
meaning: joy

## brahma-yoga-yukta-ātmā
ids: gita-05-21-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: brahma-yoga-yukta-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: He whose mind, the inner organ, is yukta, the past participle of yuj, to brahma-yoga. Śaṅkara takes that yoga as samādhi in Brahman, the mind collected there.
meaning: he whose mind is held in the yoga of Brahman (collected on Brahman)

## sukham
ids: gita-05-21-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sukha
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he reaches, so it is accusative. Written sukhama; the m takes the a of akṣayam.
meaning: joy

## akṣayam
ids: gita-05-21-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: a-kṣaya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṣi
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: a- and kṣaya, from kṣi, what does not waste. It agrees with the second sukham. The initial a joins that sukham, written kṣayama, and the m takes the a of aśnute.
meaning: unfailing
