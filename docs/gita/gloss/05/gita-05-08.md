# gita-05-08

## na
ids: gita-05-08-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written nai; the a of na joins the e of eva.
meaning: not

## eva
ids: gita-05-08-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e joins na, so the slice keeps only v.
meaning: at all

## kiṃcit
ids: gita-05-08-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: kim
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The particle cit makes kim indefinite, and the neuter form is nominative or accusative. It is what “I” do, so it is accusative.
meaning: anything

## karomi
ids: gita-05-08-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
class: tan
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: Written karomī; the i joins the i of iti.
meaning: I do

## iti
ids: gita-05-08-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The initial i joins karomi, so the slice is ti.
meaning: that

## yuktaḥ
ids: gita-05-08-w6
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: yuj
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written yukto before manyeta. Śaṅkara reads it as collected in mind, agreeing with tattva-vit.
meaning: steadied (his mind collected)

## manyeta
ids: gita-05-08-w7

number: singular
meaning: should think

## tattva-vit
ids: gita-05-08-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: tattva-vid
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: vid
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Agent noun, “knower of the truth.” Śaṅkara reads it as one who knows the self as it really is.
meaning: who knows the truth (the real nature of the self)
