# gita-05-14

## na
ids: gita-05-14-w1, gita-05-14-w3, gita-05-14-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## kartṛtvam
ids: gita-05-14-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kartṛtva
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṛ
note: Written kartṛtvaṃ before na. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative, and it is what he does not create, so it is accusative.
meaning: agency

## karmāṇi
ids: gita-05-14-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
root: kṛ
note: The neuter -āni is nominative or accusative, and it is what he does not create, so it is accusative. Śaṅkara takes these as the things produced, a chariot, a pot, a palace, and the like.
meaning: things produced (a chariot, a pot, a palace)

## lokasya
ids: gita-05-14-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: loka
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: Śaṅkara puts this with karmāṇi: the things people most want.
meaning: of people

## prabhuḥ

kind: noun

stem: prabhu
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular

## karma-phala-saṃyogam
ids: gita-05-14-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karma-phala-saṃyoga
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: yuj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Written karma-phala-saṃyogaṃ before svabhāvaḥ; saṃyoga is from sam and yuj. Śaṅkara reads it as the connection of the doer with the fruit.
meaning: the joining of action to its fruit

## svabhāvaḥ
ids: gita-05-14-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sva-bhāva
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhū
compound: karmadhāraya
note: Written svabhāvas before tu. Śaṅkara analyzes it as his own being, and reads that as ignorance, the primal nature, and māyā.
meaning: one's own nature (ignorance, the primal nature)
