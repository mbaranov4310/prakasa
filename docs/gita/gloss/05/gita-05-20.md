# gita-05-20

## na
ids: gita-05-20-w1, gita-05-20-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The second is written no; its a joins the u of udvijet.
meaning: not

## prahṛṣyet
ids: gita-05-20-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: hṛṣ
prefix: pra
class: div
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The subject is not a word in the verse. Śaṅkara takes the one who sees the self alone, and then, further on, describes the knower.
meaning: should exult

## priyam
ids: gita-05-20-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: priya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The adjective stands as a noun. priyam is masculine accusative or neuter. Śaṅkara glosses iṣṭam, a thing wanted, so it is neuter, and it is what he gains, so it is accusative.
meaning: what is dear (what one wants)

## prāpya
ids: gita-05-20-w4, gita-05-20-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: āp
prefix: pra
note: Absolutive of āp with pra.
meaning: having gained

## udvijet
ids: gita-05-20-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vij
prefix: ud
class: tud
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The initial u joins na, written dvijet. Same unstated subject as prahṛṣyet.
meaning: should be shaken

## ca
ids: gita-05-20-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written cā; the a merges with the a of apriyam.
meaning: and

## apriyam
ids: gita-05-20-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: a-priya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Negation of priya. The initial a joins ca, written priyam. Śaṅkara glosses aniṣṭam, so it is neuter, and it is what he gains, so it is accusative.
meaning: what is unwelcome

## sthira-buddhiḥ
ids: gita-05-20-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sthira-buddhi
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: He whose understanding is firm, free of doubt that the self in all beings is one and flawless. Written sthira-buddhir before asaṃmūḍhaḥ.
meaning: whose understanding is firm (free of doubt that the self in all beings is one and flawless)

## asaṃmūḍhaḥ
ids: gita-05-20-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: asaṃmūḍha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: muh
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Negated past participle of muh with sam. The line writes saṃmūḍho, with anusvāra: the initial a joins the r of sthira-buddhiḥ, and ḥ becomes o before brahmavid. Śaṅkara glosses it saṃmoha-varjita.
meaning: free of bewilderment

## brahma-vit
ids: gita-05-20-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahma-vid
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: vid
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: brahman and vid, one who knows Brahman. Written brahmavid; final t is voiced before brahmaṇi.
meaning: the knower of Brahman

## brahmaṇi
ids: gita-05-20-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahman
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
meaning: in Brahman

## sthitaḥ
ids: gita-05-20-w14
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with brahmavid. Śaṅkara supplies syāt and takes the abiding as doing no action, every action laid down.
meaning: abiding (doing no action, every action laid down)
