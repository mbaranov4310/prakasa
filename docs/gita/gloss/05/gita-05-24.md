# gita-05-24

## yaḥ
ids: gita-05-24-w1, gita-05-24-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The first is written yo, and the a of antaḥ-sukhaḥ is dropped. The second is yaḥ before the danda.
meaning: who

## antaḥ-sukhaḥ
ids: gita-05-24-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: antaḥ-sukha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose joy is within, in the self. The slice drops the initial a. Written antaḥsukho, and the a of antaḥ-ārāmaḥ is dropped.
meaning: he whose joy is within (in the self)

## antaḥ-ārāmaḥ
ids: gita-05-24-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: antaḥ-ārāma
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose delight, his play, is within the self. The slice drops the initial a, antaḥ shows r before ārāma, and the visarga becomes s before tathā.
meaning: he whose delight is within (his play is in the self)

## tathā
ids: gita-05-24-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The ā absorbs the a of the following antaḥ.
meaning: and

## antaḥ-jyotiḥ
ids: gita-05-24-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: antaḥ-jyotis
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose light is within the self. jyotis is neuter; the bahuvrīhi is masculine with yaḥ. The slice drops the initial a, antaḥ shows r before jyotiḥ, and the slice keeps the e of eva.
meaning: he whose light is within (the light is in the self)

## eva
ids: gita-05-24-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e joins jyotiḥ, so the slice is va. Śaṅkara reads it as within the self alone.
meaning: alone

## saḥ

kind: pronoun

gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written sa before yogī.
meaning: he

## yogī

## brahma-nirvāṇam
ids: gita-05-24-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahma-nirvāṇa
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: vā
prefix: nir
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he reaches, so it is accusative. Śaṅkara glosses it as release, peace in Brahman. Written brahmanirvāṇaṃ.
meaning: the peace of Brahman (release)

## brahma-bhūtaḥ
ids: gita-05-24-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: brahma-bhūta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhū
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: bhūta is the past participle of bhū, “having become Brahman.” Written brahmabhūto, and the a of adhigacchati is dropped. Śaṅkara: while still alive.
meaning: having become Brahman (while still alive)

## adhigacchati
ids: gita-05-24-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: gam
prefix: adhi
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The a is dropped after brahma-bhūtaḥ. Śaṅkara glosses it prāpnoti, “reaches.”
meaning: reaches
