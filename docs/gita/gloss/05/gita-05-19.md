# gita-05-19

## iha
ids: gita-05-19-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written ihai; the a merges with the e of eva.
meaning: here (while still alive)

## eva
ids: gita-05-19-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e joins the a of iha, so the line writes va.
meaning: indeed

## taiḥ
ids: gita-05-19-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: taiḥ has one form for all genders. Śaṅkara takes it of the same-seeing wise, so it is masculine. Written tair before jitaḥ.
meaning: by them (by those who see the same)

## jitaḥ
ids: gita-05-19-w4
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: ji
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with sargaḥ.
meaning: conquered

## sargaḥ
ids: gita-05-19-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarga
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: sṛj
note: From sṛj. Śaṅkara glosses it as janma, birth. Written sargo before yeṣām.
meaning: birth (the changing creation)

## yeṣām
ids: gita-05-19-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
note: yeṣām is masculine or neuter. It is the same people as taiḥ, so it is masculine.
meaning: of whom

## sāmye
ids: gita-05-19-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sāmya
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
note: Śaṅkara reads the sameness as Brahman, the same in every being.
meaning: in sameness (Brahman the same in every being)

## sthitam
ids: gita-05-19-w8
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It agrees with manaḥ, so it is nominative.
meaning: settled

## manaḥ
ids: gita-05-19-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: manas
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: man
note: manaḥ is nominative or accusative. It is what is settled, so it is nominative. Śaṅkara calls it the inner organ.
meaning: the mind

## nirdoṣam
ids: gita-05-19-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nir-doṣa
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: nis and doṣa, in which fault is absent. It agrees with brahma. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is predicated of Brahman, so it is nominative.
meaning: flawless (untouched by the defects others see in it)

## hi
ids: gita-05-19-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: for

## samam
ids: gita-05-19-w12
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sama
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: It agrees with brahma. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is predicated of Brahman, so it is nominative.
meaning: the same (not divided by qualities of its own)

## brahma
ids: gita-05-19-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahman
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: brahma is nominative or accusative. It is what is flawless and the same, so it is nominative.
meaning: Brahman

## tasmāt
ids: gita-05-19-w14
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: tasmāt is masculine or neuter. It takes up the reason just given, so it is neuter. Written tasmād before brahmaṇi.
meaning: therefore

## brahmaṇi
ids: gita-05-19-w15
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahman
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
meaning: in Brahman

## te
ids: gita-05-19-w16
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: With sthitāḥ, te is masculine nominative plural, those same people.
meaning: they

## sthitāḥ
ids: gita-05-19-w17
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: It agrees with te. The participle is the abiding.
meaning: abide
