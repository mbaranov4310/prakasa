# gita-05-04

## sāṃkhya-yogau
ids: gita-05-04-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sāṃkhya-yoga
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: dual
compound: dvandva
note: A dvandva, the path of knowledge and yoga. The masculine dual -au is nominative or accusative. It is what the childish call different, so it is accusative.
meaning: the path of knowledge and yoga (renunciation and the yoga of action)
