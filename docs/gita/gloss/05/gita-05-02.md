# gita-05-02

## śrī-bhagavān
ids: gita-05-02-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śrī-bhagavat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: śrī, “blessed,” with bhagavat, “the Lord.” The u of uvāca is joined to the final n.
meaning: the blessed Lord

## uvāca
ids: gita-05-02-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The initial u is joined to the n of bhagavān.
meaning: said

## saṃnyāsaḥ
ids: gita-05-02-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃnyāsa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: as
note: The action noun of as with the prefixes sam and ni. The actions are the ones named in the question.
meaning: renunciation (of actions)

## karma-yogaḥ
ids: gita-05-02-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karma-yoga
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: yoga is the action noun of yuj: the yoga of action. Written karma-yogaś before ca.
meaning: the yoga of action (doing those actions)

## ca
ids: gita-05-02-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## niḥśreyasa-karau
ids: gita-05-02-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: niḥśreyasa-kara
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: dual
root: kṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: kara is the agent noun of kṛ, “making.” niḥśreyasa is release. The masculine dual -au is nominative or accusative. It agrees with ubhau, so it is nominative. The u of ubhau is joined to the final v.
meaning: bringing about release

## ubhau
ids: gita-05-02-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: ubha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: dual
note: The masculine dual -au is nominative or accusative. Both are what bring about release, so it is nominative. The initial u is joined to the v of karau.
meaning: both

## tayoḥ
ids: gita-05-02-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: dual
note: The genitive dual is the same for all genders. It takes up saṃnyāsa and karma-yoga, both masculine, so it is masculine. Written tayos before tu.
meaning: of these two

## tu
ids: gita-05-02-w9
