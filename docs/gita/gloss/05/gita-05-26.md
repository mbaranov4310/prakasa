# gita-05-26

## kāma-krodha-viyuktānām
ids: gita-05-26-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kāma-krodha-viyukta
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
root: yuj
prefix: vi
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Freed from desire and anger. viyukta is the past participle of vi-yuj, and the two are a dvandva. Written kāmakrodhaviyuktānāṃ.
meaning: of those freed from desire and anger

## yatīnām
ids: gita-05-26-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yatin
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
note: yati with the suffix -in, one who strives. Śaṅkara: renunciates. Written yatīnāṃ.
meaning: of the strivers (renunciates)

## yata-cetasām
ids: gita-05-26-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yata-cetas
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose mind is held in. Śaṅkara: whose inner organ is restrained.
meaning: of those whose minds are held in

## abhitaḥ
ids: gita-05-26-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written abhito before brahma. Śaṅkara glosses it ubhayataḥ, while living and when dead.
meaning: on both sides (while living, and when dead)

## brahma-nirvāṇam
ids: gita-05-26-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahma-nirvāṇa
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: vā
prefix: nir
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what lies there, so it is nominative. Written brahmanirvāṇaṃ.
meaning: the peace of Brahman (release)

## vartate
ids: gita-05-26-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vṛt
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
meaning: lies

## vidita-ātmanām
ids: gita-05-26-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vidita-ātman
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose self is known. vidita is the past participle of vid, and Śaṅkara takes them as those who see rightly.
meaning: of those by whom the self is known
