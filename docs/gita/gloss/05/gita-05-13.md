# gita-05-13

## sarva-karmāṇi
ids: gita-05-13-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-karman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
root: kṛ
compound: karmadhāraya
note: The neuter -āni is nominative or accusative, and it is what he renounces, so it is accusative. Śaṅkara counts obligatory actions, occasional ones, those prompted by desire, and the forbidden.
meaning: all actions

## manasā
ids: gita-05-13-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: manas
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: man
note: Śaṅkara reads the mind here as discrimination.
meaning: with the mind

## saṃnyasya
ids: gita-05-13-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: as
prefix: sam-ni
note: Absolutive of as, to throw, with sam and ni. Written saṃnyasyā: the ā of āste stays in this slice.
meaning: having renounced

## āste
ids: gita-05-13-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: ās
class: ad
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The initial ā is written with saṃnyasya, so the slice is ste.
meaning: rests

## sukham
ids: gita-05-13-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written sukhaṃ before vaśī. The neuter accusative singular of sukha, used as an adverb.
meaning: easily (without strain)

## vaśī
ids: gita-05-13-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vaśin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The -in stem. It agrees with dehī.
meaning: self-controlled (master of the senses)

## nava-dvāre
ids: gita-05-13-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nava-dvāra
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: A city that has nine gates. Śaṅkara counts seven doors of perception in the head and two lower outlets.
meaning: in the nine-gated

## pure
ids: gita-05-13-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pura
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
meaning: city (the body)

## dehī
ids: gita-05-13-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dehin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The -in stem stands as the man. Śaṅkara takes him as knowing the self apart from the body, and so as resting in that body.
meaning: the embodied one

## na
ids: gita-05-13-w10, gita-05-13-w13
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The first is written nai; the a joins the e of eva. The second is unchanged before kārayan.
meaning: not

## eva
ids: gita-05-13-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e joins na, so the slice keeps only v.
meaning: at all

## kurvan
ids: gita-05-13-w12
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The present stem is kurva. Joined to the following na, so the print shows kurvann.
meaning: doing

## kārayan
ids: gita-05-13-w14
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Causative of kṛ. Śaṅkara says the self neither does nor causes action by its own nature.
meaning: causing to act
