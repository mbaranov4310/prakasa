# gita-05-15

## na
ids: gita-05-15-w1, gita-05-15-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The first is written nā; its a joins the ā of ādatte.
meaning: not

## ādatte
ids: gita-05-15-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dā
prefix: ā
class: hu
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The initial ā joins na, so the slice is datte. Śaṅkara glosses it as gṛhṇāti.
meaning: takes

## kasyacit
ids: gita-05-15-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: kim
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: kasya is masculine or neuter. Śaṅkara takes it of a person, even a devotee, so it is masculine. cit is the indefinite particle.
meaning: of anyone

## pāpam
ids: gita-05-15-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pāpa
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what he does not take, so it is accusative.
meaning: evil

## ca
ids: gita-05-15-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written cai; the a merges with the e of eva.
meaning: and

## eva
ids: gita-05-15-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e joins the a of ca, so the line writes va.
meaning: even

## sukṛtam
ids: gita-05-15-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: su-kṛta
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: kṛ
compound: karmadhāraya
note: su and the past participle of kṛ, “well done,” standing as a noun. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is the second thing he does not take, so it is accusative.
meaning: good work

## vibhuḥ
ids: gita-05-15-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: vibhu
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhū
note: From vi and bhū, the one who pervades.
meaning: the all-pervading

## ajñānena
ids: gita-05-15-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: a-jñāna
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: jñā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Negation of jñāna. The final a joins the ā of āvṛtam, written ajñānenā.
meaning: by ignorance

## āvṛtam
ids: gita-05-15-w11
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: vṛ
prefix: ā
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The initial ā joins the final a of ajñānena. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It agrees with jñānam, so it is nominative.
meaning: covered

## jñānam
ids: gita-05-15-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: jñā
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what is covered, so it is nominative. Śaṅkara takes it as viveka, discrimination.
meaning: knowledge (discrimination)

## tena

kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious

gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: tena is masculine or neuter. It takes up the ignorance, which is neuter, so it is neuter.
meaning: by that
