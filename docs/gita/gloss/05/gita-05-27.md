# gita-05-27

## sparśān
ids: gita-05-27-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sparśa
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
root: spṛś
note: Sound and the other objects of the senses.
meaning: contacts (sound and the other objects)

## kṛtvā
ids: gita-05-27-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
note: Absolutive of kṛ. Śaṅkara carries it over to the eye as well.
meaning: having put

## bahiḥ
ids: gita-05-27-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written bahir before bāhyān.
meaning: outside

## bāhyān
ids: gita-05-27-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: bāhya
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
note: It agrees with sparśān. Written bāhyāṃś before cakṣuḥ.
meaning: external

## cakṣuḥ
ids: gita-05-27-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: cakṣus
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: cakṣ
note: The neuter -uḥ is nominative or accusative. It is what he puts between the brows, so it is accusative. Written cakṣuś before ca.
meaning: the eye

## ca
ids: gita-05-27-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The a joins the e of eva, so the slice is cai.
meaning: and

## eva
ids: gita-05-27-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e joins ca, and the a joins antare, so the slice is vā.
meaning: indeed

## antare
ids: gita-05-27-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: antara
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
note: Locative of antara, the interval. The a is absorbed into eva.
meaning: between

## bhruvoḥ
ids: gita-05-27-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhrū
gender: feminine
case: genitive
number: dual
note: The genitive and locative dual share this ending. antare takes the genitive: between the two brows.
meaning: the two brows

## prāṇa-apānau
ids: gita-05-27-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: prāṇa-apāna
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: dual
root: an
compound: dvandva
note: The dual -au is nominative or accusative. They are what he makes even, so it is accusative. Out-breath and in-breath, from pra-an and apa-an.
meaning: the out-breath and the in-breath

## samau
ids: gita-05-27-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sama
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: dual
note: It agrees with prāṇa-apānau. Having made the two breaths even.
meaning: even

## kṛtvā
ids: gita-05-27-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
note: Absolutive of kṛ.
meaning: having made

## nāsā-abhyantara-cāriṇau
ids: gita-05-27-w13
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nāsā-abhyantara-cārin
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: dual
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Moving inside the nostrils. It agrees with prāṇa-apānau, so the dual -au is accusative. cārin is car with the suffix -in.
meaning: moving inside the nostrils
