# gita-05-06

## saṃnyāsaḥ
ids: gita-05-06-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṃnyāsa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: as
note: The action noun of as with the prefixes sam and ni. Written saṃnyāsas before tu.
meaning: renunciation (the highest)

## tu
ids: gita-05-06-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: but

## mahā-bāho
ids: gita-05-06-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mahā-bāhu
gender: masculine
case: vocative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose arm is great.” The adjective stands as a noun of address. Vocative singular -o.
meaning: O mighty-armed

## duḥkham
ids: gita-05-06-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The neuter accusative singular of duḥkha, used as an adverb. The ā of āptum is joined to it.
meaning: with difficulty

## āptum
ids: gita-05-06-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: āp
note: Infinitive of āp. The initial ā is joined to duḥkham.
meaning: to reach

## ayogataḥ
ids: gita-05-06-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The suffix -tas has ablative sense: from the absence of yoga, so without yoga. The initial a is joined to āptum.
meaning: without yoga

## yoga-yuktaḥ
ids: gita-05-06-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga-yukta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: yukta is the past participle of yuj. Yoked to yoga. Written yoga-yukto before muniḥ.
meaning: yoked to yoga (action offered to the Lord, not for its fruit)

## muniḥ
ids: gita-05-06-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: muni
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: man
note: From man, “to reflect.” Written munir before brahma.
meaning: the sage (who reflects on the Lord's nature)

## brahma
ids: gita-05-06-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter brahma is nominative or accusative. It is what the sage reaches, so it is accusative.
meaning: the highest renunciation (steadfastness in knowledge of the self)

## na
ids: gita-05-06-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

## cireṇa
ids: gita-05-06-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: cira
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: The instrumental is the same for masculine and neuter. It is a length of time, so it is neuter. Written cireṇā, joined to adhigacchati.
meaning: after a long time

## adhigacchati
ids: gita-05-06-w12
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: gam
prefix: adhi
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The initial a is joined to cireṇa.
meaning: reaches
