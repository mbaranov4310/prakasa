## kevalaiḥ
ids: gita-05-11-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kevala
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: Written kevalairi: the visarga becomes r, and the i of indriyaiḥ stays in this slice. The plural matches the senses; Śaṅkara also takes kevala with the body, the mind, and the intellect, each separately.
meaning: alone (free of the sense of 'mine')

## indriyaiḥ
ids: gita-05-11-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: The initial i is written with kevalaiḥ. The visarga becomes r before api, and the a of api stays with that r, so the slice is ndriyair.
meaning: with the senses

## api
ids: gita-05-11-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The initial a is written with indriyaiḥ, so the slice is pi.
meaning: also

## yoginaḥ
ids: gita-05-11-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yogin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: yuj
note: yoginaḥ is genitive singular or nominative plural, and the plural verb settles it as nominative plural. Śaṅkara reads them as men of action.
meaning: yogins (men of action)

## karma

## saṅgam
ids: gita-05-11-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saṅga
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: sañj
note: Written saṅgaṃ before tyaktvā. The -am is nominative or accusative, and it is what they give up, so it is accusative.

## tyaktvā
ids: gita-05-11-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: tyaj
note: Absolutive of tyaj. The print joins it to ātma-śuddhaye with no avagraha.
meaning: having given up

## ātma-śuddhaye
ids: gita-05-11-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātma-śuddhi
gender: feminine
case: dative
number: singular
root: śudh
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The initial ā merges into the ā of tyaktvā, and the print has no avagraha, so the slice is tmaśuddhaye. Cleansing of the self. Śaṅkara reads that self as sattva.
meaning: for the cleansing of the self
