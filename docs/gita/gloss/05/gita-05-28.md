# gita-05-28

## yata-indriya-manaḥ-buddhiḥ
ids: gita-05-28-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yata-indriya-manaḥ-buddhi
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose senses, mind, and understanding are held in. buddhi is feminine; the bahuvrīhi is masculine with muniḥ. manaḥ is written mano before buddhi, and the final visarga becomes r before muniḥ.
meaning: whose senses, mind, and understanding are held in

## muniḥ
ids: gita-05-28-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: muni
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: man
note: Śaṅkara derives it from manana, reflecting, and takes him as a renunciate. The absolutives of the previous verse lean on him. Written munir before mokṣa.
meaning: the sage (a renunciate, from his reflecting)

## mokṣa-parāyaṇaḥ
ids: gita-05-28-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mokṣa-parāyaṇa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Whose highest course is release. Śaṅkara supplies bhavet: the sage should be such.
meaning: whose highest course is release

## vigata-icchā-bhaya-krodhaḥ
ids: gita-05-28-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vigata-icchā-bhaya-krodha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Desire, fear, and anger have gone from him. vigata is written vigate before icchā, and the final visarga becomes o before yaḥ.
meaning: he from whom desire, fear, and anger have gone
