# gita-05-18

## vidyā-vinaya-saṃpanne
ids: gita-05-18-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vidyā-vinaya-saṃpanna
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
root: pad
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: vidyā and vinaya, learning and humility, and saṃpanna, the past participle of pad with sam. It agrees with brāhmaṇe.
meaning: in one endowed with learning and humility (a quieted mind)

## gavi
ids: gita-05-18-w3
kind: noun

stem: go

case: locative
number: singular
note: go is masculine or feminine. Śaṅkara gives it feminine adjectives, a cow, so it is feminine.
meaning: in a cow

## ca
ids: gita-05-18-w6, gita-05-18-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The first is written cai; its a merges with the e of eva.
meaning: and

## eva
ids: gita-05-18-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The e joins the a of ca, so the line writes va.
meaning: indeed

## sama-darśinaḥ
ids: gita-05-18-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sama-darśin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: sama and darśin, from dṛś, one who sees the same. Śaṅkara takes that same as the one Brahman, untouched by the qualities. It agrees with paṇḍitāḥ.
meaning: see the same (the one Brahman, untouched by the qualities and their traces)
