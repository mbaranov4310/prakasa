# gita-05-16

## jñānena
ids: gita-05-16-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: jñā
note: Śaṅkara calls it discriminating knowledge whose object is the self.
meaning: by knowledge (discrimination whose object is the self)

## tu
ids: gita-05-16-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: but

## tat
ids: gita-05-16-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written tad before ajñānam. The neuter tat is nominative or accusative. It agrees with ajñānam, so it is nominative.
meaning: that (the ignorance named above)

## ajñānam
ids: gita-05-16-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: a-jñāna
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: jñā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Negation of jñāna. The initial a joins tad, written jñānaṃ. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what is destroyed, so it is nominative.
meaning: ignorance

## yeṣām
ids: gita-05-16-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
note: yeṣām is masculine or neuter. Śaṅkara supplies the creatures of the previous verse, so it is masculine.
meaning: of whom

## nāśitam
ids: gita-05-16-w6
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: naś
participle: past
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Causative of naś. Written nāśitamā; the m joins the ā of ātmanaḥ. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It agrees with ajñānam, so it is nominative.
meaning: destroyed

## ātmanaḥ
ids: gita-05-16-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ātman
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: The initial ā joins nāśitam, written tmanaḥ. ātmanaḥ is genitive or ablative. It is the self the ignorance belongs to, so it is genitive.
meaning: of the self

## teṣām
ids: gita-05-16-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
note: Written teṣāmā; the m joins the ā of ādityavat.
meaning: of them

## ādityavat
ids: gita-05-16-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: āditya with the suffix -vat. The initial ā joins teṣām, and t becomes j before jñānam, written dityavaj.
meaning: like the sun (as the sun lights every form)

## jñānam
ids: gita-05-16-w10

judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: jñā
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what illumines, so it is nominative.

root: kāś

## tat
ids: gita-05-16-w12
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter tat is nominative or accusative. It is what knowledge illumines, with param, so it is accusative.
meaning: that

## param
ids: gita-05-16-w13
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: para
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The adjective para stands as a noun, the supreme reality. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what knowledge illumines, so it is accusative.
meaning: the supreme (the highest reality)
