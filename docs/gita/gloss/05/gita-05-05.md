# gita-05-05

## yat
ids: gita-05-05-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The neuter yat is nominative or accusative. It is the place that is reached, so it is nominative. Joined to sāṃkhyaiḥ.
meaning: what

## sāṃkhyaiḥ
ids: gita-05-05-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sāṃkhya
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
note: The adjective stands as a noun, the men of knowledge.
meaning: by the men of knowledge (renouncers, steady in knowledge)
