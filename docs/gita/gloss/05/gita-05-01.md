## śreyaḥ
ids: gita-05-01-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śreyas
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Comparative of praśasya. The neuter -aḥ of this stem is nominative or accusative. It is the predicate of yat, so it is nominative.
meaning: the better

## etayoḥ
ids: gita-05-01-w12
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: dual
note: The genitive dual is the same for all genders. It takes up saṃnyāsa and yoga, both masculine, so it is masculine. Written etayor before ekam.
meaning: of these two

## ekam
ids: gita-05-01-w13
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: eka
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It stands with tat, the thing he asks to be told, so it is accusative. The e is joined to the r of etayor.
meaning: one

## tat
ids: gita-05-01-w14
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter tat is nominative or accusative. It is what he asks to be told, so it is accusative. Written tan before me.
meaning: that

## me
ids: gita-05-01-w15
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: dative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The enclitic me is dative or genitive. The meaning is “to me,” so it is dative.
meaning: to me

## brūhi

## suniścitam
ids: gita-05-01-w17
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: su-niścita
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: ci
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: niścita is the past participle of ci with the prefix nis. su means well. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It agrees with tat, so it is accusative.
meaning: settled (what you hold)
