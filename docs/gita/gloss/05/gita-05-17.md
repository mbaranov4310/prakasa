# gita-05-17

## tat-buddhayaḥ
ids: gita-05-17-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: tat-buddhi
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Those whose understanding (buddhi) has gone to that, to Brahman. Written tad-buddhayas: t becomes d before b, and the visarga becomes s.
meaning: whose understanding is set on that (on Brahman)

## tat-ātmānaḥ
ids: gita-05-17-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: tat-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Those whose self is that supreme Brahman. Written tad-ātmānas: t becomes d before ā, and the visarga becomes s.
meaning: whose self is that (that supreme Brahman)

## tat-niṣṭhāḥ
ids: gita-05-17-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: tat-niṣṭha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Those whose firm stand is in that Brahman, every action laid down. Written tan-niṣṭhās: t becomes n before n, and the visarga becomes s.
meaning: whose stand is in that (every action laid down, they abide in Brahman)

## tat-parāyaṇāḥ
ids: gita-05-17-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: tat-parāyaṇa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Those whose highest course is that. para and ayana, the supreme goal; they delight in the self alone.
meaning: whose highest course is that (they delight in the self alone)

## gacchanti
ids: gita-05-17-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: gam
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: Written gacchanty before the a of apunar-āvṛttim.
meaning: go

## apunar-āvṛttim
ids: gita-05-17-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: apunar-āvṛtti
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: vṛt
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: a- with punar-āvṛtti, from ā and vṛt, a return that does not come again. The initial a joins gacchanti, written punar-āvṛttiṃ.
meaning: to non-return (to no further bond with a body)

## jñāna-nirdhūta-kalmaṣāḥ
ids: gita-05-17-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna-nirdhūta-kalmaṣa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: Those whose stain (kalmaṣa) is shaken off (nirdhūta, from dhū with nis) by knowledge. The stain is pāpa and the rest, the cause of saṃsāra.
meaning: whose stain is shaken off by knowledge (pāpa and the rest, the cause of saṃsāra)
