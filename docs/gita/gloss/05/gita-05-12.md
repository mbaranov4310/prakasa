# gita-05-12

## yuktaḥ
ids: gita-05-12-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yukta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
note: Past participle of yuj, standing as the man. Śaṅkara reads him as collected: he works for the Lord, not for the fruit.
meaning: the steadied man

## karma-phalam
ids: gita-05-12-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karma-phala
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: Written karma-phalaṃ before tyaktvā. The neuter -am is nominative or accusative, and it is what he gives up, so it is accusative.
meaning: the fruit of action

## tyaktvā
ids: gita-05-12-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: tyaj
note: Absolutive of tyaj.
meaning: having given up

## śāntim
ids: gita-05-12-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śānti
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: śam
note: Written śāntimā: m joins the ā of āpnoti, and that ā stays in this slice.
meaning: peace (release)

## āpnoti
ids: gita-05-12-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: āp
class: su
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The initial ā is written with śāntim, so the slice is pnoti.
meaning: reaches

## naiṣṭhikīm
ids: gita-05-12-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: naiṣṭhika
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: It agrees with śāntim and is formed from niṣṭhā. Śaṅkara takes that peace as reached through purity of sattva, knowledge, renunciation, and then steadfastness in knowledge.
meaning: belonging to steadfastness (purity, then knowledge, then renunciation)

## ayuktaḥ
ids: gita-05-12-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ayukta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
note: Negated participle of yuj, standing as the man. Śaṅkara reads him as not collected.
meaning: the unsteadied man

## kāma-kāreṇa
ids: gita-05-12-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kāma-kāra
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: kṛ
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The prompting (kāra) of desire (kāma). Śaṅkara takes kāra as the driving, so this is by being impelled by desire.
meaning: by the drive of desire
