# gita-05-23

## śaknoti
ids: gita-05-23-w1
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: śak
class: su
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Written śaknotī. The i of iha joins the i of śaknoti.
meaning: is able (has the strength to bear it)

## iha
ids: gita-05-23-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The i joins śaknoti, and the a joins the e of eva, so the slice is hai. Śaṅkara reads ihaiva as while still alive.
meaning: here (while still alive)

## eva
ids: gita-05-23-w3

judgment: obvious
note: The e joins iha, so the slice is va.
meaning: indeed
