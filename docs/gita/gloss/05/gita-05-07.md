# gita-05-07

## yoga-yuktaḥ
ids: gita-05-07-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yoga-yukta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: yukta is the past participle of yuj. Yoked to yoga. Written yoga-yukto before viśuddha-ātmā.
meaning: yoked to yoga

ids: gita-05-07-w2

judgment: obvious
stem: viśuddha-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi

meaning: whose mind is pure

## sarva-bhūta-ātma-bhūta-ātmā
