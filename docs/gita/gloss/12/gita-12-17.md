# gita-12-17

ids: gita-12-17-w1, gita-12-17-w12
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular

## śubha-aśubha-parityāgī
ids: gita-12-17-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śubha-aśubha-parityāgin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: tyaj
prefix: pari
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: parityāgin, from pari-tyaj with the suffix -in, is “one whose way it is to give up”; what he gives up is the good and the bad (śubha-aśubha), which Śaṅkara takes as the two kinds of action.
meaning: giving up good and bad actions
