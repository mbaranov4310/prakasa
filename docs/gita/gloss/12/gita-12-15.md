# gita-12-15

## yasmāt
ids: gita-12-15-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: singular
note: The ablative singular is the same in masculine and neuter. It stands for a person, so it is masculine. Written yasmān before na.
meaning: from whom

## na
ids: gita-12-15-w2, gita-12-15-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Both times its a and the u of udvijate join into o: nodvijate.
meaning: not

## udvijate
ids: gita-12-15-w3, gita-12-15-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vij
prefix: ud
class: tud
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: What one shrinks from stands in the ablative.
meaning: shrinks

## harṣa-amarṣa-bhaya-udvegaiḥ
ids: gita-12-15-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: harṣa-amarṣa-bhaya-udvega
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: plural
compound: dvandva
note: Four things joined: elation (harṣa), intolerance (amarṣa), fear (bhaya) and agitation (udvega). With muktaḥ the instrumental says what he is free of. Written -udvegair before muktaḥ.
meaning: from elation, intolerance, fear and agitation
