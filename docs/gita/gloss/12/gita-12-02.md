# gita-12-02

## śrī-bhagavān
ids: gita-12-02-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śrī-bhagavat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: bhagavat is bhaga (“fortune, lordship”) with the suffix -vat, “having.” śrī is a title of honour set before it.
meaning: the Blessed Lord

## uvāca
ids: gita-12-02-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vac
tense: distant past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
meaning: said

## mayi
ids: gita-12-02-w3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: locative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written mayy before āveśya.
meaning: on me (the Lord in the universal form)

## āveśya
ids: gita-12-02-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: viś
prefix: ā
note: Causative of viś. An absolutive in -ya: “having made (the mind) enter.”
meaning: having fixed

## manaḥ
ids: gita-12-02-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: manas
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: man
note: The neuter manaḥ is nominative or accusative. It is what is fixed, so it is accusative. Written mano before ye.
meaning: the mind

## ye
ids: gita-12-02-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
meaning: those who

## mām
ids: gita-12-02-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender.
meaning: me

## nitya-yuktāḥ
ids: gita-12-02-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nitya-yukta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: yuj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: yukta is the past participle of yuj. The visarga drops before upāsate.
meaning: ever steadfast

## upāsate
ids: gita-12-02-w9
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: ās
prefix: upa
class: ad
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: plural
meaning: worship

## śraddhayā
ids: gita-12-02-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śraddhā
gender: feminine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: dhā
note: śraddhā is from dhā, “to place,” with śrad set before it.
meaning: with faith

## parayā
ids: gita-12-02-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: para
gender: feminine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: It agrees with śraddhayā. Its final ā and the u of upetāḥ join into o: parayopetāḥ.
meaning: supreme

## upetāḥ
ids: gita-12-02-w12
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: i
prefix: upa
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: “Gone to,” and so “endowed with”; what one is endowed with stands in the instrumental. Written upetās before te.
meaning: endowed

## te
ids: gita-12-02-w13
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
meaning: they

## me
ids: gita-12-02-w14
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: me is the short form of asmad for the dative or the genitive. With the participle mata the one who holds the view stands in the genitive, and Śaṅkara glosses it mama. asmad has no gender.
meaning: by me

## yuktatamāḥ
ids: gita-12-02-w15
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yuktatama
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: yuj
note: Superlative, with -tama, of yukta, the past participle of yuj. The visarga drops before matāḥ.
meaning: the best yogins

## matāḥ
ids: gita-12-02-w16
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: man
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The participle is the predicate. “Are” is understood.
meaning: are held to be
