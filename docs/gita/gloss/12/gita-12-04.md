# gita-12-04

## saṃniyamya
ids: gita-12-04-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: yam
prefix: sam-ni
note: An absolutive in -ya. Its final a and the i of indriya join into e: saṃniyamyendriya.
meaning: having fully restrained

## indriya-grāmam
ids: gita-12-04-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: indriya-grāma
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “The group (grāma) of the senses (indriya).”
meaning: the group of senses

## sarvatra
ids: gita-12-04-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: An adverb, “everywhere.” Śaṅkara takes it of time: at every time.
meaning: at all times

## sama-buddhayaḥ
ids: gita-12-04-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sama-buddhi
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those whose understanding (buddhi) is even (sama).” buddhi by itself is feminine; the compound is masculine because it describes the worshippers.
meaning: even-minded (whether the wanted or the unwanted comes)

number: plural

## sarva-bhūta-hite
ids: gita-12-04-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-bhūta-hita
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
root: dhā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “The good (hita) of all beings (sarva-bhūta).” hita is the past participle of dhā, used as a neuter noun.
meaning: in the good of all beings

root: ram

gender: masculine

number: plural
