## samuddhartā
ids: gita-12-07-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: samuddhartṛ
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: hṛ
prefix: sam-ud
note: An agent noun in -tṛ: “one who lifts out.”
meaning: the deliverer

## mṛtyu-saṃsāra-sāgarāt
ids: gita-12-07-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mṛtyu-saṃsāra-sāgara
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: “The ocean (sāgara) that is saṃsāra, which is bound up with death (mṛtyu).” Śaṅkara says saṃsāra is itself the ocean, being hard to cross.
meaning: from the ocean of mortal saṃsāra

## āveśita-cetasām
ids: gita-12-07-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: āveśita-cetas
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those whose mind (cetas) has been made to enter (āveśita).” āveśita is the past participle of the causative of ā-viś.
meaning: whose minds are fixed
