# gita-12-08

## mayi
ids: gita-12-08-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: locative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written mayy before eva.
meaning: on me

## eva
ids: gita-12-08-w2, gita-12-08-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: alone

## manaḥ
ids: gita-12-08-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: manas
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: man
note: The neuter manaḥ is nominative or accusative. It is what is to be fixed, so it is accusative. The visarga drops before ādhatsva.
meaning: the mind (which wills and wavers)

## ādhatsva
ids: gita-12-08-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: dhā
prefix: ā
class: hu
tense: command
voice: ātmanepada
person: 2nd
number: singular
meaning: fix

## mayi
ids: gita-12-08-w5, gita-12-08-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: locative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. The second is written mayy before eva.
meaning: in me

## buddhim
ids: gita-12-08-w6

stem: buddhi

case: accusative
number: singular
root: budh
meaning: the intellect (which decides)

## niveśaya
ids: gita-12-08-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: viś
prefix: ni
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: Causative of viś.
meaning: settle

## nivasiṣyasi
ids: gita-12-08-w8
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: vas
prefix: ni
tense: simple future
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: The usual future of vas is vatsyasi. This is the form with the linking i, and Śaṅkara glosses it with nivatsyasi.
meaning: you will dwell

## ataḥ
ids: gita-12-08-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: “From this,” with the ending -tas. Śaṅkara takes “this” as the fall of the body. The visarga drops before ūrdhvam.
meaning: from now (when the body falls)

## ūrdhvam
ids: gita-12-08-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The neuter accusative singular of ūrdhva, “upward, later,” used as an adverb. With ataḥ: “after this.”
meaning: onward

ids: gita-12-08-w13

meaning: no

number: singular
