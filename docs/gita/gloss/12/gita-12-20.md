# gita-12-20

## ye
ids: gita-12-20-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
meaning: those who

## tu
ids: gita-12-20-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: but

## dharmya-amṛtam
ids: gita-12-20-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dharmya-amṛta
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
compound: karmadhāraya
note: “What is in keeping with dharma (dharmya) and is also nectar (amṛta).” The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what they follow, so it is accusative.
meaning: the nectar of dharma (in keeping with dharma, and leading to immortality)

## idam
ids: gita-12-20-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: idam
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter idam is nominative or accusative. It goes with dharmya-amṛtam, so it is accusative.
meaning: this

## yathā-uktam
ids: gita-12-20-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yathā-ukta
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: vac
note: yathā (“as”) with ukta, the past participle of vac: “as was said.” Śaṅkara takes it of the teaching set out from 12.13 on, so it agrees with dharmya-amṛtam.
meaning: as it has been taught

kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: ās
prefix: pari-upa
class: ad
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: plural
meaning: follow

## śraddadhānāḥ
ids: gita-12-20-w7
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: dhā
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The present participle (ātmanepada) of dhā, “to place,” with śrad set before it: “placing trust.” The visarga drops before mat-paramāḥ.
meaning: having faith

## mat-paramāḥ
ids: gita-12-20-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mat-parama
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those for whom I (mat) am the highest (parama) goal.” The visarga drops before bhaktāḥ.
meaning: holding me (the Imperishable Self) as the highest goal

## atīva

number: singular

meaning: to me
