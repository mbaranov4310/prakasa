# gita-12-19

## tulya-nindā-stutiḥ
ids: gita-12-19-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: tulya-nindā-stuti
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One for whom blame and praise (nindā, stuti) are equal (tulya).” Written -stutir before maunī.
meaning: to whom blame and praise are alike

## maunī
ids: gita-12-19-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: maunin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: mauna, “silence,” with the suffix -in, “having.”
meaning: silent (restrained in speech)

## saṃtuṣṭaḥ
ids: gita-12-19-w3
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: tuṣ
prefix: sam
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: What one is content with stands in the instrumental. Written saṃtuṣṭo before yena.
meaning: content

## yena
ids: gita-12-19-w4
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: The instrumental singular is the same in masculine and neuter. It stands for a thing left unnamed, so it is neuter.
meaning: with whatever

## kenacit
ids: gita-12-19-w5
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: kim
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: kena, the instrumental of kim, with the particle cit, which makes it indefinite: “anything.” yena kenacit together is “with whatever comes.”
meaning: with anything at all (that keeps the body going)

## aniketaḥ
ids: gita-12-19-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: aniketa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: a- (“without”) with niketa, “dwelling.”
meaning: homeless (with no fixed dwelling)

## sthira-matiḥ
ids: gita-12-19-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sthira-mati
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose understanding (mati) is steady (sthira).” Written -matir before bhaktimān.
meaning: steady in understanding (of the highest truth)

## bhaktimān
ids: gita-12-19-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: bhaktimat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: bhakti, “devotion,” with the suffix -mat, “having.”
meaning: full of devotion

## me
ids: gita-12-19-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: me is the short form of asmad for the dative or the genitive. With priya it is genitive, as in 12.14. asmad has no gender.
meaning: to me
