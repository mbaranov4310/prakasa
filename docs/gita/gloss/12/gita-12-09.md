# gita-12-09

## atha
ids: gita-12-09-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: atha opens the other case. Śaṅkara supplies “if” (cet) with the verb.
meaning: but if

## cittam
ids: gita-12-09-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: citta
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: cit
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what is to be fixed, so it is accusative.
meaning: the mind

## samādhātum
ids: gita-12-09-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: dhā
prefix: sam-ā
note: An infinitive in -tum.
meaning: to fix

## na
ids: gita-12-09-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: not

root: śak
class: su

person: 2nd
number: singular

## mayi

## sthiram
ids: gita-12-09-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious

meaning: steadily

## abhyāsa-yogena
ids: gita-12-09-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: abhyāsa-yoga
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: yuj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “The yoga, a collected mind, that comes of practice (abhyāsa).”
meaning: by the yoga of practice (bringing the mind back again and again)
