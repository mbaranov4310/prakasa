# gita-12-16

## anapekṣaḥ
ids: gita-12-16-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: anapekṣa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: an- (“without”) with apekṣā, “looking to, wanting,” from apa-īkṣ.
meaning: wanting nothing

## śuciḥ
ids: gita-12-16-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śuci
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written śucir before dakṣaḥ.
meaning: pure (outwardly and inwardly)

## dakṣaḥ
ids: gita-12-16-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: dakṣa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The visarga drops before udāsīnaḥ.
meaning: capable (quick to see what is to be done)

## udāsīnaḥ
ids: gita-12-16-w4
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: ās
prefix: ud
participle: present
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: āsīna is the present participle (ātmanepada) of ās, “to sit.” With ud: “sitting apart,” and so taking no side. Written udāsīno before gata-vyathaḥ.
meaning: impartial (taking no side)

## gata-vyathaḥ
ids: gita-12-16-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: gata-vyatha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose fear (vyathā) is gone (gata).” gata is the past participle of gam.
meaning: free from fear

## sarva-ārambha-parityāgī
ids: gita-12-16-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-ārambha-parityāgin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: tyaj
prefix: pari
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: parityāgin, from pari-tyaj with the suffix -in, is “one whose way it is to give up”; what he gives up is every undertaking (sarva-ārambha).
meaning: giving up every undertaking (done for results here or hereafter)

## yaḥ
ids: gita-12-16-w7
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written yo before mat-bhaktaḥ.
meaning: who

## mat-bhaktaḥ
ids: gita-12-16-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mat-bhakta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhaj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: mat is the form asmad takes at the front of a compound; it is written mad before bhaktaḥ.
meaning: my devotee

## saḥ
ids: gita-12-16-w9
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written sa: the visarga of saḥ drops before a consonant.
meaning: he

## me
ids: gita-12-16-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: me is the short form of asmad for the dative or the genitive. With priya it is genitive, as in 12.14. asmad has no gender.
meaning: to me

ids: gita-12-16-w11
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: priya
