# gita-12-14

## saṃtuṣṭaḥ
ids: gita-12-14-w1
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: tuṣ
prefix: sam
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
meaning: content

## satatam
ids: gita-12-14-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The neuter accusative singular of satata, “constant,” used as an adverb.
meaning: always

## yogī
ids: gita-12-14-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: yogin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: yuj
note: A stem in -in; the nominative singular ends in -ī.
meaning: a yogin (with the mind collected)

## yata-ātmā
ids: gita-12-14-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yata-ātman
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose self (ātman), here his nature, is held in check (yata).” yata is the past participle of yam.
meaning: self-controlled

## dṛḍha-niścayaḥ
ids: gita-12-14-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: dṛḍha-niścaya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose conviction (niścaya) is firm (dṛḍha).”
meaning: firm in conviction (about the truth of the Self)

## mayi
ids: gita-12-14-w6
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: locative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. Written mayy before arpita.
meaning: to me

## arpita-manaḥ-buddhiḥ
ids: gita-12-14-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: arpita-manaḥ-buddhi
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One whose mind and intellect (manas, buddhi) have been placed (arpita).” arpita is the past participle of the causative of ṛ. Written -mano-buddhir before yaḥ.
meaning: whose mind and intellect are given over

## yaḥ
ids: gita-12-14-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written yo before mat-bhaktaḥ.
meaning: who

## mat-bhaktaḥ
ids: gita-12-14-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mat-bhakta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhaj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: mat is the form asmad takes at the front of a compound; it is written mad before bhaktaḥ.
meaning: my devotee

## saḥ
ids: gita-12-14-w10
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Written sa: the visarga of saḥ drops before a consonant.
meaning: he

## me
ids: gita-12-14-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: me is the short form of asmad for the dative or the genitive. With priya the genitive is the usual case, and Śaṅkara points back to 7.17, where the same thought has mama. asmad has no gender.
meaning: to me

## priyaḥ
ids: gita-12-14-w12
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: priya
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
meaning: dear
