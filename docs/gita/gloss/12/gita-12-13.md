# gita-12-13

## adveṣṭā
ids: gita-12-13-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: adveṣṭṛ
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: dviṣ
note: a- (“not”) with dveṣṭṛ, the agent noun in -tṛ of dviṣ: “not a hater.” What is hated stands in the genitive.
meaning: one who does not hate

## sarva-bhūtānām
ids: gita-12-13-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-bhūta
gender: neuter
case: genitive
number: plural
root: bhū
compound: karmadhāraya
note: bhūta, “a being,” is the past participle of bhū used as a neuter noun. The ending -ānām is the same in every gender.
meaning: any being

ids: gita-12-13-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: maitra
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: From mitra, “friend,” with the first vowel strengthened: “friendly.”

## karuṇaḥ
ids: gita-12-13-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: karuṇa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: “Having karuṇā (compassion).” The visarga drops before eva.

## nirmamaḥ
ids: gita-12-13-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nirmama
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: nis (“without”) with mama, “mine,” the genitive of asmad. Written nirmamo before nirahaṃkāraḥ.
meaning: free from the notion 'mine'

## nirahaṃkāraḥ
ids: gita-12-13-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: nirahaṃkāra
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: nis (“without”) with ahaṃkāra, “the making of I.”
meaning: free from the notion 'I'

## sama-duḥkha-sukhaḥ
ids: gita-12-13-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sama-duḥkha-sukha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One for whom pain and pleasure (duḥkha-sukha) are the same (sama).”
meaning: the same in pain and pleasure

## kṣamī
ids: gita-12-13-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kṣamin
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kṣam
note: From kṣam, “to bear,” with the suffix -in. Śaṅkara glosses it “having forbearance (kṣamā).”
meaning: forbearing
