# gita-12-10

## abhyāse
ids: gita-12-10-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: abhyāsa
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
root: as
prefix: abhi
note: This as is “to throw” (asyati); with abhi, “to do again and again.” The a of api is dropped after its final e.
meaning: in practice

## api
ids: gita-12-10-w2, gita-12-10-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The first has lost its a after abhyāse and is written py before asamarthaḥ.
meaning: even

## asamarthaḥ
ids: gita-12-10-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: asamartha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: a- (“not”) with samartha, “able.” Written asamartho before the a of asi, and that a is dropped.
meaning: unable

## asi
ids: gita-12-10-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
meaning: you are

## mat-karma-paramaḥ
ids: gita-12-10-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mat-karma-parama
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “One for whom work for me (mat-karma) is the chief thing (parama).” Written -paramo before bhava.
meaning: intent on work for me

## bhava
ids: gita-12-10-w6
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
meaning: be

## mat-artham
ids: gita-12-10-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: mat with artha, “purpose,” in the neuter accusative singular, used as an adverb. It cannot agree with the plural karmāṇi. Written mad before artham.
meaning: for my sake

## karmāṇi
ids: gita-12-10-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karman
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: plural
root: kṛ
note: The neuter plural -āṇi is nominative or accusative. It is what is done, so it is accusative.
meaning: actions
