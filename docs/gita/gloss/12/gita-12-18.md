# gita-12-18

## samaḥ
ids: gita-12-18-w1, gita-12-18-w9
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sama
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: What he is the same toward, or in, stands in the locative.
meaning: the same

## śatrau
ids: gita-12-18-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śatru
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
meaning: toward an enemy

## ca
ids: gita-12-18-w3, gita-12-18-w5
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## mitre
ids: gita-12-18-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mitra
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
note: mitra, “friend,” is a neuter noun.
meaning: toward a friend

## tathā
ids: gita-12-18-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: likewise

## māna-apamānayoḥ
ids: gita-12-18-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: māna-apamāna
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: dual
compound: dvandva
note: Honour (māna) and dishonour (apamāna), a pair, so dual. The dual -ayoḥ is genitive or locative. It continues the row of śatrau and mitre, so it is locative.
meaning: in honour and dishonour

## śīta-uṣṇa-sukha-duḥkheṣu
ids: gita-12-18-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śīta-uṣṇa-sukha-duḥkha
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: plural
compound: dvandva
note: Four things joined: cold (śīta), heat (uṣṇa), pleasure (sukha) and pain (duḥkha). The gender is that of the last member.
meaning: in cold and heat, pleasure and pain

## saṅga-vivarjitaḥ
ids: gita-12-18-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: saṅga-vivarjita
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: vṛj
prefix: vi
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: vivarjita is the past participle of the causative of vi-vṛj, “left without, deprived of.” With saṅga, “attachment”: without attachment.
meaning: free from attachment
