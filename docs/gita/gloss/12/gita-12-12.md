# gita-12-12

## śreyaḥ
ids: gita-12-12-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śreyas
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
note: A comparative in -īyas, “better.” It is neuter to agree with jñānam. Written śreyo before hi.
meaning: better

## hi
ids: gita-12-12-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: indeed

## jñānam
ids: gita-12-12-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: jñā
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. No verb acts on it; it is the thing called better, so it is nominative.
meaning: knowledge

## abhyāsāt
ids: gita-12-12-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: abhyāsa
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: singular
root: as
prefix: abhi
note: Ablative of comparison, “than.” This as is “to throw” (asyati). Written abhyāsāj before jñānāt.
meaning: than practice

## jñānāt
ids: gita-12-12-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: jñāna
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
root: jñā
note: Ablative of comparison, “than.” Written jñānād before dhyānam.
meaning: than knowledge

## dhyānam
ids: gita-12-12-w6
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dhyāna
gender: neuter
case: nominative
number: singular
root: dhyai
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is the thing that excels, the subject of viśiṣyate, so it is nominative.
meaning: meditation (joined with knowledge)

## viśiṣyate
ids: gita-12-12-w7
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: śiṣ
prefix: vi
tense: present
voice: passive
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Passive in form, “is set apart,” and so “excels.” What it surpasses stands in the ablative.
meaning: is superior

## dhyānāt
ids: gita-12-12-w8
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dhyāna
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
root: dhyai
note: Ablative of comparison, “than.”
meaning: than meditation

## karma-phala-tyāgaḥ
ids: gita-12-12-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: karma-phala-tyāga
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: tyaj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “The giving up (tyāga) of the fruit (phala) of action (karma).” Written -tyāgas before tyāgāt.
meaning: giving up the fruit of action

## tyāgāt
ids: gita-12-12-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: tyāga
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: singular
root: tyaj
note: Ablative of what peace comes from. Written tyāgāc before śāntiḥ, whose ś then becomes ch.
meaning: from that giving up

## śāntiḥ
ids: gita-12-12-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śānti
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: śam
note: Written chāntir: the ś becomes ch after tyāgāc, and the visarga becomes r before anantaram.
meaning: peace (the end of saṃsāra and its cause)

## anantaram
ids: gita-12-12-w12
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: The neuter accusative singular of anantara, “with nothing between,” used as an adverb.
meaning: at once
