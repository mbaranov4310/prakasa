# gita-12-11

## atha
ids: gita-12-11-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Its final a and the e of etat join into ai: athaitat.
meaning: but if

## etat
ids: gita-12-11-w2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: etad
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter etat is nominative or accusative. It is what he is unable to do, so it is accusative. Written etad before api.
meaning: this

## api
ids: gita-12-11-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written apy before aśaktaḥ.
meaning: even

## aśaktaḥ
ids: gita-12-11-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: aśakta
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: śak
note: a- (“not”) with śakta, the past participle of śak. Written aśakto before the a of asi, and that a is dropped.
meaning: unable

## asi
ids: gita-12-11-w5
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
meaning: you are

## kartum
ids: gita-12-11-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
note: An infinitive in -tum.
meaning: to do

## mat-yogam
ids: gita-12-11-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mat-yoga
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: yuj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: mat is the form asmad takes at the front of a compound; it is written mad before yogam.
meaning: my yoga (doing actions as an offering to me)

## āśritaḥ
ids: gita-12-11-w8
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: śri
prefix: ā
participle: past
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The past participle of ā-śri is active in sense, “having resorted to,” and takes an accusative. Śaṅkara reads it with the command kuru, not with the “if” clause.
meaning: relying on

## sarva-karma-phala-tyāgam
ids: gita-12-11-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarva-karma-phala-tyāga
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: tyaj
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: “The giving up (tyāga) of the fruit (phala) of all actions (sarva-karma).”
meaning: giving up the fruit of all actions

## tataḥ
ids: gita-12-11-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: then

## kuru
ids: gita-12-11-w11
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
class: tan
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
meaning: do

## yata-ātmavān
ids: gita-12-11-w12
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: yata-ātmavat
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: yata, “held in check” (past participle of yam), with ātmavat, “having a self,” here the mind. Śaṅkara glosses it “with the mind restrained.”
meaning: self-controlled
