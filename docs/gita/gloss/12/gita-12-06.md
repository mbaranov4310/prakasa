## saṃnyasya
ids: gita-12-06-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
root: as
prefix: sam-ni
note: An absolutive in -ya. This as is “to throw” (asyati), not “to be”; with sam-ni, “to lay down, give up.”
meaning: having given up

## mat-parāḥ
ids: gita-12-06-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mat-para
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: “Those for whom I (mat) am the highest (para).” mat is the form asmad takes at the front of a compound.
meaning: holding me as the supreme

## ananyena
ids: gita-12-06-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: ananya
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: an- (“not”) with anya, “other.” Śaṅkara unpacks it as “having no other support.” It agrees with yogena. Its final a and the e of eva join into ai: ananyenaiva.
meaning: that has no other object
