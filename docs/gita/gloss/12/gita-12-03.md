# gita-12-03

## ye
ids: gita-12-03-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
meaning: those who

## tu
ids: gita-12-03-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written tv before akṣaram.
meaning: but

## akṣaram
ids: gita-12-03-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: akṣara
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: The neuter -am is nominative or accusative. It is what they contemplate, so it is accusative.
meaning: the Imperishable

## anirdeśyam
ids: gita-12-03-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: anirdeśya
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: diś
prefix: nir
note: a- (“not”) with nirdeśya, the potential participle in -ya of nir-diś: “not to be pointed out.” It agrees with akṣaram.
meaning: indefinable (words cannot point to it)

## avyaktam
ids: gita-12-03-w5
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: avyakta
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
note: a- (“not”) with vyakta, “made manifest.” It agrees with akṣaram.
meaning: unmanifest (no means of knowledge reveals it)

## paryupāsate

judgment: obvious
root: ās
prefix: pari-upa
class: ad
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 3rd
number: plural
meaning: contemplate steadily

## sarvatra-gam
ids: gita-12-03-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sarvatra-ga
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: gam
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: sarvatra (“everywhere”) and -ga, “going,” from gam: “going everywhere.” It agrees with akṣaram.
meaning: all-pervading (like space)

## acintyam
ids: gita-12-03-w8

case: accusative
number: singular
root: cint
note: a- (“not”) with cintya, the potential participle in -ya of cint: “not to be thought.” It agrees with akṣaram.
meaning: unthinkable

## ca
ids: gita-12-03-w9
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and

## kūṭa-stham
ids: gita-12-03-w10
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: kūṭa-stha
gender: neuter
case: accusative
number: singular
root: sthā
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: -stha, “standing,” from sthā: “standing in kūṭa.” Śaṅkara takes kūṭa as māyā, over which it stands as witness; his second sense is “standing like a heap.” It agrees with akṣaram.
meaning: unchanging (the witness seated in māyā)
