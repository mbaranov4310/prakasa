# Dhanvantari

## Proposal

Devanagari:

शङ्खं चक्रं जलौकां दधदमृतघटं चारुदोर्भिश्चतुर्भिः ।
सूक्ष्मस्वच्छातिहृद्यांशुक परिविलसन्मौलिमम्भोजनेत्रम् ।
कालाम्भोदोज्ज्वलाङ्गं कटितटविलसच्चारुपीताम्बराढ्यम् ।
वन्दे धन्वन्तरिं तं निखिलगदवनप्रौढदावाग्निलीलम् ॥

IAST:

śaṅkhaṃ cakraṃ jalaukāṃ dadhad amṛtaghaṭaṃ cārudorbhiś caturbhiḥ |
sūkṣmasvacchātihṛdyāṃśuka parivilasanmaulim ambhojanetram |
kālāmbhodojjvalāṅgaṃ kaṭitaṭavilasac cārupītāmbarāḍhyam |
vande dhanvantariṃ taṃ nikhilagadavanaprauḍhadāvāgnilīlam ||

Padaccheda: no traditional padapāṭha found. Proposed from the compounds (this is śārdūlavikrīḍita, one śloka, four long pādas — same job as Viṣṇu’s `शान्ताकारं`):

शङ्खं चक्रं जलौकां दधत्-अमृत-घटं चारु-दोर्भिः चतुर्भिः । सूक्ष्म-स्वच्छ-अति-हृद्य-अंशुक परि-विलसत्-मौलिम् अम्भोज-नेत्रम् । काल-अम्भोद-उज्ज्वल-अङ्गं कटि-तट-विलसत्-चारु-पीताम्बर-आढ्यम् । वन्दे धन्वन्तरिं तं निखिल-गद-वन-प्रौढ-दावाग्नि-लीलम् ॥

Translation: “I bow to that Dhanvantari who holds, in his four beautiful arms, the conch, the discus, a leech, and the pot of nectar; whose diadem shines with a fine, pure, lovely cloth, whose eyes are lotuses; whose body gleams like a dark rain-cloud, whose hips are adorned with a beautiful yellow garment; whose play is a raging wildfire that burns down the whole forest of diseases.”

Function: dhyānaśloka that ends in namaskāra (`वन्दे`). The pīta-vastra / amṛta-kalaśa visualization people extract as the Dhanvantari verse (mantra-paddhati dhyāna; Śrīvaiṣṇava print treats this one śloka as the whole stotra). Heavier than वक्रतुण्ड (same metre-family as `शान्ताकारं` for Viṣṇu), not a kavaca.

Names searched: धन्वन्तरि, धन्वन्तरी, अब्ज, अमृतधर, औषधिपति; Tamil தன்வந்திரி; Telugu ధన్వంతరి; Malayalam ധന്വന്തരി; queries श्लोक, प्रार्थना, ध्यान श्लोक, नमस्कार, स्तुति, स्तोत्रम्, दैनिक / नित्य, धन्वन्तरि जयन्ती / धनतेरस / धनत्रयोदशी, आयुर्वेद. अब्ज is a Purāṇa name (Hari-vaṃśa: Viṣṇu addresses the ocean-born physician as Abja) — no short greeting turned up under अब्ज / अमृतधर / औषधिपति as incipits.

## Claims

(a) exists: yes — one śārdūlavikrīḍita dhyāna, printed as the dhyānam of the Dhanvantari mantra and, alone, as a stotra
(b) traditionally used: yes — mantra-paddhati dhyāna (printed mantra books collated on Sanskrit Documents); Śrīraṅgam Āṇḍavan / Prapatti pāṭha extracts this verse as the entire stotra; Stotra Nidhi and Vaidika Vignanam keep it in the nitya-mantra dump
(c) standard short prayer: unclear — this is the extracted dhyāna people actually say as *the* Dhanvantari verse; the spoken hello in homes and Ayurveda japa is often the mūla mantra (`ॐ नमो भगवते … धन्वन्तरये अमृतकलशहस्ताय…`), not a śloka; vaidyas also nitya-recite Bhāgavata 8.8.31–35 as a “Dhanvantari stotra”
Confidence in (c): medium

## Variants

Do not splice.

- `जलौकां` (Prapatti, Stotra Nidhi, Vignanam; popular) vs `जलूकां` (Sanskrit Documents’ printed mantra books). Both mean leech.
- `चारुदोर्भिः` (popular) vs `चापि दोर्भिः` (some printed books; Sanskrit Documents)
- Second pāda: `सूक्ष्मस्वच्छातिहृद्यांशुक परिविलसन्मौलिम्` (अंशुक = cloth; lead) vs printed-book split `सूक्ष्मस्वच्छातिहृद्यां शुकपरिविलसन्मौलिम्` and a further parenthesis `सूक्ष्मं स्वच्छन्दहृद्यं कुशपरिविलसन्मौलिम्` (Sanskrit Documents). Do not mix.
- `प्रौढदावाग्निलीलम्` (almost all) vs `वातदावाग्निलीलम्` (one printed-book reading)
- `पीताम्बराढ्यम्` (pāda-final ṃ) vs `पीताम्बराढ्यं` sandhi into `वन्दे` in some Stotra Nidhi dumps
- Optional `ॐ` prefixed (Prapatti); not in the mantra-book dhyāna or Vignanam
- Orthography only: `शङ्खं` / `शंखं`; `अङ्गं` / `अंगं`; `आढ्यम्` / `आढ्यं`

A different long dhyāna, same iconography, must not be merged: `शङ्खं चक्रमुपर्यधश्च करयोर्दिव्यौषधं दक्षिणे वामेनान्यकरेण सम्भृतसुधाकुम्भं जलौकावलिम् । … भगवान्धन्वन्तरिः पातु नः ॥` (Sanskrit Documents श्रीधन्वन्तरि ध्यानम्, VSM; https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_vishhnu/dhanvantaridhyAnam.html). Hands and metre differ.

## Other candidates

- `अच्युतानन्त गोविन्द विष्णो नारायणाऽमृत । रोगान्मे नाशयाऽशेषानाशु धन्वन्तरे हरे ॥ आरोग्यं दीर्घमायुष्यं बलं तेजो धियं श्रियं । स्वभक्तेभ्योऽनुगृह्णन्तं वन्दे धन्वन्तरिं हरिम् ॥` — two anuṣṭubhs, vakratuṇḍa-sized prārthanā. Stotra Nidhi prints this as the *primary* ध्यानम् of the mahāmantra; Vignanam the same, then the proposal verse, then फलश्रुति `धन्वन्तरेरिमं श्लोकं भक्त्या नित्यं पठन्ति ये । अनारोग्यं न तेषां स्यात् सुखं जीवन्ति ते चिरम् ॥`. Closest length-rival. Not led because (1) printed mantra books and the Āṇḍavan extract use the pīta-vastra dhyāna, (2) the first pāda is easily confused with the *different* Viṣṇu nāma-auṣadha `अच्युतानन्तगोविन्द नामोच्चारणभेषजात् । नश्यन्ति सकला रोगाः…` — do not splice those. Sanskrit Documents’ composite धन्वन्तरिस्तोत्रम् keeps only the first of these two anuṣṭubhs (https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_vishhnu/dhanvantarIstotram.html).
- Bhāgavata 8.8.31–35: `अथोदधेर्मथ्यमानात्काश्यपैरमृतार्थिभिः । उदतिष्ठन्महाराज पुरुषः परमाद्भुतः ॥ दीर्घपीवरदोर्दण्डः कम्बुग्रीवोऽरुणेक्षणः । श्यामलस्तरुणः स्रग्वी सर्वाभरणभूषितः ॥ पीतवासा महोरस्कः सुमृष्टमणिकुण्डलः । स्निग्धकुञ्चितकेशान्तः सुभगः सिंहविक्रमः ॥ अमृतापूर्णकलशं बिभ्रद्वलयभूषितः । स वै भगवतः साक्षाद् विष्णोरंशांशसम्भवः ॥ धन्वन्तरिरिति ख्यात आयुर्वेददृगिज्यभाक् ।` — the Purāṇa pīta-vastra / amṛta-kalaśa darśana. Dhanteras paddhati prārthanā; वैद्य nitya-pāṭha according to the चरित्रकोश. Four–five anuṣṭubhs, over the 1–2 śloka yardstick; narrative (`महाराज`) is Śuka to Parīkṣit, not a child’s namaskāra. Sanskrit Documents stotra recasts the same block with `एवं धन्वन्तरिं ध्यायेत् साधकोऽभीष्टसिद्धये` and a `नीलकुञ्चित` vs `स्निग्धकुञ्चित` variant — keep as Bhāgavata, do not splice the stotra glue into the mūla.
- `देवान् कृशानसुरसंघनिपीडिताङ्गान् दृष्ट्वा दयालुरमृतं विपरीतुकामः । पाथोधिमन्थनविधौ प्रकटोऽभवद्यो धन्वन्तरिः स भगवानवतात् सदा नः ॥` — TransLiteral धनत्रयोदशी पूजन-विधि dhyāna before षोडशोपचार (https://www.transliteral.org/pages/z101101211644/view). One śloka, paddhati. Only this print found; `विपरीतुकामः` looks like a slip for `वितरीतुकामः`. Do not lead on a single recension.
- `क्षीरोदमथनोद्भूतं दिव्यगन्धानुलेपिनम् । सुधाकलशहस्तं तं वन्दे धन्वन्तरिं हरिम् ॥` — perfect one-anuṣṭubh namaskāra with sudhā-kalaśa. Found inside the Sanskrit Documents composite stotra, not independently as a children’s / paddhati hello in this hunt.
- `ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय धन्वन्तरये अमृतकलशहस्ताय [वज्रजलौकहस्ताय] सर्वामयविनाशनाय त्रैलोक्यनाथाय श्रीमहाविष्णवे स्वाहा ॥` — the living japa hello (Stotra Nidhi, Vignanam, Ārṣa Vidyā Gurukulam: https://arshavidya.org/dhanvantari-maha-mantra/). Sanskrit Documents’ printed books give a shorter 41-akṣara cousin: `ॐ नमो भगवते धन्वन्तरये अमृतकलशहस्ताय सर्वामयविनाशनाय त्रिलोकनाथाय विष्णवे स्वाहा ॥`. Mantra, not a śloka of this book.
- Longer mantra पाठान्तर: `ॐ नमो भगवते महासुदर्शनाय वासुदेवाय धन्वन्तरये अमृतकलशहस्ताय सर्वभयविनाशाय सर्वरोगनिवारणाय त्रैलोक्यपतये त्रैलोक्यनिधये श्रीमहाविष्णुस्वरूप श्रीधन्वन्तरीस्वरूप श्री श्री श्री औषधचक्र नारायणाय स्वाहा ॥` — same collections; still not a śloka.
- Dhanvantari gāyatrī: `ॐ वासुदेवाय विद्महे सुधाहस्ताय धीमहि । तन्नो धन्वन्तरिः प्रचोदयात् ॥` and tāraka `ॐ धं धन्वन्तरये नमः` — Vignanam / Stotra Nidhi. Japa, not the hello-śloka.
- Aṣṭāṅgahṛdaya 1.1 `रागादिरोगान् सततानुषक्तान् अशेषकायप्रसृतानशेषान् । औत्सुक्यमोहारतिदाञ्जघान योऽपूर्ववैद्याय नमोऽस्तु तस्मै ॥` — Ayurveda pāṭhaśālā maṅgalācaraṇa (Vāgbhaṭa). The mūla does not name Dhanvantari; modern college notes often identify `अपूर्ववैद्य` with him. This hunt did not read Aruṇadatta / Hemādri naming him. Keep off the lead.
- `शरीरे जर्जरीभूते व्याधिग्रस्ते कलेवरे । औषधं जाह्नवीतोयं वैद्यो नारायणो हरिः ॥` and `अयं मे हस्तो भगवान्…` — Ayurveda / Atharvan healing maxims in the composite stotra; they name Nārāyaṇa / the hand, not Dhanvantari.

## Uncertainties

- No Purāṇa / āgama / well-known bhāṣya verse-number for the proposed śārdūlavikrīḍita. Strong as a living mantra-dhyāna and Śrīvaiṣṇava extract, not as a pinned mūla. Bhāgavata 8.8 is the Purāṇa iconography — a different text.
- (c) is medium because three tracks compete: this dhyāna, the two-anuṣṭubh `अच्युतानन्त` prārthanā, and the mūla mantra (plus vaidya nitya-pāṭha of Bhāgavata 8.8). No children’s pāṭha found.
- Padaccheda is proposed, not a traditional padapāṭha. `अंशुक` vs `शुकपरि` is a real print fork; lead keeps cloth-on-the-crown.
- `जलौका` (leech) is the Ayurvedic emblem in this dhyāna; some popular pages paraphrase it away — not followed.
- Dhanteras / धन्वन्तरि जयन्ती paddhati on TransLiteral opens with a *different* one-śloka dhyāna and closes with Bhāgavata, not with the proposal. Festival use is not uniform.
- Name अब्ज is Purāṇic; अमृतधर / औषधिपति did not yield an independent short śloka in this hunt (gāyatrī has सुधाहस्त; aṣṭottara has सुधापूर्णकलशाढ्यकर).
