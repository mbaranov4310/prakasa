# Gaṅgā

Names searched: गङ्गा, भागीरथी, जाह्नवी, त्रिपथगा, मन्दाकिनी, गङ्गा देवी; Hindi गंगा जी; Tamil கங்கை / கங்கா; Telugu గంగ / గంగా దేవి. Queries: श्लोक, प्रार्थना, ध्यान, नमस्कार, स्तुति, स्तोत्रम्, दैनिक / स्नान / घाट, नित्य पारायण, बाल पाठ; Tamil தினசரி பிரார்த்தனை / நமஸ்காரம்; Telugu శ్లోకం / స్నాన మంత్రం.

There is no वक्रतुण्ड-shaped children’s hello that turned up in bāla-pāṭha. The spoken greeting is often nāma (`हर हर गङ्गे` / `जय माँ गंगे` / `ॐ गङ्गायै नमः`). Below is the one-śloka namaskāra people extract at the ghāṭ / snāna, not the fourteen-verse stotra.

## Proposal

Devanagari:

विष्णुपादाब्जसम्भूते गङ्गे त्रिपथगामिनि ।
धर्मद्रवेति विख्याते पापं मे हर जाह्नवि ॥

IAST:

viṣṇupādābjasambhūte gaṅge tripathagāmini |
dharmadraveti vikhyāte pāpaṃ me hara jāhnavi ||

Padaccheda: no traditional padapāṭha found. Proposed from grammar (all five -e/-ini forms are feminine vocative):

विष्णु-पाद-अब्ज-सम्भूते गङ्गे त्रि-पथ-गामिनि । धर्म-द्रव इति विख्याते पापं मे हर जाह्नवि ॥

Word gloss:
- विष्णु — Viṣṇu
- पाद — feet
- अब्ज — lotus
- सम्भूते — O you born from
- गङ्गे — O Gaṅgā
- त्रि — three
- पथ — paths
- गामिनि — O you who course
- धर्म — dharma
- द्रव — liquid
- इति — as / thus
- विख्याते — O you who are famed
- पापम् — sin
- मे — my
- हर — take away (2sg imperative of हृ)
- जाह्नवि — O Jāhnavī (daughter of Jahnu)

Translation: “O Gaṅgā, born from Viṣṇu’s lotus-feet, who courses the three paths, famed as dharma in liquid form — take away my sin, O Jāhnavī.”

Function: prārthanā / snāna-namaskāra (the ghāṭ dip and home-bath Gaṅgā-āvāhana). Anuṣṭubh; same length as वक्रतुण्ड.

## Claims

(a) exists: yes — four-pāda snāna anuṣṭubh in Purāṇa stotra sequence and in living paddhati/āvāhana prints
(b) traditionally used: yes — snāna / yajña-āvāhana; Skanda Daśaharā recitation; Gayatri Parivar āvāhana as *the* Gaṅgā verse
(c) standard short prayer: unclear — yes as the one-śloka people extract at water; no as a school-child hello like वक्रतुण्ड (that job is nāma, or the Śaṅkara opening)
Confidence in (c): medium

## Sources

Hindu sources only. Count: 5 (strong as a circulating snāna verse; recension split धर्म / ब्रह्म — see Variants; do not treat the split as two prayers)

2. All World Gayatri Parivar (AWGP), यज्ञ का ज्ञान विज्ञान, देवावाहनम्, Gaṅgā āvāhana verse 19 — https://www.awgp.org/en/literature/book/yagya_ka_gyan_vigyan/v1.28 — same recension as the lead (`धर्मद्रवेति`), used as the standalone Gaṅgā verse in a sampradāya paddhati
3. Padma Purāṇa, Sṛṣṭikhaṇḍa, Gaṅgāmāhātmya (Sanskrit pāṭha colophon: प्रथमे सृष्टिखण्डे गङ्गामाहात्म्यं नाम द्विषष्टितमोऽध्यायः) — the same four pādas with `धर्मद्रवेति`, but split across two consecutive ślokas (`दिवि भुव्यन्तरिक्षे… त्रिपथगामिनि` then `धर्मद्रवेति… विष्णुपूजिता`); the chapter itself says to bathe with these ślokas (`त्रिभिः श्लोकवरैरेभिर्यः स्नायाज्जाह्नवी जले`). Not a Gītā Press page-collation; do not invent a verse-number for the extracted four-pāda unit
4. Godham Pathmeda (Hindu āśrama), posted as a standalone snāna line with `हर हर गङ्गे` — https://godhampathmeda.org/news/458 — same recension as the lead (`धर्मद्रवेति`)
5. TransLiteral Foundations, स्नानकी विधि — https://www.transliteral.org/pages/z181125195422/view — not this anuṣṭubh; the related twelve-name snāna smaraṇa (below). Counted as paddhati witness that Gaṅgā-snāna has a fixed short recitation, not as a second copy of the lead verse

Also seen (not counted separately): Kailāśa Siddhāśrama snāna print of the Padma three-śloka set starting from `विष्णुपादाब्जसम्भूते` (grammatical drift `त्रिपथगामिनी` / `विख्याता`); Hindi ārati blogs repeating `नमामि गङ्गे`. Twenty copies of one blog are one source.

## Variants

Do not splice.

- `धर्मद्रवेति` (lead; AWGP, Pathmeda, Padma sequence) vs `ब्रह्मद्रवेति` (Skanda Daśaharā v. 38 as a complete anuṣṭubh). Same prayer; different third-pāda word. Do not mix `धर्म` into a Skanda verse-number or `ब्रह्म` into the Padma split
- `विख्याते` (vocative; lead) vs `विख्याता` (Kailāśa Siddhāśrama snāna dump)
- `त्रिपथगामिनि` (vocative of -गामिनी; lead) vs `त्रिपथगामिनी` (same dump)
- `सम्भूते` vs `संभूते` (orthography)
- Optional `हर हर गङ्गे` prefixed (Pathmeda) — nāma, not part of the anuṣṭubh
- Padma does *not* print this as one isolated śloka. The preceding pāda is `दिवि भुव्यन्तरिक्षे च तानि ते सन्ति जाह्नवि` and the following is `विष्णुपादप्रसूता त्वं वैष्णवी विष्णुपूजिता`. Do not graft those pādas onto the lead
- Do not splice this vocative anuṣṭubh with the twelve-name smaraṇa (`विष्णुपादाब्जसम्भूता गङ्गा त्रिपथगामिनी` — nominative, Gaṅgā speaking)

## Other candidates

- `देवि सुरेश्वरि भगवति गङ्गे त्रिभुवनतारिणि तरलतरङ्गे । शङ्करमौलिविहारिणि विमले मम मतिरास्तां तव पदकमले ॥` — verse 1 of the fourteen-verse श्रीगङ्गास्तोत्रम् traditionally ascribed to Śaṅkara (colophon: शङ्करसेवकशङ्कररचितम्). Same recension: Sanskrit Documents https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_devii/gangastotra.html ; Stotra Nidhi Hindi / English / Tamil / Telugu (and their Prabhāta Stotranidhi book) https://stotranidhi.com/hi/ganga-stotram-in-sanskrit/ ; Vaidika Vignanam https://vignanam.org/samskritam/ganga-stotram.html ; Kāñcī Kāmakoṭi Pīṭham lists गङ्गा स्तोत्रम् among Śaṅkara’s river hymns. Pajjhaṭikā (the stotra names its own metre in v. 13). The verse people *recite* as Gaṅgā-stuti; first among likely candidates, not crowned. Full stotra is the wrong size; this is the extract. Vignanam prints vocative marks (`देवि! सुरेश्वरि!`)
- `नन्दिनी नलिनी सीता मालती च महापगा । विष्णुपादाब्जसम्भूता गङ्गा त्रिपथगामिनी ॥ भागीरथी भोगवती जाह्नवी त्रिदशेश्वरी । द्वादशैतानि नामानि यत्र यत्र जलाशये ॥ स्नानोद्यतः स्मरेन्नित्यं तत्र तत्र वसाम्यहम् ॥` — twelve-name snāna smaraṇa. TransLiteral स्नानकी विधि; Ācāraprakāśa / Ācārendu (cited on Vyākhyā.org). Gaṅgā speaking: remember these names, I dwell in that water. Real paddhati; ~2½ anuṣṭubhs; nominative list, not a vocative hello. Variant `महापगा` / `मलापहा`; `स्मरेन्नित्यं` / `पठेयस्तु`
- `नमामि गङ्गे तव पादपङ्कजं सुरासुरैर्वन्दितदिव्यरूपम् । भुक्तिं च मुक्तिं च ददासि नित्यं भावानुसारेण सदा नराणाम् ॥` — Hindi Ganga-mātā ārati opening (Vyākhyā.org quotes it next to the snāna verses; festival ārati pages). Not in Stotra Nidhi / Sanskrit Documents / Vignanam as a pinned śloka. (a) as ārati; not the Sanskrit hello
- `गङ्गे च यमुने चैव गोदावरि सरस्वति । नर्मदे सिन्धु कावेरि जलेऽस्मिन् सन्निधिं कुरु ॥` — the nitya snāna āvāhana of seven rivers. Vaidika Vignanam नित्य पारायण; Kāmakoṭi Kumbh message. Used everywhere, including Tamil கங்கே ச யமுநே. Not Gaṅgā-only
- `गङ्गा गङ्गेति यो ब्रूयाद्योजनानां शतैरपि । मुच्यते सर्वपापेभ्यो विष्णुलोकं स गच्छति ॥` — nāma-mahimā. Skanda Daśaharā v. 40 (same stotra as the lead’s cousin); Kāmakoṭi Jagadguru Kumbh message. About *saying* her name, not a greeting *to* her (same problem as राम रामेति)
- `नमः शिवायै गङ्गायै शिवदायै नमो नमः । नमस्ते रुद्ररूपिण्यै शाङ्कर्यै ते नमो नमः ॥` — Daśaharā opening (Sanskrit Documents v. 1). Genuine namaskāra; first of a long stotra, not independently the hello
- `गाङ्गं वारि मनोहारि मुरारिचरणच्युतम् । त्रिपुरारिशिरश्चारि पापहारि पुनातु माम् ॥` — Vālmīki गङ्गाष्टकम् v. 7 (Sanskrit Documents). Compact water-blessing; names मुरारि / त्रिपुरारि not गङ्गे. Sometimes printed as a preamble before the Śaṅkara stotra. Not the vocative hello
- `भगवति तव तीरे नीरमात्राशनोऽहं… देवि गङ्गे प्रसीद` — Śaṅkara गङ्गाष्टकम् (Stotra Nidhi; Sanskrit Documents; Kāmakoṭi lists Gaṅgāṣṭakam). Eight-plus verses, mālinī / long metres. Wrong size
- `मातः शैलसुतासपत्नि… भागीरथीं प्रार्थये` — Vālmīki aṣṭaka opening. Śārdūlavikrīḍita; eight verses
- `ॐ जय गंगे माता` — living Hindi ārati, not a Sanskrit śloka
- `ॐ गङ्गायै नमः` / `हर हर गङ्गे` — living nāma; Stotra Nidhi aṣṭottara opens ओं गङ्गायै नमः । ओं विष्णुपादसम्भूतायै नमः

Long forms not proposed: Brahmavaivarta विष्णुपदी / गङ्गास्तोत्र (Sanskrit Documents); Kalki-purāṇa गङ्गास्तव (Stotra Nidhi); Kanva / oṣadhi Gaṅgā stotras in Brahma Purāṇa; 108 names.

## Uncertainties

- No bāla-pāṭha / school-reader Gaṅgā śloka turned up. Tamil and Telugu prints of a short hello are the Śaṅkara stotra (or the seven-river snāna verse), not a separate regional four-pāda
- (c) is medium because three real shorts compete: this ghāṭ anuṣṭubh, the Śaṅkara opening, and the twelve-name paddhati list — and the everyday greeting is often nāma
- `धर्मद्रवेति` vs `ब्रह्मद्रवेति` is a genuine recension, not a typo. Lead follows the living āvāhana; keep Skanda’s `ब्रह्म` in Variants
- Padma chapter numbering is from a circulating Sanskrit pāṭha, not a Gītā Press page check. The four-pāda *extract* is not “Padma verse N”
- Śaṅkara ascription of the fourteen-verse stotra is traditional (pīṭha lists, stotra colophon); not verified here against a critical edition
- Padaccheda of the lead is proposed, not from a traditional padapāṭha
