# Varuṇa

## Proposal

Devanagari:

नमो नमस्ते स्फटिकप्रभाय सुश्वेतहाराय सुमङ्गलाय ।
सुपाशहस्ताय झषासनाय जलाधिनाथाय नमो नमस्ते ॥

IAST:

namo namaste sphaṭikaprabhāya suśvetahārāya sumaṅgalāya |
supāśahastāya jhaṣāsanāya jalādhināthāya namo namaste ||

Padaccheda: no traditional padapāṭha found. Proposed from the dative vocatives in the paddhati line:

नमः नमः ते स्फटिक-प्रभाय सु-श्वेत-हाराय सु-मङ्गलाय । सु-पाश-हस्ताय झष-आसनाय जल-अधि-नाथाय नमः नमः ते ॥

Word gloss:
- नमो नमस्ते (नमः + नमः + ते) — salutation again and again to you
- स्फटिकप्रभाय — to the one whose radiance is like crystal
- सुश्वेतहाराय — to the one with a very white garland / necklace
- सुमङ्गलाय — to the highly auspicious one
- सुपाशहस्ताय — to the one who holds a noose well in the hand
- झषासनाय — to the one whose seat is a jhaṣa (fish / sea-creature)
- जलाधिनाथाय — to the overlord of the waters
- नमो नमस्ते — salutation again to you

Translation: “Salutation again and again to you, crystal-radiant, wearing a pure-white garland, highly auspicious; salutation to you who holds the noose, whose seat is the sea-creature, overlord of the waters.”

Function: namaskāra (closing Varuṇa namaskāra in kalasha-sthāpana prārthanā; some Maithil / prayoga pages print it in a dhyāna block). One verse, four pādas of eleven syllables (triṣṭubh), still the 1–2 line greeting length.

Names searched: वरुण, अपांपति / अपां पति, प्रचेतस्, जलदेव, वरुणदेव; Tamil வருணன்; Telugu వరుణుడు; Kannada ವರುಣ; Malayalam വരുണൻ; Hindi / Marathi / Bengali वरुण देव, বরুণ. Queries: श्लोक, प्रार्थना, ध्यान श्लोक, नमस्कार, स्तुति, स्तोत्रम्, दैनिक प्रार्थना / नित्य प्रार्थना / बाल पाठ, and the same in those languages.

## Claims

(a) exists: yes — one-verse namaskāra in North Indian / Marathi kalasha-sthāpana and in Maithil / Vedic-prayoga Varuṇa prārthanā
(b) traditionally used: yes, narrowly — recited to Varuṇa in the pot at kalasha-sthāpana / ṣoḍaśopacāra close; not shown as a pāṭhaśālā or school-walk greeting
(c) standard short prayer: no — there is no vakratuṇḍa-grade independently memorized hello for this deity
Confidence in (c): low

The living short formula in the same rites is `ॐ अपां पतये वरुणाय नमः` (nāma, not a śloka). Vedic Varuṇa ṛks and Sandhyā water-mantras are a different collection.

## Variants

Do not splice.

- Spacing / sandhi only: `स्फटिकप्रभाय` vs `स्फटिक प्रभाय`; `नमो नमस्ते स्फटिक` vs `नमो नमस्तेस्फटिकप्रभाय`; `सुपाशहस्ताय` vs `सुपाश हस्ताय`; `जलाधिनाथाय` vs `जलाधि नाथाय`
- `सुश्वेतहाराय` (lead; TransLiteral Hindi, Mithila, D.P.) vs `सुश्‍वेतहाराय` (TransLiteral Marathi orthography of the same word)
- Corrupt cousins on mantra-blogs: `सुश्वेताराय`, `सुपशहस्ताय`, `झशासनाय` — do not mix into the lead
- Optional `ॐ` prefixed on some dhyāna pages
- Some prints treat this as verse 5 of a multi-verse कलश-प्रार्थना (`देवदानवसंवादे` 1–4, this namaskāra 5, `पाशपाणे` 6). That is sequence, not a variant of these four pādas.

Do not splice the namaskāra with `देवदानवसंवादे…`, `पाशपाणे नमस्तुभ्यं…`, or `ॐ अपां पतये वरुणाय नमः`.

## Other candidates

- `येन प्रसूयते सर्वं यस्मिन् सर्वं प्रलीयते । वरुणाय जलेशाय पाशहस्ताय ते नमः ॥` — clean anuṣṭubh namaskāra. Īśānaśivagurudevapaddhati, kriyāpāda paṭala 49, verse 116 (Śaiva mahotsava dikpāla-bali: `ॐ वरुणाय जलाधिपतये सायुधवाहनपरिवाराय रुद्रपार्षदे नमः` then this śloka) — https://vishvasa.github.io/AgamaH/AryaH/hinduism/sAmya-vaiShamye/kriyA/sangrahaH/IshAna-shivaH/3_kriyA-pAdaH/49/ . Sanskrit Documents अष्टदिक्पालकस्तुतिः verse 5 is the same octet extracted — https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_deities_misc/aShTadikpAlakastutiH.html — not an independent second mūla. Why not lead: it is the western-guardian slot in an eight-god hymn, not a free-standing Varuṇa hello.
- `नागपाशधरं हृष्टं रक्तौघद्युतिविग्रहम् । शशाङ्कधवलं ध्यायेत् वरुणं मकरासनम् ॥` — अष्टदिक्पालकस्तोत्रम् verse 5 (Sanskrit Documents; different text from the stuti above). Iconographic dhyāna, excerpt.
- `पाशपाणे नमस्तुभ्यं पद्मिनीजीवनायक । पुण्याहवाचनं यावत् तावत् त्वं सुस्थिरो भव ॥` (variant last pāda: `सान्निध्यं कुरु मे देव प्रसन्नो भव सर्वदा`) — TransLiteral पुण्याहवाचन labels this वरुण प्रार्थना; hariharharatmak same. Right length, actually used as a one-śloka address. Why not lead: the common second pāda is “stay in the pot until puṇyāhavācana is done,” a rite-stay request, not a daily hello.
- `वरुणः पाशभृत् सौम्यः प्रतीच्यां मकराश्रयः । पाशहस्तात्मको देवो जलराश्याधिपो महान् ॥` — TransLiteral Hindi kalasha prārthanā. Iconography only; no नमामि / नमः.
- `वरुणं सततं वन्दे सुधाकलशधारिणम् । पाशहस्तं शतभिषग्देवतां देववन्दितम् ॥` — Śatabhiṣā-nakṣatra namaskāra on a Hindu nakṣatra page (yugsamvad). Narrow, not the general greeting.
- `ॐ अपां पतये वरुणाय नमः` / `ॐ वरुणाय नमः` / `ॐ वं वरुणाय नमः` — the formulas people actually say (kalasha āvāhana, Āpastamba-line Varuṇa pūjā nāma-arcana: वरुणाय / प्रचेतसे / अपां पतये / मकरवाहनाय / जलाधिपतये / पाशहस्ताय). Nāma, not a śloka of this book.
- Varuṇa gāyatrī `ॐ जलबिम्बाय विद्महे नीलपुरुषाय धीमहि । तन्नो वरुणः प्रचोदयात् ॥` — nānāvidha-gāyatrī lists and rain-japa pages (Tamil dheivegam and Hindi mantra dumps). Mantra, not a maṅgala-śloka.
- `इमं मे वरुण श्रुधी हवमद्या च मृळय । त्वामवस्युरा चके ॥` and `तत्त्वा यामि ब्रह्मणा वन्दमानः…` — Ṛgveda (Śunaḥśepa); daily in sāyam Sandhyā upasthāna and as kalasha āvāhana. Vedic; the job asked to keep these in the other collection unless a ṛk is independently the daily hello. It is the Sandhyā Varuṇa ṛk, not a children’s greeting.
- `उदुत्तमं वरुणपाशमस्मद्…` / `कितवासो यद्रिरिपुर्न दीवि…` — Varuṇa-sūkta / kṣamā ṛks; Vedic, too long as a set, Sandhyā / puṇyāha track.
- झूलेलाल चालीसा (`जय जय जल देवता…`) — Sindhi vernacular to Jhūlelāl as Varuṇa-avatāra; wrong size and language.
- वरुणकृत शिवस्तोत्रम् (Hālāsyamāhātmya; Stotra Nidhi) and वरुणदेवकृता सीतास्तुतिः — Varuṇa praising another deity; wrong direction.
- `यं ब्रह्मा वरुणेन्द्ररुद्रमरुतः…` — Viṣṇu dhyāna that *mentions* Varuṇa; not a Varuṇa greeting.

Could not find a short standalone greeting in bāla-pāṭha, nitya-prārthanā, or school readers under वरुण / अपांपति / प्रचेतस् / जलदेव / वरुणदेव, nor a Tamil வருணன் / Telugu వరుణుడు / Kannada ವರುಣ / Malayalam വരുണൻ / Marathi / Bengali / Hindi children’s hello. Stotra Nidhi बाल श्लोकाः and Vaidika Vignanam नित्य पारायण श्लोक have no Varuṇa stanza. South Indian Vaidika Varuṇa pūjā (Prayoga-ratnam / Āpastamba-line) uses the Vedic ṛks and the nāma list, not this namaskāra.

## Uncertainties

- No Purāṇa / āgama chapter-verse pin for the sphatika namaskāra. Strong as a living kalasha paddhati line, not as a pinned mūla. Do not invent a Purāṇa cite.
- (c) is no: the verse sits inside kalasha-prārthanā; the everyday address is nāma; Vedic ṛks belong to Sandhyā / āvāhana.
- झषासन: jhaṣa is a fish / sea-creature. Paddhati iconography of Varuṇa’s vāhana is usually मकर; this hunt did not find a traditional gloss equating the two in *this* verse. Keep झष as printed; do not silently replace with मकर.
- Padaccheda and glosses are proposed from grammar and the dative string, not from a named bhāṣya.
- Two kalasha tracks must stay distinct: (1) this namaskāra; (2) `देवदानवसंवादे` addressed to the *kumbha*, plus Vedic `तत्त्वा यामि`. TransLiteral’s Lakṣmī-pūjā वरुणस्थापना uses `ॐ वरुणाय नमः` and the kumbha-prārthanā without the sphatika line.
- Īśānaśiva dikpāla namaskāra is a real āgama-paddhati verse with a number; it is the better *textual* pin, but it is not the household Varuṇa hello. Keep it in Other candidates.
