# Lakṣmī

## Proposal

Devanagari:

नमस्तेऽस्तु महामाये श्रीपीठे सुरपूजिते ।
शङ्खचक्रगदाहस्ते महालक्ष्मि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥

IAST:

namaste ’stu mahāmāye śrīpīṭhe surapūjite |
śaṅkhacakragadāhaste mahālakṣmi namo ’stu te ||

Padaccheda: not attested as a grammatical padapāṭha. Children’s pāṭha (Sathya Sai Balvikas Group I) splits by pāda for recitation: नमस्तेऽस्तु महामाये / श्रीपीठे सुरपूजिते / शंखचक्रगदाहस्ते / श्री महालक्ष्मी नमोऽस्तुते. Proposed grammatical split: नमः ते अस्तु महा-माये श्री-पीठे सुर-पूजिते । शङ्ख-चक्र-गदा-हस्ते महा-लक्ष्मि नमः अस्तु ते ॥

Word gloss:
- नमः — salutation, bow
- ते — to you
- अस्तु — let there be
- महा — great
- माये — O Māyā
- श्री — Śrī
- पीठे — O you on the seat
- सुर — of the gods
- पूजिते — O worshipped one
- शङ्ख — conch
- चक्र — discus
- गदा — mace
- हस्ते — O you who hold in hand
- लक्ष्मि — O Lakṣmī
- नमोऽस्तु ते — let there be salutation to you

Translation: “Salutations to you, O Mahāmāyā, who dwell on the seat of Śrī and are worshipped by the gods. O Mahālakṣmī, who hold conch, discus, and mace — salutations to you.”

Function: namaskāra (also verse 1 of इन्द्र-कृत महालक्ष्म्यष्टकम्)

## Claims

(a) exists: yes — printed as verse 1 of Indra-kṛta Mahālakṣmyaṣṭakam across independent stotra collections
(b) traditionally used: yes — home Friday / Dīpāvalī pāṭha, children’s pāṭhaśālā, Kolhapur Mahālakṣmī listed as the temple’s principal short stotra
(c) standard short prayer: yes — isolated as a one-śloka Lakṣmī hello in children’s prayer (Balvikas Group I, kids’ śloka lists), same job as वक्रतुण्ड; adults on Friday often recite the whole aṣṭaka
Confidence in (c): medium

## Variants

Do not splice.

- `नमोऽस्तु ते` (Sanskrit Documents first text, Stotra Nidhi) vs `नमोऽस्तुते` / `नमोस्तुते` (sandhi written solid)
- `नमस्तेऽस्तु` vs popular `नमस्तेस्तु` (avagraha dropped)
- `महालक्ष्मि` (correct vocative) vs `महालक्ष्मी` (long *ī*, common in children’s copies)
- Balvikas: `श्री महालक्ष्मी नमोऽस्तुते` (extra श्री; nominative-looking *ī*)
- `शङ्ख` vs `शंख`; occasional space `शङ्खचक्र गदाहस्ते`

Traditional label on the aṣṭaka: इन्द्र-कृत; many Hindu pages say पद्मपुराण उत्तरखण्ड. No adhyāya / śloka number found. Do not encode a Purāṇa citation until one is pinned.

## Other candidates

- Full महालक्ष्म्यष्टकम् (8 stuti verses + phalaśruti) — the living Friday pāṭha; too long for this book. The proposal is its opening, which *does* circulate alone.
- `पद्मालयां पद्मकरां पद्मपत्रनिभेक्षणाम् । वन्दे पद्ममुखीं देवीं पद्मनाभप्रियामहम् ॥` — true Purāṇa namaskāra (Viṣṇu-purāṇa 1.9, Indra-stuti, Gītā Press / Sanskrit Documents). Verse 3 of a longer stuti, not the independently memorized hello.
- `नमस्ये सर्वलोकानां जननीमब्जसम्भवाम् । श्रियमुन्निद्रपद्माक्षीं विष्णुवक्षःस्थलस्थिताम् ॥` — opening of that same Viṣṇu-purāṇa stuti (var. सर्वभूतानां). Real mūla; still a stotra excerpt, not the school greeting.
- `लक्ष्मीं क्षीरसमुद्रराजतनयां श्रीरङ्गधामेश्वरीं … वन्दे मुकुन्दप्रियाम्` — paddhati dhyāna (Vaidika Vignanam Lakṣmī nitya pūjā; often after Śrī-sūkta). Śārdūlavikrīḍita, Sarasvatī-length; Śrīraṅgam flavour. Wrong size for a vakratuṇḍa-shaped hello.
- `या सा पद्मासनस्था … नित्यं सा पद्महस्ता मम वसतु गृहे सर्वमाङ्गल्ययुक्ता` — same nitya-pūjā dhyāna set; too long; petition for the house, not a namaskāra.
- `वन्दे पद्मकरां प्रसन्नवदनां सौभाग्यदां भाग्यदां …` — dhyāna before लक्ष्मी अष्टोत्तरशतनाम. Too long.
- `ॐ महालक्ष्म्यै च विद्महे विष्णुपत्न्यै च धीमहि । तन्नो लक्ष्मीः प्रचोदयात् ॥` — gāyatrī / japa, not a greeting śloka.
- `कराग्रे वसते लक्ष्मीः करमध्ये सरस्वती । करमूले तु गोविन्दः प्रभाते करदर्शनम् ॥` — morning hand-darśana; three deities, not a Lakṣmī hello.
- `सर्वमङ्गलमाङ्गल्ये शिवे सर्वार्थसाधिके । शरण्ये त्र्यम्बके गौरि नारायणि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥` — Devī / Durgā / Nārāyaṇī; turns up in Lakṣmī pūjā, not Lakṣmī-specific.
- Śrī-sūkta (`हिरण्यवर्णां हरिणीं…`) — Vedic; out of this book’s yardstick.
- Kanakadhārā, Aṣṭalakṣmī stotram, Agastya `जय पद्मपलाशाक्षि` — real texts, too long.

## Uncertainties

- This *is* aṣṭaka verse 1. (c) holds because pāṭhaśālā / children’s lists isolate it; it fails if the human wants a verse that was never part of a longer stotra. The Viṣṇu-purāṇa padmālaya line is the cleaner “one śloka from a Purāṇa,” but it is not the common hello.
- Padma-purāṇa ascription is traditional, not a found chapter number. Popularity is not provenance.
- Vocative `महालक्ष्मि` vs popular `महालक्ष्मी`; Balvikas extra `श्री`.
- `श्रीपीठे` is vocative in the grammar of the verse (parallel to महामाये / सुरपूजिते); some glosses treat it as “on the Śrī-pīṭha” (locative sense).
- Iconography: conch–discus–mace is Viṣṇu’s set on Mahālakṣmī (Kolhapur Ambābāī holds a different set). The verse is pan-Indian stotra language, not a temple mūrti-dhyāna.
