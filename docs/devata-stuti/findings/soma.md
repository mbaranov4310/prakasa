# Soma

## Proposal

Cannot find a short standalone greeting to Soma as the Vedic plant-deity (सोम, सोमदेव, पवमान, इन्दु-as-drop, अमृत-as-Soma), distinct from Candra the moon.

The only 1–2 śloka “Soma” prayers in living Hindu collections are moon / navagraha Candra verses. They name सोम but bow to शशिन् / निशाकर / रोहिणीश / शम्भु-मुकुट-भूषण. A parallel Candra job owns those. They are not crowned here.

## Claims

(a) exists: no — no vakratuṇḍa-shaped standalone śloka to Soma-the-plant turned up. Vedic Soma ṛks exist in plenty (yajña, not this book’s shape). Short ślokas that *say* सोम exist, but they are Candra.
(b) traditionally used: Soma is used in somayāga, pañcāmṛta kṣīra-snāna (`आ प्यायस्व`), and as nāma (`ॐ सोमाय नमः`); not as a home / school / temple-walk greeting śloka
(c) standard short prayer: no
Confidence in (c): high (that there is no such hello)

## Sources

Hindu sources only. Count: 6 for the hunt (what *is* printed as “Soma,” and what children’s / nitya lists omit). None is a Purāṇa verse-number for a Soma-plant greeting — none was found to cite.

1. Stotra Nidhi, बाला श्लोकाः — https://stotranidhi.com/hi/slokas-for-kids-in-sanskrit/ — children’s namaskāras for guru, Gaṇeśa, Sarasvatī, Rāma, Hanumān, Viṣṇu, Kṛṣṇa, Śiva, Annapūrṇā. No Soma.
2. Vaidika Vignanam, नित्य पारायण श्लोकाः — https://vignanam.org/marathi/nitya-parayana-slokas.html — prabhāta to śayana set; no Soma-plant (or Candra) hello.
3. Stotra Nidhi, नवग्रह स्तोत्रम् (printed in *Prabhāta Stotranidhi*) — https://stotranidhi.com/hi/navagraha-stotram-in-sanskrit/ — verse 2 is `दधिशङ्खतुषाराभं … नमामि शशिनं सोमं शम्भोर्मुकुटभूषणम्`. Candra graha namaskāra that *uses the name* सोम. Same four pādas as dhyāna in चन्द्र ग्रहस्य बीज मन्त्र जपम् — https://stotranidhi.com/hi/chandra-graha-beeja-mantra-in-sanskrit/
4. Sanskrit Documents, नवग्रहस्तोत्रम् (व्यासविरचितम्), verse 2 — https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_navagraha/navanew.html — same Candra verse. Their सोमस्तोत्रम् (from a Grantha/Tamil *Ādityādi Navagraha Stotra* book) — https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_navagraha/somastotram.html — opens `प्रणमामि चन्द्रं`, verse 2 `रोहिणीशः`, phalaśruti `इदं निशाकरस्तोत्रं`. Title सोम, deity the moon.
5. Sanskrit Documents, गायत्रीमन्त्राः — https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_major_works/gaayatriimantraaH_devanagari.html — Soma is filed under **चन्द्र** (`ॐ निशाकराय विद्महे कलानाथाय धीमहि तन्नः सोमः प्रचोदयात्` and cousins). No separate plant-Soma gāyatrī in that list.
6. Stotra Nidhi, पञ्चामृताभिषेक मन्त्राः — https://stotranidhi.com/hi/panchamrutha-abhishekam-mantras-in-sanskrit/ — milk-snāna uses the Soma ṛk `आ प्यायस्व समेतु ते विश्वतः सोम वृष्णियम्` (labelled तै.सं. ३-२-५); sugar-snāna uses RV 9.85.6 `स्वादुः पवस्व दिव्याय जन्मने`. Ritual snāna, not a greeting. Same `आ प्यायस्व` is the kṣīra-āvāhana in Baudhāyana-line सर्वदेवसामान्यपूजा (Brahma-karma-samuccaya) with `ॐ भूर्भुवःसुवः सोमाय नमः सोममावाहयामि`.

Also seen, not counted separately: Vignanam चन्द्र ग्रह पञ्चरत्न (opens with the दधिशङ्ख verse; verse 5 `तं सोमं प्रणमाम्यहम्` still Rohiṇī’s moon); Stotra Nidhi नक्षत्र सूक्तम् मृगशीर्ष (`सोमो राजा मृगशीर्षेण आगन्` — Vedic iṣṭi); Stotra Nidhi चन्द्र कवचम् ( meru / white-horse dhyāna; `सोमो देवता` = graha); Sanskrit Documents Śiva pūjā (`क्षीरे सोमाय नमः` — Soma as the milk-deity in pañcāmṛta, not a hello); Viṣṇu pūjā-vidhi `ॐ सोमाय नक्षत्राधिपतये … नमः` (Soma as lord of the nakṣatras = moon). Hindi/English “Soma stotra” blogs reprint the navagraha Candra hymn; twenty copies of one blog are one source.

## Variants

No Soma-plant greeting recension to collate.

Do not splice Candra pādas into this slot. The moon verse that names सोम is printed `दधिशङ्खतुषाराभं क्षीरोदार्णवसम्भवम् / संभवम्` and `शम्भोर्मुकुटभूषणम् / शम्भोर्मकुट भूषणम्` (Vignanam pañcaratna spacing). That is Candra’s file.

## Other candidates

- `दधिशङ्खतुषाराभं क्षीरोदार्णवसम्भवम् । नमामि शशिनं सोमं शम्भोर्मुकुटभूषणम् ॥` — the only short “Soma” śloka people actually extract. Vyāsa navagraha stotra verse 2; Stotra Nidhi Chandra-bīja dhyāna; Vignanam Chandra pañcaratna 1. Iconography is the moon (śaśin, kṣīrasāgara, Śiva’s crest). **Do not crown as Soma.** Parallel Candra agent.
- सोमस्तोत्रम् dhyāna `श्वेताम्बरोज्ज्वलतनुं … प्रणमामि चन्द्रं` (Sanskrit Documents; HinduNidhi reprint). Same hymn later `सोम देहि ममारोग्यं` / `रक्ष मां रजनीकर` / `राजा नाथश्चौषधीनां`. Oṣadhi-rāja is an epithet of the moon here, not a plant-deity hello. Too long; wrong deity for this job.
- Chandra / Soma gāyatrī `ॐ निशाकराय विद्महे कलानाथाय धीमहि तन्नः सोमः प्रचोदयात्` (Sanskrit Documents gāyatrī list, under Candra). Astro-site cousin `ॐ सोमराजाय विद्महे महाराजाय धीमहि तन्नश्चन्द्र प्रचोदयात्`. Mantra, not a maṅgala-śloka; labelled moon.
- `आ प्यायस्व समेतु ते विश्वतः सोम वृष्णियम् । भवा वाजस्य सङ्गथे ॥` — RV 1.91.16 / Taittirīya; genuine address *to* Soma. Used as kṣīra-snāna and (in one Baudhāyana-line paddhati) as Soma āvāhana. Living print also files it as a Chandra-graha japa. Vedic ṛk, not independently a daily hello of this book’s shape. Out of yardstick.
- नक्षत्र सूक्त, मृगशीर्ष: `सोमो राजा मृगशीर्षेण आगन् … तस्मै ते सोम हविषा विधेम` (Stotra Nidhi, Vignanam). Vedic iṣṭi, not a greeting.
- RV 9.1.1 `स्वादिष्ठया मदिष्ठया पवस्व सोम धारया । इन्द्राय पातवे सुतः ॥` — the usual Pavamāna opening. Somayāga / ninth-maṇḍala; not a child’s namaskāra.
- `ॐ सोमाय नमः` / `ॐ सोमाय स्वाहा` / `ॐ सोमाय नक्षत्राधिपतये सवाहनपरिवाराय नमः` — the actual short formula. Nāma, not a śloka; the third is explicitly nakṣatra-adhipati (moon).
- `वयं सोम व्रते तव मनस्तनूषु बिभ्रतः । प्रजावन्तो अशीमहि ॥` — Vedic; used in mahānyāsa (Stotra Nidhi दिक्संपुट, Kubera quarter), not a Soma hello.
- सोमसुन्दराष्टकम् (`तं वन्दे सोमसन्दरम्`) — Śiva at Madurai as Somasundara (Stotra Nidhi; Hālāsya-māhātmya). Wrong deity.
- सोमनाथ / सोमेश्वर — Śiva; Monday (सोमवार) in the south is Śiva’s day, not a plant-Soma pāṭha.
- सोमोत्पत्तिस्तोत्रम् (Sanskrit Documents, navagraha folder) — how the moon wanes and is refilled; śrāddha / parvan. Long; moon.
- Hindi Candra ārati / “Soma stotra” news-pages that tell you to set up a Candra mūrti on Monday — same moon hymn, wrong size/language.

Could not find a short standalone greeting in bāla-pāṭha, nitya-prārthanā, or school readers under सोम / सोमदेव / पवमान / सोमलता / इन्दु-as-plant / अमृत-as-Soma, nor a Tamil / Telugu / Kannada / Malayalam / Marathi / Bengali children’s hello to that deity.

## Uncertainties

- Later Hindu usage often *identifies* Soma with Candra (gāyatrī lists, navagraha stotra, nakṣatrādhipati nāma). That merger is real in paddhati. This job asked to keep the plant-deity slot distinct; under that rule there is no hello to encode.
- `आ प्यायस्व` is the closest Soma-addressed short recitation still in nitya karma (milk-abhiṣeka). It is Vedic, and homes that say it are usually bathing a liṅga or a Candra-graha, not greeting Soma-the-plant. If the human later wants a Vedic exception, that is the ṛk to audit — not a Candra śloka.
- No Purāṇa / āgama citation was invented for a missing verse.
- Western philology on the Soma plant was not used.
