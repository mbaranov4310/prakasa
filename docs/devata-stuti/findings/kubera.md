# Kubera

## Proposal

Devanagari:

धनाध्यक्षाय देवाय नरयानोपवेशिने ।
नमस्ते राजराजाय कुबेराय महात्मने ॥

IAST:

dhanādhyakṣāya devāya narayānopaveśine |
namaste rājarājāya kuberāya mahātmane ||

Padaccheda: no traditional padapāṭha found. Grammar of the anuṣṭubh is dative namaskāra throughout. Proposed:

धन-अध्यक्षाय देवाय नर-यान-उपवेशिने । नमः ते राज-राजाय कुबेराय महा-आत्मने ॥

Word gloss:
- धन — wealth
- अध्यक्षाय — to the overseer
- देवाय — to the god
- नर — human
- यान — vehicle
- उपवेशिने — to him seated
- नमस्ते (नमः + ते) — salutation to you
- राजन् — king
- राजाय — to the king
- कुबेराय — to Kubera
- महा — great
- आत्मने — to the soul / self

Translation: “Salutation to the god who oversees wealth, seated on a human-borne vehicle; salutation to you, king of kings, the great-souled Kubera.”

Function: namaskāra. Printed as a standalone one-verse कुबेरस्तुतिः in a short-hymn saṅgraha; also the closing प्रार्थना of a North-Indian tijoṛī / cash-box Kubera pūjana (Dīpāvalī / Dhanatrayodaśī home paddhati). Same length as वक्रतुण्ड.

Names searched: कुबेर / कुबेरः, यक्षराज, धनद, वैश्रवण, राजराज, अलकाधिप / अलकाधीश, धनेश्वर, धनाध्यक्ष, निधीश, नरवाहन; Tamil குபேரன்; Telugu కుబేరుడు; Kannada ಕುಬೇರ; Malayalam കുബേരൻ; Hindi कुबेर जी. Queries: श्लोक, प्रार्थना, ध्यान श्लोक, नमस्कार, स्तुति, स्तोत्रम्, दैनिक प्रार्थना, धनतेरस / दीपावली कुबेर, and the same in those languages. No pan-Indian children’s school greeting on the वक्रतुण्ड model turned up.

## Claims

(a) exists: yes — one anuṣṭubh namaskāra, printed as कुबेरस्तुतिः in a Hindu short-stotra saṅgraha and as the tijoṛī-pūjana prārthanā
(b) traditionally used: yes — home paddhati (tijoṛī / sandūk Kubera pūjana at Dīpāvalī); also extracted as the one-verse Kubera stuti in a printed stotra-ratnākara
(c) standard short prayer: unclear — cannot find a kids’-school hello; among ślokas this is the saṅgraha’s one-verse Kubera namaskāra. The recitation people actually know for Dhanateras / Dīpāvalī is the japa-mantra `ॐ यक्षाय कुबेराय… स्वाहा`, which is not a śloka
Confidence in (c): low

## Sources

Hindu sources only. Count: 3 for this verse (2–3 good; two lineages). No Purāṇa verse-number.

1. Bṛhat Stotra Ratnākara, Śivadutta Śāstrī ed. — via Sanskrit Documents, सर्वविधदेवानां स्तोत्रसङ्ग्रहः — https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_deities_misc/devadevatAstutisangrahaH.html — labelled `कुबेरस्तुतिः`; same recension (`नरयानोपवेशिने` / `महात्मने`)
2. TransLiteral Foundations, कुबेर पूजनम् (तिजोरी या बक्सा में) — https://www.transliteral.org/pages/z130128032524/view — same four pādas as the paddhati प्रार्थना after āvāhana; sindūra-svastika on the safe / cash-box. Print `महात्ने` (see Variants)
3. Vedadhara, धन वृद्धि के लिए कुबेर मंत्र — https://www.vedadhara.com/kuber-mantra-hindi — same namaskāra (with the āvāhana couplet printed in front; do not splice)

Also seen, not counted separately: mithilapuja.com Lakṣmī-Kubera pūjana — same tijoṛī sequence and this namaskāra as puṣpāñjali close; one paddhati reprint of (2), not a third lineage.

## Variants

Do not splice.

- `महात्मने` (lead; Bṛhat Stotra Ratnākara / Sanskrit Documents, Vedadhara, mithilapuja) vs TransLiteral OCR `महात्ने` — treat as a dropped म, not a second prayer
- Spacing only: `राजराजाय` vs `राज राजाय`; `नरयानोपवेशिने` vs `नरयानो पवेशिने`
- Paddhati prints often pair this namaskāra with the āvāhana `आवाहयामि देव त्वामिहायाहि कृपां कुरु । कोशं वर्धय नित्यं त्वं परिरक्ष सुरेश्वर ॥` — a different verse; keep it off the lead recension
- Optional `ॐ` is not in the saṅgraha print

## Other candidates

- `धनदाय नमस्तुभ्यं निधिपद्माधिपाय च । भवन्तु त्वत्प्रसादान्मे धनधान्यादिसम्पदः ॥` — closest rival: ṣoḍaśopacāra closing prārthanā (Stotra Nidhi श्री कुबेर षोडशोपचार पूजा, https://stotranidhi.com/en/sri-kubera-puja-vidhanam-in-english/) and the Kubera offering-verse in TransLiteral Marathi Dīpāvalī लक्ष्मी-कुबेर पूजा (`ॐ धनदाय नमस्तुभ्यं…`, https://www.transliteral.org/pages/z71023102124/view). Same length, request-shaped like वक्रतुण्ड, and it *is* a Kubera śloka used at Dīpāvalī. Not led because it does not name कुबेर / वैश्रवण, and the saṅgraha’s one-verse Kubera stuti is the namaskāra above. Variants, do not splice: `निधिपद्माधिपाय च` (Stotra Nidhi) vs `निधिपद्मपतेऽनघ` (one web stotra dump) vs TransLiteral Hindi Dīpāvalī `निधिपद्माधिपायं च । भगवन् त्वत्प्रसादेन धनधान्यादिसम्पदः` (drops `भवन्तु`; https://www.transliteral.org/pages/z101103212752/view). HinduNidhi / Pavitra Granth reprint the Stotra Nidhi paddhati — not extra sources.
- `मनुजवाह्यविमानवरस्थितं गरुडरत्ननिभं निधिनायकम् । शिवसखं मुकुटादिविभूषितं वरगदे दधतं भज तुन्दिलम् ॥` — living ṣoḍaśopacāra / Dīpāvalī dhyāna (Stotra Nidhi same paddhati; TransLiteral Hindi Dīpāvalī कुबेर पूजन). Hindu tantra compilation (Rameśacandra Śarmā Miśra, तन्त्रोक्त देव पूजा रहस्य) prints it as the dhyāna of the 35-akṣara Kubera mantra, citing the मन्त्रमहोदधि / मन्त्रमहार्णव mantra-set. Right deity, wrong size (12-syllable pādas, not a child’s two-line hello). Variants, do not splice: `मनुजवाह्य…स्थितं` / Stotra Nidhi `manuja bāhya vimāna vara sthitaṃ` vs aṣṭottara `मनुजबाह्यविमानवरस्तुतं … वररुचिं तमहमुपास्महे सदा` (Sanskrit Documents कुबेराष्टोत्तरशतनामावलिः, https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_deities_misc/kuberAShTottaranAmAvalI.html).
- `कुबेरं मनुजासीनं सगर्वं गर्वविग्रहम् । स्वर्णच्छायं गदाहस्तं उत्तराधिपतिं स्मरेत् ॥` — anuṣṭubh dhyāna printed *after* the longer dhyāna in the same Stotra Nidhi ṣoḍaśopacāra. Not shown alone as a greeting.
- `ॐ यक्षाय कुबेराय वैश्रवणाय धनधान्याधिपतये धनधान्यसमृद्धिं मे देहि दापय स्वाहा` — the recitation Dhanateras / Dīpāvalī pages and temple-style japa actually use. Sanskrit Documents aṣṭottara opening; Stotra Nidhi Kubera mantrapuṣpa; Hindu mantra-śāstra lists as the 35-akṣara / पञ्चत्रिंशदक्षर Kubera mantra (अष्टाक्षर `ॐ वैश्रवणाय स्वाहा` and षोडशाक्षर `ॐ श्रीं ॐ ह्रीं श्रीं ह्रीं क्लीं श्रीं क्लीं वित्तेश्वराय नमः` sit in the same set). Japa / homa mantra with स्वाहा, not a maṅgala-śloka of this book’s shape. Variants: `धनधान्याधिपतये` vs `धनधान्यादिपतये`; `धनधान्यसमृद्धिं` vs aṣṭottara `धनधान्यादि समृद्धिं`.
- `ॐ यक्षराजाय विद्महे वैश्रवणाय धीमहि । तन्नो कुबेरः प्रचोदयात् ॥` — Kubera gāyatrī in the same ṣoḍaśopacāra mantrapuṣpa. Aṣṭottara variant `यक्षराजाय विद्महे अलकाधीशाय धीमहि`. Mantra, not a śloka.
- `ॐ राजाधिराजाय प्रसह्यसाहिने नमो वयं वैश्रवणाय कुर्महे । स मे कामान् कामकामाय मह्यं कामेश्वरो वैश्रवणो ददातु । कुबेराय वैश्रवणाय महाराजाय नमः` — Taittirīya Āraṇyaka 1.31.6 (Jñāna Prabodhinī mantra-puṣpāñjali note; Stotra Nidhi prints it as the Kubera pūjā mantrapuṣpa). Real Vaiśravaṇa namaskāra, and it *is* used. Vedic; this book’s yardstick is non-Vedic short greetings. Out of scope as lead.
- `कुबेरो धनद श्रीदः राजराजो धनेश्वरः । धनलक्ष्मीप्रियतमो धनाढ्यो धनिकप्रियः ॥` — verse 1 of an 18-verse कुबेर स्तोत्रम् (Stotra Nidhi https://stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-kubera-stotram-in-sanskrit/; Vaidika Vignanam same). Name-list, not a standalone hello; the stotra is too long.
- `कुबेर त्वं धनाधीश गृहे ते कमला स्थिता । तां देवीं प्रेषयाशु त्वं मद्गृहे ते नमो नमः ॥` — actually a Kubera śloka (asks Kubera to send Kamalā / Lakṣmī into the house); Dhanateras / Dīpāvalī news and bhakti pages circulate it. Not found in the paddhatis or stotra-saṅgrahas above. Navbhārat Times ascribes it to मन्त्रमहोदधि without a locus; a Hindu tantra compilation that *does* quote that mantra-set does not print this verse. Do not invent the citation. Weak as (b)/(c).
- `ॐ कुबेराय नमः` / `ॐ वैश्रवणाय नमः` / `ॐ यक्षराजाय नमः` — the short formula; not a śloka.
- Hindi Kubera ārati (`जय यक्ष कुबेर हरे…`) and Kubera cālīsā — used, wrong size/language.
- `नमस्तेऽस्तु महामाये श्रीपीठे सुरपूजिते…` — already encoded as the Lakṣmī hello (Indra-kṛta Mahālakṣmyaṣṭaka 1). Dhana-Lakṣmī overlap: do not steal it. Stotra Nidhi’s Kubera ṣoḍaśopacāra wraps Śrī-sūkta around the upacāras; that is Lakṣmī Veda, not a Kubera śloka.

Could not find a short standalone greeting in bāla-pāṭha, nitya-prārthanā, or school readers under कुबेर / यक्षराज / धनद / वैश्रवण / राजराज / अलकाधिप / धनेश्वर, nor a Tamil / Telugu / Kannada / Malayalam / Hindi children’s hello.

## Uncertainties

- No Purāṇa / āgama verse-number for the proposed anuṣṭubh. Provenance is a printed stotra-saṅgraha plus a home tijoṛī paddhati, not a pinned mūla.
- (c) is low because Kubera is not a school-hello deity; the living short recitation is the 35-akṣara mantra, and `धनदाय नमस्तुभ्यं` is a real rival Diwali prārthanā. Either śloka is extractable; they are not the same prayer.
- `नरयानोपवेशिने`: traditional iconography is नरवाहन (men as mount / palanquin-bearers), not Nārāyaṇa. Popular pages that read `नारायणोपवेशिने` are a different word; do not “correct” the received पाद.
- TransLiteral `महात्ने` is almost certainly `महात्मने` with a missing म; keep it in Variants, do not mix into the lead.
- Padaccheda is proposed from grammar, not from a traditional padapāṭha of this anuṣṭubh.
- VastuCart “Kubera aṣṭakam / stotram” pages that name Padma / Skanda / Viṣṇudharmottara without a verse-locus are not counted. Do not invent those citations.
