# Gaṇeśa

## Proposal

Devanagari:

वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय सूर्यकोटि समप्रभ ।
निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव सर्वकार्येषु सर्वदा ॥

IAST:

vakratuṇḍa mahākāya sūryakoṭi samaprabha |
nirvighnaṃ kuru me deva sarvakāryeṣu sarvadā ||

Padaccheda: no single canonical padapāṭha found. Children’s pāṭha (Śrī Sathya Sai Bālavikās) and Indian prayer-book glosses split on the vocatives and the request. Proposed:

वक्र-तुण्ड महा-काय सूर्य-कोटि सम-प्रभ । निर्-विघ्नं कुरु मे देव सर्व-कार्येषु सर्वदा ॥

Word gloss:
- वक्रतुण्ड (वक्र + तुण्ड) — O curved-trunked one
- महाकाय (महा + काय) — O large-bodied one
- सूर्यकोटि (सूर्य + कोटि) — a crore of suns
- समप्रभ (सम + प्रभ) — of equal radiance (vocative)
- निर्विघ्नं (निर् + विघ्नम्) — obstacle-free (accusative object of कुरु)
- कुरु — make / do (2sg imperative of कृ)
- मे — for me / my
- देव — O god
- सर्वकार्येषु (सर्व + कार्येषु) — in all undertakings (locative plural)
- सर्वदा — always

Translation: “O curved-trunked, large-bodied Lord, whose radiance equals a crore of suns — make my every undertaking always free of obstacles.”

Function: prārthanā (also living as maṅgalācaraṇa / opening invocation before any work, pūjā, or school day)

## Claims

(a) exists: yes — printed across stotra collections, nitya-pārāyaṇa lists, and pūjā paddhatis
(b) traditionally used: yes — paddhati (Gaṇapati pūjana, Satyanārāyaṇa / Vināyaka vrata), nitya pārāyaṇa, maṭha śloka pages, bāla-pāṭha
(c) standard short prayer: yes — the independently memorized hello for this deity; first śloka many children learn
Confidence in (c): high

## Variants

Do not splice.

- `सूर्यकोटि समप्रभ` (lead; Kāmakoṭi, Stotra Nidhi, Bālavikās) vs `सूर्यकोटिसमप्रभ` (one compound; prātaḥsmaraṇa) vs `कोटिसूर्यसमप्रभ` (TransLiteral paddhati; Sanskrit Documents ślokasaṅgraha 1)
- `समप्रभ` (vocative, no visarga) vs `समप्रभः` (nitya pārāyaṇa print)
- `निर्विघ्नं` (almost all) vs `अविघ्नं` (one TransLiteral pūjā-vidhi dhyāna dump)
- Optional `ॐ` prefixed in japa prints
- Sandhi of the second pāda is stable: `निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव सर्वकार्येषु सर्वदा`

`चन्द्रकोटि` was named in the inventory as a possible north/south wording. This hunt did not find it in the Hindu sources above.

Mudgala Purāṇa khaṇḍa 1 (Vakratuṇḍa-carita) uses the *name* Vakratuṇḍa as the first of eight avatāras; a search of that text does not yield this four-pāda prayer. Devotional sites that say “from the Mudgala Purāṇa” are naming the avatāra, not citing a verse.

## Other candidates

- `शुक्लाम्बरधरं विष्णुं शशिवर्णं चतुर्भुजम् । प्रसन्नवदनं ध्यायेत् सर्वविघ्नोपशान्तये ॥` — genuine short vighna-śānti; printed as Gaṇeśa dhyāna on Kāmakoṭi and Stotra Nidhi, and as the first kārya-prārambha verse in Vaidika Vignanam nitya pārāyaṇa. Literally Viṣṇu (white-clad, four-armed); also the opening dhyāna of Viṣṇu-sahasranāma. Used *for* Gaṇeśa in many homes, but it is not the Gaṇeśa-named hello.
- `अगजानन पद्मार्कं गजाननमहर्निशम् । अनेकदं तं भक्तानामेकदन्तमुपास्महे ॥` — school favourite; namaskāra / upāsanā; often paired *after* vakratuṇḍa in nitya pārāyaṇa. Not the first greeting.
- `गजाननं भूतगणादिसेवितं कपित्थजम्बूफलसारभक्षितम् । उमासुतं शोकविनाशकारणं नमामि विघ्नेश्वरपादपङ्कजम् ॥` — dhyāna; often opens longer Gaṇeśa stotras. Same length, less independently memorized as *the* hello.
- `मूषिकवाहन मोदकहस्त चामरकर्ण विलम्बितसूत्र । वामनरूप महेश्वरपुत्र विघ्नविनायक पाद नमस्ते ॥` — Tamil children’s Vināyaka namaskāra (பிள்ளையார்); strong in the south, not the pan-Indian short prayer.
- `अभीप्सितार्थसिद्ध्यर्थं पूजितो यः सुरासुरैः । सर्वविघ्नहरस्तस्मै गणाधिपतये नमः ॥` — namaskāra; Marathi śloka collections; a closing/obeisance verse, not the request-hello.
- Gaṇapati Atharvaśīrṣa; Nārada-purāṇa Saṅkaṭanāśana-stotra (eight verses); `ॐ गं गणपतये नमः` — real and used, but not a 1–2 line standalone greeting of this book’s shape.

## Uncertainties

- No Purāṇa / āgama / well-known bhāṣya verse citation. Strong as a living paddhati, pāṭhaśālā, and home verse, not as a pinned mūla.
- First-line compound vs two vocatives (`सूर्यकोटि समप्रभ` / `सूर्यकोटिसमप्रभ`) is a print difference, not two prayers.
- `कोटिसूर्यसमप्रभ` is a real paddhati cousin; keep it in Variants, do not mix into the lead recension.
- Padaccheda is proposed from grammar and bāla-pāṭha splits, not from a traditional padapāṭha of this anuṣṭubh.
