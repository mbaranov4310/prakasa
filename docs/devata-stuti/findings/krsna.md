# Kṛṣṇa

## Proposal

Devanagari:

```
कृष्णाय वासुदेवाय देवकीनन्दनाय च ।
नन्दगोपकुमाराय गोविन्दाय नमो नमः ॥
```

IAST:

```
kṛṣṇāya vāsudevāya devakīnandanāya ca |
nandagopakumārāya govindāya namo namaḥ ||
```

Padaccheda: Gauḍīya anvaya (Vedabase SB 1.8.21) splits: कृष्णाय | वासुदेवाय | देवकी-नन्दनाय | च | नन्द-गोप-कुमाराय | गोविन्दाय | नमः | नमः. Printed mūla usually keeps the compounds closed.

Word gloss:
- कृष्णाय — to Kṛṣṇa
- वासुदेवाय — to Vāsudeva (son of Vasudeva)
- देवकी-नन्दनाय — to the son / delight of Devakī
- च — and
- नन्द-गोप-कुमाराय — to the boy of the cowherd Nanda (Gauḍīya anvaya: to the son of Nanda and the gopas)
- गोविन्दाय — to Govinda (protector of cows; Gauḍīya: who enlivens the cows and the senses)
- नमः नमः — salutations, again and again (`नमो नमः` by sandhi)

Translation: “Again and again I bow to Kṛṣṇa, son of Vasudeva, joy of Devakī, child of the cowherd Nanda — to Govinda.”

Function: namaskāra (one verse of Kuntī’s stuti, extracted and said on its own)

## Claims

(a) exists: yes — Bhāgavata Purāṇa 1.8.21, spoken by Kuntī
(b) traditionally used: yes — Kuntī-stuti pāṭha, Pāṇḍava Gītā extract, Gauḍīya Bhāgavata recitation
(c) standard short prayer: unclear — it *is* a common namaskāra hello, but it does not own the slot the way वक्रतुण्ड does for Gaṇeśa
Confidence in (c): medium

## Variants

The four pādas are stable across these prints. Cosmetic only:

- spacing: `देवकीनन्दनाय` vs `देवकी नन्दनाय`; `नन्दगोपकुमाराय` vs `नन्दगोप कुमाराय`
- last visarga: `नमः` vs `नम:`

Do not confuse with the other Purāṇa namaskāra that shares the first two words (candidate A below).

## Other candidates

- `कृष्णाय वासुदेवाय हरये परमात्मने । प्रणतक्लेशनाशाय गोविन्दाय नमो नमः ॥` — Bhāgavata 10.73.16. Often the *living* daily hello: kids’ pages (Hindu Janajagruti), Krishna ṣoḍaśopacāra prārthanā (Stotra Nidhi), Marathi/Bengali namaskāra, current Vṛndāvana japa (e.g. Premanand Maharaj). Pāṇḍava Gītā gives it to Arundhatī. Why not lead: Hari / Paramātman leans toward generic Viṣṇu; frequently treated as a japa-mantra with `ॐ`, not a school śloka. Variant: `प्रणतः क्लेशनाशाय` vs the compound `प्रणतक्लेशनाशाय` (Bhāgavata mūla is the compound).
- `वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् । देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥` — opening of कृष्णाष्टकम्; the school-assembly vandana. Sanskrit Documents (`krishna8-3`) marks Author: Unknown; Śaṅkara ascription is traditional, not demonstrated. Why not lead: it is verse 1 of an eight-verse stotra (people do extract it); no Purāṇa pin. Note the first word is **वसुदेव**सुतं, not वासुदेवसुतं.
- `कस्तूरीतिलकं ललाटफलके वक्षःस्थले कौस्तुभं / नासाग्रे नवमौक्तिकं करतले वेणुं करे कङ्कणम् । / सर्वाङ्गे हरिचन्दनं च कलयन् कण्ठे च मुक्तावलिं / गोपस्त्रीपरिवेष्टितो विजयते गोपालचूडामणिः ॥` — the Gopāla *dhyāna*. कृष्णकर्णामृत 2.108 (Bilvamaṅgala / Līlāśuka, Sanskrit Documents); also dhyāna of Gopāla-sahasranāma (Vaidika Vignanam) and of Krishna ṣoḍaśopacāra pūjā (Stotra Nidhi). Why not lead: longer meter (śārdūlavikrīḍita), visualization before pūjā, not a folded-hands hello. Variants — do not splice: `ललाटफलके` / `ललाटपटले`; `नवमौक्तिकं` / `वरमौक्तिकं`; `च कलयन्` / `सुललितं`.
- `नमो ब्रह्मण्यदेवाय गोब्राह्मणहिताय च । जगद्धिताय कृष्णाय गोविन्दाय नमो नमः ॥` — Viṣṇu Purāṇa (Prahlāda). Used in Krishna pūjā prārthanā, but also as a Viṣṇu śānti verse. Why not lead: the Viṣṇu job owns it.
- `कराविन्देन पदारविन्दं मुखारविन्दे विनिवेशयन्तम् । वटस्य पत्रस्य पुटे शयानं बालं मुकुन्दं मनसा स्मरामि ॥` — Bāla-Mukunda dhyāna (often verse 1 of बालमुकुन्दाष्टकम्). Why not lead: infant-on-the-banyan-leaf, not the common temple-walk greeting.
- `श्री कृष्ण गोविन्द हरे मुरारे हे नाथ नारायण वासुदेव` — kīrtana couplet (covers मुरारि). Why not lead: song line, not a śloka of this collection’s shape.
- `ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय` — dvādaśākṣara. Why not lead: mantra, and shared with Viṣṇu.

Yaśodānandana: found as `ॐ यशोदानन्दनाय नमः` and in the pūjā line `नमस्तुभ्यं जगन्नाथ देवकीतनय प्रभो । वसुदेवसुताऽनन्त यशोदानन्दवर्धन ॥` — no independent four-pāda hello under that name.

## Uncertainties

- Kṛṣṇa has several competing hellos by region and sampradāya. This proposal is the Purāṇa namaskāra that is unmistakably Kṛṣṇa (Nanda, Devakī), not a claim that everyone says this one first.
- Which “कृष्णाय वासुदेवाय” people mean: 1.8.21 (this) vs 10.73.16 (the japa). They must not be mixed.
- वसुदेवसुतं vs वासुदेवसुतं is a common muddle; the aṣṭakam is the former.
- No separate short मुरारि / यशोदानन्दन śloka turned up as a standard hello.
