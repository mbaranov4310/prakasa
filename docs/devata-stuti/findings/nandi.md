# Nandī

Temple-walk greeting at Śiva temples was in scope.

## Proposal

Devanagari:

नन्दिकेश महाभाग शिवध्यानपरायण ।
गौरीशङ्करसेवार्थं अनुज्ञां दातुमर्हसि ॥

IAST:

nandikeśa mahābhāga śivadhyānaparāyaṇa |
gaurīśaṅkarasevārthaṃ anujñāṃ dātumarhasi ||

Padaccheda: no traditional padapāṭha found. The verse is printed as one anuṣṭubh in temple-walk kramas. Proposed:

नन्दि-केश महा-भाग शिव-ध्यान-परायण । गौरी-शङ्कर-सेवार्थम् अनुज्ञाम् दातुम् अर्हसि ॥

Word gloss:
- नन्दिकेश (नन्दि + ईश) — O Nandikeśa (Nandi as lord / lord of the Nandis)
- महाभाग (महा + भाग) — O highly distinguished / fortunate one
- शिवध्यानपरायण (शिव + ध्यान + परायण) — (you) whose whole bent is meditation on Śiva
- गौरीशङ्करसेवार्थम् (गौरी + शङ्कर + सेवार्थम्) — for the sake of serving Gaurī-Śaṅkara
- अनुज्ञाम् — permission / leave
- दातुम् — to give
- अर्हसि — you ought; be pleased to (2sg of अर्ह्)

Translation: “O Nandikeśa, highly distinguished one, ever intent on meditating on Śiva — be pleased to grant me permission to serve Gaurī and Śaṅkara.”

Function: anujñā-prārthanā / namaskāra — the two-line permission asked of Nandī before Śiva-darśana on a temple walk. Not a home/school maṅgalācaraṇa.

## Claims

(a) exists: yes — anuṣṭubh printed as श्रीनन्दिकेश्वर प्रार्थना, first stop in शिवालयदर्शनस्तोत्रक्रम compilations, and as the Nandi-precinct verse in a Cidambaram temple-stotra
(b) traditionally used: yes — Giri Traders Śiva-temple walk book (via P. R. Ramachander); living Tamil precinct stotra (Cidambaram); South-Indian temple-etiquette teaching (take Nandikeśvara’s leave before the liṅga)
(c) standard short prayer: unclear — this is the circulating two-line *Sanskrit* hello at the Nandi of a Śiva temple among people who recite a śloka; the everyday act is often silent namaskāra, looking through the horns, and whispering a wish in the ear, not this verse. Not found in bāla-pāṭha / nitya-pārāyaṇa children’s lists
Confidence in (c): low

## Variants

Do not splice. The first pāda is stable; the *whom one asks leave to serve* changes with the shrine.

- `गौरीशङ्करसेवार्थं अनुज्ञां दातुमर्हसि` (lead; TransLiteral / Jagran generic krama) vs sandhi print `गौरीशङ्करसेवार्थमनुज्ञां दातुमर्हसि` (lalitaalaalitah)
- `उमाशङ्करसेवार्थम् अनुज्ञां दातुमर्हसि` (Giri / Ramachander; Cidambaram precinct 59 श्रीनन्दिकेश्वरः, verse 60)
- Cidambaram precinct 2: `नटेश्वरस्य सेवार्थं अनुज्ञां देहि मे प्रभो` — same first line, different second (Naṭeśvara, not Gaurī-Śaṅkara)
- Cidambaram precinct 19: `शिवाध्यानपरायण` and `महेश्वर्या सुसेवार्थं अनुज्ञां दातुमर्हसि` — first-line `शिवा` vs `शिव`; second pāda asks leave to serve Maheśvarī
- Cidambaram precinct 108: `नन्दिकेशं महाभागं शिवध्यानपरायणम् । अष्टमूर्तेरधिष्ठानं नमामि सुयशापतिम् ॥` — accusative namaskāra, not anujñā; same name-cluster, different job
- Trust print `अनुज्ञानम्` vs stotra `अनुज्ञां` — keep the stotra reading
- Spacing only: `शिवध्यानपरायण` / `शिवध्यान परायण`; `गौरीशङ्कर` / `गौरिशन्कर`

A Telugu/Andhra *cousin*, not a variant of this verse (do not mix pādas):

नन्दीश्वर नमस्तुभ्यं साम्बानन्दप्रदायक ।
महादेवस्य सेवार्थम् अनुज्ञां दातुमर्हसि ॥

(Āndhra Amṛtam temple-entry post: `నందీశ్వర నమస్తుభ్యం సాంబానంద ప్రదాయక … అనుజ్ఞాం దాతుమర్హసి`.) Advaita-l Pradosha note and a family Rudrādhyāya notebook print the same first couplet with `अनुज्ञां देहि मे प्रभो`, then extra dhyāna lines (`वेदपादं विशालाक्षं तीक्ष्णशृङ्गं… वृषं भजे`). Same function, different opening.

## Other candidates

- `नन्दीश्वर नमस्तुभ्यं साम्बानन्दप्रदायक…` — closest rival: same length and job, living in Telugu Śiva-kṣetra custom (Śrīśailam / Vemulavāḍa / Kālahasti named on those pages). Not led because the Giri / शिवालयदर्शन / Cidambaram cluster is the wider temple-walk print, and splicing it onto `नन्दिकेश महाभाग` would invent a third verse
- Nandikeśvara gāyatrīs (several recensions): `ॐ तत्पुरुषाय विद्महे नन्दिकेश्वराय धीमहि । तन्नो वृषभः प्रचोदयात् ॥` and `ॐ तत्पुरुषाय विद्महे चक्रतुण्डाय धीमहि । तन्नो नन्दिः प्रचोदयात् ॥` (HinduNidhi mantra page; mantra.tips). Real japa before Śiva-pūjā; mantra, not a maṅgala-śloka of this book’s shape
- Vaidika Vignanam, नन्दिकेश्वराष्टोत्तरशतनामावली — https://vignanam.org/hindi/nandikeshwara-ashtottara-sata-namavali.html — 108 names beginning `ॐ नन्दिकेशाय नमः । ॐ ब्रह्मरूपिणे नमः । ॐ शिवध्यानपरायणाय नमः` (the third name matches this verse’s epithet). Namāvali, not a two-line greeting
- Tamil பிரதோஷ நந்தி 108 போற்றி (aanmeegamlyrics; Tamil Samayam) — living Pradosha praise; 108 lines, wrong size
- `नमामि नन्दिकेश्वरं शिलादजं गणेश्वरम्` — refrain of श्रीनन्दिकेशपञ्चरत्नमालिकास्तुतिः (Śaṅkara ascription; Sanskrit Documents, from the same नटराजस्तवमञ्जरी as the Cidambaram walk). Right deity (Śilāda’s son, gaṇa-lord, vṛṣānana); five verses plus phalaśruti, not the hello
- `भवेशं भवेशानमीड्यं सुरेशं… भजे नन्दिकेशं दरिद्रार्तिनाशम्` — नन्दिकेश्वरस्तोत्रम् verse 1 (Kāñcī Kāmakoṭi; Sanskrit Documents). Eight verses, drawn from श्रीचामुण्डानन्दिकेश्वर इतिहास एवं माहात्म्य. Iconography is Śiva (नील कण्ठ, Gaṅgā in the jaṭā, ardhanārī, महोक्षाधिरूढ — *he rides* the bull). Ramachander files it as a prayer to “the god of Nandi,” i.e. Śiva. Keep off this slot
- Practice-śloka of looking through the horns: `वृषस्य वृषणं स्पृष्ट्वा ईश्वरस्यावलोकनम् । शृङ्गमध्ये शिवं दृष्ट्वा पुनर्जन्म न विद्यते ॥` (Advaita-l Pradosha note). Temple vidhi, not a greeting *to* Nandī
- `ॐ नन्दिकेशाय नमः` / whispering a wish in Nandi’s ear — the actual spoken custom; not a śloka
- Stotras *by* Nandikeśvara in praise of Śiva or Gaṇeśa (Śivarahasya; Mudgala Purāṇa 8.11) — wrong direction
- No hit in Stotra Nidhi or Vaidika Vignanam *nitya pārāyaṇa* under नन्दि / नन्दीश्वर as a children’s two-liner. No Gītā Press / Bālavikās Nandi pāṭha found

## Uncertainties

- No Purāṇa / āgama / well-known bhāṣya verse-number for the anujñā. Provenance is temple-walk krama and a Cidambaram precinct stotra, not a pinned mūla
- Slot is thin. The greeting exists; it is not vakratuṇḍa-grade. If (c) is read as “what a child independently memorizes at home,” this hunt did not find one — say so rather than promote the eight-verse stotra or a gāyatrī
- Two permission-verses (महाभाग vs नमस्तुभ्यं) run in parallel by region. Record both; do not merge
- गौरीशङ्कर vs उमाशङ्कर vs नटेश्वर vs महेश्वरी are shrine-readings of the same first line, not four prayers
- Padaccheda is proposed from grammar, not from a pāṭhaśālā split
- नन्दिकेश्वरस्तोत्रम् is a real Hindu print (Kāmakoṭi) but almost certainly Śiva under that name; do not encode it here without a human call
- Giri book title/ISBN was not recovered beyond Ramachander’s acknowledgement; the verse itself is independently in TransLiteral and Cidambaram
