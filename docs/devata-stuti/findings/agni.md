# Agni

## Proposal

Devanagari:

अग्निं प्रज्वलितं वन्दे जातवेदं हुताशनम् ।
सुवर्णवर्णममलं समिद्धं विश्वतोमुखम् ॥

IAST:

agniṃ prajvalitaṃ vande jātavedaṃ hutāśanam |
suvarṇavarṇam amalaṃ samiddhaṃ viśvatomukham ||

Padaccheda: no traditional padapāṭha found. Paddhati prints treat it as one anuṣṭubh at the bow after the fire is lit. Proposed:

अग्निम् प्र-ज्वलितम् वन्दे जात-वेदम् हुत-अशनम् । सुवर्ण-वर्णम् अ-मलम् सम्-इद्धम् विश्वतः-मुखम् ॥

Word gloss:
- अग्निम् — Agni (accusative)
- प्रज्वलितम् — blazing / kindled
- वन्दे — I bow to / I praise
- जातवेदम् — Jātavedas (traditional epithet: knower of all that is born; accusative of जातवेदस्)
- हुताशनम् — Hutāśana, eater of the offering (हुत + अशन)
- सुवर्णवर्णम् — golden-coloured
- अमलम् — stainless / pure
- समिद्धम् — well-kindled (सम् + इद्ध, from √इन्ध्)
- विश्वतोमुखम् — facing all directions / whose face is everywhere

Translation: “I bow to blazing Agni, Jātavedas, eater of the offering — golden-coloured, pure, well-kindled, facing every direction.”

Function: namaskāra at agni-mukha / havan upasthāna after the fire is lit (paddhati bow, not a child’s home–school–temple-walk hello)

## Claims

(a) exists: yes — one anuṣṭubh printed as the Agni namaskāra in tantra paddhati (Mantra-mahodadhi), in a Prapañcasāra dhyāna compilation, and in gṛhya / havan vidhi
(b) traditionally used: yes, narrowly — the bow *inside* homa after the fire is kindled (`इति प्रणमेत्` / `अग्निं प्रार्थयेत्` / अग्निपूजन-उपस्थान); not shown as pāṭhaśālā / school / temple-walk greeting
(c) standard short prayer: no — there is no vakratuṇḍa-grade independently memorized hello for Agni; daily Agni is Vedic ṛk, gāyatrī, or dīpa-verse, which this book does not take
Confidence in (c): low

## Variants

Do not splice.

- `सुवर्णवर्णममलं समिद्धं` (lead; Mantra-mahodadhi, Prapañcasāra mañjarī) vs `सुवर्णवर्णमनलं समिद्धं` (Baudhāyana agnimukha; अनल = Agni’s name, not a misspelling of अमल)
- `हिरण्यवर्णममलं` (North Indian havan: TransLiteral, karmkandvidhi, Hariom Group) — same sense as सुवर्णवर्ण
- `समिद्धं` (kindled; Mahodadhi, Prapañcasāra, Baudhāyana, TransLiteral havan, Hariom) vs `समृद्धं` (prosperous; karmkandvidhi, Vadicjagat Devi-Bhāgavata pathavidhi). Do not mix; समिद्धम् is the fire-word
- `हुताशनम्` (almost all) vs `हुताशम्` (TransLiteral havan dump)
- Optional `ॐ` prefixed in havan prints
- Spacing `जात वेदं`, `सुवर्ण वर्णममलं` in some Hindi pages — print, not a different text

`अनलं` and `अमलं` are two real readings of pāda 3. Keep them in Variants; do not “correct” one into the other.

## Other candidates

- `अग्निमीळे पुरोहितं यज्ञस्य देवमृत्विजम् । होतारं रत्नधातमम् ॥` — Ṛgveda 1.1.1; Stotra Nidhi अग्नि सूक्तम्; Vaidika Vignanam नित्य पारायण lists the whole sūkta. The living Agni recitation. Vedic ṛk / Sandhyā–homa book, out of this yardstick unless independently used as a daily *śloka* hello — it is not
- `ॐ वैश्वानराय विद्महे ललीलाय (var. अग्निदेवाय) धीमहि । तन्नो अग्निः प्रचोदयात् ॥` — Agni gāyatrī; japa, not a greeting śloka
- `अहं वैश्वानरो भूत्वा प्राणिनां देहमाश्रितः । प्राणापानसमायुक्तः पचाम्यन्नं चतुर्विधम् ॥` — Gītā 15.14; Sathya Sai Bālavikās Group II food prayer. Kṛṣṇa speaking as the digestive Vaiśvānara, not an Agni namaskāra
- `ब्रह्मार्पणं ब्रह्म हविः…` — same Bālavikās food set; Brahman in the offering-fire, not a hello to Agni-deva
- `शुभं करोति कल्याणं…` / `दीपज्योतिः परं ब्रह्म…` — dīpa namaskāra at lamp-lighting; home/school use is real, but the addressee is the lamp, not Agni
- Mārkaṇḍeya-purāṇa अग्निस्तोत्र (Stotra Nidhi; Sanskrit Documents, adhyāya ~96 / भौत्यमन्वन्तर). ~40 verses. Verse 19 `नमस्तेऽनल पिङ्गाक्ष नमस्तेऽस्तु हुताशन` is a genuine namaskāra pāda-pair inside the stotra, not isolated as the hello
- सहदेवकृता अग्निस्तुतिः, Mahābhārata Sabhāparvan (Sanskrit Documents; ~9 verses). Yajña-stuti, too long; verse 1 `त्वदर्थोऽयं समारम्भः कृष्णवर्त्मन्नमोऽस्तु ते` is not independently the greeting
- सीताकृत वह्न्यष्टकम्, Kūrma-purāṇa pūrvabhāga 34 (Sanskrit Documents; karmkandvidhi). Eight verses. Opening is narrative (`उपतस्थे महायोगं… रामपत्नी`); verse 2 `नमस्यामि महायोगं…` is namaskāra-shaped but not shown as a one-śloka hello
- Paddhati dhyāna (same TransLiteral स्थण्डिलकर्म / Vaidika-mantra-kalpalatā / Prapañcasāra second verse): `इष्टां शक्तिं स्वस्तिकं… ध्यायेद्वह्निं बद्धमौलिं जटाभिः` — iconography, Mandākrāntā-length, not a vakratuṇḍa hello
- `वैश्वानर नमस्तेऽस्तु हुताशन नमोऽस्तु ते । देवानां प्रीणनार्थाय तिष्ठात्र मम स्थण्डिले ॥` — TransLiteral स्थण्डिलकर्म, when the fire is set on the sthaṇḍila. Right length; installation formula, not a greeting
- `एह्येहि सर्वामरहव्यवाह मुनिप्रवर्यैरभितोऽभिजुष्ट…` — same sthaṇḍila āvāhana; too long
- `मन्त्रहीनं क्रियाहीनं भक्तिहीनं हुताशन । यद्धुतं तु मया देव परिपूर्णं तदस्तु ते ॥` — homa kṣamā-prārthanā (Āpastamba / Baudhāyana aupāsana dumps); closing apology, not a hello
- `ॐ अग्नये नमः` and the eight mūrti names (जातवेदाः, सप्तजिह्व, हव्यवाहन…) — namaskāra formulas in Mantra-mahodadhi, not a śloka of this kind
- अग्नेः अष्टोत्तरशतनाम / सहस्रनाम — too long
- VastuCart “अग्नि स्तोत्रम् / अग्नि अष्टकम्” (`वैश्वानरं महाज्योतिं… नमाम्यग्निं हुताशनम्`) — untraced outside that site; no Purāṇa pin. Do not crown
- `ॐ अग्नये स्वाहा` / agnihotra vyāhṛtis — ritual mantras, different book

Could not find a short standalone greeting in bāla-pāṭha, nitya-prārthanā, or school readers under the names above, nor a Tamil / Telugu / Kannada / Malayalam / Marathi / Bengali / Gujarati children’s Agni hello. Stotra Nidhi’s Agni pages are the Vedic sūkta and the long Mārkaṇḍeya stotra; Vignanam nitya pārāyaṇa files अग्नि सूक्तम्, not a four-pāda namaskāra.

## Uncertainties

- No Purāṇa / āgama verse-number for `अग्निं प्रज्वलितं वन्दे`. Traditional homes are Mantra-mahodadhi (Mahīdhara) and Prapañcasāra (Śaṅkara attribution). Do not invent a Purāṇa cite
- (c) fails on the Gaṇeśa yardstick: children and school readers do not isolate this verse. (b) holds because independent havan / agnimukha paddhatis use it as the one-śloka bow. If the human wants only a home–school hello, the honest result is **cannot find one**
- South (Baudhāyana `अनलं`) and tantra (`अमलं`) and North (`हिरण्यवर्ण` / occasional `समृद्धं`) are recension forks, not one verse with typos
- Padaccheda and glosses are proposed from grammar and Hindi paddhati sense, not from a named bhāṣya of this anuṣṭubh
- `विश्वतोमुख` is an old Vedic epithet reused in this later namaskāra; the verse itself is not a ṛk
- Agni as dikpāla (āgneya) has temple sculpture and longer dhyāna; no short dikpāla-hello turned up that is independently memorized
