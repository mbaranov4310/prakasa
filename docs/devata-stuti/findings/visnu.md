# Viṣṇu

## Proposal

Devanagari:

```
शान्ताकारं भुजगशयनं पद्मनाभं सुरेशं
विश्वाधारं गगनसदृशं मेघवर्णं शुभाङ्गम् ।
लक्ष्मीकान्तं कमलनयनं योगिभिर्ध्यानगम्यं
वन्दे विष्णुं भवभयहरं सर्वलोकैकनाथम् ॥
```

IAST:

```
śāntākāraṃ bhujagaśayanaṃ padmanābhaṃ sureśaṃ
viśvādhāraṃ gaganasadṛśaṃ meghavarṇaṃ śubhāṅgam |
lakṣmīkāntaṃ kamalanayanaṃ yogibhirdhyānagamyaṃ
vande viṣṇuṃ bhavabhayaharaṃ sarvalokaikanātham ||
```

Padaccheda: not attested as a traditional padapāṭha of a mūla; proposed from bāla-pāṭha glosses (Sathya Sai Balvikas word table, same splits in common school teaching):

```
शान्त-आकारम् भुजग-शयनम् पद्म-नाभम् सुर-ईशम्
विश्व-आधारम् गगन-सदृशम् मेघ-वर्णम् शुभ-अङ्गम्
लक्ष्मी-कान्तम् कमल-नयनम् योगिभिः ध्यान-गम्यम्
वन्दे विष्णुम् भव-भय-हरम् सर्व-लोक-एक-नाथम्
```

Translation: “I bow to Viṣṇu — of peaceful form, reclining on the serpent, lotus-naveled, lord of the gods; support of the universe, vast as the sky, cloud-hued, of auspicious limbs; beloved of Lakṣmī, lotus-eyed, reached by yogis in meditation; remover of the fear of worldly existence, the one Lord of all the worlds.”

Function: dhyānaśloka that ends in namaskāra (`वन्दे`). The school / home / temple-walk Viṣṇu hello. Four pādas, longer than वक्रतुण्ड (same job as या कुन्देन्दु for Sarasvatī).

Names searched: विष्णु, नारायण, हरि, अच्युत, गोविन्द (Govinda hits that are Kṛṣṇa-specific are under other candidates). Also Tamil சாந்தாகாரம் / Telugu శాంతాకారం / Kannada ಶಾಂತಾಕಾರಂ; Hindi दैनिक प्रार्थना, ध्यान श्लोक, बाल श्लोक.

## Claims

(a) exists: yes — printed as a standalone Viṣṇu dhyāna across stotra dumps, VSN prefatory dhyāna, nitya pūjā, and children’s pāṭha
(b) traditionally used: yes — Viṣṇu nitya pūjā dhyāna, Viṣṇu-sahasranāma dhyāna (Śivānanda / Divine Life Society pāṭha), Veṅkaṭeśvara ṣoḍaśopacāra, Gītā-pāṭha dhyāna, Balvikas Group I
(c) standard short prayer: yes — this is the independently memorized Viṣṇu verse; the one a child is taught
Confidence in (c): high

## Variants

Do not splice.

- `विश्वाधारं` (this recension: school, Gītā dhyāna, VSN dhyāna 3 in Sanskrit Documents / Divine Life Society) vs `विश्वाकारं` (nitya pūjā, kavaca, Veṅkaṭeśvara pūjā, some VSN prints)
- `योगिभिर्ध्यानगम्यं` (this recension) vs `योगिहृद्ध्यानगम्यं` (paddhati / some VSN and kids’ prints). Sanskrit Documents VSN lists the second in parentheses
- Mixed print: Stotra Nidhi Bāla-ślokāḥ has `विश्वाधारं` with `योगिहृद्ध्यानगम्यम्` — two recensions colliding
- Careless copies drop anusvāra (`मेघवर्ण` / `शुभांगम्` / `वन्दे विष्णु भवभयहरं`)
- Some pūjā files prefix `ॐ`

## Other candidates

- `मङ्गलं भगवान् विष्णुर्मङ्गलं गरुडध्वजः । मङ्गलं पुण्डरीकाक्षो मङ्गलायतनं हरिः ॥` — anuṣṭubh, vakratuṇḍa-sized. Function is maṅgala (weddings, Satyanārāyaṇa close), not the visualization hello. Printed as verse 1 of Viṣṇvaṣṭamaṅgalam on Sanskrit Documents (https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_vishhnu/viShNvaShTamangalam.html). Strong second if a shorter namaskāra is wanted
- `मेघश्यामं पीतकौशेयवासं श्रीवत्साङ्कं कौस्तुभोद्भासिताङ्गम् । पुण्योपेतं पुण्डरीकायताक्षं विष्णुं वन्दे सर्वलोकैकनाथम् ॥` — VSN dhyāna 4; iconographic, same closing as the lead verse; not independently the school hello
- `सशङ्खचक्रं सकिरीटकुण्डलं सपीतवस्त्रं सरसीरुहेक्षणम् । सहारवक्षःस्थलशोभिकौस्तुभं नमामि विष्णुं शिरसा चतुर्भुजम् ॥` — VSN dhyāna 6; namaskāra shape; still lives inside the VSN dhyāna set
- `नारायणाय विद्महे वासुदेवाय धीमहि । तन्नो विष्णुः प्रचोदयात् ॥` — Viṣṇu gāyatrī in the Vignanam nitya pūjā right after the dhyāna. Mantra, not a śloka greeting
- `शुक्लाम्बरधरं विष्णुं शशिवर्णं चतुर्भुजम् । प्रसन्नवदनं ध्यायेत् सर्वविघ्नोपशान्तये ॥` — Gaṇeśa dhyāna. The word `विष्णुं` here is “the all-pervading one,” not Viṣṇu-as-deity. Printed as the Ganeśa opening of VSN and of Viṣṇu nitya pūjā. Do not steal it for Viṣṇu
- `अच्युतं केशवं रामनारायणं कृष्णदामोदरं वासुदेवं हरिम् …` — Acyutāṣṭakam. Kṛṣṇa / Rāma name-string, not Viṣṇu-as-such
- `शङ्खचक्रगदापाणे द्वारकानिलयाच्युत । गोविन्द पुण्डरीकाक्ष रक्ष मां शरणागताम् ॥` — Draupadī’s Kṛṣṇa stuti (TransLiteral: द्रौपदीकृतश्रीकृष्णस्तुति). Kṛṣṇa-specific
- `कृष्णाय वासुदेवाय हरये परमात्मने । प्रणतः क्लेशनाशाय गोविन्दाय नमो नमः ॥` — Kṛṣṇa namaskāra; park under Kṛṣṇa
- `क्षीरोदन्वत्प्रदेशे शुचिमणिविलसत्सैकते … मुकुन्दः` — traditional VSN dhyāna 1 (Mukunda). Real paddhati verse, too long, not a child’s hello
- `प्रातः स्मरामि फणिराजतनौ शयानं …` — three-verse Viṣṇu prātaḥ-smaraṇa (Brahmānanda). Too long

## Uncertainties

- No Purāṇa / āgama / Mahābhārata verse-number. This is not in the Anuśāsana-parvan VSN mūla; it is a later dhyāna attached to recitation. Popularity is not provenance. Do not encode a Purāṇa label
- Recension: `विश्वाधारं` + `योगिभिर्ध्यानगम्यं` (school / Gītā / Divine Life Society) vs `विश्वाकारं` + `योगिहृद्ध्यानगम्यं` (nitya pūjā / kavaca / some VSN). Proposal follows the children’s and Gītā-pāṭha reading
- Longer than वक्रतुण्ड. If the book wants anuṣṭubh only, lead with मङ्गलं भगवान् विष्णुः instead — that is a closer, not a hello
- `शुक्लाम्बरधरं` sits next to this verse in the same pūjā / VSN opening; easy to mis-attribute
