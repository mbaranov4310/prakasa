# Garuḍa

## Proposal

Devanagari:

कुङ्कुमाङ्कितवर्णाय कुन्देन्दुधवलाय च ।
विष्णुवाह नमस्तुभ्यं पक्षिराजाय ते नमः ॥

IAST:

kuṅkumāṅkitavarṇāya kundendudhavalāya ca |
viṣṇuvāha namastubhyaṃ pakṣirājāya te namaḥ ||

Padaccheda: no traditional padapāṭha found. Nitya-pārāyaṇa prints and Tamil/Kannada pāṭha split on the vocative and the two namaskāras. Proposed:

कुङ्कुम-अङ्कित-वर्णाय कुन्द-इन्दु-धवलाय च । विष्णु-वाह नमः तुभ्यम् पक्षि-राजाय ते नमः ॥

Word gloss:
- कुङ्कुम — kuṅkuma / vermilion
- अङ्कित — marked
- वर्णाय — to the one whose colour
- कुन्द — white jasmine
- इन्दु — moon
- धवलाय — to the white one
- च — and
- विष्णु — Viṣṇu
- वाह — O bearer / vehicle
- नमः — salutation
- तुभ्यम् — to you
- पक्षि — birds
- राजाय — to the king
- ते — to you
- नमः — salutation

Translation: “Salutation to you, O bearer of Viṣṇu, whose hue is marked with vermilion and who is white as jasmine and the moon; salutation to you, O king of birds.”

Function: namaskāra (Viṣṇu-temple vāhana greeting; also south-Indian nitya pārāyaṇa and śubha-prayāṇa)

Names searched: गरुड / गरुड़, तार्क्ष्य, वैनतेय, सुपर्ण, पक्षिराज, गरुत्मत्; Tamil கருடன்; Telugu గరుడ; Kannada ಗರುಡ; Malayalam ഗരുഡൻ. Queries: श्लोक, प्रार्थना, ध्यान श्लोक, नमस्कार, स्तुति, स्तोत्रम्, दैनिक प्रार्थना / nitya pārāyaṇa, and the same in Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Hindi. Temple vāhana greeting kept in scope. Viṣṇu verses that only name Garuḍa as vehicle or dhvaja were set aside.

## Claims

(a) exists: yes — two-line anuṣṭubh namaskāra printed as गरुड स्वामि स्तोत्रम् in nitya-pārāyaṇa lists and said as the Tamil Garuḍa añjali
(b) traditionally used: yes — Viṣṇu-temple / seeing-Garuḍa greeting (Tamil Śrīvaiṣṇava practice); Kannada children’s nitya pāṭha under Viṣṇu-vāhana; also recited for śubha-prayāṇa
(c) standard short prayer: yes as the south-Indian vāhana hello of this length; not a pan-Indian school-child first śloka on the vakratuṇḍa model
Confidence in (c): medium

## Variants

Do not splice.

- `कुन्देन्दुधवलाय` (one compound; proposed) vs `कुन्देन्दु धवलाय` (nitya-pārāyaṇa print split)
- `विष्णुवाह` (lead; nitya pārāyaṇa, Tamil, Upaniṣad vocative, Kannada prayāṇa line) vs `विष्णुवाहन` (Kannada children’s deity-list line). `विष्णुवाहन` overfills the pāda; keep it in Variants
- Last pāda `पक्षिराजाय ते नमः` (lead) vs run-together `पक्षिराजायते नमः` (Tamil / Kannada pāṭha)
- A mixed print (Smruti.org slides) keeps the living first line and takes the Upaniṣad’s `क्षेमं कुरु सदा मम` as pāda 4. That is a third recension, not this namaskāra
- Upaniṣad 8 is a different anuṣṭubh: `कुङ्कुमारुणसर्वाङ्गं कुन्देन्दुधवलाननम्` / `क्षेमं कुरु सदा मम`. Related dhyāna-request, not this greeting
- Optional `ॐ` not seen on the nitya-pārāyaṇa prints of this verse

## Other candidates

- `नमः पन्नगनद्धाय वैकुण्ठवशवर्तिने । श्रुतिसिन्धुसुधोत्पादमन्दराय गरुत्मते ॥` — opening namaskāra of Vedānta Deśika’s श्रीगरुडदण्डकम् (Stotra Nidhi; Sanskrit Documents; Prapatti Desika-stotramālā in Sanskrit / Tamil / Telugu / Kannada / Malayalam; TransLiteral). Strong Śrīvaiṣṇava paddhati verse, same length, clearly *to* Garuḍa. Not led because the kuṅkumāṅkita line is the one nitya-pārāyaṇa lists and temple-walkers say as the hello; this sits in front of a long daṇḍaka
- `सुपर्णं वैनतेयं च नागारिं नागभीषणम् । जितान्तकं विषारिं च अजितं विश्वरूपिणम् ॥ गरुत्मन्तं खगश्रेष्ठं तार्क्ष्यं कश्यपनन्दनम् । द्वादशैतानि नामानि गरुडस्य महात्मनः ॥` — Bṛhat Tantrasāra द्वादशनाम (Sanskrit Documents, colophon `इति बृहद्तन्त्रसारे`; Stotra Nidhi; Gītā Press कल्याण year 84 no. 6, June 2010). Nitya recitation at bath or sleep for viṣa-rakṣā. Two name-verses plus phalaśruti, not a greeting
- `ॐ तत्पुरुषाय विद्महे सुवर्णपक्षाय धीमहि । तन्नो गरुडः प्रचोदयात् ॥` — Garuḍa Gāyatrī (Stotra Nidhi विविध गायत्री मन्त्राः, no. 5). Real and used; mantra, not a 1–2 line namaskāra of this book’s shape
- `कुङ्कुमारुणसर्वाङ्गं कुन्देन्दुधवलाननम् । विष्णुवाह नमस्तुभ्यं क्षेमं कुरु सदा मम ॥` — Garuḍopaniṣad 8, last of an eight-verse dhyāna. Better mūla than the lead; not independently listed as the children’s / temple hello
- `स्वस्तिको दक्षिणं पादं वामपादं तु कुञ्चितम् । प्राञ्जलीकृतदोर्युग्मं गरुडं हरिवल्लभम् ॥` — Garuḍopaniṣad dhyāna 1. Short iconographic verse; lives inside the same dhyāna-set, not extracted as a hello
- `अमृतकलशहस्तं कान्तिसम्पूर्णदेहं सकलविबुधवन्द्यं वेदशास्त्रैरचिन्त्यम् । कनकरुचिरपक्षोद्धूयमानाण्डगोलं सकलविषविनाशं चिन्तयेत्पक्षिराजम् ॥` — dhyāna of श्रीगरुडाष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रम्, colophon ब्रह्माण्डपुराण (Sanskrit Documents; Prapatti lists the stotra). One verse, distinctly Garuḍa, too heavy for a child namaskāra
- Bindiganavile गरुडाष्टकस्तुति (Prapatti) — Karnataka Garuḍa-kṣetra aṣṭaka, eight verses
- Garuḍa Pañcāśat; Garuḍa Kavaca (Nārada–Garuḍa saṃvāda, Stotra Nidhi) — real, too long
- `ॐ गं गरुडाय नमः` / `ॐ गरुडाय नमः` — nāma, not a śloka
- Set aside as Viṣṇu verses that only mention Garuḍa: `गरुडगमन तव चरणकमलम्…` (Bhāratī Tīrtha, Stotra Nidhi / Vaidika Vignanam); Annamayya `गरुड गमन गरुडध्वज`; `मङ्गलं भगवन् विष्णुः … मङ्गलाय गरुडध्वजः`. Not greetings *to* Garuḍa
- Set aside as passing Vainateya: `अगस्त्यं वैनतेयं च शमीं च बडबालनम् । आहार परिणामार्थं स्मरामि च वृकोदरम् ॥` (digestion smaraṇa); `रामं स्कन्दं हनुमन्तं वैनतेयं वृकोदरम् । शयने यः स्मरेन्नित्यं दुःस्वप्नस्तस्य नश्यति ॥` (sleep-rakṣā list)
- Vedic `स्वस्ति नस्तार्क्ष्यो अरिष्टनेमिः` — real Tārkṣya-svasti; this book is not Vedic

## Uncertainties

- No Purāṇa / āgama verse-number for the proposed anuṣṭubh. Strong as a living south-Indian nitya-pāṭha and temple-vāhana namaskāra. The nearest mūla is Garuḍopaniṣad 8, which is a cousin, not this recension
- (c) is medium because the verse is regional (Tamil añjali, Kannada/Telugu nitya pārāyaṇa), Deśika’s `नमः पन्नगनद्धाय` is the Śrīvaiṣṇava maṭha opening, and the dvādaśanāma is the north-print nitya viṣa-stotra. None of those three is a pan-Indian school hello
- Padaccheda is proposed from grammar and pāṭha splits, not from a traditional padapāṭha
- Malayalam hunt found Deśika’s daṇḍaka on Prapatti, not this kuṅkumāṅkita line as a separate children’s pāṭha
- Do not credit this greeting to the Garuḍa Purāṇa. That text is Viṣṇu-kathā named after Garuḍa; it is not the source of this namaskāra
