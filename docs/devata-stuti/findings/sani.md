# Śani

## Proposal

Devanagari:

```
नीलांजनसमाभासं रविपुत्रं यमाग्रजम् ।
छायामार्तण्डसंभूतं तं नमामि शनैश्चरम् ॥
```

IAST:

`nīlāṃjanasamābhāsaṃ raviputraṃ yamāgrajam | chāyāmārtaṇḍasaṃbhūtaṃ taṃ namāmi śanaiścaram ||`

Padaccheda: not attested as a split recitation (printed as compounds). Proposed: नीलांजन-समाभासम् रवि-पुत्रम् यम-अग्रजम् । छाया-मार्तण्ड-संभूतम् तम् नमामि शनैश्चरम् ॥

Word gloss:
- नील — blue-black
- अंजन / अञ्जन — collyrium (kājal); orthographic twin नीलाञ्जन
- सम — like / equal
- आभासम् — appearance / lustre (acc.)
- रवि — Ravi (the Sun)
- पुत्रम् — son
- यम — Yama
- अग्र — first / elder
- जम् — born
- छाया — Chāyā
- मार्तण्ड — Mārtaṇḍa (the Sun)
- संभूतम् — born of
- तम् — that one
- नमामि — I bow
- शनैः — slowly
- चरम् — who moves

Translation: “I bow to Śanaiścara, who has the lustre of blue-black collyrium, son of the Sun, elder brother of Yama, born of Chāyā and Mārtaṇḍa.”

Function: namaskāra that is also used as dhyānaśloka (paddhati pages label it ध्यानम्). Same length as vakratuṇḍa.

Names searched: शनि, शनैश्चर / शनैश्वर, मन्द, छायासुत, सौरि, कोणस्थ, रविपुत्र, शनिदेव; Tamil சனி / சனீஸ்வரன்; Marathi शनि महाराज. Queries: श्लोक, प्रार्थना, ध्यान श्लोक, नमस्कार, स्तुति, स्तोत्रम्, दैनिक प्रार्थना, and Indian-language equivalents. No pan-Indian children’s school greeting on the vakratuṇḍa model turned up. This is the short temple / navagraha namaskāra people actually extract and say.

## Claims

(a) exists: yes — verse 7 of the व्यास-विरचित नवग्रहस्तोत्रम्, and printed alone as Śani dhyāna / mantra
(b) traditionally used: yes — japa-paddhati dhyāna, temple mantra pages (Tirunallar, Nastanpur), nitya-pārāyaṇa navagraha collections
(c) standard short prayer: unclear — cannot find a kids’-school hello; this is the best short standalone namaskāra in wide temple use
Confidence in (c): low

## Sources

Hindu sources only. Count: 5 (strong as a circulating verse; none of these is a Purāṇa verse-number)

1. Sanskrit Documents, Navagrahastotra (Vyāsa), verse 7 — https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_navagraha/navanew.html?lang=sa — same recension (`नीलांजनसमाभासं`, `छायामार्तण्डसंभूतं`); colophon `इति श्रीव्यासविरचितं नवग्रहस्तोत्रं सम्पूर्णम्`
2. Vaidika Vignanam, Nava Graha Stotram (Devanagari; filed under Daily Prayers / नित्यदिन) — https://vignanam.org/devanagari/nava-graha-stotram.html — same verse as the Śani stanza (`नीलाञ्जन समाभासं` / `छाया मार्ताण्ड सम्भूतं`)
3. Stotra Nidhi, Śani-graha bīja-mantra japa (dhyānam before the bīja) — https://stotranidhi.com/hi/shani-graha-beeja-mantra-in-sanskrit/ — same verse used as standalone dhyāna (`नीलाञ्जनसमाभासं` / `छायामार्ताण्डसम्भूतं`)
4. Tirunallar Saneeswaran Temple, “Saneeswara Mantras” — https://thirunallartemple.com/mantras/ — prints this as “Shani Mantra 1” for daily recitation at the main Tamil Śani kṣetra
5. Śrī Śani Mahārāj Mandir, Nastanpur (Nāśik, Maharashtra) — http://www.shanimandirnastanpur.com/shani-mantra.html — labels it `शनिचा व्यासविरचित मंत्र` and prints the same four pādas as the temple’s first mantra

Same verse also opens Vignanam’s Śani Graha Pañcaratna as verse 1 (independent extraction, same house as #2 — not a sixth source).

## Variants

Do not splice.

- नीलांजन (this recension: Sanskrit Documents, Nastanpur) vs नीलाञ्जन (Stotra Nidhi, Vignanam) — same word, anusvāra vs ñ
- मार्तण्ड / मार्तंड (this recension) vs मार्ताण्ड (Stotra Nidhi, Vignanam)
- संभूतं vs सम्भूतं
- Spaced printing (`नीलाञ्जन समाभासं` … `छाया मार्ताण्ड सम्भूतं`) vs one compound per pāda
- Stotra Nidhi japa also prints a longer iconographic dhyāna *before* this verse: `नीलद्युतिः नीलवपुः किरीटी गृध्रस्थितश्चापकरो धनुष्मान् । चतुर्भुजः सूर्यसुतः प्रशान्तः सदास्तु मह्यं वरमन्दगामी ॥` — different text, not a variant of these four pādas

## Other candidates

- `सूर्यपुत्रो दीर्घदेहो विशालाक्षः शिवप्रियः । मन्दचारः प्रसन्नात्मा पीडां हरतु मे शनिः ॥` — verse 7 of नवग्रह पीडाहर स्तोत्रम् (Vignanam); Tirunallar and Nastanpur print it as a second / “Purāṇa-ukta” mantra. Same size, but the job is graha-pīḍā remedy (`पीडां हरतु`), not a hello. Why not lead.
- `कोणस्थः पिङ्गलो बभ्रुः कृष्णो रौद्रोऽन्तको यमः । सौरिः शनैश्चरो मन्दः पिप्पलादेन संस्तुतः ॥` — ten names (Pippalāda), often with the phala `एतानि दश नामानि प्रातरुत्थाय यः पठेत्…`. Nāma-list, not a greeting.
- Pippalāda Śani stotra, three verses beginning `नमोऽस्तु कोणसंस्थाय पिङ्गलाय नमोऽस्तु ते` (Vignanam, Stotra Nidhi) — real short namaskāra, but a three-verse dawn stotra against pīḍā, not one hello.
- Daśaratha-kṛta Śani stotra (traditionally Skanda / Brahmāṇḍa) — real, too long.
- Bījamantra `ॐ प्रां प्रीं प्रौं सः शनैश्चराय नमः` / `ॐ शं शनैश्चराय नमः` — mantra, not a śloka.
- Tamil Tirunallar thuti (`சங்கடங்கள் தீர்ப்பாய் சனி பகவானே…`) — temple Tamil, not a pan-Indian Sanskrit greeting.

Could not find a short standalone children’s school greeting.

## Uncertainties

- No Purāṇa chapter/verse number for this four-pāda. Traditional ascription is Vyāsa (stotra colophon and Nastanpur’s “Vyāsa-viracita”). Web pages that dump it into Brahmāṇḍa or Viṣṇu Purāṇa are mixing it with the Daśaratha stotra or the pīḍāhara set — do not encode a Purāṇa label.
- It lives both as verse 7 of a nine-verse navagraha stotra *and* as a standalone dhyāna/mantra. Temple and paddhati use support extraction; it is still not a morning-assembly verse like vakratuṇḍa.
- Orthography of नीलांजन / नीलाञ्जन and मार्तण्ड / मार्ताण्ड.
- Tirunallar’s Devanagari on that page is sloppy (`निलान्जनम`, `शनैश्वर`); the intended pādas are the same.
