# Brahmā

## Proposal

Devanagari:

अक्षमालां स्रुवं दक्षे वामे स्रुचं कमण्डलुम् ।
लम्बकूर्चं च जटिलं ब्रह्माणं वै नमोऽस्तु ते ॥

IAST:

akṣamālāṃ sruvaṃ dakṣe vāme srucaṃ kamaṇḍalum |
lambakūrcaṃ ca jaṭilaṃ brahmāṇaṃ vai namo ’stu te ||

Padaccheda: not attested, proposed: अक्ष-मालाम् स्रुवम् दक्षे वामे स्रुचम् कमण्डलुम् । लम्ब-कूर्चम् च जटिलम् ब्रह्माणम् वै नमः अस्तु ते ॥

Word gloss:
- अक्ष-मालाम् — a rosary (of beads / akṣa-seeds)
- स्रुवम् — the small sacrificial ladle (sruva)
- दक्षे — in the right (hand)
- वामे — in the left (hand)
- स्रुचम् — the large sacrificial ladle (sruc)
- कमण्डलुम् — a water-pot
- लम्ब-कूर्चम् — (him) with a long hanging beard
- च — and
- जटिलम् — wearing matted locks
- ब्रह्माणम् — Brahmā
- वै — indeed
- नमः — salutation
- अस्तु — let there be
- ते — to you

Translation: “Salutation to you, Brahmā — rosary and sruva in the right [hands], sruc and water-pot in the left, long-bearded and matted-haired.”

Function: namaskāra (closing salute in a ṣoḍaśopacāra Brahmā pūjā paddhati — not a child’s hello)

## Claims

(a) exists: yes — printed as the namaskāra-mantra in a Varanasi Brahmā-pūjā paddhati (and reprinted on a Brahmā-upāsanā site)
(b) traditionally used: yes, narrowly — used as namaskāra *inside that paddhati*; not shown as a pāṭhaśālā / school / temple-walk greeting
(c) standard short prayer: no — there is no vakratuṇḍa-grade “hello” for Brahmā
Confidence in (c): low

## Sources

Hindu sources only. Count: 2 for this verse (one paddhati lineage, two printings). Iconography independently in Agni Purāṇa (different text). Not 5.

1. Brahmārcana paddhati (Brahmārcanapaddhatiḥ), Nava Śakti Prakāśan, Varanasi, 2000 — compiled by Indian paṇḍitas (Vidyādhara Śukla / Mṛtyuñjaya Tripathi) from Veda/Purāṇa/karmakāṇḍa because, they say, Indian karmakāṇḍa had no ready Brahmā pūjā-vidhi. Prints this as the namaskāra after puṣpāñjali. Same verse.
2. brahmadev.in, देवाधिदेव श्री ब्रह्माजी की दैनिक पूजा-विधि — https://brahmadev.in/page/devadhidev-shri-brahma-ji-ki-dainik-puja-vidhi/ — same paddhati sequence; labelled नमस्कार. Same verse.
3. Agni Purāṇa mūrti-lakṣaṇa (quoted in Indian Purāṇa discussion, maghaa.com) — `चतुर्मुखश्चतुर्बाहुः … लम्बकूर्च्चो जटायुक्तो ब्रह्मा … दक्षिणे चाक्षसूत्रं च स्रुवो वामे तु कुण्डिका` — same attributes, **not** this namaskāra. (a) of the type only.

The blogspot “ब्रह्मा पूजन विधि” copies (2); not a third source.

## Variants

- `लम्बकूर्चं` (brahmadev.in) vs OCR `लम्बकूचं` in the English Brahmārcana paddhati dump — kūrca (beard-tuft) is the intended word; do not mix.
- Grammar of the received line: ब्रह्माणम् (accusative) with ते (dative) after नमोऽस्तु. Stotra idiom; do not “correct” it.
- Same paddhati’s longer dhyāna doubles the first couplet with `चतुर्मुखं महाकायं वनमालाविभूषितम् । नवीनं नवरूपाढ्यं लोकानामभिलाषदम् ॥` — a different recitation, not a variant of this namaskāra.

## Other candidates

- Paddhati dhyāna: `दिव्यं रूपं सदा ध्यायेद् ब्रह्माणं तेजसाकुलम् । सावित्रीशक्तिसहितं परमात्मानमीश्वरम् ॥` — right length; visualization before āvāhana, same source lineage, not a greeting.
- Paddhati prārthanā (first of three): `कृष्णाजिनाम्बरधर पद्मासन चतुर्मुख । जटाधर जगत्त्रात प्रसीद कमलोद्भव ॥` — vocative + request, vakratuṇḍa-shaped; still the same compiled paddhati, and it is recited as a three-verse close, not alone.
- Brahma-stuti pañcakam 2 (Stotra Nidhi): `चतुराननमम्भोज निषण्णं भारतीसखम् । अक्षमाला वराभीति कमण्डलुधरं भजे ॥` — right length; verse 2 of five, not shown as an independent hello. Stotra Nidhi (Telugu stotra-pāṭha book) + reprints.
- Pañcakam 4: `नमस्ते लोकनाथाय नमस्ते सृष्टिकारिणे । नमस्ते वेदरूपाय नमस्ते ब्रह्मणे नमः ॥` — namaskāra-shaped; same five-verse stotra.
- Brahmā gāyatrī (several recensions), e.g. `ॐ चतुर्मुखाय विद्महे हंसारूढाय धीमहि । तन्नो ब्रह्मा प्रचोदयात् ॥` — used by purohitas in nānāvidha-gāyatrī lists; mantra, not a maṅgala-śloka.
- Dhyānabindu Upaniṣad 31: `कुम्भकेन हृदिस्थाने चिन्तयेत् कमलासनम् । ब्रह्माणं रक्तगौराभं चतुर्वक्त्रं पितामहम् ॥` — real Hindu text; yogic kumbhaka visualization of Viṣṇu–Brahmā–Śiva, not a greeting.
- `ॐ ब्रह्मणे नमः` — the actual short formula people use; not a śloka.
- Hindi Brahmā ārati (`पितु मातु सहायक स्वामी सखा…`) and Brahmā cālīsā (`जय ब्रह्मा जय स्वयम्भू…`) — used, but wrong size/language for this book.
- Hiraṇyagarbha sūkta (Ṛgveda 10.121) — Vedic; out of scope.
- Bhāgavata 10.14 “Brahma-stuti” — Brahmā praising Kṛṣṇa, wrong direction.
- `गुरुर्ब्रह्मा गुरुर्विष्णुः` — guru-stotra, not a Brahmā hello.
- `ब्रह्मा मुरारिस्त्रिपुरान्तकारी…` — graha / suprabhātam, not a Brahmā greeting.
- `कराग्रे वसते लक्ष्मीः … करमूले तु गोविन्दः / ब्रह्मा` — kara-darśana, not a greeting to Brahmā.
- VastuCart “Brahma aṣṭakam” (`चतुर्वक्त्रं चतुर्बाहुं … ब्रह्माणं प्रणमाम्यहम्`) — untraced outside that site; Brahma-Purāṇa ascription not shown. Do not crown.

Could not find a short standalone greeting in bāla-pāṭha, nitya-prārthanā, or school readers under ब्रह्मा / पितामह / चतुरानन / विधाता / कमलासन / विरिञ्चि / प्रजापति / पद्मज / स्वयम्भू, nor a Tamil / Telugu / Kannada / Malayalam / Marathi / Bengali / Gujarati children’s hello.

## Uncertainties

- The proposed verse has no Purāṇa verse-number. The paddhati that carries it is a circa-2000 Varanasi compilation, assembled because a systematic Indian Brahmā pūjā-vidhi was missing. That is Hindu, not Western philology — and it is also evidence against (c).
- brahmadev.in and the printed paddhati are one lineage, not two independent recensions.
- No Pushkar temple pāṭha of a short Sanskrit hello turned up (ārati timings, snāna, `ॐ ब्रह्मणे नमः` — not a śloka).
- `सरस्वत्या समायुक्तं ब्रह्माणं वाक्यदं हरम् । वन्दे विद्याप्रदं देवं हंसवाहं चतुर्मुखम् ॥` appeared on one mantra page only; left unproposed.
- Names searched: ब्रह्मा, पितामह, चतुरानन / चतुर्मुख, विधाता, कमलासन, विरिञ्चि, प्रजापति, पद्मज / पद्मयोनि, स्वयम्भू, हिरण्यगर्भ, वेधस् / विधि, हंसवाहन.
