# Rāma

## Proposal

Devanagari:

रामाय रामभद्राय रामचन्द्राय वेधसे ।
रघुनाथाय नाथाय सीतायाः पतये नमः ॥

IAST:

rāmāya rāmabhadrāya rāmacandrāya vedhase |
raghunāthāya nāthāya sītāyāḥ pataye namaḥ ||

Padaccheda: attested in traditional vyākhyā (Vyākhyā.org on this śloka): रामाय | रामभद्राय | रामचन्द्राय | वेधसे | रघुनाथाय | नाथाय | सीतायाः | पतये | नमः । Anvaya there: रामभद्राय रामचन्द्राय वेधसे रघुनाथाय नाथाय सीतायाः पतये रामाय नमः ।

Word gloss:
- रामाय — to Rāma
- रामभद्राय — to Rāmabhadra (Rāma the auspicious / protective)
- रामचन्द्राय — to Rāmacandra (Rāma, moon-like)
- वेधसे — to the Vidhātā / creator (Rāma as vedhas)
- रघुनाथाय — to the lord of the Raghus
- नाथाय — to the lord / protector
- सीतायाः — of Sītā
- पतये — to the husband
- नमः — salutation

Translation: “Salutation to Rāma — Rāmabhadra, Rāmacandra, the Creator, lord of the Raghu line, Lord of all, husband of Sītā.”

Function: namaskāra

## Claims

(a) exists: yes — anuṣṭubh namaskāra, verse 27 of Budhakauśika’s श्रीरामरक्षास्तोत्रम्
(b) traditionally used: yes — inside the most-recited Rāma kavaca (Gītā Press nitya-sādhana gutka; Stotra Nidhi printed stotra book); also extracted as a standalone japa / namaskāra
(c) standard short prayer: yes — among Sanskrit ślokas this is the common two-line Rāma namaskāra; the spoken hello in homes is often nāma (जय श्री राम / श्री राम जय राम), not a śloka
Confidence in (c): medium

## Variants

- Orthography only: रामचन्द्राय / रामचंद्राय; सीतायाः / सीताया:
- Prefixed ॐ on some dainik-mantra pages (ॐ रामाय रामभद्राय…) — not in the stotra recension above; do not splice
- Do not mix the namaskāra with the aṣṭottara namāvali (ॐ श्रीरामाय नमः as separate names)

## Other candidates

- राम रामेति रामेति रमे रामे मनोरमे । सहस्रनाम तत्तुल्यं रामनाम वरानने ॥ — Viṣṇu Sahasranāma uttarapīṭhikā, Īśvara to Pārvatī (Stotra Nidhi: श्रीराम राम रामेति…; Vaidika Vignanam same; Sanskrit Documents श्रीरामरामरामेति / रामरामेति रामेति). Also Rāmarakṣā 38. Nāma-mahimā, addressed to वरानने, sits inside a Viṣṇu text — not a greeting *to* Rāma; keep off the Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa slot.
- आपदामपहर्तारं दातारं सर्वसम्पदाम् । लोकाभिरामं श्रीरामं भूयो भूयो नमाम्यहम् ॥ — Rāmarakṣā 35 and the frame of श्रीराम आपदुद्धारकस्तोत्रम् (Sanskrit Documents). Closest rival: Ganeśa-length prārthanā, independently recited. Not led because the proposed verse is the namaskāra and matches the राम / रामभद्र / रघुनाथ / सीतापति name-set.
- दक्षिणे लक्ष्मणो यस्य वामे च जनकात्मजा । पुरतो मारुतिर्यस्य तं वन्दे रघुनन्दनम् ॥ — Rāmarakṣā 31 (variant वामे तु). Compact darśana; still an excerpt, less used as *the* hello.
- श्रीरामचन्द्रचरणौ मनसा स्मरामि / वचसा गृणामि / शिरसा नमामि / शरणं प्रपद्ये ॥ — Rāmarakṣā 29. Four-line śaraṇāgati; longer than the yardstick.
- श्रीराघवं दशरथात्मजमप्रमेयं सीतापतिं रघुकुलान्वयरत्नदीपम् । आजानुबाहुमरविन्ददलायताक्षं रामं निशाचरविनाशकरं नमामि ॥ — invocatory namaskāra before the Padma Purāṇa aṣṭottara / sahasranāma (Sanskrit Documents raama108st). One verse, distinctly Rāma (दशरथात्मज, सीतापति); four pādas, a bit heavier than the two-line hello.
- वैदेहीसहितं सुरद्रुमतले हैमे महामण्डपे… रामं भजे श्यामलम् ॥ — ṣoḍaśopacāra dhyāna (Stotra Nidhi श्री राम षोडशोपचार पूजा). Temple/paddhati, too long (śārdūlavikrīḍita).
- ध्यायेदाजानुबाहुं धृतशरधनुषं… रामचन्द्रम् ॥ — Rāmarakṣā dhyāna. Same problem: long iconographic verse, not a child’s namaskāra.
- रामं लक्ष्मणपूर्वजं रघुवरं सीतापतिं सुन्दरं… वन्दे लोकाभिरामं रघुकुलतिलकं राघवं रावणारिम् ॥ — Rāmarakṣā 26. Names रघुवर / सीतापति / दशरथतनय; metre too long.
- श्री राम जय राम जय जय राम — living nāma, not a śloka.

## Uncertainties

- No Purāṇa / āgama verse-number for this anuṣṭubh as a free-standing mūla. Provenance is Budhakauśika’s Rāmarakṣā (tradition: Śiva taught it in a dream). A *different* Rāmarakṣā is sometimes tied to Padma Purāṇa / Viśvāmitra — do not merge.
- (c) is medium because the verse is an excerpt, आपदामपहर्तारं also circulates as a daily two-liner, and the everyday greeting is often nāma-japa rather than this śloka.
- वेधसे: traditional vyākhyā = विधाता; some popular pages force a Veda-etymology — not followed here.
- षोडशोपचार dhyāna set is a separate paddhati track (not this namaskāra).
