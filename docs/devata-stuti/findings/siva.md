## Proposal

Devanagari:

```
कर्पूरगौरं करुणावतारं संसारसारं भुजगेन्द्रहारम् ।
सदा वसन्तं हृदयारविन्दे भवं भवानीसहितं नमामि ॥
```

IAST:

```
karpūragauraṃ karuṇāvatāraṃ saṃsārasāraṃ bhujagendrahāram |
sadā vasantaṃ hṛdayāravinde bhavaṃ bhavānīsahitaṃ namāmi ||
```

Padaccheda: not attested as a traditional pāṭhaśālā split; proposed:

कर्पूर-गौरं करुणा-अवतारं संसार-सारं भुजग-इन्द्र-हारम् ।
सदा वसन्तं हृदय-अरविन्दे भवं भवानी-सहितं नमामि ॥

Word gloss:
- कर्पूर-गौरं — camphor-white (fair/luminous as camphor)
- करुणा-अवतारं — the embodiment / descent of compassion
- संसार-सारं — the essence of the world (here: worldly existence)
- भुजग-इन्द्र-हारम् — wearing the king of serpents as a garland
- सदा — always
- वसन्तं — dwelling (acc., agreeing with भवं)
- हृदय-अरविन्दे — in the lotus of the heart (loc.)
- भवं — Bhava (Śiva)
- भवानी-सहितं — together with Bhavānī (Pārvatī)
- नमामि — I bow

Translation: “I bow to Bhava, together with Bhavānī — camphor-white, compassion incarnate, the essence of the world, garlanded with the serpent-king — who always dwells in the lotus of the heart.”

Function: namaskāra + dhyānaśloka. In living use it is the ārati / nīrājana closer (camphor flame and the first word match). Paddhati also uses it as the opening Śiva dhyāna before ṣoḍaśopacāra. Same length as वक्रतुण्ड; the moment in the visit is often the end, not the hello.

Meter: classical upajāti (11-syllable pādas). Not Vedic.

## Claims

(a) exists: yes — printed in recitation saṅgrahas and Śiva pūjā paddhatis
(b) traditionally used: yes — nitya-pūjā dhyāna; nīrājana closer in home and temple ārati (including after non-Śiva ārati)
(c) standard short prayer: yes — the four-line Śiva verse people actually know; job is often *closing* ārati, not an opening namaskāra like वक्रतुण्ड
Confidence in (c): high as the common short verse; medium if “standard” means opening greeting only

## Sources

Hindu sources only. Count: 4 independent (good). No Purāṇa / āgama verse-number. Stotra Nidhi’s ṣoḍaśopacāra is the same paddhati as Vignanam — not a fifth.

1. Sanskrit Documents, Śloka saṅgraha 2 (common recitation set) — https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_general/shloka2.html — same verse, `सदा वसन्तं`
2. Vaidika Vignanam, Śiva nitya pūjā vidhānam (dhyāna before ṣoḍaśopacāra) — https://vignanam.org/devanagari/shiva-nitya-puja-vidhanam.html — paddhati; variant `सदा रमन्तं` (also in Telugu/Tamil/Kannada/etc. scripts on the same site)
3. Sanskrit Documents, Devī nīrājanam (closing verse of a Devī ārati) — https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_devii/devInIrAjanam.html — same frame, `सदा रमन्तं`; shows the ārati-closer job, not Śiva-only
4. Living ārati: Hindi `ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा` then this śloka; Marathi pages treat it as the verse said in every pūjā; Tamil prints கற்பூரகௌரம் as the Śiva ārati close — (b)/(c), not a mūla citation

Same paddhati reprint (do not double-count): Stotra Nidhi, Śrī Śiva ṣoḍaśopacāra pūjā, dhyāna 1 — https://stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-shiva-shodasopachara-puja-in-sanskrit/ — `सदा रमन्तं`, then `वन्दे महेशं…` and `ध्यायेन्नित्यं महेशं…`

Not used as authority: blogs that call this “Yajurveda / Śiva Yajur mantra” or “Viṣṇu first sang it.” False. Upajāti is not a saṃhitā mantra. Sanskrit Documents’ Śivāṣṭottaraśatanāmāvali opens with this dhyāna; the Śivarahasya note on that page is for the 108 names, not demonstrated for this verse.

## Variants

Do not splice.

- `सदा वसन्तं` (this proposal; recitation saṅgraha / common ārati) vs `सदा रमन्तं` (paddhati dhyāna, Devī nīrājana) — dwelling vs sporting/delighting
- Spacing only: `कर्पूरगौरं` / `कर्पूर गौरं`; `भुजगेन्द्रहारम्` / `भुजगेन्द्र हारम्`; `भवानीसहितं` / `भवानी सहितं`
- `संसारसारं` vs `संसारसारम्` (anusvāra vs म्)
- Some ārati books append a second verse (`मन्दारमाला…`); that is another text, not this śloka

## Other candidates

- `नमस्ते अस्तु भगवन् विश्वेश्वराय महादेवाय त्र्यम्बकाय… सदाशिवाय श्रीमन्महादेवाय नमः` — long epithet-namaskāra used with Rudrābhiṣeka / some Rudram recitations (inserted, not Taittirīya namakam itself). Wrong size; Vedic-ritual job, not a child’s four-liner.
- `नागेन्द्रहाराय त्रिलोचनाय भस्माङ्गरागाय महेश्वराय । नित्याय शुद्धाय दिगम्बराय तस्मै नकाराय नमः शिवाय ॥` — verse 1 of Śiva pañcākṣara stotra (Śaṅkara ascription; sanskritdocuments / Vignanam). Real stotra, five verses; people know line 1, but it is not the standalone hello.
- `वन्दे शम्भुमुमापतिं सुरगुरुं वन्दे जगत्कारणम्… वन्दे शिवं शङ्करम्` — namaskāra in śārdūlavikrīḍita; Stotra Nidhi prints a two-verse Śiva stuti. Known, less universal than कर्पूरगौरं.
- `ध्यायेन्नित्यं महेशं रजतगिरिनिभं… पञ्चवक्त्रं त्रिनेत्रम्` — standard paddhati dhyāna (Vignanam nitya pūjā verse 3). Too long; iconographic visualization, not a greeting.
- `करचरणकृतं वाक्कायजं… जय जय करुणाब्धे श्रीमहादेव शम्भो` — last verse of Śivāparādha-kṣamāpaṇa stotra. Closing apology, not hello; also reused for other iṣṭas by swapping the last name.
- `त्र्यम्बकं यजामहे…` — Vedic (Mahāmṛtyuñjaya). Out of scope for this book (not Vedic).
- `ॐ नमः शिवाय` — mūla mantra, not a śloka.
- `ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा` / Marathi `आरती ओवाळू तुज कर्पुरगौरा` — living ārati, not Sanskrit śloka.
- Liṅgāṣṭakam, Śivamahimnaḥ, Rudrāṣṭakam, Śiva-mānasa-pūjā — real, too long.

## Uncertainties

- Origin untraced. Do not encode Yajurveda, Śiva Purāṇa, or Rudrayāmala unless someone produces a verse number.
- `वसन्तं` vs `रमन्तं`: lead with ārati recension (`वसन्तं`); paddhati often `रमन्तं`.
- Function is often the close of *any* ārati (Devī nīrājanam prints it), so it is a Śiva verse that escaped into a general closer. A family that wants an *opening* Śiva hello might prefer pañcākṣara verse 1 or `वन्दे शम्भुम्`.
- No traditional padaccheda found; split above is proposed for glossing.
- South paddhati stacks three dhyānas (`कर्पूरगौरं`, `वन्दे महेशं`, `ध्यायेन्नित्यं महेशं`) before `ॐ नमः शिवाय`; the first verse also circulates alone.
