No short standalone namaskāra / dhyānaśloka / school hello of this book’s shape (Gaṇeśa yardstick: 1–2 anuṣṭubh, independently memorized). What exists is Vedic ṛk and sandhyā-paddhati, which this hunt does not crown.

## Claims

(a) exists: yes for Vedic stuti (many Ṛgveda sūktas to उषस्); no for a post-Vedic 1–2 line greeting-śloka
(b) traditionally used: yes, narrowly — Uṣas is देवता of a prātaḥ sandhyā arghya-ṛk and is named in sandhyā tarpaṇa (`ॐ उषस्तृप्यताम्`); not as a pāṭhaśālā / temple-walk hello
(c) standard short prayer: no — cannot find one
Confidence in (c): high (that there is none of this book’s type)

## Variants

None for a lead verse (there is no lead verse).

Do not splice:

- Sandhyā ṛk vs full Uṣas sūkta vs Bhāgyasūkta ṛk 7 — three different recitations.
- `उषस्तृप्यताम्` (tarpaṇa formula) vs a constructed `ॐ उषसे नमः` / `ॐ उषस्यै नमः` on blogs — the latter was not found in a namāvali / aṣṭottara / paddhati namaskāra independent of sandhyā.
- अरुण (Sūrya’s charioteer, male) vs उषस् (dawn goddess). अरुणा as a dawn colour-word in ṛks is not a separate school deity-hello.
- Purāṇic उषा, daughter of Bāṇa / wife of Aniruddha (Bhāgavata 10.62; Bayana Uṣā temple) — a different figure; no short namaskāra to her turned up either.

## Other candidates

Could not find a short standalone greeting.

Adjacent material, all off the yardstick:

- `उषस्तच्चित्रमाभरास्मभ्यं वाजिनीवति । येन तोकं च तनयं च धामहे ॥` (Ṛgveda 1.92.13) — the one short ṛk still used daily, as prātaḥ prāyaścitta-arghya in Śukla Yajurveda sandhyāvandana, देवता Uṣas. Vedic; immediately folded into a Sūrya-nārāyaṇa namaskāra. Not a school hello; not proposed.
- `ॐ उषस्तृप्यताम्` — sandhyā tarpaṇa; a formula, not a śloka.
- Ṛgveda Uṣas sūktas (among them 1.48, 1.49, 1.92, 1.113, 1.123–124, 3.61, 4.51–52, 5.79–80, 6.64–65, 7.75–81, 10.172) — real Hindu stuti, wrong size and register for this book.
- भाग्यसूक्त / प्रातःसूक्त (Ṛgveda 7.41) — morning hymn to several gods; Uṣas is देवता of one ṛk, not the whole greeting.
- रात्रि सूक्तम् (Stotra Nidhi) — Night’s hymn; Uṣas appears as sister (`निरु स्वसारमस्कृतोषसं`). Wrong deity.
- `उषसि मागधमङ्गलगायनैः … जागृहि जागृहि जागृहि` in Devi catusṣaṣṭyupacāra pūjā (Stotra Nidhi) — locative “at dawn,” addressed to Jagadambā, not to Uṣas.
- कराग्रे वसते लक्ष्मीः … प्रभाते करदर्शनम् — children’s morning verse; प्रभात is the time, not this deity.
- जपाकुसुमसङ्काशं … प्रणतोऽस्मि दिवाकरम् — the Sūrya hello (already packaged under Sūrya). Dawn worship in the home went to Sūrya / Gāyatrī, not to Uṣas as a separate child-recitation.
- Constructed nāma `ॐ उषसे नमः` — plausible Sanskrit, not attested here as a traditional aṣṭottara or bāla-pāṭha line.
- English “Salutation of the Dawn” / Sanskrit उषावंदन on Sanskrit Documents — not a Hindu mūla.
- No उषा अष्टोत्तर, उषा कवच, or Purāṇa-pinned Uṣas stotra found on Stotra Nidhi, Sanskrit Documents, Kāmakoṭi, or TransLiteral paddhati pages. No Tamil உஷஸ் / உஷை school ஸ்லோகம்; no Marathi / Telugu / Kannada / Malayalam / Bengali children’s hello under उषा / उषस् / प्रभात-देवी.

## Uncertainties

- A village or unpublished pāṭhaśālā two-liner could exist; it did not surface in Hindu stotra collections, nitya-pārāyaṇa lists, or children’s śloka books used for the other deities in this queue.
- `ॐ उषसे नमः` may be said ad hoc; that is not the same as (c).
- Sandhyā namaskāra wording around Padminī / Uṣā / Saṃjñā / Chāyā varies by print (`सौज्ञा` in one Vignanam dump looks like संज्ञा); irrelevant once the ṛk is not proposed.
- Do not treat Bāṇa’s daughter Uṣā (Bhāgavata / Harivaṃśa) as the Vedic dawn goddess, and do not invent a Purāṇa citation for a greeting that was not found.
