# Durgā / Devī

## Proposal

Devanagari:

सर्वमङ्गलमाङ्गल्ये शिवे सर्वार्थसाधिके ।
शरण्ये त्र्यम्बके गौरि नारायणि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥

IAST:

sarvamaṅgalamāṅgalye śive sarvārthasādhike |
śaraṇye tryambake gauri nārāyaṇi namo 'stu te ||

Padaccheda: not attested as pada-pāṭha in the Purāṇa. Proposed from bāla-pāṭha splits (Sri Sathya Sai Bālavikās padavurai; school readers), keeping the two long vocatives whole:

सर्वमङ्गलमाङ्गल्ये शिवे सर्वार्थसाधिके शरण्ये त्र्यम्बके गौरि नारायणि नमः अस्तु ते

Word gloss:
- सर्वमङ्गलमाङ्गल्ये — O you who are the auspiciousness (māṅgalya) of all that is auspicious (sarva-maṅgala) (vocative)
- शिवे — O Śivā / O auspicious one (vocative; Gita Press: कल्याणदायिनी शिवा)
- सर्वार्थसाधिके — O you who accomplish all aims (the puruṣārthas) (vocative)
- शरण्ये — O you who are fit to take refuge in / refuge of those who come (vocative; Gita Press: शरणागतवत्सला)
- त्र्यम्बके — O three-eyed one (vocative of tryambakā; Gita Press: तीन नेत्रों वाली)
- गौरि — O Gaurī (vocative; fair / radiant)
- नारायणि — O Nārāyaṇī (vocative; the Devī of this stuti)
- नमः — salutation
- अस्तु — let it be
- ते — to you

Translation: “O Nārāyaṇī, you who are the auspiciousness of every auspicious thing, O benevolent Śivā, O accomplisher of every human aim, O refuge of those who come to you, O three-eyed Gaurī — salutations to you.”

(Gita Press arth, slightly tightened: नारायणि! तुम सब प्रकार का मंगल प्रदान करने वाली मंगलमयी हो। कल्याणदायिनी शिवा हो। सब पुरुषार्थों को सिद्ध करनेवाली, शरणागतवत्सला, तीन नेत्रों वाली एवं गौरी हो। तुम्हें नमस्कार है।)

Function: namaskāra / stuti (one anuṣṭubh extracted from the Nārāyaṇī-stuti; also used as pūjā samāpan)

## Claims

(a) exists: yes — Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa, Devī Māhātmya (Durgā Saptashatī / Caṇḍī) adhyāya 11, verse 10 in the Gita Press numbering
(b) traditionally used: yes — recited inside Caṇḍī-pāṭha; also verse 3 of the Durgā Saptashlokī short pāṭha; used as Devī-pūjā closing namaskāra
(c) standard short prayer: yes — independently memorized as the four-pāda Devī / Durgā hello (school / Bālavikās / daily prārthanā), not only as a line inside the long chapter
Confidence in (c): high

## Variants

Do not splice. Lead recension is Gita Press / Sanskrit Documents compact sandhi: `सर्वमङ्गलमाङ्गल्ये` … `त्र्यम्बके गौरि` … `नमोऽस्तु ते`.

- `गौरि` (this recension; classical vocative) vs `गौरी` (Vaidika Vignanam Saptashlokī and some ch. 11 prints)
- `त्र्यम्बके` vs `त्रयम्बके` (Vignanam ch. 11) vs oral `त्र्यम्बिके`
- `नमोऽस्तु ते` vs written as one word `नमोऽस्तुते`
- Compact sandhi `सर्वमङ्गलमाङ्गल्ये` vs spaced `सर्व मङ्गल माङ्गल्ये` (Vignanam ch. 11)
- Gita Press print sometimes shows `सर्वमङ्‌गलमंङ्‌गल्ये` (anusvāra placement); same verse
- Some Mahālakṣmī booklets append a cousin with `देवि` for `गौरि`. That is a Lakṣmī-job overlap, not this Caṇḍī recension. Do not mix.

## Other candidates

- `या देवी सर्वभूतेषु शक्तिरूपेण संस्थिता । नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमो नमः ॥` — Devī Māhātmya adhyāya 5 (Tantrokta Devī-sūkta refrain). One couplet is short enough and widely said (especially Bengal Durgā Pūjā), but the living recitation is a long repeating cycle (buddhi, nidrā, kṣudhā, śakti, lakṣmī, …). Too long if taken as the hymn people actually chant; a single refrain is a possible alternate hello, not the lead.
- `जयन्ती मङ्गला काली भद्रकाली कपालिनी । दुर्गा शिवा क्षमा धात्री स्वाहा स्वधा नमोऽस्तु ते ॥` — Argala-stotra 2 (aṅga of Saptashatī). Names दुर्गा explicitly; short namaskāra. Name-list rather than the usual four-pāda greeting. Variant: `दुर्गा क्षमा शिवा धात्री`.
- `सर्वस्वरूपे सर्वेशे सर्वशक्तिसमन्विते । भयेभ्यस्त्राहि नो देवि दुर्गे देवि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥` — same chapter (Gita Press 11.24); Saptashlokī verse 5. Names `दुर्गे` twice. Close runner-up if the human wants the word Durgā in the verse; less the pan-Indian extracted hello than सर्वमङ्गलमाङ्गल्ये.
- `सिंहस्था शशिशेखरा मरकतप्रख्यैश्चतुर्भिर्भुजैः … दुर्गा दुर्गतिहारिणी भवतु नो रत्नोल्लसत्कुण्डला ॥` — Durgā dhyāna in ṣoḍaśopacāra / nitya-pūjā paddhati (e.g. TransLiteral; Pavitragranth Durgā pūjā). Śārdūlavikrīḍita, too ornate for the child-hello yardstick.
- `दुर्गे स्मृता हरसि भीतिमशेषजन्तोः …` — Devī Māhātmya ch. 4; Saptashlokī verse 2. Triṣṭubh, longer than two anuṣṭubh lines.
- `नमो देव्यै महादेव्यै शिवायै सततं नमः । नमः प्रकृत्यै भद्रायै नियताः प्रणताः स्म ताम् ॥` — opening of adhyāya 5. Used, but less independently memorized as *the* Durgā hello.
- महिषासुरमर्दिनी (`अयि गिरिनन्दिनि`) / Durgā Cālīsā — too long.
- `कात्यायनि महामाये …` — Kātyāyanī vrata; Pārvatī job, not this one.

## Uncertainties

- Chapter-internal verse numbers move with recension (whether the ṛṣir uvāca / opening triṣṭubhs are counted). Gita Press and Stotra Nidhi: 11.10. Sanskrit Documents Nārāyaṇī-stuti dump: also 10. Some Mārkaṇḍeya chapter counts (whole Purāṇa, not Saptashatī) differ. The wording of this śloka does not.
- `त्र्यम्बके`: Gita Press = three-eyed Devī. Bālavikās Tamil padavurai = wife of the three-eyed one (Śiva). Lead gloss follows Gita Press.
- `नारायणि`: Caṇḍī / Gita Press = vocative of the Goddess of this stuti. Popular Tamil gloss “sister of Nārāyaṇa” is later bāla-pāṭha, not the Purāṇa arth.
- `शिवे` is “O auspicious Śivā,” not automatically “O Pārvatī.” The verse does address `गौरि` / `त्र्यम्बके`, which are Pārvatī names, but the living use is the Durgā / Devī / Caṇḍī greeting. Do not give this verse to the Pārvatī findings file.
- No separate āgama verse-number beyond the Purāṇa / Saptashlokī frame. Provenance is the Devī Māhātmya, not “because the web is full of it.”
- Regional hello can be the Ya Devī refrain (Bengal) or a Tamil Ambā song; सर्वमङ्गलमाङ्गल्ये is still the pan-Indian Sanskrit short prayer.
