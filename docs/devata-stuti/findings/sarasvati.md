# Sarasvatī

## Proposal

Devanagari:

```
या कुन्देन्दुतुषारहारधवला या शुभ्रवस्त्रावृता
या वीणावरदण्डमण्डितकरा या श्वेतपद्मासना ।
या ब्रह्माच्युतशङ्करप्रभृतिभिर्देवैः सदा वन्दिता
सा मां पातु सरस्वती भगवती निःशेषजाड्यापहा ॥
```

IAST:

```
yā kundendutuṣārahāradhavalā yā śubhravastrāvṛtā
yā vīṇāvaradaṇḍamaṇḍitakarā yā śvetapadmāsanā |
yā brahmācyutaśaṅkaraprabhṛtibhir devaiḥ sadā vanditā
sā māṃ pātu sarasvatī bhagavatī niḥśeṣajāḍyāpahā ||
```

Meter: śārdūlavikrīḍita (19 syllables a pāda, yati 12 + 7). Longer than vakratuṇḍa (anuṣṭubh). That is the school-assembly shape: four sung lines, not two. Keep it if this is the hello; do not trim pādas.

Padaccheda: no Vedic-style padapāṭha found. SSS Balvikas (Hindi/Telugu Group I prayer) splits it for children; proposed from that table plus sandhi:

या कुन्द-इन्दु-तुषार-हार-धवला या शुभ्र-वस्त्र-आवृता
या वीणा-वर-दण्ड-मण्डित-करा या श्वेत-पद्म-आसना ।
या ब्रह्म-अच्युत-शङ्कर-प्रभृतिभिः देवैः सदा वन्दिता
सा मां पातु सरस्वती भगवती निःशेष-जाड्य-अपहा ॥

Word gloss:
- या — she who
- कुन्द — white jasmine
- इन्दु — moon
- तुषार — frost / snow
- हार — garland
- धवला — white (fem.)
- कुन्देन्दुतुषारहारधवला — white like kunda, the moon, and a garland of snow
- शुभ्र — bright / pure white
- वस्त्र — garment
- आवृता — wrapped, covered
- वीणा — vīṇā
- वर — excellent
- दण्ड — neck / staff of the vīṇā (Balvikas; see Variants)
- मण्डित — adorned
- करा — whose hands
- श्वेत — white
- पद्म — lotus
- आसना — whose seat
- ब्रह्म — Brahmā
- अच्युत — Acyuta (Viṣṇu)
- शङ्कर — Śaṅkara
- प्रभृतिभिः — by (the gods) beginning with
- देवैः — by the gods
- सदा — always
- वन्दिता — praised / bowed to
- सा — she
- मां — me
- पातु — may she protect
- सरस्वती — Sarasvatī
- भगवती — Blessed Lady
- निःशेष — without remainder
- जाड्य — dullness / stupor / ignorance

Translation: “She who is white like jasmine, the moon, and a garland of snow; who is wrapped in pure white cloth; whose hands are adorned with the excellent neck of the vīṇā; who sits on a white lotus; who is ever praised by the gods beginning with Brahmā, Acyuta, and Śaṅkara — may that Blessed Lady Sarasvatī, who utterly removes dullness, protect me.”

Function: dhyānaśloka that closes as prārthanā (*pātu*). Lived use is school / assembly vandana, Vasant Pañcamī, and the dhyāna step of Sarasvatī pūjā.

## Claims

(a) exists: yes — printed as a complete śārdūlavikrīḍita verse in more than one stotra frame (Agastya-prokta opening; Rudrayāmala Brahma-stava v. 4; Kāñcī Stotramañjarī dhyānam v. 3)
(b) traditionally used: yes — pūjā dhyāna, vidyārambha / Vasant Pañcamī, bāla-pāṭha (SSS Balvikas Group I), pan-Indian school vandana
(c) standard short prayer: yes — this is the greeting children actually say; not a 108-name stotra and not a 20-verse excerpt in living use
Confidence in (c): high

## Variants

Do not splice.

- *वन्दिता* (this proposal: school vandana, Balvikas, Rudrayāmala v. 4) vs *पूजिता* (Agastya opening, Kāñcī dhyānam v. 3, Vignanam)
- *निःशेष* vs *निश्शेष* (same word; optional ś-assimilation)
- *देवैः सदा* vs *देवैस्सदा*
- *शङ्कर* vs *शंकर* (orthography)
- *वीणा-वर-दण्ड* (excellent vīṇā-neck: Balvikas Hindi/Telugu) vs *वीणा-वरदण्ड* (vīṇā and boon-staff: some Hindi school glosses). Iconography favours the veena-neck reading; Sarasvatī’s extra hand is usually book / akṣamālā / abhaya, not a separate staff.

## Other candidates

- `सरस्वति नमस्तुभ्यं वरदे कामरूपिणि । विद्यारम्भं करिष्यामि सिद्धिर्भवतु मे सदा ॥` — anuṣṭubh, vakratuṇḍa length. Vidyārambha / “I am starting studies,” also verse 5 of the Agastya stotra. Strong (a)(b); function is start-of-lesson, not the assembly hello. Lead only if the book wants the short namaskāra instead of the vandana everyone sings.
- `शुक्लां ब्रह्मविचारसारपरमामाद्यां जगद्व्यापिनीं … बुद्धिप्रदां शारदाम्` — same meter; often printed as verse 2 after this one in pūjā books (Drik, Rudrayāmala stava v. 3). Śāradā dhyāna. Known, but school kids lead with *yā kundendu*.
- `नमस्ते शारदे देवि काश्मीरपुरवासिनि । त्वामहं प्रार्थये नित्यं विद्यादानं च देहि मे ॥` — first verse of a seven-verse Śāradā stotra. Real, used; Kashmir / Śāradā-pīṭha colour. Regional, not the pan-Indian hello.
- Full Agastya stotra (21 verses), Sarasvatī aṣṭottara, Yājñavalkya-kṛta stotra — real texts, wrong size.

## Uncertainties

- Agastya ascription is a stotra colophon, not a checked mūla. Rudrayāmala / Brahma-viracita is another traditional frame for the same floating dhyāna. Both cannot be “the” origin; do not encode a Purāṇa citation.
- Recension choice: school *vanditā* (this file) vs stotra *pūjitā*. Pick one; do not mix.
- *वरदण्ड* split (veena-neck vs boon-staff).
- Not Vedic. Blogs that call it a Veda mantra are wrong.
