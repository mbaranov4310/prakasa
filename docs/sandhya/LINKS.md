# Sentence links — one chapter

A link says which word leans on which. It uses word ids that already exist. It does not change the chapter or the meaning strings.

Roles:

- subject — who is doing the verb
- object — what the verb is done to
- describes — the adjective or participle agrees with this word
- complement — the word a participle or indeclinable is aimed at (`gone into` the state, `namaḥ` toward the dative)
- same — this word and that word are the same person (`yaḥ` and `saḥ`)

`obvious` means one head and one role. `unsure` means two legal heads or two legal roles. An unsure link leaves `to` blank and says why in one sentence. A word whose case is already unsure does not grow a guessed link. An indeclinable with nothing to lean on is omitted.

```
## subject
from: s-smaret
to: s-yah
judgment: obvious

## describes
from: s-bahya
judgment: unsure
why: The case ending sits on the next member.
```
