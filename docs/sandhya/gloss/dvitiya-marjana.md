# dvitiya-marjana

## dadhikrāvṇo
ids: dm-dadhikravnno
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dadhikrāvan
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: dadhikrāvṇaḥ before a vowel. An n-stem, so -ṇaḥ is genitive here, agreeing with aśvasya. The same spelling is also ablative singular or accusative plural.
meaning: Dadhikrāvan

## akāriṣam
ids: dm-akarisam
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
tense: recent past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 1st
number: singular
note: A Vedic iṣ-aorist. The classical form is akārṣam.
meaning: I have performed

## jiṣṇo
ids: dm-jisnoh

gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
root: ji
note: jiṣṇoḥ before a vowel is jiṣṇor. The r is written on the next word.

## raśvasya

## vājinaḥ
ids: dm-vajinah
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: vājin
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: vājin is an in-stem, so -aḥ is genitive here, not nominative. It agrees with aśvasya.
meaning: of the vigorous (possessed of vāja)

## surabhi
ids: dm-surabhi
kind: adjective
judgment: unsure
why: surabhi with mukhā is a Vedic neuter plural with a short ending, and the same spelling reads as a neuter singular.
meaning: fragrant
