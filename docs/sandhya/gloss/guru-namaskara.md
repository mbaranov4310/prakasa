# guru-namaskara

## oṃ
ids: g-om
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: primordial sound

## śrī
ids: g-sri
kind: adjective
judgment: unsure
why: śrī is an honorific front member of śrīgurubhyaḥ with no ending of its own, and it reads either as the adjective “auspicious” or as the noun “glory.”
meaning: auspicious; an honorific of reverence

## gurubhyo
ids: g-gurubhyo
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: guru
gender: masculine
case: dative
number: plural
note: gurubhyaḥ before a voiced consonant. -bhyaḥ also forms the ablative plural, but namaḥ takes the dative, “to the Gurus.”
meaning: to the Gurus (the lineage of teachers)

## namaḥ
ids: g-namah
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: reverence, salutation
