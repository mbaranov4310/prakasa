# guru-namaskara

## oṃ
ids: g-om
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: primordial sound

## śrī
ids: g-sri
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: auspicious; an honorific of reverence

## gurubhyo
ids: g-gurubhyo
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: The form is gurubhyaḥ before namaḥ, and -bhyaḥ is both dative and ablative plural.
meaning: to the Gurus (the lineage of teachers)

## namaḥ
ids: g-namah
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: reverence, salutation
