# pranayama

## oṃ
ids: p-om-bhuh, p-om-bhuvah, p-om-mahah, p-om-janah, p-om-tapah, p-om-gayatri, p-om-siro, p-om-close
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: primordial sound

## ogm
ids: p-ogm-suvah, p-ogm-satyam
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: ogm is oṃ recited with a nasal glide.
meaning: primordial sound

## bhūḥ
ids: p-bhuh
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: bhūḥ is both nominative and vocative singular of bhū, and the list has no verb to decide between them.
meaning: earth (here: this world)

## bhuvaḥ
ids: p-bhuvah
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: bhuvas is an indeclinable vyāhṛti. Its s is written as visarga at a pause.
meaning: mid-region (here: the atmospheric realm)

## suvah
ids: p-suvah
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: suvar is the Taittirīya spelling of svar, an indeclinable. Its r is written as visarga at a pause.
meaning: heaven (here: the celestial realm)

## mahaḥ
ids: p-mahah
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: mahaḥ is nominative or accusative singular of the neuter mahas, and the stored sense “great” reads as an adjective.
meaning: great (here: the great realm)
