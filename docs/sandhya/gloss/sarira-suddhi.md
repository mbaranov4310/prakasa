# sarira-suddhi

## oṃ
ids: s-om
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: primordial sound

## apavitraḥ
ids: s-apavitrah
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: apavitra
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
meaning: impure

## pavitro
ids: s-pavitro
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: pavitra
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: pavitraḥ before a voiced consonant.
meaning: pure

## vā
ids: s-va-1, s-va-2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: or

## sarvāvasthāṅ
ids: s-sarvavasthan
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sarvāvasthā
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: sthā
note: sarvāvasthām before g, written with ṅ. avasthā is built from ava + sthā.
meaning: every state

## gato
ids: s-gatah
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: gam
participle: past
stem: gata
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: gataḥ before a. The a of api drops.
meaning: being in

## ’pi
ids: s-api
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: api after o. Its first a is elided and written with an avagraha.
meaning: even

## yaḥ
ids: s-yah
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
meaning: he who

## smaret
ids: s-smaret
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: smṛ
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
meaning: remembers

## puṇḍarīkākṣaṃ
ids: s-pundarikaksam
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: puṇḍarīkākṣa
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: puṇḍarīkākṣam before s, written with anusvāra.
meaning: Lotus-Eyed Lord

## sa
ids: s-sah
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: saḥ before a consonant.
meaning: he

## bāhyā
ids: s-bahya
kind: adjective
judgment: unsure
why: bāhya is the first member of bāhyābhyantaraḥ. The ending sits on abhyantaraḥ.
meaning: outer

## bhyantaraḥ
ids: s-abhyantarah
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: bāhyābhyantara
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The last member of bāhyābhyantaraḥ. The long ā at the join is written on bāhyā.
meaning: inner

## śuciḥ
ids: s-sucih
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śuci
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
