# bhutocchatanam

## uttiṣṭhantu
ids: b-uttisthantu
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: ud
class: bhū
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
meaning: let them rise (here: let them depart)

## bhūta
ids: b-bhuta
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: This is the prior member of bhūtapiśācāḥ, so it shows the stem with no case ending, and the same shape is masculine vocative singular.
meaning: subtle being

## piśācāḥ
ids: b-pisacah
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: The ending -āḥ is both nominative and vocative plural.
meaning: restless spirit

## ye
ids: b-ye-1, b-ye-2
kind: pronoun
judgment: unsure
why: ye is masculine nominative plural of yad, and also feminine and neuter nominative and accusative dual.
meaning: who

## te
ids: b-te
kind: pronoun
judgment: unsure
why: te is masculine nominative plural of tad, also feminine and neuter dual, and also the enclitic dative or genitive.
meaning: they

## bhūmi
ids: b-bhumi
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: This is the prior member of bhūmibhārakāḥ, so the word shows the stem and the case ending sits on bhārakāḥ.
meaning: earth

## bhārakāḥ
ids: b-bharakah
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: The ending -āḥ is both nominative and vocative plural.
meaning: those who burden

## teṣām
ids: b-tesam
kind: pronoun
judgment: unsure
why: teṣām is genitive plural of tad in both masculine and neuter.
meaning: of them

## avarodhena
ids: b-avarodhena
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: avarodha
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: rudh
meaning: by obstruction

## brahma
ids: b-brahma
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: This is the prior member of brahmakarma, so it shows the stem with no case ending, and the same shape is nominative, accusative, or vocative singular of brahman.
meaning: Vedic

## karma
ids: b-karma
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: karma is the nominative, accusative, and vocative singular of karman.
meaning: act

## samārabhe
ids: b-samarabhe
kind: verb
judgment: unsure
why: samārabhe is present and ordinary past, both first-person singular ātmanepada, because the augment merges with the prefix ā.
meaning: I begin
