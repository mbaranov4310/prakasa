# bhutocchatanam

## uttiṣṭhantu
ids: b-uttisthantu
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: sthā
prefix: ud
class: bhū
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
note: ud + tiṣṭhantu, with d becoming t before t. sthā takes the present stem tiṣṭha.
meaning: let them rise (here: let them depart)

## bhūta
ids: b-bhuta
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: bhūta is the first member of bhūtapiśācāḥ. The ending sits on piśācāḥ.
meaning: subtle being

## piśācāḥ
ids: b-pisacah
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūtapiśāca
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: This is the ending of the dvandva bhūtapiśācāḥ, “bhūtas and piśācas.”
meaning: restless spirit

## ye
ids: b-ye-1, b-ye-2
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: The form is also a feminine or neuter dual, but nothing in the chapter is dual.
meaning: who

## te
ids: b-te
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
meaning: they

## bhūmi
ids: b-bhumi
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: bhūmi is the first member of bhūmibhārakāḥ. The ending sits on bhārakāḥ.
meaning: earth

## bhārakāḥ
ids: b-bharakah
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūmibhāraka
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: bhṛ
meaning: those who burden

## teṣām
ids: b-tesam
kind: pronoun
judgment: unsure
why: teṣām is genitive plural of tad, and the form is the same in masculine and neuter.
meaning: of them

## avarodhena
ids: b-avarodhena
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: avarodha
gender: masculine
case: instrumental
number: singular
root: rudh

## brahma
ids: b-brahma
kind: noun
judgment: unsure

## karma
ids: b-karma
kind: noun

tense: present
