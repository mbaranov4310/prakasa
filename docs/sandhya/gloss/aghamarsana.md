# aghamarsana

## drupadāt
ids: ag-drupadat-1, ag-drupadat-2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: drupada
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
meaning: post (here: wooden foot-block / fetter)

## iva
ids: ag-iva-1, ag-iva-2, ag-iva-3, ag-iva-4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: like

## muñcatu
ids: ag-muncatu
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: muc
class: tud
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: The present stem muñca has a nasal the root muc lacks.
meaning: may it release

## mumucānaḥ
ids: ag-mumucanah
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: muc
participle: perfect, suffix āna
stem: mumucāna
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
meaning: one who has been released

## svinnaḥ
ids: ag-svinnah
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: svid
participle: past
stem: svinna
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
meaning: having sweated

## snātvī
ids: ag-snatvi
kind: indeclinable
judgment: unsure
why: snātvī carries the Vedic absolutive ending -tvī, where the classical form is snātvā.
meaning: having bathed

## malāt
ids: ag-malat
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: malāt is ablative singular, and mala can be masculine or neuter.
meaning: from dirt

## pūtam
ids: ag-putam
kind: participle
judgment: unsure
why: pūtam can be neuter nominative or accusative with ājyam, or masculine accusative describing the implied speaker.
meaning: purified

## pavitreṇa
ids: ag-pavitrena
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pavitra
gender: neuter
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: pavitreṇa joins iva as pavitreṇeva in recitation.
meaning: with the strainer / purifier

## ājyam
ids: ag-ajyam
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: ājyam is nominative or accusative singular of ājya.
meaning: ghee

## āpaḥ
ids: ag-apah
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: āpaḥ is nominative or vocative plural of ap. The third-person śundhantu suggests a subject, but the chapter's phrase translates it as “O waters.”
meaning: waters

## śundhantu
ids: ag-sundhantu
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: śundh
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: plural
meaning: may they cleanse

## me
ids: ag-me
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: genitive
number: singular
note: me is the enclitic form for both genitive and dative. The meaning “my” is the genitive. asmad has no gender.
meaning: my

## manasaḥ
ids: ag-manasah
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: manasaḥ is genitive or ablative singular of manas. The meaning says “of the mind,” but no word in the line is the thing possessed, and “cleanse my mind” would need the accusative manaḥ.
meaning: of the mind
