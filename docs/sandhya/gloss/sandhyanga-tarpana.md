# sandhyanga-tarpana

## oṃ
ids: st-om-sandhya, st-om-gayatri, st-om-brahmi, st-om-nimrji
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: primordial sound

## sandhyāṃ
ids: st-sandhyam
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sandhyā
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
note: sandhyām with the final m written as anusvāra.
meaning: Sandhyā (here: the meeting of night and day)

## tarpayāmi
ids: st-tarpayami-sandhya, st-tarpayami-gayatri, st-tarpayami-brahmi, st-tarpayami-nimrji

judgment: obvious

number: singular
note: Causative of tṛp.
meaning: I offer / I satiate
