# bhagavan-namaskara

## namo
ids: bn-namo
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: namaḥ before astu, whose initial a is elided.
meaning: salutations

## ’stva
ids: bn-astu
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: astu after namo, with its initial a elided. Its u becomes v before the a of anantāya.
meaning: may there be

## nantāya
ids: bn-anantaya
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ananta
gender: masculine
case: dative
number: singular
note: anantāya. Its initial a is written with the v of astu.
meaning: to the Infinite

## sahasra
ids: bn-sahasra-1, bn-sahasra-2, bn-sahasra-3, bn-sahasra-4
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: Each sahasra is the first member of a compound the chapter splits, and the ending sits on that compound's last member (mūrtaye, bāhave, nāmne, dhāriṇe).
meaning: thousand

## mūrtaye
ids: bn-murtaye
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sahasramūrti
gender: masculine
case: dative
number: singular
note: The bahuvrīhi sahasramūrti, “having a thousand forms,” agrees with anantāya. mūrti is feminine on its own.
meaning: to the form

## pādā
ids: bn-pada
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: pāda is a middle member of sahasrapādākṣiśirorubāhave, its long ā is pāda joined with akṣi, and the ending sits on bāhave.
meaning: feet

## kṣi
ids: bn-aksi
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: akṣi is a middle member of sahasrapādākṣiśirorubāhave, its a is merged into pādā, and the ending sits on bāhave.
meaning: eyes

## śiro
ids: bn-siras
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: śiras is a middle member of sahasrapādākṣiśirorubāhave, its join with ūru cannot be split cleanly, and the ending sits on bāhave.
meaning: head

## ru
ids: bn-uru
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: ūru is a middle member of sahasrapādākṣiśirorubāhave, its ū is merged into śiro, and the ending sits on bāhave.
meaning: thighs

## bāhave
ids: bn-bahave
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sahasrapādākṣiśirorubāhu
gender: masculine
case: dative
number: singular
note: The bahuvrīhi “having a thousand feet, eyes, heads, thighs, and arms” agrees with anantāya. The plural sense belongs to the members; the singular ending belongs to the compound.
meaning: arms

## nāmne
ids: bn-namne
kind: adjective
judgment: unsure
why: nāmne is dative singular of nāman, ending the bahuvrīhi sahasranāman that agrees with puruṣāya, but the stored meaning “to the names” reads as a dative plural.
meaning: to the names

## puruṣāya
ids: bn-purusaya
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: puruṣa
gender: masculine
case: dative
number: singular
meaning: to the Puruṣa

## śāśvate
ids: bn-sasvate
kind: adjective
judgment: unsure
why: śāśvate reads as a dative only from a consonant stem śāśvat; from the usual stem śāśvata it is a locative, whose dative would be śāśvatāya.
meaning: to the eternal

## koṭī
ids: bn-koti
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: koṭī is a middle member of sahasrakoṭīyugadhāriṇe with a lengthened ī, and the ending sits on dhāriṇe.
meaning: crore

## yuga
ids: bn-yuga
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: yuga is a middle member of sahasrakoṭīyugadhāriṇe, and the ending sits on dhāriṇe.
meaning: age

## dhāriṇe
ids: bn-dharine
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: sahasrakoṭīyugadhārin
gender: masculine
case: dative
number: singular
root: dhṛ
note: The n of -in is ṇ after the r.
meaning: to the sustainer

## namaḥ
ids: bn-namah
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: salutations
