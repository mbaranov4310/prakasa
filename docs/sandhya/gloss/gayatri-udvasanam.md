# gayatri-udvasanam

## uttame
ids: gu-uttame
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: uttama
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
meaning: on the highest

## śikhare
ids: gu-sikhare
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: śikhara
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
meaning: peak

## jāte
ids: gu-jate
kind: participle
judgment: unsure
why: jāte is a locative singular agreeing with śikhare, or a feminine vocative singular addressing the Devī.
meaning: born / arisen

## bhūmyāṃ
ids: gu-bhumyam
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: bhūmi
gender: feminine
case: locative
number: singular
note: bhūmyām with anusvāra before a consonant.
meaning: on the earth

## parvata
ids: gu-parvata
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: parvata is the first member of parvatamūrdhani. The ending sits on mūrdhani.
meaning: mountain

## mūrdhani
ids: gu-murdhani
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: parvatamūrdhan
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
meaning: on the summit

## brāhmaṇebhyo
ids: gu-brahmanebhyah
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: brāhmaṇebhyaḥ is dative or ablative plural, and the meaning “by” asks for an instrumental the ending does not have.
meaning: by the brāhmaṇas

## ’bhyanujñātā
ids: gu-abhyanujnata
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: jñā
prefix: abhi, anu
participle: past
stem: abhyanujñātā
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: abhyanujñātā with its first a dropped after brāhmaṇebhyo (avagraha).
meaning: having been permitted

## gaccha
ids: gu-gaccha
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: gam
class: bhū
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: singular
note: gam takes the present stem gaccha.
meaning: go

## devi
ids: gu-devi
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: devī
gender: feminine
case: vocative
number: singular
meaning: O Devī

## yathā
ids: gu-yatha
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: yathā is the first member of the adverb yathāsukham.
meaning: as

## sukham
ids: gu-sukham
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: The meaning is the noun sukha, but sukham closes the adverb yathāsukham, so its -am is a frozen ending, not a live accusative.
meaning: comfort

## stuto
ids: gu-stuto
kind: participle
judgment: unsure
why: stuto reads as masculine nominative stutaḥ, but the one praised, varadā vedamātā, is feminine, and the usual text has stutā.
meaning: praised

## mayā
ids: gu-maya
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: instrumental
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender.
meaning: by me

## varadā
ids: gu-varada
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: varadā
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: dā
meaning: boon-giver

## veda
ids: gu-veda
kind: noun
judgment: unsure

meaning: Veda

## mātā
ids: gu-mata
kind: noun
judgment: obvious

gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular

## pracodayantī
ids: gu-pracodayanti
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: cud
prefix: pra
participle: present
stem: pracodayantī
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: Causative of cud.
meaning: impelling

## pavane
ids: gu-pavane
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: pavane is locative singular of pavana, but the line reads it as “like the vital air,” which the locative does not carry, and the usual text has pāvamānī here.
meaning: wind / vital air

## dvijātā
ids: gu-dvijata
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: dvijātā reads as a feminine nominative singular with vedamātā, but the verse takes the twice-born as those she impels, and the usual text has dvijānām.
meaning: twice-born

## āyuḥ
ids: gu-ayuh
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: āyuḥ is nominative or accusative singular of āyus.
meaning: long life

## pṛthivyāṃ
ids: gu-prthivyam
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: pṛthivī
gender: feminine
case: locative
number: singular
note: pṛthivyām with anusvāra before a consonant.
meaning: on earth

## draviṇaṃ
ids: gu-dravinam
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: draviṇam is nominative or accusative singular of draviṇa.
meaning: wealth

## brahma
ids: gu-brahma-1, gu-brahma-2
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: brahma is the first member of brahmavarcasam and of brahmalokam. The ending sits on the next word.
meaning: Brahman / Veda

## varcasam
ids: gu-varcasam
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: varcasam is nominative or accusative singular of brahmavarcasa.
meaning: radiance

## mahyaṃ
ids: gu-mahyam
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: dative
number: singular
note: asmad has no gender. mahyam with anusvāra before a consonant.
meaning: to me

## dattvā
ids: gu-dattva
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Absolutive (-tvā) of dā, “having given.” It does not decline.
meaning: having given

## prajātuṃ
ids: gu-prajatum
kind: indeclinable
judgment: unsure
why: prajātum looks like a -tum infinitive, but no root gives jātum with the sense “depart,” and the usual text has vrajata.
meaning: to go forth / to depart

## lokam
ids: gu-lokam
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: brahmaloka
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
meaning: world
