# bhumy-akasa-abhivandanam

## idam
ids: ba-idam
kind: pronoun
judgment: unsure
why: idam is nominative or accusative singular neuter of idam.
meaning: this

## dyāvā
ids: ba-dyava
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: dyāvā is the first member of dyāvāpṛthivī. The ending sits on pṛthivī.
meaning: Heaven

## pṛthivī
ids: ba-prthivi
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: pṛthivī closes the dual compound dyāvāpṛthivī, and -ī reads as nominative, accusative, or vocative dual.
meaning: Earth

## satyam
ids: ba-satyam
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: satyam can be the noun “truth” or the adjective “true,” and the form is nominative or accusative.
meaning: truth

## yadi
ids: ba-yadi
kind: pronoun
judgment: unsure
why: The syllables ya di carry yad plus the i of iha, and the meaning gives both the pronoun “which” and the indeclinable “if.”
meaning: which / if

## iha
ids: ba-iha
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Written ho. iha + upa joins as ihopa, and the i of iha is written with yadi.
meaning: here

## upa
ids: ba-upa
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Preverb of bruve, written pa after iha.
meaning: toward

## bruve
ids: ba-bruve
kind: verb
judgment: obvious

class: ad
tense: present
voice: ātmanepada
person: 1st
number: singular
meaning: I speak

## vām
ids: ba-vam
kind: pronoun
judgment: unsure
why: vām is the enclitic dual of yuṣmad for the accusative, dative, and genitive, and upa bruve can take the ones addressed as an accusative.
meaning: to you two

## bhūtam
ids: ba-bhutam
kind: verb
judgment: unsure
why: bhūtam is a Vedic root-aorist command, 2nd dual, of bhū, and the same spelling is the past participle bhūtam.
meaning: be ye
