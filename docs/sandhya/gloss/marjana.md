# marjana

## oṃ
ids: m-om
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: primordial sound

## āpo
ids: m-apo-1, m-apo-2
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: āpaḥ is the nominative or the vocative plural of ap, and the two forms are identical beside a second-person verb.
meaning: waters (here: sacred)

## hi
ids: m-hi
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: indeed

## ṣṭhā
ids: m-stha
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: plural
note: stha, with ṣ after hi and the final a lengthened for the meter.
meaning: you are

## mayo
ids: m-mayo
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: mayo is the first member of mayobhuvaḥ. The ending sits on bhuvaḥ.
meaning: joy

## bhuvaḥ
ids: m-bhuvah
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: mayobhū
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: plural
root: bhū
note: This is the ending of mayobhuvaḥ, predicate with ṣṭhā and agreeing with āpaḥ. The form also reads as accusative plural or genitive singular, but ṣṭhā takes the nominative.
meaning: becoming / source

## tā
ids: m-ta
kind: pronoun
judgment: unsure
why: tāḥ is the nominative or accusative plural feminine of tad.
meaning: they

## na
ids: m-na-1
kind: pronoun
judgment: unsure
why: naḥ can be accusative (“set us in strength”) or dative (“bestow on us”) with dadhātana.
meaning: us

## ūrje
ids: m-urje
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ūrj
gender: feminine
case: dative
number: singular
meaning: for strength

## dadhātana
ids: m-dadhatana
kind: verb
judgment: unsure
why: dadhātana carries the Vedic second plural ending -tana.
meaning: bestow

## mahe
ids: m-mahe
kind: adjective
judgment: unsure
why: mahe is dative singular of mah and can agree with raṇāya or with cakṣase, which differ in gender.
meaning: for the great

## raṇāya
ids: m-ranaya
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: raṇa
gender: masculine
case: dative
number: singular
meaning: for joy / for battle

## cakṣase
ids: m-caksase
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: cakṣas
gender: neuter
case: dative
number: singular
root: cakṣ
note: The same dative also serves as a Vedic infinitive, “to see.”
meaning: for sight

## yo
ids: m-yo
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yad
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: yaḥ before a voiced consonant.
meaning: which

## vaḥ
ids: m-vah
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: yuṣmad
case: genitive
number: plural
note: yuṣmad has no gender. vaḥ also stands for the accusative and dative; here it is “your” essence.
meaning: your

## śivatamo
ids: m-sivatamo
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: śivatama
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: śivatamaḥ before a voiced consonant. Superlative of śiva.
meaning: most auspicious

## rasaḥ
ids: m-rasah
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: rasa
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
meaning: essence

## tasya
ids: m-tasya
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: tad
gender: masculine
case: genitive
number: singular
note: It refers back to rasaḥ. The neuter form is the same.
meaning: of that

## bhājayate
ids: m-bhajayate
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhaj
tense: command
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: plural
note: Causative of bhaj. The word is bhājayata; its final a joins the i of iha to make e.
meaning: grant a share

## ha
ids: m-iha
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: iha, with its i merged into the e of bhājayate.
meaning: here

## naḥ
ids: m-nah-2, m-nah-3
kind: pronoun
judgment: obvious
stem: asmad
case: accusative
number: plural
note: asmad has no gender. naḥ also stands for the dative and genitive; the causatives bhājayata and janayatha take the person in the accusative.
meaning: us

## uśatīri
ids: m-usatir
kind: participle
judgment: unsure
why: uśatīḥ is a Vedic feminine plural of the present participle of vaś (classical uśatyaḥ), and it reads as nominative or accusative.
meaning: loving

## va
ids: m-iva
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: iva, with its i joined to the r of uśatīr.
meaning: like

## mātaraḥ
ids: m-matarah
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: mātṛ
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: plural
meaning: mothers

## tasmā
ids: m-tasma
kind: pronoun
judgment: unsure
why: tasmā is tasmai before a vowel, dative singular masculine or neuter, and the chapter gives three different things it refers to.
meaning: for that

## araṃ
ids: m-aram
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: A frozen accusative, “fittingly.”
meaning: fittingly / quickly

## gamāma
ids: m-gamama
kind: verb
judgment: unsure
why: gamāma is a Vedic aorist subjunctive of gam, which has no lesson tense name.
meaning: let us go

## vaḥ
ids: m-vah-to
kind: pronoun
judgment: unsure
why: vaḥ can be the accusative goal of gamāma or a dative with araṃ gam.
meaning: to you

## yasya
ids: m-yasya
kind: pronoun
judgment: unsure
why: yasya is genitive singular masculine or neuter, and the chapter gives three different things it refers to.
meaning: whose

## kṣayāya
ids: m-ksayaya
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kṣaya
gender: masculine
case: dative
number: singular
root: kṣi
note: Both senses, destruction and dwelling, are masculine kṣaya from kṣi.
meaning: for destruction / for the abode

## jinvatha
ids: m-jinvatha

judgment: obvious
root: jinv
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada

number: plural
meaning: you invigorate / nourish

## janayathā
ids: m-janayatha
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: jan
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 2nd
number: plural
note: Causative of jan. The final a is lengthened for the meter.
meaning: you produce

## ca
ids: m-ca
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: and
