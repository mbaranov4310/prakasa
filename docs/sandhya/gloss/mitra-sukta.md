## śravo
ids: ms-sravo
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: śravaḥ is nominative or accusative singular of śravas. Nothing in the line is a verb that would take it as an object.

## devasya
ids: ms-devasya
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: devasya can belong with mitrasya (the god Mitra) or with śravaḥ (the god’s fame).
