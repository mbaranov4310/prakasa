# gayatri-udvasanam

## describes
from: gu-uttame
to: gu-sikhare
judgment: obvious

## describes
from: gu-jate
judgment: unsure
why: jāte can agree with śikhare as a locative, or address the Devī as a feminine vocative.

## complement
from: gu-parvata
to: gu-murdhani
judgment: obvious
note: parvata is the front of parvatamūrdhani and has no case of its own. The summit is the mountain's.

## complement
from: gu-brahmanebhyah
to: gu-abhyanujnata
judgment: obvious
note: Dative or ablative plural. Either way the brāhmaṇas are who gave the permission.

## describes
from: gu-abhyanujnata
judgment: unsure
why: abhyanujñātā is nominative and describes the unspoken “you” of gaccha, while devi is vocative, so no word in the line agrees with it.

## complement
from: gu-sukham
to: gu-gaccha
judgment: obvious
note: yathāsukham is an adverb, “as one pleases,” that goes with gaccha.

## complement
from: gu-maya
to: gu-stuto
judgment: obvious
note: Instrumental agent, “praised by me.”

## describes
from: gu-stuto
judgment: unsure
why: stuto is masculine, but the one praised, varadā vedamātā, is feminine, so it agrees with no word in the line.

## describes
from: gu-varada
to: gu-mata
judgment: obvious

## complement
from: gu-veda
to: gu-mata
judgment: obvious
note: veda is the front of vedamātā and has no case of its own. She is the mother of the Vedas.

## describes
from: gu-pracodayanti
to: gu-mata
judgment: obvious

## describes
from: gu-dvijata
judgment: unsure
why: dvijātā can agree with vedamātā as a nominative, but the verse takes the twice-born as those she impels.

## object
from: gu-dattva
to: gu-ayuh
judgment: obvious
note: dattvā is an absolutive, and the line has no finite verb. āyuḥ is nominative or accusative, and dattvā needs an object.

## object
from: gu-dattva
to: gu-dravinam
judgment: obvious
note: dattvā is an absolutive. draviṇam is nominative or accusative, and dattvā needs an object.

## object
from: gu-dattva
to: gu-varcasam
judgment: obvious
note: dattvā is an absolutive. brahmavarcasam is nominative or accusative, and dattvā needs an object.

## complement
from: gu-mahyam
to: gu-dattva
judgment: obvious
note: Dative. I am the one it is given to.

## complement
from: gu-brahma-1
to: gu-varcasam
judgment: obvious
note: brahma is the front of brahmavarcasam and has no case of its own. The radiance is Brahman's.

## complement
from: gu-brahma-2
to: gu-lokam
judgment: obvious
note: brahma is the front of brahmalokam and has no case of its own. The world is Brahman's.
