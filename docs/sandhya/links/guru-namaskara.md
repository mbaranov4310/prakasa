# guru-namaskara

## complement
from: g-gurubhyo
to: g-namah
judgment: obvious
note: Dative with namaḥ. There is no finite verb, so namaḥ is the head. The Gurus are who the salutation is to.

## describes
from: g-sri
judgment: unsure
why: śrī is a front member with no case of its own, and it may be an adjective or the noun “glory,” so it does not plainly agree with gurubhyaḥ.
