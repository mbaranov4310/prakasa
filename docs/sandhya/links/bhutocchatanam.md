# bhutocchatanam

## subject
from: b-uttisthantu
to: b-pisacah
judgment: obvious
note: bhūta is the front of the compound, so the subject is the word that carries the ending.

## same
from: b-ye-1
to: b-te
judgment: obvious

## describes
from: b-bharakah
to: b-te
judgment: obvious
note: ye is that same group.

## complement
from: b-bharakah
to: b-bhumi
judgment: obvious
note: The earth is what they burden. bhūmi is the front of the compound and has no case of its own.

## complement
from: b-tesam
judgment: unsure
why: teṣām can be partitive with ye (those of them who) or the possessor of avarodhena (their obstruction).

## complement
from: b-avarodhena
judgment: unsure
why: Its line has no verb, and taking it with samārabhe would make the act begin by obstruction, which the stored meanings do not say.

## object
from: b-samarabhe
to: b-karma
judgment: obvious
note: karma is nominative or accusative. samārabhe is first person, so the act is what is begun.
