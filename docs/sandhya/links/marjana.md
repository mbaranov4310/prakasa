# marjana

## complement
from: m-bhuvah
to: m-mayo
judgment: obvious
note: Joy is what the waters are the source of. mayo is the front of the compound and has no case of its own.

## describes
from: m-bhuvah
to: m-apo-1
judgment: obvious
note: mayobhuvaḥ is predicate with ṣṭhā and agrees with āpaḥ, whether āpaḥ is nominative or vocative.

## subject
from: m-dadhatana
to: m-ta
judgment: obvious
note: tāḥ is nominative or accusative. It is the “you, such as you are” who bestow.

## complement
from: m-urje
to: m-dadhatana
judgment: obvious
note: Dative. Strength is what they bestow for.

## describes
