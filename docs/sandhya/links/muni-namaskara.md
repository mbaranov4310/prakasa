## object
from: mn-vardhayantu

note: cirañjīvitam is nominative or accusative. te is already the subject, so long life is what they increase.

## describes
from: mn-atmanah
to: mn-te
judgment: obvious
note: prasannātmānaḥ, “gracious in spirit,” agrees with te.

## describes
from: mn-prasanna
judgment: unsure
why: The ending sits on the next member, and prasanna can describe ātman inside the compound or te through the whole compound.

## complement
from: mn-me
judgment: unsure
why: As a genitive, me goes with cirañjīvitam (“my long life”), and as a dative, it goes with vardhayantu (“increase for me”).
