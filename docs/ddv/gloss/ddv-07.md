# ddv-07

## chāyā'
ids: ddv-07-w1
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: chāyā is the first member of the dvandva chāyāhaṅkāra (the avagraha marks the elided a of ahaṅkāra), so it has no case of its own and the ending sits on haṅkārayo.
meaning: reflection

## haṅkārayo
ids: ddv-07-w2
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: haṅkārayo is ahaṅkārayoḥ, the dual ending of the dvandva chāyāhaṅkāra, and -ayoḥ on an a-stem is either genitive or locative dual.
meaning: and the ego

## raikyaṃ
ids: ddv-07-w3
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: raikyaṃ is aikyam with the r of ahaṅkārayor carried onto it, and aikyam is nominative or accusative singular of the neuter aikya.
meaning: identity

## taptā
ids: ddv-07-w4
kind: participle
judgment: unsure
why: taptā is tapta, the first member of taptāyaḥpiṇḍavat joined to the a of ayaḥ, so it has no case of its own and the compound ends on piṇḍavan.
meaning: heated

## yaḥ
ids: ddv-07-w5
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: yaḥ is ayaḥ, a middle member of taptāyaḥpiṇḍavat whose a is written on taptā, so it has no case of its own and the compound ends on piṇḍavan.
meaning: iron

## piṇḍavan
ids: ddv-07-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: piṇḍavat, the -vat of comparison (“like”), closing the compound taptāyaḥpiṇḍavat. piṇḍavat before m is written piṇḍavan.
meaning: like a ball

## matam
ids: ddv-07-w7
kind: participle
judgment: unsure
why: matam is the past participle of man, and the form is neuter nominative or accusative, or masculine accusative, singular.
meaning: is held (by the wise)

## tada
ids: ddv-07-w8
kind: pronoun
judgment: unsure
why: tada is tad, the first member of tadahaṅkāratādātmyād carrying the a of ahaṅkāra, so it has no case of its own and the ending sits on tādātmyād.
meaning: that

## haṅkāra
ids: ddv-07-w9
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: haṅkāra is ahaṅkāra, a middle member of tadahaṅkāratādātmyād whose a is written on tada, so the ending sits on tādātmyād.
meaning: ego

## tādātmyād
ids: ddv-07-w10
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
compound: tatpuruṣa
stem: tadahaṅkāratādātmya
gender: neuter
case: ablative
number: singular
note: tādātmyāt before a voiced consonant.
meaning: from identification

## dehaś
ids: ddv-07-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: deha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: dih
note: dehaḥ before c.
meaning: the body

## cetanatāma
ids: ddv-07-w12
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: cetanatā
gender: feminine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: cit
note: cetanatām, written with the augment a of agāt joined to it.
meaning: sentiency

## gāt
ids: ddv-07-w13
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: i
tense: recent past
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: agāt. gā replaces i in the recent past, and the augment a is written on cetanatāma.
meaning: has attained
