## sac
ids: ddv-25-w2
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: sac is the first member of saccidānandaḥ. The ending sits on nandaḥ.
meaning: Existence

## cidā
ids: ddv-25-w3
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: cidā is the middle member of saccidānandaḥ, carrying the ā of ānanda. The ending sits on nandaḥ.
meaning: Consciousness

## nandaḥ
ids: ddv-25-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saccidānanda
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: This is the last member of saccidānandaḥ. Its initial ā is written on cidā.
