# ddv-17

## asya
ids: ddv-17-w1
kind: pronoun
judgment: unsure
why: asya is genitive singular of idam, and the form is the same for masculine and neuter.
meaning: of this

## jīvatvamā
ids: ddv-17-w2
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: jīvatvam is nominative or accusative singular of jīvatva, and the stored spelling also carries the first ā of āropāt.
meaning: jīva-hood

## ropāt
ids: ddv-17-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: āropa
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: singular
root: ruh
note: The initial ā of āropāt is written on the previous word (jīvatvam āropāt joined as jīvatvamāropāt). āropa is built on the causative of ruh with ā.
meaning: from superimposition

## sākṣiṇya
ids: ddv-17-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sākṣin
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
note: sākṣiṇi before api; the final i becomes y, and the a of api is written on this word.

## pya
