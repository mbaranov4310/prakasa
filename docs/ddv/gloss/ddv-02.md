## bhedataḥ
ids: ddv-02-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: bheda (from bhid) with the suffix -tas, a frozen ablative sense “on account of.” It closes the compound nīlapītasthūlasūkṣmahrasvadīrghādibhedataḥ. Visarga in pause before the daṇḍa.
meaning: on account of difference
