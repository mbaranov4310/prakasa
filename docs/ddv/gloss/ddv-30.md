# ddv-30

## dehā
ids: ddv-30-w1
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: dehā is the front of dehābhimāne (deha + abhimāne, a + a → ā), and the ending sits on bhimāne.
meaning: the body

## bhimāne
ids: ddv-30-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: dehābhimāna
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
root: man
prefix: abhi
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: The initial a of abhimāne has merged into dehā by sandhi. Locative absolute with galite.
meaning: when identification with

## galite
ids: ddv-30-w3

number: singular
