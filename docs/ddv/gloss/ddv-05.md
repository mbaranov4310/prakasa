# ddv-05

## no
ids: ddv-05-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: na joined with the first vowel of udeti (na + udeti → nodeti). The u of udeti is written here.
meaning: not

## deti
ids: ddv-05-w2
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: i
prefix: ud
class: ad
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: This is udeti. Its first vowel u is written in no.
meaning: rises

## nā
ids: ddv-05-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: na joined with the first vowel of astam (na + astam → nāstam). The a of astam is written here.
meaning: not

## stame
ids: ddv-05-w4
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: This is astam, an indeclinable that means “setting” when it goes with eti. Its first vowel a is written in nā, and the first vowel e of eti is written here.
meaning: setting
