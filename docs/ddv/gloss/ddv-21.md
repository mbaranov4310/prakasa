# ddv-21

## kha
ids: ddv-21-w1
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: kha is the first member of khavāyvagnijalorvīṣu, and the locative ending sits on jalorvīṣu.
meaning: ether

## vāyva
ids: ddv-21-w2
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: vāyva is vāyu written as vāyv before the a of agni inside the compound, and the locative ending sits on jalorvīṣu.
meaning: air

## gni
ids: ddv-21-w3
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: gni is agni inside the compound with its a written on vāyva, and the locative ending sits on jalorvīṣu.
meaning: fire

## jalorvīṣu
ids: ddv-21-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: khavāyvagnijalorvī
gender: feminine
case: locative
number: plural
compound: dvandva
note: jala + urvī join as jalorvī. The ending is on urvī, a feminine ī-stem, and a dvandva of five members is plural.
meaning: water and earth

## deva
ids: ddv-21-w5
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: deva is the first member of devatiryaṅnarādiṣu, and the locative ending sits on narādiṣu.
meaning: gods

## tiryaṅ
ids: ddv-21-w6
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: tiryaṅ is tiryañc as a compound member written tiryaṅ before n, and the locative ending sits on narādiṣu.
meaning: animals

## narādiṣu
ids: ddv-21-w7
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: devatiryaṅnarādi
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: The ending is on ādi, “and the rest.” -iṣu is the i-stem locative plural, and the beings named are masculine.
meaning: humans, etc.

## abhinnāḥ
ids: ddv-21-w8
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: bhid
participle: past
stem: abhinna
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: Negative a- with bhinna. The form alone could also be feminine nominative or accusative plural; it agrees with ānandāḥ, which is masculine nominative plural.
meaning: undivided

## sac
ids: ddv-21-w9
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: sac is sat written sac before c as the first member of saccidānandāḥ, and the ending sits on nandāḥ.
meaning: Existence

## cidā
ids: ddv-21-w10
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: cidā is cit written cid with the first ā of ānanda, as a member of saccidānandāḥ, and the ending sits on nandāḥ.
meaning: Consciousness

## nandāḥ
ids: ddv-21-w11
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: saccidānanda
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: plural
compound: dvandva
note: This is the end of saccidānandāḥ; the first ā of ānanda is written on cidā. On a masculine a-stem, -āḥ is only nominative plural, and three members make the plural.
meaning: Bliss
