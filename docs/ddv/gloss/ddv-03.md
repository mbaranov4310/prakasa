# ddv-03

## āndhya
ids: ddv-03-w1
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: āndhya is the first member of āndhyamāndyapaṭutveṣu. The ending sits on paṭutveṣu.
meaning: blindness

## māndya
ids: ddv-03-w2
kind: noun
judgment: unsure

ids: ddv-03-w3
