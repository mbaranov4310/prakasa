# ddv-11

## antaḥkaraṇa
ids: ddv-11-w1
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: antaḥkaraṇa is the first member of antaḥkaraṇavṛttiḥ, and the ending sits on vṛttiś.
meaning: inner organ

number: singular

## citi
ids: ddv-11-w4
kind: noun

ids: ddv-11-w5

judgment: unsure
