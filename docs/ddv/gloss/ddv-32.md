## cidā
ids: ddv-32-w2
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: cidā can be the instrumental singular of cit, or the front of cidābhāsaḥ with the ā belonging to ābhāsa and the ending sitting on the next word.
meaning: Consciousness

## bhāsas
ids: ddv-32-w3
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: The initial ā of ābhāsa is stored on cidā, and bhāsas alone reads either as bhāsa nominative singular (bhāsaḥ before t) or as a case of the s-stem bhās.
meaning: the reflection
