# ddv-28

## akhaṇḍai
ids: ddv-28-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: unsure
why: akhaṇḍai is the front of akhaṇḍaikarasam, carrying the ai that akhaṇḍa and eka fuse into, and the ending sits on karasaṃ.
meaning: undivided

## karasaṃ
ids: ddv-28-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: unsure
why: karasaṃ is the end of akhaṇḍaikarasam, and -am on an a-stem can be masculine accusative or neuter nominative or accusative singular.
meaning: of one taste

## vastu
ids: ddv-28-w3
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: vastu is nominative or accusative singular of the neuter u-stem vastu.
meaning: the Reality

## sac
ids: ddv-28-w4
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: sac is sat before c and the front of saccidānandalakṣaṇam, so the ending sits on lakṣaṇam.
meaning: Existence

## cidā
ids: ddv-28-w5
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: cidā is cit before a vowel plus the first ā of ānanda, a middle member of saccidānandalakṣaṇam whose ending sits on lakṣaṇam.
meaning: Consciousness

## nanda
ids: ddv-28-w6
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: nanda is ānanda without the ā carried on cidā, a middle member of saccidānandalakṣaṇam whose ending sits on lakṣaṇam.
meaning: Bliss

## lakṣaṇam
ids: ddv-28-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: unsure
why: lakṣaṇam ends saccidānandalakṣaṇam, and -am on an a-stem can be masculine accusative or neuter nominative or accusative singular.
meaning: whose mark is

## itya
ids: ddv-28-w8
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: iti becomes ity before a vowel. The final a is the first letter of avicchinna, stored on this word.
meaning: thus

## vicchinna
ids: ddv-28-w9
kind: participle
judgment: unsure
why: vicchinna is the front of avicchinnacintā, its negative a sits on itya, and the ending sits on cinte.
meaning: uninterrupted

## cinte
ids: ddv-28-w10
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: cinte is cintā fused with the i of iyam, and as written it also reads as the dual or vocative of cintā.
meaning: thought

## yaṃ
ids: ddv-28-w11
kind: pronoun
judgment: unsure
why: yaṃ is iyam with its i fused into cinte, but as written it is also yam, the masculine accusative singular of yad.
meaning: this
