# ddv-10

## ahaṅkāra
ids: ddv-10-w1, ddv-10-w8
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: ahaṅkāra is the front member of a compound the chapter splits across words, so it has no case of its own and the ending sits on the last member (laye in ahaṅkāralaye, rdhaḥ in ahaṅkāravikāsārdhaḥ).
meaning: ego

## laye
ids: ddv-10-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: ahaṅkāralaya
gender: masculine
case: locative
number: singular
root: lī
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: laye is the last member of ahaṅkāralaye, “on the dissolution of the ego.”
meaning: on the dissolution

## suptau
ids: ddv-10-w3
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: supti
gender: feminine
case: locative
number: singular
root: svap
note: The stored meaning makes this the noun supti, where -au is only the locative singular. It is not the dual of the participle supta.
meaning: in deep sleep

## bhaved
ids: ddv-10-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhū
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: bhavet before a voiced consonant (deho) is written bhaved.
meaning: becomes

## deho'
ids: ddv-10-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: deha
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: dih
note: dehaḥ before short a is written deho, and the a of api is dropped (the avagraha mark).
meaning: the body

## pya
ids: ddv-10-w6
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: This is api. Its a is dropped after deho, and its final i becomes y before the a of acetanaḥ, which is written in this syllable.
meaning: also

## cetanaḥ
ids: ddv-10-w7
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: acetana
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The negative a- of acetanaḥ is written in the syllable pya before it. It agrees with dehaḥ.
meaning: unconscious

## vikāsā
ids: ddv-10-w9
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: vikāsā is the middle member of ahaṅkāravikāsārdhaḥ, with its final a merged with the a of ardha, so it has no case of its own and the ending sits on rdhaḥ.
meaning: expansion
