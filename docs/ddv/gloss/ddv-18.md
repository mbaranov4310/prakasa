## sarga
ids: ddv-18-w2
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: sarga is the first member of sargabrahmaṇoḥ, and the ending sits on brahmaṇoś.

## brahmaṇoś
ids: ddv-18-w3
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: brahmaṇoḥ (written brahmaṇoś before ca) is genitive or locative dual, and the form does not choose between them.
meaning: and Brahman
