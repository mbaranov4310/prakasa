# ddv-22

## upekṣya
ids: ddv-22-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Absolutive of īkṣ with the prefix upa (upa + īkṣ + ya), “having disregarded.”
meaning: having disregarded

## nāma
ids: ddv-22-w2
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: nāma is the first member of the dvandva nāmarūpe, and the ending sits on rūpe.
meaning: name

## rūpe
ids: ddv-22-w3
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: rūpe, the last member of nāmarūpe, can be nominative or accusative dual or locative singular, and the form alone does not settle which.
meaning: and form

## dve
ids: ddv-22-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: unsure
why: dve is nominative or accusative dual of dvi, feminine or neuter.
meaning: the two

## sac
ids: ddv-22-w5
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: sac is sat before c, the first member of saccidānandatatparaḥ, and the ending sits on tatparaḥ.
meaning: Existence

## cidā
ids: ddv-22-w6
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: cidā is cit (t written d before a vowel) joined to the first ā of ānanda, a front member of saccidānandatatparaḥ whose ending sits on tatparaḥ.
meaning: Consciousness

## nanda
ids: ddv-22-w7
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: nanda is ānanda with its first ā written on the previous word, a front member of saccidānandatatparaḥ whose ending sits on tatparaḥ.
meaning: Bliss

## tatparaḥ
ids: ddv-22-w8
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: saccidānandatatpara
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
note: The nominative ending of the whole compound saccidānandatatparaḥ.
meaning: intent on

## samādhiṃ
ids: ddv-22-w9
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: samādhi
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: singular
root: dhā
note: Built from dhā with sam and ā. samādhim before a consonant, written with anusvāra.
meaning: samādhi

## sarvadā
ids: ddv-22-w10
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: always

## kuryād
ids: ddv-22-w11
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: kṛ
class: tan
tense: potential
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: kuryāt before h, with t written d.
meaning: one should practise

## dhṛdaye
ids: ddv-22-w12
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: dhṛdaye is hṛdaye after kuryād (h written dh), and hṛdaye is locative singular or nominative or accusative dual of the neuter hṛdaya.
meaning: in the heart

## vā'
ids: ddv-22-w13
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: vā before athavā; the avagraha marks the a of athavā merged into vā.
meaning: or

## thavā
ids: ddv-22-w14
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: athavā, with its first a merged into the preceding vā.
meaning: or else

## bahiḥ
ids: ddv-22-w15
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: outside
