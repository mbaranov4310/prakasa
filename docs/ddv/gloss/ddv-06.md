# ddv-06

## cic
ids: ddv-06-w1
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: cic is cit, the first member of the compound cicchāyāveśataḥ, and the ending sits on veśato.
meaning: Consciousness

## chāyā''
ids: ddv-06-w2
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: chāyā is a middle member of the compound cicchāyāveśataḥ, and the ending sits on veśato.
meaning: reflection

## veśato
ids: ddv-06-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: āveśataḥ, a frozen ablative in -taḥ from āveśa (ā + viś), “from the entry”; its ā is marked by the avagraha on chāyā'', and -taḥ is written -to before the voiced b of buddhau.
meaning: from the entry
