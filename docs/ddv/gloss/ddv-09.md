# ddv-09

## sambandhinoḥ
ids: ddv-09-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: unsure
why: sambandhinoḥ is genitive or locative dual of sambandhin, masculine or neuter, so the case and gender are not settled by the form.
meaning: of the two that are related

## sator
ids: ddv-09-w2
kind: participle
judgment: unsure
why: sator is satoḥ before a vowel, and satoḥ is genitive or locative dual of the present participle sat (root as), masculine or neuter.
meaning: of those taken as real

## nā
ids: ddv-09-w3
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: na joined with asti (na + asti → nāsti); the long ā carries the a of asti.
meaning: not

## sti
ids: ddv-09-w4
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: as
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: This is asti; its initial a merged into nā (na + asti → nāsti).
meaning: there is

## nivṛttiḥ
ids: ddv-09-w5
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: nivṛtti
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: vṛt
note: Built from vṛt with the prefix ni.
meaning: cessation

## sahajasya
ids: ddv-09-w6
kind: adjective
judgment: unsure
why: sahajasya is genitive singular of the a-stem sahaja, but it can be masculine or neuter and the noun it describes is not in the line.
meaning: of the natural (identification)

## tu
ids: ddv-09-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
meaning: but

## karma
ids: ddv-09-w8
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: karma is the first member of karmakṣayāt; the ending sits on kṣayāt.
meaning: of karma

## kramādu
ids: ddv-09-w13
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: The written syllable du carries the u of ubhe, and kramāt “in order” can be a live ablative of krama or a frozen adverb.
meaning: in order

## bhe
ids: ddv-09-w14
kind: pronoun
judgment: unsure
why: This is ubhe with its u written on kramādu, and ubhe can be nominative or accusative dual, feminine or neuter.
meaning: both
