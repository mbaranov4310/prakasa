# ddv-04

## kāmaḥ
ids: ddv-04-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: kāma
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: kam
meaning: desire

## saṅkalpa
ids: ddv-04-w2
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: saṅkalpa is the first member of saṅkalpasandehau, and the ending sits on sandehau.
meaning: determination

## sandehau
ids: ddv-04-w3
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: sandehau, the last member of the dvandva saṅkalpasandehau, is nominative or accusative dual of sandeha.
meaning: and doubt

## śraddhā'
ids: ddv-04-w4
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: śraddhā is the first member of śraddhā'śraddhe, and the ending sits on the next word.
meaning: faith

## śraddhe
ids: ddv-04-w5
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: This is aśraddhe with its a elided after śraddhā (the avagraha), and -e on the feminine ā-stem is nominative or accusative dual.
meaning: and lack of faith

## dhṛtī
ids: ddv-04-w6
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: dhṛti is the first member of dhṛtītare (dhṛti + itare), and the ending sits on the next word.
meaning: steadiness

## tare
ids: ddv-04-w7
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: This is itare with its i merged into dhṛtī, and the dual ending -e is nominative or accusative.
meaning: and its opposite

## hrīr
ids: ddv-04-w8
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: hrīḥ (written hrīr before a voiced consonant) is nominative or vocative singular, since the monosyllabic ī-stem hrī has the same form for both.
meaning: modesty

## dhīr
ids: ddv-04-w9
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: dhīḥ (written dhīr before a voiced consonant) is nominative or vocative singular, since the monosyllabic ī-stem dhī has the same form for both.
meaning: understanding

## bhīri
ids: ddv-04-w10
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: The stored form bhīri carries the first i of iti, so the ending of bhī cannot be read from this word alone.
meaning: fear

## tye
ids: ddv-04-w11
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: This is iti: its first i is written on bhīri, its final i becomes y before a vowel, and the e belongs to evam.
meaning: thus

## vamādīn
ids: ddv-04-w12
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: evamādi
gender: masculine
case: accusative
number: plural
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: The e of evam is written on tye. No noun is expressed, and the masculine plural agrees with the understood mental states.
meaning: and so on

## bhāsayatye
ids: ddv-04-w13
kind: verb
judgment: obvious
root: bhās
tense: present
voice: parasmaipada
person: 3rd
number: singular
note: Causative of bhās. bhāsayati becomes bhāsayaty before the e of ekadhā, which is written here.
meaning: illumines

## kadhā
ids: ddv-04-w14
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: This is the adverb ekadhā, whose e is written on bhāsayatye.
meaning: as one
