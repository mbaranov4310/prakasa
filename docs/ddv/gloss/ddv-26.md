# ddv-26

## svā
ids: ddv-26-w1
kind: adjective
judgment: unsure
why: svā is sva fused with the a- of anubhūti at the front of the compound svānubhūtirasāveśāt, and the ending sits on veśād.
meaning: one’s own

## nubhūti
ids: ddv-26-w2
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: nubhūti is the member anubhūti (its a- written on svā) inside svānubhūtirasāveśāt, and the ending sits on veśād.
meaning: experience

## rasā
ids: ddv-26-w3
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: rasā is the member rasa fused with the ā- of āveśa inside svānubhūtirasāveśāt, and the ending sits on veśād.
meaning: taste

## veśād
ids: ddv-26-w4
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: svānubhūtirasāveśa
gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: singular
root: viś
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: This is the last member āveśa (ā-viś), its ā- written on rasā. āveśāt before a voiced consonant is written āveśād; it is still ablative.
meaning: from absorption

## dṛśya
ids: ddv-26-w5
kind: participle
judgment: unsure
why: dṛśya is the front member of the dvandva dṛśyaśabdau, and the ending sits on śabdāvu.
meaning: seen

## śabdāvu
ids: ddv-26-w6
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: śabdau (written śabdāv before the u- of upekṣya, which is stored here) is nominative or accusative dual of the dvandva dṛśyaśabda.
meaning: and word

## pekṣya
ids: ddv-26-w7
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: Absolutive in -ya of īkṣ with upa, “having disregarded.” The u- of upekṣya is stored on śabdāvu.
meaning: having disregarded

meaning: but
