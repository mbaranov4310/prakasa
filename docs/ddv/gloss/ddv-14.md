# ddv-14

## sṛṣṭir
ids: ddv-14-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: sṛṣṭi
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: sṛj
note: sṛṣṭiḥ before a voiced consonant.
meaning: creation

## nāma
ids: ddv-14-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: A frozen nāma, “by name / so called,” not a live case of nāman.
meaning: is called

## brahma
ids: ddv-14-w3
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: brahma is the first member of brahmarūpe, and the ending sits on rūpe.
meaning: Brahman

## rūpe
ids: ddv-14-w4
kind: adjective
judgment: obvious
stem: brahmarūpa
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
compound: bahuvrīhi
note: The form is also a neuter dual, but the meaning “in” settles the locative, and it agrees with vastuni.
meaning: in that which has the nature of

## sac
ids: ddv-14-w5
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: sat, written sac before c, is the first member of saccidānandavastuni, and the ending sits on vastuni.
meaning: Existence

## cidā
ids: ddv-14-w6
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: cidā is cit joined to the first vowel of ānanda inside saccidānandavastuni, and the ending sits on vastuni.
meaning: Consciousness

## nanda
ids: ddv-14-w7
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: nanda is the rest of ānanda, a front member of saccidānandavastuni, and the ending sits on vastuni.
meaning: Bliss

number: singular

## abdhau

## phenā
ids: ddv-14-w10
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: phenā is phena joined to the first vowel of ādi, a front member of phenādivat, and the form ends on the next word.

## divat
ids: ddv-14-w11
kind: indeclinable

meaning: and so on, like
