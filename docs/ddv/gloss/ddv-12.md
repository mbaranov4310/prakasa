## mano'
ids: ddv-12-w1
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: mano' is manas as the front of the compound mano'haṅkṛtyupādānam, so the ending sits on haṅkṛtyupādānaṃ.
meaning: mind

## haṅkṛtyupādānaṃ
ids: ddv-12-w2
kind: adjective
judgment: unsure
why: This is the last member of mano'haṅkṛtyupādānam, and its -am ending can be neuter nominative or accusative singular, or masculine accusative singular.
meaning: having ego as material
