# ddv-29

## stabdhī
ids: ddv-29-w1
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: stabdhī is the first member of stabdhībhāvaḥ. The ending sits on bhāvo.
meaning: stillness

## bhāvo
ids: ddv-29-w2
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: stabdhībhāva
gender: masculine
case: nominative
number: singular
root: bhū
note: bhāvaḥ before a voiced consonant.
meaning: a state of

## rasā
ids: ddv-29-w3
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: rasa is the first member of rasāsvādāt, and its final a has merged with the ā of āsvāda. The ending sits on svādāt.
meaning: rasa

## svādāt
ids: ddv-29-w4

gender: masculine
case: ablative
number: singular
root: svad
compound: tatpuruṣa
note: āsvāda is ā + svad. Its opening ā is written in the sandhi with rasa.
meaning: from tasting
