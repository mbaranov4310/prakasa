# ddv-19

## atrā
ids: ddv-19-w1
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: atra before api. The final a of atra and the initial a of api merge into ā.
meaning: here

## pyā
ids: ddv-19-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: This is api. Its a is merged into atrā, its i becomes y before the ā that begins āvṛti, and that ā is written here.
meaning: too

## vṛti
ids: ddv-19-w3
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: vṛti is the front of the compound āvṛtināśena, with its ā written in pyā, and the ending sits on nāśena.
meaning: of the veiling
