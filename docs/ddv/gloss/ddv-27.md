# ddv-27

## hṛdī
ids: ddv-27-w1
kind: noun
judgment: obvious
stem: hṛd
gender: neuter
case: locative
number: singular
note: hṛdi before iva. The final i and the i of iva fuse into the long ī of hṛdīva.
meaning: in the heart

## va
ids: ddv-27-w2
kind: indeclinable
judgment: obvious
note: This is iva. Its i fused with the final i of hṛdi.
meaning: as

## bāhya
ids: ddv-27-w3
kind: adjective
judgment: unsure
why: bāhya is the first member of bāhyadeśe. The ending sits on deśe.
meaning: outer

## pi
ids: ddv-27-w5
