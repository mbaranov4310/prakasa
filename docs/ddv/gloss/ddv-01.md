# ddv-01

## rūpaṃ
ids: ddv-01-w1
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: rūpaṃ is nominative or accusative singular of the neuter rūpa.
meaning: form / object of seeing

## dṛśyaṃ
ids: ddv-01-w2
kind: participle
judgment: unsure
why: dṛśyaṃ is the neuter singular of the gerundive dṛśya, and the form is nominative or accusative.
meaning: seen / perceived

## locanaṃ
ids: ddv-01-w3

## dṛk
ids: ddv-01-w4, ddv-01-w7
kind: noun
judgment: unsure
why: dṛk is the singular of the root noun dṛś, whose gender the form does not fix, and as a neuter it would be nominative or accusative.
meaning: the seer

## dṛśyā
ids: ddv-01-w10
kind: participle
judgment: obvious
root: dṛś
participle: potential (-ya)
stem: dṛśya
gender: feminine
case: nominative
number: plural
note: dṛśyāḥ before a voiced consonant, so the visarga drops. Gerundive in -ya. The -āḥ ending is also accusative, and it agrees with vṛttayaḥ.
meaning: the seen (pl.)

## dhī
ids: ddv-01-w11

judgment: unsure

meaning: of the intellect
