```samhita
कामः सङ्कल्पसन्देहौ श्रद्धाऽश्रद्धे धृतीतरे ।
ह्रीर्धीर्भीरित्येवमादीन् भासयत्येकधा चितिः ॥
```

```samhita_iast
kāmaḥ saṅkalpasandehau śraddhā'śraddhe dhṛtītare |
hrīrdhīrbhīrityevamādīn bhāsayatyekadhā citiḥ ||
```

```translation
Consciousness illumines, as a unity, desire, determination and doubt, faith and want of faith, steadiness and its opposite, modesty, understanding, fear, and such other mental states.
```

```phrase
Desire, resolve and doubt, faith and lack of faith, steadiness and its opposite —
---
modesty, understanding, fear, and the rest: Consciousness illumines them all as one.
```
