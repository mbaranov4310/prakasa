```samhita
नीलपीतस्थूलसूक्ष्मह्रस्वदीर्घादि भेदतः ।
नानाविधानि रूपाणि पश्येल्लोचनमेकधा ॥
```

```samhita_iast
nīlapītasthūlasūkṣmahrasvadīrghādi bhedataḥ |
nānāvidhāni rūpāṇi paśyellocanamekadhā ||
```

```translation
Forms appear as various on account of distinctions such as blue, yellow, gross, subtle, short, and long. The eye, remaining one, perceives them.
```

```phrase
Forms differ as blue, yellow, gross, subtle, short, long, and so on.
---
The eye sees those many forms while remaining one.
```
