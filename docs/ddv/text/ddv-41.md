```samhita
व्यावहारिकजीवो यस्तज्जगद्व्यावहारिकम् ।
सत्यं प्रत्येति मिथ्येति मन्यते पारमार्थिकः ॥
```

```samhita_iast
vyāvahārikajīvo yastajjagadvyāvahārikam |
satyaṃ pratyeti mithyeti manyate pāramārthikaḥ ||
```

```translation
The empirical jīva takes that empirical world as true; the absolute thinks it false.
```

```phrase
The empirical jīva (takes) that empirical world

as true; the absolute thinks it false.
```
