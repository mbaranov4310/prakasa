```samhita
नोदेति नास्तमेत्येषा न वृद्धिं याति न क्षयम् ।
स्वयं विभात्यथान्यानि भासयेत्साधनं विना ॥
```

```samhita_iast
nodeti nāstametyeṣā na vṛddhiṃ yāti na kṣayam |
svayaṃ vibhātyathānyāni bhāsayetsādhanaṃ vinā ||
```

```translation
This Consciousness neither rises nor sets; it does not increase or decay. Shining of itself, it illumines all else without any other aid.
```

```phrase
This (Consciousness) does not rise or set, grow or decay.
---
It shines of itself, and lights up everything else without any other means.
```
