```samhita
चिच्छायाऽऽवेशतो बुद्धौ भानं धीस्तु द्विधा स्थिता ।
एकाहङ्कृतिरन्या स्यादन्तःकरणरूपिणी ॥
```

```samhita_iast
cicchāyā''veśato buddhau bhānaṃ dhīstu dvidhā sthitā |
ekāhaṅkṛtiranyā syādantaḥkaraṇarūpiṇī ||
```

```translation
Buddhi appears to shine by the entry of the reflection of Consciousness. Intelligence is of two kinds: one is egoity, the other is the inner organ.
```

```phrase
By the entry of the reflection of Consciousness, Buddhi appears luminous; that intelligence stands in two ways.
---
One is egoity; the other is the inner organ (mind).
```
