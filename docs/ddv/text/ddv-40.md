```samhita
प्रातिभासिकजीवो यस्तज्जगत् प्रातिभासिकम् ।
वास्तवं मन्यतेऽन्यस्तु मिथ्येति व्यावहारिकः ॥
```

```samhita_iast
prātibhāsikajīvo yastajjagat prātibhāsikam |
vāstavaṃ manyate'nyastu mithyeti vyāvahārikaḥ ||
```

```translation
The apparent jīva takes that apparent world as real; another, the empirical jīva, thinks it false.
```

```phrase
The apparent jīva (takes) that apparent world

as real; the other, the empirical, thinks it false.
```
