```samhita
रूपं दृश्यं लोचनं दृक् तद्दृश्यं दृक्तु मानसम् ।
दृश्या धीवृत्तयः साक्षी दृगेव न तु दृश्यते ॥
```

```samhita_iast
rūpaṃ dṛśyaṃ locanaṃ dṛk taddṛśyaṃ dṛktu mānasam |
dṛśyā dhīvṛttayaḥ sākṣī dṛgeva na tu dṛśyate ||
```

```translation
Form is perceived and the eye is its perceiver. The eye is perceived and the mind is its perceiver. The mind with its modifications is perceived, and the Witness is the perceiver. But the Witness is not perceived.
```

```phrase
Form is seen; the eye is its seer. That (eye) is seen; the mind is its seer.
---
The mind’s modifications are seen; the Witness alone is the seer — and it is not seen.
```
