# ddv-13

## complement
from: ddv-13-w1
to: ddv-13-w2
judgment: obvious
note: śakti is the first member of śaktidvayaṃ, “a pair of powers.” It has no case of its own.

## complement
from: ddv-13-w4
judgment: unsure
why: māyāyāḥ is genitive or ablative, and as a genitive it can belong to dvayam or to śakti inside the compound.

## describes
from: ddv-13-w7

note: rūpakam matches dvayam in case, whether that case is nominative or accusative.
